Exact degree-two obstruction to blind fiber recovery
===================================================
Root derivation, November15. Needs independent audit before theorem use.

Fix the known simple cycle base C_k, k>=3. Sample an N-lift by independent
uniform edge matchings, then hide the k equal fibers by uniform relabeling
of all kN vertices. The task is the UNORDERED fiber partition (not a base
orientation or a gauge choice). Suppose only the final labeled graph G
is observed. A canonical spanning-tree gauge leaves one uniform monodromy
permutation pi in S_N. The number R of connected components of G is the
number of cycles of pi; every component is a cycle of length k times the
corresponding permutation-cycle length.

For a component cycle of length k*l, every locally bijective map to C_k
is determined by its first image (k choices) and direction (2 choices),
so there are exactly 2k maps. Each puts l vertices in every fiber. Thus
for G with R components there are (2k)^R valid LABELED covering maps,
all with global fiber sizes N. Two maps produce the same UNORDERED fiber
partition iff they differ by ONE GLOBAL automorphism of C_k, of which
there are 2k. Therefore the number of distinct consistent fiber partitions
is exactly (2k)^(R-1).

The hidden-fiber prior is uniform and the matching likelihood is constant
for every valid covering map: with a canonical vertex order within each
fiber, the observed graph fixes every edge permutation. Hence the posterior
is uniform on these unordered partitions. Its Bayes-optimal conditional
success is (2k)^(1-R), achieved by choosing any consistent partition.

For a uniform permutation pi, the standard exact cycle generating function
is E[t^R]=t(t+1)...(t+N-1)/N!. Consequently the optimal success probability
for blind unordered-fiber recovery is EXACTLY

 P_opt = 2k * Gamma(N+1/(2k)) /
                  [Gamma(1/(2k))*Gamma(N+1)]
       ~ [2k/Gamma(1/(2k))] N^(-1+1/(2k)).

Check N=1: success1. For k=3,N=2: monodromy identity has two components
and success1/6; transposition has one and success1; average7/12, matching
the formula. No computational limitation is involved: success tends zero
even with unlimited computation, even with the base supplied in advance.

This contrasts with the candidate regular-frame theorem at every d>=3.
It would give a sharp branching-degree boundary for blind random-lift
fiber recovery, with an explicit degree-two Bayes exponent. Conditioning
on the lift being connected changes the model: then R=1 and the cycle
fiber partition is unique; the unconditioned N-lift is connected with
probability1/N. The final theorem must retain this model qualification.

Standard inputs: permutation monodromy/gauge and the cycle-count rising
factorial identity. Historical priority of the recovery formulation is
unverified. The Aut(C_k) quotient, posterior uniformity, and unoriented
cycle-cover map count should be checked carefully by a fresh reviewer.
