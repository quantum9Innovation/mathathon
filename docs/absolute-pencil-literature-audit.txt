Absolute-pencil block method: primary literature audit -- 2026-11-14
===================================================================

Finding and scope
-----------------
Read the actual completed Section9 of docs/block-threshold-audit.txt and
docs/block-absolute-independent-audit.txt before searching. The relevant
comparison is to the full block construction, not an r-times-scalar solve.
Only this dedicated file is changed in this continuation.

Absolute-value preconditioning, preservation of inertia by SPD congruence,
preconditioned Lanczos/Arnoldi counts, block interior eigensolvers,
Chebyshev-filtered random subspaces, and root-squaring/coefficient methods
are established antecedents. The closest combined precedent is
Vecharynski--Yang's preconditioned eigenvalue-counting framework (2016/17),
built on Vecharynski--Knyazev's AV framework (2013).

The specific remaining contribution supported by the present audit is the
uniform weighted quadratic-defect certificate from a coarse Gram proxy,
its conversion of O(alpha) strong action error into O(alpha*h) error in
the original pencil, and its raw-factor block cost. The inspected primary
theorems do not state that combined separate-factor relative spectral-tail
bound. This is a reasoned closest-antecedents comparison, not a global
novelty or SOTA certificate.

1. Original absolute-value preconditioning
------------------------------------------
Eugene Vecharynski and Andrew V. Knyazev, Absolute Value Preconditioning
for Symmetric Indefinite Linear Systems, SIAM J. Sci. Comput.35(2),
A696--A718 (2013):
https://doi.org/10.1137/120886686
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.4530

Primary theorem scope: Theorem2.1 observes that |A|^(-1) gives two-step
PMINRES. Definition2.2 formalizes AV spectral equivalence
delta0*T^(-1)<=|A|<=delta1*T^(-1), with delta0>0. Theorem2.3 bounds
the positive and negative eigenvalues of TA through those of T|A|;
Corollary2.4 places them in [-delta1,-delta0] union [delta0,delta1].
The analysis explicitly permits noncommuting A,T. Its practical construction
is multigrid for a shifted Laplacian, not arbitrary dense product Grams.

Own comparison with the proposed F
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Write the new pencil K=t*Bmass-Q, D=tI+Q0, and F=JJ^T. The proposed
finite construction has -F<K<F and

  0<=Delta:=F-K F^(-1)K<=36hD.

It is an AV-motivated SPD regularization, but it is not claimed to satisfy
the original AV definition with a uniform delta0. At a threshold where K
has a null vector, F stays SPD, so no positive delta0 can satisfy
delta0*F<=|K|. Arbitrarily near such a threshold, the best delta0 can
be arbitrarily small. Thus the original uniformly separated two-interval
convergence theorem cannot simply be invoked with constants independent
of the product's spectral separation.

This distinction is relevant because the new wrapper must allow all
positive thresholds, including repeated eigenvalues and exact threshold
eigenvalues. It tolerates near-zero preconditioned eigenvalues rather than
assuming an AV gap around zero. The weighted additive defect above is the
appropriate certificate for that tolerance. Describing the construction
as a new AV-preconditioning principle would overstate its novelty.

2. Closest counting/sign precedent
----------------------------------
Eugene Vecharynski and Chao Yang, Preconditioned iterative methods for
eigenvalue counts (arXiv2016; book chapter2017):
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.02306
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-62426-6_8

Primary scope: Section2 expresses negative inertia as trace of a step
function and preserves it by C=M(A-tI)M*. Sections3--5 approximate
random quadratic forms by preconditioned Lanczos/Arnoldi quadrature.
Proposition1 gives exact quadrature after p steps when C has p distinct
eigenvalues. Theorem1 identifies the iteration with a Hermite-interpolating
polynomial. Section6 discusses AV and modified incomplete-LDL SPD
preconditioners; construction of a universally good preconditioner is not
part of its theorem. It estimates projector traces, rather than supplying
an exact-rank spectral approximation to a factored product.

Own reduction assessment
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The proposed pencil fits this mathematical model directly: the new
congruence factor is J^(-1), and Sylvester inertia exactly identifies the
number of generalized eigenvalues above t. Hence inertia-preserving AV
preconditioning plus Lanczos is a direct antecedent, not merely a related
application. The distinction lies in the quantitative guarantees needed
after that transformation.

The inspected counting result does not supply a uniform finite
q=~O(sqrt(h/theta)) quantile bound when C can have eigenvalues arbitrarily
close to zero, nor a width-r physical subspace and its original-product
tail guarantee. Its few-step result assumes a favorable clustered spectrum;
the two-step idealization uses |K|^(-1), unavailable or singular at the
thresholds which the new wrapper handles.

Random trace accuracy is also not itself the required ordered-eigenvalue
certificate. Even an exact application of the sign projector needs a
separate sampling error analysis to distinguish adjacent integer counts.
The current method instead estimates the (r+1)-st eigenvalue of I-C via
the per-vector block theorem and uses a deterministic one-sided quotient.
The trace-count paper supplies the overall sign/inertia strategy but not
this particular full-output cost or its successful-event stopping rule.

3. Block interior eigensolvers and a quadratic sign identity
-----------------------------------------------------------
Eugene Vecharynski and Andrew Knyazev, Preconditioned Locally Harmonic
Residual Method for Computing Interior Eigenpairs of Certain Classes of
Hermitian Matrices, SIAM J. Sci. Comput.37(5), S3--S29 (2015):
https://doi.org/10.1137/14098048X
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.0042

Primary scope: Section4/Algorithm2 explicitly gives a block PLHR method
with matrix-block and preconditioner-block kernels. Section5 proposes
T approximately |A-sigma B|^(-1). For the ideal pseudo-inverse AV choice,
it uses I-(T(A-lambda B))^2 as the nullspace projector. Theorem2.1
analyzes an idealized preconditioned residual iteration; the practical
eigensolver and its block extension are assessed numerically. No inspected
theorem gives the present global gap-free rank-r spectral-tail complexity.

Own assessment: the identity C^2 approximately I and extraction of
near-null/selected modes from it are old AV ideas. The new defect
Delta=J(I-C^2)J^T is an approximate version of this algebra. What must be
proved separately is its weighted size O(hD), and how a strong residual
certificate transfers with the extra factor alpha instead of losing h.
The old ideal projector identity alone does not prove that quantitative
transfer for a noncommuting, approximate, regularized AV factor.

4. The strong action transfer: exact additional obligation
---------------------------------------------------------
This is an independent algebraic comparison, not a claim that the lemma
cannot appear elsewhere. Suppose C=J^(-1)KJ^(-T), F=JJ^T and
0<=Delta=F-KF^(-1)K<=epsilonD. If an orthonormal U satisfies

  ||(I-UU^T)(I-C)||<=1+a,

then b=(1+a)^2-1 and w perpendicular to U give

  w^T(C^2-2C)w<=b||w||^2.

Putting w=J^T y on the dual complement gives

  2y^T K y >= y^T K F^(-1)K y-b y^T F y
             =(1-b)y^T K F^(-1)K y-b y^T Delta y
             >=-b*epsilon*y^T D y, when b<=1.

Thus the original-pencil loss is O(a*epsilon), uniformly without an
eigenvalue gap. For epsilon=36h this is O(alpha*h), as required.
The multiplicative b on Delta is the crucial part; simply translating a
Loewner lower bound C>=-O(alpha)I would yield -O(alpha)F instead.

The weaker AV facts alone cannot yield this improvement. For example take
F=D=I and K=-aI. Then -F<K<F and ||I-C||=1+a, but K=-aD rather
than -O(a*h)D for h arbitrarily small. Its defect is (1-a^2)I, and
precisely fails the additional O(hD) hypothesis. This is a concrete reason
that merely citing bounded preconditioned spectrum or inertia would omit
a necessary theorem, even though the complete transfer proof is elementary.

The lift also matters. The dual complement J^T Z^T x perpendicular to U
corresponds to the physical space range(ZJU). Congruence preserves inertia
but does not in general preserve Euclidean eigenspaces or approximation
residuals. Therefore a sign-projector or PLHR subspace theorem is not a
physical spectral-tail theorem until this lift and its norm inequalities
are included. Section9 does include them.

5. Randomized block Krylov and polynomial projection antecedents
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cameron Musco and Christopher Musco, Randomized Block Krylov Methods for
Stronger and Faster Approximate Singular Value Decomposition, NeurIPS2015:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.05477

Primary scope: Algorithm2 and Theorem12 give simultaneous per-vector
quality at block degree ~O(1/sqrt(epsilon)). For target r+1, the compressed
positive matrix yields an additive squared-eigenvalue error at most
epsilon times the next squared singular value. The printed confidence is
99/100. This theorem is exactly the source of the new quantile accuracy;
the ordinary spectral-LRA theorem alone is insufficient for that inference.

Own cost and wrapper assessment: apply the source to T=I-C>=0 and
target r+1. Deterministic compression min--max makes every estimated
mu_(r+1) an upper estimate, so taking the minimum across independent runs
amplifies confidence without selecting or validating a successful run.
The coefficient-power bound supplies the required one-sided Ritz value.
These are valid additional reductions of the published block theorem.

The final width-r Chebyshev-filtered Gaussian space is also within the
classical randomized range-finding/polynomial-filtering paradigm. It needs
only a separation band of width alpha, permits uncontrolled polynomial
growth in the head, and avoids computing a true invariant projector at an
arbitrarily small actual gap. The degree alpha^(-1/2) comes from the usual
endpoint Chebyshev growth. The contribution is its combination with the
weighted original-pencil certificate, not a new Chebyshev construction.

Related primary filtering/counting sources:
  Edoardo Di Napoli, Eric Polizzi, Yousef Saad, Efficient estimation of
  eigenvalue counts in an interval, Numer. Linear Algebra Appl.23 (2016):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.4275
  Sections3--4 use polynomial/rational spectral-projector approximations
  and stochastic trace estimation, including generalized problems.

  Zhongxiao Jia, Kailiang Zhang, A FEAST SVDsolver based on
  Chebyshev--Jackson series for computing partial singular triplets:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.02901
  Its stated target is singular triplets in a specified interval, using
  approximate projectors and subspace iteration. It is an antecedent for
  polynomial spectral slicing; no inspected statement gives the weighted
  h-to-theta error transfer or separate-factor runtime used here.

6. Coefficient quantiles are a classical root-squaring mechanism
---------------------------------------------------------------
Victor Y. Pan, New Progress in Classic Area: Polynomial Root-squaring and
Root-finding (2022), Section1.2, equations2--5:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.01727

This primary account explicitly recalls Dandelin--Lobachevsky--Graeffe
root-squaring and extraction of powered roots from coefficient ratios.
For a matrix V, characteristic coefficients of V^(2^s) use exactly
the same powered roots. Therefore powering and coefficient-ratio recovery
must be presented as a classical mechanism. The new note's elementary
positive-root inequality

  v_j/j <= e_j(V)/e_(j-1)(V) <= (k-j+1)v_j

is a finite, gap-free way to bound that recovery, including repeated roots;
it does not require the separated-root asymptotic in the introductory
Graeffe discussion. No novelty claim for root-squaring itself is justified.

Vincent Neiger, Clement Pernet, Gilles Villard, Computing Krylov iterates
in the time of matrix multiplication, ISSAC2024, Corollary5.2:
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.07345v2

This supplies the deterministic fast Frobenius-form field-operation
subroutine actually needed for characteristic coefficients. The assumption
is an explicit small matrix over the base field. It neither provides the
implicit product threshold nor charges for raw A/B access. Combining it
with repeated squaring is valid in the stated exact arithmetic model;
neither this audit nor the source makes enormous intermediate coefficients
a bounded-word or numerically stable operation.

7. Full block cost and closest-antecedents conclusion
----------------------------------------------------
The applicable new leading block cost is

  ~O(I+G_h+m^(omega+zeta)
      +sqrt(h/theta)*Acal(r+1)
      +k*Lraw^2+Acal(r)+n*r^2
      +np+TMM(n,r,p)),
  Acal(b)=TMM(n,m,b)+TMM(p,m,b),
  Lraw=~O((r+1)*sqrt(h/theta)).

Its q*Acal(r+1) is a block cost. Replacing it by q*I*r in comparison
with fast batched Krylov would be unfair. Conversely, calling an AV or
PLHR preconditioner an O(1) primitive would omit both G_h and its core
construction; a PDE-specific multigrid bound is not a general dense
two-factor setup theorem.

For N=m^(59/50), r=m^(1/10), theta=m^(-21/50), h=m^(-7/100):
q=m^(7/40), Acal(r+1)=m^(109/50+o(1)), and the largest checked
construction exponent is the sketched Gram 3362/1425<59/25. The block
action exponent is 471/200, safe raw-basis QR is at most 31/20, and
physical QR is 69/50. All are below output. Total is N^(2+o(1)); fair
ordinary block Krylov is N^(239/118+o(1)) and named exact Gram is
N^(7056/3481+o(1)). The prior linear-r stochastic accelerated term has
m-exponent 489/200 and does not subsume this regime. These remain
comparisons of applicable upper bounds, not algorithmic lower bounds.

Closest-antecedents conclusion: the architecture 'AV-precondition a shifted
matrix, preserve inertia, and run Krylov/random spectral filtering' is
already explicit in the cited literature. The candidate's potential new
result is a product-specific quantitative theorem: build a smooth coarse
proxy at accounted cost; bound the weighted quadratic defect uniformly
at every threshold; use a strong-action rank-r certificate and dual lift
to retain alpha*h error; and wrap the resulting quantile with no actual
gap or product-condition parameter. No inspected source supplied all of
those obligations or the displayed total runtime. The independent proof
audit supports that theorem conditional on its declared model. Its global
priority still requires assessment beyond this targeted source comparison.

Tyler Chen, Caroline Huber, Ethan Lin, Hajar Zaid, Preconditioning without

Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis65, 63--92 (2026):
https://etna.ricam.oeaw.ac.at/vol.65.2026/pp63-92.dir/pp63-92.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1553/etna_vol65s63

Theorem3.3 proves that augmented block-CG matches or improves a broad

matrix loads. Theorem3.5 separately charges block-Lanczos arithmetic;
Section3.2 explicitly disclaims domination in arithmetic operations or
total matrix products. This is an important current block-method
comparison, but its result is a positive-system solve guarantee, not an
indefinite quantile or physical spectral-tail guarantee. Applying it to
the already transformed positive T=I-C does not supply the missing

Krylov space for that T. Applying it directly to K_t violates its SPD
hypothesis at the interior thresholds in question. No reduction was

weighted-defect argument and preconditioner setup.

Nian Shao and Yuji Nakatsukasa, Convergence analysis of a nonlinear
eigensolver based on rational approximation of the resolvent (2026):
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.10377

Theorems3.2/3.4 analyze poles given accurate rational approximations of
sketched resolvents; constants depend on poles, residues and associated
conditioning. The method samples T(z)^(-1) between probing blocks, then
uses AAA/pole extraction. It does not furnish a uniform raw-factor oracle
for those shifted inverses, nor the required gap-free rank-r product tail
bound. Counting a sampled shifted inverse as a block matvec would omit the
operation that has to be bounded. This is a concrete missing reduction,
not a criticism of the paper's different nonlinear-eigenvalue objective.

Jess Banks, Jorge Garza-Vargas, Archit Kulkarni, Nikhil Srivastava,
Pseudospectral Shattering, the Sign Function, and Diagonalization in Nearly
Matrix Multiplication Time:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10208-022-09577-5

Its spectral divide-and-conquer routines compute sign-based projectors
and deflate explicit dense matrices in nearly MM time. This is close
component-level background, but inputting the true coefficient pencil
requires forming its true Gram information; inputting only the h-accurate
core leaves an h-scale modeling error. The theorem does not state a way
to correct that error from separate A,B within the proposed block cost.

A direct information check explains the latter distinction. Let G=I,
Q1=diag(1+e,1), Q2=diag(1,1+e), and Q0=(1+e/2)I, with e of order h.
The same Q0 is an admissible h-relative proxy for both true Grams. For
any fixed rank-one left space, its orthogonal unit vector x has
max{x^T Q1 x,x^T Q2 x}>=1+e/2, while each best rank-one squared
residual is 1. Thus a procedure using only that proxy cannot guarantee
1+theta relative spectral residual uniformly when theta is much smaller
than h. True-factor corrective actions, such as those in the candidate,
are essential. This is not an obstruction to methods which use them.

The recent 'low-rank matrix-sign' terminology also has a different target:
Chuan He, Zhanwang Deng, Zhaosong Lu, Low-rank Orthogonalization for
Large-scale Matrix Optimization with Applications to Foundation Model
Training:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.11983
It approximates the polar/rectangular sign of an already supplied gradient
matrix through a low-rank projection, and proves optimization stationarity
results. It is not a threshold sign projector with a relative spectral-tail
arithmetic theorem for arbitrary separate factors. It does not subsume
the present result merely by sharing the words 'low rank' and 'sign'.

Cost qualification for the simplified uniform expression
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The full Section9 statement charges k*Lraw^2 for conservative QR, in
addition to q*Acal(r+1). If one writes only

 ~O(I+G_h+m^omega+sqrt(h/theta)*Acal(r+1)+explicit output),

under Lraw<=m, absorbing basis construction into m^omega additionally
requires fast algebra. One can select independent raw columns by fast
rank-profile elimination, form their Gram, perform Cholesky, and multiply
by its inverse factor, all in ~O(m^omega) real operations for dimensions
at most m. Mere width<=m does not absorb conventional O(m*Lraw^2) QR
into m^omega. The concrete displayed parameter instance is already safe
with conventional QR and does not require this refinement.

After this bounded pass, no full-algorithm subsumption was established.
The reasoned claim remains the new proof's accounted correction of coarse
Gram information and its weighted original-product guarantee. Neither a
negative search result nor matching component names determines priority.
