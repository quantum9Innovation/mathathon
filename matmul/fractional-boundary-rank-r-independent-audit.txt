1. CONSTANT-THRESHOLD AND RESIDUAL RATIONAL BOUNDS
------------------------------------------------

For a rank-one class at h=exp(-tau L), replacing the threshold by
exp(-vL) is exactly the original theorem at L'=vL, tau'=tau/v.
The normalized coefficient is v^2*C_alpha(tau/v). Sandwiching a fixed
t*exp(-L) between exp(-(1+eta)L) and exp(-(1-eta)L), then taking
eta down to zero, proves the stated constant-threshold invariance.
Continuity includes tau=0 and both transition points. The same sandwich
is uniform for t in any fixed compact positive interval.

The fixed-[0,2] scalar rational approximation and rational-difference
rank bound use only endpoint spectra and perturbation rank. They do
not require perturbation norm one. A fixed threshold factor changes
L by a constant and therefore gives (5), including residual updates
with norm smaller than one. Positivity is not needed for this auxiliary
singular-count bound.

2. NORM-ONE, SATURATED MULTIPLICITY, AND FIXED SPECTRUM
----------------------------------------------------

PSD E with ||E||=1 has a unit eigenvector w at eigenvalue one.
Subtracting ww^T leaves a PSD residual of rank at most r-1. The
telescoped corrections are positive by operator monotonicity, their
endpoints remain below B, and the first correction obeys the rank-one
joint hypotheses with the same h. Best-rank truncations at epsilon/2
sum to an epsilon-accurate approximant, proving the strict-count upper.

For the lower, direct sums add strict counts and keep operator norm
one. One boundary block supplies C_alpha(tau), while each scaled R=2
block has update eigenvalue 1/2, endpoints <=1, and correction scaled
by the constant 2^(-alpha). Its coefficient remains c0. The resulting
update has exact rank r and spectrum {1,1/2,...,1/2}. Thus both rank
classes have coefficient (r-1)c0+C_alpha(tau).

Peeling s mutually orthogonal eigenvectors at eigenvalue one gives s
unit rank-one steps with the same boundary bound, followed by residual
rank <=r-s. Applying the constant-threshold bounds to s+1 summands
gives the stated upper (r-s)c0+s*C_alpha(tau). Direct sums attain it
with exact rank r and exact saturated multiplicity s. No commutation
between A and E is assumed. When E is a projection, s=r gives
r*C_alpha(tau).

For each fixed subcritical eigenvalue e in (0,1), a constant
1<R_e<1/e exists. Scaling a unit-update lower witness at R_e by e
gives endpoint bound e*R_e<1 and exact update eigenvalue e. The
constant correction scale preserves c0. Thus the full fixed-spectrum
formula is valid. It gives no assertion for moving subcritical spectra.

3. PROJECTION NUCLEAR RESIDUAL AND FINITE CUTOFF
----------------------------------------------

For E=P and an orthonormal basis w_j of its range, all telescoped
rank-one steps end below B<=1+h. The complementary projection
Q=I-P is below every I-w_jw_j^T. Positivity of each D_j therefore
transfers its rank-one trace bound to tr(QD_jQ)<=h^alpha. Summing
gives tr(QDQ)<=r*h^alpha without an additional trace inequality.

With U=(w_1 ... w_r), Y=D^(1/2)U and K=Y^T Y, the matrix
Y*K^dagger*Y^T is the orthogonal projection onto range(Y). Consequently
F=D U K^dagger U^T D is PSD, <=D, and has rank at most r. The range
identity also gives (D-F)U=0, including singular K. Thus the residual
is supported on Q and has trace <=tr(QDQ), proving the nuclear bound.

It follows that the sum after the top r eigenvalues is <=r*h^alpha.
When this quantity is <=epsilon, no (r+1)st eigenvalue is strictly
above epsilon. A=0,B=P attains exactly r counts whenever epsilon<1.
The source's condition (13) and threshold
L>=log(r)/(alpha*tau-1) for tau>1/alpha follow directly. At equality
tau=1/alpha the condition holds for r=1 but not r>1, as qualified.

4. CONNECTED MASS-WEIGHTED PATH TRANSFER
--------------------------------------

Boundary blocks can use the stronger endpoint bound 1+h_L/2 without
changing their leading coefficient: for each fixed eta>0,
exp(-(tau+eta)L)<=h_L/2<=exp(-tau L) eventually. Endpoint-class
monotonicity and continuity of C_alpha then give the required squeeze,
also for tau=0. Pointwise rank-one path equality applies separately at
each finite L; it preserves the unit update norm exactly.

Scaled subcritical path blocks retain exact update eigenvalues e_j
and endpoint norms strictly below one. Their changed edges have
disjoint vertex supports across blocks, so the direct-sum update has
the exact prescribed spectrum and saturated multiplicity.

Joining consecutive path endpoints adds the SAME mass-normalized PSD
bridge matrix C to both A and B. The common block-diagonal mass matrix
is held fixed. Hence E is unchanged exactly, including its spectrum,
norm, rank and saturated multiplicity; no degree renormalization enters.
Every changed edge remains an existing edge in the final connected path.

At each finite L the blocks have endpoint norm <=1+h_L/2. Positive
bridges can be chosen with ||C||<h_L/2 and arbitrarily smaller still.
Fractional-power norm continuity preserves each chosen finite strict
count for sufficiently small bridges. For instance the operator-Holder
bound controls the correction change by <=2*||C||^alpha. The finite
positive count margins and endpoint margin are therefore simultaneously
preserved. No uniform lower bound on bridge sizes or mass ratios is
required as L varies. The graph lower and matrix upper consequently
match for all the stated coefficient classes.

These are valid corollaries of the reviewed rank-one theorem and
classical rational rank upper. The source's corollary label and its
restrictions on runtime, ordinary unit masses and moving subcritical
eigenvalues are appropriate. No independent novelty claim is certified.
