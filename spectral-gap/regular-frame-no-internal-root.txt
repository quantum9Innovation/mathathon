Potential extension to every fixed regular degree frame, including lifts
======================================================================
Root derivation, November15. NEW CANDIDATE, NOT YET AUDITED.

Take a connected fixed degree frame Q with ALL row sums equal to d>=3.
Allow internal degree d_ii=0 or1; cross factors and internal factors are
independent uniform simple regular/biregular graphs. Fixed block ratios
satisfy Ni*dij=Nj*dji. This includes random lifts of a fixed connected
simple d-regular base: d_ii=0, d_ij in {0,1}, each cross factor a random
perfect matching. No even-size restriction except actual admissibility.

The coarse unknown-frame recovery proof may extend with NO lower bound
on internal degrees, by averaging omission over all d physical slots.

For root type i, let X be its finite-alphabet root value and let
Y_1,...,Y_d be the neighbor labels (slot types may all differ). For any
candidate intertwiner equation AY=YR, the sum of neighbors determines
any omitted Y_t from X and the other d-1 labels. Let
 J_it=sum_(s!=t)H(Y_s|X)-H((Y_s)_(s!=t)|X)>=0.
Then exactly
 H(star_i)=H(X)+(d-1)/d *sum_s H(Y_s|X)-(1/d)sum_t J_it.
This needs no exchangeability across different neighbor types. Permuting
slots within one type still supplies the correct single-edge laws.

Substitute into the exact weighted star/edge exponent. Reversibility
Ni*dij=Nj*dji and regularity make EVERY vertex-entropy coefficient zero:
 Ni+(d-1)/d *Ni*d-Ni*d=0.
Therefore, writing E_ij for the number of undirected type-ij edges,
 Phi=-(d-2)/d *sum_(undirected type pairs) E_ij I_ij
        -sum_i (Ni/d)sum_t J_it <=0.
Internal pair terms use E_ii=Ni*dii/2, so the factor2 is correct.

Consequences to verify:
1. For any fixed candidate quotient R and finite field vector alphabet,
   exact configuration type counting gives a polynomial first moment
   for the number of modular intertwiners AY=YR, ALL admissible sizes.
   The aggregate-fiber refinement may give an exponent independent of d.
2. For real one-hot labels, Phi0 forces EVERY I_ij=0 and EVERY J_it=0.
   Thus root and neighbor are independent and retained neighbors are
   conditionally independent given the root. Let V_s be the trace
   variance of the marginal neighbor label at slot s. Under the exact
   INTEGER equation and conditional independence, the omitted label has
        V_t=sum_(s!=t) V_s,
   for EVERY t. Summing gives (d-2)sum_s V_s=0. Since d>=3, every
   neighbor root law is a point mass. Connectedness forces all old-block
   root laws point masses, hence candidate partitions are coarsenings.
   Candidate R need not have distinct rows or be nonsingular: conditional
   variance avoids any injectivity assumption on the target root image.
3. Compactness yields a strict macroscopic entropy gap. Near point maps,
   the already proved clean-vertex QT=TR argument plus vector-valued
   no-unique-neighbor exposure applies since minimum degree is d>=3.
4. Enumerating finite candidate R with at most a fixed K classes and row
   sums<=d, binary intertwiners, exact one-hot checks, and selecting the
   finest partition should recover the unknown planted frame/partition.

This would prove polynomial recovery of the FINEST equitable partition
among at most K classes in random lifts of every fixed connected regular
base of degree>=3, without any old/new eigenvalue gap. It does NOT imply
one-dimensionality of an embedded eigenvalue or exclusion of new atoms.
It is compatible with multiple coarse partitions of a base such as the
cube: those are coarsenings of the finer base-fiber partition.

Critical audit points: all-slot entropy factors and internal doublecounts,
zero-phase conditional variance when slot types differ, exact configuration
counts for internal degree0/1 and cross matchings, smallest-degree support
exposure, unknown candidate-frame labels and finite K quantifiers, and
primary prior art on random-cover uniqueness/base reconstruction.
The d=2 boundary has c=0 and the variance sum vanishes, so this argument
does not assert recovery for cycles or degree-two covers.
