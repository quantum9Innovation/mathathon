Main result
-----------
For fixed 0<alpha<1 and integer d>=3, let W_d,alpha(epsilon) be the
supremum of the strict relative fractional correction count over finite
connected simple unweighted graphs of maximum degree at most d, when
one existing unit edge is doubled. Then

 lim_(epsilon->0) epsilon W_d,alpha(epsilon)
                       = alpha log[2d/(d+2)].

The same coefficient holds when the edge must be a bridge, when hosts
must be bipartite, and with both restrictions simultaneously. In
particular the requested cubic bridge coefficient is alpha log(6/5).

The complete standalone proof is local-global-bridge-budget.tex,
compiled without warnings or overfull boxes to
local-global-bridge-budget.pdf (11 pages).
The filename retains the original bridge-focused scope; the final
theorem also includes the independently audited all-existing-edge
extension supplied by the root's free/wired research session.

Proof components
----------------
1. Positive trace-class infinite relative response C, with
   tr C<=alpha tau and logdet_F(I+C)=alpha log(1+tau), even when the
   bottom of the Laplacian spectrum is zero. This uses A+delta I and
   dominated convergence in trace norm, with no inverse-domain shortcut.

2. Rooted finite-support generalized Ritz transfer: only x^alpha
   functional calculus is transported. It forces every fixed number
   of local eigenvalues into the finite response. Subtracting the
   entire local determinant leaves at most alpha log[2/(1+tau_wired)]
   for epsilon times the strict count in the bridge case.

3. The bridge half capacities are at most d-2, hence tau_wired>=2/d.
   For arbitrary local edges, deleting the edge and retaining boundary
   vertices individually during Kron reduction proves
     Cw(H;u,v)-Cf(H;u,v)
       <= cap_H(u)cap_H(v)/(cap_H(u)+cap_H(v)) <= (d-2)/2.
   Therefore w>=r/[1+(d-2)r/2] for local free/wired resistances r,w.
   Finite leverages have limsup<=r. The same residual logarithmic
   budget is bounded by alpha log[2d/(d+2)] for all existing edges.

4. The lower construction shares Q repeated colored leaves among the
   Q ports of ONE reservoir per color. Two q=d-1 ary access trees give
   ideal terminal impedance
     diag(ell_i/Q)+[r_b+2(1-1/Q)/(d-2)]11^T+R_bottom/Q.
   Uniform-gap reservoirs have port contact matrices 0<=H_i<=R I.
   Equal-current trial flows show that true impedance differs by a
   positive matrix <=(R/Q)(I+11^T), uniformly in all contacts.
   The limiting pair is
     K=(diag(t_i)+d/(d-2)11^T)^(-1),
     K'=(diag(t_i)+(d+2)/(2(d-2))11^T)^(-1).
   Its reverse log pivots can be made exactly s/m for ANY integer m,
   for every s<log[2d/(d+2)]. Separated masses flatten the fractional
   responses to exp(alpha s/m)-1. Arbitrary m avoids a dyadic liminf
   loss. A compressed-resolvent fractional-integral proof transfers
   these masses to ordinary unit-mass expander reservoirs by generalized
   Ritz. The naive single-leaf 1/m^2 increment is never used.

5. Bipartite double-cover reservoirs with selected length-three-edge
   ports retain a uniform gap. Identical block-label depths make the
   attachment colors consistent. The same coefficient follows.

6. Saturation rigidity: every rooted local limit of a sequence attaining
   the sharp count coefficient is the d-regular tree, and finite edge
   leverages tend to one. A strict finite-distance branching deficit
   makes a half capacity strictly less than d-2.

floating-point checks, not the lower proof or a finite-precision theorem.
