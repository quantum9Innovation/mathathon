SPECTRAL GAPS GIVE LOGARITHMIC-RANK FRACTIONAL GRAPH UPDATES
=========================================================

This deduction combines the spectral-gap input actually reviewed in
spectral-gap/ with the matrix-function update objective in docs/. Its
mechanism is classical polynomial analyticity and low-rank telescoping.
It is a concrete conditional algorithm and random-regular corollary,
not a new proof of Friedman's theorem or a certified novelty claim.

1. DETERMINISTIC THEOREM
=======================
Let L,L' be connected weighted undirected graph Laplacians on N vertices.
Let k be the number of distinct changed unordered edges (merge repeated
changes first), and let M count edges in their union. Write

  E=L'-L=U J U^T,
  U_e=sqrt(|d_e|)(e_u-e_v), J=diag(sign d_e).

Signed changes are allowed, provided the endpoint edge weights remain
nonnegative. Suppose supplied constants 0<gamma<Lambda satisfy

  gamma I <= L|_(1-perp), L'|_(1-perp) <= Lambda I.       (1)

Fix 0<alpha<1 and 0<epsilon<1. There is a deterministic exact-real
algorithm returning P,Q of width kq, with

  ||PQ^T-(L'^alpha-L^alpha)|| <=epsilon||E||^alpha,       (2)

where, putting

  c=(Lambda+gamma)/2,
  r=(Lambda-gamma)/(Lambda+gamma), sigma=1-r,

it suffices to choose any integer q>=1 satisfying

  2 alpha r^q/sigma <=epsilon.                          (3)

Thus q=O_(Lambda/gamma)(log(2/epsilon)). An explicit conservative choice is

  q >= sigma^-1 log(max(1,2 alpha/(epsilon sigma))).     (4)

The logarithm is not a required unit-cost operation: testing (3) with
successively generated r^q determines an admissible q in O(q) scalar
arithmetic. Both q and coefficient generation are charged below.

Construction, including every factor entry, costs

  O((M+N) k q),                                         (5)

using only sparse endpoint products, scalar operations and square roots
when alpha=1/2. General fixed alpha additionally needs the ONE scalar
value c^(alpha-1) charged/evaluated in the scalar model. The coefficients
themselves use arithmetic with alpha. Dense expansion adds O(N^2 kq),
and dense output storage is O(N^2). All constants in (5) are ordinary
arithmetic-operation constants; there is no fast matrix multiplication
or eigendecomposition in the algorithm.

The algorithm outputs the correction. It does not require L^alpha, but
recovering the full updated function needs a base representation.
The exact matrix PQ^T is symmetric, although its two factors differ.
The norm guarantee concerns exact arithmetic; finite-word numerical
stability has not been proved.

If E=0, return zero. The degenerate interval gamma=Lambda forces
L=L'=gamma(I-11^T/N), hence E=0; it can be handled before the theorem.

2. WHY NO DENSE PROJECTOR OR SMALL-UPDATE ASSUMPTION IS NEEDED
===========================================================
Let H=1-perp. Every edge incidence column lies in H, and both symmetric
Laplacians preserve H. Their common kernel is span{1}, because (1)
gives a strictly positive spectral gap. Define sparse affine actions

  X=L/c-I, Y=L'/c-I.

Although X,Y have eigenvalue -1 on constants, their RESTRICTIONS to H
have norm at most r<1. Every update column and every vector generated
below remains in H in exact arithmetic. All norm estimates involving
r are applied only on H. Nothing requires materializing 11^T/N,
computing a basis for H, or applying a dense projection.

For arbitrary matrices X,Y and j>=1, noncommutative telescoping gives

  Y^j-X^j=sum_(l=0)^(j-1) X^l(Y-X)Y^(j-1-l).           (6)

Restricting to H and using Y-X=E/c yields

  ||Y^j-X^j|| <= j r^(j-1)||E||/c.                      (7)

On constants the difference is zero, so (7) also bounds its FULL
operator norm. The factor ||E|| in this estimate is the reason no
lower bound on the update magnitude appears in q.

3. INTRINSIC ERROR FROM THE BINOMIAL TAIL
=======================================
Put b_j=binom(alpha,j), b_0=1. For 0<alpha<1,

  b_1=alpha, b_(j+1)=b_j (alpha-j)/(j+1),
  j|b_j|=alpha product_(i=1)^(j-1)(1-alpha/i)<=alpha
  for every j>=1.                                      (8)

The scalar binomial series and uniform convergence on [-r,r] give,
on H,

  L^alpha=c^alpha sum_(j>=0) b_j X^j,
  L'^alpha=c^alpha sum_(j>=0) b_j Y^j.

Their difference is therefore approximated by

  F_q=c^alpha sum_(j=1)^q b_j(Y^j-X^j).                (9)

The two series are only invoked on H; on constants both the true
difference and F_q are zero. No value of a truncated polynomial at
the kernel has to approximate zero individually.

Equations (7),(8) and a geometric series prove

  ||(L'^alpha-L^alpha)-F_q||
   <=c^(alpha-1)||E|| sum_(j=q+1)^infinity
                                   j|b_j| r^(j-1)
   <=alpha c^(alpha-1)||E|| r^q/sigma.                  (10)

Both endpoint restrictions lie in [gamma,Lambda]. Consequently

  -(Lambda-gamma)I <= E|_H <=(Lambda-gamma)I,
  ||E||<=Lambda-gamma<=Lambda.

Since Lambda/c<=2 and 1-alpha in (0,1),

  c^(alpha-1)||E||^(1-alpha) <=2^(1-alpha)<=2.          (11)

Combining (10),(11),(3) proves (2). This estimate remains valid for
arbitrarily tiny, cancelling, or singular signed perturbations.

For (4), use r^q<=exp(-sigma q). This proves the explicit degree
choice. The constants are conservative;
Chebyshev or rational approximation can improve condition dependence.

4. COMPLETE FACTORS BY FORWARD POWERS AND BACKWARD HORNER
=======================================================
Substitute E=UJU^T into (6), then (9), and define for 0<=l<=q-1

  V_l=X^l U,
  W_l=sum_(j=l+1)^q b_j Y^(j-1-l)U.

The exact factor identity is

  F_q=c^(alpha-1) sum_(l=0)^(q-1) V_l J W_l^T.         (12)

Generate V forward:

  V_0=U, V_(l+1)=L V_l/c-V_l.

Generate W by backward Horner:

  W_q=0, W_l=b_(l+1)U+L'W_(l+1)/c-W_(l+1).

Concatenate the k-column blocks

  P_l=c^(alpha-1)V_l J, Q_l=W_l.

Their product is exactly (12), of rank at most kq. Each forward or
backward recurrence uses one sparse endpoint times N-by-k block and
O(Nk) vector arithmetic. There are O(q) recurrences, so (5) includes
all factors. Scalar coefficients need O(q) operations. Initial sparse
U needs O(k) work and O(Nk) if stored densely. No N k^2 or k^3 term
is hidden by orthogonalization or a dense small core.

The displayed sum is symmetric because it equals the polynomial
difference (9) EXACTLY. If a signed symmetric factor Z Sigma Z^T
is required, apply the elementary sum-and-difference identity to each
term in (12), giving width at most 2kq in the same work order.
No sign-definiteness of the approximation is asserted.

5. ACTUAL SPECTRAL-GAP INPUT AND A RANDOM-REGULAR COROLLARY
========================================================
The imported project already uses the following source in its
finite-defect theorem:

  spectral-gap/snapshot/results/050-finite-defect.md, Section 2;
  spectral-gap/snapshot/results/spectral-research/batch-050/
      review-m2-accepted.md.

For a uniform simple d-regular graph G_N, fixed integer d>=3, and
admissible N with Nd even, Friedman/Bordenave gives

  max(lambda_2(A_N), |lambda_N(A_N)|)
       <=2sqrt(d-1)+zeta

with probability tending to one, for every fixed zeta>0. The condition
lambda_2, rather than a definition omitting repeated eigenvalue d,
ensures connectivity on this event.

Choose 0<zeta<d-2sqrt(d-1), and set

  gamma=d-2sqrt(d-1)-zeta>0.

For the combinatorial Laplacian L=dI-A_N, the good event guarantees
L|_H>=gamma I. Also L<=2d I deterministically. Fix a conductance
budget W_0>=0, independent of N, and consider ANY k edge additions
whose total added weight is at most W_0. Then E>=0, E1=0, and

  ||E||<=tr E=2 sum_e d_e<=2W_0.

Therefore the same event guarantees (1) for EVERY such update, with

  Lambda=2d+2W_0.

The algorithm in Sections 1--4 consequently has

  q=O_(d,W_0,alpha)(log(2/epsilon)),
  width O_(d,W_0,alpha)(k log(2/epsilon)),
  build O_(d,W_0,alpha)((N+k)k log(2/epsilon)),
  dense correction O_(d,W_0,alpha)(N^2 k log(2/epsilon)).

All statements hold with probability 1-o(1) over the HOST graph and,
on that event, simultaneously for all allowed additions. Thus an
update may be chosen after seeing the host; it need not be independent
of the graph. The algorithm is deterministic conditional on the input
and the stated gap bound. This uniformity costs no union bound over
possible edge choices. It also allows growing k if W_0 remains fixed,
with the displayed k factors counted explicitly.

For fixed k and inverse-polynomial epsilon, the total explicit
correction costs O(N^2 log N), while thin construction costs O(N log N).
For fixed epsilon these become O(N^2) and O(N), respectively. This is
an output-sensitive graph matrix-function statement, not unrestricted
quadratic matrix multiplication.

Primary source checked directly:
Charles Bordenave, "A new proof of Friedman's second eigenvalue Theorem
and its extension to random lifts," arXiv:1502.04482v4.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.04482
Theorem 1, PDF page 2, states the uniform simple d-regular model and
vanishing failure probability. Its equation (2) additionally gives,
for each fixed 0<a<1, a constant c=c(d,a) and failure <=N^-a at
deviation c(log log N/log N)^2. Hence for the fixed zeta above, the
corollary may be strengthened to probability >=1-N^-a for all
sufficiently large admissible N. Neither an explicit universal N_0
nor an arbitrary prescribed finite-N failure delta is asserted here.

6. SIGNED UPDATE VARIANT AND LIMITS
==================================
The deterministic theorem already includes signed edits whenever
both endpoints satisfy the SAME supplied interval (1). For example,
if the host has gap gamma_0 and the total removed conductance is
at most W_-<gamma_0/2, Weyl's inequality gives endpoint gap at least
gamma_0-2W_->0; positive additions do not reduce it. Endpoint upper
bounds follow from host degree and total added conductance. Thus
bounded deletions also fit after explicitly reducing gamma.

Without a surviving positive lower spectral bound on 1-perp, this
logarithmic-degree proof fails: r reaches one, and the binomial tail
estimate no longer decays geometrically. The condition-free
log-squared fractional-update theorem is a different guarantee that
still covers critical deletions and changes of connected components.

Polynomial low-rank updates, analyticity-based convergence, and graph
spectral-gap consequences all have substantial prior literature. In
particular Beckermann--Kressner--Schweitzer (2018) and their rational
extension (2021) treat polynomial/rational exactness for matrix-function
updates, including graph applications:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.03045
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.11501

The result above makes the intrinsic epsilon||E||^alpha guarantee,
common-kernel restriction, full factor arithmetic, adaptive update
uniformity, and source-model transfer explicit. No novelty certificate
or new random-graph spectral theorem is claimed. It uses the spectral
gap already reviewed in spectral-gap/, but does not use the sharper
SG-11 mixed-product envelope or SG-04 cubic moment threshold. Those
more specific permutation results require a separate sketch/moment
analysis before they can support an AMM improvement.

7. ALGEBRA DIAGNOSTIC
====================
NumPy diagnostic, seed 20261115, tested 225 cases: N=4,...,12,
five weighted complete-graph pairs per size, three signed edge changes,
and degrees q=1,2,4,8,16. Supplied gamma and Lambda were checked from
both endpoints' eigenvalues solely for this diagnostic. Forward/backward
factors (12) agreed with independently formed polynomial differences
to at most 3.30e-16 in operator norm scaled by max(1,||F_q||).
The largest observed true square-root-update tail divided by bound
(10) was 0.261. Both assertions passed. These finite floating tests
detect factor-order/sign mistakes; the general result rests on the
written telescoping and binomial-tail proof, not the experiment.
