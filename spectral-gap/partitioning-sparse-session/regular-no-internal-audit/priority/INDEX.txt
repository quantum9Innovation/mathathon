Important comparisons:
 * ICALP2026 Isaev et al.: actual Theorem1 gives no nontrivial equitable
   partitions for uniform UNPLANTED RRG at every sufficiently large
   constant degree; this does not apply to the conditioned lift law.
 * Fixed-target H-Cover NP-completeness is a worst-case decision result.
 * RegularCover in the algorithmic literature imposes a deck-group action;
   it is not arbitrary random lifting of a regular base.
 * Amit-Linial's unlabeled cover objects and trivial cover automorphisms
   do not establish uniqueness of a hidden projection partition.
 * Coarsest WL partitions differ from finest partitions bounded by K.
 * Noiseless lift detection is already easy/known; noisy hardness remains
   a separate problem. Spectral frame recovery with separation is known.
