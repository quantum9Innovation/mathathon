1. EXACT INTEGER NUMPY ELIMINATION: CORRECT
--------------------------------------------
rank_mod_numpy copies the input into int64 and reduces it modulo p.
It searches for a nonzero pivot in rows rank onward, swaps full rows,
normalizes the pivot, and eliminates only below it. Updating only the
suffix starting at the current column is valid: every earlier pivot
column is already zero in all remaining rows, and every earlier skipped
column was zero on that remaining subspace and stays zero under these
row combinations. A row swap among remaining rows preserves those
zeros. No missing update to a previous column can affect rank.

The factors are explicitly copied before broadcasting, and active rows
exclude the pivot row, so the vectorized update has no aliasing error.
For the actual p=3 experiment, reduced entries and inverses are in
{0,1,2}. Products are at most four and subtractions lie between -4
and 2 before the next remainder. Thus int64 overflow is impossible,
regardless of graph size. The study never calls floating rank, SVD,
an eigensolver, or an approximate linear solver.

The independent reference uses pure-Python integer RREF, normalizes
ENTIRE rows, and eliminates ALL other rows, so it does not duplicate
the NumPy tail-update implementation. Results:

  * 16 shifted rank comparisons on eight exactly replayed prefix graphs
    (two per N=12,13,30,31): every NumPy rank matched pure Python and
    the corresponding original CSV record.
  * 100 additional new matrices over F3/F5, including rectangular shapes,
    zero matrices, row swaps, leading zero columns, duplicate rows,
    and negative input entries: every rank matched.

No full sampler rerun or independent reranking of all 7200 graphs was
performed. The algebraic invariant argument above, not those samples
alone, justifies the elimination procedure.

2. UNIFORM RANDOM LIFT MODEL: CORRECT AND UNCONDITIONED
--------------------------------------------------------
For each of K4's six undirected base edges, sample_k4_lift draws a
uniform permutation of the N vertices, independently using a fresh
permutation draw from its per-N random stream. It places that perfect
matching and its transpose. Distinct base edges join distinct pairs
of fibers, and one permutation has no repeated image, so there are
no parallel edges or loops. Every vertex has exactly one neighbor in
each of the other three fibers, hence degree three.

There is NO conditioning on connectivity, twin absence, rank, or
corank. Disconnected lifts are legitimate draws from the stated
uniform independent-matching model and must remain in the study.
The prefix replay confirmed symmetry, zero diagonal, and degree three.

The main groups use SeedSequence.spawn child streams. Interleaving
50-draw batches does not change any per-N stream. The independent
pilot is excluded from all histograms; targets [3000,3000,600,600]
were fixed before the main samples. Main sampling does not stop or
change its target in response to an observed discrepancy. Targets
can depend on an independent runtime pilot without biasing these
fresh main-sample marginal frequencies.

3. SHIFTS AND OLD CORANKS: CORRECT, EVEN WHEN 3 DIVIDES N
--------------------------------------------------------
For the four-vertex quotient R=J_4-I_4 over F3,

  dim ker R = 1,
  dim ker(R-2I) = dim ker J_4 = 3.

Indeed 4=1 modulo 3, so R has scalar 0 on constants and scalar -1=2
on the three-dimensional sum-zero subspace. The study also checks
these dimensions by brute kernel enumeration.

The lifted old vectors are fiber constant, and their embedding is
injective because each fiber is nonempty. This needs NO division by
N and remains valid when 3 divides the degree and the fiber size.
Thus subtracting t=1 for lambda=0 and t=3 for lambda=2 is correct.
Since sampled adjacency diagonals are zero, setting the second
shift's diagonal to -2 is exactly A-2I, not a different update.
The recorded excesses are D0-1 and D2-3, and every raw row satisfies
those identities with nonnegative excess.

5. PAIRING, INTERVALS, AND COMPARISON CAUTIONS
----------------------------------------------
Within one fixed N and one shift, the marginal bin count is binomial
under independent lift draws, so the reported marginal standard
error and Wilson interval are appropriate. Different bins are
multinomially related, and lambda=0 and lambda=2 use the SAME graph.
They are not two independent graph samples. A direct within-graph
contrast between shifts would require the paired covariance; the
report does not incorrectly compute such an independent-shift test.
The between-N contrasts use different streams, so combining their
standard errors by a sum of variances is correct.

Wilson intervals are pointwise, nominal 95% intervals, not simultaneous
bands across all N, shifts, and bins. A zero-count plug-in standard
error is zero; the nonzero Wilson upper endpoint correctly displays
remaining uncertainty. Moment means can be dominated by a few large
excesses; their sample SE is not a guarantee of a Gaussian confidence
interval or of uniform integrability in an asymptotic theorem.

The empirical TV distance has sampling fluctuation and positive
finite-sample noise even if the true law equals the reference. A
small observed TV is not proof of exact finite-N agreement.
The original divisibility contrasts compare N with N+1, so they are
not an isolated test of N modulo three; in the original design,
divisibility is also confounded with parity (12/30 even versus 13/31
odd) and with graph size. The report correctly avoids a causal or
asymptotic conclusion from these contrasts.

8. AN EXACT FINITE-SIZE MECHANISM: ADJACENT TRUE TWINS
------------------------------------------------------
There is a concrete lambda=2-specific local mechanism. If adjacent
vertices u,v have identical closed neighborhoods, then

  A(e_u-e_v)=-(e_u-e_v),

so over F3 this is an exact eigenvector at lambda=2. For N>1 it is
not fiber constant and is independent of the lifted old base-kernel
subspace; the event therefore forces positive excess at lambda=2.

For any one of the 6N matching edges, condition on its endpoints.
Their remaining neighbors lie in the two other old fibers. In each
such fiber, the two relevant independent matching images coincide
with probability 1/N. The two coincidences use disjoint base-edge
matchings. Thus the endpoint pair is a true adjacent twin with
probability exactly 1/N^2. By linearity,

  E[number of true-adjacent-twin edges] = 6/N.

This tends to zero, so it is compatible with an asymptotic law and
provides no positive limiting-TV counterexample. It predicts a
substantial lambda=2-specific finite-size obstruction at N=12 or 30.
It is NOT a proof that twins account for the entire observed corank
bias or that the full convergence error is O(1/N).

The author's replay-only twin diagnostics are consistent with this:
mean twin counts near 6/N, and every recorded twin graph has positive
lambda=2 excess. The conditional excess-zero frequencies among
no-twin graphs are about .499,.480,.633,.617 for N=12,13,30,31.
There is still substantial small-N bias at 12/13. Conditioning on
no twins changes the graph law, so this posthoc conditional histogram
is a diagnostic, not a replacement unconditioned experiment or proof
of a conditional universality theorem. Independent replay of all 7200
matchings was performed by the author, not by this audit.
