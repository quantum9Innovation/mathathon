1. The exact twin event and its constant
---------------------------------------
Put beta=d-2 and a_d=binom(k,2). A vertex in old fiber V_a has one
neighbor in EVERY V_b, b!=a, and no neighbor in V_a. For u in V_a,
v in V_b, a!=b, let I_uv require their adjacency and equality of
their neighbors in each of the other d-1 old fibers.

The adjacency probability is 1/N. For each remaining color c, the
two matching images of u,v in V_c are independent uniform vertices,
so coincide with probability 1/N. All these coincidences use distinct
independent base-edge matchings. Therefore EXACTLY

  P(I_uv)=N^(-d),
  E X=binom(k,2)N^2N^(-d)=a_d N^(-beta),                  (A1)

where X counts unordered physical cross-fiber center pairs.

On I_uv the closed neighborhoods N[u],N[v] coincide. Their transposition
fixes all other vertices and is a graph automorphism. Swapping u,v
between their old fibers gives a second balanced K_k-cover partition.
For N>=2 it is a different UNORDERED partition, not a global renaming.
Every fixed K>=k includes this alternative. The asymptotic claims
properly use large N; at N=1 swapping singleton fibers would not
create a new unordered partition.


2. Motif expansion and complete factorial-second-moment control
----------------------------------------------------------------
The graph F consists of the two centers, their joining edge, and one
leaf in every other old fiber, each joined to both centers. Its values are

  v(F)=k=d+1,       e(F)=2d-1,       e(F)-v(F)=beta.

Every actual twin pair determines its leaves uniquely through the
matching images. Thus it corresponds to exactly ONE colored copy
of F, without a (d-1)! factor. Conversely a copy saturates all d
edges at each center and gives I_uv. Each base-edge type occurs at
most once in a single copy.

For a fixed overlap identification of two copies, write m=v(J),h=e(J)
for their intersection, INCLUDING all common vertices even if isolated
in the intersection. There are O_d(N^(2k-m)) choices of their physical
vertices. If matching compatibility fails the probability is zero.
Otherwise each base-edge matching has at most two distinct prescribed
edges, with exact probability 1/(N)_r for r=0,1,2. Independence gives
an O_d(N^(-(2(2d-1)-h))) bound. Hence the overlap contribution is

  O_d(N^(-2beta+h-m)).                                  (A2)

The crucial bound h<=m for DISTINCT feasible motifs is correct:

  * If the physical center sets coincide, matching injectivity forces
    every leaf to coincide. These are the identical physical motif,
    omitted from X(X-1). Different leaf choices with those same
    centers are incompatible, so have joint probability zero.

  * Otherwise choose a center u of the first copy that is not a center
    of the second. If absent in the second, the intersection is a
    subgraph of F-u, a star. If present, its intersection degree is
    at most two, since it is a leaf in the second prescribed motif.
    Deleting u leaves a forest. Restoring a vertex with at most two
    incident edges creates at most one cycle, so h<=m. If the remaining
    graph is empty, the restored vertex is isolated and the same
    inequality holds. Extra isolated common vertices only help.

This reasoning concerns prescribed motif edges, so possible extra
edges between leaves in the full graph do not invalidate it. There
are finitely many colored overlap types. Thus

  E X(X-1)=O_d(N^(-2beta)).                               (A3)

Bonferroni and Markov give

  P(X>=1)=a_d N^(-beta)+O_d(N^(-2beta)),
  P(X>=2)=O_d(N^(-2beta)).                               (A4)

Twin events are not presumed independent. For example several pairs
can coexist in one larger twin class; those cases are included in
(A2)--(A3). At d=3 a lifted K_4 component has six twin pairs, but
its probability is of the lower order N^-2 and causes no leading
constant inflation.


3. Specialized macroscopic exclusion
-------------------------------------
For any one-hot integer equitable candidate Y with r<=K and quotient
R, its old-fiber star laws P_i obey sum_(j!=i)e_(Y_ij)=R_(X_i).
Their exact expected type count is

  product_i [N!/product_s T_i(s)!]
      *product_(i<j)[product_(x,y)m_ij(x,y)!/N!].

The first product already assigns root labels. Each second factor
is the exact probability of matching the required root/neighbor label
categories on one independent uniform base-edge matching. There is
one slot per old neighbor type, so no across-type symmetrization is
needed. The entropy per graph vertex is

  Phi=(1/k)sum_i H(P_i)-(1/k)sum_(i<j)H(mu_ij).

Lossless omission of EACH neighbor slot and averaging over all d
slots gives the correct exact identity

  -Phi=(d-2)/(dk)sum_(i<j)I_ij
                    +1/(dk)sum_i sum_t J_it.

The source's quantitative comparison uses retained independent
unconditional-neighbor laws, divergence J_it+sum_(s!=t)I_is,
and a squared vector function with range [0,2d^2]. Summing its
variance differences over every omission gives
(d-2)sum_s V_s+dB_i>=0. Pinsker and Cauchy--Schwarz yield, for
delta=(1/k)sum_i(1-max root_probability_i),

  Phi<=-c_d delta^2,
  c_d=(d-2)^3/[4k d^6(d-1)]>0.                           (A5)

All constants match the general all-slot derivation. Delta is exactly
minimum row disagreement from ANY old-type point map, allowing merges
and unused labels. Candidate rows of R have mass d, since every cell
is nonempty in a d-regular graph. The variance identity is over ordinary
integer one-hot vectors, not modular values.

There are polynomially many fixed-degree/finite-alphabet type tables
and finitely many integer quotients for fixed d,K. Uniform zero-safe
Stirling bounds thus give exponentially small probability for any
candidate at distance at least fixed eta*n from all point maps,
where n=kN. No spectral hypothesis or minimum candidate cell size
enters this estimate.


4. Small supports and the improved remainder
--------------------------------------------
For a bad support S, no outside vertex has exactly one edge into S.
If s=|S| and w exterior vertices are touched, counting all incident
edges gives

  e-s-w>=ceil(beta s/2),       e<=ds,       w<=ds/2.

For s<=N/(4d), a prescribed typed partial matching has probability
at most (2/N)^e. The witness count

  binom(n,s)binom(n,w)2^(dw)(2ds)^e

includes every bad support's incident edges. After summing the
O_d(s^2) choices of e,w and absorbing fixed factors in C_d, it gives

  P(exists bad S of size s)<=C_d^s(s/n)^ceil(beta s/2).

For eta sufficiently small, the larger real-power sequence
u_s=C_d^s(s/n)^(beta s/2) decreases geometrically. Starting at THREE
therefore gives

  P(B_>=3)=O_d(N^(-3beta/2)).                             (A6)

Using the real exponent may be loose when ceil(3beta/2) is larger;
it is still a valid bound and suffices for the claimed remainder.
Singletons are not bad because the lift is simple and d>0.

Every bad size-two set is EXACTLY a twin pair:

  * Same-fiber vertices have distinct matching images in each other
    fiber, giving unique outside neighbors.
  * Different-fiber nonadjacent u,v: u's neighbor in v's old fiber
    is outside {u,v}, and cannot be adjacent to v because there are
    no within-fiber edges. It is a unique outside neighbor.
  * For adjacent u,v, all remaining neighbors must be common or
    else give a unique outside neighbor. Matching degrees force
    exactly the coincidences in I_uv. Conversely these coincidences
    plainly make the pair bad.

This classification is genuinely specific to the complete simple
base. It is the extra step needed to identify the leading constant;
an order-only O(N^-beta) support estimate would not suffice.


5. Every noncoarsening candidate is detected by those events
------------------------------------------------------------
Take eta small enough for (A6) and (d+1)eta*n<N. Outside the macro
event, every candidate Y is close to a point map JT. Its changed
rows and all their neighbors occupy at most (d+1)|S| vertices, so
each old fiber has a clean unchanged closed neighborhood. Evaluating
the integer equation there gives QT=TR BEFORE taking a difference.
Thus V=Y-JT satisfies AV=VR. Its nonzero row support must be bad,
independently of R and candidate dimensions.

If Y is not a planted coarsening, V is nonzero. Outside B_>=3 its
support is a size-two twin pair. Conversely every twin pair produces
a noncoarsening balanced k-class partition by its transposition.
Therefore the source's inclusions are valid:

  {X>=1} subset A_K
      subset {X>=1} union M_eta union B_>=3.

Together with (A4)--(A6), this proves

  P(A_K)=a_d N^(-beta)+O_(d,K)(N^(-3beta/2)).              (A7)

The macro event and small-support graph event cover all candidate
quotients simultaneously after a finite union. Candidate partitions
are not assumed balanced in (A7), nor are candidate point maps
assumed onto before the local argument.


6. Exact known-base posterior and the half constant
---------------------------------------------------
The statistical target is the unordered equal-size k-fiber partition
for KNOWN base K_k, hidden by a uniform global vertex shuffle.
Equivalently the ordered balanced partition has uniform prior.
Given any such partition, the independent edge-matching likelihood
of a compatible graph is exactly

  (N!)^(-binom(k,2)).

All k! labelings of a compatible unordered partition are compatible
because every permutation is an automorphism of K_k. Their equal
prior and likelihood imply a UNIFORM posterior on the Z(G) compatible
unordered balanced K_k-cover partitions. Hence Bayes error is

  E[1-1/Z(G)].                                           (A8)

This likelihood calculation does not apply unchanged to an unspecified
prior on unknown bases; the source correctly restricts (A8).

To identify Z on the dominant event, work outside M_eta and B_>=3
and with X<=1. Every compatible balanced k-cover is close to JT
with support <eta*n<N. If T omitted one candidate label, all N
vertices in its actual class would be disagreements, a contradiction.
Thus T maps k old types onto k labels and is a permutation. Relabel
candidate names to make it identity.

Every different candidate then differs on a size-two twin pair. Since
both old class sizes must stay N, the two changed distinct labels
must be swapped. For a fixed physical pair this gives exactly ONE
unordered alternative. Therefore:

  X=0 implies Z=1,            X=1 implies Z=2

on this event. There is no independent orientation or cell-name
choice left after the global permutation quotient. An alternative
obtained with different original candidate names is the same partition
of the observed vertex set.

The exceptional probability is bounded by

  P(X>=2)+P(M_eta)+P(B_>=3)=O_(d,K)(N^(-3beta/2)).

On Z=2 posterior error is exactly 1/2; on Z>=3 it is at most one.
Using (A4) proves

  R_N^*=(a_d/2)N^(-beta)+O_(d,K)(N^(-3beta/2)).           (A9)

This is a Bayesian information-theoretic obstruction under the stated
prior, not a failure rate asserted at one hardcoded fixed partition.


7. Larger candidate quotients and the computational remark
-----------------------------------------------------------
The source's claim that no r>k survives on the good event is also
sound. For support exactly {u,v}, an outside common neighbor gives
V_u+V_v=0. If the point labels are A,B and changed labels x,y,
one-hot equality means e_x+e_y=e_A+e_B. If A=B there is no change.
If A!=B and both rows change, x=B,y=A: it is a swap and creates
no new label. Hence every occupied label was already used by T,
so nonempty candidate classes force r<=k. Coarse compatible maps
and their swapped versions may occur; the two maximal k-partitions
are the ones counted above when X=1.

The computation remark distinguishes structural ambiguity from cap
interruption. From expected work O(n^(B+3)), a cap n^(B+d+2) gives
interruption O(n^(-(d-1)))=o(N^-beta). Complete known-base enumeration
and uniform sampling of its compatible partitions then attains the
leading Bayes constant. Uniform sampling over labeled indicators is
also valid here because every unordered K_k-cover has the same k!
multiplicity; a uniform reservoir can avoid storing the whole list.
Returning a partial enumeration as if complete would be invalid, and
is not proposed. The claim is theoretical polynomial work, not a
practical-runtime guarantee.


8. Connected conditioning, including its posterior
---------------------------------------------------
Every connected component of a cover of connected K_k meets every
fiber in the same number m: each edge matching bijects its component's
intersection with one fiber to its intersection with the other.
If disconnected, some component has 1<=m<=N/2.

For fixed m-subsets of all k fibers, each independent base matching
preserves those subsets with probability binom(N,m)^(-1). Counting
the binom(N,m)^k subset choices and all binom(k,2) matchings gives

  P(disconnected)<=sum_(m=1)^floor(N/2)binom(N,m)^(-r0),
  r0=binom(k,2)-k=k beta/2.

At k>=4, r0>=2 and r0>beta. The m=1 term is N^-r0; for m>=2,
binom(N,m)>=binom(N,2), giving O(N^(1-2r0)). Therefore

  P(disconnected)=O_d(N^-r0)=o(N^-beta).                  (A10)

Conditioning on connectedness changes any bounded error or ambiguity
probability by O(P(disconnected)), preserving both leading constants.
In fact r0>=2beta, so this is smaller than the displayed remainder
in (A7),(A9). Connectedness is determined by the observed G; conditioning
on that observation event does not change its posterior on compatible
partitions. The conditional Bayes conclusion is therefore justified,
not just a comparison of unconditioned motif expectations.


9. Final scope assessment
-------------------------
The result is a sharp universal obstruction witnessed by one fixed
base family, K_(d+1). It does not claim every base has this same
leading rate or motif. Fixed d and fixed K>=d+1, independent uniform
base-edge matchings, and an unlabeled planted partition are essential
to the statements audited here. Unknown-base lower bounds may use
the known-base problem as extra-information comparison, but an
unknown-base posterior needs its own specified prior.

No correction to the actual source was required. The leading motif
constant, its half-factor Bayes risk, and their survival under connected
conditioning pass this independent adversarial audit. Historical
priority and claims about noisy or growing-base models remain outside
this correctness certificate.
