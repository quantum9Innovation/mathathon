Regular-frame recovery with no internal-degree lower bound
=========================================================

Mathematical source review is separate from historical priority.

1. Exact theorem and quantifiers
-------------------------------
Fix supplied constants K>=1 and D>=3. The true fixed nonnegative integer
frame Q=(d_ij) has 1<=k<=K types, connected off-diagonal support (including
the one-type case), and EVERY row sum equals the SAME d, 3<=d<=D.
Internal degrees a_i=d_ii may be ZERO or ONE. Nonempty sizes N_i satisfy
N_i*d_ij=N_j*d_ji and N_i*a_i even. Omit zero factors. Independently
sample uniform simple internal a_i-regular and active cross
(d_ij,d_ji)-biregular factors. The connected frame fixes positive block
proportions alpha_i=N_i/n, n=sum_i N_i.

THEOREM. Along every admissible size sequence, with probability tending
to one, every equitable partition into at most K nonempty classes is a
compatible coarsening of the true planted partition. Consequently that
partition is the unique finest equitable partition within the class
family with at most K classes. Given only A and the fixed bounds K,D,
the planted partition, k and Q can be recovered with high probability
by the exact algorithm below. Its untruncated expected bit work is
polynomial, and a deterministic polynomial cap with explicit abstention
has worst-case polynomial work and high-probability success.

The row-REGULARITY assumption is substantive. This theorem does not
replace it by merely bounding different row sums. The earlier coarse
theorem instead allows different totals while requiring every a_i>=2.
These are distinct, overlapping model scopes.

2. Exact count and all-slot entropy identity
-----------------------------------------
The full count is proved in entropy-count/all-slot-intertwiner-moment.tex.
For the vector alphabet F_2^r and any r-by-r candidate R, ordered stars
obey sum_(t=1)^d Y_t=X R. Omitting ANY physical neighbor slot determines
its vector from the root and retained neighbors with coefficient one.
Thus the legal-star alphabet has exactly (2^r)^d elements, even when
there are no internal slots. Standard basis vectors remain distinct over
F_2^r. No invertibility, simple spectrum, or scalar-sign encoding is used.

For a star type T_i, the exact assignment factor is N_i!/prod_s T_i(s)!.
It already chooses root labels. Nonzero internal factors contribute
  [prod_(x<y)m_ii(x,y)! prod_x(m_ii(x,x)-1)!!]/(N_i*a_i-1)!!;
cross factors contribute prod_(x,y)m_ij(x,y)!/(N_i*d_ij)!.
Internal tables are symmetric with even diagonal; cross tables reverse.
Zero internal factors are omitted. Loops contribute TWO to adjacency.

Normalize the entropy exponent by n. Write w_ii=alpha_i*a_i/2 and
w_ij=alpha_i*d_ij=alpha_j*d_ji for i<j. Let I_ij denote the mutual
information of the pooled type-ij root/neighbor pair. Symmetrize slots
WITHIN each neighbor type; this preserves those laws and legal support,
and increases star entropy. It never permutes slots of different types.

For every omitted slot t, let J_it be the retained-neighbor conditional
total correlation given the root. Lossless deletion and averaging over
ALL d slots give exactly
  H(star_i)=H(root_i)+(d-1)/d *sum_s H(Y_s|root_i)
                       -(1/d)sum_t J_it.
Substitution and edge balance cancel every root-entropy coefficient,
yielding the exact symmetrized identity
  L=-Phi=(d-2)/d *sum_(undirected types i<=j) w_ij I_ij
                         +(1/d)sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it >=0.       (E)
The internal weight w_ii includes the necessary factor one-half. The
unsymmetrized exponent is no larger. No internal degree lower bound
appears in (E).

Uniform factorial estimates and polynomially many types therefore give
  E_config 2^h_R <= (3D)^F (n+1)^B,
  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K),   B=K*2^(KD)+F,
where h_R is the nullity of AY=YR over F_2. The estimate holds for every
R and includes rare configurations with large nullity.

Conditioning on all factors being simple costs a fixed factor. Its
limiting exponent is
  Lambda=sum_(i:a_i>=1)(a_i^2-1)/4
                +sum_(i<j:d_ij>0)(d_ij-1)(d_ji-1)/2.
An absent internal factor contributes ZERO, not -1/4. Internal degree
one and cross (1,1) factors are already simple. Finite frame choices and
alpha_min>=1/[K D^(K-1)] make the eventual constants uniform. Thus
  E_simple 2^h_R <= C(K,D)(n+1)^B.                       (P)

3. Real one-hot rigidity and quantitative gap
-------------------------------------------
The independent proof is in rigidity/all-slot-onehot-rigidity.tex.
Use the SAME exact count with one-hot labels and the INTEGER local
equation. There are at most r^d legal stars. For old root law rho_i set
  delta=sum_i alpha_i(1-max_x rho_i(x)),
the exact minimum normalized row disagreement with all planted point
maps JT, including maps merging old types or leaving labels unused.

For each omitted t, an auxiliary law keeps the root law and samples all
retained neighbors independently from their root-INDEPENDENT marginals.
Its KL divergence from the actual retained law is
  kappa_it=J_it+sum_(s!=t) I_is.
From (E), alpha_i sum_t kappa_it <=2d(d-1)/(d-2)*L.
Writing mu_s=E e_(Y_s), V_s=1-||mu_s||_2^2, use
  f_t=||R_X-sum_(s!=t)e_(Y_s)-mu_t||_2^2 in [0,2d^2].
The actual expectation is V_t. Under the auxiliary law it is
  sum_(s!=t)V_s+B_i,
where B_i=E||R_X-sum_s mu_s||_2^2>=0. Summing over t gives expectation
difference (d-2)sum_s V_s+d B_i. Pinsker and Cauchy--Schwarz imply
  L >=alpha_i (d-2)^3/[4d^6(d-1)] * (sum_s V_s)^2.
Some old type j has root variance at least delta, and has an incident
cross or internal slot because d>=3 and balance holds. Hence
  Phi <=-alpha_min (d-2)^3 delta^2/[4d^6(d-1)]
       <=-alpha_min delta^2/(8D^6).                     (G)
This covers k=1 as well as internal degrees zero and one. Candidate R
need not have distinct rows or be nonsingular. Its occupied rows have
nonnegative entries of mass d by the verified integer equation, which
justifies the range bound under BOTH probability laws.

At zero entropy every old root law is a point mass. Pair marginals force
all neighbor labels to those old-type points, and the local constraint
is exactly QT=TR. Conversely compatible point maps have entropy zero.

For any fixed eta>0, applying (G) in the exact factorial count bounds
macroscopic alternative candidates by
  polynomial(n)*exp[-alpha_min*eta^2*n/(8D^6)].
Here n*Phi is the count exponent. Neither compactness nor a candidate
class lower-size bound is required.

4. Local closure and exact unknown-frame algorithm
-------------------------------------------------
SUPPORT_AND_ALGORITHM.txt gives the complete independent support count,
compatibility argument, and complexity calculation. Total minimum degree
is d>=3. A typed-configuration witness count gives a fixed eta(K,D)>0
such that with failure probability O(n^(-(d-2))), no nonempty set S with
|S|<=eta*n lacks an outside vertex having exactly one edge into S.
This single event excludes all small row supports of AV=VR, for every
field, vector dimension, and R simultaneously.

Choose eta<alpha_min/(D+1). Every integer equitable candidate within
eta*n changed rows of JT leaves an unchanged closed neighborhood in
each old class. Its exact equation at such a vertex gives QT=TR.
Then V=Y-JT satisfies AV=VR; the support event forces V=0. This closes
all near-point candidates, including tiny classes near unused labels.
Combining with the macroscopic event proves the theorem's structural
claim for every bounded candidate quotient, connected or disconnected.

Enumerate every nonnegative integer candidate R of order r<=K and row
sums<=D, at most sum_(r<=K)(D+1)^(r^2) matrices. Compute the binary
kernel of I_r tensor A-R^T tensor I_n. Enumerate all 2^h_R vectors;
require one-hot rows and nonempty classes, and verify INTEGER AY=YR.
Retain an accepted partition with the largest number of classes.

Binary elimination costs O_K(n^3) bit work. A generous O_(K,D)(n^2)
per vector includes all basis combinations and verification. From the
UNCONDITIONAL moment(P), expected total bit work is O_(K,D)(n^(B+3)).
A deterministic n^(B+5) cap interrupts with probability O_(K,D)(n^-2)
by Markov. On interruption return FAILURE/ABSTENTION, never certify an
unfinished maximum. Space is O_K(n^2) bits. This theoretical exponent
is enormous and is not a practical-runtime claim.

On the structural event all accepted Y=JT. Nonempty classes make T onto,
so r<=k. The planted pair(J,Q) is always included; maximum r is k, and
then T is a permutation. This recovers the partition, k, and Q by exact
neighbor counts, without knowing any of them or an old/new spectral gap.

5. Random-lift corollary and boundaries
--------------------------------------
For any fixed connected SIMPLE d-regular base H with d>=3 and at most K
vertices, take Q=A_H, all N_i=N, and independent uniform perfect matchings
for its base edges. This is exactly the random N-lift model. There are
no internal factors, and all cross factors are(1,1); the resulting graph
is simple automatically. Every N is admissible. The theorem recovers the
base fibers and unknown bounded frame with high probability.

The recovery concerns the finest equitable partition AMONG at most K
classes. The n-singleton partition always exists outside that bounded
family. Multiple coarse partitions of H, such as those in a cube, are
consistent with the theorem: they coarsen the recovered fibers.

No claim follows for growing K/base size, degree-two covers, arbitrary
chosen deterministic lifts, noisy switches/edits, or numerical spectral
stability. Neither real eigenspace simplicity nor exclusion of exact
new/old eigenvalue collisions is asserted. In covering theory 'regular
cover' may mean a transitive deck group; that property is not assumed.

Primary random-cover reconstruction and equitable-partition comparisons
are investigated in priority/. Historical firstness is not certified by
this proof or by a finite source search.
