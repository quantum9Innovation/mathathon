1. Precise positive theorem
--------------------------
Fix constants K>=2 and D>=3. Let the true degree frame be any fixed
nonnegative integer k-by-k matrix Q=(d_ij), with 2<=k<=K, a_i=d_ii>=2,
and total degrees d_i=sum_j d_ij<=D. Assume the off-diagonal support is
connected. Let the nonempty planted classes have sizes N_i with
  N_i*d_ij=N_j*d_ji,
and all parity conditions N_i*a_i even. The connected fixed frame fixes
their positive proportions alpha_i=N_i/n, n=sum_i N_i.

Independently sample a uniform simple a_i-regular graph inside each
class, and a uniform simple (d_ij,d_ji)-biregular graph between every
active pair of classes. Every cross factor is random and independent
in THIS theorem. No additional parity restriction is imposed on N_i.

THEOREM. With probability tending to one along all admissible sizes,
EVERY equitable partition into at most K nonempty classes is an
equitable coarsening of the true planted classes. It follows that:
  * the planted partition is the unique finest such partition;
  * its number of classes and its unknown degree frame are recoverable;
  * the explicit modular-intertwiner algorithm below has expected
    polynomial bit complexity and a worst-case polynomial truncation
    with high-probability success.
The algorithm is given the fixed bounds K,D, but not Q,k, the class
sizes, a spectral signal parameter, or an outlier gap.

Equitable means one exact neighbor-count vector at every vertex in each
class. It is a rigid exact-degree model, not the independent-edge SBM.
In particular the assertion is not a new Kesten--Stigum threshold.

2. The finite-alphabet configuration moment
-------------------------------------------
Let p=2, r<=K, and take ANY fixed r-by-r candidate integer matrix R
with entries>=0 and row sums<=D. Form the binary linear operator
  L_R(Y)=A Y-Y R,    Y in F_2^(n by r).
Equivalently its matrix is I_r tensor A - R^T tensor I_n, up to the
chosen vectorization convention. Let h_R be its binary nullity. Then
the number of modular intertwiners is 2^h_R.

The exact factorial derivation in
  entropy-count/configuration-intertwiner-proof.tex
gives, already in the typed multigraph configuration model,
  E 2^h_R <= (3D)^F (n+1)^B,
where the following explicit constants depend ONLY on K,D:
  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K),
  B=K*2^(KD)+F.
Its root/neighbor alphabet is the vector space F_2^r, not scalar signs.
The e_1,...,e_r labels remain distinct, including at p=2. The local
equation determines one omitted internal-neighbor vector with coefficient
one, so neither an odd prime nor a matrix inverse is required.

For an ordered-star type T_i, count its assignment to labeled vertices
by N_i!/prod_s T_i(s)!. Internal stub tables m_ii must be symmetric,
with even diagonal; their pairing ratio is
  [prod_(x<y)m_ii(x,y)! prod_x(m_ii(x,x)-1)!!]
        /(N_i*a_i-1)!!.
The cross ratio is
  prod_(x,y)m_ij(x,y)! /(N_i*d_ij)!.
These exact formulas count root labels once, and retain distinguishable
stub slots. Loops count twice in adjacency and degree. Uniform factorial
bounds, including zero entries, give an entropy exponent plus a fixed
polynomial factor. The allowed-star alphabet has at most 2^(r*d_i)
elements because one neighbor is omitted, hence there are polynomially
many types, with the displayed explicit exponent.

To check the exponent's sign, symmetrize neighbor slots WITHIN each
neighbor type. This preserves pooled pair laws and local linear support,
and increases star entropy. If I_ii and I_ij are internal and cross
root-neighbor mutual informations, and J_i is retained-neighbor total
correlation conditioned on its root, the exact weighted cancellation is
  -Phi=sum_i alpha_i(a_i/2-1) I_ii
          +sum_(i<j) alpha_i*d_ij I_ij +sum_i alpha_i J_i >=0.
This works for a_i=2 and unequal positive proportions. For modular labels
no equality classification is needed; nonpositivity alone proves the
polynomial moment, independent of R.

Conditioning every factor on simplicity costs a fixed positive factor.
The companion proof checks this directly by elementary bad-pattern
factorial moments, or one may use the classical regular/biregular
configuration estimate. Its limiting probability is exp(-Lambda), with
  Lambda=sum_i (a_i^2-1)/4
          +sum_(i<j) (d_ij-1)(d_ji-1)/2 <= K^2 D^2.
Conditioning gives precisely independent uniform simple factors, since
every simple factor has the same labeled-stub multiplicity. Thus
  E_simple 2^h_R <= C(K,D) (n+1)^B
eventually. This bound is UNCONDITIONAL over the simple graph draw,
including rare graphs on which h_R is large.

The ratio bound alpha_min>=1/[K D^(K-1)] is available for the ACTUAL
frame from balance along paths. Since there are finitely many frames
with k<=K and maximum row sum D, the eventual simplicity constant and
the polynomial exponent may be chosen uniformly over them. The input
K is a fixed supplied or hardcoded bound. No growing-K claim is made.

3. Real one-hot rigidity: a quantitative macroscopic exclusion
------------------------------------------------------------
For an actual integer equitable partition Y, with rows in {e_1,...,e_r},
apply the SAME exact configuration count using this real one-hot
alphabet and the INTEGER equation AY=YR. Let rho_i be the empirical
law of candidate labels on old class i, and define
  delta=sum_i alpha_i (1-max_x rho_i(x)).
This is exactly n^-1 times the least number of rows changed in replacing
Y by a blockwise point map J T, where J is the old planted indicator
matrix and T is a k-by-r one-hot map. T may merge old classes and may
have unused candidate labels.

The full independent real-variance proof is in
  rigidity/onehot-entropy-rigidity.tex.
Here is its crucial reconstruction, which does not assume internal
root-neighbor independence when a_i=2. After slot symmetrization, use
the exact cancellation in Section2. Keep each root law and each original
conditional internal-neighbor law, make retained internal neighbors
conditionally independent, and replace cross-neighbor conditionals by
their root-independent neighboring-class marginals. Call this auxiliary
retained-star law Q_i and the actual law P_i. Then
  kappa_i=KL(P_i||Q_i)=J_i+sum_(j!=i) d_ij I_ij,
  alpha_i*kappa_i <= L=-Phi.

Let m_i(X) be the conditional mean internal one-hot vector, v_i its
expected conditional variance trace, and V_j=1-||rho_j||_2^2. Let W be
the omitted vector inferred from R_X minus the retained neighbor sum.
Under P_i, W is the actual omitted one-hot neighbor. For
  f=||W-m_i(X)||^2,
its expectation under P_i is v_i. Under Q_i, conditional independence
gives the EXACT difference
  E_Qi f-E_Pi f
   =(a_i-2)v_i+sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j
        +E||R_X-a_i m_i(X)-sum_(j!=i)d_ij E e_(X_j)||^2
   >=sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j.
Every occupied row R_X is a nonnegative count vector of total mass d_i.
The retained sum plus m_i(X) has the same mass, so 0<=f<=2d_i^2 under
BOTH laws. Pinsker therefore gives
  L >= alpha_i [sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j]^2/(2 d_i^4).

Since V_j>=1-max_x rho_j(x), some j has V_j>=delta. Connectedness and
k>=2 supply a cross-neighbor i of that j. Hence
  Phi <= -alpha_min*delta^2/(2D^4).                    (G)
The unsymmetrized entropy is no larger, and its root laws are unchanged,
so (G) holds for every original empirical type. In particular Phi0
forces all old root laws to be points; the deterministic local degree
equation at those points is exactly Q T=T R. Conversely a compatible
point map has deterministic stars and entropy zero.

For any fixed eta>0, the expected number of integer candidates at
distance at least eta*n from EVERY point map is at most
  polynomial(n)*exp[-alpha_min*eta^2*n/(2D^4)].
This follows by the same uniform factorial upper bound. There are only
polynomially many types. The union over all candidate matrices R,r is
finite and keeps this probability exponentially small. No candidate
class lower-mass assumption is necessary for this conclusion.

4. Near a point map: no unexamined small-defect step
-------------------------------------------------
The complete arbitrary-support typed-configuration proof is in
  support/small-support-and-frame-identification.tex.
Because a_i>=2 and the cross-frame is connected with k>=2, the minimum
TOTAL degree d_min is at least three. Take any nonempty set S of s
vertices and let W be its outside neighbors, w=|W|. If no outside
vertex has exactly one edge into S, every vertex of W has at least
two such edges. Write a for edges within S (loops count once), b for
S-W edges, e=a+b. Then
  2a+b>=d_min*s,  b>=2w,
  e-s-w >= (d_min-2)s/2.

Counting choices of S,W, selected stubs and their prescribed pairings
in each independent typed factor gives, for all s<=eta*n and a small
fixed eta=eta(K,D)>0,
  E[#such S of size s]
    <= C(D,rho)^s (s/n)^ceil((d_min-2)s/2),
where rho=alpha_min may be replaced by1/[K D^(K-1)]. Every prescribed
partial configuration with e pairs costs at most (2/(rho*n))^e.
The estimate covers arbitrary compositions of S among planted classes,
loops, and parallel edges. Its sum over all small nonempty S is o(1).
After simple conditioning singleton S are impossible, and the stated
companion bound is O(n^-(d_min-2)).

Choose eta even smaller than alpha_min/[2(D+1)]. For ANY verified
integer candidate Y at distance |S|<=eta*n from a point map J T, each
old class contains a vertex whose own row and every neighbor row are
unchanged: its changed closed neighborhoods occupy at most(D+1)|S|
vertices globally. At one such vertex in each old class,
  (QT)_i=(A J T)_v=(A Y)_v=(Y R)_v=(T R)_i.
Thus QT=TR EXACTLY, not just modulo2. Consequently
  V=Y-JT satisfies A V=V R,
and its nonzero row support is S. At a vertex outside S having exactly
one edge into S, the corresponding row of A V would be one nonzero
row of V, while the row of V R is zero. This is impossible over any
field. The single graph event excluding small bad S therefore forces
S empty simultaneously for EVERY R and Y, without a union over their
coefficients or vector dimensions.

Intersect this small-support event with Section3's macroscopic event.
Every integer equitable partition into at most K nonempty classes is
then EXACTLY a compatible point map J T, proving the rigidity theorem.
Neither spectral simplicity nor a positive numerical spectral gap is
asserted or used.

5. An explicit unknown-frame algorithm and its complete cost bound
----------------------------------------------------------------
Given A and fixed K,D:

For every r=1,...,K and every nonnegative integer r-by-r matrix R whose
row sums are at most D, perform binary Gaussian elimination on L_R.
Enumerate its full binary kernel using any basis. For every matrix Y:
  (i) require every row is exactly one standard basis vector e_j;
 (ii) require every candidate class is nonempty;
(iii) verify the ORIGINAL integer equality A Y=Y R.
Retain an accepted partition with the largest value of r. The old
planted pair(J,Q) appears among the candidates for every graph draw.

There are at most M=sum_(r=1)^K (D+1)^(r^2) candidate matrices, a fixed
constant. Computing each binary kernel costs O_K(n^3) bit operations.
Enumeration has exactly 2^h_R vectors; a deliberately loose bound of
O_K(n^2) work per vector covers basis combination, row checks, and
integer verification. Memory need not store the full list: a kernel
basis and one running candidate suffice, using O_K(n^2) bits plus
integer neighbor counters of bounded size.

Using Section2's bound UNCONDITIONALLY gives
  E[total bit work] <= C(K,D) n^(B+3).
This does not condition work on success or discard rare expensive
instances. With a hard cap n^(B+5), Markov bounds cap failure by
O_(K,D)(n^-2). The truncated algorithm has worst-case polynomial work.
Combine this event with the rigidity event to obtain high-probability
correct recovery. The exponent B is explicit above, depends only on
the supplied constant bounds, and is enormous. This is a theoretical
polynomial-time result, not a practical or nearly-linear algorithm.

On the rigidity event, every accepted partition is Y=JT. Nonempty
candidate classes make T onto, so r<=k. The planted k-class candidate
is present, hence the maximum is k. At r=k, T is a permutation of the
old classes and the algorithm recovers them up to their unavoidable
names. Exact neighbor counts then recover Q in that labeling.

One MAY restrict candidate R to connected off-diagonal support: the
true Q is then still among candidates, and edge balance gives every
candidate class size at least n/[K D^(K-1)]. This is a valid optional
restriction, as in the root proposal. It is not needed for this proof:
the explicit macroscopic gap includes unused labels, and the small-
support step rules out near-point tiny classes. No unknown unbounded
number of classes or growing alphabet is covered.

6. Qualifications, primary comparison, and what changed
-------------------------------------------------------
The theorem needs random independent cross factors for the exact type
count. It does NOT inherit the earlier strong-mixing theorem's allowance
of arbitrary cross matrices. On the other hand it treats all admissible
parities, internal degree TWO, unequal proportions, unknown frame, and
repeated or interior spectral modes. These are separate proof scopes.

The connected k>=2 assumption and minimum total degree three matter.
With k=1,a1=2, an even cycle's alternating two-class labeling gives
zero entropy away from a point map and small cycle components supply
small supports. This excluded case is an actual obstruction, not a
technical condition silently dropped.

This is exact-model recovery. A single degree-preserving switch can
break an exact planted equation; no robust noisy-edit allowance follows.
The computed binary kernel is NOT asserted to have the minimum possible
dimension or to coincide with an old real eigenspace. We only bound the
expected number of its vectors polynomially and filter them in integers.
