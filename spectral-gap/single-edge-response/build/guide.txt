For d = 2, a fixed cutoff parameter ρ = 1/2 suffices. For d ≥ 3, use ρ = 1/k, where k is the least integer
with 2k ≥ 4/ε, found by repeated doubling. Here the error guarantee is relative operator norm at most ε;
rank and error hold on the same success event. The remainder is uniform over the graph class, without
an effective rate. The sharp bridge lower examples force this constant for any rank approximant, even
without positivity.
The expected arithmetic work is O  eα (m + n). Suppressed factors are polynomial in logarithms of n, 1 +
τ, 1/ε, 1/δ; they contain no graph gap, spectral norm, or conductance ratio. Both raw and Gram quadra-
ture widths are Oα (log2 (2 + n(1 + τ )/ε)). The proof charges feasible electrical flows, all shifted SDD
solves, small-Gram formation, Jacobi rotations, and every factor entry. Certified primal/dual resistance
intervals and explicit work guards control expected work even on failed solver calls.
Model and scope. This is a positive-edit, unit-vertex-mass, exact-real arithmetic theorem. General
fixed-α scalar powers are scalar operations; the square-root case has an arithmetic-and-square-root im-
plementation. It is not a finite-word stability result. The computed factor has two-sided relative error;
it need not lie below the true update. The algorithm may reconstruct solver data per edit. It does not
prove a sublinear maintained dynamic data structure.
If P = Lα + XX T , then (1 − ε)P ⪯ L′α ⪯ (1 + ε)P . Applying P −1 still requires a separately charged
baseline L−α action. The theorem constructs the correction and proves its quality; it does not make that
baseline action free.

Classical inputs and status. Araki, Weyl–Horn/Kostant, Schur asymptotics, total positivity, resistor
laws, coarse graining, Stahl approximation, Laptev/Widom asymptotics, expander existence, Fredholm
determinants, free/wired networks and Kron reduction, rational matrix-function updates, sinc/contour
quadrature, and nearly linear electrical-flow solvers are credited in the manuscript. Internal independent
reviews found no remaining mathematical blocker in the integrated claims. These reviews are not formal
or external peer review, and do not certify historical novelty. Efficient extremal graph sizes, effective
higher-degree asymptotic rates, and finite-word stability remain open here.
