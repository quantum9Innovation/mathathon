1. Exact operator and sparse complexity
---------------------------------------
Let A=A^T be n-by-n, R be r-by-r, and X n-by-r. Stack the COLUMNS
of X. Then exactly

  vec(AX-XR)=K vec(X),
  K=I_r tensor A-R^T tensor I_n.                          (A1)

Its transpose acts as vec(Y)->vec(AY-YR^T). The transpose on R in
(A1) is necessary. For nonsymmetric R, replacing it by R solves
a different equation. Both K and K^T can be applied from A and R;
there is no hidden transpose-oracle assumption.

Set m=rn, w=nnz(K). Both products cost

  O(r nnz(A)+n nnz(R))

field operations. With fixed r and bounded graph/quotient degrees,
m=O(n), w=O(n). A dense Kronecker matrix is unnecessary; the
structured black boxes or explicit sparse block representation have
the claimed linear cost. Integer quotient entries are bounded neighbor
counts, so the final integer-check bit lengths are controlled as well.


2. Block linearization and the right projection
------------------------------------------------
Over Fp form the implicitly applied symmetric matrix

  S=[0 K; K^T 0],                   size 2m.

For (u,v), S(u,v)=(Kv,K^Tu). Consequently

  ker S=ker K^T direct-sum ker K,
  rank S=2 rank K,
  nullity S=2h,            h=m-rank K.                    (A2)

The identities hold over every field and remain true under extension.
No positivity, Gram identity, or semisimplicity of K is used. One S
product uses one K and one K^T product and its sparse count is 2w.

Apply the PER-INPUT symmetric full-kernel primitive to S, of order
2m and corank 2h. Its conditional expected bit work becomes

  soft-O_p((2m)(2w+2m)(2h+1))
     =soft-O_p(m(w+m)(h+1)).                             (A3)

This computes a basis, not an enumeration of the doubled kernel.
There is no application of a graph moment theorem to S as if it
were an adjacency matrix.

Let B be any full basis of ker S. Its bottom m-coordinate projections
span ker K: every v in ker K gives (0,v) in ker S, and expanding
that vector in B gives v from the projections. Their rank is exactly
h. Select h independent projected columns by exact elimination;
do not take an arbitrary first h columns, which may vanish or be
dependent. The top projection would instead give the LEFT kernel.

Choose h pivot rows J and normalize the selected matrix B_right as

  C=B_right(B_right[J,:])^(-1),      C[J,:]=I_h.

Projection, selection, pivoting, and normalization cost
O(mh^2+h^3), bounded by O(m^2(h+1)). This fits (A3) because w+m>=m.
Their storage is O(mh), even if the complete doubled basis is retained
temporarily. At h=0 the right basis is empty; at h=m the procedure
returns the whole right space. These boundary cases are covered.


3. Exact base-field descent
---------------------------
The primitive may first produce an extension-field basis. Either
descend the full S-kernel by its previously audited pivot normalization
before projection, or project first and normalize a complete right
basis. In the latter case the target subspace is still ker K, which
is defined over Fp. Frobenius x->x^p preserves it.

For fixed pivot rows J, restriction from ker K to the h pivot
coordinates is an isomorphism. Thus the basis normalized to identity
on J is unique. Its coefficientwise Frobenius transform is another
such normalized basis, so equals C. Hence every entry lies in Fp.
Explicitly check positive-degree extension coefficients vanish,
extract the base coefficients, and recheck KC=0 over Fp.

This does not attempt to enumerate arbitrary extension-field probes
using only base-field coefficients. It first obtains a complete
base-defined space and uses its unique normalized basis. No division
by a graph cell size is involved, so p dividing a fiber size is
irrelevant to this step.


4. Compact right-kernel certificate and its soundness
------------------------------------------------------
The final certificate need not include the unused left basis or the
entire 2m-by-2h S-basis. Retain the primitive's verified extension-field
definition, invertible diagonal preconditioner D, scalar probes u,v,
and shifted sequence information. Include only the base-field C,
its distinct pivot rows J, and the claimed length ell.

The checker reconstructs S, regenerates the shifted scalar sequence

  u^T(DS)^(j+1)v,             0<=j<4m,

and recomputes its TRUE shortest finite BM length ell. It does not
trust a polynomial display degree, padding, a supplied recurrence,
or a claim of generic rank. The old unconditional argument gives

  ell<=rank(DS)=rank S=2 rank K.                           (A4)

It is valid for every random draw, including nilpotent blocks and
unlucky probes: the shifted start lies in the invariant image of DS.
Require ell=2(m-h), where h is the number of C columns, and verify
KC=0 and C[J,:]=I_h. The latter gives independence and rank K<=m-h.
Together with (A4),

  m-h=ell/2<=rank K<=m-h.

Thus every ACCEPTED certificate proves a COMPLETE right kernel with
zero verification error. An odd length or inconsistent claimed corank
is rejected; no division by two in the field is used, only integer
dimension arithmetic. The chain also handles h=0 and h=m.

Verification costs soft-O_p(m(w+m)): O(m) sparse S products and
quadratic BM work, plus O(wh+h^2) base residual/pivot checks, all
within that bound because h<=m. Certificate size is
O_p(mh+m log m) bits. Extension construction and irreducibility
checks remain exactly those of the primitive, at order 2m; its
extension degree is O(log m), so all arithmetic and random-bit
charges remain polylogarithmic per operation.

This compact certificate certifies the full RIGHT KERNEL. Individual
integer partition checks then certify each listed partition. To check
an untrusted claim of list completeness one can replay enumeration
from C; its cost is charged separately below. A worst-case compact
kernel-verifier bound is not being conflated with a worst-case
polynomial verifier of an exponentially long partition list.


5. Enumeration needs neither a squared nor a weighted moment
------------------------------------------------------------
Enumerate the h-dimensional right kernel over Fp using C. Never
enumerate ker S, whose size p^(2h) can have an unbounded expectation
even when E p^h is bounded. The source's example h=n with probability
p^(-n), and zero otherwise, correctly exhibits that failure.

A further hidden factor h is also avoided. Maintain the current
vector under a base-p odometer or Gray code. Across all p^h states,
the total digit changes are O_p(p^h); each adds a scalar multiple
of one basis column in O(m) operations. Generation is O_p(m p^h),
not a fresh O(mh) multiplication for each state. E p^h alone would
not control E[h p^h], so this distinction is mathematically necessary.

Reshape according to column-stacked vectorization. Require EXACTLY
one coordinate equal to 1 and all others 0 in every row, not merely
a row sum of 1 modulo p. Lift those entries to integer 0/1 and check

  AX=XR over the INTEGERS.

Require nonempty classes and any sizes genuinely specified by the
recovery task. For a blind unknown-frame search, no prescribed cell
sizes are known: use nonemptiness, integer verification, and the
maximum-class criterion from the identifiability theorem instead
of imposing a nonexistent size promise. These checks still have
O_(r,d)(n) cost per candidate. Keeping column-size counters adds
at most logarithmic bit factors.

Every true integer one-hot intertwiner reduces to this field kernel,
and distinct one-hot labels stay distinct at any prime. Conversely
integer verification rejects modular artifacts. Thus the list is
complete on EVERY input, including rare graphs with high modular
corank and extra real eigenspaces. A scalar sign-kernel theorem
or a corank-one theorem is not substituted for this vector statement.


6. The exact expectation and storage calculation
------------------------------------------------
For a fixed input A,R, the complete conditional expected work is

  soft-O_p(m(w+m)(h+1)+m p^h).                           (A5)

Projection and descent are included in the first term. If the actual
input law satisfies (M), h+1<=p^h implies E(h+1)<=C_R. Taking graph
expectation in (A5) gives, at fixed r and bounded degree,

  E total work=soft-O_(p,r,d,C_R)(n^2).

The order of expectation is legitimate: the primitive is Las Vegas
with its per-input bound on EVERY matrix. Rare large-corank graphs
are charged through their actual p^h moment, not discarded or sent
to an uncharged dense fallback. No statistical good-input event is
conditioned on in this runtime expectation.

Per-input working storage is soft-O_p(m(h+1)+w), with fixed factors
for the doubled basis. Under (M) its expectation is soft-O(n).
Stream the partition list so output is not scratch storage. At most
p^h candidate assignments can pass, each with O(n) bits at fixed r,
so expected output is O(n). The kernel certificate has expected
O(n log n) size as well. Retaining only a best partition uses O(n)
extra storage for a recovery application.


7. Finite unknown quotient family and integer balance
------------------------------------------------------
At fixed upper bounds K,d, there are only finitely many nonnegative
integer quotient candidates of orders r<=K with row sums<=d. Apply
the SAME field p and construction separately to every candidate.
If (M) holds for each, the maximum/sum of their finitely many moment
constants is finite. Linearity of expectation preserves near-quadratic
total work without any independence requirement between the runs.
Reusing workspace preserves the expected linear storage bound.

All actual candidate class sizes may be unequal. For undirected A,
integer one-hot AX=XR implies

  X^TAX=diag(N_1,...,N_r)R is symmetric,
  N_i R_ij=N_j R_ji.                                     (A6)

If equal positive sizes are PRESCRIBED, R must be symmetric and one
may reject nonsymmetric R immediately. This cannot be inferred merely
because the TRUE planted lift fibers have equal sizes while the search
also permits unequal-size coarsenings. The generic blind search must
retain nonsymmetric balanced quotients, and the block reduction does.
Equation (A6) is over integers and survives p dividing any N_i.

For a fixed prescribed R, arbitrary permutations of columns need not
preserve its equation; use the allowed quotient automorphisms for
unlabeled deduplication. In an unknown-R search one may simultaneously
canonicalize the pair (X,R), accepting only its canonical relabeling.
At fixed K this costs a constant number of O(n) scans and needs no
exponential-memory hash table. Simply retaining the largest-class
verified candidate also tolerates constant label multiplicities.

The structural regular-frame theorem then says that every bounded-class
partition coarsens the planted one with high probability. Selecting a
maximum class count recovers its finest planted partition on that
event. This statistical uniqueness statement is a separate input;
on exceptional ambiguous graphs the algorithm still computes correct
complete kernels and valid complete candidate lists.


8. Statistical dependency and what is NOT being promoted
---------------------------------------------------------
The moment required is for the SAME fixed odd p and the vector
intertwiner operator for every candidate R on the ACTUAL law. A
binary-only moment is not automatically a moment over the odd field
used by the audited primitive. A scalar bound for A-lambda I does
not automatically bound arbitrary nonsymmetric vector intertwiners.

The new bounded-moment statement read above is for independent uniform
N-lifts of a fixed connected SIMPLE d-regular base, d>=3, with fixed
p,r,R. It explicitly proposes a bounded supremum in N and a finite
maximum over R, so its mathematical interface is exactly (M). The
nonbipartite scope has a separate frozen root audit; the all-bipartite
assembly was being audited independently while this algorithm audit
was written. No local-limit or zero-phase theorem is being newly
certified here. Once that model theorem is accepted, its bounded
actual-law moments compose directly with Sections 1--7.

The similarly named general regular/biregular-factor bounded-moment
extension file was marked UNAUDITED PROPOSAL at inspection. The
algebraic algorithm applies to those models too IF their own version
of (M) is proved, but the earlier polynomial moment by itself gives
only polynomial expected listing work. It does NOT justify the
near-quadratic conclusion for every regular factor model.

Raw expectations proved in a configuration law may transfer by
E[Z|simple]<=E Z/P(simple) when that probability stays positive.
Qualitative contiguity or high-probability real simplicity alone
does not transfer the unbounded variable p^h. No such shortcut is
used in the two actual intertwiner sources.


9. Fresh read-only numerical/interface checks
---------------------------------------------
I ran existing prototype ROUTINES on new fixed examples by importing
them with Python -B; no main test driver or artifact writer was invoked.
No pycache, certificate JSON, source, or existing fixture was written.
The read-only harness compared projected right spaces to its separate
base-field Gaussian elimination, verified compact right certificates,
enumerated only right vectors, and rejected dimension-consistent
incomplete certificates (remove one column and increase ell by two).

All five examples over F3 passed:

  3-by-3 nilpotent Jordan block: h=1, 3 right vectors, not 9.
  Nonsymmetric matrix with different left/right kernels: h=1, 3, not 9.
  Zero 3-by-3: h=3, 27, not 729.
  Full-rank triangular 3-by-3: h=0, one zero right vector.
  Rank-one matrix with isotropic first column (1,1,1): h=2, 9, not 81.

Every nonempty incomplete basis forgery was rejected by the regenerated
BM length despite its altered dimensions being mutually consistent.
These finite checks supplement the rank-chain proof, and are not a
benchmark or a substitute for the full per-input probability analysis.

The unweighted Gram temptation is genuinely unsafe: that last F3
example has K^TK=0 despite rank K=1. Likewise diagonal scaling of
a nonsymmetric nilpotent K does not restore the symmetric primitive's
zero-semisimplicity guarantee. The audited block construction avoids
both mistakes. The optional random weighted-Gram route is unnecessary
for this verdict and is not relied on.
