Therefore c must lie in ker(Q-lambda I), and conversely every such c

14e. All phases sum to 2p^t without overlap

For a scalar F_p alphabet, the only subspaces are 0 and F_p. The
nonbipartite classification therefore leaves the uniform phase and
exactly p^t point phases c with Qc=lambda c. Each point assignment
is an exact type-constant eigenvector on EVERY configuration. Its
zero-defect count is one. The core sparse estimate with restricted
space zero bounds all its nonzero defects by O(N^(-(d-2)/2)).

Choose point neighborhoods disjoint using the positive old-type size
coefficients; distinct maps differ on an entire old class. Make the
uniform full-table neighborhood small and disjoint from them. Its
root margins are close to uniform and cannot be close to a point.
The compact remainder has negative entropy and contributes o(1).
Thus point neighborhoods contribute p^t+o(1), the uniform one
contributes p^t+o(1), and the exact total is 2p^t+o(1).

14f. The two-block real-simplicity consequence is valid, including cubic

For Q=[[a,b],[b,a]], lambda=a-b and any odd p not dividing b,
Q-lambda I has rank one, so t=1. The old support is nonbipartite
because a>0 supplies internal self-incidences, including at a=2.
Therefore the configuration moment is 2p+o_p(1) for every fixed
a>b>0 and every admissible block size.

The planted integer sign vector is always a real and modular kernel
vector. Extra real nullity makes every order-(2N-1) integer minor
vanish, hence forces modular nullity >=2 for every prime. Markov
therefore gives limsup Pr(real extra nullity)<=2/p. Subtracting the
deterministic planted modular line improves this to 1/(p-1); either
bound suffices. First take N to infinity at a FIXED p, then let
admissible fixed odd primes tend to infinity. This proves real
nullity one with high probability. No growing-prime uniformity or
polynomial real failure rate is inferred.

The event transfers to the actual uniform-simple law by dividing its
configuration probability by the fixed positive simplicity probability,
which is independent of p. The sharp moment itself need not remain
2p after conditioning, and the sources do not assert that. All
admissible simple sizes, odd when a is even, are covered. Rational
nullspace elimination recovers the planted signs on this event.
The known polynomial-probability local ambiguity lower bounds are
fully compatible with a qualitative high-probability simplicity result.
