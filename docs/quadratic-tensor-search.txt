QUADRATIC / TENSOR SEARCH -- INDEPENDENT FRESH TRACK
2026-11-14--15. Owned by the quadratic/tensor worker.

STATUS

No improved matrix-multiplication exponent or unrestricted original
rank-one raw-factor LRA theorem has been established here. The requested
general quadratic objective remains open and active. The concrete objects
below sharpen the central-slice extraction problem; near-minimal rank and
new tensor identities are not being reported as that breakthrough.

1. THE CENTRAL TERNARY FAMILY AS A CODE TENSOR

Put d=L-1 and V_L=Sym^d C^3. Its monomial basis is

  Delta_d = {alpha in Z_>=0^3 : |alpha|=d},
  n_L=|Delta_d|=L(L+1)/2.

Define

  T_L(f,g,h)=[x^d y^d z^d](fgh),  f,g,h in V_L.

Dehomogenizing x=1 identifies V_L with the triangular polynomial space

  W_L={f(y,z): total degree(f)<=L-1}.

The tensor becomes

  [y^d z^d](fgh),  f,g,h in W_L,

so it is an exact restriction of the Frobenius structure tensor of

  C[y,z]/(y^L,z^L) = (C[y]/y^L) tensor (C[z]/z^L).

The familiar L^2 evaluation decomposition is precisely the restriction
of the two-variable roots-of-unity formula to this triangular space.
Indeed the sum over y^L=z^L=1 selects exponents of the form
(d+kL,d+ell L). Since the product has degree<=3d, only k=ell=0
can occur. Thus rank(T_L)<=L^2.

This also realizes T_L as a trilinear coordinate-product form on a
degree-d Reed--Muller evaluation code of length L^2 and dimension n_L.
An MM restriction would amount to three compatible matrix-multiplication
embeddings into this particular code. Merely observing that the code
has rate approximately 1/2 supplies no such embedding.

2. EXACT ASYMPTOTIC SUBRANK: Q~(T_L)=L(L+1)/2

This is an actual calculation, rather than a numerical entropy guess.
The support consists of triples (alpha,beta,gamma) in Delta_d^3 with

  alpha+beta+gamma=(d,d,d).

It is tight. For example, M>d makes alpha -> alpha_1+M alpha_2
injective on Delta_d. Use this weight on the first two legs and subtract
d(1+M) from the same weight on the third leg; support weights sum to 0.

There is an explicit support distribution with all three marginals
uniform on Delta_d: choose alpha uniformly, and output

  (alpha, cyc(alpha), cyc^2(alpha)).

Every such triple lies in the support because the three cyclic coordinate
rotations sum to (d,d,d). Each marginal is uniform, hence has entropy
log_2 n_L. Strassen's tight-three-tensor subrank theorem gives monomial
asymptotic subrank at least n_L. Flattening dimension gives the reverse
bound, so both monomial and unrestricted asymptotic subrank equal n_L.

Primary source for the exact theorem used:
  Christandl--Vrana--Zuiddam, Asymptotic tensor rank of graph tensors:
  beyond matrix multiplication, Computational Complexity 28 (2019),
  Theorem 1.37 (Strassen 1991), PDF page 16:
  https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/28609/28609.pdf

Consequently this family has no entropy deficit in asymptotic subrank.
Its irreversibility is at most log(L^2)/log(n_L), which tends to 1.
This does NOT give a matrix-multiplication conversion: diagonal tensors
also have irreversibility 1, and subrank measures conversion TO a
diagonal tensor, not conversion to matrix multiplication.

3. EXACT DIGIT RESTRICTIONS

For positive integers a,b, there is an exact monomial restriction

  T_a tensor T_b <= T_(ab).

On each leg send basis (alpha,beta), with |alpha|=a-1 and |beta|=b-1,
to the degree-ab-1 monomial of exponent alpha+a beta. The map is
injective by the base-a remainders (the a=1 case is immediate).

For a surviving source coefficient, summing the low digits in each
coordinate gives a nonnegative number congruent to a-1 mod a. The
sum across coordinates is 3a-3. Since the three minima already total
3a-3, every low-coordinate sum is exactly a-1. No carry occurs, and
the three high-coordinate sums must each be b-1. Conversely these
two separate support conditions clearly give a surviving source term.
All nonzero coefficients are 1, proving the exact restriction.

The gain in leg dimension over the product, n_(ab)/(n_a n_b), tends
to 2 when both a,b grow. It is real unused tensor structure, but does
not itself improve the rank cost: the known rank budgets on both sides
are a^2 b^2.

4. A COMPLETE BINARY-DIGIT DECOMPOSITION OF T_(2b)

This is a more informative recursion than the single digit restriction.
Every degree-(2b-1) monomial in three variables has odd-coordinate
count either 1 or 3. Accordingly each leg splits exactly as

  U_0 = C^3 tensor Sym^(b-1) C^3,
  U_1 = Sym^(b-2) C^3,

where U_1 is zero for b=1. Explicitly, U_0 consists of
x_i f(x^2,y^2,z^2), and U_1 consists of xyz f(x^2,y^2,z^2).
The dimension check is

  3 n_b + n_(b-1) = n_(2b).

The U_0^3 block is exactly T_2 tensor T_b. The U_1^3 block is
exactly T_(b-1). Every block with TWO U_1 legs and one U_0 leg
vanishes: its low-digit column sums have pattern (3,2,2), whereas
the target exponent is odd in every coordinate.

Blocks with ONE U_1 leg survive only when the low one-hot indices on
the other two legs agree. For low index i, their high-digit tensor is

  [X_i^(b-2) product_(j!=i) X_j^(b-1)](fgh),
  deg f=b-2, deg g=deg h=b-1.

Call this asymmetric central slice A_(b,i). The first input is shared
across the three i blocks. Restricting the first leg of T_(2b) to U_1
therefore gives an exact semidirect sum

  A_(b,1) partialsum_1 A_(b,2) partialsum_1 A_(b,3)
       partialsum_1 T_(b-1).                         (B)

Each A_(b,i) restricts to T_(b-1) with the shared first leg untouched:
multiply its second and third inputs by the two different variables
X_j,X_k with {i,j,k}={1,2,3}. The coefficient identity is immediate.
Thus T_(2b) restricts to four shared-first copies of T_(b-1).

This four-copy relation has only the weak scaling consequence p<=1
when combined with rank(T_L)<=L^2. It is NOT an exponent improvement.
The nonzero one-U_1 cross blocks are precisely why one may NOT replace
this recursion by the tempting direct sum
(T_2 tensor T_b) directsum T_(b-1).

For b=2 the decomposition is especially concrete. Arrange the nine
U_0 coordinates as a 3-by-3 matrix V=(v_ij), and call the U_1 scalar s.
With the literal diagonal cubic F(f)=T_4(f,f,f),

  F(V,s)=6 per_3(V)
          +6s(v_12 v_13+v_21 v_23+v_31 v_32)+s^3.   (C)

Here v_ij is the coefficient of x_i x_j^2. Formula (C) supplies a
small exact cubic test object for trying nonmonomial changes of basis.

5. A PRECISE LIMIT FOR ONE-COPY MONOMIAL MM RESTRICTIONS

Suppose MM_n is an exact monomial restriction of T_L: each matrix-entry
basis vector maps to a distinct source monomial, up to nonzero scaling.
Use two exponent coordinates, the third being determined by total degree.
Write their images as A_ij,B_jk,C_ki in Z^2. Every desired MM coefficient
then requires

  A_ij+B_jk+C_ki=(d,d),  all i,j,k.

Subtracting two such equations yields separated forms

  A_ij=u_i+v_j+a,
  B_jk=-v_j+w_k+b,
  C_ki=-w_k-u_i+c,  a+b+c=(d,d).

Absence of unwanted support implies that the n^3 sums u_i+v_j+w_k
are distinct: equality of two such sums gives the unwanted triple
A_ij,B_(j',k),C_(k',i') unless all three indices agree.

After fixing one base index, the u,v,w coordinate differences have
magnitude at most d, because each appears as a difference of two
source exponent vectors in [0,d]^2. Hence these n^3 distinct sums
lie in a square of O(d^2) lattice points. Therefore

  n^3 = O(L^2).

So one-copy exact monomial restrictions of growing T_L cannot turn
the known L^2 rank decomposition into a subcubic exponent. This
statement does not cover nonmonomial restrictions, cancellations,
catalytic conversions, arbitrary tensor powers, or a general laser
method. Those distinctions matter; this is not a barrier to T_L itself.

6. HIGHER-ORDER COMPLETE GRAPHS: CURRENT PRIMARY CHECK AND LIMIT

A fresh current primary source is Huanyi Zhou,
Asymptotic Rank of Weighted Graph Tensors via Completely Positive Bounds,
arXiv:2610.34273v3, November 11, 2026:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34273
  https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34273v3

It proves log_2 R~(GT_2(K_n))<=n^2/4, with equality for even n,
and recovers 9/4 on a triangle. This is stronger higher-order data
than the original three-leg polynomial family, but simple regrouping
does not yield a missed square-MM improvement.

For n=3s, grouping vertices into three sets of size s and contracting
internal edges leaves MM_(2^(s^2)). The rank bound gives
omega<=((3s)^2/4)/s^2=9/4 again (use a multiple of 6 if even n is
desired). The cut between one group and the other two has 2s^2 edges,
so no local routing operation within this fixed grouping can produce
more than s^2 EPR dimensions on each side of the target triangle.
Thus direct grouping/routing cannot improve this calculation.

The paper's weighted CP/path relaxation also has value 9/4 on the
unweighted triangle; this was checked directly in its current abstract
and technical development. A genuinely new conversion would be needed.

7. NONMONOMIAL/JORDAN LINE: AN EXACT FORMULATION OF THE GATE

Symmetric matrices of size n have dimension n(n+1)/2, the same as
the input dimension of T_n. It is tempting to identify the central
cubic with X -> tr(X^3), but dimension equality does not give a tensor
identity. For L=2, T_2 is the xyz cubic, whereas tr(X^3) on symmetric
2-by-2 matrices is a linear factor times a rank-3 quadratic, already
excluding the naive identification.

The correct useful target is a restriction/degeneration from T_L
(or several copies with full costs counted) to the cubic tr(X^3) on
zero-diagonal symmetric n-by-n matrices with L=n^(1+o(1)). Such a
construction would prove omega=2 because these symmetric cubic families
capture the matrix-multiplication exponent. This last equivalence is
established, not newly claimed:

  Chiantini--Hauenstein--Ikenmeyer--Landsberg--Ottaviani,
  Polynomials and the exponent of matrix multiplication,
  Theorem 1.1, sections 2--4:
  https://academicweb.nd.edu/~jhauenst/preprints/chiolSymmetricMatrix.pdf

No such restriction has been found. In particular, replacing the
coefficient tensor by a determinant or by a Jordan trace cubic silently
changes coefficients and is invalid. The tiny rank budgets already
make some such replacements impossible.

8. BOUNDED NUMERICAL DIAGNOSTIC (NOT A THEOREM)

An inline NumPy Levenberg--Marquardt search tried three independent
leg maps from T_3 to MM_2 and from T_5 to MM_3. Tensor entries were
constructed directly from the coefficient-support definition; the target
was the literal MM tensor. No packages/files or permanent scripts were
added outside this note.

For T_3 -> MM_2, 35 starts of 500 steps gave a smallest squared residual
about 8.6e-8. A separate 15000-step start reached about 7.4e-10 while
the sum of factor Frobenius norms grew to about 177. This is consistent
with a degeneration or with gauge ill-conditioning, and certifies
neither. For T_5 -> MM_3, four 500-step starts had residuals at least
about 5.54; these local failures prove no impossibility. No approximate
numerical factors are being treated as an exact rank algorithm.

9. STILL-OPEN CONSTRUCTIVE GATE

The substantive unresolved step is to use the retained digit cross
blocks, a nonmonomial change of basis, or another compatible family
to obtain an MM extraction whose value exceeds the actual rank budget
at omega=9/4. Formulae (B)--(C), exact maximal asymptotic subrank, and
the code interpretation make that gate more explicit; none crosses it.
The broad N^2 goal therefore remains open.

10. A NEW CROSS-FAMILY CONSTRAINT FROM A SMOOTH QUADRIC

Unlike an isolated identity or dimension model, this gives genuine
relations between a tensor PRODUCT of the old binary polynomial family
and the ternary family. It is not an exponent improvement yet.

Write S=C[x,y,z], q=y^2-xz, and

  R=S[u]/(u^2-q).

The invertible linear coordinates
  w00=x, w11=z, w01=y+u, w10=y-u
identify R with C[w00,w01,w10,w11]/(w00 w11-w01 w10), the Segre ring
of P^1 x P^1. Consequently multiplication R_(a-1) x R_(b-1) ->
R_(a+b-2), denoted Q_(a,b), is exactly isomorphic to

  Q_(a,b) = P1_(a,b) tensor P1_(a,b).

In the quadratic-cover coordinates it has the vector-space decomposition

  R_j = S_j directsum u S_(j-1),

with negative-degree pieces interpreted as zero. An input is
f0+u f1 and its product with g0+u g1 is

  h0=f0 g0+q f1 g1,
  h1=f0 g1+f1 g0.

10.1 Exact shared-first restriction

Restrict the first input to f1=0. The two independent second-input
pieces g0 and g1 then go to independent output pieces h0 and h1, so

  P2_(a,b) partialsum_1 P2_(a,b-1)
       <= P1_(a,b) tensor P1_(a,b),       b>=2.        (D)

The shared first leg is the literal space S_(a-1), and no first-leg
change depending on the summand is needed. The cases of absent
negative-degree terms can equivalently be included using a zero tensor.

10.2 Exact three-product reverse restriction

For a,b>=2 choose ell=x and compute three ordinary ternary products:

  P0=f0 g0,
  P1=f1 g1,
  P2=(f0+ell f1)(g0+ell g1).

Recover h0=P0+qP1 and

  h1=(P2-P0-ell^2 P1)/ell.

The division here is a FIXED LINEAR map on ell S_(a+b-3), obtained
by decrementing the ell exponent. Its argument is identically in that
image. Thus it is a tensor restriction, with no nonlinear division or
input-dependent map. This proves

  P1_(a,b) tensor P1_(a,b)
       <= P2_(a,b) directsum P2_(a,b)
                    directsum P2_(a-1,b-1).          (E)

This is Karatsuba's quadratic-extension identity applied to the quadric
section ring. The ordinary algebra identity is classical; no claim of
novelty for Karatsuba multiplication is intended.

10.3 Consequences and an actual relaxed-model incompatibility

Every actual spectral point phi, with p=log_2 phi(<1,1,2>)>0,
must satisfy the cross-family constraints

  phi(P2_(a,b))^(1/p)+phi(P2_(a,b-1))^(1/p)
       <= phi(P1_(a,b))^(2/p),

  phi(P1_(a,b))^2
       <=2 phi(P2_(a,b))+phi(P2_(a-1,b-1)).           (F)

The first uses the proved semidirect-sum inequality; the second follows
from exact spectral additivity on direct sums and (E).

These constraints rule out one tempting COMBINATION of relaxed models:
the exact old binary operational assignment Fstar, together with the
simple dimension-only ternary assignment in raw-factor-fresh-search
Section 13.9. This does not invalidate that section's deliberately
limited barrier theorem; cross-family products were explicitly excluded.

Indeed, for a=b=t, the old binary Fstar is exactly (3t-1)/2. On the
ternary dimension model put A=t(t+1)/2. Its value is

  Phi2(t,t)=[2A-1+3*2^(-4/3)*(A-1)^(4/3)]^(3/4),

which is asymptotic to (3^(3/4)/4)t^2. Therefore the left side of
the second inequality (F) is asymptotic to (9/4)t^2, while the
right side is asymptotic to (3^(7/4)/4)t^2. Since 9>3^(7/4), (F)
fails for all sufficiently large t. A diagnostic finds a violation
already at t=20 (870.25 versus approximately 835.85).

This is a real extra constraint, rather than merely a larger-dimensional
copy of an interior Pieri inequality. But it does NOT rule out all
p=3/4 assignments. For example, the leading product-based candidate
Phi2(a,b) approximately 2^(-3/4)*Fstar(a,b)^2 has room in both
inequalities. A full finite formula, vector boundary, rank bounds,
ternary quadrupling, and any further cross-family closures must be
handled together before claiming an exponent gain.
