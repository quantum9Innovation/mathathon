RSBM(2,1) finite-field first moment: exact types and entropy phases
Prepared 2026-11-15. Independent derivation; no novelty claim.

STATUS
The exact type formula and nonpositive entropy identity below are proved.
They exclude every macroscopic nonuniform phase over a fixed prime field.
They do NOT prove E|ker|=O(p), because the saddle point prefactors and
the neighborhoods of the constant phases remain to be controlled.

MODEL
Relabel the cross perfect matching to identity. The two internal matrices
C_+,C_- are independent degree-two configuration matrices on N vertices,
with two distinguished stubs at each vertex. Conjugating A-I by the planted
sign diagonal gives M=[[C_+-I,-I],[-I,C_--I]]. Thus M(x,z)=0 means

  C_+ x=x+z,     C_- z=x+z.

The configuration model permits loops (counted twice on the diagonal)
and parallel edges. Conditioning both components simple yields precisely
the requested independent uniform simple degree-two graphs. This
conditioning has probability tending to exp(-3/2)>0.

EXACT TYPE FORMULA
Work over F_p, p prime. At coordinate i let A=x_i and D=z_i. Let B,C be
the x-values at the endpoints of the two stubs incident to i in C_+;
let E,F be the corresponding z-values for C_-. The equations require

  B+C=A+D=E+F.

Hence a local ordered profile is determined by (A,D,B,E) in F_p^4,
with C=A+D-B and F=A+D-E. Let n_{a,d,b,e} count the N coordinates of
each profile. Define integer edge-stub tables

  m^+_{uv}=sum n_{a,d,b,e} 1{a=u}(1{b=v}+1{a+d-b=v}),
  m^-_{uv}=sum n_{a,d,b,e} 1{d=u}(1{e=v}+1{a+d-e=v}).

Only types with m^+ and m^- symmetric, and their diagonal entries even,
can be paired. For such a table define

  W(m)= product_{u<v} m_{uv}! product_u (m_{uu}-1)!!,

where (-1)!!=1. A fixed ordered profile assignment has exactly W(m^+)
compatible plus pairings and W(m^-) compatible minus pairings. Therefore

  E |ker_Fp M|
   = sum_{n:sum n=N, valid tables}
       [N! / product n_{a,d,b,e}!]
       W(m^+) W(m^-) / ((2N-1)!!)^2.                    (1)

This is exact. The node profiles keep the two stubs distinguished, so
no missing factors of two arise when the two neighbor values differ.

ENTROPY EXPONENT
Let T=n/N be the probability law of (A,D,B,E), with C,F as above, and
let Q^+=m^+/(2N), Q^-=m^-/(2N). Uniform Stirling estimates for fixed p
(including zero factorial arguments) give the logarithm of a summand as

  N Phi(T)+O_p(log N),
  Phi(T)=H(A,D,B,E)-H(Q^+)-H(Q^-).                       (2)

The admissibility constraints make Q^+,Q^- symmetric. First symmetrize
T under B<->C and E<->F. These are commuting involutions of F_p^4;
symmetrization leaves Q^+,Q^- unchanged and weakly increases H(T).
Thus it suffices to bound Phi for invariant T. Then

  Q^+=law(A,B)=law(A,C),  Q^-=law(D,E)=law(D,F).

Symmetry of these edge tables gives law(A)=law(B)=law(C) and
law(D)=law(E)=law(F). A direct entropy chain rule gives the exact identity

  Phi(T)
   = -I(B;E | A,D) - I(A;D)
     -I(D;B | A) - I(A;E | D) <= 0.                    (3)

For example, subtracting H(A,B) and H(D,E) from H(A,D,B,E), first
remove I(B;E|A,D); the remaining expression is
H(D|A,B)+H(A|D,E)-H(A,D), which is the other three negative terms.

EQUALITY CLASSIFICATION OVER A PRIME FIELD
If Phi(T)=0 before symmetrization, strict concavity of entropy forces
T already invariant under both involutions. Equation (3) gives

  law(A,D,B,E)=mu(A) nu(D) alpha_A(B) beta_D(E).          (4)

Invariance B<->C says, for every a with mu(a)>0,

  B | A=a  has the same law as  a+D-B | A=a,

where D has law nu and is independent of B. For any nonzero Fourier
frequency t in F_p this implies

  |alpha_hat_a(t)|=|nu_hat(t)| |alpha_hat_a(t)|.

If nu is nonconstant, every nonzero character is faithful and
|nu_hat(t)|<1. Thus alpha_a is uniform. Since law(B)=law(A), mu is
uniform. The corresponding minus-block argument then makes nu and
beta_d uniform as well. Equation (4) is the completely uniform law
on (A,D,B,E) in F_p^4.

If nu is constant, say D=c, the marginal identities make E=F=c.
The local equation E+F=A+D forces A=c. The plus marginals then make
B=C=c. Thus T is a point mass at (c,c,c,c). The same conclusion holds
if mu is constant. Consequently the entire zero set of Phi consists of

  one uniform phase, and p constant phases.

For every fixed p and delta>0, compactness therefore gives a strictly
negative uniform entropy gap outside delta-neighborhoods of these p+1
phases. The number of types is polynomial in N for fixed p, so all such
types contribute exponentially little to (1).

WHAT REMAINS
This proves that a positive-entropy nonuniform type cannot invalidate
the proposed O(p) first-moment route. It does not determine the Gaussian
constant near the uniform type, nor control all mesoscopic deviations
from constant types. In particular, an O_p(1) total moment bound is not
enough for real simplicity: its dependence on p must be o(p^2), ideally
2p+o_N(1). Taking p to infinity is legitimate only after N tends to
infinity for each fixed p.

No transfer from a permutation 2-factor model is used in (1)--(4).
