                                                                                            Abstract
                                                    For fixed integers s ≥ 3 and r satisfying log(r!) > r(s−1)

                                                 uniform perfect matchings of the complete s-uniform hypergraph is mutually contiguous to the
                                                 uniform simple r-regular s-uniform hypergraph, as n → ∞ through multiples of s. In particular,
                                                 r = ⌈es ⌉ suffices. The main input is the sharp entropy inequality for random permutations

                                           A random r-regular graph, r ≥ 3, on n vertices is contiguous to the union of r random perfect
                                           matchings provided n is even, conditioned on the union of the matchings being simple. This has
                                           been known for some time, see for example Wormald [9]. In this paper we extend this decomposition
                                           to random regular hypergraphs.
