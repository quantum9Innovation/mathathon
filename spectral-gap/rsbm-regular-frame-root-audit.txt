1. Model and the correct scope of the conclusion
------------------------------------------------
Fix K,D. The true frame Q=(d_ij), of order k<=K, is nonnegative integer
with the SAME row sum d at every type, 3<=d<=D. Sizes N_i>0 obey

  N_i d_ij=N_j d_ji,          N_i d_ii even.

Off-diagonal support is connected, with the one-type case allowed.
All active internal and cross factors are independently sampled
uniformly among simple regular/biregular graphs. Let n=sum_i N_i,
alpha_i=N_i/n, and let J be the planted indicator matrix. Then AJ=JQ.

Connectedness and bounded integer degrees give a UNIFORM proportion
bound. Along a path, each adjacent size ratio lies between 1/D and D.
Thus

  alpha_min>=rho=1/[K D^(K-1)].                            (A1)

The connected frame fixes all positive size ratios; there are only
finitely many possible frames for fixed K,D. This justifies uniform
eventual constants. It does not permit K or the base size to grow.

Every equitable partition of a d-regular graph has a nonnegative integer
quotient R with each occupied row of mass d. Its indicator Y obeys
the INTEGER equation AY=YR. Candidate cell sizes may be arbitrarily
small or unequal. For correctness of the eventual algorithm it is
harmless to enumerate every integer R of order r<=K with row sums<=D:
the integer check discards occupied rows of the wrong mass.

The assumption true k<=K is essential. If K were smaller than k,
incomparable coarse quotients could have the same class count, without
a unique finest member. Without any class bound, the singleton partition
always exists. The sources correctly state the bounded-family theorem.


2. Exact configuration count: zero and one internal degrees
-----------------------------------------------------------
For the moment use alphabet A=F_p^r, size q=p^r. A class-i ordered
star contains its root x and ALL d individually labeled physical
neighbor slots, with their old neighbor types preserved. The local
constraint is sum_t z_t=xR over the field. The root and d-1 retained
labels determine ANY omitted slot. Hence precisely q^d stars are legal,
even if there is no internal slot and without assumptions on R.

Let T_i(s) count stars and m_ij(x,y) the pooled directed stub table.
The exact assignment factor is N_i!/product_s T_i(s)!; it already
assigns the root labels. There is no extra root-label multinomial.
Pairing compatibility is

  m_ii(x,y)=m_ii(y,x),      m_ii(x,x) even,
  m_ij(x,y)=m_ji(y,x)      for cross factors.

For an active internal factor, its precise probability multiplier is

  [product_(x<y)m_ii(x,y)! product_x(m_ii(x,x)-1)!!]
                              /(N_i d_ii-1)!!.

For a cross factor with M=N_i d_ij=N_j d_ji stubs on each side, it is

  product_(x,y)m_ij(x,y)! / M!.

Degree zero is an empty factor and is OMITTED. Degree one internally
is a perfect matching with one stub at each vertex, for even N_i;
the same exact formula applies and is automatically simple. Cross
(1,1) factors are ordinary uniform perfect matchings, also without
any parity requirement on N_i. Loops count twice in adjacency until
conditioning, exactly matching the sum of stub-neighbor labels.

For every configuration and root labeling there is a unique star
assignment, and compatible labels are paired by exactly these
category bijections/double factorials. Independence of the graph
factors is what permits multiplication. I found no missing slot,
orientation, or factor-two correction.


3. All-slot entropy cancellation, including internal edges
----------------------------------------------------------
Write P_i for each star law, mu_i for its root law, and pi_ij for
the pooled pair law. Matching compatibility gives marginals mu_i,mu_j
and pair reversal. The unnormalized exponent is

  Psi=sum_i N_i H(P_i)-sum_(i<=j)E_ij H(pi_ij),
  E_ii=N_i d_ii/2,       E_ij=N_i d_ij (i<j).              (A2)

Permute slots WITHIN an old neighbor type. This preserves legal
support, root and pooled pair laws, and can only increase star entropy.
Different neighbor types are not assumed exchangeable.

For every physical slot t, lossless deletion gives

  H(P_i)=H(mu_i)+sum_(s!=t)H(Y_s|X_i)-J_it,

where J_it is nonnegative conditional total correlation of the d-1
retained slots. Average the d exact identities:

  H(P_i)=H(mu_i)+(d-1)/d sum_s H(Y_s|X_i)
                         -(1/d)sum_t J_it.               (A3)

After within-type symmetrization,
sum_s H(Y_s|X_i)=sum_j d_ij[H(pi_ij)-H(mu_i)]. Therefore

  H(P_i)=(2-d)H(mu_i)+(d-1)/d sum_j d_ij H(pi_ij)
                         -(1/d)sum_t J_it.

The weighted pair sum over directed slots is twice the undirected
sum in (A2), including internal terms because N_i d_ii=2E_ii.
After subtracting pair entropies, their coefficient is
2(d-1)/d-1=(d-2)/d. Use H(pi_ij)=H(mu_i)+H(mu_j)-I_ij.
Reversibility and the common row sum d give

  sum_(i<=j) E_ij[H(mu_i)+H(mu_j)]=d sum_i N_i H(mu_i).

Every root-entropy coefficient cancels. With Phi=Psi/n and
w_ij=E_ij/n, the exact SYMMETRIZED identity is

  L:=-Phi=(d-2)/d sum_(i<=j)w_ij I_ij
                  +(1/d)sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it>=0.      (A4)

The original unsymmetrized Phi is no larger. Internal weights include
their one-half, absent factors give no term, and degree one causes
no exceptional sign. Dividing by d here is an operation on real
entropy, not division in F_p. Thus p|d is immaterial to the moment.


4. Polynomial first moment and the explicit factorial remainder
---------------------------------------------------------------
The source finite-size errors also check. Put q_*=p^K and

  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]q_*^2,
  B=K q_*^d+F.

The elementary factorial error is between zero and log(u+1)+1.
For even u, the double-factorial error from half of u log u-u is
bounded by 2[log(u+1)+1]. All counts are <=dn. The upper error per
root multinomial is at most one such unit after dropping denominator
errors. A cross factor costs at most q^2 units. An internal factor
costs at most

  q(q-1)/2+2q+2<=2q^2

units, valid also at q=2. At most K roots, K internal factors and
K(K-1)/2 cross factors give the stated F. Zero factors are skipped.

There are at most (n+1)^(Kq_*^d) ordered-star count collections.
Applying (A4) and the uniform errors bounds the configuration moment
by

  E Z_R<= (3d)^F(n+1)^B,
  Z_R=|{Y in F_p^(n by r):AY=YR}|.                        (A5)

This applies to EVERY R, including singular or nondiagonalizable
ones, and to the whole finite-field solution space. It is not a
moment conditioned on successful recovery. With d<=D one may enlarge
B to K p^(KD)+F, as done in the synthesis.


5. Quantitative real one-hot rigidity
--------------------------------------
The variance step uses INTEGER one-hot labels, separately from the
modular moment. Let delta=sum_i alpha_i(1-max_x mu_i(x)), the minimum
normalized disagreement from any old-type point map. It need not be
onto and may merge old classes. The following derivation verifies the
exact constant in the new rigidity source.

For fixed old type i and omitted slot t, compare its actual retained
law P_i^(-t) to Q_it: keep the same root law and sample all retained
neighbors independently from their UNCONDITIONAL neighboring root
laws. The divergence is

  kappa_it=J_it+sum_(s!=t)I(X_i;Y_s).                      (A6)

It is finite because every positive joint atom has positive marginals.
If E=sum w_ij I_ij and J=sum alpha_i sum_t J_it, then
alpha_i sum_s I(X_i;Y_s)<=2E and alpha_i sum_t J_it<=J.
Consequently (A4) gives

  alpha_i sum_t kappa_it
    <=J+2(d-1)E <=[2d(d-1)/(d-2)]L.                     (A7)

Let mu_s=E e_(Y_s), V_s=1-||mu_s||^2, and on retained data put

  f_t=||R_(X_i,*)-sum_(s!=t)e_(Y_s)-mu_t||^2.

Under P_i^(-t), the omitted label is reconstructed exactly, so
E_P f_t=V_t. Under Q_it, independent retained neighbors give

  E_Q f_t=sum_(s!=t)V_s+B_i,
  B_i=E||R_(X_i,*)-sum_s mu_s||^2>=0.

The same B_i occurs for every omission. Summing differences gives

  sum_t(E_Q f_t-E_P f_t)=(d-2)sum_s V_s+dB_i.             (A8)

Individual differences need not be nonnegative; only their sum is
used. Each occupied row of R is a nonnegative vector of mass d,
as follows from the exact local equation. The retained sum plus
mu_t is another nonnegative vector of mass d, also on auxiliary
tuples. Thus 0<=f_t<=2d^2 under BOTH laws. The range form of total
variation, Pinsker, and Cauchy--Schwarz yield

  (d-2)sum_s V_s
    <=2d^2 sum_t sqrt(kappa_it/2)
    <=2d^3 sqrt[(d-1)L/(alpha_i(d-2))].

Squaring proves

  L>=alpha_i c_d (sum_s V_s)^2,
  c_d=(d-2)^3/[4d^6(d-1)].                               (A9)

For a probability vector, 1-||mu_j||^2>=1-max mu_j. Some type j
therefore has variance at least delta. It is a neighbor type of
some i: positive row degree and reversible balance supply a reverse
incident edge, allowing i=j for an internal slot. Apply (A9) at i:

  Phi<=-alpha_min c_d delta^2
       <=-alpha_min delta^2/(8D^6).                      (A10)

This last uniform simplification is valid because
(d-2)^3/(d-1)>=1/2 for every integer d>=3.

The same constant also follows from centering by the actual conditional
mean of the omitted label, which I derived independently before reading
the completed module. The source's unconditional center gives the
cleaner exact identity (A8). Neither version assumes distinct rows
of R, invertibility, or a positive mass in every candidate class.

At zero entropy all root laws are point masses. Pair marginals then
force every type-j neighbor to carry that type's point label, and
the local constraint is exactly QT=TR. Conversely such point maps
give deterministic legal stars and entropy zero. Directly at L=0,
all I and J vanish; conditional variance for EVERY omission gives
V_t=sum_(s!=t)V_s, hence (d-2)sum_s V_s=0. This confirms the
root proposal's different-slot variance argument as well.

7. Small supports and the source constants
------------------------------------------
Call S bad if no outside vertex has exactly one edge into S, counting
configuration multiplicities. With s=|S|, w exterior touched vertices,
a edges inside S and b boundary edges,

  2a+b=ds,       b>=2w,
  e=a+b<=Ds,     e-s-w>=ceil((d-2)s/2).

Each active typed pool has at least rho n stubs by (A1). For
s<=rho n/(4D), any valid prescribed partial configuration with e
edges has probability <=(2/(rho n))^e: all its successive pool
denominators stay at least rho n/2. Invalid color prescriptions may
be counted with zero probability.

Choose S,W, select W stubs, and pair all selected stubs. The source
upper count

  binom(n,s)binom(n,w)2^(Dw)(2Ds)^e

includes every witness. All S stubs are used; an actual witness has
precisely 2e selected stubs. This count may include incompatible types
or extra-invalid pairings, harmless for an upper bound. Combining
with the edge probability gives, for fixed s,w,e,

  exp(s+w)2^(Dw)(4D/rho)^e
      *(s/n)^(e-s-w)*(s/w)^w.

For w=0 the last factor is one. Otherwise it is <=exp(s/euler),
by maximizing w log(s/w). Using w<=Ds/2, e<=Ds, and summing at
most (Ds+1)^2 choices gives the stated

  E Z_s<=C^s(s/n)^ceil(c s),       c=(d-2)/2,
  C=4(D+1)^2 exp(1+D/2+1/euler)
       *2^(D^2/2)*(4D/rho)^D.                            (A12)

For example (Ds+1)^2<=(D+1)^2 s^2 and s^2<=4^s justify its
polynomial-to-exponential absorption. No field coefficients or
candidate matrices are enumerated in this graph event.

The source eta chosen below rho/(4D), (2C exp(c))^(-1/c), and
rho/[2(D+1)] makes the consecutive ratio of
u_s=C^s(s/n)^(c s) at most 1/2. Sum from s=2:

  sum_(2<=s<=eta n) E Z_s<=2C^2(2/n)^(d-2).              (A13)

On a SIMPLE positive-degree graph singleton sets are never bad.
Their nonsimple configuration occurrences are correctly excluded
before conditioning, rather than incorrectly applying the simple
minimum-support assertion to the configuration model. Thus the
simple-model bad-set probability is O_(K,D)(n^(-(d-2))).

For ANY matrix V over ANY field with AV=VR, its nonzero row support
is bad: an outside unique neighbor would give one nonzero row in
AV but a zero row in VR. The same graph event excludes all small

Let verified integer Y be within eta n rows of an arbitrary point

  (QT)_i=(AJT)_v=(AY)_v=(YR)_v=(TR)_i.

This is why applying the support lemma directly to Y-JT without

9. Simplicity conditioning and uniformity
-----------------------------------------
The new source correctly uses the limiting exponent

  Lambda=sum_(i:a_i>=1)(a_i^2-1)/4
              +sum_(i<j:d_ij>0)(d_ij-1)(d_ji-1)/2,

where a_i=d_ii. At a_i=0 the factor is absent and its contribution
is zero, NOT -1/4. At a_i=1 it is a simple matching and the formula
contributes zero. Cross factors with a degree-one side cannot have
parallel edges; their exponent is also zero.

The elementary defect factorial moments supply these limits:
internal loops have mean (a-1)/2 and internal double-edge pairs
(a-1)^2/4; cross double pairs have mean (b-1)(c-1)/2. Disjoint
defects give products, consistent overlaps lose a power of n, and
finite Bonferroni truncations give the Poisson zero-event limit.
All active pools have linear size by (A1). This supplies a uniform
eventual positive conditioning probability over the finite frame
family; Lambda<=K^2D^2 is more than sufficient.

Simple internal graphs have constant stub multiplicity (a_i!)^(N_i),
including degrees zero and one; cross graphs have multiplicity
(d_ij!)^(N_i)(d_ji!)^(N_j). Thus conditioning gives precisely
independent uniform simple factors. It is not a claim that an
unconditioned multigraph law is uniform over multigraphs. For
nonnegative moments, dividing by this positive probability gives
the UNCONDITIONAL simple-model bound used by the algorithm.


10. Unknown-frame enumeration and full expected cost
----------------------------------------------------
Enumerate all integer r-by-r R, 1<=r<=K, with nonnegative entries
and row sums<=D. At most sum_(r<=K)(D+1)^(r^2) matrices occur,
a constant at fixed K,D. Each true equitable quotient is included.

For each R compute the F2 kernel of

  I_r tensor A - R^T tensor I_n.

Column-major vectorization identifies it with AY=YR. Standard basis
vectors remain distinct in F2^r, so one-hot coding is faithful even
though scalar signs would collapse modulo two. Enumerate every
kernel vector; require one-hot rows, nonempty classes, and verify
the ORIGINAL INTEGER identity. Modular membership alone is not
accepted as an equitable partition.

The moment (A5), conditioned simple, bounds E 2^(h_R) by a fixed
polynomial uniformly over R. Binary elimination has O_K(n^3) bit
cost and storage O_K(n^2). Forming a basis combination and verifying
a candidate takes at most O_(K,D)(n^2) bit operations, a loose bound
covering all bookkeeping. Finite candidate enumeration therefore
has expected work O_(K,D)(n^(B+3)), including rare high-nullity inputs.

A deterministic n^(B+5) work cap has interruption probability
O(n^-2) by Markov. On interruption the source returns FAILURE or
ABSTENTION and does not certify a partially enumerated maximum.
The generous exponent absorbs work-counter overhead. This is
worst-case polynomial time and expected polynomial untruncated
time, with enormous theoretical exponents; practical speed is not
implied.

On the structural event, accepted Y=JT with nonempty classes makes
T onto, hence r<=k. The planted pair (J,Q) always appears in the
complete search. Therefore the maximum accepted class count is k,
and every onto k-to-k map is a permutation. Selecting any complete
maximum recovers the planted unordered classes and k. Exact neighbor
counts recover Q up to simultaneous relabeling. No Q, k, size ratios,
or approximate eigenvalues need be supplied, beyond fixed bounds K,D.

The source only promises high-probability capped success. Its stated
n^-2 cap can dominate the structural O(n^(-(d-2))) rate when d>4;
there is no contradiction because it does not claim that sharper
rate for the capped algorithm. A larger fixed polynomial cap could
be used if that rate were desired.


11. Random lifts and boundaries that were tested
------------------------------------------------
For a fixed connected SIMPLE d-regular base H, d>=3, set Q=A_H,
all N_i=N, and one independent uniform matching for each base
edge. This is exactly its random N-lift law. All diagonal factors
are absent; each cross factor has degrees (1,1). The lifted graph
is automatically simple and every N is admissible. Thus the full
theorem applies directly, with no contiguity or conditioning loss.

Bipartite bases, repeated quotient eigenvalues, identical base-neighbor
sets, and multiple coarse equitable quotients do not invalidate the
proof. Coarse quotients are allowed; only noncoarsening bounded-class
partitions are excluded with high probability. A covering-theoretic
'regular cover' need not mean a regular-degree base, so the sources
correctly avoid any deck-transitivity assertion.

Two substantive boundaries:

  * Independence of edge factors is essential. For example choose
    independent vertex-sheet permutations pi_i and set each edge
    matching to pi_j pi_i^(-1). Each marginal edge matching is uniform,
    but all cycle products are identity and the lift is N disjoint
    base copies. For a base with automorphisms, independently choosing
    different base automorphisms on different copies gives many
    noncoarsening fiber partitions. Uniform marginals alone do not
    supply the theorem.

  * Degree two is a real obstruction. A random lift of a cycle has
    connected components corresponding to cycles of one uniform
    sheet permutation. Except with probability 1/N it has more than
    one component. Separating one component from the others is an
    equitable two-cell partition with quotient 2I, not a union of
    whole planted fibers. This contradicts the analogous bounded-K
    structural claim at d=2. The source's vanishing entropy and
    variance coefficients correctly mark that boundary.

No claim was verified for irregular total degrees, arbitrary chosen
deterministic lifts, growing K, a growing base, noisy perturbations,
or numerical spectral stability. The earlier internal-degree>=2
theorem and the new regular-total-degree theorem have distinct scopes.
