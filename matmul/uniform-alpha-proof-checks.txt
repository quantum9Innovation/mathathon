MAIN CLAIMS
-----------
H(alpha,s) is the optimal Schatten POWER constant for positive endpoints,
normalized by Tr|B-A|^(alpha*s). beta=1-alpha.
Theorem1.1 proves the RELATIVE joint law
  H(1-beta,s) ~ beta^(2s-1)/(2*pi^2*s^2)
for every joint beta,s->0, including arbitrary superexponential beta.
Consequently beta*s^2*H ->exp(-2u)/(2*pi^2) when -s*log(beta)->u.
Theorem1.3 proves fixed-s Theta_s(beta^(2s-1)) for s<1/2, a critical
Theta(log(1/beta)) at s=1/2, and bounded but strictly discontinuous
behavior at alpha=1 for each 1/2<s<1. It does not assert a fixed-s
exact multiplicative limit or a limit's existence in the last case.
