NONREGULAR BLIND-FIBER EXTENSION -- RESULT, 2026-11-15
===================================================

The unrestricted extension is FALSE
----------------------------------
A random N-lift of the fixed simple connected base K_(3,12), of minimum
degree 3 and maximum degree 12, has exponentially many distinct compatible
fifteen-class fiber partitions with high probability. They have the same
quotient and all classes still have size N. This remains true when the
three degree-12 fibers are revealed and held fixed, and after conditioning
the lift to be connected. All N are admissible. There are no internal
edges in this example.

Thus neither observable degrees nor a genie revealing the entire smaller
side can restore unique-finest-partition rigidity for every nonregular
base of minimum degree at least three. This is a genuine ambiguity of
the fibers, beyond the failure of the proposed weighted entropy argument.

General complete-bipartite family and exact constants
----------------------------------------------------
Fix b>r>=3. Reveal the r small-side fibers L_i of size N, but choose the
ordered partition of the bN other labelled vertices uniformly into b
size-N fibers. Choose all rb matchings independently and uniformly.
The base and all parameters may be given to the estimator.

Number of equally likely marked lifts:
  T_N=(bN)!(N!)^((r-1)b).
An observation consists of r balanced maps V -> L_i, so its support has
size at most
  U_N=[(bN)!/(b!)^N]^r.
Not every such map tuple needs to be a lift; this is an upper bound.
If Z(G) is the number of compatible ORDERED hidden-fiber partitions,
the posterior is uniform on those Z(G) marks. Put W(G)=Z(G)/b!, counting
UNORDERED partitions. Then every randomized, computationally unrestricted
estimator has success probability at most

  E[1/W] = b!*|support(G)|/T_N
         <= s_N := b![(bN)!]^(r-1) / [(b!)^(rN)(N!)^((r-1)b)].

For fixed r,b, let
  gamma_(r,b)=r log(b!)-(r-1)b log b,
  theta_(r,b)=(r-1)(b-1)/2.
Stirling gives the full prefactor

  s_N = b! b^((r-1)/2) (2 pi N)^(-theta_(r,b))
             exp[-gamma_(r,b) N] (1+O_(r,b)(1/N)).

For 0<c<gamma_(r,b), Markov applied to 1/W gives
  P(W<=exp(cN)) <= exp(cN) s_N -> 0.
The distribution of W is the same for each fixed planted partition by
right-vertex relabelling. Therefore this multiplicity result also holds
for the usual fixed-partition lift law, not just the randomized prior.

At (r,b)=(3,12), gamma=0.32388389207363844... and theta=11.
The sign is certified exactly by (12!)^3>12^24. This is the first positive
counting exponent in the r=3 family, NOT a claimed sharp recovery
threshold. A nonpositive exponent does not prove recoverability.

The estimator impossibility is explicitly a hidden-partition inference
statement. An estimator told a fixed canonical partition can print it.
The structural exponential multiplicity statement has no such caveat:
it holds for every fixed planted partition and supplies alternative
equitable partitions with exactly the same number of nonempty classes.

The directional q system symmetrizes. Its exact feasible region is a
CAPACITATED FRACTIONAL 2-FACTOR, stronger than an ordinary fractional
perfect matching. For a simple equal-fiber base H:
  q_e in [0,1/2], sum_(e incident v) q_e=1,
or y_e=2q_e in [0,1], sum_(e incident v)y_e=2.
It exists exactly when, for every S subset V(H),
  2*i(H-S)+n_1(H-S) <= 2*|S|.
Here i and n_1 count isolated and degree-one vertices of H-S.
For a reversible frame with proportions alpha_i, the exact criterion is
  2 alpha(I_0(S))+alpha(I_1(S)) <= 2 alpha(S),
where I_a(S) contains old types outside S of remaining physical degree a.
The proof includes loops, unequal proportions, separation and layer-cake
reduction of every dual vector to a signed indicator.

The exact weighted all-slot identity is
  Phi = sum_i alpha_i[1-sum_j d_ij q_ji] H(root_i)
       -sum_(i<=j) w_ij[1-q_ij-q_ji] I_ij
       -sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it J_it.
Outgoing weights sum to one per physical star. Under the proposed two
normalizations and edge caps, the root terms cancel and Phi<=0. This
gives a polynomial finite-alphabet intertwiner moment, with all degrees
bounded and without an internal-degree lower bound.

If every q is positive and every edge coefficient 1-q_ij-q_ji is strictly
positive, the local variance argument also proves one-hot zero-phase
rigidity for minimum degree at least three. The boundary feasible case
is NOT silently given that stronger conclusion. This note does not
assemble a full restricted-class recovery algorithm.

The strict class contains nonregular bases. For K5 minus one edge, assign
q=1/3 on edges from its two degree-3 vertices to its three degree-4
vertices, and q=1/6 on edges among the latter. Every vertex sum is one,
and all weights and mutual-information coefficients are positive.

The edge cap is a real extra obstruction. Two copies of K3,4 joined by
a bridge between their surplus-side vertices have an integral perfect
matching, but every fractional perfect matching puts mass one on that
bridge, violating q<=1/2. Thus ordinary fractional perfect matching is
not the exact criterion asked for in the root prompt.
