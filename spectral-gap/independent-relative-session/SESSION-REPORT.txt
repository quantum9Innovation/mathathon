are correct as stated. The unit-mass weighted-path transfer also checks

positive fractional power. The bridge leverage stays exact throughout.

The original off-diagonal factor is essential:
  (a_i/a_j)^((1-alpha)/2) [1-(a_i/a_j)^alpha]/[1-a_i/a_j].
It tends to zero for exactly the claimed open exponent range.
The reverse-pivot telescoping leverage identity is exact at every scale.

COMPLETE FIXED-INPUT-SPECTRUM EXTENSION

Fix any r_1>=...>=r_n>0, not necessarily all >=1, and 0<alpha<1.
Among real SPD A,B with
  spec(A^(-1/2) B A^(-1/2)) = r
exactly, the closure of the relative fractional spectra is
  {q>0 : log q is majorized by alpha log r}.

The complete proof is in araki-horn-relative-range.tex/PDF, Theorem 1.1.
It combines Araki necessity, Weyl--Horn prescribed triangular diagonal
and singular values, and the reverse-Schur fractional scaling lemma.
The new note includes a self-contained short proof of that lemma using
principal minors and two elementary eigenvector bounds. The scaling
mechanism itself has prior sources, credited explicitly.

For every k, the exact universal matrix upper envelope is
  sup lambda_k(Q_alpha) = (product_{i<=k} r_i)^(alpha/k).
There is a corresponding bottom-k lower envelope. Both strict-count
optima are given explicitly in Corollary 3.2. These yield graph batch
certificates using the small effective-resistance Gram matrix
  G=U^T L^dagger U.
Higher-rank arbitrary-matrix sharpness is not asserted to hold inside a
fixed graph class. Rank-one path sharpness follows from the candidate.

EXACT RANK-ONE ATTAINMENT, BEYOND CLOSURE

Theorem 2.1 of the same PDF proves that the actual attainable lists are
precisely
  q_1>...>q_m>1, q_(m+1)=...=q_n=1,
  product_i q_i=(1+tau)^alpha,
for some 1<=m<=n (tau>0). Thus every admissible SIMPLE positive part is
attained at finite positive condition number; every positive collision is
excluded at finite condition number. No useful condition-number bound is
claimed.

Necessity uses the strictly totally positive Cauchy Gram representation
of the correction on the cyclic subspace. Compound Perron--Frobenius
gives simplicity. Sufficiency uses uniform graded convergence on a small
ball in the fixed-sum log simplex and Brouwer applied to x*+x-F_delta(x).
The detailed independent proof remains in proof-audit/exact-attainability.md.

For a graph edge b, the exact response rank equals the number of distinct
positive Laplacian eigenvalues whose projectors do not annihilate b;
all positive response eigenvalues are simple. An endpoint incidence is
cyclic on the positive support of every weighted unit-mass path. Hence
its fractional response has rank N-1 and simple positive eigenvalues
for every nonzero positive edit. Exact matrix attainment must not be
conflated with the graph realization theorem, which is a closure theorem
allowing additional eigenvalues tending to zero.

SUBSTANTIAL SIGNED GRAPH EXTENSION

graph-extension/signed-relative-closure.tex/PDF proves the following.
Let 0<ell<1<u and let one edge of a connected unit-mass weighted path
increase by D_+>=1 and a different edge decrease by D_-^(-1), D_->=1.
The exact unrestricted worst-case minimum symmetric correction rank for
  P=L^alpha+F >0 on 1-perp,
  ell P <= L'^alpha <= u P
is
  J(alpha log D_+/log u) + J(alpha log D_-/[-log ell]),
where J(x)=max(0,ceil(x)-1).

The proof combines separate signed logarithmic budgets, an exact
rank/inertia lemma, and weak concatenation of positive/negative path
witnesses. The weak-connector proof explicitly controls the small mode:
the two small eigenvalues have the same linear coefficient, so the
fractional difference remains small enough after inverse normalization.

Reciprocal edge edits D and D^(-1) preserve the nonzero Laplacian
determinant exactly, yet for ell=1-epsilon,u=1+epsilon the exact worst-case
rank is
  2 alpha log D / epsilon + O_(alpha,D)(1).
It is unbounded as D grows at fixed epsilon despite net determinant 1.
Separate positive/negative budgets are therefore necessary information
for signed dynamic preconditioning.

The signed matrix two-budget closure is also proved explicitly, including
cancellation. It is a corollary of the full Araki--Horn range theorem:
for n>=2 its input list can be fixed at
  (D_+,1,...,1,D_-^(-1)).
The LDL construction is retained as independent algebraic verification,
not presented as an independent matrix-theory breakthrough.

FUNCTIONAL EXTENSION

proof-audit/function-rank-characterization.md proves that for
  f(x)=a+bx+integral x/(x+t) mu(dt),
a positive complete Bernstein function, a rank-one update on cyclic
dimension m has exact response rank
  min(m, #supp(mu)+1_(b>0)).
All positive relative eigenvalues are simple. Uniform exact-rank bounds
over dimensions occur precisely for rational functions in this class.
The ingredients are classical Cauchy/Loewner-rank theory; rational update
rank bounds predate this work, including Bernstein--Van Loan (2000),
DOI 10.1137/S0895479898333636. This derived characterization is not
presented with a historical-novelty claim.
