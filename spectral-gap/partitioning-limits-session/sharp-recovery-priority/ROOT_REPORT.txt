This is a bounded priority/subsumption audit of the exact ambiguity and

1. OUTCOME
----------

No inspected primary source directly states either full target law:

  two-class RSBM: ambiguity ~ C N^(-nu), Bayes risk ~ (C/2) N^(-nu),
  complete-base lifts: ambiguity ~ binom(d+1,2) N^(-(d-2)),
                       Bayes risk ~ binom(d+1,2)/2 N^(-(d-2)).

The strongest qualification is substantial: the LOCAL motif enumeration
behind these constants is established random-graph counting. In the lift
case, Friedman--Kohler Theorem 2.4.7 directly gives the occurrence constant
one for each colored twin motif. Once that motif is specified, its exact
expectation and the finite-union leading coefficient are elementary
specializations. The RSBM motif coefficient likewise follows from standard
fixed-pattern probabilities after the witness family has been identified.

The potentially distinct contribution is therefore the exhaustive leading
ambiguity classification: proving that all other compatible partitions
are lower order, and proving that the leading event has exactly TWO
compatible unordered partitions. These assertions turn classical local
motif counts into the full statistical ambiguity law. The posterior
half-factor is elementary once the two-state assertion is established.

The closest inspected RSBM uniqueness proofs, cubic decomposition counts,
metastable-state calculations, and deterministic cover-coloring results
do not establish that combination in the target ensembles. The precise
comparisons and limitations follow; an absence claim remains bounded by
the actual sources inspected.

2. EXACT TARGETS AND NORMALIZATION
---------------------------------

RSBM: fixed integers a>b>0; two blocks each of admissible size N; independent uniform
simple a-regular graphs internally and a uniform simple b-regular
bipartite graph across. Put delta=a-b, t=delta+1, D=a+b, nu=(D-2)t, and

  C = ((bt)!)^2 (a!)^(2t)
      / ((t!)^2 (b!)^(2t) a^(t delta)).

The claimed probability is that the observed labeled graph admits another
unordered compatible balanced (a,b) partition. The Bayes statement uses
the stated uniform hidden balanced-partition prior and exact recovery up
to swapping the two block names. For (a,b)=(2,1), ambiguity is 4/N^2 and
Bayes risk is 2/N^2. If graph order is denoted n=2N, these are 16/n^2 and
8/n^2. Constants must not be transported without this conversion.

Lift: fixed d>=3, k=d+1, N vertices in each base fiber of K_k, independent
uniform perfect matchings over base edges. The stronger structural target
allows any noncoarsening equitable partition with at most a fixed K>=k
classes. Its leading probability is binom(k,2) N^(-(d-2)). The Bayes claim
concerns compatible hidden balanced K_k-cover partitions, with unordered
color names, and has half this coefficient. For total order n=kN the
ambiguity coefficient becomes binom(k,2) k^(d-2). These are particular
complete-base and prior assumptions, not a universal cover law.

3. RSBM: CLOSEST OVERLAP ENUMERATION AND LOCAL FORMULAS
-----------------------------------------------------

Brito--Dumitriu--Ganguly--Hoffman--Tran, Recovery and Rigidity in a
Regular Stochastic Block Model, SODA 2016; inspected arXiv v4 (2020):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00930v4
  https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611974331.ch108

Theorem 1 gives a.a.s. uniqueness in a sufficiently large fixed-degree
regime. Section 3.2.2 counts mixed clusters by overlap alpha N and sums
upper bounds over alpha=1/N,...,1/2. It does not give a matching lower
bound, the minimum-witness coefficient C, a two-partition leading law,
or a Bayes constant. Minimum flips alpha=t/N eventually lie in its I1
range, so microscopic overlaps cannot simply be excluded from comparison.

There is a rate issue in the displayed proof: (3.15) is polynomial
(printed <=1/N; its penultimate expression is N^-2), whereas the final
combination after (3.20) asserts a tau^N bound. The exponential statement
does not follow from those displayed estimates. The proof also conditions
on internal expansion known only a.a.s.; its failure probability must be
restored before claiming any unconditional sharp rate. In particular,
the target's finite witnesses can violate that expansion condition in
some degree regimes. Do not describe them as contradicting a bound
conditional on expansion. An unconditional exponential bound for the full
mixed-cluster union would nevertheless be false: one fixed feasible
degree-saturated flip motif already has positive polynomial probability.
The paper's stated a.a.s. uniqueness theorem is unaffected by this point.

Karmakar--Podder, Uniqueness of communities in regular stochastic block
models, arXiv:2002.05577v2 (2021), Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 3.2:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.05577v2
This is k>=3 large fixed degree-matrix uniqueness via overlap-profile
upper bounds. No matching local ambiguity coefficient or Bayes law was
found in the inspected source. It is relevant structural prior art but
does not supply the target two-class exact constant.

Barucca, Spectral partitioning in equitable graphs, PRE 95 (2017), 062310:
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.95.062310
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.02668
The full published paper and latest v5 text were inspected. Section IVA
explicitly treats the cubic a=2,b=1 case in Figs. 2 and 4. Its IPR
comparisons and Fig. 5 IPR gap are not ambiguity frequencies. No 4/N^2
failure or 2/N^2 Bayes asymptotic was found. The cubic model and a broad
conjectural recovery claim themselves are established prior art.

Zhu--Zhu, arXiv:2312.03300v3 (September 2026), Sections 3.3 and 9:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03300v3
The RSBM statements inspected are high-probability statements; they do
not supply these sharp coefficients. Other eigenvector probability rates
in that paper should not be mistaken for RSBM ambiguity probabilities.

Closest established local counting:

  Gao--Ohapkin, Random Structures & Algorithms 62 (2023), 911--934,
  Theorem 4; inspected arXiv:2007.02216v4:
    https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.02216v4
    https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.21123
  For fixed H and bounded specified degrees, the theorem's product bounds
  give P(H subset G)=(1+O(1/N)) prod_v (d_v)_(deg_H(v))
       / prod_(j=1)^e(H) (M-2j+2), with M=sum_v d_v.

  Larkin--McKay--Tian, arXiv:2510.24276v1 (2025), generic Theorem 4.3 /
  Corollary 4.4 and bipartite Theorem 5.4 / Corollary 5.5:
    https://arxiv.org/html/2510.24276v1
  The corresponding leading fixed-pattern products include generic
  prod_v[d_v]_(h_v)/(2^e[m]_e) and bipartite
  prod_i[s_i]_(x_i) prod_j[t_j]_(y_j)/[m]_e, under their hypotheses.

Once the target's distinct-boundary minimum witness is specified, these
formulas reproduce its local calculation. The witness has
v0=2t+2bt, e0=t delta+4bt, e0-v0=nu. Its embedding-family size is

  A_N=binom(N,t)^2 binom(N-t,bt)^2 [(bt)!/(b!)^t]^4.

The leading probability of each specified colored pattern is
(a! b!)^(2t) a^(-t delta) N^(-e0). Multiplication yields C N^(-nu).
This is an inference from standard local enumeration and the target's
chosen motif, not a formula claimed to appear explicitly in those papers.
Neither generic counting theorem identifies ALL alternative partitions.

4. LIFTS: A DIRECT CLASSICAL SPECIALIZATION
------------------------------------------

Friedman--Kohler, The Relativized Second Eigenvalue Conjecture of Alon,
arXiv:1403.3462v1 (2014), Section 2.4.2, Theorem 2.4.7, printed pp.65--67:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.3462v1
The actual theorem and proof were inspected. For a fixed feasible etale
B-graph of order r=e-v, expected injections are N^(-r)+O(N^(-r-1)).
If the motif is pruned and each component has positive order, occurrence
probability is N^(-r)/|Aut_B|+O(N^(-r-1)).

For a prescribed unordered center-color pair {a,b}, the twin motif has
one vertex of every base color: an edge ab plus two edges from the centers
to each remaining color. Thus v=d+1, e=2d-1, r=d-2, and |Aut_B|=1.
The theorem directly yields occurrence probability
N^(-(d-2))+O(N^(-(d-1))). More specifically its expectation is exactly
N^(d+1) N^(-(2d-1))=N^(-(d-2)). A finite overlap check then yields the
binom(d+1,2) union coefficient. This motif count should be credited as
classical. The theorem counts a specified subgraph, not all equitable
partitions or the number of hidden cover decompositions.

The authors explicitly trace the argument to Friedman, A Proof of Alon's
Second Eigenvalue Conjecture and Related Problems, Theorem 4.7. The author
PDF inspected is the May 2004 revision, printed pp.36--39:
  https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~jf/pubs/web_stuff/alon.pdf
Its labeled-tangle expectation and occurrence bounds already give the
same order/automorphism counting principle in permutation models; the
general-base statement above is the direct complete-base reference.

Foucaud--Perarnau, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 19(1) (2012), P32,
proof of Theorem 19, printed p.20, explicitly bounds adjacent true-twin
probability by O(n^(-(d-2))) in the uniform d-regular model:
  https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v19i1p32/pdf/0
Thus the mechanism and exponent have older explicit appearances, even
outside lift counting. That bound does not give the target lift constant
or classify all alternative covers. Its ensemble has different weights.

The exact cover-coloring object is also classical. A K_(d+1) cover
partition is a totally silver coloring, equivalently a proper coloring
of G^2 with d+1 colors, or a partition into independent efficient
dominating sets. Ghebleh--Mahmoodian, arXiv:1302.2986, Corollary 2.7,
states the equal-class/perfect-matching characterization:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1302.2986
Also inspected were Silver Cubes (2008), structural/defining-set results,
and portions of Bakker--van Leeuwen--Tan, Perfect Colorings (1990).
No matching random hidden-cover ambiguity constant was found. These
deterministic characterizations must be credited; their existence or
generation theorems alone do not supply a sharp probability of uniqueness.

Silas, arXiv:1607.01330v2, Theorem 4.1, already gives a sharper connected
lift probability 1-N^(-(ell-1))+O(N^(-ell)) for base Betti number ell:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.01330v2
For K_k, ell-1=k(d-2)/2>d-2. Connected conditioning at the target leading
scale is therefore consistent with established lift connectivity theory;
it is not a separate new recovery constant.

5. CUBIC DECOMPOSITION STATISTICS AND MODEL WEIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------

An equitable (2,1) partition of a cubic graph is exactly a perfect matching
cut: every vertex has one cross neighbor. Such a cut is automatically
balanced. Le--Telle, The Perfect Matching Cut Problem Revisited (2022),
is a primary terminology/complexity reference:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.06399
Its algorithmic results are not random planted failure asymptotics.

Goedgebeur--Renders--Van Overberghe, Generation of Cycle Permutation Graphs
and Permutation Snarks, arXiv:2411.12606v4 (7 May 2026), Table 4:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.12606v4
This is real prior finite decomposition-count evidence. Strong versus
weak canonical generation gives 123 versus 127 outputs at total order 16,
and 152626831184 versus 153848448652 at order 34. Duplications concern
different permutation 2-factors after graph isomorphism is considered.

These counts are not planted ambiguity or Bayes probabilities. A cycle
permutation graph requires EACH internal 2-factor to be one Hamilton
cycle; the RSBM permits arbitrary simple 2-regular internal graphs.
Moreover the tables count unlabelled graphs and canonical decomposition
orbits, rather than labeled compatible partitions. Two partitions related
by a graph automorphism remain distinct hidden states under labeled exact
recovery, but can contribute only one orbit to weak generation. Therefore
the quoted 99.21% strong/weak ratio is not a statistical uniqueness rate,
even in the Hamilton-cycle restriction.

Kwak--Lee's older cycle-permutation formulas use natural isomorphisms
preserving the designated cycles. The 1998 primary scanned paper,
Theorem 1, printed p.141, reproduces the characterization through dihedral
conjugation and inversion orbits of N-cycles; p.147 tabulates bipartite
positive-natural classes. The 1992 cover interpretation was inspected
only at primary-abstract level. These are genuine cover-enumeration
predecessors, with designated decompositions and a different quotient:
  https://combinatorialpress.com/article/ars/Volume%20050/volume-50-paper-10.pdf
  https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(92)90137-5

Lefevre--Dean, Metastable states of a ferromagnet on random thin graphs,
cond-mat/0011265 (published 2001):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0011265
Equation (4) defines the uniform simple degree-conditioned ensemble;
equations (18)--(19) count fixed-energy states and give their annealed
logarithmic entropy. Section 3 also fixes magnetization. In the cubic
case, metastability gives signed local field at least one. Energy per
vertex E=-1/2 forces all these fields to equal one, hence exactly the
perfect-matching-cut/(2,1) states. This is a close earlier analytical
counting object, not merely unrelated generic spectral prior art.

Its unconditional random-regular ensemble and annealed leading entropy
do not supply the target planted second-decomposition probability or
its relative 4/N^2 correction. To make the distinction exact, let U be
uniform simple cubic graphs on the fixed labeled vertex set, and let
K(G) count unordered perfect matching cuts. The planted RSBM marginal is

  P_pl(G) = K(G) P_U(G) / E_U K.

Consequently
  P_pl(K>=2) = E_U[K 1_(K>=2)] / E_U K,
  R_Bayes = E_U[(K-1)_+] / E_U K.

These identities are audit deductions from uniform compatible-generation
counts, not statements attributed to Lefevre--Dean. An annealed first
moment E_U K alone cannot determine either numerator. Exponential-scale
agreement cannot determine the target relative polynomial coefficient.
Equivalently, the cubic Bayes law requires
P_U(K>=1)/E_U K = 1-2/N^2+o(N^(-2)). The denominator itself has the exact
classical pair count [binom(2N,N)/2] g_2(N)^2 N! / g_3(2N), where g_r(m)
counts labeled simple r-regular graphs. The missing information is the
relative refinement distinguishing graphs from graph/cut pairs.

6. ATTRIBUTION AND RESIDUAL SEARCH LIMITS
---------------------------------------

Both hidden-prior models have constant likelihood over compatible
unordered partitions. If Z is their number, posterior exact-recovery
error is 1-1/Z. The half-factor follows when Z=2 throughout the leading
ambiguity event and Z>=3 is lower order. Attribute the substantive new
work, if the proof is correct, to that event classification and dominance
argument; do not advertise the finite Bayesian identity as a new method.

Recommended bounded wording:
  We determine sharp ambiguity and exact-recovery Bayes-error asymptotics
  in these fixed-degree planted ensembles by classifying the finite
  witnesses that exhaust their leading nonuniqueness events. The local
  witness probabilities use classical graph enumeration.

Do not claim a first exact twin law, a new random-lift counting principle,
the first cubic equitable model, or historical firstness from this audit.

The searches used RSBM uniqueness/overlap, exact failure and Bayes terms;
perfect matching cuts, cycle permutation graphs and decomposition counts;
random covers/lifts, automorphisms, true twins, locally bijective maps;
perfect/strong/totally silver colorings, square colorings and defining sets;
and metastable-state counts. Close primary formulas were compared by
their event, model, weighting and precision, not merely by title.
