Primary comparison for the positive finite-field direction
========================================================
Read date: 15 November2026. Historical novelty remains UNVERIFIED.
This is a focused primary-source sweep, not an exhaustive claim to know
every paper, nor a substitute for an external research review.

The claim being checked is exact quotient-eigenvalue multiplicity and
polynomial-bit exact recovery WITHOUT a spectral-outlier condition in
rigid regular block models. The equal-block derivation was developed
in the primary session; this directory proves the unequal-block case.

Imported machinery, not claimed new
----------------------------------
Mészáros, arXiv1806.03736v4, Theorem1.6, official PDFp3 and matching
model definitionp1: strong sum-over-inputs maximum-atom mixing for sums
of d>=3 random perfect matchings on EVEN vertex sets. Over F_p, p odd,
the nonconstant reference vectors are uniform on one sum hyperplane.
This is the difficult published input. We do not claim its proof.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.03736v4

Nguyen–Wood, arXiv1806.10068v2, proofTheorem1.6, official PDFp8:
explicit matching-union/loopless-configuration contiguity and bounded
probability conditioning to obtain uniform simple graphs; references
Janson1995Thm11 and Molloy–Robalewska–Robinson–Wormald1997Thm3.
Its inspected main results concern unshifted adjacency cokernels and
nonsingularity, not planted block quotient multiplicity.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.10068v2

Huang, Invertibility of adjacency matrices for random d-regular graphs,
arXiv1807.06465v2: inspected via the primary session's actual-source
audit. Theorem1.3/Remark1.4 are unshifted nonsingularity results for
the uniform/configuration graph, not the planted block-conditioned
ensemble. They cannot simply be cited for our model and shifted mode.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.06465v2

Closest exact regular-block literature inspected
----------------------------------------------
Brito–Dumitriu–Ganguly–Hoffman–Tran, arXiv1507.00930v4 (4May2020),
Sections1,2.3,4, Theorem2: standing minimum within/across degrees>=3;
efficient recovery proof uses an old planted eigenvalue outside the
universal-cover edge, and even internal degree in its lift comparison.
Its uniqueness theorem has sufficiently large fixed degrees. The lift
proof bounds new eigenvalues in absolute value, so does not exclude
an exact collision at a planted value inside that support.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00930v4

Barucca, Spectral partitioning in equitable graphs, arXiv1610.02668v5,
SectionsIII.A andIV.A–V, actual PDFpp4–5: exact community eigenvectors
can sit in the Kesten–McKay bulk; their IPR is1/N; full-regime efficient
two-class recovery is a conjecture supported by numerics. The method
is an antecedent for interior-mode identification, not a rigorous proof
of the no-extra-eigenvalue event used in our theorem.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.02668v5

Zhu–Zhu, arXiv2312.03300v3 (25Sep2026), Sections1.3,3.3,9: explicitly
still describes Barucca's full-regime efficient recovery conjecture as
open. Theorem3.10/Corollary3.11 work in the separated real-NB regime.
Lemma9.1 has unqualified displayed simplicity wording, but its cited
old lift proof uses separation and supplies no embedded collision
argument. The paper's actual scoped main theorem, not an unqualified
reading of that lemma, is the appropriate comparison.
https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03300v3

Karmakar–Podder, Uniqueness of communities in regular stochastic block
models, arXiv2002.05577v2 (18Jan2021), model and Theorem3.1 inspected
in official HTML/PDF. Equal-size K>=3 classes, constant total degree,
possibly unequal internal/across degrees. Theorem3.1 requires all
frame entries sufficiently large and degree-comparison hypotheses;
it gives high-probability UNIQUENESS, not an efficient recovery
algorithm or exact prescribed-eigenvalue multiplicity. It must be
credited in any general-frame extension. Its homogeneous case allows
cross degrees much larger than within degrees, so do not misdescribe
its uniqueness statement as requiring assortativity or an outlier gap.
https://arxiv.org/html/2002.05577v2

Wan–Meila, A class of network models recoverable by spectral clustering,
NeurIPS2015, actual paper Theorem3 and assumptions inspected. Preference
Frame Models include heterogeneous models, but the main recovery
result requires logarithmic expected/observed minimum degrees and a
leading-spectrum separation assumption. It does not imply exact kernel
recovery at fixed degree from a prescribed interior mode.
https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/17c3433fecc21b57000debdf7ad5c930-Paper.pdf

Known easy detection is distinct from our recovery theorem
--------------------------------------------------------
Bandeira–Banks–Kunisky–Moore–Wein, Spectral Planting and the Hardness of
Refuting Cuts, Colorability, and Communities in Random Graphs, COLT2021,
Remark3 (PDFp7), Section1.1.4 and Conjecture8 inspected. Remark3 already
describes polynomial-time noiseless equitable-model distinguishing for
all parameter settings. Their conjectured hard model ADDS rewiring
noise precisely to defeat brittle algebraic algorithms. Noisy-model
hardness is not contradicted by our noiseless exact-kernel result.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v134/bandeira21a/bandeira21a.pdf

He–Huang–Wang, Bulk universality of random regular graphs,
arXiv2610.08127v1 (6Oct2026), main fixed-energy point-process theorem
and Theorems1.1–1.2/Corollary1.3 inspected. Uniform simple fixed-degree
RRGs, not exact block-conditioned graphs. Fixed-energy Sine1 convergence
excludes any prescribed exact bulk eigenvalue in the null model, an
easy modern sufficient input for distinguishing. The rare conditioning
that forces a planted mode prevents a direct transfer to RSBM recovery.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08127v1

Huang–Xu, Local law and eigenvector delocalization for random lifts,
arXiv2610.37463v1 (27Oct2026), main definitions/theorems inspected:
fixed SIMPLE base, minimum degree>=3, near-microscopic resolution away
from singular energies. Neither growing-base constants nor absence
of one exact collision at a planted bulk energy follow from that
resolution alone. A two-vertex loop quotient is outside the stated
simple-base scope.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37463v1

Known BH/robust SBM thresholds deliberately excluded
--------------------------------------------------
See literature-robust/LITERATURE_REPORT.txt for exact primary statements.
Dong–McKenzie2610.41802 already gives raw BH recovery at KS for every
fixed common expected degree>1. Their2608.16672 handles negative counts
down to that degree. Mohanty–Raghavendra–Wu2402.13921 already provides
robust recovery above KS with boosted BH and sparse robust PCA.
The independent-edge SBM and its Kesten–Stigum threshold are different
from the exact-degree planted algebraic equations used here.

What this sweep does and does not establish
-----------------------------------------
No inspected primary theorem directly states the derived even-block
no-outlier recovery/multiplicity conclusion for arbitrary prechosen
simple cross blocks. A published strong mixing theorem makes the
proof short once the correct affine comparison is identified. Absence
of a matching theorem in this focused sweep does NOT certify historical
firstness. External researchers may know an immediate corollary or an
additional source not found here. The verified mathematical statement,
its dependence on published machinery, and historical priority are
separate claims.
