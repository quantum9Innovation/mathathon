1. OUTCOME AND ATTRIBUTION BOUNDARY
----------------------------------

No inspected primary theorem directly subsumes the current untruncated
raw bound for every fixed reversible frame satisfying the strict
nonnegative omission certificate, including forced zero weights.
This is a bounded-search finding, not a proof that no such result exists.

Substantial closer prior art was found and must be credited:

* Huang's 2024 thesis and Huang--Vontobel's 2026 paper provide an ACTUAL

  It would be false to say all prior cover results give only logarithmic

  hard-linear target example; it does not cover the full present class.
* A 2023 cryptanalysis report already uses Gaussian elimination and
  grouping identical affine coordinate forms to recover hidden K_4
  fibers experimentally. This is a direct algebraic algorithm ancestor.
  The random K_4-cover recovery question was explicit in 1993.

The defensible candidate advance is the model-wide UNTRUNCATED moment
bound, with complete treatment of tied point/coset phases and rare
configurations, and the separately justified general-frame recovery and
expected-work guarantee. Neither finite-field counting, arbitrary local
constraints, Gaussian elimination, nor the cubic grouping idea itself
should be claimed as new.

2. THE CURRENT TARGET, SEPARATE FROM EARLIER FALLBACKS

a_i with a_i d_ij=a_j d_ji, and n_i=a_i N. Total degrees may vary. Every
cross factor is a uniform POOLED stub bijection; every internal factor
is a uniform pairing. The factors are independent. Loops count twice;
configuration parity is retained. Direct simplicity conditioning gives
the specified independent uniform simple regular/biregular factors.

Assume symmetric weights on present factors satisfying

  0<=q_ij=q_ji<1/2,       sum_j d_ij q_ij=1.             (C)

The current actual theorem states, for every fixed prime p, dimension r
and R in Mat_r(F_p),

  sup_(admissible N) E #{Y in F_p^(n by r): A_N Y=Y R}<infinity.

This is the RAW count: no exceptional-graph deletion, normalization by
the old kernel, or truncation of the algorithm's search. It also bounds
all fixed powers. The actual induction theorem permits different root
and output spaces and injective channels; positive-weight channels are
isomorphisms. Those spaces close under restriction to entropy-zero phases.

3. EXACT PROTOGRAPH COUNTS AND INTERIOR RAW PREFACTORS ARE CLASSICAL
------------------------------------------------------------------

Fogal--McEliece--Thorpe, Enumerators for Protograph Ensembles of LDPC Codes,
ISIT 2005, DOI 10.1109/ISIT.2005.1523728, equations (5),(8),(11),(13):
the primary indexed text gives the exact expected-type starting identity
and a maximum-entropy expression for its exponential rate. Direct PDF
retrieval failed; the report records this access qualification.

Dolecek--Divsalar--Sun--Amiri, Non-Binary Protograph-Based LDPC Codes:
Enumerators, Analysis, and Designs, IEEE IT 60 (2014), 3913--3941:
  https://www.seas.ucla.edu/csl/files/publications/NBPB_ToDistribute_ForFeedbackProject.pdf
Definition 4 fixes each base-edge field label and averages independent
permutations. Theorems 3--4, equations (23)--(24), give exact frequency
enumerators through local check-word multinomials and variable-type
multinomial denominators. Section IV-C studies their normalized logarithmic
growth. This is direct multitype linear-constraint enumeration prior art;
the inspected results do not bound the total raw count uniformly in lift
size for the target symmetric model and all its boundary sectors.

Vontobel, Counting in Graph Covers, arXiv:1012.0065v2 / IEEE IT 59 (2013):
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.0065
Lemma 29 gives the exact graph-cover type ratio, Theorems 30 and 33 its
Bethe entropy and exponential growth. These own the general counting
framework, not merely an analogous heuristic. A zero exponent alone does
not give O(1) expectation.

Mori--Tanaka, arXiv:1210.2592v2, Lemma 16, and Mori's 2013 thesis,
arXiv:1303.2168, Theorem 5.4 / Corollary 5.5, printed pp.55 and 57:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.2592
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.2168
These give raw expectation asymptotics of the form
  E Z_cover = exp(-M min F_Bethe) K(G) sum_min sqrt(zeta(u)) (1+o(1)),
with finitely many minima, ALL in the required interior, appropriate
constraint dimension, and nondegenerate Hessians. Thus central O(1)
prefactors and lattice factors cannot be claimed as new.
Mori--Tanaka's Central Approximation, arXiv:1202.0655, Theorem 5, also
treats a regular bipartite POOLED configuration ensemble with exact lattice
correction; pooled central counting itself is likewise established prior.

The thesis's Section 5.3.1, printed p.59, explicitly discusses linear
constraints, zeta=1 and a lattice factor encoding finite-base rank loss;
it writes Z_AB^(1)(G)=Z(G). This is an especially close finite-base
linear-code identity, not a full raw expectation theorem for critical
cover systems with tied point/coset minima. A support restriction can
analyze a face but does not by itself bound solutions leaking out of it.

Arbitrary fixed vector alphabets and matrix R can be represented by local
factors in these frameworks. That formal expressiveness is not a sound
standalone novelty distinction.

4. AN ACTUAL UNIFORM RAW BOUND WITH A DIFFERENT CONDITION
-------------------------------------------------------

Huang, 2024 thesis arXiv:2412.05942, Theorem 91 / Appendix E, and
Huang--Vontobel, arXiv:2506.16250v2 (17 August 2026), Theorem 46,
condition (40), Appendix G, Lemma 57:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05942
  https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16250v2

Put B=Z_B,SPA* and P=product_f sum |f_LCT|. Their condition B>(2/3)P
gives alpha=(P-B)/B<1/2. The proof explicitly obtains
  E Z_cover / B^M <= 1+sum_(j=1)^M alpha^j <=1/(1-alpha)<2.
This is a genuine raw bound. The transform permits zero message components.

It does not directly cover the natural hard-linear representation here.
For example, take base K_4 over F_3 and target R=0. The base has three
kernel labelings. The star/edge-pair encoding has Bethe maximum one
(nonpositive entropy, with a uniform equality phase). The diagonal
standard-to-double-edge embedding preserves its partition function.
With B=1, even M=1 contradicts the displayed strict upper bound <2.
Thus the required dominance condition cannot hold in that encoding.
This is an explicit scope obstruction, not a claim that the source lacks
raw bounds or that every possible reparameterization has been excluded.

5. SPARSE RANK THEOREMS DO NOT SUPPLY THE NEEDED RAW ESTIMATE
-----------------------------------------------------------

Coja-Oghlan--Gao--Hahn-Klimroth--Lee--Mueller--Rolvien, The full rank
condition for sparse random matrices, CPC 33 (2024), 643--707:
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S096354832400021X
Theorem 1.1, printed p.645, has a strict potential inequality excluding
the square critical case: the paper explicitly derives mean column
degree < mean row degree. Its Tanner graph law has prescribed row/column
degrees and independent nonzero coefficients, without target reciprocal
adjacency blocks or fixed old-type margins.

More decisively, equations (2.7)--(2.9), printed p.652, use a typical event
O and a TRUNCATED second moment E[Z^2 1_O]~(E Z)^2, where Z counts solutions
with a random right-hand side. Section 7.1, p.676, correspondingly controls
the truncated kernel count. The source explains why atypical matrices can
dominate the raw expectation. Neither whp full rank nor this truncation
gives the target's unconditional moment. The RANDOM 2023 version, p.54:7,
also describes a direct Laplace raw-moment calculation under an annealed
maximizer criterion; that is method prior art, not the full stated frame
theorem under (C).

van der Hofstad--Mueller--Zhu, arXiv:2508.02813v1 (2025), Theorems 2.4/3.1:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02813
This is closer symmetric prescribed-degree configuration-model prior art.
It proves normalized rank convergence under empirical-degree and further
generating-function assumptions, not uniform raw moments. The matching
is global/uncolored rather than within fixed frame pools. Its erasing
conventions are compared by o(n) rank changes, insufficient for raw moments.
The target certificate need not imply its log-concavity hypothesis: the
frame [[2,1],[1,6]], equal sizes, q_11=q_12=q_21=1/3, q_22=1/9 qualifies,
but degrees 3 and 7 give psi''(x)=3x+21x^5 with positive second logarithmic
derivative at x^4=1/7. The detailed report records the other theorem branch.

Bapst--Coja-Oghlan's 2015 typed factor-graph Bethe results and Alamino--Saad's
2008/2009 sparse kernel/eigenspace replica formulas provide further close
enumeration and entropy predecessors. Their inspected conclusions concern
normalized logarithms or dimension densities, not the required all-phase
O(1) raw expectation. The separate ALAMINO_SAAD_COMPARISON.txt pins the
actual raw replica expression as well as the logarithmic quantity computed.

The earlier d653cd94... regular-lift priority audit already covers the
strong global permutation/matching mixing results of Meszaros and the
Nguyen--Wood cokernel consequences. Those are genuine raw-moment ancestors.
This search did not relabel them as merely normalized-rank results or redo
that completed global-model search. A fixed type-conditioned frame law is
an additional model requirement; contiguity does not transfer an unbounded
expectation.

One further attribution caution: all shifted scalar moments over finite
extensions would already imply bounded matrix-R intertwiners. Over a
splitting field K/F_p, triangularize R; each successive column's affine
fiber has at most |ker_K(A-lambda_j I)| elements. Taking the extension-
degree root and applying Holder bounds the F_p count by scalar moments.
Thus arbitrary R alone is not an independent novelty defense if a prior
theorem already has those stronger scalar hypotheses.

6. DIRECT HIDDEN-FIBER ALGORITHM PREDECESSORS
---------------------------------------------

Fellows--Koblitz, Kid Krypto, CRYPTO '92 proceedings, LNCS 740 (1993),
371--389, Section 4.5, printed p.385:
  https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-48071-4_27
It constructs four sets and six random one-to-one correspondences,
explicitly identifies K_4 covers and asks about average-case recovery of
the hidden vertex partition. The scan's 1998 copyright footer is not its
publication date. The random construction matches the lift object; it
does not formally specify an independent-uniform probability axiom.

Cottaar et al., Report on evaluation of KpqC candidates, 2023/1853,
Chapter 3, Section 3.1, printed p.17 / PDF page 23 (ONE-indexed):
  https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1853
The IPCC7 second attack is secret-key recovery from the public graph.
The chapter credits this attack to Bernstein, Cottaar and Lange.
It solves (A+I)v=1 by echelon reduction and groups identical affine
coordinate forms. In ten four-by-64 random-cover examples, seven give
the four hidden fibers; three give cells 1,1,63,63,64,64 and two possible
partitions. It explicitly anticipates further ambiguities.

This is direct prior algebraic fiber recovery, not just ciphertext
decryption or generic worst-case cover recognition. Its inspected passage
gives experiments and an informal high-probability assessment, not an
asymptotic all-frame raw-moment theorem, a proved uniform-simple recovery
guarantee, or unconditional soft-O(n^2) expected bit complexity. The
present theorem may add those guarantees and scope, if its separate
composition audits hold; it does not invent the cubic method.

The omission certificate also has classical LP geometry: x_e=2q_type(e)
gives degree equations sum_(e incident v) x_e=2 with 0<=x_e<1, counting
loop incidences twice. Positive omissions additionally give x_e>0.
This is a type-invariant fractional 2-factor feasibility condition with
strict unsaturated upper bounds. Classical feasibility/counting results
for fractional factors are not themselves raw linear-kernel theorems.
Greenhill--Janson--Rucinski's random-lift perfect-matching counts already
combine fractional-factor polytopes and full lattice Laplace factors.

7. ADDITIONAL SCALAR CORANK LAW: SEPARATE AND CLASSICALLY NORMALIZED
------------------------------------------------------------------

The new scope supplied during this audit is fixed NONBIPARTITE connected
simple d-regular base lifts, fixed ODD p and scalar lambda, with
t=dim ker(B-lambda I). It claims all fixed moments
  E p^(r(dim ker(A_N-lambda I)-t)) -> product_(0<=j<r)(1+p^j),
and a separate determinacy argument gives total-variation convergence to
  pi_p(k)=c_p / product_(j=1)^k(p^j-1),
  c_p=product_(j>=1, j odd)(1-p^(-j)).

The comparator is classical: Fulman--Goldstein, arXiv:1211.0504v4,
Section 4, equation (20) gives the limiting law; equation (21) gives the
finite-size uniform symmetric rank count, and Theorem 4.1 its TV bounds:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0504v4
No inspected primary fixed-base-cover corank law directly subsumes this
graph-specific statement. The search did not certify firstness. The
formula and moment method must be credited, with candidate novelty
limited to the fixed-base lift distribution. No characteristic-two,
bipartite, all-frame, growing-prime or cokernel/group-law extension is
being asserted by this comparison.
