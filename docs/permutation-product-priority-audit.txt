Actual spectral-gap input and a rejected graph-product novelty claim
===================================================================

Research/audit note, 2026-11-14. This note records evidence that changes
the next research action; it is not a breakthrough or priority claim.

The newly mounted spectral-gap project was read at CONTRIBUTION.md and
CLAIM_LEDGER.md. Its main new inputs concern exact mixed moments of
centered permutation cells, their attained extrema, and uniform diagonal
moment asymptotics. These may supply a sharper moment lemma for a sparse
matrix-product sketch, but an actual sketch and collision-pattern count
are still required. Replacing that missing construction by a general
graph-function update theorem does not use those specific new results.

A plausible alternative was to combine matrix products with spectral
gaps of independent regular-graph factors. Its stationary rank-one
component is trivially an exact best rank-one approximation; the
interesting question would be the sharp residual norm. The equal-degree
free-probability residual constant is ALREADY IN PRIMARY PRIOR ART:

  Vedat Levi Alev and Shravas Rao,
  Rotating Walks, Derandomized Squares, and Permutation Conjugated Walks,
  submitted November 10, 2026, arXiv:2611.13693.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13693
  https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13693v1

The full primary HTML Sections 2.3, 3.2 and 3.3 were inspected. Their
equation (13), Lemma 3.2, and Theorem 3.4 give the S-transform and the
exact norm for products of freely independent regular-tree adjacency
operators, with random-permutation-model consequences. In particular,
for two equal-degree factors a,b of degree d,

  ||ab||^2 = (27/4) d(d-1).

Thus the candidate residual constant (3 sqrt(3)/2)sqrt(d(d-1)) is not a
new result here. Earlier Cohen--Maor (STOC 2023) rotating-walk work also
has closely related constants and finite-free-probability mechanisms.
The current source is especially close and must not be omitted.

For completeness, an elementary unequal-degree extension was derived
before this source was located. It is retained as a calculation, with
no novelty claim. For free regular-tree adjacency operators of degrees
d,e>=2, squared singular values of ab have inverse moment series

  I(w)=w(d+w)(e+w)/[d^2 e^2 (1+w)^3],

using the classical S-transform multiplication law and
S_(a^2)(w)=(d+w)/[d^2(1+w)]. The unique positive critical point solves

  (d+e-3)w^2+2(de-d-e)w-de=0.

Consequently the upper singular edge candidate is

  ||ab||^2=d^2 e^2(1+w)^3/[w(d+w)(e+w)].

For d=e this has w=d/(2d-3) and reduces to the known formula above.
The same positive-coefficient inverse-series/Pringsheim argument used
in the primary source establishes the spectral-edge interpretation;
the unequal-degree specialization has not received an independent
exact-source mathematical review in this worktree. It is a routine
extension candidate, not evidence of the requested seminal advance.

Two still-meaningful routes
---------------------------
1. Build a balanced sparse embedding for AMM from random permutations,
   then apply SG-11/14/16 to its actual Gram trace moments. A theorem
   requires counting the embedding's collision patterns; the exact mixed
   envelope alone does not imply an OSE or a new AMM runtime.
2. Use the actual finite-defect/clustered-attachment graph results in the
   snapshot together with matrix-function/resolvent update machinery.
   This requires a concrete spectral or arithmetic improvement and a
   comparison to existing low-rank graph perturbation methods.

No completion claim follows from this bounded audit. It prevents a
specific false novelty attribution and preserves the actual research gap.
