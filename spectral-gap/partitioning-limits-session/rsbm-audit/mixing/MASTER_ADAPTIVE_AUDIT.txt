1. PRIMARY MWW THEOREM AND CLEANNESS IMPLICATION
----------------------------------------------
Primary PDF actually opened and inspected:
McKay--Wormald--Wysocka, Short cycles in random regular graphs (2004),
https://users.monash.edu/~nwormald/papers/girth.pdf
Exact inspected portions: model convention and assumptions PDFpp1-2,
Theorem2 PDFp3, Equation(2.9) and its context PDFp5.

The model convention explicitly says uniform among labelled graphs in
the specified class. Thus no contiguity step is hidden in the use here.
Its standing condition is(d-1)^(2g-1)=o(N), with growing g allowed. Its
Theorem2 for forbidden-length setC={ell} gives exactly
  P(no ell-cycle)=exp[-2^ell/(2ell)+o(1)]
at d3,g=ell, including the o(1) IN THE EXPONENT. Both required limits
hold for ell=floor(loglogN). Thus existence follows with high probability.

The primary Equation(2.9) really bounds the probability that ANY cycle
whose length lies in C shares an edge with a different cycle of length
at most g, by O((d-1)^(2g-1)/N). With C={ell},g=ell this is precisely
the event ruled out by the manuscript, with no missing union factor.

For a chord, the shorter arc has length between2 andfloor(ell/2), and
adding the chord makes a different cycle of length<=floor(ell/2)+1.
For a repeated outside third neighbor, its two incident cycle vertices
are distinct; their shorter arc has length at least1. That arc plus
the two boundary edges is a simple different cycle, length at most
floor(ell/2)+2. Its outside vertex is not on the original cycle, and
the shorter arc contains an original cycle edge. For ell>=4 these
lengths are<=ell. Both obstructions therefore contradict the precise
edge-sharing event excluded by(2.9). Cubicity supplies exactly one
outside neighbor once chordlessness is established.

2. DETERMINISTIC MATCHING AND LOCALIZED EIGENVECTOR
-------------------------------------------------
Clean cycles X,Y have disjoint boundary sets U,W in their respective
blocks; each has exactly ell vertices. Every boundary vertex has exactly
one neighbor on its cycle, because the cycle-to-boundary map is injective
and onto. Thus the three displayed bijections use disjoint source sets
and disjoint target sets and exhaust both blocks, giving a genuine simple
perfect matching, not a fractional or weighted substitute.

The eigenvector calculation is exact, vertex by vertex:
* X vertices see two+1 entries internally and zeros at both other neighbors.
* U vertices see one+1 cycle neighbor and one-1 cross-matching neighbor.
* Y vertices similarly see two-1 entries; W entries cancel.
* Remaining vertices see neither cycle internally nor through their matching.
Consequently zeta=(1_X,-1_Y) has eigenvalue2 and support exactly2ell.
The hypothesis0<ell<N ensures zeros outside its support, so it is linearly
independent of sigma. The sign vector sigma-2zeta has equal block sizes;
its eigenvalue2 identity in a4-regular graph implies exactly3 same-side
and1 opposite-side neighbors. It is distinct from both sigma and-sigma.

No statement about eigenspace multiplicity was inferred merely from
approximate vectors. No inference from a second partition to a statistical
lower bound is needed; the final source expressly separates those claims.

3. DEFINITE SELECTION AND RUNNING TIME
------------------------------------
In a cubic graph all nonbacktracking walks of length ell can be generated
in at most3N2^(ell-1) branches. Testing a branch for a simple closed cycle,
a chord, and injective boundary is possible in O(ell) operations with
ordinary adjacency lists and temporary vertex marks. Choosing the first
passing branch in a fixed traversal order is a definite deterministic
choice of the required first cycle. Thus O(N ell2^ell) is valid and
polynomial, and the three matching bijections can be chosen in sorted
label order. The rare no-cycle event is explicitly assigned a fixed
fallback matching, so the function is defined on every input pair.

4. PAIRWISE INDEPENDENCE: ADVERSARIAL CONDITIONAL-LAW CHECK
---------------------------------------------------------
This is the nontrivial strengthening, and it survives the conditional
dependence check. Start with independent uniform labelled cubic C,D and
independent uniform permutations pi,rho. For any deterministic graph d'
and permutation r,
  P(D'=d',rho=r)=P(r D r^-1=d')/N!
                =1/(|G_N,3| N!),
so D' is exactly independent of rho, despite the deterministic relation
D'=rho D rho^-1. Likewise C' is independent of pi. Because the two input
pairs are independent, (C',D') is independent of(pi,rho) jointly.

For B independence from C, the safe conditioning is exactly that in the
master: condition on(C,pi,D'), NOT on(C,D,pi,D'). Then m'=F(C',D') is
fixed, and rho remains uniform and independent of everything conditioned
upon. For any fixed target bijection b there is exactly ONE permutation
rho satisfying rho^-1 m' pi=b. Hence
  P(m=b | C,pi,D')=1/N!,
and averaging proves P(m=b|C)=1/N!. This establishes full independence
of the random matching B from the entire labelled matrix C, not merely
independence of selected edge indicators.

For B independence from D, condition symmetrically on(D,rho,C'). Then
m' is fixed and pi is still independent uniform. Again exactly one pi
produces any prescribed m, yielding P(m=b|D)=1/N!.
The pair(C,D) was never resampled and remains independent. Thus the
THREE factors are truly pairwise independent with the required exact
marginals. They are generally not jointly independent.

For the high-probability failure assertion, (C',D') has the independent
uniform cubic law, so the deterministic function F fails to make an extra
eigenvector with probability o(1). Pulling its matching back by the block
permutations gives exactly m=rho^-1 m' pi, the same full graph with labels
changed within each block. The localized eigenvector, planted block sign
vector and second partition therefore pull back without changing support
size or any eigenvalue. No inappropriate conditioning on the success event
is used in proving any marginal or independence statement; fallback inputs
are symmetrized as well.

5. FINAL SCOPE
--------------
The two quantifier statements sup_fixedB P(failure)=o(1) and
P(exists B:failure)=1-o(1) are logically compatible. The corollary supplies
a concrete coupling showing that even exact pairwise independence does
not recover the joint independence needed by the positive proof. This
is a dependence-model boundary, not a contradiction of the independent
RSBM theorem. The second partition differs on2ell=o(N) memberships.
