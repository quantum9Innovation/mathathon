1. MAIN UNKNOWN-FRAME RESULT
---------------------------
The model requires independent uniform simple internal regular and cross
biregular factors, fixed positive balanced proportions, fixed bounds K,D,
and connected true type support. The symmetric omission certificate has
0<=q_ij<1/2 and physical row sum one. Weights need not be supplied.
This includes every common degree d>=3 and arbitrary fixed simple regular
base lifts. Minimum degree three alone is not asserted to suffice.

The structural proof handles nonempty candidate classes with no lower
size bound, unknown quotient and sizes, and zero omission weights. It
identifies the unique finest bounded-class partition with failure
O(n^(-(d_min-2))). It does not claim uniqueness among unbounded-class
partitions. A summary sentence was corrected: the mixed-frame example
with internal degrees at least two requires at least two true types.

The raw finite-field channel moment is uniformly bounded for every fixed
field, target dimension, and matrix target. The zero-weight proof retains
heterogeneous root/output spaces and selected cancellation-edge witnesses.
It does not replace a raw expectation by a high-probability statement or
transfer an unbounded moment through qualitative contiguity.

The complete RIGHT-kernel certificate uses a symmetric block reduction,
then projects and rank-reduces before enumeration. This charges p^h,
not p^(2h). Candidate generation and exact integer checks are charged on
every input before averaging. The listing algorithm is always correct
and almost surely terminates. Expected nearly quadratic bit work and
expected nearly linear workspace are distinct from worst-case bounds.
A capped algorithm returns abstention if interrupted, never an incomplete
maximum presented as a recovered plant.
