1. CLOSEST EXACT FORMULA: FRIEDMAN--KOHLER
----------------------------------------
Primary source:
J. Friedman and D.-E. Kohler, The Relativized Second Eigenvalue Conjecture
of Alon, arXiv:1403.3462v1,2014, Section2.4.2, Theorem2.4.7,
printedpp65-67 (PDFpp72-74).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.3462v1

The actual theorem statement, definitions of B-graphs and automorphisms,
and its falling-factorial/inclusion-exclusion proof were inspected from
the primary PDF and the retained complete primary-text extraction.

For a fixed feasible etale B-graph phi:psi->B of order
r=|E(psi)|-|V(psi)|, the theorem gives expected injections
N^(-r)+O(N^(-r-1)). If psi is pruned and every connected component has
positive order, its probability of at least one occurrence is
  N^(-r)/|Aut_B(phi)|+O(N^(-r-1)).

EXACT SPECIALIZATION TO THE TARGET MOTIF:
For a fixed unordered center-type pair{a,b} in K_k, let psi be the edge
ab together with two edges from each other color c to those two centers.
There is one vertex of EVERY base color, the two centers have degree d,
the other d-1 vertices degree2, and its order is d-2. Projection to K_k
is locally injective, so it is etale. It is connected and pruned, and
its order is positive for d>=3. A B-automorphism must fix every vertex,
because its base color is unique, so |Aut_B(phi)|=1, NOT2.
The theorem therefore gives
  P(this center-color motif occurs)
     =N^(-(d-2))+O(N^(-(d-1))).

The root note's own exact expectation uses no falling-factorial correction:
there are N^k assignments, each prescribing2d-1 distinct base-edge matching
values, so E X_ab=N^(k-(2d-1))=N^(-(d-2)) EXACTLY. Summing the binom(k,2)
center-color choices gives the displayed a_d. Intersections of two distinct
feasible motifs have union order at least2(d-2), by the root's short overlap
argument. Thus the leading union coefficient is obtainable by the classical
fixed-subgraph calculus plus this elementary finite overlap check.

The generic FK remainder O(N^(-(d-1))) for one motif is weaker than the
root note's special exact-expectation/second-factorial-moment remainder
O(N^(-2(d-2))) when d>=4. That strengthens a remainder, but does not turn
the leading motif count into a new counting method.

LIMIT OF SUBSUMPTION: FK counts the occurrence of a SPECIFIED bounded
subgraph, not the existence of an arbitrary other equitable partition,
and it does not count hidden cover partitions Z or evaluate their posterior.
Its eigenvalue-outlier/tangle theorems do not automatically replace the
root's all-partition upper bound. The extra twin vector has eigenvalue-1,
inside the usual new-spectrum bulk; an outlier theorem is not enough.

4. OTHER CLOSE LIFT FORMULAS: WHAT THEY ACTUALLY COUNT
----------------------------------------------------
S. Silas, Randomly Generated Subgroups of the Symmetric Group and Random
Lifts of Graphs, arXiv:1607.01330v2, Theorem4.1 and Section4.1.1.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.01330v2
Actual primary theorem/proof inspected. For connected base of Betti number
l, the probability of a connected lift is
  1-N^(-(l-1))+O(N^(-l)).
For K_k, l-1=binom(k,2)-k=k(d-2)/2. Thus the connected-conditioning
observation in the root note is within established sharper lift probability
theory. It is a different, smaller-order event, not the claimed recovery
constant at exponent d-2.

complete graphs, arXiv:2109.13347v1(2021), introductory scope and
Theorems1.1/1.2 inspected.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.13347
This is ordinary proper coloring, with first/second moments and small
subgraph conditioning. Ordinary colorings do NOT force one neighbor of
every other color and do not represent alternative cover partitions.
The paper explicitly distinguishes the random-lift model from uniform
regular graphs. It does not provide the sharp planted-cover ambiguity
or Bayes coefficient under discussion.

5. THE POSTERIOR HALF-FACTOR: HOW TO ATTRIBUTE IT
-----------------------------------------------
Under the root's stated uniform hidden-fiber prior, every compatible
ordered K_k-cover has the same likelihood. Quotienting color names gives
the same k! multiplicity for every compatible unordered partition. Thus
the posterior is uniform and conditional exact-recovery error is1-1/Z.
This is an elementary finite Bayesian counting identity, not a new
statistical method or a special new lower-bound principle.

The nontrivial model-specific assertion behind coefficient a_d/2 is that,
at the ENTIRE leading ambiguity scale, Z=2 rather than merely Z>=2;
events Z>=3 and all nonlocal alternative partitions have lower order.
A twin-count first moment or an asymmetry theorem alone does not establish
that assertion. The root note supplies a separate argument for it. No
primary exact formula found here already establishes this assertion for
this hidden complete-base lift ensemble.
