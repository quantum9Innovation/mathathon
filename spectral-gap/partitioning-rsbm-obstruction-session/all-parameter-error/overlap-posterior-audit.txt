1. MINIMAL FLIPS IN EVERY PARAMETER REGIME
-----------------------------------------
Every equitable sign vector tau satisfies A tau=delta tau and is
balanced, since A1=D1 and D-delta=2b>0. Relative to the plant sigma,
w=(sigma-tau)/2 is +1 on S_+, -1 on S_-, and zero elsewhere. The two
support sets have equal size k. Global exchange permits k<=N/2.

For delta>=0, the old inequality delta<=k-1 gives k>=t=delta+1.
At equality, both support sets are K_t cliques with no cross edge.
For delta=0 this means singletons with no edge, not a nontrivial clique.
Since D>=3 and a=b in this case, a=b>=2. Each center has a=b internal
boundary neighbors and b cross boundary neighbors, and the leading
motif is exactly K_(2,2a). It is connected with minimum degree two.
The formula reduces to nu=2a-2 and C=(a!)^2, as proposed.

For delta=-s<0, at a positive support vertex

 -s=internal_support_neighbors-cross_support_neighbors >= -k.

Thus k>=s=b-a. At equality, the internal count is zero and the cross
count is exactly s at EVERY center. The core is K_(s,s), with no
same-side support edge. Each center has a residual internal boundary
edges and b-s=a residual cross boundary edges.

At every exterior affected vertex, the zero equation requires equal
numbers m_v of internal and cross support neighbors. The sum on each
old side is as, so each exterior W set has size at most as. The leading
case has exactly as vertices per side and every m_v=1. This supplies
all leading patterns; it is not restricted to matching same-index
center vertices. All four maps W->S have exactly a preimages per
specified center.

At a=0, s=b>=3, there are NO exterior vertices. K_(b,b) consumes every
stub at all its vertices, so it is exactly an isolated component.
No assertion about absent internal factors is obtained by substituting
zero into a positive-degree denominator.

2. NEW MINIMUM MOTIFS ARE CONNECTED AND HAVE IDENTIFIABLE CENTERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
For a<b and a>0, the K_(s,s) core is connected even when s=1; every W
has one internal and one cross center neighbor and joins the two core
sides. Centers have motif degree D, and W have motif degree two.
For a=0, the core K_(b,b) itself is connected and every vertex is a
center of degree D=b>=3. For delta=0 the two centers are connected
through the nonempty exterior set and again have degree D>2, while
W have degree two.

Thus ALL leading motifs throughout the proposed parameter range are
connected with minimum degree at least two, and their degree-D vertices
are EXACTLY the centers. The latter statement still identifies the
entire support when a=0 and W is empty. This fact is sufficient to
remove image-identical pairs from the factorial second moment.

3. EXACT COEFFICIENT CHECK FOR a<b, INCLUDING a=0
------------------------------------------------
Choose the two center sets of size s, and the exterior sets of size as.
For fixed labeled sets, each balanced boundary function has
(as)!/(a!)^s choices. There are four functions, so the exact data count is

 A_N=binom(N,s)^2 binom(N-s,as)^2 [(as)!/(a!)^s]^4.

Each realized leading flip determines those data uniquely. Conversely
the data saturate every center and make the two support contributions
cancel at each exterior vertex. Extra edges away from centers do not
matter. There is no automorphism, orientation, or additional s! factor.
For N>2s each alternative unordered partition has a unique minimum
orientation relative to the plant.

The motif has

 v=2s+2as,   e=s^2+4as,
 e-v=s(s+2a-2)=s(D-2)=nu.

For a>0, physical stub assignments number
(a!b!)^(2s)(ab)^(2as). The internal prescribed edge count is as in
EACH factor; the cross count is s^2+2as. Different assignments are
disjoint because every prescribed edge has a saturated center endpoint.
The leading denominator powers are a^(2as) and b^(s^2+2as), so the
W-stub numerator cancels both boundary-degree powers. With the simple-
completion ratio tending to one, a fixed data event has probability

 (1+o(1))(a!b!)^(2s)b^(-s^2) N^(-e).

Multiplication by A_N yields exactly

 EX ~ [((as)!)^2(b!)^(2s) /
        ((s!)^2(a!)^(2s)b^(s^2))] N^(-nu).

At a=0, derive the probability separately: A_N=binom(N,b)^2, the only
factor is the cross bijection, physical assignments number (b!)^(2b),
and the prescribed probability is (b!)^(2b)/(bN)_(b^2), before the
residual-simple ratio. This gives directly

 C=(b!)^(2b-2)/b^(b^2),   nu=b(b-2).

No 0^0 convention is needed. Hand checks give (1,2): C=2,nu=1;
(1,3): C=16,nu=4; and (2,2): C=4,nu=2.

Completion detail to preserve in the full proof: at a=0 the internal
factors contribute probability ONE. The old expression
exp[-(a^2-1)/2-(b-1)^2/2] must not be substituted literally at a=0.
The correct joint limit is

 exp[-1_{a>=1}(a^2-1)/2-(b-1)^2/2].

At a=1 the internal factors and their residual factors are matchings,
and hence are automatically simple. In the disassortative minimum
motif, internal residual totals are aN-2as and cross residual totals
bN-(s^2+2as). Prescribed edges always touch a residual-zero center.
The residual degree sequences differ at O(1) vertices, and all their
bounded-degree defect means retain the original limits. Hence the
same positive limit is obtained for original and residual factors,
uniformly over the finite boundary topologies, proving the ratio 1+o(1).
At a=0 this is simply the residual b-regular cross model on N-b active
vertices per side. At a=1 the internal residual total remains even.

4. BOUNDARY COLLISIONS AND NONMINIMAL SUPPORTS
---------------------------------------------
For a<b a nonleading minimum flip loses at least one exterior vertex:
|W_+|+|W_-|<=2as-1. The number of prescribed edges remains EXACTLY e;
centers still use all a internal and b cross stubs. For fixed a,b there
are finitely many valid simple typed patterns, each with at most v-1
vertices and e edges. Prescribed-stub probability and vertex placement
therefore give O(N^(-nu-1)). Invalid patterns contribute zero.
At a=0 the exterior is empty, so no such collision exists. More strongly,
collisions also cannot occur when a=1 or s=1, since m_v<=min(a,s).
At delta=0,t=1 they are likewise impossible. These refinements are not
needed for the asserted upper bound.

The general incident-support exposure proof does not require positive
delta or a>=2. At an exterior zero coordinate, a unique incident support
edge cannot cancel for ANY adjacency eigenvalue. For support size h,
with e_in internal support edges and B boundary edges, all incident
exposures contain at least e_in+B/2=Dh/2 collisions. At h<=epsilon N,
each ACTIVE typed pool retains Theta(N) stubs and at most O_D(h) targets
lie in the support or previously touched exterior vertices. If a=0, the
internal pool is simply absent; there is no division by a. If a=1 it is
an ordinary pairing pool on N vertices. The same conditional collision
bound C_D h/N, and hence

 Pr(exists such support size h)
 <=[A_D(h/N)^((D-2)/2)]^h,

hold throughout these cases. The sum starting at h=2(k_min+1), where
k_min=t for delta>=0 and s for delta<0, is

 O(N^(-(D-2)(k_min+1)))=O(N^(-nu-(D-2)))
                         =O(N^(-nu-1)).

5. THE MACROSCOPIC GAP REALLY COVERS a=0,1 AND delta=0
-------------------------------------------------------
The actual all-slot one-hot source was inspected. It symmetrizes only
within old neighbor types and averages the entropy identity over ALL D
physical slots. It therefore never assumes an internal slot exists.
The normalized entropy loss is exactly

 L=((D-2)/D) sum_(i<=j) w_ij I_ij
           +(1/D) sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it.

The mutual-information coefficient is positive because D>=3, and the
internal i=i contribution has its required factor one-half. Omitted
one-hot neighbor values are determined by the root count-vector and all
retained slots. The auxiliary law independently samples retained slots
from their old neighbor root marginals. Averaging the variance comparison
over all omissions gives (D-2) sum_s V_s, so it works with zero or one
internal slots and without distinct rows of the candidate frame.

The source proves an explicit negative gap c_D*defect^2, not just a
zero classification. For two equal old blocks and a candidate with flip
fraction r<=1/2 on both sides, defect is exactly r. Use the candidate
two-class quotient R=[[a,b],[b,a]]. Integer one-hot legality is equivalent
to exactly a same-sign and b opposite-sign neighbors. A point map sending
both old classes to the same candidate label is not legal when b>0.
Thus the only point maps are the two planted orientations, including
a=b where R has equal rows, and a=0 where all slots are cross slots.

The exact all-slot configuration count in the second source includes
root-label multinomials and gives only a polynomial remainder in N for
fixed degrees and alphabet. Hence the macroscopic flip interval has
exponentially small probability in all cases needed here. No application
of the old a>=2 internal-delete variance argument is being smuggled into
the new a=0,1 regimes.

6. FACTORIAL SECOND MOMENT, INCLUDING nu=1
-----------------------------------------
Use the elementary lemma: for connected H of minimum degree at least two,
every proper subgraph F with a nonempty vertex set has

 e(F)-v(F)<=e(H)-v(H)-1.

If spanning, an edge was removed. Otherwise remove r>0 vertices with
B>=1 edges to the retained set and E_R internal removed-set edges.
Minimum degree gives 2E_R+B>=2r, hence E_R+B>r and at least r+1 edges
are removed. Isolated common vertices must be retained in v(F).

Every new leading motif meets these hypotheses. Two identical motif
images have exactly the same degree-D center set; planted vertex types
then identify S_+,S_- separately. Their boundary data are determined by
the realized incident edges. Thus distinct flip sets cannot yield an
identical motif image. Different boundary data for the same support
cannot co-occur because center stubs are saturated.

For each fixed proper overlap, the union J has

 e(J)-v(J)=2nu-[e(F)-v(F)]>=nu+1.

The finite typed union count is O(N^(v(J))) times O(N^(-e(J))); impossible
typed degrees contribute zero. Disjoint images contribute O(N^(-2nu)).
Crucially the new range has nu>=1, so 2nu>=nu+1 remains true. Equality
occurs only at the smallest case nu=1, namely (a,b)=(1,2), and is still
sufficient. Thus

 E[X(X-1)]=O(N^(-nu-1)).

At a=0, there is an even stronger check: distinct isolated K_(b,b)
components cannot partially overlap. One common vertex would force its
full opposite neighbor set to agree, and saturation then forces the
whole component to agree. Distinct leading components are disjoint.

At (1,2), the leading motif is K_4 missing the edge between the two W
vertices: an adjacent twin pair. Adding that last edge produces an
isolated K_4 with three valid unordered bisections. This has excess two,
exactly nu+1, and therefore is correctly included in the lower-order
multiple-partition event, not a leading-order counterexample.

The factorial inequalities give Pr(X>=1)=EX+O(N^(-nu-1)) and
Pr(X>=2)=O(N^(-nu-1)). Outside nonminimal/collision events, K=1+X.
Hence ambiguity has the stated first-order coefficient and
Pr(K>=3)=O(N^(-nu-1)) in all the new cases.

7. UNIFORM POSTERIOR AND THE BAYES FACTOR ONE-HALF
-------------------------------------------------
For fixed balanced plant, every compatible graph has likelihood
1/[G_a(N)^2 B_b(N)]. This remains valid at a=0, with G_0(N)=1, and at
a=1, with the internal perfect-matching count. Uniform hidden balanced
plants therefore give an exactly uniform posterior on the valid ordered
signs, and exactly two ordered signs per valid unordered partition.
Thus the posterior on K(G) unordered partitions is uniform and
R_N=E[1-1/K(G)].

Ambiguity is invariant under relabeling, so averaging over the hidden
plant does not change its fixed-plant probability. There is no additional
division by two for an ambiguous graph having two possible plants.

The K=2 event gives error exactly one-half, while

 0<=R_N-(1/2)Pr(K>=2)<=(1/2)Pr(K>=3)
                             =O(N^(-nu-1)).

This proves C/2 as the leading Bayes constant. At a=0 each connected
b-regular component is balanced between the old sides. Flipping one
minimal isolated component while the rest is connected yields exactly
two unordered valid partitions. Multiple components produce more
partitions but have already been controlled by the same bounds.

8. OPTIONAL POLYNOMIAL ESTIMATOR: SOURCE SCOPE
---------------------------------------------
The actual all-slot intertwiner moment source allows any matrix R over
any fixed prime field and internal degree zero or one. For scalar
R=delta it supplies E|ker_F3(A-delta I)|=O_D(N^(B_D)) for some explicit
fixed-degree exponent B_D. It does NOT state the degree-independent
exponent 50 in this source. The old aggregate-fiber note explicitly
assumes a>=2; an extension using the all-slot entropy would be a new
deduction if a uniform N^50 exponent is desired for a=0,1.

No such strengthening is necessary for the statistical conclusion.
Use a cap N^(B_D+nu+2). Enumerate the F_3 kernel, reject zero coordinates,
lift 1 to +1 and 2 to -1, verify the original integer equation, and choose
any verified valid unordered partition. The cap event has probability
O(N^(-nu-2)), negligible at the leading ambiguity scale. Completed runs
are conditionally Bayes-optimal because every posterior mass is 1/K.
The cap makes worst-case time a fixed polynomial; a ternary odometer
gives O(N) amortized update and fixed-degree verification cost per vector.
Thus a correctly stated all-parameter polynomial estimator can attain
the same leading Bayes constant using the available source.

LIMITS
------
These arguments concern fixed a>=0,b>=1,D>=3 and the actual independent
uniform-simple internal/random-cross model, all admissible N, and a
uniform hidden balanced prior. They do not assert a matching asymptotic
for arbitrary extra real eigenvectors, adversarial prescribed cross
graphs, noisy data, or growing degrees. The D>=3 restriction is essential
in the all-slot entropy and variance coefficients; degree-two cycles are
not covered.
