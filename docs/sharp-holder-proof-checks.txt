RESULT
  Fix 0<alpha<1, beta=1-alpha. The optimal POWER constant is
    H_s(F)=sup tr|F(B)-F(A)|^s / tr|B-A|^(alpha*s).
  For positive endpoints and F(A)=A^alpha,
    lim s^2 H_s(F)=1/(2*pi^2*alpha*beta).
  For Hermitian |A|^alpha and sign(A)|A|^alpha, and for rectangular
  right/left absolute powers and the polar map X|X|^(alpha-1),
    lim s^2 H_s(F)=2/(pi^2*alpha*beta).
  These are power constants; the ordinary norm constant is H_s^(1/s).
  The factor four must not be misreported as an ordinary norm ratio.
  Polar and odd-Hermitian power constants agree at every fixed s>0.

  At error epsilon*||B-A||^alpha, epsilon=exp(-L), the optimal worst-case
  approximation-rank coefficient is half the corresponding power-limit
  coefficient, multiplied by each FIXED perturbation rank r. Dimensions
  are unrestricted and the extremizing input may depend on L or s.
