CARE branch certificate and raw-factor solver bridge -- 2026-11-14
================================================================
Dedicated worker note. Only this file is edited by this worker.

STATUS
The raw-factor CARE solver is still missing. This note proves a global
threshold wrapper for an approximate PSD Riccati solution. The wrapper
does NOT assume that the positive index of H is already at most r.
It therefore closes a logical gate in the proposed threshold algorithm,
but does not establish its requested arithmetic complexity.

1. GLOBAL RESIDUAL / RANK DICHOTOMY
---------------------------------
Let H=H^T be m-by-m, ||H||<=1, 1<=r<m, and s=r+1. Let 0<e<=1.
Choose a forcing matrix F, a known bound L>=||F||, and alpha>0 with

  alpha L<=e,      E=alpha^2 F F^T.

For a PSD approximate solution X, let

  R=H X+X H-X^2+E,          ||R||<=eta.                  (1)

Suppose the following two capture conditions hold for some b>0:

  (C1) If lambda_s(H)>=16e, and U_s comprises fixed top-s
       orthonormal eigenvectors of H, then
       sigma_min(U_s^T F)>=b.

  (C2) If lambda_s(H)<16e, let U comprise ALL H eigenvectors
       with eigenvalues >16e (at most r vectors). If U is nonempty,
       sigma_min(U^T F)>=b.

Assume also 0<b<=L. These are only conditions on fixed H spectral subspaces, not on an
iterate-dependent subspace. Assume

  eta <= alpha^2 e b^2 /100.                             (2)

Since b<=L whenever its capture condition is used, (2) also implies
eta<=e^3/100. The following dichotomy is valid without ANY assumption
on n_+(H):

  A. If X has at least s eigenvalues >4e, then
     lambda_s(H)>e.

  B. Otherwise, let P project onto the X eigenvectors with
     eigenvalues >4e. Then rank(P)<=r and

       (I-P) H (I-P) <=35e (I-P).                        (3)

In case B one additionally has ||X-2H_+||<=66e.
The constants are deliberately loose. This is an absolute-error
threshold oracle with a constant gray interval. A physical pencil
transfer must set e to the desired theta-scaled budget in its metric.

The only computational inputs to this wrapper are a low-rank PSD X and
its certified Riccati residual. No stability test for H-X, no independent
inertia computation, and no prior positive-index promise is needed.

2. PROOF: NEGATIVE BRANCHES ARE ALMOST UNFORCED H-INVARIANT SPACES
----------------------------------------------------------------
Put S=X-H and K=H^2+E, so R=K-S^2. A top X eigenvector gives

  ||X|| <=1+sqrt(1+e^2+eta)<3,     ||S||<4.               (4)

Let V have orthonormal columns spanning any S spectral subspace below
-t, so S V=-V T, tI<T<4I. Write X_V=V^T X V>=0. Compressing (1),

  V^T R V=V^T X^2 V+X_V T+T X_V+V^T E V.               (5)

The inverse of Y->TY+YT preserves PSD order by its integral formula.
Consequently

  0<=X_V<=eta/(2t) I,
  ||XV||^2<=3eta/(2t),
  ||F^T V||^2 <=(eta+4eta/t)/alpha^2.                  (6)

For t=e<=1 the last expression is <=5eta/(alpha^2 e);
using 7 instead of 5 below leaves additional slack. Also

  H V=V T+X V.                                         (7)

Thus every substantially negative branch of S is almost H-invariant,
with a positive compressed H spectrum, and it sees almost no forcing.

3. PROOF: A DIMENSION COUNT HANDLES TOO MANY POSITIVE MODES
---------------------------------------------------------
Use t=e in (6), and put W=V^perp. Pinch H across V,W:

  H'=P_V H P_V+P_W H P_W.

The off-diagonal block in (7) is P_W X V. A symmetric matrix with
off-diagonal blocks C,C^T has norm ||C||, so

  ||H-H'||<=d:=sqrt(3eta/(2e)).                          (8)

Suppose lambda_s(H)>=16e. Let Z be the H' spectral subspace above 8e.
Sylvester separation of the H' block <=8e from H U_s>=16e U_s gives

  ||(I-P_Z)U_s||<=d/(8e).

No gap within either spectral block is used. The inverse Sylvester norm
bound follows from the semigroup integral, with separation 8e. Therefore

  sigma_s(P_Z F)>=b-dL/(8e).                            (9)

Because H' is block diagonal across V,W, Z splits as Z_V plus Z_W.
If dim(Z_W)<s, the matrix P_(Z_W)F has rank<s. Singular-value
perturbation and (6) would then give

  sigma_s(P_Z F)<=||P_(Z_V)F||<=||F^T V||
                  <=sqrt(7eta/(alpha^2 e)).             (10)

But (2) and alpha L<=e imply

  dL/(8e)<=sqrt(3/200)b/8 <b/50,
  sqrt(7eta/(alpha^2 e))<=sqrt(7/100)b <0.27b,

contradicting (9). Thus dim(Z_W)>=s. Since S_W>=-e I,

  X_W=H'_W+S_W>=H'_W-eI,

and hence lambda_s(X)>=7e. We have proved the useful count implication

  lambda_s(X)<=4e   ==>   lambda_s(H)<16e.              (11)

This is the missing argument when n_+(H)>r. Injectivity on the ENTIRE
positive space is unnecessary: a rank/singular-value argument uses only
a fixed s-dimensional strong head.

4. PROOF: THE SAFE CASE IGNORES SMALL POSITIVE BULK
------------------------------------------------
Suppose lambda_s(X)<=4e, so (11) holds. Let U span all H eigenvectors
>16e, of dimension <=r. The remaining H block is <=16e I.
If U is empty, H<=16e I and X>=0 already imply S>=-16e I.
If U is nonempty, suppose S has a nonempty spectral subspace V below
-32e. Necessarily 32e<4 by (4). Use t=32e in (6) and (7).
Sylvester separation of H_(Uperp)<=16e I from T>=32e I yields

  d_0:=||(I-UU^T)V||
        <=sqrt(3eta/(64e))/(16e)
        <=sqrt(3)b/(1280L)<b/(500L).                    (12)

Thus for every unit v in V,

  ||F^T v||>=b sqrt(1-d_0^2)-Ld_0>b/2.                 (13)

Since t<4, (6) gives

  ||F^TV||^2 <=8eta/(alpha^2 t)
              =eta/(4alpha^2 e)<=b^2/400,             (14)

a contradiction. We conclude S>=-32e I in both cases.

Let S_+ be the positive part of S. Then

  ||S-S_+||<=32e,
  ||S_+^2-K||<=1024e^2+eta.

For PSD matrices A,B, the square-root inequality
||sqrt(A)-sqrt(B)||<=sqrt(||A-B||) follows by Loewner sandwiching
and the scalar inequality sqrt(x+d)-sqrt(x)<=sqrt(d). It gives

  ||S-sqrt(K)||<=32e+sqrt(1024e^2+eta)<65e.

Also 0<=sqrt(H^2+E)-|H|<=alpha L I<=e I, so

  ||X-2H_+||<=66e.                                      (15)

Compressing 2H_+<=X+66e I to the complement of the X eigenvectors
>4e proves (3), since H<=H_+.

Finally, the other output is sound for EVERY PSD X with the residual
bound, without either capture condition:

  S<=|S|=sqrt(H^2+E-R)<=|H|+sqrt(e^2+eta) I,
  X<=2H_++sqrt(e^2+eta) I<2H_++2e I.                    (16)

Therefore lambda_s(X)>4e implies lambda_s(H)>e, proving case A.

5. GAUSSIAN FORCING AND H-SANDWICHED FORCING
-------------------------------------------
Let Omega be m-by-ell standard Gaussian, with
ell=C(r+1+log(1/delta)). For fixed H, its top-(r+1) eigenspace is
fixed. A standard Gaussian smallest-singular-value bound gives

  sigma_min(U_(r+1)^T Omega)>=b_0=sqrt(ell)/2

with high probability for sufficiently large absolute C. Every prefix
of at most r+1 rows inherits the same lower bound. One can use the
elementary high-probability Frobenius bound

  ||Omega||<=L_0=sqrt(2m ell/delta)

and absorb the two failures into delta. This loose bound suffices;
all required residual accuracies remain inverse-polynomial.

Plain forcing. Take F=Omega, L=L_0, b=b_0, and any alpha L_0<=e.
Both capture conditions hold on the same fixed-head Gaussian event.
The required residual is eta<=alpha^2 e b_0^2/100. At alpha=e/L_0
this is the old e^3 b_0^2/(100L_0^2) budget, now WITHOUT the old
n_+(H)<=r promise.

Weighted forcing. Take F=H Omega, L=L_0, any alpha L_0<=e, and

  eta<=alpha^2 e^3 b_0^2/100.                            (17)

Whenever C1 is needed, lambda_s(H)>=16e implies its capture bound
with b=16e b_0. Whenever C2 is needed, every U eigenvalue is >16e,
so the same capture bound holds. Set b=min{16e b_0,L_0}; (17) is
stronger than requirement (2) with that b, since e b_0<=L_0.
Thus the exact same dichotomy holds.

This weighted version is compatible with the H-sandwiched forcing
used for the conditioning of the RADI graph. In particular it allows

  alpha^2=min{e^2/L_0^2, b_0^2/(4L_0^4)}

when the graph-conditioning proof requests that smaller alpha. At
alpha=e/L_0, (17) becomes eta<=e^5 b_0^2/(100L_0^2). The two extra
powers of e cost only logarithmic arithmetic precision / convergence
factors if a genuinely logarithmic-step solver exists. They do not
establish that solver's raw shifted-inverse costs.

6. COMPUTATION THAT IS AND IS NOT CHARGED
----------------------------------------
For X=YY^T with k columns and E=alpha^2 FF^T, write

  Z=[Y,HY,F],
  J_R=[[-Y^TY,I,0],[I,0,0],[0,0,alpha^2 I]],
  R=Z J_R Z^T.

One H-action on k right hand sides plus thin QR and a (2k+ell)-core
eigenvalue computation certifies ||R||. The P space in the dichotomy
comes from the k-core Y^TY, followed by lifting. If a block H-action
costs T_H(k), ordinary arithmetic charges

  T_H(k)+O(m(k+ell)^2+(k+ell)^3).

Weighted forcing also requires the initial H Omega action. None of
these are free inverse calls. In the actual matrix-product reduction,
one must instantiate T_H with all applications of true raw factors
and any needed accurately preconditioned Gram solves counted.

The wrapper itself neither forms the full raw Gram nor reads new
tall factors beyond those actions. It can adaptively certify a failed
threshold (too many positive modes) or return an r-dimensional safe
space even though the original branch lemma only covered q<=r.

The unresolved gate is STILL producing this PSD Y Y^T to the certified
residual using cheap raw H actions / shifted solves. No bound
O~(Ir+m^omega), O~(Ir+m^3), or general quadratic matrix-product bound
has been established by this note. No novelty claim has been audited.

7. EXACT TRANSFER TO THE ORIGINAL RAW PRODUCT
-------------------------------------------
The safe-space certificate has a direct physical interpretation. First
assume A has full column rank, and put

  G=A^T A,    Q=B B^T,    H_0=Q-tG^(-1).

Suppose a coefficient subspace P (orthonormal columns, rank<=r) obeys

  (I-PP^T)H_0(I-PP^T)<=d(I-PP^T).                       (18)

Set U=orth(A P), and return

  X_out=U,      Y_out=B^T A^T U,      C=U Y_out^T.

Then rank(C)<=r and

  ||AB-C||^2 <=t+d||G||.                                (19)

Proof: write any u in col(A) as u=A G^(-1)x, so x=A^T u and
||u||^2=x^T G^(-1)x. If u is orthogonal to AP, then P^T x=0.
Equation (18) gives

  ||B^T A^T u||^2=x^T Qx
     <=t x^T G^(-1)x+d||x||^2
     <=(t+d||G||)||u||^2.

The component outside col(A) contributes zero to B^T A^T. Taking the
supremum on U^perp proves (19). No approximate-eigenvector perturbation
or spectral gap is used in this transfer.

If the global wrapper was applied to H=H_0/c with ||H||<=1, its safe
output has d=35ce. Thus the physical error bound is

  ||AB-C|| <=sqrt(t+35ce||G||).                           (20)

Choosing e to fit the desired physical squared-norm slack is necessary;
the normalized e parameter is not automatically equal to theta.

Rank deficiency can be removed from the coefficient space without a
true raw Gram. A coarse embedding of A has exactly the same nullspace
on its successful subspace-embedding event. Factor its sketched Gram as
L^T L, with L of full row rank rho<=m, and define the explicit small
right inverse R_L=L^T(LL^T)^(-1). Work with

  Abar=A R_L,          Bbar=L B.

Since R_L L projects onto row(A), Abar Bbar=AB. The nonzero-column
matrix Abar has rho independent columns, and its true Gram satisfies

  (1-eta_0)I <=Abar^T Abar <=(1+eta_0)I

when the original coarse event is
(1-eta_0)L^T L<=A^TA<=(1+eta_0)L^T L. Neither Abar nor Bbar needs
to be formed: all block actions use raw A,B plus explicit m-cores.
The factor ||G|| in (19) is then at most 1+eta_0.

In these coordinates a true Gbar^(-1) action is genuinely cheap:
constant-conditioned Richardson with identity preconditioner uses
O(log(1/xi)) raw Gbar actions for absolute operator-action error xi.
A Gbar action on k columns costs O(Ik+m^2k) conventionally. Qbar
actions also use only B, B^T and small core products. Thus approximate
H_0 actions to the residual certification accuracy cost

  O~(Ik log(1/xi)+m^3)

after the coarse preparation, with the explicit thin-QR/core residual
terms of Section 6 included if k+ell is not bounded by m. Inner errors
must be budgeted into eta; taking them inverse-polynomially smaller
than eta only adds logarithmic factors. This does NOT make H_0 shifted
inverses cheap: those have the threshold cancellation missing from the
constant-conditioned Gbar inverse.

A known upper bound c>=||H_0|| can also be obtained without forming a
true Gram. Sketch B^T with constant distortion, transfer that small
sketched Gram by L, compute its norm in the explicit m-core, and combine
the resulting Qbar norm bound with t/(1-eta_0). All work is
O~(I+m^3) conventionally. Any dependence of a desired relative guarantee
on c/t must be retained when setting e and logarithmic accuracy factors;
this note does not suppress arbitrary spectral dynamic range.

Finally, lifting AP, orthogonalizing its <=r columns, forming Y_out,
and expanding C cost

  O(Ir+(n+p)r^2+r^3+npr).

This makes the CERTIFICATION-AND-OUTPUT route concrete for arbitrary raw
factors once a low-rank PSD approximate CARE solution is supplied. The
construction of that solution remains the unresolved step.

8. CHECKED BRIDGES THAT DO NOT YET IMPLEMENT THE SOLVER
-----------------------------------------------------
Scalar stable drift / descriptor feedback. Moving all threshold
dependence into the quadratic CARE coefficient is not sufficient.
For example, with drift -aI and forcing FF^T, the differential CARE is

  X'=-2aX-X K X+FF^T,       X(0)=0.

If P projects onto span(F), then X=PXP implies X'=PX'P. Hence its
zero-initialized PSD trajectory, whenever defined, remains in the
forcing span. The same invariant holds for the elementary low-rank
Riccati updates: scalar drift plus a feedback update with range in P
maps P into itself. Such a formulation cannot discover a general H
positive eigenspace through a fixed narrow raw forcing span. It would
need a different branch mechanism, an already informative forcing
transform, or another operation not supplied by this invariant.
This is not a no-go theorem for all descriptor/KYP formulations.

Zero-shift cancellation. Weighted forcing gives the exact identity
H^(-1)(H Omega)=Omega when H is nonsingular. But RADI's update at an
exact zero shift has a vanishing real-shift prefactor. Taking shifted
derivatives / confluent steps immediately calls for H^(-1)Omega.
The single cancellation does not yield an iterated raw solver.

Bounded right-hand-side transfer. The operator
(H-zeta I)^(-1)H is bounded when zeta=-a+ia and H is Hermitian, but
the corresponding raw pole is still close to the original spectral
threshold. Already for the simpler physical threshold H=S-gamma I,
the raw shifted solve is

  S-(gamma-zeta)I,

and the off-axis angle in the raw-factor theorem is

  c=|Im zeta|/|gamma-zeta|=Theta(a/gamma)  (a<<gamma).

A fixed coarse Gram with distortion eta_0 only gives a Richardson
contraction bound eta_0/c, which does not contract at these critical
shifts. Weighted forcing does not change that preconditioner identity.
One must prove an additional cancellation-aware iteration or a new
preconditioner; bounded exact output alone does not supply it.

9. REMAINING LOGICAL / COMPLEXITY OBLIGATIONS
-------------------------------------------
The proved global wrapper applies whenever the low residual has been
attained, including q>r. A solver theorem stated only for q<=r is still
insufficient to supply a bounded-time global threshold routine: an
infeasible threshold can have many positive modes, and a solver could
grow to large rank before meeting the residual target.

Exact monotone RADI iterates X_j<=X_E do permit sound early rejection:
X_E<=2H_++alpha L I means sufficiently many large X_j eigenvalues
already certify sufficiently many positive H eigenvalues. What is not
proved here is a uniform polylogarithmic time to either that rejection
or the required residual for arbitrary positive index. A random
threshold dither can remove extremely near-zero eigenvalues, but its
combination with a bounded-rank early-stopping convergence theorem
requires a separate proof.

More fundamentally, even the positive-index-promised RADI solve needs
the critical shifted-H inverse discussed in Section 8. The raw off-axis
batched theorem does not currently implement it. Consequently this
work establishes a rigorous global certificate and physical output
transfer, not the proposed near-input-plus-core construction or a
general N^2 algorithm. No formal external job was started or stopped.
