2. THE GENIE EXPERIMENT IS AN ORDINARY HIDDEN RANDOM-LIFT MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------
Fix labeled left fibers L_1,...,L_r of size N and a labeled right
vertex set V of size bN. Choose the ordered balanced right partition
uniformly, and every one of the rb edge matchings independently
uniformly. This is a random N-lift of the named K_(r,b) base with
the right fibers hidden. Every positive N is admissible; all factors
are cross perfect matchings, and the ambient graph is automatically
simple because different base edges join distinct old-type pairs.

The estimator gets the full base, parameters, and all left fibers.
Its only target is the unordered partition of V. This is easier than
blind full-fiber inference. Since b>r, the left and right degree
classes are observable already; giving the individual left fibers
really is additional genie information, not concealing degree data.

For a standard fully relabeled hidden-lift experiment, conditioning
on any revealed left fibers gives the same count and bound below.
Averaging over their positions therefore cannot circumvent the
genie obstruction. A successful full-fiber estimator would in
particular recover the right unordered fibers in this easier
experiment.

3. EXACT MARKED AND OBSERVATION COUNTS
-------------------------------------
For a fixed ordered right partition, there are (N!)^(rb) choices of
the independent matchings. Distinct choices give distinct graphs:
once the partition is specified, restricting G to L_i and R_j
recovers its unique matching. There are (bN)!/(N!)^b ordered right
partitions. Thus the exact number of equally likely marked pairs is

 T_N=(bN)!(N!)^((r-1)b).

The mark is the ordered partition; no within-fiber coordinate or
graph-automorphism factor is missing. Multiple marks may yield the
same observed labeled graph, which is precisely the ambiguity.

Every observation is uniquely specified by the r functions
f_i:V->L_i assigning each right vertex its one neighbor in L_i.
Each f_i has exactly b preimages at every left vertex. The number
of such balanced functions is (bN)!/(b!)^N. Since the L_i are
disjoint, ANY tuple of such functions gives a simple graph with the
stated genie degrees. Therefore the full possible-observation class
has exactly

 U_N=[(bN)!/(b!)^N]^r

graphs. Some of these may not admit a compatible right partition,
so the PLANTED support has size <=U_N. The argument needs only this
upper bound and does not falsely assume every map tuple is a lift.

4. EXACT POSTERIOR, UNORDERED FACTOR, AND INVERSE-COUNT TAIL
-----------------------------------------------------------
For a supported graph G, let Z(G) count compatible ordered right
partitions. Every compatible pair has probability 1/T_N and its
edge matchings are uniquely determined, so Pr(G)=Z(G)/T_N and
the posterior on these marks is exactly uniform.

All b right classes are nonempty. Every unordered partition has
exactly b! distinct ordered versions, all equally compatible since
the right vertices of K_(r,b) have interchangeable base names.
Thus W(G)=Z(G)/b! counts distinct unordered targets. There is no
additional graph-automorphism quotient: observed vertices are
labeled and different vertex subsets are different target blocks.

The optimal conditional success is 1/W(G); randomization cannot
improve it. Consequently every estimator's average success is at
most the exact MAP value

 E[1/W]=sum_(supported G) [Z(G)/T_N][b!/Z(G)]
       =b! |support(G)|/T_N
       <=s_N=b![(bN)!]^(r-1)
                 /[(b!)^(rN)(N!)^((r-1)b)].

This cancellation is finite and exact. In particular no entropy
concentration, typical-null-factorization assumption, or second
moment is hidden in the posterior step.

For any t>=1, the event W<=t implies 1/W>=1/t. Markov applied to
the nonnegative inverse count gives Pr(W<=t)<=t E[1/W]<=t s_N.
It is legitimate despite W being heavily size-biased under the
planted law. This is the structural multiplicity estimate required
below, rather than an unjustified inference from a large first
moment under a different law.

5. STIRLING RATE, PREFACTOR, AND THE FIRST r=3 POSITIVE CASE
-----------------------------------------------------------
Independent simplification gives

 gamma_(r,b)=r log(b!)-(r-1)b log b,
 theta_(r,b)=(r-1)(b-1)/2,

 s_N=b! b^((r-1)/2)(2pi N)^(-theta_(r,b))
                  *exp[-gamma_(r,b)N](1+O_(r,b)(1/N)).

The factorial powers cancel the N log N and linear N terms.
The remaining square-root ratio is precisely
b^((r-1)/2)(2pi N)^[-(r-1)(b-1)/2]. No b!, N!, or factor-r
has been lost. At N=1 the exact finite count also checks:
T_1=b!, U_1=1, W=1 and s_1=1, as required when the right classes
are singletons.

At (r,b)=(3,12), theta=11 and an independent 60-digit Decimal
calculation gives

 gamma_3,12=0.3238838920736510030390596450321433583435654372796017827253...

The source's displayed long decimal differs only at about 1.3e-14;
its shorter approximation 0.323883892 and every rate/sign conclusion
are unaffected. More decisively, exact integer arithmetic confirms
(12!)^3>12^24, and confirms (b!)^3<=b^(2b) for all 3<=b<=11.
This verifies the first positive exponent within the stated r=3
counting family. A nonpositive exponent supplies no converse
recovery theorem, and the source correctly says so.

For fixed r, Stirling also gives
gamma_(r,b)=b log b-rb+(r/2)log(2pi b)+O_r(1/b),
so positive examples occur for sufficiently large b.

6. EXPONENTIALLY MANY PARTITIONS, EVEN FOR EACH FIXED PLANT
----------------------------------------------------------
For fixed 0<c<gamma, insert t=exp(cN) in the exact inverse-count
bound. It gives

 Pr[W<=exp(cN)]<=O_(r,b)(N^-theta)
                          *exp[-(gamma-c)N] ->0.

The permutation group of the right labeled vertices acts transitively
on ordered balanced right partitions. Relabeling takes the conditional
independent matching law for any fixed plant to that for any other.
W is invariant under this action. Its distribution is therefore the
SAME under every fixed plant and under the uniform mixture. The
exponential lower tail transfers to each fixed-plant lift law.

This transfer concerns a graph PROPERTY, not the performance of an
arbitrary estimator at a specially hardcoded plant. The source makes
that distinction correctly.

Each compatible right partition has b size-N classes. Together with
the SAME revealed L_i it gives an equitable partition into r+b
size-N classes with quotient exactly K_(r,b): each right vertex
has one neighbor in every L_i, and each left vertex has one neighbor
in every right class. Distinct right unordered partitions produce
distinct full unordered fiber partitions. They have the same number
of nonempty classes, so cannot be proper coarsenings of one another.
Thus the structural conclusion genuinely refutes the unrestricted
unique-finest bounded-class rigidity assertion for nonregular bases.

7. CONNECTEDNESS CONDITIONING IS VALID
-------------------------------------
Gauge the base spanning-tree matchings to identity by transporting
fiber coordinates. Conditional on these tree matchings, each remaining
edge permutation is still independent uniform: left/right multiplication
by fixed coordinate changes preserves its uniform law. There are

 beta=rb-(r+b)+1=(r-1)(b-1)

non-tree permutations. Lift connectedness is exactly transitivity of
their action on [N]. A nontransitive action has an invariant nonempty
set of size s<=N/2. For any fixed such set, one uniform permutation
preserves it with probability binom(N,s)^(-1). The union bound gives

 Pr(disconnected)<=sum_(s=1)^floor(N/2)binom(N,s)^(1-beta)
                  =O_(r,b)(N^(1-beta))=o(1).

For the last equality, the s=1 term is N^(1-beta), and the remaining
terms are <=(N/2)binom(N,2)^(1-beta)=O(N^(3-2beta)). This is no
larger because beta>=2 (indeed beta>=6 in the stated parameter range).

Conditioning any estimator-success event or the bad lower-tail
event on connectedness divides its probability upper bound by
Pr(connected)=1-o(1). The exponential conclusions persist. The
connectedness probability is the same for each fixed plant by the
same relabeling symmetry, so the fixed-plant structural conclusion
persists as well. No claim that connectivity implies unique fibers
is used or needed.

8. INFORMATION-THEORETIC SCOPE
------------------------------
The success bound is a Bayes/hidden-partition statement against all
estimators, including unbounded and randomized ones. For a publicly
declared fixed canonical right partition an estimator could print
that partition. The proof correctly does not contradict this trivial
pointwise possibility. In contrast its exponentially large W(G)
statement is a structural theorem for EVERY fixed planted partition.

The counterexample base is fixed, connected, simple, and has minimum
degree three and maximum degree twelve. All lift sizes are admissible,
all graph factors are independent random matchings, and the graph
is simple without conditioning. There are no internal edges. The
example invalidates a universal positive nonregular extension; it
does not invalidate the separate positive regular-base theorem or
classify recoverability of K_(3,4), other smaller examples, or all
nonregular frames. Failure of an omission-weight criterion alone
would not prove this obstruction; the finite posterior count does.
