AI mathematics research runs: evidence for a 24-hour campaign
Checked 2026-11-14. This report distinguishes elapsed time, aggregate compute,
concurrent workers, and total agent launches. None is interchangeable with another.
The initial survey used best-match X leads. The targeted supplement below audits
the subsequently named examples: Elliot Glazer, Navier-Stokes, and Jacobian.

3. OpenAI Navier-Stokes campaign (September 2026)
Source:
https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/

Known: an internal model described as significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra;
the successful group involved about 10,000 concurrent agents. Discovery took about

4. Gemini/Colosseum research: SparseStack (November 9, 2026)
Sources:
https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12867v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2508.21189

Known: the SparseStack AI disclosure states Gemini 4 plus the Colosseum harness.
Humans independently established key structural lemmas, checked all mathematics,
and audited the correspondence of Lean statements with the intended theorems.
Theorem 1.1 gives a one-sided embedding: for every fixed orthonormal n-by-r Q,
r >= 2, a normalized concatenation of zeta = Theta(sqrt(log r)) independent
CountSketch blocks, total dimension k = Theta(r), satisfies
Pr[sigma_min(Omega^T Q)^2 >= alpha] >= 19/20 for a universal alpha > 0.
Applying this sketch to B costs O(nnz(B) sqrt(log r)). Unknown: result-specific
agent count, elapsed time, tokens, and peak concurrency.

Relevance limit: the cited upstream low-rank consequences (2508.21189, Theorem
1.3) are constant-factor Frobenius-error, bicriteria rank-O(r) RSVD/generalized
Nystrom, or trace-norm PSD Nystrom. They do not establish the requested rank-r
(1+theta) sigma_(r+1) spectral error. The explicit-matrix sketch cost must also be
re-derived for factor access M=AB. It is a useful mathematical lead, not a solution
to the spectral cutoff target.

6. AlphaProof Nexus: useful 24-hour controls (May/June 2026)
Source:
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22763

Known: Gemini 3.1 Pro provers, with Gemini 3.0 Flash raters in the full system;
ten subagents per full-system proof attempt. The original search resolved 9 of
353 open Erdos problems, and 44 of 492 OEIS conjectures. The main architecture
comparisons allowed 48 hours. Separate 100-agent, 24-hour runs with weaker
Gemini 3.0 Flash or 3.1 Flash-Lite solved none of the nine Erdos targets. A
post-selection Codex GPT-5.5 goal-mode comparison reproduced 7/9 within twelve
hours (one target needed a second attempt); exact Codex worker count, tokens,
and costs were unavailable. The paper explicitly excludes the full search cost
of identifying tractable targets from per-success costs. This is good evidence
for narrow research tasks with executable proof checks, and weak evidence for
predicting success on a fresh arbitrary target.

PROPOSED FOUR-SLOT, 24-HOUR RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

This is an allocation judgment, not a success probability inferred from the runs.
Four concurrent slots including the coordinator provide at most 96 agent-hours,
of which at most 72 belong to three workers; tool waiting and integration reduce
effective throughput. Sixty total launches do not require sixty simultaneous
slots, but repeated tiny launches should not substitute for sustained reasoning.

Hours 0-3: coordinator fixes target theorem, input/access model, accuracy and
confidence dependencies, and a cost ledger. Three independent workers audit
novelty/baselines, search for counterexamples or reductions, and examine the
strongest product-aware sketch/core construction. Each returns a precise claim,
its first risky lemma, and one decisive falsification test.

Hours 3-12: rotate workers across three genuinely different routes: interaction-
preserving cores, factor-aware spectral filtering, and alternative certificates
or restricted-regime results. Preserve failed routes with explicit reasons.
Every promising lemma gets a fresh reader, including checks at rank-deficient,
cancellation-heavy, flat-tail, and very small theta inputs. A route advances only
when the main reduction and its arithmetic cost are both explicit.

Hours 12-18: select the strongest surviving route; give one worker the bottleneck
proof and two workers independent attempts to break it, one mathematical and
one against exact runtime/output/failure-probability accounting. If no route
survives, target a sharp obstruction or a genuinely improved theorem under
clearly stated assumptions while retaining the unrestricted goal as open.

Hours 18-24: freeze a theorem statement early enough for independent verification;
finish proofs, explicit total-cost comparison, confidence bookkeeping, and a
reproducible artifact. Formalize a narrow pivotal lemma if the environment and
time support it. Do not spend the verification window polishing an unproved
manuscript. A realistic deliverable is either a rigorously delimited new lemma
and proof, or a decisive obstruction plus a next bottleneck. None of the public
run disclosures establishes a guaranteed breakthrough at this scale in one day.

11. Jacobian conjecture: explicit disproof, with original run telemetry absent
Original announcement:
https://x.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566
Readable mirror:
https://zamantika.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566
Primary independent mathematical explanation:
https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/07/21/a-digestion-of-the-jacobian-conjecture-counterexample/
First-hand account of the formal verification:
https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2026/07/20/human-mathematicians-are-being-outcounterexampled/
Human formalization PR:
https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/4474
November follow-up mathematical paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00222

Exact scope: a degree-seven polynomial map C^3 -> C^3 has constant nonzero
Jacobian determinant -2 yet maps three distinct inputs to the same output.
Thus it has no inverse. Appending identity coordinates supplies counterexamples
for every dimension at least three, refuting the universal all-dimensions
conjecture; the two-dimensional case remains open in the retrieved accounts.
Tao gives the map, exact determinant and collision checks, and a geometric
explanation. The original post credits Akhil Mathew for asking and Fable for
working during the World Cup final. Buzzard reports Paul Lezeau independently
formalized the example manually against DeepMind's human-formalized conjecture.
Known: human problem selection and a remarkably compact, independently checkable
certificate. Unknown: precise Fable version in the original post, search prompt,
prior attempts, exact run time, token budget, launches, and concurrency. The phrase
about the match is not a measured start-to-finish runtime. The short certificate
does not establish that a single agent found it, or that the search was cheap.

HOW THE TARGETED EXAMPLES CHANGE OUR TACTICS AND 24-HOUR EXPECTATIONS

These examples support attempting an ambitious discovery within 24 hours; they
do not justify forecasting one from a generic clock budget. Elliot's reported
hard-theorem replications took about 60 hours each; OpenAI's NS campaign ran
88+17 hours at vastly greater concurrency and with a different model; the original
Jacobian telemetry remains undisclosed. Conversely, Jacobian shows that a search
can yield an unexpectedly small decisive certificate, so a portfolio should retain
construction and counterexample tracks rather than only long affirmative proofs.

Adapt the four-slot plan above in three concrete ways:

- Keep the main unrestricted spectral target alive while asking each route for
  its smallest nontrivial testable lemma. A core approximation whose key lemma
  restates the original problem has not advanced the proof.
- Use two independent construction routes and a rotating falsifier, with the root
  maintaining a shared ledger of exact hypotheses, dimensions, arithmetic costs,
  and killed mechanisms. Exchange ideas after each route has exposed its own
  obstruction; do not repeatedly feed every worker the same favored sketch.
- Prefer candidates with short exact certificates: an explicit interaction matrix,
  a proved one-sided inequality plus quantified leakage, an adversarial family,
  or a verifiable spectral cutoff. Reserve time to check the translation from any
  lemma to the desired (1+theta) sigma_(r+1) guarantee and full-output runtime.

For reporting, record our actual launches, concurrency, elapsed time, tests and
human steering. Distinguish a new discovery from rediscovery, a construction from
its proof, a conditional theorem from the target, and a compiled statement from
the intended mathematics. That gives a useful comparison to these cases without
inventing undisclosed agent counts or converting a prompt's time request into a
successful measured run.
