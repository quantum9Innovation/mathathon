2. PRECISE SCOPE
----------------
Write the integer degree frame as d_ij, n_i=a_i N with fixed positive
integer a_i, a_i d_ij=a_j d_ji. Internal even-stub conditions determine
the configuration-admissible scales. Every active cross factor is an
independent uniform labeled-stub bijection and every active internal
factor is an independent uniform pairing; loops give two incidences.

The certificate has q_ij=q_ji, 0<q_ij<1/2 and sum_j d_ij q_ij=1.
It implies every local total d_i>=3. Neither the local P/Q comparison
nor the sparse forcing uses a common value of d_i. Size ratios, all
dimensions, maps, prime and degrees are fixed. No growing-field,
growing-frame, noisy, correlated-factor or degree-two conclusion follows.

The one-space induction allows arbitrary fixed LINEAR maps at old
types. For bipartite support it permits two vector spaces of potentially
different dimensions, including dimension zero, and linear maps into
the opposite space. The central module validly allows affine maps as
well. The boundary module's first bipartite phrase 'arbitrary maps'
should be read as arbitrary linear maps, as required by its homogeneous
kernel comparison; this wording clarification was sent to the parent.
There is no nonlinear-map theorem in the proof.

3. WEIGHTED ENTROPY AND COMPLETE ZERO PHASES
------------------------------------------
Within-old-target slot symmetrization preserves legality and pooled
pair tables. Its entropy loss is nonnegative and is zero only for a
slot-symmetric star law. The exact symmetric exponent is

 Phi = -sum_(i<=j) w_ij(1-2q_ij) I_ij
       -sum_i alpha_i sum_(physical omissions t) q_it J_it.

Here w_ii=alpha_i d_ii/2, w_ij=alpha_i d_ij for i<j. Expanding the
retained-star chain rule gives the root coefficient
alpha_i[(2-d_i)+sum_j d_ij(1-2q_ij)]=0. Thus the internal half factor
and variable total degrees are correctly accounted for.

Every coefficient is strictly positive under the actual hypothesis.
At zero entropy ALL present pair mutual informations and ALL physical
omission total correlations vanish. The unsymmetrized star law must
also have zero symmetrization loss. This last observation is necessary
to classify zeroes of the actual type exponent, rather than only of
the symmetrized upper bound.

Condition on any supported root. Pair independence makes all neighbor
marginals root-independent, and each retained d_i-1 tuple has product
law. The fixed-sum finite-group lemma applies at local d_i>=3: every
neighbor marginal is uniform on a coset of a common local subspace.
Its reflection/third-coordinate argument needs no invertible degree
and handles repeated neighbor types and every field characteristic.
Every old type is a neighbor somewhere, so its root law is also a
uniform coset c_i+H_i.

Subspaces agree along every length-two old walk. Connected nonbipartite
support therefore has one common H. An internal factor is a genuine
self-incidence, hence gives an odd walk. Connected bipartite support
has two even-walk components, with H_L and H_R. Repeated physical slots
create no additional parity class.

For linear on-site T_i the necessary and sufficient residual conditions
are T_i H_i subset K_i and T_i c_i-sum_j d_ij c_j in K_i, where K_i
is the common neighbor subspace. The coset sum is sum_j d_ij c_j+K_i,
even when d_i or d_ij is zero in the field: independent subspace
variables sum to the same subspace, rather than to d_i times one fixed
variable. Uniform root plus any d_i-1 independent uniform neighbors
then gives the unique legal zero-phase star law on those cosets.

For a genuinely affine T_i(x)=L_i x+b_i, invariance is L_i H_i subset
K_i and the offset condition uses L_i c_i+b_i. The boundary proof is
stated for linear maps, so this observation is not needed to enlarge
its induction class.

4. EXACT NORMALIZER: THE MAIN GLOBAL GATE
----------------------------------------
For the full physical-slot tables, at old type i let V_i be the root
space and U_i the common neighbor space. The legal star count is
|V_i||U_i|^(d_i-1), and the unrestricted law has independent root and
all d_i neighbors. With exact factor weight W and

 K_N=product_i (|V_i||U_i|^(d_i-1))^n_i,

the count gives E Z=K_N E_P W, while each graph and root assignment
determine exactly one ordered-neighbor assignment, giving

 E_Q W=product_i |U_i|^(-d_i n_i),
 K_N E_Q W=product_i (|V_i|/|U_i|)^n_i.

The latter factor cannot simply be discarded in a nonregular proof.
It is one for a common alphabet. In the bipartite two-space class,
weighted row balance proves exactly

 sum_L n_i = sum_(L,R) n_i d_ij q_ij
           = sum_(R,L) n_j d_ji q_ji = sum_R n_j.

Hence the normalizer (|V_L|/|V_R|)^(n_L-n_R) is exactly one. If E_N
is the physical edge count, K_N=(|V_L||V_R|)^E_N. This is the correct
variable-degree replacement for the old common-d formula.

The normalized law nu=K_N Q W is therefore precisely the law of the
actual configuration with iid uniform roots. The equality remains
valid for every restricted pair H_L,H_R, since physical populations
and the certificate do not change. This closes the global normalizer
issue at every induction level, not just at the top ambient alphabet.

5. CENTRAL LOCAL LIMIT AND TAIL ESTIMATE
---------------------------------------
The module keeps separate tables for EACH physical slot. At type i
the real dimension is (|V_i|-1)+d_i|V_i|(|U_i|-1). Conditional on the
root, any two legal neighbors are independent uniform because d_i>=3.
Thus legal and unrestricted local laws have the same positive
covariance (|V_i||U_i|)^(-1)[Id+(d_i-1)P_row]. Their eigenvalues are
d_i/(|V_i||U_i|) on common-row modes and 1/(|V_i||U_i|) on the others.
Singleton alphabets remove modes without creating a missing determinant.

The three-slot character calculation identifies the COMPLETE maximal
lattice: one U_i-valued congruence per root label and local index
|U_i|^(|V_i|). These congruences are independent by within-row
transfers. Legal affine lattices lie inside unrestricted lattices at
every size because legal stars are also unrestricted stars. Factor
parity/reversal stays in W; an incompatible residue contributes zero.

Uniform tilting on a fixed mean neighborhood gives relative lattice
local limits with the actual covolumes and determinants. Equality of
mean and covariance makes the rate-function difference cubic, yielding
P/Q <= C I_* exp(C||Delta||^3/N^2), with ratio I_*(1+o(1)) on the
sqrt(N) log N window. This is a fixed-neighborhood comparison, not an
unsupported extrapolation of a central-window estimate.

Under the exact weighted law nu, permutation transpositions change at
most four directed incidences and a root-label change at most 2Dmax.
The associated exposure martingale gives a global subgaussian table
tail. Internal same-vertex pairing changes the independent mean by
only O(1), with probability (d_ii-1)/(n_i d_ii-1) per stub; cross
means are exact. The cubic likelihood ratio integrates against this
tail after shrinking the fixed neighborhood. Its annulus is o(1),
and the central contribution has limsup at most I_*.

6. SPARSE CHARGE, AFFINE FIBERS AND DIMENSION CLOSURE
---------------------------------------------------
A phase offset is only an exact quotient point map. It need not solve
the ambient equation. Subtract it after quotienting, and for each
quotient labeling count its ambient lifts as an affine system. Such
a fiber is empty or a translate of the homogeneous restricted kernel.
This is exactly the comparison used by the new boundary source; it
does not assume an unjustified ambient point-map lift.

All neighbor quotient spaces at an exterior vertex agree (common H
or the opposite parity subspace). A quotient support S therefore has
no exterior vertex with exactly one nonzero incident value. If a,b
count internal/boundary certificate edges and t exterior vertices,
then 2a+b=sum_S d_v>=dmin|S| and b>=2t. Consequently
E-(|S|+t)>=(dmin-2)|S|/2, while E<=Dmax|S|. Loops count twice in
degree and once in E; support-one configurations are retained.

The actual forcing couplings preserve conditional uniformity: a
cross image switch has m preimages, and an internal prescribed-pair
switch has exactly m-1 preimages. At most E switches change at most
4r_*E scalar equation rows. The rank inequality charges the conditional
restricted kernel moment by p^(4r_*E), so sparse-event/bulk-kernel
correlations have not been ignored.

All positive stub pools have Theta(n) size. Certificate counting with
the above excess, including quotient values and this rank charge,
gives M_H(n) Dmax s B^s(s/n)^(beta s), beta=(dmin-2)/2>0.
Summing from s=1 gives O(n^-beta) M_H(n). All constants are fixed.

Every proper phase reduces the common dimension, or the sum of the
two side dimensions, and its restricted maps stay in the same class.
The zero-dimensional system has one solution. The finite proper-phase
family is therefore bounded by induction, while the full phase is
controlled centrally. Outside these neighborhoods the feasible type
region is compact and has strictly negative exponent. The exact
configuration count supplies only a fixed polynomial prefactor there,
which is dominated by exp(-c n). This closes the complete moment.

For simple factors, direct conditioning costs the reciprocal of a
positive limiting probability. Labeled-stub multiplicities are constant
over each simple factor, so this gives exactly the intended independent
uniform simple laws, not an unjustified contiguity transfer for an
unbounded statistic. Small scales where simple factors do not exist
are not claimed; the finite existing exceptions can be absorbed.

7. EXAMPLES AND EXCLUSIONS
-------------------------
Common degree d>=3 admits q_ij=1/d on every active factor. If each
internal d_ii>=2 and k>=2, set active cross weights to epsilon>0 and
q_ii=(1-epsilon b_i)/d_ii, b_i=sum_(j!=i)d_ij. Connectedness gives
b_i>0, so sufficiently small epsilon gives strict positivity and
strict upper bounds, even when d_ii=2. The singleton case requires
d_11>=3 and uses 1/d_11. K5 with one edge removed is also covered by
the frozen weights 1/3 between groups and 1/6 within the degree-four
group. These really are variable-total-degree examples.

No constant-moment conclusion is made for any frame for which every
certificate requires zero on an active factor. Its weighted one-hot
gap and polynomial recovery extension are different arguments. A
weight one half is also excluded. The fixed-prime moment bound is not
a uniform bound for primes tending with n, an exact scalar asymptotic,
or by itself a practical or quadratic-time recovery implementation.
