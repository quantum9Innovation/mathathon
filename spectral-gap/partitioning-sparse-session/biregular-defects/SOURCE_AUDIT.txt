Primary-source audit, embedded equitable modes -- 2026-11-15
============================================================

All writes of this child session are in this directory. No historical
firstness is claimed. The embedded-mode-proof.tex/pdf contains
elementary proved statements and a clearly marked unproved probabilistic
target. It is not a solution of that target.

1. Barucca, Spectral partitioning in equitable graphs (2017)
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.02668
   Latest inspected PDF says arXiv v5, 30 Jun 2017, eight pages.
   Inspected model definition, sections III.A and IV.A, discussion IV.B/V.
   The official source gives lambda_com=c_in-c_out as an exact block-sign
   eigenvalue, even when it sits inside the Kesten--McKay bulk.
   Equation20 computes IPR=1/N for that vector, while the bulk IPR≈3/N
   comparison is a random-eigenvector heuristic and numerical evidence.
   PDF page5, paragraph after equation20 and paragraph after the discussion
   of previous uniqueness results, proposes efficient full recovery for
   all c_in>c_out>0 as a CONJECTURE. Figures3/4 include the cubic (2,1)
   case. The proposed proof packet does not rebrand this conjecture as new.

2. Zhu--Zhu, Non-backtracking eigenvalues and eigenvectors ...
   https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03300v3
   Actual official HTML inspected: 25 Sep 2026, v3. Sections1.3,3.3,4,9.
   Section1.3 explicitly says Barucca's full-regime efficient exact
   recovery conjecture remains open to the authors' knowledge.
   Theorem3.10(2): real planted NB pair when
       (d1-d2)^2>4(d1+d2-1).
   Theorem3.10(3), Corollary3.11 add d1 even for high-probability isolated
   multiplicity-one statements and exact recovery from those real modes.
   Remark3.12 identifies parity as a lift/contiguity technical restriction.
   Lemma4.1 gives the exact quadratic relation to adjacency eigenvalues.
   Section9 Lemma9.1 is displayed without the separation/parity conditions,
   yet its proof cites the old lift-gap proof. That proof does not establish
   embedded planted-eigenvalue simplicity. It would also conflict with the
   paper's explicit open-conjecture discussion to read it that broadly.
   We therefore do not use its unqualified displayed wording as a theorem
   below the universal-cover edge. No assertion that this typo refutes the
   paper's explicitly scoped main theorem is made.

3. Brito--Dumitriu--Ganguly--Hoffman--Tran (SODA2016), v4 2020
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00930v4
   Inspected Section1 (including standing assumptions), Theorem2,
   Section2.3 and Section4 actual proof. Section1 says min(d1,d2)>=3
   throughout. Theorem2 assumes spectral separation and d1 even.
   Section4 constructs a two-vertex base with d1/2 loops at each endpoint
   and d2 parallel cross edges. Its two old eigenvalues are d1+d2,d1-d2.
   New eigenvalues are bounded by the universal-cover edge plus epsilon.
   Separation therefore ensures the second old eigenvalue is simple;
   the proof supplies NO extra argument excluding exact collisions when
   this eigenvalue lies inside that support. The v4 abstract notes that
   an erroneous appendix was removed, producing the parity restriction.
   Thus do not invoke older versions to erase that restriction.
   First natural cubic test=(2,1), outside the paper's standing assumptions.
   First assortative even-internal test within those assumptions=(4,3).

4. Huang--Xu, Local law and eigenvector delocalization for random lifts
   https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37463
   Official arXiv v1, submitted27 Oct2026, 70pages.
   Inspected abstract, Sections1.1--1.2, Theorems1.2--1.3.
   Fixed connected SIMPLE base, minimum degree>=3; regular bulk counting
   at intervals >=log^C(N)/N, complete delocalization away from singular
   cover energies. The two-vertex loop base is outside this stated scope.
   Even an extension to it would not, merely from this resolution, exclude
   ONE extra exact eigenvalue at lambda. The (normalized) smoothed density
   contribution of one eigenvalue is O(1/(N eta))=O(log^-C N) there.
   No exact collision exclusion or full simplicity result was found in the
   inspected main statements. We have not read/audited the entire70pages.

5. Current homogeneous SBM BH results -- do not claim as open
   Official abstracts were independently opened and verified:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16672 (Dong--McKenzie,17Aug2026):
       BH negative eigenvalue counting for every fixed common degree d>1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41802 (Dong--McKenzie,30Oct2026):
       BH outlier eigenvector weak recovery down to KS, all fixed d>1.
   These are for the homogeneous expected-degree SBM, not the rigid
   exact-degree planted-eigenvector problem. Only abstracts were inspected
   in this child session; another parent-coordinated agent covers robustness.

Mathematical checks and limits
-----------------------------
* H(r)sigma=[r²-(a-b)r+a+b-1]sigma is an exact identity. Completing
  the square proves the all-real-r negative-eigenspace exclusion below
  the adjacency cover edge. This is stronger in scope than checking only
  r=sqrt(d-1), but it is elementary and is not claimed historically new.
* The NB roots in the embedded regime are NONREAL and ON the bulk circle.
  Calling them real insiders would be wrong. The real insider mechanism
  of Zhu--Zhu belongs to the already separated regime.
* Exact nullity one is sufficient for unique exact equitable recovery.
  No necessity is claimed. Modular rank failure is inconclusive; odd
  primes dividing2b necessarily fail because both1 andsigma are kernels.
* A classical weighted matrix-tree specialization makes the target even
  more concrete: any principal cofactor of(lambda I-A) equals the number
  of spanning trees with an even number of cut edges minus the number with
  an odd number. P_A'(lambda)=N times this signed tree count. Nonvanishing
  of one principal cofactor is therefore enough; no scan of minors is
  needed under the equitable-bisection promise. The proof uses incidence
  Cauchy--Binet, and does not posit positivity for the signed weights.
* A degree-preserving two-switch can destroy the exact planted equation
  at four vertices, with normalized residual4/sqrtN. This establishes
  brittleness of the exact equation, not impossibility of robust inference.
* Cube lifts give three perfectly flat valid assignments with identical
  BH/NB behavior, local tree likeness and optimal full nontrivial gap.
  They are NOT uniform RSBM samples. Their identifiability obstruction is
  valid for deterministic spectral hypotheses, not a refutation of Barucca.
