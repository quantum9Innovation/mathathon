THEOREMS
  For fixed 0<alpha<1, beta=1-alpha and a,b complex, put
    f(x)=a*x_+^alpha+b*(-x)_+^alpha
         =kappa*(-i*x)^alpha+lambda*(i*x)^alpha.
  If m is the number of nonzero analytic coefficients kappa,lambda,
    lim s^2 H_s(f)=m/(pi^2*alpha*beta),
  where H_s is the best ratio of OUTPUT s-power trace to INPUT
  alpha*s-power trace. Only the two analytic lines b=a*exp(+-i*pi*alpha)
  have m=1; every other nonzero function has m=2.
  Thus PSD powers, one analytic branch, and any nonzero real homogeneous
  fractional function have moment coefficients in the ratio 1:2:4.
  This is NOT an ordinary Schatten quasi-norm constant ratio.

  The final manuscript proves the stronger UNIFORM formula
    s^2 H_s(kappa*g_+ +lambda*g_-)
      ~ (|kappa|^s+|lambda|^s)/(pi^2*alpha*beta),
  uniformly over every nonzero coefficient pair, including coefficients
  varying arbitrarily with s. For f_theta=g_++theta*g_-, |theta|<=1,
  the accuracy count is uniformly
    r*b*[L^2+(L+log|theta|)_+^2]+o_r(L^2),
    b=1/(2*pi^2*alpha*beta).
  Zero and superexponentially small branches are included.

  Put c=m/(2*pi^2*alpha*beta). For each fixed perturbation rank r, the
  exact worst-case compression-rank coefficient is r*c at error
  epsilon*||E||^alpha, epsilon=exp(-L), L->infinity.
  If n*s^2->t>0, the finite-dimension optimal power constant satisfies
    s^2 H_(s,n)(f) ->2c[1-(1+sqrt(t/c))*exp(-sqrt(t/c))].
  Both endpoint regimes and capped rank counts are included. The bounded-
  input semifinite theorem recovers finite matrix degree D via H_s->D,
  and unbounded degree gives the same universal channel coefficient.

  Joint coefficient/dimension limit: if u=-s*log|theta_s| and n*s^2->t,
    s^2 H_(s,n)(f_theta) -> integral_0^infinity
       exp(-x)*min(t,b*[x^2+(x-u)_+^2]) dx.
  Closed forms and uniformity on compact positive n*s^2 ranges are proved.
  Arbitrary overall coefficient sizes are normalized by their maximum
  magnitude to power s; the error is o(M_s^s), not absolute o(1) if unbounded.
