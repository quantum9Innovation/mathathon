A. Classical necessity: determinant, Loewner--Heinz, ALT

For B=A+uu^T, the determinant lemma and spectral calculus already imply
det(A^(-alpha/2) B^alpha A^(-alpha/2))=(1+tau)^alpha.
Loewner--Heinz gives q_i>=1. The strict-count upper bound follows at once.
None of these upper bounds should be described as newly discovered.

Araki--Lieb--Thirring gives
  lambda(A^(-alpha/2) B^alpha A^(-alpha/2))
       prec_log lambda(A^(-1/2) B A^(-1/2))^alpha.
In the rank-one case the right list is ((1+tau)^alpha,1,...,1).
The logmajorization constraints are equivalent to q_i>=1 plus the
determinant equality. ALT proves necessity, not the converse construction.

Primary accessible comparator: K.M.R. Audenaert, An Araki--Lieb--Thirring
inequality for geometrically concave and geometrically convex functions,
Linear Algebra Appl.438(2013),3454--3462; arXiv:1204.3418v2.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3418
Its stated ALT relation directly specializes to the displayed necessity.

B. Graded scaling lemma: explicit prior-theorem derivation

Usevich--Barthelme, Computing asymptotic eigenvectors and eigenvalues of
perturbed symmetric matrices, arXiv:2407.17047v2, Theorem21, identifies
the leading eigenvalues of diagonally graded symmetric matrices through
successive Schur complements. For B=D C D with D^2=diag(a_i),
a_1>>a_2>>...>>a_n, it gives lambda_i(B)/a_i -> p_i, where p_i are the
ordinary leading LDL pivots of C.
https://arxiv.org/html/2407.17047v2#S4.SS1

Gissler--Auger--Hansen, Asymptotic estimations of a perturbed symmetric
eigenproblem, Applied Mathematics Letters150(2024),108951;
arXiv:2403.06983v1, Theorem1, gives for B=D(I+zz^T)D an eigenbasis U
with |U_ji| <= C sqrt(min(a_i,a_j)/max(a_i,a_j)). This is exactly the
rank-one scaling model used here, with constant independent of grading.
https://arxiv.org/html/2403.06983v1#S1

Together these already imply
  diag(a)^(-alpha/2) B^alpha diag(a)^(-alpha/2)
       -> diag(p_i^alpha),             0<alpha<1.

For completeness, the diagonal term arising from i<j is bounded by a
constant times (a_j/a_i)^(1-alpha); the term from i>j is bounded by
(a_i/a_j)^(1+alpha). Both vanish. For j<k, splitting the eigenvalue sum
at i=j and i=k bounds the off-diagonal by a fixed constant times
(a_k/a_j)^((1-alpha)/2), which also vanishes. The i=j diagonal term
has limit p_j^alpha. Thus the manuscript's separated-scale limit is
independently derivable from these two existing theorems. Its short
resolvent proof is useful but the underlying grading mechanism is prior.

These sources do not explicitly characterize attainable relative power
spectra or prove the exact graph sharpness statement.

C. Prescribed pivots: multiplicative Schur--Horn is prior

Huang--Tam, Some asymptotic behaviors associated with matrix decompositions,
Theorem3.3, gives the full set of Cholesky pivot vectors on a positive
matrix's unitary orbit as the logmajorization polytope of its eigenvalues.
It is a matrix instance of Kostant's nonlinear convexity theorem (1973).
Primary author copy:
https://webhome.auburn.edu/~tamtiny/asymptotic-matrix-lie-6.pdf
Read Section3, especially Theorem3.3 and the surrounding comparison.
The author PDF has a provisional front-page bibliographic header; use
the published metadata only after checking it separately.

For C=I+zz^T, pivots p_i>=1 and product p_i=1+tau are the rank-one
specialization. The explicit telescope z_i^2=(p_i-1)prod_(j<i)p_j
(with descending a_i, reversed for ascending a_i) is elementary.
Do not claim that freedom to prescribe these Cholesky pivots is new.

Possible larger theorem: for arbitrary fixed relative eigenvalues s_i,
the same sources combined with ALT suggest that the closure of relative
fractional spectra is exactly log q prec alpha log s. The parent reports
an independent session is proving this broader statement. This note does
not claim it is new; it is naturally a consequence of existing majorization
and grading theory, even if its explicit formulation is useful.

D. Graph mass removal: large-conductance quotient is prior

Koke, Di-Graphs with tightly connected Clusters: Effective Graph
Laplacians and Resolvent Convergence, arXiv:2601.18057v2 (January2026),
Section3, Theorems3.5 and3.12, proves convergence to quotient Laplacians
when intracluster conductances diverge. Quotient vertex masses aggregate.
Primary source inspected in detail:
https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18057v1#S3
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18057

This directly overlaps the fixed-block, T->infinity step of the
manuscript's unit-mass block simulation. Norm-resolvent convergence alone
does not immediately justify unbounded positive fractional powers.
The manuscript uses cancellation of the two fractional powers and the
integrable estimate t^alpha min(2/t,||E||/t^2); the exact relative-metric
transfer is an additional argument. No inspected Koke statement gives
the arbitrary target fractional-relative spectrum, preservation of a
single edited edge with exactly fixed leverage, or the sharp count law.

The Lanczos/Jacobi/ground-state transformation to a mass path is classical
and already used in the repository. The new combined claim must be
distinguished from invention of those transformations.

E. Later exact-attainment/simplicity target

The parent reports a strengthening: all positive correction eigenvalues
are simple, and every distinct positive determinant-admissible list is
exactly attained; repeated positive values occur only as limits.

Close primary comparator: Bhatia--Friedland--Jain, Inertia of Loewner
Matrices, arXiv:1501.01505, Theorem1.1(v), proves every nonzero eigenvalue
of the power divided-difference Loewner matrix is simple. Its proof uses
strict sign regularity and Perron on exterior powers.
https://arxiv.org/html/1501.01505v1
Read the theorem and its strict-sign-regularity proof.

The nonlinear rank-one-update resolvent kernel has a changed positive
integration weight, rather than exactly that Loewner matrix. Strict total
positivity of its Cauchy Gram matrix follows from classical integral
composition/Cauchy determinant methods. Thus simplicity is plausibly a
new application of a classical mechanism; it must not be promoted as a
new total-positivity theorem. No exact matching nonlinear inverse-spectrum
statement was located in these inspected sources.

Direct/inverse spectral results for A+uv^* itself (e.g. Dobosevych--Hryniv,
Integral Equations and Operator Theory93(2021),18) concern the original
rank-one perturbation's spectrum, not the relative spectrum after taking
fractional matrix powers. Their hypotheses and conclusions do not directly
subsume this nonlinear target.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00020-021-02630-y

F. Existing low-rank fractional update bounds

Beckermann--Cortinovis--Kressner--Schweitzer arXiv:2008.11501 and
Shmueli--Drineas--Avron arXiv:2201.13156 / NLA e2528 supply prior
matrix-update approximation and square-root decay results. They address
absolute correction errors and bounds that depend on spectral separation.
The current claim concerns congruence by the baseline fractional energy;
it neither contradicts nor supersedes those algorithms. The repository
already records their detailed comparison. No claim to have newly read
both complete papers in this worker's pass is made.
