Let c0=1/(4*pi^2*alpha), h=epsilon^tau and C_alpha(tau) be the reviewed
rank-one boundary coefficient. All counts are strict, dimension is
unrestricted but finite, alpha in (0,1), tau>=0 and update rank r fixed.
The leading count coefficients for A>=0, E>=0, ||E||=1,
A+E<=(1+h)I are:

  rank(E)<=r or rank(E)=r:             (r-1)c0+C_alpha(tau);
  eigenvalue-one multiplicity >=s:     (r-s)c0+s*C_alpha(tau);
  E an orthogonal rank-r projection:   r*C_alpha(tau).

The middle coefficient also holds with rank exactly r and multiplicity
exactly s, or with all r positive eigenvalues prescribed and fixed,
s equal to one and the rest strictly below one. No theorem about moving
subcritical eigenvalues is inferred. Thus update spectrum matters:
the projection class and unrestricted norm-one rank-r class have
different boundary laws when r>1 and tau>0.

All these leading coefficients already occur on connected weighted paths
with arbitrary positive common vertex masses and exactly r increased
existing edges with disjoint vertex supports. Masses, dimension and weak
bridge conductances may depend on epsilon. No unit-mass sharpness or
connected positive graph realization at exactly R=1 is claimed.

For E=P an orthogonal rank-r projection there is also an explicit
PSD rank-at-most-r Schur correction F with
  D-F>=0, ||D-F||_1<=r*h^alpha.
Consequently the matrix supremal strict count is exactly r whenever
0<epsilon<1 and r*h^alpha<=epsilon. This applies eventually for every
fixed tau>1/alpha. At equality tau=1/alpha the finite conclusion is
asserted only for r=1; for r>1 the leading coefficient is zero but the
displayed bound does not certify an exact finite count r.
