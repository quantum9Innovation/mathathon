Detection is already easier than embedded recovery
=================================================
15 November 2026. Formal corollary of an inspected current primary main
theorem; not a claim of a new technique, historical priority, or an audit
of its 78-page proof.

He, Huang, Wang, Bulk universality of random regular graphs,
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08127v1 (6 October 2026).
Read: official abstract and introduction, fixed-energy main theorem,
Theorem1.1, Theorem1.2, Corollary1.3 and their precise model scope.
The main theorem concerns the uniform SIMPLE fixed-d regular graph,
NOT an exact equitable block model and NOT its new spectral space.

Let lambda be any fixed energy strictly inside (-2sqrt(d-1),2sqrt(d-1)).
For adjacency A_N of the uniform simple d-regular null model, the theorem
asserts convergence of the point process
  Xi_N=sum_j delta_{N*pi*rho_d(lambda)*(lambda_j(A_N)-lambda)}
to Sine_1, whose intensity is1/pi and which has no atom at0 almost surely.
Then
  Pr{det(A_N-lambda I)=0} ->0.                         (D)

Proof of the corollary: for any fixed epsilon>0, the event on the left
implies Xi_N([-epsilon,epsilon])>=1. The limiting process has no atom at
the two deterministic interval endpoints, so convergence of the point
process implies convergence in distribution of this interval count.
Consequently its limiting probability of being nonzero is at most its
mean, 2epsilon/pi. Taking epsilon to0 proves(D). This uses probabilities
of bounded-window events, NOT an unjustified expectation limit for an
unbounded counting functional.

In an exact two-community (a,b) RSBM, d=a+b and lambda=a-b is ALWAYS an
integer adjacency eigenvalue because the planted sign vector is exact.
Thus exact rational rank/determinant computation distinguishes the RSBM
from the uniform d-regular null, with true-positive probability1 and
false-positive probability tending to0, throughout the embedded regime,
conditional only on the cited primary theorem. Exact integer elimination
has polynomial bit complexity. Efficient noiseless equitable-model
distinguishing was ALREADY described by Bandeira, Banks, Kunisky, Moore,
Wein, Spectral Planting and the Hardness of Refuting Cuts, Colorability,
and Communities in Random Graphs, COLT2021, Remark3 (PDFp7):
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v134/bandeira21a/bandeira21a.pdf .
The 2026 point-process theorem is one sufficient modern proof input for
the exact-eigenvalue null-model obstruction, not a claim that efficient
detection is a new result.

This does NOT recover the planted partition. If the kernel has dimension
greater than1, finding its balanced sign vectors can be a separate problem.
The cube lift supplies three exact examples even with an optimal full
nontrivial spectral gap. The expanding flat-outlier construction supplies
exponentially many examples. Conversely, under the null, excluding a fixed
eigenvalue is an UNCONDITIONAL assertion. Conditioning on an exact
equitable partition is a very rare event that forces this eigenvalue.
Neither contiguity to the null nor a transfer of its atom-exclusion theorem
has been shown; they cannot be silently assumed.

Precise original positive target: for the uniform simple RSBM, exclude
an ADDITIONAL exact eigenvalue at the forced lambda, equivalently prove
rank(A_N-lambda I)=N-1 with high probability. This would give certified
exact recovery even though all scalar Bethe-Hessian negative-mode tests
miss the forced direction. Already the (a,b)=(4,3) case is substantial.

A possible sufficient input is new-space fixed-energy point-process
convergence, after removing exactly the known old planted mode, to ANY
locally finite process with no atom at0. The same small-window proof then
excludes additional exact collisions. Full GOE universality is far stronger
than necessary. An atom bound alone would suffice. A local law at scale
log^C(N)/N does not alone supply this extra microscopic assertion.
