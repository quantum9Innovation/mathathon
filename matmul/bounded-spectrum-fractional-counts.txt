SHARP FRACTIONAL COMPRESSION WITH A BOUNDED ENDPOINT-TO-UPDATE RATIO
=================================================================

Research deduction, 2026-11-15 UTC. This note gives exact leading
compression-count and small-Schatten-exponent constants when the endpoint
norms are bounded relative to the perturbation norm. The scalar rational
upper is a direct application of Stahl's classical theorem. The lower
uses the already checked weighted-Cauchy argument on ONE spectral tail.
At the critical endpoint bound there is a further loss of one rank channel.
Historical priority for these precise matrix/graph consequences is not
certified here. This is a compression theorem, not an arithmetic runtime
or constructive-pole algorithm theorem.

1. THE CLASS AND THE EXACT LEADING CONSTANTS
===========================================
Fix 0<alpha<1, an integer r>=1, and a real number R>=1. Let C_(R,r)
be the class of all finite real symmetric pairs (A,B) satisfying

  0<=A,B<=R I,  E=B-A,  ||E||=1,  rank(E)<=r.             (1)

The dimension is unrestricted. Put Delta=B^alpha-A^alpha, and define

  N_(R,r)(L)=sup_(A,B in C_(R,r))
                 #{j: sigma_j(Delta)>exp(-L)},
  H_(R,r)(s)=sup_(A,B in C_(R,r)) tr|Delta|^s.             (2)

The strict threshold convention is intentional. The two conclusions are

  lim_(L->infinity) N_(R,r)(L)/L^2
    = r/(4 pi^2 alpha),                 R>1,
    = (r-1)/(4 pi^2 alpha),             R=1;              (3)

  lim_(s down to 0) s^2 H_(R,r)(s)
    = r/(2 pi^2 alpha),                 R>1,
    = (r-1)/(2 pi^2 alpha),             R=1.              (4)

Already positive perturbations E>=0 attain all the leading lower
constants. For R=1,r=1 the stronger exact conclusion is that Delta has
one nonzero singular value, equal to one. Thus N_(1,1)(L)=1 for L>0
and H_(1,1)(s)=1. When R<1 the class (1) is empty, since
-R I<=B-A<=R I.

By homogeneity, (1) is equivalent to the scale-free assumption

  max(||A||,||B||)<=R||B-A||,

with approximation error eps||B-A||^alpha. For every fixed R>1 the
coefficient (3) improves the unrestricted-endpoint coefficient
r/[4 pi^2 alpha(1-alpha)] by the factor 1-alpha. At alpha=1/2,
the coefficients are respectively r/(2 pi^2) and r/pi^2.
The discontinuity at R=1 concerns a supremum over all finite sizes.
The extremizing pair may depend on L or s.

2. CLASSICAL SCALAR UPPER AND A FULL RATIONAL RANK PROOF
=====================================================
Stahl's Theorem 1, equation (2.1), in

  H. Stahl, Best uniform rational approximation of x^alpha on [0,1],
  Bulletin AMS 28 (1993), 116-122,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9301217
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9301217

states, for 0<alpha<1,

  E_(q,q)(x^alpha,[0,1])
   =4^(1+alpha)sin(pi alpha) exp(-2pi sqrt(alpha q))(1+o(1)). (5)

The full primary PDF was inspected at Theorem 1 and equations (2.1)-(2.2).
Scaling [0,1] to [0,R] shows that real rational functions f_q=P_q/Q_q
of type (q,q), without poles in [0,R], can be chosen with

  sup_[0,R]|f_q(x)-x^alpha|
       <=C_(alpha,R)exp(-2pi sqrt(alpha q))               (6)

for all sufficiently large q. The constant and threshold do not depend
on matrix dimension or on the input pair. Common polynomial factors can
be canceled so that Q_q is nonzero on [0,R]. The following rank statement
does not require any information about the pole locations outside [0,R].

If rank(B-A)<=r, then

  rank(f_q(B)-f_q(A))<=rq.                               (7)

Here is a proof valid for nonscalar, noncommuting A,B. The polynomial
P_q(x)Q_q(y)-Q_q(x)P_q(y) is divisible by x-y, with quotient of degree
at most q-1 in each variable. Write that quotient as
sum_(i,j=0)^(q-1) c_ij x^i y^j. Left-right evaluation gives

  P_q(B)Q_q(A)-Q_q(B)P_q(A)
      =sum_(i,j=0)^(q-1)c_ij B^i(B-A)A^j.               (8)

This evaluation is valid because left multiplication by B and right
multiplication by A commute as operators on matrices. Its right side
has column range inside sum_(i=0)^(q-1)range(B^i E), of dimension at
most rq. Moreover the left side of (8) equals

  Q_q(B)[f_q(B)-f_q(A)]Q_q(A).

Both Q_q(A),Q_q(B) are invertible, proving (7). This is the usual
rational low-rank exactness mechanism, not a new scalar approximation
or new matrix-function update principle.

Spectral calculus and (6) give

  ||Delta-[f_q(B)-f_q(A)]||
       <=2C_(alpha,R)exp(-2pi sqrt(alpha q)).             (9)

Taking q=ceil((L+C')^2/(4pi^2 alpha)), enlarged by a fixed minimum
integer if needed, makes (9) at most exp(-L). Consequently, uniformly
over (1) and all L>=0,

  N_Delta(L)<= [r/(4pi^2 alpha)]L^2+C L+K.               (10)

Here C,K may depend on alpha,R,r only. This proves the upper in (3)
for R>1, and provides the uniform quadratic count bound needed below.
It is an existence proof using best rational approximants. No unit-cost
oracle for their coefficients or negative shifted poles is asserted.

For later use every pair in (1) obeys ||Delta||<=1. Indeed ||A-B||<=1
implies A<=B+I, so Loewner--Heinz monotonicity and scalar calculus for B
give A^alpha<=(B+I)^alpha<=B^alpha+I. Interchange A,B for the other
inequality. Thus -I<=Delta<=I. Layer cake and (10) imply

  tr|Delta|^s=s int_0^infinity exp(-sL)N_Delta(L)dL,
  limsup_(s down to 0)s^2 H_(R,r)(s)<=r/(2pi^2 alpha).   (11)

3. A BOUNDED-ENDPOINT RANK-ONE LOWER FROM ONE SPECTRAL TAIL
========================================================
Fix R>1. Choose 0<h<(R-1)/4 and 0<eta<1. On the half-line
x<=log h use the probability density

  rho(x)=eta h^(-eta)exp(eta x),
  m(t)=int_(-infinity)^(log h) rho(x)/(exp(x)+t) dx.      (12)

For T>0 set I_T=[log h-T,log h]. On L2(I_T), let H_T be multiplication
by exp(x), and let w_T=sqrt(rho)|_(I_T). Then

  0<H_T<=h I,  ||w_T||^2=t_T=1-exp(-eta T)<1.

Take T large enough that t_T>=1/2. Dividing BOTH endpoints
H_T and H_T+w_Tw_T^* by t_T gives a positive rank-one update of norm
exactly one, with endpoint norms bounded by

  ||H_T/t_T||<=2h,
  ||(H_T+w_Tw_T^*)/t_T||<=1+2h<R.                       (13)

Thus there is a fixed strict margin in the endpoint norm restriction.
No later limit consumes that margin.

The weighted-Cauchy lower lemma proved in sharp-fractional-holder.tex
(lem:convolution, eq:gamma-integral, and its positive-input lower proof)
applies verbatim on I_T. To spell out the change of tail, write
c_alpha=sin(pi alpha)/pi and beta=1-alpha. The exact Woodbury integral
and m_(I_T)<=m imply that the unnormalized correction Delta_T dominates
T_T T_T^*, where the weighted Cauchy operator has squared weight product

  q(x)=c_alpha rho(x)exp(-beta x)/(1+m(exp(x))).          (14)

For 0<s<=1/2, the proved convolution inequality gives

  tr Delta_T^s
    >= [J_(1/2,s)/(2pi)] int_(I_T)q(x)^s dx,             (15)

where J_(1/2,s) is the Fourier-symbol integral in that source and

  lim_(s down to 0)s J_(1/2,s)=1/pi.                    (16)

Unlike the unrestricted-endpoint lower construction, (12) has no
positive infinite spectral tail. As x->-infinity, substitution u=exp(x)
and the beta integral give

  m(t)~[eta h^(-eta)pi/sin(pi eta)]t^(eta-1),
  q(x)~[sin(pi alpha)sin(pi eta)/pi^2]exp(alpha x).     (17)

The weight q is positive and continuous up to the finite right endpoint
log h. Exponential-tail sandwiching therefore proves

  lim_(s down to 0)s int_(-infinity)^(log h)q(x)^s dx
       =1/alpha.                                      (18)

For clarity, these infinite-dimensional objects only establish finite
matrix lower bounds. Approximate exp(x) uniformly on the fixed I_T by
a step function with values in (0,h]. Keep w_T itself. On the cyclic
span of its restrictions to the step cells, the step multiplication
operator and rank-one update are genuine finite matrices, with update
norm t_T EXACTLY. Off that span the correction vanishes. Their two
endpoint functions converge in operator norm to the bounded-interval
operators. Singular-value min--max transfers each finite partial power
trace from below, then taking the supremum over its length gives the
lower semicontinuity needed in (15). Equivalently this is the finite
transfer lemma already proved in sharp-fractional-holder.tex.

Normalize these finite endpoints by t_T as in (13). This multiplies
their correction by t_T^(-alpha)>=1 and preserves the strict norm bound
in (13). Hence the supremum over FINITE pairs in C_(R,1) is at least
the right side of (15). Let T->infinity for each fixed s first, using
scalar monotone convergence, then use (16)-(18). The result is

  liminf_(s down to 0)s^2 H_(R,1)(s)>=1/(2pi^2 alpha).  (19)

All lower examples have positive rank-one E. Direct sums of r copies
preserve endpoint norm <=R and total update operator norm one, have
rank r, and add all power traces. Combining (11),(19) proves (4) when
R>1.

4. WHY THE MOMENT LOWER GIVES THE SHARP COUNT LOWER
=================================================
A moment asymptotic for one fixed operator would not suffice. The
applicable result is the uniform supremal-count lemma lem:deficit in
sharp-fractional-holder.tex. For completeness its mechanism is as follows.

For a norm-at-most-one family with uniform count upper
N_Delta(L)<=cL^2+C L+K, choose approximate power-trace maximizers
Delta_s. Put n_s(x)=s^2 N_(Delta_s)(x/s). Layer cake gives
int_0^infinity exp(-x)n_s(x)dx ->2c whenever the supremal power trace
satisfies s^2 H_s->2c. Also n_s(x)<=cx^2+Csx+Ks^2.

If at a fixed x0>0 one had n_s(x0)<=cx0^2-delta along a subsequence,
monotonicity of n_s would keep a positive deficit against cx^2 on an
interval immediately to the left of x0. The uniform quadratic upper
permits only o(1) weighted excess elsewhere. This contradicts the
integral limit. Thus liminf n_s(x0)>=cx0^2. Taking x0=1 and s=1/L
proves the lower count asymptotic. The upper already follows from (10).

Use c=r/(4pi^2 alpha) and the norm bound and moment limit proved above.
This proves (3) for every fixed R>1. This is a supremum over finite
examples depending on L, not a count asymptotic of a fixed matrix pair.

5. THE CRITICAL BOUND R=1 REMOVES ONE PERTURBATION-RANK CHANNEL
============================================================
Let 0<=A,B<=I and ||E||=1. Since E is Hermitian, it has at least one
eigenvalue +1 or -1. If Ew=w with ||w||=1, then

  1=<w,Bw>-<w,Aw>,  0<=<w,Aw>,<w,Bw><=1.

Equality forces Aw=0 and Bw=w. Similarly Ew=-w forces Aw=w,Bw=0.
These are vector identities, not only Rayleigh quotients, since A and
I-B (or I-A and B) are positive semidefinite. Every saturated eigenvector
therefore belongs to a common reducing subspace of A,B. On it the
fractional correction has singular value exactly one.

Let t>=1 be the total multiplicity of the +1 and -1 eigenvalues of E.
The residual pair on their orthogonal complement remains in [0,I],
and its perturbation has rank at most r-t. The residual perturbation
need not have norm one, but the absolute scalar-error/rational-rank
argument (6)-(9) does not need that normalization. Consequently

  N_Delta(L)<=t+(r-t)q(L)
            <=r+(r-1)q(L),                            (20)

where q(L)=L^2/(4pi^2 alpha)+O_(alpha)(L+1). Layer cake gives the
corresponding upper moment constant (r-1)/(2pi^2 alpha).

For the matching lower take r-1 positive rank-one witnesses from the
already proved R=2 class, scale both endpoints in every block by 1/2,
and append one scalar block (A,B)=(0,1). All endpoints now lie in [0,I].
The total positive update has norm one and rank r. Its correction has
one singular value one, and the other power traces are multiplied by
2^(-alpha s), which tends to one as s->0. This proves the lower in
(4) at R=1. The uniform-count mechanism of Section 4 gives (3) for
r>=2. For r=1 the reducing-subspace argument itself gives the exact
rank-one conclusion stated in Section 1.

The entire boundary argument applies to arbitrary signed Hermitian E.
The matching lower examples are positive. It would be incorrect to
claim that the full rank-r critical class collapses to rank r in output:
only its saturated directions decouple, while r-1 nonsaturated update
channels can still have unbounded fractional-correction rank as the
ambient dimension grows.

6. CONNECTED MASS-WEIGHTED PATHS RETAIN THE R>1 CONSTANT
======================================================
Fix R>1 and r=1 first. The exact path realization in
graph-path-realization.txt approximates any finite PSD positive rank-one
update by a single existing-edge conductance increase on a connected
path with arbitrary fixed positive vertex masses. It preserves update
norm exactly one. We must additionally protect the endpoint bound.

This can be done for EVERY bounded-endpoint positive rank-one witness.
If B=A+ww^T, ||w||=1, and ||A||,||B||<=R, replace A by lambda A and
B by lambda A+ww^T for 0<lambda<1. The update is unchanged and

  ||lambda A||<=lambda R<R,
  ||lambda A+ww^T||
      =||lambda B+(1-lambda)ww^T||
      <=lambda R+(1-lambda)<R.                         (21)

The correction approaches the original as lambda->1. Every strict
singular count therefore survives at some lambda<1 with an endpoint
norm margin. The finite-ground-mass path realization then approaches
that pair in norm, so large enough finite ground mass preserves BOTH
the strict count and the bound <=R. Thus at EVERY eps>0 the supremal
strict count for positive rank-one matrix pairs with (1) equals that
for one-edge increases on connected mass-weighted paths obeying (1).
Rank-one signed PSD pairs reduce to positive ones by interchanging
the two endpoints, so this is also the entire rank-one matrix count.

For fixed r, take r such path witnesses in disjoint blocks and connect
the paths by arbitrarily weak identical endpoint edges before and
after the update. Start with a strict margin below R, as in (13) or
(21). Norm continuity preserves that margin for sufficiently weak
positive bridges. The perturbation remains block diagonal with norm
one and rank r, and all strict counts survive. The generic upper (10)
then gives the exact coefficient r/(4pi^2 alpha) already on ONE connected
mass-weighted path with r edge conductance increases.

The R=1 graph boundary has a different structural qualification. A
connected host with a positive graph update cannot meet the normalized
constraints 0<=A,B<=I and ||E||=1. Its saturated update vector would
have to lie in ker A by Section 5, whereas a connected fixed-mass graph
has ker A=span(M^(1/2)1), and every edge update annihilates that vector.
Thus this positive connected-host class is empty at R=1. No connected
path lower at R=1 is claimed. Disconnected graph classes may exhibit
the matrix boundary phenomenon, but that realization is not needed here.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ADD
================================
The exact matrix coefficient at fixed endpoint-to-update ratio loses the
1/(1-alpha) contribution from the unrestricted high-energy spectral tail.
The lower proof shows that the remaining low-energy branch tail already
forces its entire coefficient. At the exact critical ratio R=1 a unit
eigenvalue of the perturbation saturates the endpoint order interval,
removes at least one update-rank channel, and produces the r-1 law.
Both phenomena survive arbitrary matrix dimension in their stated form.

Stahl's rational approximation rate, rational matrix-function low-rank
exactness, the Cauchy/convolution input, and the graph path realization
are credited ingredients. The specific constants, norm-ratio restriction,
and critical-rank transition are the conclusions being deduced here.
Bounded searching has not established historical priority. This note
does not assert a constructive sparse algorithm attaining the improved
constant, because the chosen best rational approximants have not been
given a charged pole/coefficient realization in this note. It proves no
general quadratic matrix multiplication theorem.

No epsilon-dependent crossover such as R-1=eps^tau is claimed. The
fixed-R proofs use margins and constants that may depend on R, and
cannot be made uniform across that boundary without additional work.
