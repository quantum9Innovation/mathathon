Exact leading ambiguity for all fixed two-community degrees

1. STATEMENT
Fix integers a>=0,b>=1,D=a+b>=3. On two prescribed N-vertex classes,
sample independent uniform simple internal a-regular graphs and a
uniform simple b-regular bipartite cross graph. N ranges through every
admissible size, including aN even. Let K count valid unordered equitable
(a,b) bisections. Put delta=a-b and

  q=delta+1 if delta>=0; q=-delta if delta<0;
  nu=(D-2)q.

Then, with C as below,

  P(K>=2)~C N^(-nu),       P(K>=3)=O(N^(-nu-1)).       (A1)

For delta>=0,

 C= ((bq)!)^2 (a!)^(2q)
       /[(q!)^2(b!)^(2q)a^(q delta)].                 (A2)

For delta<0,

 C= ((aq)!)^2 (b!)^(2q)
       /[(q!)^2(a!)^(2q)b^(q^2)].                    (A3)

Formula (A3) applies directly at a=0, with 0!=1. Under a uniform hidden
balanced unordered planted partition, Bayes exact-recovery error is
asymptotic to (C/2)N^(-nu).

The new endpoint nu=1 occurs at (a,b)=(1,2). The second-moment comparison
needs only nu>=1, which holds throughout the theorem.

2. FLIP EQUATIONS AND MINIMUM SUPPORT
Let sigma be the planted sign vector. Every valid sign tau obeys
A tau=delta tau. Conversely this equation for a sign vector gives the
required local degrees and, since D-delta=2b>0, forces balance by taking
inner product with the all-ones vector. Thus an alternate has k flipped
vertices in each planted class. Set

  w=(sigma-tau)/2=1_(S+)-1_(S-),  |S+|=|S-|=k.

Then Aw=delta w. Choose the global orientation of tau so k<=N/2.
At a positive support vertex,

  delta=within-S+ neighbors - cross-S- neighbors.       (A4)

If delta>=0, simplicity gives within-S+<=k-1, so k>=delta+1=q.
Equality k=q forces two internal K_q cliques and no cross support edges.
If delta<0, write s=-delta=q. Equation (A4) gives -s>=-k, so k>=s.
Equality forces no internal support edges and all cross edges K_(s,s).
Both conclusions hold identically on the other support class.

Define L=b in the first regime and L=a in the second. At k=q each
support vertex has L prescribed internal and L prescribed cross edges
to vertices outside S+ union S-. Every outside vertex has equally many
neighbors of the two support signs. Calling this number m_v, on each
old side sum m_v=Lq. Thus at most Lq outside vertices are affected on
each side. A minimum flip is leading exactly when all positive m_v=1.

3. NONMINIMAL SUPPORTS ARE LOWER ORDER: SMALL SUPPORT
This step is independent of the sign of delta, and uses real integer
sign equations even at delta=0. For a fixed support S, |S|=h, every
outside vertex incident to S must have at least two incident support
edges, since one nonzero summand cannot cancel. Expose every edge
incident to S in the colored configuration model, skipping already
paired support stubs. If e edges are internal to S and B leave it,
the process has e+B steps and at most B/2 first-hit outside vertices.
Hence at least e+B/2=Dh/2 steps hit S or a previously hit outside vertex.
When h<=epsilon N, each such conditional collision probability is at
most C_0 h/N: every nonempty typed pool still has Theta(N) stubs.
An internal degree-zero pool is simply absent. The assertion is equally
valid for internal degree one. Padding the exposure indices to Dh gives

  P(S has no unique outside neighbor)
       <=2^(Dh)(C_0 h/N)^(Dh/2).

Union bounding over supports yields

  P(exists such support of size h)
       <=[A(h/N)^((D-2)/2)]^h.                        (A5)

For sufficiently small fixed epsilon, consecutive terms have ratio
bounded below one. Nonminimal flips have h=2k>=2q+2, and summing (A5)
through epsilon N gives

  O(N^(-(D-2)(q+1)))=O(N^(-nu-(D-2))).                (A6)

Conditioning simplicity costs only a positive constant, proved below.
Minimum flip sizes were invoked only in the simple model.

4. MACROSCOPIC FLIPS: DIRECT TWO-CLASS ENTROPY PROOF
This independent proof does not require a general common-frame theorem.
Use the relative signs t_v=tau_v/sigma_v. Their minus fraction in each
old class is r=k/N. At every vertex, the ordered internal labels Y_i
and cross labels Z_j satisfy

  sum_(i=1)^a Y_i - sum_(j=1)^b Z_j = delta X,

where X is its root label. An exact configuration count using ordered
star types has per-N entropy exponent

  Phi=H(star+)+H(star-)
       -(a/2)[H(internal_pair+)+H(internal_pair-)]
       -b H(cross_pair).                              (A7)

The root-label multinomials are included in H(star). Uniform factorial
bounds leave polynomial prefactors; finitely many star labels give
polynomially many types. Symmetrize within the internal slots and within
the cross slots. Pair laws are unchanged and star entropy increases,
so an upper bound for symmetric stars suffices. Write I+,I- for the
internal-pair mutual informations and I_c for the cross-pair mutual
information. All root marginals have minus fraction r.

Case a>=2. Omit one internal sign W from each star; it is determined
by the root and retained signs. If J+,J- are retained conditional total
correlations given the root, the exact entropy identity is

  L_0=-Phi=((a-2)/2)(I++I-)+b I_c+J++J-.               (A8)

For the plus retained law P, compare with Q obtained by keeping the
actual root law, independently drawing all retained internal slots from
their actual conditional one-slot law, and drawing every cross slot
independently from the opposite root marginal. Then

  KL(P||Q)=J++b I_c<=L_0.

Let m(X) be the actual conditional mean of the omitted internal sign.
Under P, E(W-m(X))^2=E Var(W|X). Under Q, conditional independence
shows this expectation is at least

  (a-1)E Var(W|X)+b Var(opposite root),

plus a nonnegative conditional-mean square. Since a-1>=1, its increase
is at least 4b r(1-r). The squared function is bounded by
4 max(a,b)^2, because |W|<=|delta|+(a-1)+b=2max(a,b)-1.
Pinsker therefore gives

  Phi<=-2b^2/max(a,b)^4 * r^2(1-r)^2.                 (A9)

The replacement max(a,b) is essential when extending the old a>b range
bound to a<b. The entropy identity itself is unchanged.

Case a=1, necessarily b>=2. Instead omit one cross sign W. Now

  L_0=-Phi=(a/2)(I++I-)+(b-2)I_c+J++J-.               (A10)

Compare P with Q that independently draws retained cross slots from
their actual conditional laws, and its a internal slots from the
unconditional same-class root law. Then

  KL(P||Q)=J++a I+<=2L_0.

Writing m(X) for the omitted cross mean, the same variance calculation
gives increase at least a Var(same-class root)=4a r(1-r), since b-1>=1.
Here |W|<=a+(b-1)+|delta|=2b-1, so the squared range is 4b^2. Pinsker
and KL<=2L_0 yield

  Phi<=-a^2/b^4 * r^2(1-r)^2.                         (A11)

Case a=0, b>=3. The extremal equation sum_j Z_j=bX forces every
cross neighbor to have the root's relative label. In the configuration
model, the exact expected number of flips of size k is

  binom(N,k)^2 / binom(bN,bk).                         (A12)

Its exponent is Phi=-(b-2)H(r), strictly negative away from r=0,1.

Thus every regime has a uniform negative entropy gap for r in any fixed
compact subinterval of (0,1). Polynomial type counts and prefactors in
(A7), or direct Stirling in (A12), prove exponentially small expected
macro alternatives. Together with (A6), the probability of ANY least-
oriented alternative with k>=q+1 is O(N^(-nu-1)).

5. BOUNDARY COLLISIONS
At minimum support, every edge incident to S is fixed by the core and
the remaining 4Lq boundary edges. The edge count is always

  e_0=q(D-2L)+4Lq=q(D+2L).

Leading patterns have v_0=2q+2Lq and excess e_0-v_0=nu. A nonleading
pattern loses at least one outside vertex while retaining e_0 edges.
There are finitely many possible simple typed patterns for fixed a,b.
Each has O(N^v) placements and probability O(N^(-e_0)) by finite stub
assignment counting and bounded simplicity conditioning. Their expected
total count is therefore O(N^(-nu-1)).

For clarity, collisions are impossible for delta=0 (q=1). In the
disassortative regime m_v<=min(a,q), so they are also impossible if a<=1
or q=1. When a>=2,q>=2 a single one-side m=2 collision is feasible:
merge two leading outside vertices with distinct support parents of
both signs. This uses two internal and two cross neighbors, respecting
all capacities. Its excess is exactly nu+1. No invalid repeated graph
edges are included by this argument.

6. EXACT LEADING COUNT, INCLUDING a=0/1
Choose S+ and S- of size q, W+ and W- of size Lq disjoint from their
corresponding S sets, and four boundary functions, each having exactly
L preimages at every support vertex. The number of data choices is

 A_N=binom(N,q)^2 binom(N-q,Lq)^2
                [(Lq)!/(L!)^q]^4.                    (A13)

The domains and targets are labeled sets, so there is no extra q! or
automorphism factor. A leading flip set uniquely determines these data
whenever it occurs. Conversely the complete motif saturates every
support vertex and guarantees its flip equation; every W exchanges one
internal and one cross support neighbor. Extra W-to-W edges are irrelevant.

When L>0, a fixed pattern has physical stub numerator

  c_H=(a! b!)^(2q) (ab)^(2Lq).                        (A14)

Support vertices prescribe all a internal and b cross neighbors. Every
W prescribes one of each type. Distinct physical assignments are disjoint:
each prescribed edge touches a saturated support vertex, so a completed
configuration cannot contain an additional parallel copy while retaining
all the other distinct required neighbors. At a=0, L=0, omit the internal
factor entirely and use c_H=(b!)^(2b), avoiding 0^0.

For delta>=0, prescribed internal edges per block number
q delta/2+Lq, and prescribed cross edges number 2Lq. For delta<0,
these numbers are respectively Lq and q^2+2Lq=qD. The exact probability
of any specified physical assignment is the product of the corresponding
internal odd-reciprocal products and cross falling-factorial reciprocal.
Consequently its asymptotic after multiplying (A14) is

  (a! b!)^(2q) a^(-q delta) N^(-e_0), delta>=0;
  (a! b!)^(2q) b^(-q^2) N^(-e_0),     delta<0.        (A15)

The second line remains valid at a=0 by the direct calculation above.

7. EXACT SIMPLE-CONDITIONING RATIO AND PARITY
After prescribing a leading assignment, support residual degrees are
zero; W residual degrees are (a-1,b-1); other vertices retain (a,b).
Every fixed edge has a zero-residual-degree endpoint. Hence full
simplicity is exactly residual-factor simplicity, with no additional
forbidden-pair condition.

For delta>=0, residual internal total per block is aN-qD, and residual
cross total per old side is bN-2Lq. Here qD=delta(delta+1)+2b(delta+1)
is even. For delta<0, these totals are respectively

  aN-2aq,       bN-qD.                                (A16)

The first is even at every admissible N. At a=0 internal pools are
absent; at a=1 the residual internal graph is a matching and automatically
simple. No extra even-N assumption is introduced beyond aN even.

In every nonempty degree-a internal pool with a>=1, original simplicity
has limiting probability exp[-(a^2-1)/4]. A bounded-degree configuration
with these finite modifications has the same limit: loop and unordered
double-edge counts have means tending to (a-1)/2 and (a-1)^2/4, and
their joint factorial moments tend to the independent Poisson moments.
Overlapping bad structures contribute O(1/N); the regular bulk gives the
same disjoint-structure limits after any finite modification. The standard
factorial-moment/sieve conclusion gives the same zero-event probability.
For a=0 the simplicity factor is 1, not exp(+1/4).

The cross degree-b factor similarly has no loops and unordered parallel-
pair mean (b-1)^2/2, with Poisson factorial moments unchanged by finite
modification. Its simplicity limit is exp[-(b-1)^2/2]. Thus original and
residual joint simplicity probabilities converge to the SAME positive
limit, uniformly over leading data and physical assignments (their
residual degree sequences are identical up to labels). Their ratio tends
to one. Formula (A15) is therefore also the exact uniform-simple leading
probability, multiplied by 1+o(1).

Multiplying (A13) and (A15) proves E X~C N^(-nu), with (A2),(A3), where
X counts leading flip sets, or equivalently leading alternatives. For
N>2q opposite orientations are not double-counted.

8. FACTORIAL SECOND MOMENT AND BAYES ERROR
Every leading motif is connected with minimum degree at least two:
its support vertices have degree D>2 and W vertices degree two. The
delta=0 case is K_(2,2a), a>=2. The a=0 case is K_(b,b), b>=3, with no
W. Thus support vertices are precisely the motif-degree-D vertices.

For a connected minimum-degree-two graph H, every proper subgraph F
with nonempty vertex set has e(F)-v(F)<=e(H)-v(H)-1. To prove this,
removing r>0 vertices removes E+B edges where 2E+B>=2r and connectedness
gives B>0, hence E+B>=r+1. A spanning proper subgraph removes an edge.
Include isolated common vertices in F.

Distinct leading flip sets cannot have identical motif images because
their degree-D vertices would identify the same support. For each of
finitely many proper overlap types, the union has excess at least nu+1;
finite prescribed-stub bounds give total contribution O(N^(-nu-1)).
Disjoint pairs contribute O(N^(-2nu))=O(N^(-nu-1)), since nu>=1. Same-set
different-data pairs cannot co-occur and diagonal pairs are excluded by
X(X-1). Therefore

  E[X(X-1)]=O(N^(-nu-1)),
  P(X>=1)=E X+O(N^(-nu-1)), P(X>=2)=O(N^(-nu-1)).

Outside the lower-order collision and nonminimal events, K=1+X. This
proves (A1). Finally every fixed planted partition gives equal likelihood
1/[G_a(N)^2 B_b(N)] to every graph compatible with it, taking G_0(N)=1.
The posterior under the uniform hidden unordered prior is exactly uniform
over the K valid partitions. Hence Bayes error is E[1-1/K]. Its difference
from P(K>=2)/2 is between zero and P(K>=3)/2, proving the leading C/2.

9. CHECKS AND AUDIT CONCLUSION
(a,b)=(1,2): q=1, nu=1, C=2; core plus boundary is a diamond.
(a,b)=(1,3): q=2, nu=4, C=16.
(a,b)=(2,2): q=1, nu=2, C=4.
(a,b)=(0,b), b>=3: C=(b!)^(2b-2)/b^(b^2), nu=b(b-2),
the isolated K_(b,b) component count.

No counterexample or error in the proposed leading constants was found.
Necessary presentation corrections are the nu>=1 endpoint, omission of
the nonexistent a=0 internal simplicity factor, and correct empty-collision
cases. All old/frozen files were left untouched.
