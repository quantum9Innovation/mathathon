Higher-order / multiway frontier review -- 2026-11-15
===================================================

All mathematical claims below are attributed to primary papers. This is a
targeted source review, not a certification of the entire literature.

1. CURRENT EXACT-k PREPRINT: Yunpeng Li, A sharp higher-order Cheeger
   inequality, arXiv:2609.35369v2, revised October 5, 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35369v2
   https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35369v2

   Theorem1.1 claims phi_k <= C sqrt(lambda_k log(k+1)) for exactly k
   disjoint nonempty sets, all conductances and degrees in the original
   finite weighted graph. Sets need not cover V. Section7 uses the noisy
   hypercube to match the logarithmic dependence. v1 had only log^5, so
   search snippets can be materially stale. The proof was read through
   its geometric partition, fixed-threshold mincut improvement, adaptive
   coefficient projections, dyadic energy bound and local-eigenvalue count.
   No independent full-proof audit or publication acceptance is claimed.
   No nearly-linear runtime theorem is stated in the inspected text.

   Consequence for target selection: do not advertise either a polylog(k)
   exact-index/exact-count Cheeger bound, or the sqrt(log k) bound, as an
   untouched open problem. Improving that factor for arbitrary weighted
   graphs conflicts with the stated noisy-cube lower bound.

2. CLASSICAL BASELINE: James R. Lee, Shayan Oveis Gharan, Luca Trevisan,
   Multi-way spectral partitioning and higher-order Cheeger inequalities,
   arXiv:1111.1055v6 (2014 version).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.1055v6

   General phi_k lies between lambda_k/2 and O(k^2)sqrt(lambda_k).
   The same paper proves logarithmic bounds after relaxing the spectral
   index or number of output sets; its small-set noisy-hypercube example
   underlies the sqrt(log k) obstruction. Do not mix lambda_k with
   lambda_2k or k disjoint sets with a constant fraction of k.

3. BUFFERS ARE A DIFFERENT OBJECTIVE: Konstantin Makarychev, Yury
   Makarychev, Liren Shan, Aravindan Vijayaraghavan, Higher-Order Cheeger
   Inequality for Partitioning with Buffers, arXiv:2308.10160, SODA2024.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10160
   https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977912.80

   The constructive buffered objective has upper bound
   O_delta(log(k)/epsilon)*lambda_floor((1+delta)k), with each buffer
   at most epsilon times its component size. Removing a square-root loss
   here does not remove it for ordinary edge conductance: buffer-boundary
   edges are excluded, and both the allowed buffers and index matter.
   The paper also treats arbitrary vertex weights/edge costs. Its text
   assumes exact spectral data rather than resolving numerical precision.

4. CURRENT FULLY DYNAMIC COMPETITOR: Ben Jourdan, Peter Macgregor,
   Gregory Schwartzman, Fully Dynamic Coreset Spectral Clustering,
   ICML2026, PMLR306:54725--54740.
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/jourdan26a.html
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/jourdan26a/jourdan26a.pdf

   Formal Theorem6 states a result for arbitrary dynamic weighted graphs, insertion
   and deletion, with O(log n) worst-case edge update. Queries label Y
   with alpha(1+epsilon)/(1-epsilon) consistency in time
   O(k*dmax^2*log n + log n*log* n*k^2*epsilon^-4
     + T_NC(epsilon^-4*k^2,k) + epsilon^-8*k^4
     + min{|Y|*k^2*epsilon^-4, vol(Y)}).
   Success is high constant probability. Consistency means the returned
   labels extend to some approximate global normalized-cut partition;
   this does not assert one persistent global labeling across queries.
   No well-clustering/eigengap assumption appears in that theorem.

   A separate algebraic scope audit in the sibling literature directory
   shows that multiplicative kernel-k-means approximation alone does not
   transfer to multiplicative average conductance after the additive
   diagonal shift; see NORMALIZED_CUT_SHIFT_AUDIT.md. The statement above
   is the paper's claim, not our certification of its objective conversion.
   That audit does not show an actual run of the published algorithm fails.

   Do not claim polylog edge update for dynamic clustering alone as new.
   The degree-dependent query term and persistent-label/recourse scope
   are meaningful comparison axes; fractional response construction in
   this repository still costs nearly-linear work in the graph per edit.

5. CLOSEST CLUSTER-REFINEMENT COMPARISON INSPECTED: Shayan Oveis Gharan,
   Luca Trevisan, Partitioning into Expanders, arXiv:1309.3223v3, SODA2014.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.3223
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.3223

   Theorem1.7 turns a gap between rho(k+1) and rho(k) into a clustering
   with inside/outside expansion guarantees; the partition variant loses
   factors of k. Lemma1.13 bounds some INDUCED subgraph lambda2 by
   O(k^6 lambda_k) for a partition into at most k-1 parts. Our separate
   pointwise-leakage lemma uses degree-preserving local masses instead:
   a block comparison gives local lambda2<=lambda_k directly, but the
   missing step is controlling global boundary conductance of its split.
   This paper alone did not settle the average-leakage one-edge target;
   the later Lau draft in item7 below DOES subsume its disjoint-set form.

6. STABILITY IS ALREADY A LITERATURE: Pan Peng, Yuichi Yoshida,
   Average Sensitivity of Spectral Clustering, arXiv:2006.04094, KDD2020.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04094

   Studies expected symmetric difference of output clusters under random
   edge removal, including k-way clustering. Its stated two-way bound
   depends on lambda2/lambda3^2. This is an average/random-deletion and
   spectral-gap regime, distinct from arbitrary single-edit, gap-free
   approximation guarantees or worst-case low recourse.

7. CANDIDATE TARGET SUBSUMED BY CURRENT PRIMARY SOURCE: Lap Chi Lau,
   Algorithmic Spectral Graph Theory, draft September16 2026, §7.7
   pp99--104, Lemmas7.18--7.21 and Algorithm5.
   https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~lapchi/papers/ASGT.pdf

   Core trimming plus outside search preserves a constant fraction of
   every low-energy function on the cores. For k−1 inherited sparse sets,
   local eigenvalue counting then finds one core with local lambda2 at
   most 8lambda_k. Its two-way Cheeger split yields exactly k disjoint
   sets with conductance at most max(h,4sqrt(2lambda_k))+8sqrt(lambda_k).
   The weighted transcription replaces regular vertex masses with original
   degree volumes. The specific average-leakage disjoint-set target first
   proposed in this session is therefore a known-method specialization.
   It is withdrawn as a frontier claim. Trimming does not itself ensure
   that the sets cover all vertices or give a nearly-linear running time.

Candidate status after primary-source correction
-----------------------------------------------
See inherited-repair-target.tex. The disjoint-set average-leakage problem
is SUBSUMED by item7 and withdrawn as a frontier. A full covering partition
with C*(h+sqrt(lambda_k(G'))) and explicit nearly-linear rounding work is
a narrower unverified candidate; further completion-theorem comparisons
are required before declaring it open. The pointwise-leakage full-cover
special case is proved here by classical block minmax plus Cheeger.
The degree-aware fractional logdet certificate is proved separately;
it does not by itself supply a partitioning algorithm.
