1. TWO-CLASS THEOREMS RETAIN THE FULL CORRECT PARAMETER SCOPE
-----------------------------------------------------------
Sections 09 and 10 explicitly assume fixed a>=0,b>=1,D=a+b>=3, independent
uniform simple factors, absent degree-zero factors, and all admissible
block sizes with aN even. They do not accidentally retain an assortative
or even-N restriction. The signs are balanced because D-delta=2b>0.

The integrated piecewise m, nu, and C formulas agree exactly with 67ce:
 nonnegative delta: m=delta+1, nu=(D-2)m,
 C=((bm)!)^2(a!)^(2m)/[(m!)^2(b!)^(2m)a^(m delta)];
 negative delta: m=b-a, nu=(D-2)m,
 C=((am)!)^2(b!)^(2m)/[(m!)^2(a!)^(2m)b^(m^2)].
The formulas, sample values (1,2), (1,3), a=b, a=0, and (2,1) are
consistent. N always denotes block size; no factor 2^nu is lost.

The integrated proof preserves the crucial arguments: simple-model
minimum support; all larger supports at error O(N^(-nu-1)); exterior
collision loss of one power; four maps counted as functions on labeled
sets; disjoint physical assignments through saturated support stubs;
residual/original simplicity ratio tending to one; proper-intersection
excess; and counting flip sets rather than motif drawings. Thus it does
not introduce an orientation or automorphism factor. The exponent-one
case (1,2) correctly permits a disjoint-pair O(N^-2) contribution.

The a=0 residual omission is direct, avoiding 0^0 and a false exp(1/2)
internal simplicity contribution. At a=1 internal factors are matchings,
and the residual parity is correct. At delta=0 the nonadjacent false-twin
motif is K_(2,2a), not the disassortative support core. An O(1/N) rate for
the simplicity ratio is not needed for P(K>=3)=O(N^(-nu-1)).
