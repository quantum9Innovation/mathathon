Unknown-frame recovery in sparse block-regular graphs
===================================================
Final manuscript packet, November 15, 2026.

MAIN GUARANTEE
  An unknown fixed bounded-size degree frame, its class sizes, and its
  planted classes can be recovered with probability tending to one from
  the graph and fixed class-count/degree bounds alone. The model consists
  of independent uniform simple internal regular and cross biregular
  factors, with fixed positive balanced class proportions and connected
  type support. Its sufficient certificate is
    q_ij=q_ji, 0<=q_ij<1/2, sum_j d_ij q_ij=1.
  Zero weights are permitted; the algorithm is not given the weights.

  The plant is the unique finest equitable partition with at most the
  supplied constant K classes. Certified complete listing takes
  unconditional expected nearly quadratic bit work, expected nearly
  linear workspace, and expected linear output size. These are fixed-
  parameter asymptotic bounds, not worst-case quadratic or benchmark
  claims. The equations require exact neighbor counts and assume no noise.

FURTHER RESULTS IN THE SAME PAPER
  - Every fixed connected simple regular base of degree at least three:
    unknown-fiber recovery and disjoint old/new spectra, all lift sizes.
  - All fixed two-class a>=0,b>=1,a+b>=3: explicit leading ambiguity and
    uniform-hidden-prior Bayes error constants, including the cubic case
    with ambiguity ~4/N^2 and optimal error ~2/N^2 (N is one block size).
  - Complete-base sharp ambiguity; exact cycle-lift recovery obstruction.
  - A nonregular K_(3,12) lift has exponentially many alternative fiber
    partitions: minimum degree three alone is insufficient.
  - Separate prescribed-cross eigenspace/recovery results under their
    own internal-degree hypotheses; explicit forced-zero scope limits.

STATUS AND LIMITS
  The manuscript and companion received separate internal full-source
  mathematical and scope reviews. Finite checks supplement the proofs;
  they are not formal verification, external peer review, or a novelty
  certificate. The code checks selected identities and certified algebra
  primitives; it is not a production implementation of the entire search.
  Gaussian fiber recovery has direct 1993/2023 precedents credited in the
  paper. Historical firstness is not certified. No noise stability,
  growing-parameter uniformity, numerical spectral gap, or practical
  speed claim is made. This paper does not prove Bordenave Proposition 28.

Earlier 22-page sources and their audit are retained as historical records
in baseline-22-page-source.zip and provenance/earlier-22-page-proof-audit.txt.
They are superseded by this edition. Further breakthrough work is stopped.
