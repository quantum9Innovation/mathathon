The exact identity behind the comparison is

$$
Q_k=\frac{1}{(N-k)!}\int_0^\infty e^{-s}s^{N-k}(N-s)^k\,ds.
$$

Splitting at s=N gives two positive integrals. Even orders add them and odd orders subtract them. The classical Whittaker representation is therefore relevant, and its odd cancellation can be quantified. No claim that negative Laguerre parameters automatically exclude existing uniform asymptotics survives this check.
