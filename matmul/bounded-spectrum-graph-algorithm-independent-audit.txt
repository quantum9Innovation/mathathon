1. Normalization and exact decomposition
---------------------------------------
The sparse positive update satisfies ||E||<=a=||E||_F<=sqrt(k)||E|| and
tr(E)/a<=sqrt(k), independently of vertex masses. T=R*a therefore safely
bounds both endpoint norms. The finite geometric expansion of each
resolvent with C_A=I-A/T and C_B=I-B/T is exact. The constant terms
cancel. Integrating every remaining finite term gives beta_j from the
ordinary positive binomial recurrence, with no gamma evaluation.

The central collection identity is correct, including powers of T.
Quadrature of the remainder equals a sum of q ORIGINAL resolvent
differences plus one polynomial difference. The coefficient is
beta_j-c_alpha*sum_nu a_nu*s_nu^(alpha+1)/(1+s_nu)^(j+1).
Telescoping and the displayed forward/backward recurrences factor that
polynomial at width k(p-1). Hence total width k(q+p-1), with no factor-p
loss on each quadrature node. Noncommutativity does not change this.

2. The uniform quadrature argument
----------------------------------
The remainder is the difference of two geometric remainders. On a strip
of half-width d<pi its normalized envelope has slopes alpha and p-alpha,
and amplitude bounded by C_alpha*cos(d/2)^(-(p+1)). This follows directly
from the sector bound and ||C_A||,||C_B||<=1, not from a bound on a
noncommuting matrix product at complex arguments. Real logarithmic
derivatives grow at most linearly in p.

For the least dyadic p with p^8>=L and ell=L^(3/4), the logarithm of the
complete Gaussian boundary factor is O(p log(L+1)+p^2)=o(ell). This
includes the Lipschitz convolution factor exp(p^2/4). It is absorbed
by the omitted-window and trapezoid exponential margins. The parameter
p is not silently held fixed in this step.

Root independently checked the variable-slope node count. On each
linear half-line the interval-minimum Riemann loss is O(L*ell), with
at most O(p*ell^2) extra loss at its boundary interval. Both fit the
claimed O(L^(7/4)) remainder under p<2L^(1/8). Window enlargement,
integer rounding and the moving strip contribute no larger remainder.
The positive-part area is exactly L^2(1/alpha+1/(p-alpha))/2. Finally
L^2/(p-alpha)=O(L^(15/8)), proving the stated leading coefficient.

The retained nodes have |log s|=O_alpha(L), and total quadrature weight
is O_alpha(L), uniformly in p. No fixed-slope asymptotic theorem is
substituted without controlling its parameter dependence.

----------------------------------

factors use sparse normalized graph actions only. All node and polynomial
factor entries, O(pq) scalar compensation work and polynomial-logarithmic
scalar construction fit expected ~O_(alpha,R)((M+N)k). The same existing
median-energy-distance amplification supplies simultaneous solve accuracy.
No common linear approximate solver map or deterministic runtime cap is
required. Dense expansion and exact symmetrization costs are separated.
