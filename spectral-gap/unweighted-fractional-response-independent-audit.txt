1. Fractional compression lemma
---------------------------------
Reconstructed the compression inverse inequality by completing J_s to an
orthogonal basis and taking a Schur complement. Its direction is correct:
 J_s^T(A_s+tI)^-1 J_s >= (J_s^T A_s J_s+tI)^-1.
Subtracting t times this inequality from I gives
 0 <= J_s^T A_s(A_s+tI)^-1 J_s
    <= (J_s^T A_s J_s)(J_s^T A_s J_s+tI)^-1
    <= min(1,M/t) I.
The bound t^(alpha-1) min(1,M/t) is integrable precisely for 0<alpha<1.
The assumed resolvent convergence therefore passes through the fractional
integral by norm dominated convergence. Dimensions of A_s may grow, because
all compressed integrands live in a fixed d-dimensional matrix space.
No bound on the full norm of A_s or its fractional power is assumed or
needed. This correctly resolves the potentially dangerous fast-mode issue.

2. Graph, parity, simplicity, and the resistor simulation
---------------------------------------------------------
An unweighted network of 2p disjoint internal paths of length 2q has
conductance p/q. All its paths have even length, so its terminals have the
same bipartite color. For q=1 its distinct new internal vertices prevent
parallel edges. Every gadget is fixed in size after the finite rational
conductance approximation has been chosen.

Each mass cell K_(m_i,m_i) is simple, unweighted, connected and bipartite.
Its distinguished port can have either global color. On the macro path,
choose all ports on each side of the distinguished edge to have the same
color, and choose opposite colors across that edge. Then all even-path
resistor gadgets and the odd distinguished connector are globally compatible.
All gadget vertices are new and internally disjoint, so the physical host
remains simple. Single-port attachment of every cell is essential and is
explicit in the construction. The macro quotient being a path guarantees
that deleting the distinguished connector separates its two sides.

3. Two-stage Schur complement, with growing fast dimension
-----------------------------------------------------------
Let n_i=2m_i~sM_i. On the orthogonal decomposition into cell constants P,
gadget coordinates Z and cell mean-zero space F, the displayed block sizes
are correct for A_s=sL_s:
 PP=R_s K_PP R_s+tI, PZ=sqrt(s) R_s K_PZ,
 ZZ=sK_ZZ+tI, PF=O(sqrt(s)), ZF=O(s), FF>=c s^2 I.
Only finitely many external edges meet ports, so all external block norms
have constants independent of the growing number of cell vertices. The
nonzero eigenvalues of a K_(m_i,m_i) cell are m_i and 2m_i. Their scaled
minimum is of order s^2, and the PSD external Laplacian cannot decrease it.

Thus eliminating F changes PP by O(1/s), PZ by O(1/sqrt(s)), and ZZ by
O(1). Gadget K_ZZ is SPD because every gadget component has terminal
Dirichlet boundary. Its perturbed scaled inverse is
 (sK_ZZ+O(1))^-1=(1/s)K_ZZ^-1+O(1/s^2).
Substituting this into the second Schur complement gives
 R_s(K_PP-K_PZ K_ZZ^-1 K_ZP)R_s+tI+O(1/s)
 ->H+tI.
There is no dimensional factor in these operator-norm estimates. If Z is
empty, only the first elimination is used. The explicit PP block is
uniformly bounded and proves the compression hypothesis. Updated external
blocks are still fixed finite matrices, so the whole argument applies to
both endpoints, including the inserted-edge construction.

4. Relative Ritz eigenvalues and the exact determinant budget
--------------------------------------------------------------
The normalized cell kernel directions have coordinates sqrt(n_i). Their
directions converge to sqrt(M_i), so isometries V_s of their orthogonal
complements can be chosen convergently in the fixed macro dimension.
The physical vectors J_s V_s are orthogonal to the full graph constant
vector, even though they vanish at gadget vertices. Thus the compressed
fractional pair is a legitimate generalized Ritz pair on the common
positive support, and converges to the desired positive-definite macro
pair. Fractional monotonicity gives every Ritz relative eigenvalue >=1.
Generalized min--max gives lambda_(s,j)>=mu_(s,j) for the leading d-1
full relative eigenvalues.

Both macro and physical bridge updates have exactly the same leverage
tau. The mass normalization does not change leverage: solving the
normalized Laplacian equation shows it equals Delta w times the ordinary
terminal effective resistance. On the common positive supports, the
rank-one determinant lemma gives precisely alpha log(1+tau) for the sum
of logarithms of ALL relative eigenvalues at both levels.

The sum of the leading Ritz logarithms converges to this entire budget.
Every leading logarithmic difference and every extra relative logarithm
is nonnegative. Their total tending to zero therefore forces each leading
full eigenvalue to converge to its macro target, and forces the largest
remaining relative eigenvalue to tend to one. This proves uniform spectral
list approximation despite increasing physical dimension. It avoids any
unsupported claim of full matrix norm convergence between different spaces.
The common scaling A_s=sL_s cancels exactly from the relative matrices.

5. Exact unit effective resistance and actual edge insertion
-------------------------------------------------------------
Three internally disjoint paths of length three have effective resistance
one. Their terminals have opposite colors, so both this connector and the
additional direct unit edge preserve bipartiteness. The direct edge is
absent in the host, and all internal vertices are disjoint, so insertion
is simple and changes exactly one edge.

The entire rest of the network attaches to this connector only at one of
its two terminals: the macro quotient is a path, and each mass cell and
every other connection uses its single port. These dangling networks
carry no current in the terminal electrical problem. Hence the finite
physical host's resistance between insertion endpoints is EXACTLY one,
independently of all cell sizes. Kron elimination gives base macro
conductance one and updated macro conductance two, as required.
Thus the physical determinant budget is exactly alpha log 2 at every
finite stage, rather than only asymptotically.

6. Assembly, strict-count formula, and limits
----------------------------------------------
The input rank-one matrix theorem and mass-path transfer preserve leverage
one. Common conductance scaling normalizes the edited macro edge to one,
and rational approximation of the other edges leaves it unchanged.
These finite-dimensional pairs have constant rank and a positive gap on
their support, so their relative spectra vary continuously. Choose this
approximation first, fix its finite gadget sizes, and only then enlarge
cells. This sequential order justifies all fixed-network constants.

The product constraint gives the sharp strict count by
 (1+epsilon)^m<2^alpha.
The largest allowable integer is max(0,ceil(alpha log2/log(1+epsilon))-1),
including integral thresholds. A target of m equal entries 2^(alpha/m)
strictly above 1+epsilon is realized to a small enough approximation margin
by the theorem, proving a matching actual finite count. Extra eigenvalues
cannot exceed the target count because of the same universal upper bound.
The arbitrary bridge-increase corollary is also correct, with the updated
graph allowed its one nonunit conductance as explicitly stated.

The theorem permits growing size, degrees and cycles of length four; it
asserts approximation, not exact arbitrary spectral-list attainment, and
supplies no finite-size convergence rate, bounded-degree/girth result,
algorithm, condition bound or stability theorem. These scope limits are
accurate. Its historical novelty remains unverified by this audit.
