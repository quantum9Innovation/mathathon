\section{Model and theorem}
Fix $K\ge1,D\ge3$. Let $Q=(d_{ij})_{i,j\in[k]}$ be a connected
nonnegative integer frame, $k\le K$, with
\[
 3\le d_i:=\sum_jd_{ij}\le D.
\]
The off-diagonal support is connected; a one-type frame is allowed.
Positive block sizes $n_i$ obey
$n_id_{ij}=n_jd_{ji}$ and $n_id_{ii}$ even, with $n=\sum_i n_i$.
Every active internal regular factor and cross biregular factor is
independent and uniform simple. Absent factors are omitted; internal
degrees zero and one are allowed. Let $J$ be the planted indicator.

Assume there are symmetric real weights $q_{ij}$ on every active factor
with
\[
 0<q_{ij}<1/2,\qquad \sum_jd_{ij}q_{ij}=1.
\]
An internal weight obeys the same strict bound. Put
\[
 q_*=2^K,\quad F_*=K+\frac{K(K+3)}2q_*^2,
 \quad B_*=Kq_*^D+F_*.
\]

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Uniformly over this finite connected-frame family and its admissible
size sequences, with probability $1-o(1)$ every equitable partition
into at most $K$ nonempty classes is an equitable coarsening of $J$.
Thus $J$ is the unique finest equitable partition within that bounded
class family.

There is an algorithm using only $(A,K,D)$ which returns $J$, up to
class names, and its frame $Q$, with probability $1-o(1)$.
The untruncated algorithm has unconditional expected bit work at most
$C_{K,D}n^{B_*+3}$, including rare inputs of large binary nullity.
It uses $O_K(n^2)$ bits of storage. A deterministic work cap gives
worst-case $O_{K,D}(n^{B_*+5})$ bit work and adds only
$O_{K,D}(n^{-2})$ abstention probability. On cap exhaustion it returns
\textnormal{\textsc{Abstain}}, not a partially enumerated maximum.
\end{theorem}

\section{Uniformity and the unconditional binary moment}
Along a path of the frame, every positive size ratio is a ratio of
positive integers at most $D$. Therefore
\[
 \alpha_i:=n_i/n\ge\rho:=\frac1{KD^{K-1}}.
\]
A connected consistent frame fixes these proportions. There are
finitely many frames with $k\le K$ and row sums at most $D$.
The strictly feasible ones form a subset of this finite family; each
may have its own fixed strict weights. Thus minimum positive constants
arising from compactness may be taken over a finite set. No lower bound
on a weight is required as an input to the algorithm.

For arbitrary $r\le K$ and arbitrary $R\in\F_2^{r\times r}$, let
\[
 h_R(A)=\dim_{\F_2}\{Y\in\F_2^{n\times r}:AY=YR\}.
\]
The weighted moment proposition gives in the configuration model
\[
 \E 2^{h_R(A)}\le(3D)^{F_*}(n+1)^{B_*}.
\]
This bound is uniform in \emph{every candidate} $R$: feasibility is
required of the true frame $Q$, not of $R$.
Each old type has at most $(2^r)^{d_i}\le q_*^D$ legal ordered stars.
The exact root-star multinomial counts root assignments already; there
is no additional root-label factor. Uniform factorial estimates give
the exponent $n\Phi$ and remainder $F_*[\log(Dn+1)+1]$. Weighted
nonpositivity $\Phi\le0$ then gives the displayed moment after summing
at most $(n+1)^{Kq_*^D}$ types.

The independent-factor simplicity probability tends to $e^{-\Lambda}$,
where absent factors contribute zero, an internal degree $a\ge1$
contributes $(a^2-1)/4$, and a cross $(b,c)$ factor contributes
$(b-1)(c-1)/2$. In particular $\Lambda\le K^2D^2$.
Eventually this probability is at least $e^{-K^2D^2}/2$, uniformly over
the finite connected-frame family. Conditioning thus gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:moment}
 \E_{\rm simple}2^{h_R(A)}
 \le 2e^{K^2D^2}(3D)^{F_*}(n+1)^{B_*}.
\end{equation}
This is an unconditional expectation under the intended simple model,
not an expectation restricted to the recovery event. Matching factors
and absent factors have simplicity probability one.

\section{Structural composition for variable degrees}
For a one-hot candidate $Y$ satisfying the original integer equation
$AY=YR$, define
\[
 \delta(Y)=\min_{T\text{ one-hot}}\frac1n
                 |\{v:Y_v\ne(JT)_v\}|.
\]
Maps $T:[k]\to[r]$ need not be onto. Strict weighted one-hot rigidity
implies $\Phi=0$ only at old-type point maps. For fixed $Q,R$, the
finite-dimensional space of compatible legal star laws is compact,
and $\delta=\sum_i\alpha_i(1-\max_a\rho_i(a))$ is continuous.
Hence for every fixed $\eta>0$ there is $c>0$ such that
$\delta\ge\eta$ implies $\Phi\le-c$. Taking the minimum over the
finite true/candidate frame families makes $c=c(K,D,\eta)>0$.
The exact factorial estimate for one-hot stars and a finite union give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:macro}
 \E\#\{\text{integer candidates with }\delta\ge\eta\}
 \le C(n+1)^{B_*}e^{-cn}.
\end{equation}
Allowing unused candidate labels in this compact set is harmless.

We give the needed small-support adjustment explicitly. Call $S$ bad
if no outside vertex has exactly one incident edge into $S$, counting
multiplicity in the configuration model. Put $s=|S|$, let $W$ be its
outside neighborhood, $w=|W|$, and let $a,b$ count its internal edges
(loops once) and its edges to $W$. Then
\[
 2a+b=\sum_{v\in S}\deg(v)\ge3s,\qquad b\ge2w,
 \qquad e:=a+b\le Ds.
\]
Consequently
\[
 e-s-w\ge (2a+b)/2-s\ge s/2.
\]
Every active factor has at least $\rho n$ stubs on either side. For
$s\le\rho n/(4D)$, the probability of any valid prescription of all
$e$ touching pairs is at most $(2/(\rho n))^e$. The same witness
enumeration as in the regular packet gives
\[
 \E\#\{\text{bad }S:|S|=s\}
 \le C_0^s(s/n)^{\lceil s/2\rceil},
\]
where $C_0=C_0(K,D)$. It does not assume equal degrees in $S$.
Choose fixed $\eta>0$ small enough that
\[
 \eta\le\rho/(4D),\qquad
 C_0e^{1/2}\eta^{1/2}\le1/2,\qquad
 (D+1)\eta\le\rho/2.
\]
Summing the bound for $s\ge2$ by consecutive ratios gives $O(n^{-1})$.
In a simple positive-degree graph a singleton is not bad. Bounded-cost
simplicity conditioning therefore gives a common event of probability
$1-O_{K,D}(n^{-1})$ on which no nonempty bad support of size at most
$\eta n$ exists.

If $AV=VR$ over any field and $V$ has nonzero row support $S$, an
outside vertex with exactly one edge into $S$ contradicts the equation.
Thus the common graph event excludes every such small support,
simultaneously for all $R$ and vector dimensions.

For an integer one-hot candidate within $\eta n$ rows of $JT$, every
old block contains a vertex whose row and all its neighbors' rows
agree with $JT$: the changed vertices and their neighbors occupy at
most $(D+1)\eta n<n_i$ vertices globally. At this clean vertex the
integer equation gives $(QT)_i=(TR)_i$. Hence $QT=TR$ and
$V=Y-JT$ satisfies $AV=VR$ over the reals. The common support event
forces $V=0$. Combined with \eqref{eq:macro} and Markov, every
candidate is an exact equitable coarsening, simultaneously over the
finite family.

\section{Algorithm, correctness and bit accounting}
Enumerate $r=1,\ldots,K$ and every nonnegative integer $r$-by-$r$
matrix $R$ of row sum at most $D$. Their total number is at most
\[
 M_*:=\sum_{r=1}^K(D+1)^{r^2}.
\]
Do not require $R$ to be connected or to admit omission weights.
The true $(k,Q)$ is included regardless of the unknown class count.
For each candidate compute, over $\F_2$, the kernel of
\[
 I_r\otimes A-R^T\otimes I_n,
\]
using column vectorization. Enumerate its vectors from a basis. Accept
only those with a standard unit row at every vertex, every candidate
class nonempty, and the \emph{integer} equality $AY=YR$. Keep an
accepted partition having maximal $r$, breaking remaining ties
arbitrarily. If none is accepted, abstain. No observation of a current
candidate is used as a premature global maximum certificate.

On the structural event, each accepted $Y=JT$ with $T$ onto, so
$r\le k$. The true planted $k$-class candidate always occurs in its
kernel enumeration. Thus the maximum is exactly $k$, and an onto map
between two $k$-element sets is a permutation. The output is $J$ up to
names; exact neighbor counts recover $Q$. This argument neither knows
$k$ in advance nor assumes simple quotient eigenvalues.

Dense binary elimination costs $O_K(n^3)$ bit operations. A loose
$O_{K,D}(n^2)$ bound per enumerated vector covers forming it, unit-row
and nonemptiness checks, and exact bounded-degree neighbor counts.
There is no field operation of uncharged precision: the field is binary,
and the integer neighbor counters are bounded by $D$. A basis, its
elimination workspace, and current/best partitions occupy $O_K(n^2)$
bits; no full list of kernel vectors is stored.
By \eqref{eq:moment}, before adding a clock the total expected bit work
is at most
\[
 C_{K,D}\left[n^3+n^2\sum_R\E2^{h_R}\right]
 \le C'_{K,D}n^{B_*+2}.
\]
The untruncated algorithm needs no clock; its displayed base-work
expectation in particular is at most $O(n^{B_*+3})$. If an uncapped
binary clock is included, its width is only guaranteed to be $O(n)$,
not $O(\log n)$: deterministically $h_R\le Kn$, so the full base
work is at most $C_{K,D}n^3 2^{Kn}$. Charging $O(n)$ overhead per
operation still gives unconditional expected clocked work
$O(n^{B_*+3})$ and preserves the quadratic space bound.

To make the polynomial work cap explicit, stop after $n^{B_*+4}$
base operations of the unclocked implementation. This \emph{capped}
counter needs only $O_{K,D}(\log n)$ bits and at most that overhead
per counted operation. The capped worst-case total bit work is
$O(n^{B_*+4}\log n)\le O(n^{B_*+5})$.
Markov's inequality on the unclocked operation count gives interruption
probability $O(n^{-2})$. On interruption return only \textsc{Abstain}.
The cap never turns an unfinished enumeration into an accepted answer.
Intersecting noninterruption with the structural event proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\paragraph{Disconnected fixed frames.}
Connectedness above is used to make $\alpha_{\min}\ge\rho(K,D)$ and
the resulting structural constants uniform. The same proof works for
a disconnected fixed frame with a fixed positive proportion vector
$\alpha$, replacing $\rho$ by $\alpha_{\min}$. Its structural constants
and eventual size threshold may depend on $\alpha$. The algorithm and
its polynomial exponent are unchanged. A uniform statement over
arbitrarily vanishing block proportions is not supplied.
