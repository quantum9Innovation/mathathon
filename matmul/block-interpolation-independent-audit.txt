======================================================

Conclusion
----------
The saved direct all-depth proof passes this independent mathematical,
probability and convergence-scope audit. In particular the stronger
fractional-moment argument genuinely gives a prefactor with logarithm
O(d log(db/beta)), not the weaker O(d^2 log d) confidence recursion.
The supplied-partition gap is exactly Shao's equation (1), subject to
the normalization stated below. The result bypasses the false chi-product
conjecture and proves its intended DIRECT convergence corollary on that
partition class. It does not prove the false original KE-05 statement.

The closest inspected primary theorems do not supply this same parameter
and depth simultaneously: MMM2024 has a b-th-order gap but a longer
small-block startup; Chen et al. 2026 has k/b startup with an individual
eigenvalue-gap parameter. This is a bounded primary-source assessment,
not an exhaustive global novelty certification. The contribution is
analysis of standard block Krylov, not a new cheaper construction of a
raw long Krylov matrix or an unrestricted N^2 product algorithm.

1. Exact theorem and quantifiers audited
---------------------------------------
Fix integers b>=1,d>=2 and deterministic real diagonal b-by-b blocks
Lambda_i with pairwise disjoint spectra. All b-by-b Omega_i are mutually
independent standard REAL Gaussian matrices. Let B_i=Omega_i^{-1}
Lambda_i Omega_i and let F_i be the unique degree-(d-1) right-coefficient
fundamental polynomials under LEFT evaluation:

  P(z)=sum_l z^l C_l,  P(B)=sum_l B^l C_l,
  F_i(B_j)=delta_ij I.

If [a,c] contains all scalar nodes, L=c-a, and Delta is the minimum
CROSS-BLOCK scalar gap, the audited theorem is

 Pr{max_i sup_(z in[a,c])||F_i(z)||
       <=[768 d^8 b^5 beta^{-4} L/Delta]^{d-1}} >=1-beta.  (A)

For d=1 use F_1=I and omit Delta. There is no restriction on within-
block spacings or repetitions. The probability is for EACH FIXED
deterministic input; the same sample is not claimed to work for every
possible spectrum simultaneously. Input nodes must not adapt to Omega.

All finite b,d satisfy the proof, including growing b,d. No algorithm
enumerates the d! paths used in the probability union bound.

2. Terminal polynomial and resolvent: orientations checked
---------------------------------------------------------
For any selected q blocks, A0=diag(Lambda_i) is symmetric of order qb
and Gamma is their raw Gaussian stack. The square raw Krylov matrix
[Gamma,A0 Gamma,...,A0^{q-1}Gamma] is generically invertible: at all
Omega_i=I it permutes to b scalar q-node Vandermonde matrices. Cross-
block disjointness makes those nodes distinct. Polynomial null-set
reasoning and a finite union over subsets justify every required basis.
It also implies every earlier block Krylov rank increases by b, so no
unaccounted early block-Lanczos breakdown occurs.

Take fixed measurable QR sign choices. Write the resulting orthonormal
blocks as V_j=sum_l A0^l Gamma C_(l,j), with right polynomial P_j.
The leading coefficient is invertible. In the complete orthogonal
basis, symmetry gives the last recurrence

  A0 V_(q-1)=V_(q-2) H+V_(q-1) T.

The order in

  R_q(z)=z P_(q-1)(z)-P_(q-2)(z)H-P_(q-1)(z)T

is correct. Its raw realization vanishes, so it annihilates all selected
B_i by left evaluation. It is NOT justified by evaluating an arbitrary
noncommutative product of scalar-variable factors.

Let R_q(z)=sum_l z^l C_l. Polynomial division gives

 (zI-A0)^{-1}Gamma R_q(z)
   =sum_(l=1)^q sum_(t=0)^(l-1) z^(l-1-t) A0^t Gamma C_l.

After multiplying by V_(q-1)^T, every t<q-1 term vanishes. The only
remaining term is I, because
V_(q-1)^T A0^(q-1)Gamma C_q=V_(q-1)^T V_(q-1)=I.
Hence W_q(z)R_q(z)=I for W_q=V_(q-1)^T(zI-A0)^{-1}Gamma.
Both matrices are square, so this proves R_q^{-1}=W_q and

  ||R_q(eta)^{-1}||<=||Gamma||/dist(eta,spec A0).           (B)

The q=1 normalization also checks: the initial QR factor is incorporated
in P_0, and no missing leading-coefficient or transpose factor remains.

3. Conditional Gaussian step and direct interpolation
----------------------------------------------------
For a fresh root block k, R_(S\k) depends only on OTHER original
Gaussian blocks. Left evaluation gives

  N=Omega_k R(B_k),   row_l(N)=omega_(k,l)^T R(eta_(k,l)).

Conditional on the other blocks these rows are independent Gaussians,
with covariance R(eta)^T R(eta)>=delta_k^2/||Gamma||^2 I by (B).
Within-row entries need not be independent, and repeated eta values do
not destroy independence between rows. The elementary row-distance
lemma therefore applies without a hidden dependence assumption.

With F_k(z)=R(z)N^{-1}Omega_k, evaluation at all other blocks is zero;
at B_k it is I since N=Omega_k R(B_k). Constructing all these polynomials
proves surjectivity of the square interpolation map and hence uniqueness.
The induction identity P_j(z)=sum_i F_i(z)P_j(B_i) is consequently valid:
the right factors are constants, so left evaluation preserves this sum.

On spectrum [0,1], ||H||,||T||<=1 and ||(V_j)_i||<=1. Thus
sup||R_q||<=3q G_q max||Omega_i^{-1}||, as used in the note.

4. Probability constants, including the stronger path bound
----------------------------------------------------------
The Section 7 recursion constant 324 q^{3/2}d^3 b^5 beta^{-3}
checks exactly. Its three event collections have total failures at most
beta/3 each. Crucially a fresh-root conditional tail is integrated before
unioning with good events for OTHER roots; one must not condition on all
those events and then assert unchanged Gaussian laws.

Section 8 avoids that confidence recursion. With

  Z_(S,k)=delta_(S,k)||N_(S,k)^{-1}||/||Gamma_(S\k)||,

the conditional tail is min(1,b^{3/2}/z). Integrating it yields
E[sqrt(Z)|other blocks]<=2b^{3/4}. For each fixed nested deletion path,
the next Z depends only on the remaining blocks. Tower conditioning
therefore bounds the half moment of its PRODUCT by
(2b^{3/4})^{d-1}; no independence between Z factors is claimed.

Markov and a union over at most d! paths give the displayed product
bound (25). The common norm event has failure beta/2 with
L0^2=4d b^2/beta and U0=4d b^{3/2}/beta. It is unioned afterward,
not conditioned on in the moment calculation. Combining factors gives

 G_d<=4 beta^{-2}(d!)^2((d-1)!)^{3/2}
            [192 d^2 b^5 beta^{-2}/Delta]^{d-1},           (C)

since 3U0 L0^2=48d^2 b^{7/2}beta^{-2} and the squared half-moment
factor contributes 4b^{3/2} per elimination. The simplification of (C)
to (A) is valid and deliberately loose. Its log-prefactor is
O(d log(db/beta)), so no hidden quadratic-in-d depth remains.

5. Graph angle and standard Krylov consequences
-----------------------------------------------
For a fixed symmetric n-by-n A and a target invariant subspace Q of
dimension k=db, rotate the Gaussian start into its eigenbasis. Its k
head rows split into the required independent Omega_i. The head raw
Krylov matrix is invertible almost surely, so the full initial Krylov
space is a graph with tangent norm ||K_tail K_head^{-1}||.

For a head vector y=(y_i), use p_y(z)=sum_i F_i(z)Omega_i^{-1}y_i.
Each tail row is bounded by sqrt(d)*G_d*M*||omega_tail_row||*||y||,
where M=max_i||Omega_i^{-1}||. Thus

  tan angle(Q,K_d(A,Omega))
    <=sqrt(d)||Omega_tail||_F M G_d.                      (D)

One explicit confidence allocation is (A) at beta/3, M<=
3d b^{3/2}/beta except beta/3, and
||Omega_tail||_F<=sqrt(3b(n-db)/beta) except beta/3. This proves (D)
with a spectrum-independent angle prefactor whose logarithm is
O(d log(db/beta)+log(n/beta)). If n=db the tail is empty and the angle
is zero; n need not be a multiple of b. The theorem is an INITIAL
angle statement, and does not need a target/tail eigenvalue gap.

For a TOP invariant subspace and positive external gap g_ext, standard
Chebyshev acceleration subsequently gives depth

 d+O([d log(db/(beta*relgap_b))+log(n/(beta*epsilon))]
                                                   /sqrt(g_ext)).

For spectral/Frobenius LRA use the deterministic good-start theorem
rather than requiring an external gap. Let H=X X^T and let Q0 be an
orthonormal basis of the WHOLE K_d(H,Omega), which has k columns. Its
good-start parameter is 1+tan^2(angle), by principal-angle identities.
There is exact span equality

  K_t(H,Q0)=K_(t+d-1)(H,Omega).

Indeed multiplying the full initial raw Krylov basis by powers produces
every contiguous power, so there is no unsupported monotonic-Ritz step.
The usual good-start block-Krylov result yields relative spectral and
Frobenius LRA after additional depth O(theta^{-1/2}log(n*Lgood/theta)).
Consequently the total scalar matvec count is linear in k, up to the
displayed accuracy and logarithmic gap/dimension/confidence factors,
for the supplied-partition class. Orthogonalization, storage, extraction
and explicit product-output work still have to be counted separately.

This is exact-arithmetic convergence analysis. It is not a theorem
asserting the same behavior of finite-precision unreorthogonalized
Lanczos, nor an input-optimal raw-factor implementation.

7. Partition caution and a separately proposed padding extension
---------------------------------------------------------------
The partition-specific relgap_b must not be silently identified with
a b-th-nearest gap or with the sole condition max multiplicity<=b.
For b=3,k=6 with three distinct values each repeated twice, no two
size-three blocks can keep every repeated pair intact; every supplied
two-block cross-gap is zero. Yet multiplicity is at most b and the
ordinary b-th-nearest gap is positive.

A possible extension was sent to the parent and is being independently
audited by alternatives: for the ADDITIVE b-th-nearest distance g_b,
connect consecutive target nodes across gaps<g_b/b. Every component
has size<=b, since b+1 linked points would have diameter<g_b. Pack whole
components greedily into blocks of size<=b, then pad each block by
duplicating one of its own eigenvalues and adding independent Gaussian
rows. At most 2ceil(k/b)-1 blocks are needed; cross-gap stays>=g_b/b
and full spectral diameter is unchanged. Apply (A) to this augmented
system and prescribe zero dummy head values before projecting its
graph vectors to the original coordinates. This is not yet incorporated
in the audited exact-copy theorem. Relative nearest-neighbor gaps with
varying denominators need their own translation; no equality is asserted.
