Independent reconstruction of the zero-omission extension
Date: 2026-11-15

This is a new derivation note. No frozen source was edited.
It reconstructs the strict-gap argument from the assembled proof and
entropy companion, keeping omission weights throughout the KL/variance
step instead of dividing by their minimum.

Hypotheses
----------
Use the same reversible finite degree frame, positive old proportions,
legal real one-hot stars, compatible pooled pair laws and defect delta
as in the frozen strict-weight packet. Allow symmetric active weights
q_ij>=0, subject to sum_j d_ij q_ij=1 and q_ij<1/2 on every active factor.
Put c_e=1-2q_e and c=min_active c_e>0. In particular 0<c<=1.
The row normalization and strict upper cap imply each d_i>=3 even
without that minimum degree being separately assumed.

Work after within-neighbor-type slot symmetrization. Entropy only
increases and root laws/defect stay fixed, so an upper bound obtained
for the symmetrized exponent also holds before symmetrization. All
calculations below use natural logarithms.

1. Exact entropy and weighted retained divergences
------------------------------------------------
Weighted lossless deletion remains valid for zero weights. Write
  L=-Phi=J_q+sum_e w_e c_e I_e,
  J_q=sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it J_it.
For the actual retained law P_it and its product-marginal auxiliary
law Q_it, set
  kappa_it=KL(P_it||Q_it)=J_it+sum_(s!=t) I_i,s.
This divergence is finite for every slot, including those of weight
zero: any positive actual tuple has every coordinate in a positive
marginal support. Thus there is no undefined zero-times-infinity term.

Weight before summing omissions:
  K :=sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it kappa_it
    =J_q+sum_i alpha_i sum_s (1-q_is)I_i,s
    =J_q+2 sum_e w_e(1-q_e)I_e.
The factor two is correct internally as well: w_ii=alpha_i d_ii/2.
Since 2(1-q_e)=1+c_e,
  2(1-q_e)/c_e=1+1/c_e<=1+1/c<=2/c.
Therefore
  K<=(1+1/c)L<=(2/c)L.                         (A)
No minimum positive omission weight is used.

2. Exact weighted variance calculation
-------------------------------------
For physical slots at old type i write
  mu_is=E e_(Y_is),
  V_is=1-||mu_is||^2,
  S_i=sum_s V_is.
Use the same retained-data function
  f_it=||R_(X_i,*)-sum_(s!=t)e_(Y_is)-mu_it||^2.
Under the actual law, E_P f_it=V_it by the exact integer star equation.
Under Q_it, the root and retained neighbors are independent, giving
  E_Q f_it=sum_(s!=t)V_is+B_i,
  B_i=E||R_(X_i,*)-sum_s mu_is||^2>=0.
The same B_i occurs for EVERY omission. Explicitly, the vector under
the norm is the root-dependent term R_X-sum_s mu_s minus independent
centered retained-neighbor fluctuations. Their cross terms vanish.

Thus Delta_it:=E_Q f_it-E_P f_it=S_i-2V_it+B_i. Weighted summation is
  sum_t q_it Delta_it
    =sum_t(1-2q_it)V_it+B_i
    >=c S_i,                                      (B)
because sum_t q_it=1. This is valid when some q_it=0; such slots still
appear on the right with coefficient one in the exact expression.
Individual Delta_it need not be nonnegative. Only the weighted sum
and its lower bound are used.

3. Pinsker and constants
-----------------------
Every occupied R row has total mass d_i by legality, under both laws.
The other vector inside f_it is nonnegative of the same mass. Hence
  0<=f_it<=2d_i^2<=2D^2,
and the range form of total variation with Pinsker gives
  |Delta_it|<=sqrt(2)D^2 sqrt(kappa_it).
Weighting by alpha_i q_it and using
  sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it=1
gives, by Cauchy--Schwarz and (A),
  c sum_i alpha_i S_i
   <=sqrt(2)D^2 sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it sqrt(kappa_it)
   <=sqrt(2)D^2 sqrt(K)
   <=D^2 sqrt(2(1+1/c)L)
   <=2D^2 sqrt(L/c).                              (C)

Reversibility and minimum degree give
  sum_i alpha_i S_i
   =sum_j alpha_j d_j(1-||rho_j||^2)
   >=3 sum_j alpha_j(1-max_x rho_j(x))=3 delta.
Combining with (C) and squaring proves the proposed bound
  Phi<=-[9c^3/(4D^4)] delta^2.                     (D)
Keeping the sharper comparison in (A) instead gives
  Phi<=-[9c^3/(2D^4(1+c))] delta^2.                (E)
Both constants are valid. If a separate d_min>=3 is desired, replace
9 in either formula by d_min^2. No alpha_min or candidate-class mass
appears in these local gap constants.

4. Equality, recovery and algorithmic consequences
--------------------------------------------------
The positive constant in (D) forces root point masses when Phi=0.
Matching marginals then force deterministic neighbor labels and the
exact point-map relation QT=TR, as before. The converse remains true.
This removes the lower strictness requirement q_e>0 while keeping the
strict upper requirement q_e<1/2.

The modular moment already needed only q>=0 and the non-strict cap, so
its proof and polynomial exponent are unchanged. The support argument
uses only d_min>=3, max degree and positive old proportions, and the
unknown-frame algorithm uses neither q nor its margin. Consequently
the frozen recovery composition extends verbatim after substituting
the new entropy constant (D) or (E). For fixed K,D, qualifying true
frames still form a finite set; choose one nonnegative strict-upper
certificate per frame to make the positive c margin uniform. Boundary
weights equal to 1/2 remain outside this extension.

5. The enlarged class is genuinely larger
-----------------------------------------
Take two disjoint copies of K_(3,3) and join one vertex in the first
copy to one vertex in the second by a single bridge. This connected
simple base has 12 vertices, minimum degree three and maximum degree
four. Set q=1/3 on every original K_(3,3) edge and q=0 on the bridge.
Every vertex has omission-weight sum one and c=1/3.

Every symmetric normalized certificate on this base must have bridge
weight zero. In one K_(3,3) copy, subtract the sum of the three vertex
normalizations on one bipartite side from the sum on the other. All
internal edge terms cancel, the two demand sums are both three, and
the bridge is the only remaining term, with sign determined by its
endpoint. Its weight must therefore be zero. Thus no all-positive
certificate exists, whereas the new gap applies.

For this example D=4 and c=1/3, (D) gives gamma=1/3072; the sharper
(E) gives gamma=1/2048. Its independent matching lifts, at every N,
are a concrete new recovery specialization of the conditional addendum.

Verdict: the proposed no-q_min derivation and constant are mathematically
valid. A separately written addendum should state the altered hypothesis
explicitly rather than changing the already frozen strict-weight packet.
Historical firstness is not assessed here.
