2. PRECISE CLASS AND WHY THE EXTENSION IS SUBSTANTIVE

lies in U_i. Channels L_ij:V_j->U_i are injective on every present factor

On-site T_i:V_i->U_i is linear in the full theorem. The local equation is
  sum_(stubs vu) L_ij(Y_u)=T_i(Y_v).
The global operator can be described with distinct domain/codomain row

Zero-dimensional spaces are allowed, and the prime may be two.

3. EXACT PROFILES AND ENTROPY

Legal ordered-star multinomials assign the root and all its neighbor
labels once. There is no missing root multinomial and no second such
factor. Pooled cross tables give product m(x,y)! divided by the total
pool factorial. Internal symmetric tables give off-diagonal factorials
and diagonal double factorials divided by the full pairing count, with
even diagonal and (-1)!!=1. Loops have two incidences throughout.

These exact weights do not depend on channel maps, beyond which stars
are legal. At fixed finite alphabets, the number of profiles is polynomial
in n and uniform factorial errors are O(log(n+1)), including zero counts.
The exponent is exactly
  Phi=sum_i alpha_i H(P_i)-sum_(i<=j) w_ij H(nu_ij),
  w_ij=alpha_i d_ij (i<j),  w_ii=alpha_i d_ii/2.

Repeated-target symmetrization preserves the channel equation and pooled
pair laws, because channels agree on the exchanged slots. Its entropy
gain G_i is nonnegative and vanishes exactly for the original symmetric
law. Lossless omission holds for every present channel by injectivity;
only active omissions receive nonzero averaging weight. This yields
  H(Pbar_i)=(2-d_i)H(mu_i)
     +sum_j d_ij(1-q_ij)H(nu_ij)-sum_t q_it J_it.
After edge reversal and H(nu)=H(mu_i)+H(mu_j)-I, each root coefficient is
  alpha_i[(2-d_i)+sum_j d_ij(1-2q_ij)]=0.
The exact unsymmetrized identity is therefore
  Phi=-sum_i alpha_i G_i
      -sum_(i<=j) w_ij(1-2q_ij)I_ij
      -sum_i alpha_i sum_t q_it J_it <=0.
Internal half weight is paired with TWO root entropy copies. Equal total
degrees and division by a field degree or omission weight are not used.

Every pair MI vanishes at equality, even on inactive factors, since all
1-2q are positive. Only active retained tuples must have J_it=0. The
proof keeps this distinction and the symmetrization gain, so it loses no
asymmetric equality phases.

4. COMPLETE ZERO PHASES, INCLUDING NECESSITY AND CONVERSE

At a zero, condition on a root support value and three active physical
slots a,b,c, mapping their labels into the common LOCAL output space U_i.
Fix all other coordinates W to a product-support value. Its probability
is positive. The three active retained-product identities imply that
each conditional pair (a,b), (a,c), (b,c) still has its original product
law. Their sum is now fixed.

For three pairwise independent finite vector variables with a fixed sum,
conditioning one variable reflects the other two marginal laws. These
reflections identify their support-difference subspaces. Comparing two
support values produces invariance under each support difference, hence
under the generated F_p subspace. A distribution supported on one coset
and invariant under that whole subspace is UNIFORM on the coset. This
proves actual marginal uniformity, not merely an affine support bound.
Using two fixed active slots with every other active slot identifies one
common image subspace K_i at this old type. It is determined by the
original marginals, independently of the conditioned root and W.

Every root type j occurs as an active neighbor somewhere: its unit row
has a positive weight and symmetry/reversibility supply the reverse
active incidence. Pulling its image back through that isomorphism gives
  mu_j=Uniform(c_j+H_j),  H_j<=V_j.

  L_ij(H_j)=K_i.

For an active omission, the retained independent sum contains another
active uniform K_i variable. After all root marginals are known uniform,
its support is a coset of K_i+sum_(inactive slots) L_ij(H_j). It must
also be a coset of K_i by the omitted marginal and the local equation.

  T_i(c_i)-sum_j d_ij L_ij(c_j) in K_i.
These conditions remain valid when incidence multiplicities vanish mod p.

Conversely take any such subspaces and cosets. Choose the root and all
but one active neighbor independently uniformly in their cosets, then

affine stars and independent of the chosen active omission: freely
specifying any other active-retained tuple gives one legal star. Every
active retained law is product uniform, and any root-neighbor pair is
independent by choosing another active omission. Repeated-slot symmetry
holds. This proves sufficiency and uniqueness of the phase law.

All finite spaces have finitely many subspaces and cosets. Thus the
classification is exhaustive and finite; no continuous boundary family
