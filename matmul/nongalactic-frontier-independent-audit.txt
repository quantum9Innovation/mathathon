The proposed Lambda=||X||_F^2+a bounds both endpoints and costs

must remain visible in the complexity and rank statements.

The resolvent formula in (3.4) follows by inserting E into R_A E R_B.
Since ||sqrt(A)R_A|| and ||sqrt(B)R_B|| are at most 1/(2sqrt(t)),
its two terms sum to at most d/t^(3/2). Combining with the two old

7. INEXACT ADAPTIVE ADI: STRONGER FACTOR NORM CLOSES CONSTANT 36

The actual approximate Cayley differs from the exact contraction by

  ||Uhat_j||,||Vhat_j|| <=(1+2eta)^j,
  ||Uhat_j-U_j||,||Vhat_j-V_j||
       <=(1+2eta)^j-1<=4k*eta,

using eta=eps^2/[10^6*rho*(k+1)^2]. The tiny eta ensures

commute: they compare ordered products one factor at a time.

For clarity, the factor norm required for the displayed term constant

  ||Lhat_j||,||Rhat_j||
     <=(1+eta)(1+2eta)^k/tau_j <2/tau_j.

Expanding first in L, then in R, now gives exactly

  ||2tau_j*(Lhat_j C Rhat_j^T-L_j C R_j^T)||
