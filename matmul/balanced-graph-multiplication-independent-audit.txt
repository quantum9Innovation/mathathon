projection representation W=J+lambda P_H, and the s-row Gram factor

3. THE FIXED-STABLE-RANK O(N^2) COROLLARY
=======================================
The source gives a separate one-color proof. Taking the covariance
trace and dropping its negative PSD term gives

  E||ATB||_F^2<=a||A_0B_0||_F^2
               +b||A_0||_F^2||B_0||_F^2
              <=ar+br^2<=(4r+3r^2)/(s-1)
              <=3(r^2+2r)/(s-1).

Thus the displayed s choice and Markov's inequality give the required
epsilon||A||||B|| operator error at failure delta. The conclusion
includes full algebraic rank with bounded stable rank, but assumes
r,epsilon,delta are FIXED for the ordinary O(N^2) total-cost statement.
It is explicitly not a relative-product error under cancellation or a
(1+theta)sigma_(k+1)(AB) arbitrary-factor guarantee.

The pseudocode was checked separately: its two assignments multiply
the bucketed inputs by F=sqrt(gamma)Pi_s+q^-1/2 J_s on their bucket
coordinates. Therefore XY=(AH^T)F^2(HB)=AWB exactly. The repeated bucket
mean is computed once, so there is no hidden s-by-s multiplication.
The identity branch can form AB by ordinary multiplication, costing
O(np m)<=O(np s) because that branch has m<=s. It is not a free exact
matrix-product oracle.

4. PADDING, FULL OUTPUT AND ARITHMETIC MODEL
==========================================
When s<m and divisibility fails, zero padding to M=s ceil(m/s)<2m
preserves the exact product and both stable ranks and norms. The proof
then uses M. The identity fallback handles s>=m and small dimensions,
with width at most s. Choosing identity when m is smaller than the
proposed shared-channel width is also a sound optional optimization.

Bucket aggregation O(dm(n+p)), centering/Householder O(ds(n+p)), factor
storage O(ell(n+p)), and output expansion O(np ell) count every factor
and output entry. For the one-color constant-parameter branch with
n=p=N,m<=N this is O(N^2) ordinary arithmetic. Parameter selection is
qualified by logarithmic scalar approximation cost and the numerical
model is exact real arithmetic. No finite-word stability, measured
performance, or reduced random-bit complexity is asserted.
