GENERAL BOOLEAN NEAR-PLANTED COUNT IN THE UNIFORM RSBM
2026-11-15. Independent derivation; no historical novelty claim.

Let a=d1>b=d2>0 be fixed, d=a+b>=3, n=2N. Use the colored
configuration model: independent uniform pairings of aN internal
stubs in each block, and a uniform bijection of the bN cross stubs
on one side to those on the other. Assume the required aN is even.
Conditioning all three components simple yields exactly the desired
uniform-component model. The cross matching is NOT fixed in this
argument, even when b=1.

Let sigma be the planted sign vector. For another sign vector tau
with A tau=(a-b)tau, the difference w=sigma-tau is also an eigenvector.
Its support S is the flip set; w_v=2 sigma_v on S, so for a specified
S there is no extra choice of nonzero field values.

INCIDENT-MOTIF EXCESS
Write |S|=K. Include every edge incident to S, with loops and
multiplicities, and include the ell external vertices touched. Let
e be the number of edges internal to S, B the number with exactly
one endpoint in S, E=e+B, and V=K+ell. Then

 dK=2e+B,   E=dK-e,   ell<=B/2.

The last inequality follows from the eigenvector equation at an
external vertex: exactly one supported neighbor-edge cannot sum to
zero. Counting incident stubs handles repeated edges correctly.
Therefore, with eta=d/2-1>0,

 E-V >= eta K,       E<=dK,       V<=(1+d/2)K.           (1)

UNIFORM CERTIFICATE ENUMERATION
For 1<=K<=n/8 and all sufficiently large n, a prescribed valid colored
partial matching with E incident edges has probability at most
(8/n)^E. Indeed an internal prescribed edge pair has successive
denominators aN-1,aN-3,...; there are at most aK incident edges of
either internal color. For K<=N/4, every such denominator is at least
aN/2-1>=N/4 for large N. The cross prescribed-bijection denominators
are bN,bN-1,... with at most bK factors, also at least N/4. Since
N=n/2, the common bound 8/n is valid. Invalid colors are discarded.

Choose the K support vertices and ell exterior vertices in at most
n^(K+ell)/(K! ell!) ways. There are at most dV distinguished stubs
on them. The number of partial matchings with E pairs is at most

 binom(dV,2E)(2E-1)!! <= 2^(dV)(2E)^E
                       <= 2^(d(1+d/2)K)(2dK)^E.        (2)

This counts many invalid certificates; every actual one is present.
Combining (1), (2), and the pairing probability, and using K/n<=1,
the contribution for a fixed ell,E is at most

 2^(d(d+2)K/2) (16d)^E
   (K/n)^(eta K) [K^K/K!][K^ell/ell!].

Sum ell using sum_(ell>=0) K^ell/ell!=exp(K), use K^K/K!<=exp(K),
and sum the at most dK+1 possibilities for E. With the explicit
constant

 C_d=2^(d(d+2)/2)(16d)^d exp(2),

the expected number Z_K of valid flip sets of size K satisfies

 E_config Z_K <= (dK+1) C_d^K (K/n)^(eta K).             (3)

All constants depend only on d. The counting did not require an
independence assumption about values in the eigenvector equation.

SIMPLE GRAPHS AND THE FIRST POSSIBLE FLIP
For a candidate equitable bisection, its balance forces the same
number k of flips in each planted block, hence K=2k. At every vertex
the flip equation says the number of originally internal edges
crossing the flip set equals the number of originally cross edges
crossing it. A flipped vertex therefore has at least a-b>0 originally
internal neighbors inside the flip set. In a simple graph k=1 is
impossible. Thus every nontrivial simple-model candidate has K>=4.

Choose a fixed epsilon_d>0 sufficiently small that the right side
of (3) decreases geometrically for 4<=K<=epsilon_d n; for example

 epsilon_d=min(1/16, (1/2)(8 C_d exp(eta))^(-1/eta))

works for all sufficiently large n, since the successive ratio is
at most 2 C_d exp(eta)((K+1)/n)^eta. Consequently

 sum_(4<=K<=epsilon_d n) E_config Z_K
      =O_d(n^(-4eta))=O_d(n^(-2(d-2))).                 (4)

Conditioning simplicity divides this upper bound by a positive
limiting constant. For reference that constant is

 s=exp(-(a^2-1)/2-(b-1)^2/2)>0.

The usual factorial-moment calculation is short here: in one a-regular
internal configuration, loops and double-edge pairs converge jointly
to independent Poisson variables with means (a-1)/2 and (a-1)^2/4.
In the cross configuration, double-edge pairs have mean (b-1)^2/2
and Poisson limit. Disjoint small structures give the factorial
moments; any overlap loses one power of N. The three configurations
are independent. Absence of these defects is exactly simplicity.

Thus the uniform-simple model has no alternate equitable bisection
whose suitably oriented flip set has size at most epsilon_d n, with
failure probability O_d(n^(-2(d-2)))<=O_d(n^-2).

SCOPE
Equation (4) controls only near-planted candidates. Combining it with
the root's macroscopic Boolean entropy gap, independently checked in
boolean-variance-audit.txt, gives the full Boolean rigidity statement.
It does not prove real eigenspace simplicity. No permutation model
or degree-two contiguity transfer is used.
