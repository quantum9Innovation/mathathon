This review does not certify historical novelty or verify every statement

1. Envelope. Every admissible cut has cost c(S)+t chi(S), with chi binary.
   Minimizing inside each signature class proves the two-piece formula
   and the full optimizer-set statement. For r edits there are at most
   2^r signature classes. A representative suffices to supply an optimum
   and its value, while full tied families can contain many cuts.

2. Rotation. With beta_j=sqrt(c d_j/2), the stated formulas rotate the
   e_0,z plane orthogonally and fix its orthogonal complement. Thus the
   v_j are orthonormal. Projection of b=sqrt(2)e_0 gives b_j=sqrt(c d_j)
   and b_0=sqrt(2-c sum d_j), and no component lies outside their span.

3. Laplacian realization. The perturbation M=A-2nP has eigenvalues -1,
   d_j, and 1 on orthogonal subspaces, so ||M||<=1 and M1=0. Therefore
   every off-diagonal entry of A lies in [-3,-1], all rows sum to zero,
   and A is the claimed complete weighted graph Laplacian. Its smallest
   eigenvalue on constants-perpendicular is 2n-1.

4. Secular root. On the first interval (K-1,K+d_k), m(mu) increases
   strictly from negative to positive infinity. For every positive t,
   1+t m(mu)=0 has one simple root. All other active poles and the inactive
   K+1 eigenspace lie above this root. It is the simple Fiedler eigenvalue.
   At mu_j=K-d_j, the negative term is near -2 while the positive sum
   is at most ck=1/10, so m(mu_j)<-1. Hence 0<t_j<1<=w_e; their ordering
   follows because mu_j increases and -1/m increases where m<0.

5. Direction control. At mu_j the normalized coefficient ratio for
   v_l relative to v_j is exactly
       2 sqrt(d_l d_j)/(d_l+d_j),
   at most 2r^{|l-j|/2}. Summing both geometric tails gives the displayed
   4sqrt(r)/(1-sqrt(r)) bound. With k,n,r fixed, the relative v_0 term
   and all rotation errors tend to zero as d_1 tends to zero. A single
   sufficiently small positive d_1 controls every selected index in sup
   norm by eta/sqrt(n), ensuring exact signs and flat coordinate bounds.
   The unit-vector error <=2eta follows by normalization and the triangle
   inequality; no omitted 1/(1-eta) factor is needed for this estimate.

6. Relative energy and powers. Since t<=w_e<=3 and ||b||^2=2,
   tbb^T<=6P<=[6/(2n-1)]A on constants-perpendicular. Loewner--Heinz
   gives exactly the fractional inequality for 0<alpha<=1. Positive
   scalar powers preserve all eigenspaces and eigenvalue ordering.

7. Dimension. Walsh cut vectors are orthonormal and balanced. Their
   pairwise cut disagreement is n/2. Bessel's inequality yields
   sum_j ||P_V q_j||^2<=dim V, hence dim V>=(1-epsilon)k.
   Approximating the actual Fiedler vectors transfers by the stated
   triangle inequality with their 2eta error.

Scope checks. The proof is for weighted dense hosts and arbitrarily small
spectral gaps; it does not claim many exact optimizer transitions under
one edit, simple unweighted bounded-degree realization, or normalized
conductance optimality. This separation is mathematically essential.
