VERDICT
-------
No inspected published theorem directly supplies the candidate's full
combination: an explicit PSD thin correction after one dipole edge increase,
baseline-fractional-energy operator error, expected nearly-linear total
construction, logarithmic dependence only on n,1+tau,accuracy/confidence,
and no spectral-gap/conductance-ratio parameter. The closest update and
Riccati theorems have materially different error norms and/or charged
primitives. Their literal statements do not immediately subsume this claim.

Substantial classical-composition risk remains. Low-rank function updating,
positive integral quadrature, feasible electrical-flow approximation,
fractional-norm interpolation, small weighted-Gram truncation and the
determinant rank budget are established mechanisms. The candidate burden
is the graph-specific quantitative synthesis, especially conversion of
feasible-flow accuracy into fractional-relative column accuracy and a
computable relative Gram at fully charged gap-free cost. Do not call the
individual primitives new or describe this audit as a global SOTA proof.

1. EXACT CLAIM BEING COMPARED
----------------------------
For connected unit-mass weighted graph L and L'=L+d bb^T, b=e_p-e_q,
d>0, tau=d b^T L^dagger b, the candidate returns physical X with

 || L^(-alpha/2)[L'^alpha-L^alpha-XX^T]L^(-alpha/2) || <= epsilon.

The target is an operator error in the baseline fractional energy,
not ||Delta-F||/||Delta|| and not a relative Frobenius error to L'^alpha.
Raw and Gram quadrature widths are O_alpha(log^2(2+n(1+tau)/epsilon)).
The output rank is bounded by a near-sharp determinant threshold, but
is also at most the logarithmic raw width. All factors and small-core
work are counted. Expected nearly-linear construction can rerun graph
solver setup each edit. The source does not claim sublinear dynamic
update time, a free base fractional matrix, or finite-word stability.

2. RATIONAL MATRIX-FUNCTION UPDATES: CLOSE BUT DIFFERENT NORM/INPUTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary source actually opened and theorem/proof sections inspected:
  Beckermann, Cortinovis, Kressner, Schweitzer,
  Low-rank updates of matrix functions II: Rational Krylov methods,
  SIAM J. Numer. Anal.59(2021),1325--1347.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2008.11501v1
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.11501

Theorem4.5 bounds the ordinary operator norm update error by four times
a scalar uniform rational approximation error on a domain containing
the endpoint numerical ranges. Theorem4.6's Markov-function estimate
requires separation of the Markov support from the spectral domain.
Theorem4.10 similarly controls an ordinary update norm through that
separation/rational-approximation quantity. These are not baseline-
energy congruence errors with gap-free, weight-independent SDD work.

A naive conversion of an ordinary operator error E to the candidate
metric gives ||L^(-alpha/2) E L^(-alpha/2)|| <=lambda_2(L)^(-alpha)||E||.
Thus that conversion needs accuracy epsilon lambda_2(L)^alpha and
does not establish the new gap-free work statement. This observation
does not exclude a sharper unpublished or indirect adaptation.

The resolvent low-rank formula itself is old: BCKS Section3.3 credits
Bernstein--Van Loan, Updating the inverse of a matrix and applications,
Theorem3 (2000), for rational-function rank-one update rank identities.
The candidate must credit this, as it currently credits BCKS.

3. SQUARE-ROOT RICCATI METHODS: DIRECT COST COMPARISON
---------------------------------------------------
Primary actual theorem and algorithm/cost sections inspected:
  Shmueli, Drineas, Avron, Low-Rank Updates of Matrix Square Roots,
  Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications31(2024),e2528;
  first online2023.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2201.13156v3
  https://doi.org/10.1002/nla.2528

Theorem3 is an SPD rank-k perturbation singular-value decay bound for
Delta=sqrt(B)-sqrt(A). Its relative quantity is sigma_j(Delta), and its
parameter explicitly contains lambda_min(A). Section4 derives the
Riccati equation; Section5.5 charges O((T_sqrtA+T_invsqrtA)r^2+n r^4),
conditional on the stated Riccati solver assumptions and supplied
fractional matrix actions. These statements do not supply the candidate
metric or fully charged graph-specific construction.

Restricting graph L to 1-perp removes its zero eigenvalue but leaves
an arbitrarily small positive lambda_2. Nor does it make fractional
actions free. Therefore citing the existing SPD Riccati algorithm alone
does not establish the claimed no-gap graph theorem. The earlier
repository regularization audit concerns relative error to ||Delta||,
which is a different criterion and must not be reused as a subsumption
claim here.

4. FRACTIONAL DIFFUSION ON GRAPHS AND SINC QUADRATURE
---------------------------------------------------
Primary graph paper actually opened and its target/algorithm read:
  Benzi, Simunec, Rational Krylov methods for fractional diffusion
  problems on graphs, BIT62(2022),357--385; online2021.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2012.08389v1
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s10543-021-00881-0

Its task is a vector f(L^T)b, principally f(z)=exp(-t z^alpha).
Algorithm1 and equation(5) use rational Arnoldi; Section5 handles the
zero mode via shifts or projection. It does not output a full edge-
update correction with baseline-energy operator error or provide the
candidate's feasible-flow/relative-Gram arithmetic theorem. Singular
graph functional calculus and shifted solves themselves are prior.

Primary quadrature comparator actually read:
  Bonito, Lei, Pasciak, On Sinc Quadrature Approximations of Fractional
  Powers of Regularly Accretive Operators, arXiv:1709.06619v2(2018).
  https://arxiv.org/html/1709.06619v2
Theorem3.2 already proves exponential sinc convergence in fractional
operator-domain norms; its constants depend on operator form bounds
c0,C0. This is a close antecedent for the Gram quadrature and
fractional-norm analysis, not a dipole update theorem with arbitrary
weighted-graph condition parameters removed. The graph maximum-
principle moment bound and flow-certified column computation are
additional steps in the candidate.

Further classical quadrature credit:
  Hale, Higham, Trefethen, Computing A^alpha, log(A), and related
  matrix functions by contour integrals, SINUM46(2008),2505--2523.
  https://doi.org/10.1137/070700607
  https://appliedmaths.sun.ac.za/~nhale/publications/HaleHighamTrefethen2008.pdf
Its contour/trapezoid rational action constructions precede this work.
No innovation claim should attach to using logarithmic/trapezoid
matrix-function quadrature or shifted systems as such.

5. THE FEASIBLE-FLOW GUARANTEE IS ALREADY EXPLICIT IN KOSZ
-------------------------------------------------------
Primary complete paper actually inspected at the stated definitions,
algorithm, theorems and their proof connection:
  Kelner, Orecchia, Sidford, Allen Zhu, A Simple, Combinatorial
  Algorithm for Solving SDD Systems in Nearly-Linear Time, STOC2013.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6628
  https://math.mit.edu/~kelner/publications/ASimpleCombinatorialAlgorithmforSolvingSDDSystemsinNearly-LinearTime.pdf

Definition2.4 requires exact demand feasibility and multiplicatively
near-optimal energy. Theorem3.2 gives expected relative energy accuracy
and nearly-linear arithmetic. Theorem4.1 and Algorithm1 explicitly
preserve feasibility through cycle updates. Its theorem footnote already
notes probability amplification; Section1.2 distinguishes electrical-flow
and potential guarantees. Thus exact feasible-flow output is a supplied
published primitive, not a new solver result in this repository.

What remains additional in the candidate is its specific augmented-
ground-graph use: the divergence error simultaneously has A^dagger
norm and Euclidean norm certificates, which interpolate to the needed
A^(-alpha) norm. The same returned flows certify the low tail of the
small relative Gram integral. KOSZ does not state this fractional
matrix-function update conclusion.

6. NYSTROM FUNCTION APPROXIMATION DOES NOT DIRECTLY APPLY TO C
-----------------------------------------------------------
Primary theorem actually read:
  Persson, Meyer, Musco, Algorithm-agnostic low-rank approximation of
  operator monotone matrix functions, arXiv:2311.14023v2(2024).
  https://arxiv.org/html/2311.14023v2

Theorem1.1 transfers near-optimal nuclear-norm low-rank approximation
from A>=Ahat>=0 to f(A); operator-norm analogues are in Theorem2.7.
The target is a single f(A), not a noncommuting difference after
baseline congruence. Setting its input to C would first require an
efficient C action or representation, the very work supplied by the
candidate. It gives no automatic nearly-linear route from raw L,b to
this relative correction. It remains relevant classical background for
small spectral truncation and matrix-function low-rank approximation.
