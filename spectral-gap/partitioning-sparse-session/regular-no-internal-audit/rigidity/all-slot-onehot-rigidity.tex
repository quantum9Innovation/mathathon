\paragraph{Scope.}
This note independently audits the real one-hot rigidity step in
\texttt{regular-frame-no-internal-root.txt}. It proves an explicit entropy
gap, so no compactness argument or positive lower bound on candidate-class
sizes is needed. It does not audit configuration factorial counts,
simplicity conditioning, small supports, running time, or priority.
All entropies and divergences use natural logarithms. The vector identity
used below is over the ordinary integers or reals, not a finite field.

\paragraph{Model for the local laws.}
Let $Q=(d_{ij})_{1\leq i,j\leq k}$ be a nonnegative integer frame with
\[
 \sum_jd_{ij}=d\geq3\quad\text{for every }i.
\]
Allow $d_{ii}=0$ or $1$, and allow $k=1$. Let positive numbers
$\alpha_i$ sum to one and satisfy the reversible balance equations
$\alpha_i d_{ij}=\alpha_jd_{ji}$. Put
\[
 w_{ii}=\frac{\alpha_i d_{ii}}2,\qquad
 w_{ij}=\alpha_i d_{ij}\quad(i<j),\qquad
 \alpha_{\min}=\min_i\alpha_i.
\]
Zero-weight terms are omitted throughout. No connectedness is required
for this local inequality; the connected-frame model is a special case.

Fix a finite candidate label set $[r]$, represented by distinct unit
vectors $e_1,\ldots,e_r\in\mathbb R^r$, and a nonnegative integer
$r\times r$ matrix $R$. For old type $i$, let $P_i$ be a law on an
ordered star $(X_i,Y_{i,1},\ldots,Y_{i,d})$, with exactly $d_{ij}$ slots
whose old neighbor type is $j$. Every supported star obeys
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{s=1}^d e_{Y_{i,s}}=R_{X_i,*}.                 \label{legal}
\end{equation}
Denote the root law by $\rho_i$. Define $\pi_{ij}$ to be the pair law
$(X_i,Y_{i,s})$ pooled uniformly over all type-$j$ slots. Assume the
matching constraints
\[
 \pi_{ii}(x,y)=\pi_{ii}(y,x),\qquad
 \pi_{ij}(x,y)=\pi_{ji}(y,x).
\]
The first marginal of $\pi_{ij}$ is $\rho_i$; matching and balance give
its second marginal $\rho_j$. Define
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \Phi(P)&=\sum_i\alpha_iH(P_i)
                  -\sum_{i\leq j}w_{ij}H(\pi_{ij}),\\
 \delta(P)&=\sum_i\alpha_i\bigl(1-\max_x\rho_i(x)\bigr).
 \end{split}                                      \label{functional}
\end{equation}
The defect $\delta$ is the minimum weighted disagreement with an
old-block point map $T:[k]\to[r]$. Such a map need not be onto.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit all-slot rigidity]
For every collection of legal laws just specified,
\begin{equation}
 \Phi(P)\leq
 -\frac{\alpha_{\min}(d-2)^3}{4d^6(d-1)}\,\delta(P)^2
 \leq -\frac{\alpha_{\min}}{8D^6}\,\delta(P)^2
 \quad\text{if }3\leq d\leq D.                    \label{gap}
\end{equation}
Moreover $\Phi(P)=0$ if and only if every root law is a point mass,
$\rho_i=\delta_{T(i)}$, and the one-hot matrix of this map satisfies
$QT=TR$. There is no assumption of an internal slot, distinct rows of
$R$, invertibility of $R$, or a positive mass for every candidate label.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Symmetrize each $P_i$ over permutations of slots within each old neighbor
type, without permuting slots across different types. This preserves
\eqref{legal}, the root laws, and all pooled pair laws. Concavity of
entropy increases $H(P_i)$, so it suffices to prove the upper bound for
the symmetrized laws. The resulting laws need not be empirical integer
types: only an analytic entropy inequality is being used.

Work henceforth with these within-type exchangeable laws. For every
physical slot $t$, the legal vector sum determines $Y_{i,t}$ uniquely
from the root and all retained slots. Thus
\[
 H(P_i)=H(\rho_i)+H((Y_{i,s})_{s\ne t}\mid X_i).
\]
Let the nonnegative conditional total correlation of those retained
slots be
\[
 J_{it}=\sum_{s\ne t}H(Y_{i,s}\mid X_i)
                  -H((Y_{i,s})_{s\ne t}\mid X_i).
\]
Averaging the displayed entropy identity over \emph{all} $d$ omissions
gives exactly
\begin{equation}
 H(P_i)=H(\rho_i)+\frac{d-1}{d}\sum_{s=1}^d
 H(Y_{i,s}\mid X_i)-\frac1d\sum_{t=1}^dJ_{it}.
                                                        \label{average}
\end{equation}
No exchangeability between different old neighbor types was used.

Write $I_{ij}=I(X_i;Y_{i,s})$ for any type-$j$ slot after the within-type
symmetrization. Matching reversal makes $I_{ij}=I_{ji}$. The sum of
conditional entropies in \eqref{average} is
\[
 \sum_jd_{ij}\bigl(H(\rho_j)-I_{ij}\bigr).
\]
On weighting by $\alpha_i$, reversibility and row regularity yield
\[
 \sum_i\alpha_i\sum_jd_{ij}H(\rho_j)
       =d\sum_j\alpha_jH(\rho_j),\qquad
 \sum_i\alpha_i\sum_jd_{ij}I_{ij}
       =2\sum_{i\leq j}w_{ij}I_{ij}.
\]
The factor two in the second identity includes internal terms because
$w_{ii}=\alpha_i d_{ii}/2$. Similarly
\[
 \sum_{i\leq j}w_{ij}H(\pi_{ij})
 =d\sum_i\alpha_iH(\rho_i)-\sum_{i\leq j}w_{ij}I_{ij}.
\]
Substitution proves the exact cancellation
\begin{equation}
 L:=-\Phi(P)=\frac{d-2}{d}\underbrace{\sum_{i\leq j}w_{ij}I_{ij}}_{E}
   +\frac1d\underbrace{\sum_i\alpha_i\sum_tJ_{it}}_{J}\geq0.
                                                        \label{cancellation}
\end{equation}
In particular the internal coefficient has the correct double-counting
factor even when $d_{ii}=1$, and a zero internal degree contributes no
term at all.

For each $i,t$, let $P_i^{-t}$ be the retained-star law including the
root. Define an auxiliary law $\mathsf Q_{it}$ that keeps the same root
law $\rho_i$ and samples all retained neighbor labels independently,
independently also of the root, using their neighboring root laws.
The divergence is finite: every positive actual tuple has positive
probability under the product of its marginals. Direct expansion gives
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_{it}:=\KL(P_i^{-t}\Vert\mathsf Q_{it})
   =J_{it}+\sum_{s\ne t}I(X_i;Y_{i,s}).              \label{kl}
\end{equation}
For one fixed old type $i$, set
$A_i=\alpha_i\sum_{s=1}^dI(X_i;Y_{i,s})$.
Its internal edge contributes twice its weight in $E$ and each incident
cross edge contributes once; hence $A_i\leq2E$. Also
$\alpha_i\sum_tJ_{it}\leq J$. Summing \eqref{kl} over all omissions
therefore gives
\begin{align}
 \alpha_i\sum_t\kappa_{it}
 &\leq J+2(d-1)E\notag\\
 &\leq \frac{2d(d-1)}{d-2}\,L.                    \label{sumkl}
\end{align}
The last step compares the coefficients separately in
\eqref{cancellation}: the required maximum is
$\max\{d,2d(d-1)/(d-2)\}=2d(d-1)/(d-2)$.
It does not assume different local divergences are disjoint globally.

For each slot of the fixed old type $i$, put
\[
 \mu_s=\E e_{Y_{i,s}},\qquad
 V_s=\E\|e_{Y_{i,s}}-\mu_s\|_2^2
     =1-\|\rho_{j(s)}\|_2^2,
\]
where $j(s)$ is that slot's old neighbor type. For omission $t$ define
\[
 W_t=R_{X_i,*}-\sum_{s\ne t}e_{Y_{i,s}},\qquad
 f_t=\|W_t-\mu_t\|_2^2,
\]
as functions of retained data. Under $P_i^{-t}$, the legal equation
gives $W_t=e_{Y_{i,t}}$, so $\E_{P_i^{-t}}f_t=V_t$. Under
$\mathsf Q_{it}$, all retained neighbors are independent of each other
and of the root, giving
\begin{equation}
 \E_{\mathsf Q_{it}}f_t
 =\sum_{s\ne t}V_s+B_i,\qquad
 B_i=\E\left\|R_{X_i,*}-\sum_{s=1}^d\mu_s\right\|_2^2\geq0.
                                                        \label{variance}
\end{equation}
The same $B_i$ occurs for every omission. This calculation uses no
injectivity assumption on $x\mapsto R_{x,*}$.
Consequently, writing $\Delta_t=\E_{\mathsf Q_{it}}f_t-
\E_{P_i^{-t}}f_t$,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{t=1}^d\Delta_t=(d-2)\sum_{s=1}^d V_s+dB_i
                  \geq(d-2)\sum_sV_s.              \label{sumvariance}
\end{equation}
Individual $\Delta_t$ need not be nonnegative; only this sum is used.

Every occupied row $R_{X_i,*}$ is a nonnegative count vector of mass $d$
by \eqref{legal}. The retained sum plus $\mu_t$ is another nonnegative
vector of mass $d$, even on auxiliary tuples that are not legal stars.
Thus $0\leq f_t\leq2d^2$ under both laws. The range form of total
variation and natural-logarithm Pinsker give
\[
 |\Delta_t|\leq2d^2\TV(P_i^{-t},\mathsf Q_{it})
               \leq2d^2\sqrt{\kappa_{it}/2}.
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz, \eqref{sumkl}, and \eqref{sumvariance} now imply
\[
 (d-2)\sum_sV_s
 \leq2d^2\sqrt{\frac d2\sum_t\kappa_{it}}
 \leq2d^2\sqrt{\frac{d^2(d-1)}{\alpha_i(d-2)}L}.
\]
Squaring gives the useful local estimate
\begin{equation}
 L\geq\frac{\alpha_i(d-2)^3}{4d^6(d-1)}
             \left(\sum_sV_s\right)^2.              \label{localgap}
\end{equation}

For every probability vector $\rho$,
$1-\|\rho\|_2^2\geq1-\max_x\rho(x)$. Hence some old type $j$ has
$V_j:=1-\|\rho_j\|_2^2\geq\delta(P)$. There is an old type $i$ with
$d_{ij}>0$: old type $j$ has positive degree, and reversibility supplies
a reverse neighbor, or $i=j$ for an internal edge. One of the slots at
that $i$ has variance $V_j$, so $\sum_sV_s\geq\delta(P)$. Apply
\eqref{localgap} and $\alpha_i\geq\alpha_{\min}$ to prove the first
bound in \eqref{gap}. Since $(d-2)^3/(d-1)\geq1/2$ for integer $d\geq3$
and $d\leq D$, the second bound follows. Returning to the unsymmetrized
laws preserves $\delta$ and can only decrease $\Phi$, so it also holds
there.

If $\Phi=0$, the gap forces $\delta=0$, and every root law is a point
mass $T(i)$. Matching marginals force every old-type-$j$ neighbor label
to equal $T(j)$. Every star is therefore deterministic, and
\eqref{legal} becomes $(QT)_{i,*}=(TR)_{i,*}$ for each $i$.
Conversely such a compatible point map produces deterministic star and
pair laws, all their entropies vanish, and $\Phi=0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The proposed zero-phase conditional variance is valid.}
At $L=0$, \eqref{cancellation} forces every positive-weight pair mutual
information and every $J_{it}$ to vanish. Thus each retained law is the
root law times independent neighbor marginals. Also the omitted neighbor
is independent of the root by its pair mutual information. Taking the
conditional variance trace in \eqref{legal}, for each omission $t$,
gives $V_t=\sum_{s\ne t}V_s$. Summing over all $t$ yields
$(d-2)\sum_sV_s=0$. The explicit proof above is a quantitative version
of precisely this valid argument, including slots of different types.

\paragraph{Consequences and limits.}
If $\alpha_i=N_i/n$ are empirical old-block proportions, the corresponding
unnormalized configuration entropy is $n\Phi$. Thus types at defect at
least $\varepsilon>0$ have count exponent at most
$-n\alpha_{\min}\varepsilon^2/(8D^6)$. A separate fixed-alphabet exact
type count then gives the macroscopic exclusion needed by the parent
recovery theorem. There is no compactness boundary issue from tiny
candidate classes or unused labels; occupied rows alone were used.
Internal matchings, no internal edges, unequal positive proportions, and
the one-class regular-graph case are covered. No parity condition enters
this local entropy proof beyond feasibility of the star laws; graph
factor construction imposes its own necessary parity conditions.

The threshold $d\geq3$ is substantive. For $d=2,k=1$, an even cycle
alternating two labels has $R=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&2\\2&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
positive root defect, and $\Phi=0$. Both the coefficient in
\eqref{cancellation} and the variance-sum coefficient vanish there.
Nothing here claims a result for degree-two covers, irregular total
degrees, modular variance, noisy edits, or historical priority.
