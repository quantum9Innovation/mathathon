Support and unknown-frame algorithm for regular frames without internal edges
============================================================================
15 November 2026. Independent root reconstruction. This note proves the
support and algorithmic implications. The entropy moment and macroscopic
rigidity premises are proved in separate companion notes; this file does
not silently assume spectral separation or spectral simplicity.

1. Precise model and uniform constants
-------------------------------------
Fix supplied constants K>=1 and D>=3. Let 1<=k<=K and let Q=(d_ij) be a
fixed nonnegative integer degree frame whose row sums are all the SAME
integer d, with 3<=d<=D. The undirected off-diagonal support is connected
(a one-vertex frame is allowed). Nonempty block sizes N_i obey
  N_i*d_ij=N_j*d_ji,   N_i*d_ii even.
Internal degrees may be zero or one. Omit zero factors. Independently
sample all nonzero internal regular and cross biregular factors uniformly
among simple graphs. The planted indicator matrix J satisfies AJ=JQ.

Write n=sum_i N_i and alpha_i=N_i/n. Along a frame path, every nonzero
degree ratio is a ratio of positive integers at most D. Consequently
  alpha_min >= rho := 1/[K D^(K-1)].
For k=1 this is immediate. A connected consistent frame fixes all size
ratios. There are finitely many such frames with k<=K and row sums<=D.
Thus all constants and eventual size thresholds can be uniform over this
finite family. This does not let K grow with n.

2. Arbitrary small row supports: a complete typed-configuration count
-------------------------------------------------------------------
First sample labeled stubs in every factor and retain loops/parallel
edges. An internal loop contributes two to the adjacency diagonal and
degree. Every active pool has at least rho*n stubs because its relevant
degree is a positive integer. Every vertex has total degree d>=3.

Call a nonempty vertex set S bad when no outside vertex has exactly one
incident edge into S, with multiplicities counted. Put s=|S| and let W be
its outside neighbor set, w=|W|. Let a count edges inside S (loops once),
b count S-W edges, and e=a+b. Then
  2a+b=d*s,   b>=2w,   e<=D*s,   w<=D*s/2,
and therefore the integer e-s-w is at least ceil(c*s), c=(d-2)/2>0.

A witness prescribes S,W, every stub at S, selected stubs at W, and all
e pairs. For s<=rho*n/(4D), a valid prescription with m pairs in an
internal pool L has probability
  1/[(L-1)(L-3)...(L-2m+1)],
and a prescription in a cross pool has probability 1/(L)_m. Every
denominator factor is at least rho*n/2. Independence bounds the full
prescription probability by (2/(rho*n))^e. Invalid typed prescriptions
have probability zero and may safely be included in an upper count.

For fixed s,w,e, there are at most
  binom(n,s) binom(n,w) * 2^(D*w) * (2D*s)^e
witnesses: choose S,W, select W stubs, and pair the selected 2e stubs.
This deliberately counts pairs not respecting types or not touching S.
An actual witness is a subset of these possibilities. The expected count
is at most
  exp(s+w) 2^(D*w) (4D/rho)^e
    * (s/n)^(e-s-w) * (s/w)^w.
Read the last factor as one for w=0. Since (s/w)^w<=exp(s/euler),
w<=D*s/2, e<=D*s, and e-s-w>=ceil(c*s), summing the at most(D*s+1)^2
choices of e,w gives
  E Z_s <= C^s (s/n)^ceil(c*s),
where one explicit choice is
  C=4(D+1)^2 exp(1+D/2+1/euler) * 2^(D^2/2) * (4D/rho)^D.
This counts arbitrary support composition across old blocks.

Choose a fixed positive eta satisfying
  eta <= min{rho/(4D), (2C exp(c))^(-1/c), rho/[2(D+1)]}.
For s>=2, the upper bound u_s=C^s(s/n)^(c*s) has consecutive ratio
  u_(s+1)/u_s <= C exp(c) eta^c <=1/2
when s+1<=eta*n. Hence
  sum_(s>=2, s<=eta*n) E Z_s <=2C^2(2/n)^(d-2).
On the simplicity event singleton S cannot be bad, since every neighbor
of a singleton in a simple positive-degree graph has exactly one edge
into it.

Every internal factor with degree a>=1 has limiting simplicity
probability exp[-(a^2-1)/4]; an a=0 factor is omitted and contributes
probability one. Cross (u,v) factors have limiting probability
exp[-(u-1)(v-1)/2]. Internal degree one and cross (1,1) factors are always
simple. Fixed degrees and positive old proportions give a uniform
eventual lower bound p0(K,D)>0 for their independent product. Conditioning
gives precisely uniform simple factors because their labeled-stub
multiplicities are constant. The companion entropy-count note supplies
a direct factorial-moment proof of this simplicity estimate.

It follows that in the intended simple model
  P(exists bad S with 1<=|S|<=eta*n)
     <=p0^(-1) 2C^2(2/n)^(d-2)=O_(K,D)(n^(-(d-2))).       (S)
This graph event is independent of any candidate labels or quotient.

If a matrix V over ANY field satisfies AV=VR and has nonzero row support
S, a vertex outside S with exactly one edge into S would give a single
nonzero row on the left and zero on the right. Thus on event(S)'s
complement no nonzero V with at most eta*n nonzero rows exists, for ANY
square R, vector dimension, or entries. No union over coefficients is
needed.

3. Near-point compatibility over the integers
--------------------------------------------
Let Y be a one-hot indicator of an equitable partition and verify the
INTEGER identity AY=YR. Let T be ANY k-by-r one-hot map, including
non-onto maps, and set S={v:Y_v!=(JT)_v}. Changed vertices and all their
neighbors cover at most (D+1)|S| vertices globally. If |S|<=eta*n, then
every old class contains a vertex v whose own row and every neighbor
row are unchanged, because
  (D+1)|S| <=rho*n/2 < N_i.
At such a vertex in old class i,
  (QT)_i=(AJT)_v=(AY)_v=(YR)_v=(TR)_i.
Therefore QT=TR over the integers, not only modulo two, and
  V=Y-JT satisfies AV=VR.
On the common no-small-support event, V must vanish. Thus every verified
integer equitable partition close to a planted point map is exactly
that map. This includes near-point maps creating tiny extra classes.

4. Macroscopic premise and complete structural implication
---------------------------------------------------------
For any candidate r<=K,R, let delta(Y) be its minimum normalized number
of row changes to a map JT. For old root label law rho_i,
  delta=sum_i alpha_i(1-max_x rho_i(x)).
The all-slot entropy count and real one-hot gap in the companion notes
give, uniformly over the finite bounded candidate family,
  E[#integer candidates with delta>=eta]
     <=C'(n+1)^B' exp[-alpha_min*eta^2*n/(8D^6)].          (M)
The normalization is explicit: Phi is entropy per vertex, so the exact
count exponent is n*Phi. No lower bound on candidate class masses is
required. The constants depend on K,D only.

Markov and a finite union over candidate quotients exclude all such
macroscopic candidates with exponentially high probability. Intersect
with the no-small-support event. Every equitable partition into at most
K nonempty classes is then EXACTLY Y=JT with QT=TR. Hence it is an
equitable coarsening of the planted partition, simultaneously for every
candidate quotient, including disconnected ones.

The planted partition is the unique finest equitable partition AMONG
partitions having at most K classes. This qualification matters: the
partition into n singletons always exists without a class-count bound.

5. Unknown frame enumeration and complete expected cost
------------------------------------------------------
Enumerate r=1,...,K and all nonnegative integer r-by-r matrices R whose
row sums are at most D. There are at most
  M=sum_(r=1)^K (D+1)^(r^2)
matrices, a fixed constant. One may restrict row sums to observed d on
a regular input, but doing so is unnecessary. Candidate connectedness
is also unnecessary. The planted pair(J,Q) is always included.

For each R, compute the binary kernel of
  I_r tensor A - R^T tensor I_n.
It represents AY=YR over F_2 for matrices Y in F_2^(n by r), and has
exactly 2^h_R vectors if h_R is the binary nullity. Enumerate every vector
using any kernel basis. Require every row to be a standard basis vector,
every candidate class to be nonempty, and the ORIGINAL INTEGER equation
AY=YR. Keep an accepted partition with the largest class count r.
The one-hot vectors remain distinct over the vector alphabet F_2^r.
There is no scalar-sign identification or real Jordan-form assumption.

The entropy-count companion proves the UNCONDITIONAL simple-model moment
  E 2^h_R <= C(K,D)(n+1)^B,
with the explicit conservative constants
  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K),   B=K*2^(KD)+F.
This includes rare graphs with large binary nullity. Binary Gaussian
elimination costs O_K(n^3) bit operations. Combining a basis, testing
rows, counting classes, and verifying integer neighbor counts costs at
most O_(K,D)(n^2) bit operations per vector, a deliberately loose bound.
Bounded degree and candidate row sums bound the counter bit lengths.
A basis and current candidate/best partition use O_K(n^2) bits; no full
kernel list is stored.

Thus the untruncated algorithm's expected total bit work is at most
  C(K,D)n^(B+3).
This expectation is not conditioned on successful structural recovery.
With a deterministic n^(B+5) bit-work cap, Markov bounds interruption
probability by O_(K,D)(n^-2). On interruption return FAILURE/ABSTENTION;
do not certify a partial maximum from an unfinished enumeration. A
bounded-cost work counter implements the cap within these loose bounds.
The capped algorithm has worst-case polynomial work for fixed K,D.

On the structural event every accepted Y=JT has nonempty classes, making
T onto, so r<=k. The planted k-class candidate appears, so maximal r=k.
An onto map between k old types and k labels is a permutation, recovering
the planted partition up to names. Exact neighbor counts then recover Q.
Combining (S),(M), and noninterruption gives high-probability recovery.
The exponent B is explicit and enormous; no practical speed is claimed.

6. Random-lift specialization and limits
----------------------------------------
Let H be a fixed connected SIMPLE d-regular base with d>=3 and k<=K
vertices. Set Q=A_H, choose every N_i=N, and independently sample one
uniform perfect matching for each base edge. This is exactly the random
N-lift model, up to independently naming the N vertices in each fiber.
All internal factors are absent and all cross factors are(1,1), so the
result is simple automatically and the simplicity conditioning cost is
one. Every N is admissible. Relabeling graph vertices does not affect
the proof or algorithm.

The theorem recovers its base fibers and unknown bounded base frame
without an old/new adjacency-eigenvalue gap. It does not assert simple
embedded eigenvalues or exclusion of new exact eigenvalue collisions.
Distinct coarse equitable partitions of H (for example in a cube) are
allowed: their lifted partitions are coarsenings of the recovered fibers.

Do not call this a 'regular cover' without qualification: in covering
theory that can refer to a transitive deck group rather than regularity
of the base graph. Typical random lifts are not assumed to have such a
deck group. No assertion is made for arbitrary deterministic lifts,
degree-two covers, noisy edits, growing base size, or unbounded K.
Historical priority of random-lift fiber reconstruction is investigated
separately and is not a premise or conclusion of this mathematical note.
