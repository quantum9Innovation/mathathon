1. Precise statement proved
---------------------------
Let Q be a fixed nonnegative integer degree frame of order k<=K with ALL
row sums equal to d>=3. Internal degrees ai=dii may be zero or one.
Nonempty class sizes ni satisfy ni*dij=nj*dji and ni*ai even. Sample
all nonzero labeled-stub internal pairings and cross bijections uniformly
and independently. A loop contributes TWO to adjacency and degree.

For any prime p, any r<=K, and ANY r-by-r matrix R over Fp, let Z_R be the
number of vector labelings Y in Fp^(n by r) satisfying AY=YR. With
  q*=p^K,
  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*(q*)^2,
  B=K*(q*)^d+F,
the exact configuration model obeys
  E Z_R <= (3d)^F (n+1)^B
for every admissible size. Connectedness is not needed for this bound.
It is uniform in R and the class proportions.

For connected fixed Q, conditioning all factors to be simple gives
  E_simple Z_R <=2 exp(K^2 d^2)(3d)^F (n+1)^B
eventually along all admissible size sequences. The eventual threshold
can be uniform over the finite bounded frame family. At p=2 the exponent
is B=K*2^(Kd)+K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K). It is deliberately not optimized and
is not asserted to be independent of d.

2. Exact factorial formula reconstructed
----------------------------------------
Each ordered star records its root vector and all d physical stub-target
labels, retaining old neighbor types and distinct stub slots. The local
sum equation determines ANY omitted slot with coefficient one. Thus the
legal star alphabet has exactly q^d elements, q=p^r, even when ai=0.

An empirical star type Ti is assigned to vertices in
  ni!/product_s Ti(s)!
ways. This already chooses roots. Internal directed stub categories
must be symmetric with even diagonal. Their numerator is
  product_(x<y) mii(x,y)! * product_x (mii(x,x)-1)!!,
and denominator is(ni*ai-1)!!. Cross categories contribute
  product_(x,y) mij(x,y)! /(ni*dij)!.
Multiply over all independent active factors. At ai=0 the factor is
absent; at ai=1 the same formula counts an ordinary perfect matching.

A graph configuration plus its root labeling determines every star
uniquely. Conversely the category matchings enforce exactly the required
target-root labels. Thus this formula counts every(configuration,Y) pair
once, including loops and parallel edges. There is no extra root-label
multinomial or graph-isomorphism division.

3. Uniform finite-size remainder and exact all-slot identity
----------------------------------------------------------
Let Psi be the unnormalized exponent
  sum_i ni H(star_i)
   -sum_i ni*ai/2 H(pair_ii)
   -sum_(i<j) ni*dij H(pair_ij).
Uniform factorial bounds, including zero counts, give
  contribution(T) <=exp{Psi(T)+F[log(dn+1)+1]}.
There are at most(n+1)^(K*(q*)^d) types. This is enough for the stated
polynomial moment once Psi<=0 is established.

Symmetrize physical slots WITHIN every old neighbor type. This preserves
root laws, pooled pair laws, the exact local sum constraint and matching
consistency. It increases star entropy; therefore original Psi is at
most its symmetrized value. Symmetrization is an analytic operation on
probability laws and need not preserve integral empirical counts.

For the symmetrized law, omit slot t and let Jit be the total correlation
of all retained slots conditional on the root. The omitted label is a
function of retained data, so averaging the d EXACT omission identities
gives
  H(star_i)=H(root_i)+(d-1)/d sum_s H(slot_s|root_i)
                         -(1/d)sum_t Jit.
Slot laws of a fixed old type now equal their pooled pair marginals;
different neighbor types are never exchanged. Using reversibility,
regular total degree and the pair entropy decomposition yields EXACTLY
  Psi_sym=-(d-2)/d sum_(i<=j) Eij Iij
                    -sum_i(ni/d)sum_t Jit <=0,
where Eii=ni*ai/2 and Eij=ni*dij for i<j. The double-counted internal
directed stubs produce precisely the internal half-edge coefficient.

This identity is exact for symmetrized star laws. It should NOT be stated
as an equality for arbitrary unsymmetrized empirical types with pooled
pair entropies: their star entropy can be strictly smaller. The root
proposal already permits the needed symmetrization, so this clarification
is not a fatal gap. The correct raw-type conclusion is the upper bound.

No division by d occurs in the finite field. Averaging entropies by d
is real arithmetic, so p dividing d is harmless. Coefficient one recovers
the omitted slot in every characteristic; p=2 is valid for vector labels.

4. Simplicity and parity audit
------------------------------
An internal degree-zero factor contributes probability ONE and must be
omitted. An internal degree-one pairing is always simple and requires
only the actual parity ni even. A cross(1,1) factor is always a simple
perfect matching and may join equal ODD-sized classes.

For internal ai>=1, the standard loop/double-edge means sum to
  (ai^2-1)/4.
For active cross factors the double-edge mean is
  (dij-1)(dji-1)/2.
The resulting joint simplicity exponent is
  Lambda=sum_(i:ai>=1)(ai^2-1)/4
          +sum_(i<j:active)(dij-1)(dji-1)/2 <=K^2 d^2.
Do not substitute ai=0 into(ai^2-1)/4: that would incorrectly give a
negative contribution. The new source explicitly handles this boundary.

The note gives the elementary factorial-moment/Bonferroni proof: disjoint
bad patterns factor; consistent overlapping distinct bad patterns have
positive edge excess and vanish at order O(1/min ni). The resulting
zero-event limit is exp(-Lambda)>0. Factor independence and constant
stub multiplicities then give exactly the intended independent UNIFORM
simple factor model after conditioning. There is no extra even-ni
assumption beyond ni*ai even, and no loss of admissible odd lift sizes.

6. What this PASS does and does not imply
-----------------------------------------
The source proves the full modular first-moment input needed for the new
regular-frame algorithm, with internal degrees0/1 and random cross
matchings included. Its exact factorial count and polynomial remainder
also apply to real one-hot alphabets with an INTEGER local equation,
using legal-star count at most r^d. Real rigidity still has a separate
variance proof; modular addition cannot replace that integer equation.

In the lift specialization, the base is fixed, connected, simple and
d-regular, every internal factor is absent, and all active cross factors
are independent uniform perfect matchings. Simplicity is automatic, so
there is no conditioning loss for any lift size. No old/new spectral
gap or spectral simplicity assertion enters this moment bound.

This audit does not claim a degree-independent exponent, practical
enumeration cost, noisy robustness, growing base size, historical firstness,
or the full recovery theorem without its companion rigidity/support
arguments. The d=2 boundary retains a nonpositive moment exponent but
loses the positive mutual-information coefficient, consistent with the
cycle obstructions to the separate recovery assertion.
