Independent signing session -- research report, 15 November 2026
===============================================================

Outcome: one proved restricted signing construction, an exact characterization
of its scope, and two decisive obstructions to concrete proposed mechanisms.
No universal (2+o_g(1))*sqrt(d-1) signing theorem was obtained. Historical
novelty has not been established for the statements proved in this session.
All session artifacts are under spectral-gap/independent-signing-session/.

1. Positive result: Hermitian orientation followed by phase conversion
-------------------------------------------------------------------
If G admits a graph homomorphism to an odd cycle C_L, then

  min_sigma ||A_sigma|| <= sec(pi/(2L))*maxroot(mu_G)
                       <= 2 sec(pi/(2L))*sqrt(d-1),

where d>=2 bounds the maximum degree. The coefficient is

  2 + pi^2/(4L^2) + O(L^-4).

This follows from the EXISTING Greaves--Mohar--O orientation theorem:
choose an imaginary Hermitian adjacency H with ||H||<=maxroot(mu_G),
conjugate by phases exp(i*pi*(L-1)*color/(2L)), and take its entrywise
real part. Every edge now has absolute real part cos(pi/(2L)); real-part
contraction gives the bound. For C_L itself the matching root is
2cos(pi/(2L)) and every real signing has norm 2, so the factor relative
to the matching root is sharp. C5 maps give coefficient 2.102924...,
below 3/sqrt(2), on that restricted graph class.

The exact gap is proved, not merely noted: for a connected nonbipartite G,
nonzero constant-modulus scalar phase conversion exists iff G maps to an
odd cycle. If L(G) is the greatest such odd length, the best conversion
constant is c_*(G)=cos(pi/(2L(G))). In particular chromatic number >=4
precludes every nonzero constant-modulus conversion. The proof reduces
phases modulo pi and classifies the cyclic subgroup generated by arcsin c.

For d-regular G any phases with |sin(theta_v-theta_u)|>=c satisfy

  c^2 <= (1-lambda_min(A_G)/d)/2.

Thus bases with lambda_min >= -2sqrt(d-1)-o(1) prevent conversion loss
1/c from tending to one, for every fixed d>=3. This limits the specified
conversion, not all possible signings or orientations.

Proof: phase-rounding.tex; compiled reading copy: phase-rounding.pdf.
Independent corroboration/classification: simultaneous/
phase-rounding-and-selection-obstructions.txt, Sections 1--3.

2. Squared and polynomial dilation: arbitrary-girth obstruction
--------------------------------------------------------------
For every finite bipartite graph G and every full-support distribution on
edge signings,

  E det(xI-A_sigma^2) is real-rooted  iff  G is a forest.

For bipartition sizes a>=b, put p_sigma(x)=det(xI-B_sigma^T B_sigma).
The leaf polynomial is x^(a-b)*p_sigma(x)^2. A positive average of squares
of equal-degree monic real-rooted polynomials is real-rooted only if all
polynomials agree: divide out their common gcd, and the remaining average
is strictly positive on the real line. Any cycle makes a signed adjacency
coefficient depend nontrivially on its cycle-sign Fourier character.

This rules out the ordinary squared-characteristic-polynomial averaging
mechanism for EVERY regular bipartite cyclic graph, at every girth, even
though the original bipartite signing problem is already solved by MSS.
Suvagiya v2 already gives the C4 example; we do not claim that example.

For any fixed nonconstant even polynomial p, the same averaging fails on
arbitrarily long even cycles. If degree(p)=r, n=rm, and its leading
coefficient is c, the two cycle flux classes have

  tr p(A_+)^m - tr p(A_-)^m = 4 n c^m !=0.

This supplies the necessary distinct compressed spectra. Taking one
derivative does not universally fix the problem: it fails on connected
K3,3, with an exact Sturm certificate. Two disjoint long even cycles give
derivative failure at arbitrary girth. Connected large-girth derivative
selection remains unaddressed.

Proof and source comparisons: dilation/squared-average-obstruction.txt.
Exact verifier: dilation/verify_squared_average.py.

3. Bass-circle selection: explicit connected cubic girth-five failure
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A 14-vertex cubic graph of girth five, with all but one edge sign fixed,
has child adjacency characteristic polynomials

  p_+(x)=(x^2-2)^2[r(x)-s(x)],
  p_-(x)=(x^2-2)^2[r(x)+s(x)],

  r=x^10-17x^8+96x^6-204x^4+120x^2-4,
  s=4x^5-16x^3-8x.

Their average has every root strictly inside [-2sqrt2,2sqrt2]. This has
an exact alternating-sign certificate for r(x)=f(x^2) at
0,1/16,1,4,5,8. Consequently the conditional average REDUCED Bass
polynomial has all roots on |u|=1/sqrt2. But

  p_+(2sqrt2)=36*(188-240sqrt2)<0,

so that child has a positive outlier; reflection gives a negative outlier
for the other child. Every convex combination of the two adjacency
polynomials is real-rooted by MSS. Thus common interlacing plus circle
support of the conditional parent does not give simultaneous selection.

This is a conditional last-edge obstruction at girth FIVE; it makes no
arbitrary-girth lower-bound claim and no claim that all signings of this
graph are bad. A version with controlled girth-dependent slack is not
refuted. Full graph, proof, primary comparisons, exact BigInt verification:
nonbacktracking/bass-circle-counterexample.txt and verification.js.

Also: the one-step signed nonbacktracking matrix has singular values d-1
(n copies) and 1 (n(d-1) copies), independently of signs. Direct Hermitian
dilation of that matrix cannot optimize its spectral radius. Long powers
or an adapted metric would need substantive additional control.

MOP ALREADY proves random-signing norm at most
2sqrt(d-1)*(1+O((loglog n)^4/r^2)) with probability >=1-n^-100 when
d<=polylog n and bicycle-free radius r>>(loglog n)^2. This is genuinely
two-sided. High girth supplies tree balls with r=floor((g-2)/2), but
arbitrary g->infinity does not supply the required comparison with n.
The unresolved desired theorem is uniform over graph order, allowing
g to grow arbitrarily slowly relative to loglog n.

The supplied two audits establish the local mixed-measure barrier at
3/sqrt2; this session did not recompute it or present it as new. The friend's
announced high-girth theorem and degree-32 hardness statement were not
used or duplicated. General Bilu--Linial is treated as disproved, not open.

An additional unreviewed author artifact was found claiming exact
arbitrary-girth outliers (HuggingFace PureOne/bilu-linial-extensive-spectral-

It is preexisting and is not promoted to an independently certified result.
Its claimed exact obstruction remains compatible with 2+o_g(1).

Remaining viable target: a simultaneous selection principle with controlled
slack and an additional invariant, or a norm-preserving real conversion
beyond constant-modulus scalar phases. None is proved here. Existing
matrix-product/rational tools were not imported without a valid reduction.
