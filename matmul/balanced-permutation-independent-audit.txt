Finding: PASS of the identities, finite variance formulas, collision
obstruction, arithmetic costs and exact mixed-permutation trace reduction.
The source does not claim a completed embedding or useful new AMM width
theorem. Its unresolved weighted collision-pattern sum is a real missing
step, not discharged by the calculations below.

1. The balanced partition has H1=q1_s and HH^T=qI_s. The source's W
   therefore has W1=1 and E W=I. Pi_H=(1/q)H^T Pi_s H is exactly an
   orthogonal projection of rank s-1 on 1-perp. The identity
   W=J_0+[(m-1)/(s-1)]Pi_H and the explicit row-space factor S^T S=W
   are correct, including the q=1 endpoint W=I.

2. Bucket aggregation followed by a row mean/scaling takes O(m(n+v))
   arithmetic. A Householder transform on the bucket coordinates can
   eliminate the redundant centered row with O(s) work per data column;
   no dense s-by-s product is hidden. Independent averaging stores the
   constant direction once and has width at most 1+d(s-1), with the
   displayed construction and explicit output costs.

3. T=lambda Pi_H-Pi_0 gives E T^2=(lambda-1)Pi_0. Independence and
   centering kill cross-products for independent colors. Both Frobenius
   AMM variance upper bounds and the Markov probability consequence
   follow exactly. This is vector-output covariance, not automatically
   the quadratic-form variance relevant to a subspace embedding.

4. The quadratic variance formula was independently derived by the three
   classes of pairs: identical, sharing one coordinate, and disjoint.
   Their inclusion probabilities p, r3 and r22 and the zero-sum-vector
   identities in Section 4 are correct. For a unit contrast in m=4,q=2
   the formula gives 1/2; for the balanced sign vector it gives 2.

5. A coordinate contrast is killed exactly when its two coordinates
   collide. Otherwise its SQUARED norm is multiplied by 1/(1-p).
   Independent averaging therefore has distortion (p-K/d)/(1-p) with
   K binomial(d,p), and simultaneous erasure probability p^d. The
   norm-preservation discussion must be read in its explicitly displayed
   squared-norm convention; no lower bound on other sketches follows.

6. K_0=H_0^T H_0-qJ_0 annihilates constants, giving
   T_c=gamma(P_c-J_0)^T K_0(P_c-J_0)-Pi_0. The exact cyclic trace
   reduction for A Tbar B has 2t T factors. Expanding each quadratic
   factor gives at most 4t centered permutation occurrences. Equations
   (16)-(18) retain their correct noncommutative order and deterministic
   coefficients.

7. Grouping identical colored cells gives exactly the mixed-product
   structure in the imported spectral-gap ledger. Its singleton/conflict
   definitions and SG-11/14/16 envelopes match the source's Section 6.
   Taking 4t<=m is sufficient for the stated distinct-cell range. Empty
   monomials and one-cell powers are separately and correctly handled.
   Attainment of the scalar envelope does not imply dominance of those
   supports in the weighted matrix trace sum.
