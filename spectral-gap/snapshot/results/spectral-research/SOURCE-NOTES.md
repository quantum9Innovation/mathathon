# Source checking notes

Read on 14 November 2026. These notes distinguish primary statements read in full from arXiv metadata used as an abstract-level literature check.

## Full papers or substantial primary sections read

- [Bordenave and Collins, arXiv:1801.00876v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00876v4). Statement and overview of Theorem 2, including exact interpretation of matrix-valued coefficients and Hausdorff convergence of the new spectrum.
- [Dumitriu and Zhu, arXiv:1905.06487v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06487v5). Definitions 2.1 through 2.3, expansion interpretation, Lemma 4.5, Theorem 4.3 and the associated hypergraph bulk dictionary.
- [Song, Fan and Miao, arXiv:2310.01771v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01771v2). Introduction and definitions, Theorems 1.3 through 1.9, inherited and obvious eigenvalue conventions.
- [Hermon and Olesker-Taylor, arXiv:2102.02801v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02801v2). Theorem E and its precise parameter qualifiers.

## Abstract-level current literature checks

- [Huang, McKenzie and Yau, arXiv:2412.20263v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20263v2). Fixed-degree eigenvalue rigidity, Tracy-Widom edge limits and normalized 69 percent Ramanujan statement.
- [Huang, McKenzie and Yau, arXiv:2405.12161v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12161v1). Earlier rigidity statement with explicit probability bound.
- [Huang and Xu, arXiv:2610.37463v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37463v1). Local law and delocalization for random lifts, including exclusions around singular energies.
- [Zhu and Zhu, arXiv:2312.03300v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03300v3), revised 25 September 2026. Nonbacktracking bulk eigenvectors and reduced-matrix spectral measures for graphs and hypergraphs.
- [Faust and Sottile, arXiv:2206.13649v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.13649v5). Periodic critical-point bounds and partial spectral-edges results.
- [Dikstein, Liu and Wigderson, arXiv:2411.08839v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08839v1). Sparse abelian Cayley high-dimensional expander constructions.

## Local sources

All four directories under `.paper-mastery/` supply the statements, derivation guides and documented source-repair cautions. They are not formal certificates. The subset used here includes the executive maps, central derivations and assumptions files of each dossier and `SOURCE.md` for version control. The new papers have not been subjected to the same complete reading protocol.

## Novelty boundary

There is no exhaustive backward-and-forward citation audit in this initial map. Every proposed research direction remains a candidate until its closest literature has been checked. A recent arXiv preprint is cited with its version and exact advertised scope, not treated as independently verified mathematics.
