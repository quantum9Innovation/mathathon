Stochastic product priority/input-model audit -- 2026-11-14
==========================================================

Scope and finding
-----------------
This is a targeted primary-source audit of the completed manuscript
docs/stochastic-product-spectral.tex. Earlier source/proof audits were read
first; the independent-copy attribution, EPSI/MSP checks, and general
Gram/Krylov literature were not restarted. Only this dedicated file is
modified for this continuation.

The current result is best classified as a separate-factor access-cost
construction and a corollary of established stochastic-solver/PCA tools.
It does not introduce stochastic GEPs, covariance-product unbiasing,
nonconvex-component SVRG, accelerated proximal point, or inverse-power PCA.
The closest inspected end-to-end template is the full Allen-Zhu--Li 2017
CCA paper, Appendix F.3, which already combines essentially those solver
ideas and has the same input^(3/4) acceleration pattern. Its explicitly
stated paired-data theorem does not supply the current raw-factor product
oracle or its uniform product-relative variance bound.

No inspected primary theorem was shown to imply the entire claimed

 ~O(Ir + m^(omega+zeta) + r I^(3/4)m^(1/2)/sqrt(theta)
    +np+TMM(n,r,p)), I=m(n+p),

for arbitrary fixed A,B without additional factor energy, eigengap, or
condition parameters. This is a scoped comparison, not a global priority
certificate. The argument below identifies what a direct reduction would
have to implement, rather than relying on the absence of matching words.

1. Closest accelerated GEP/CCA theorem
-------------------------------------
Zeyuan Allen-Zhu and Yuanzhi Li, Doubly Accelerated Methods for Faster CCA
and Generalized Eigendecomposition, ICML2017. The full version contains
Theorems4.2,6.3,F.3 and Appendix F.3.1--2:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/allen-zhu17b.html
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06017

Source facts: Theorem4.2 gives a general GEP oracle reduction, charging
the cost Upsilon of B^(-1)A actions. Its gap-free consequence has inverse
square-root accuracy dependence. TheoremF.3 instead uses paired data X,Y,
A=[[0,X^T Y],[Y^T X,0]]/s, B=diag(X^T X,Y^T Y)/s, and accelerated SVRG
on indefinite sample quadratics. For one CCA vector its cost is

 ~O(J [1+sqrt(kappa')/(s^(1/4)sqrt(epsilon*sigma_1))]),
 J=nnz(X,Y),
 kappa'=2 max_i{||X_i||^2,||Y_i||^2}/lambda_min(B).

Here sigma_1 is normalized canonical correlation. The general-rank bound
also charges projection costs and contains sigma_r and rank parameters.
Appendix F.3 explicitly derives sample Hessian smoothness from paired row
norms and aggregate strong convexity from epsilon*sigma_r*lambda_min(B).
Thus both the signed quadratic and its acceleration are direct antecedents.

Own input-model reduction analysis
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Use different letters for the manuscript's factors: M=A0 B0, and its
small coefficient pencil H0=G Qc G/c with mass G. Substituting this pair
into the GEP theorem is legitimate. Because G has constant condition,
an H0 action and a solve in G can be supplied in ~O(I), giving the usual
~O(Ir/sqrt(theta)) oracle bound, plus projections and output. Supplying
the two small matrices explicitly instead pays true Gram construction.
Neither substitution yields I + I^(3/4)m^(1/2)/sqrt(theta): that improvement
needs the specific cheap sample action and expected-square bound proved
in the new manuscript. The theorem's Upsilon is not automatically a cheap
stochastic signed-pencil oracle.

The paired-data theorem is not obtained simply by setting X=A0^T,Y=B0.
Those matrices share s=m sample rows, and X^T Y=M, but CCA uses marginal
covariance metrics. After restricting feature supports, if both original
factors have full inner rank m, col(X)=col(Y)=R^m. The normalized CCA
cross matrix therefore has all m singular values equal to one: it measures
the principal angles between equal sample subspaces. This loses the
singular spectrum of M. For example A0=diag(T,T^(-1)) and
B0=diag(T^(-1),T) have M=I for every T; CCA's normalized spectrum is
uninformative about the unrestricted Euclidean product spectrum in general.

Invertible FEATURE coordinate changes preserve canonical correlations,
while generally changing the desired Euclidean PLS singular spectrum.
Reciprocal INNER/sample changes preserving A0 B0 retain the product but
change marginal covariance matrices; in the full-inner-rank direct CCA
substitution the correlations still remain all one. Neither observation
is an obstruction to every possible reduction, but it rules out the
direct identification of the two tasks.

The source also permits isotropic ridge marginals (Remark2.5). With large
ridges, its normalized cross matrix approaches a scalar multiple of M.
This does not give the desired uniform runtime by itself. In the displayed
diagonal example, take equal ridge tau much larger than T^2. Then
sigma_1 is of order 1/tau, while kappa' is of order T^2/tau. Their ratio
kappa'/sigma_1 is of order T^2, so the accelerated bound still grows with
T, although ||M||=1. Merely observing sigma_1 becoming small would be
insufficient, since kappa' also decreases; the ratio calculation is the
relevant check.

Coarse whitening removes this factor dependence analytically, but feeding
the resulting paired features to the published data theorem requires
materializing transformed factors or paying their actual row-generation
cost. A general row of (A0 W)^T is A0 times a dense coefficient vector,
not an original row available in O(n+p) time. The alternative small
representation G^(1/2) Qc^(1/2) requires true Gram roots. The new raw-row
oracle avoids both materializations. Replacing those operations by a
unit-cost feature query would discard the comparison's main bottleneck.

2. Stochastic PLS: the cross-product is covered, the bound is different
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Raman Arora, Poorya Mianjy, Teodor Marinov, Stochastic Optimization for
Multiview Representation Learning using Partial Least Squares, ICML2016:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/aroraa16.html
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/aroraa16.pdf

Source facts: Theorem3.2 bounds expected additive loss in captured
cross-covariance by O(sqrt(r/T)) after normalization; Theorem3.3 analyzes
the matrix-exponentiated-gradient variant. The target is a population PLS
objective E[x^T U V^T y]. These are sample-complexity/objective guarantees,
not an asserted high-accuracy finite-input spectral-tail arithmetic bound.

Own reduction and cost accounting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
There is an exact arbitrary-product representation: draw inner index j
with probability p_j and set

 x=A0[:,j]/sqrt(p_j), y=B0[j,:]^T/sqrt(p_j).

Then E[xy^T]=M. Thus it would be false to claim arbitrary products lie
outside the PLS input model. A sample costs O(n+p) from original factors.
The minimum possible squared Frobenius second moment among these index
distributions is

 min_p sum_j ||A0[:,j]||^2 ||B0[j,:]||^2 / p_j
   = (sum_j ||A0[:,j]|| ||B0[j,:]||)^2 = S^2.

The additive objective guarantee consequently retains S after rescaling.
Even rank-one relative top accuracy theta requires the sufficient
sample bound of order (S/||M||)^2/theta^2 from this theorem. The ratio
S/||M|| can be arbitrarily large for cancelling factors. A rank-one
relative Rayleigh guarantee could be converted to a relative spectral tail
by a suitable Nystrom finish; the objection is not that conversion is
impossible. The issue is the theorem's accuracy and variance cost before
that finish. Coarse coordinate balancing can improve variance only after
charging for transformed feature access, as above. This does not recover
the new runtime from the inspected PLS theorem alone.

3. GEP input models already include covariance polynomials
----------------------------------------------------------
Chris Junchi Li and Michael I. Jordan, Nonconvex Stochastic Scaled Gradient
Descent and Generalized Eigenvector Problems, UAI2023:
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v216/li23b/li23b.pdf

Source facts: Equations7--8 use independent unbiased matrix estimates for
products of expectations. Theorem2 and Corollary4 require local positive
tangent curvature, a specified initialization neighborhood, and a
sub-Weibull gradient parameter. Their angular rate is of order
sqrt(log(T)/T), with curvature and noise constants. Later sections provide
strict-saddle/global analysis, not the current gap-free spectral-tail cost.

Own scope conclusion: the new product matrices can be passed as suitable
unbiased polynomial estimates after their cost and tails are justified.
The abstract stochastic input model is therefore not new. Repeated or
arbitrarily clustered top eigenvalues do not furnish the uniform positive
tangent curvature needed by the inspected local theorem. No simple
substitution yields the desired theta^(-1/2) cost or all-rank tail guarantee.

Ian Gemp, Charlie Chen, Brian McWilliams, The Symmetric Generalized
Eigenvalue Problem as a Nash Equilibrium, ICLR2023:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.04993

Source facts: Section1 explicitly permits outer-product expectations;
Equation3 includes a signed covariance polynomial for ICA. Section3 uses
independent minibatches for unbiased products. Theorem2 proves
deterministic/full-batch asymptotic convergence with positive top-r gaps.
AppendixE explains remaining issues in extending that proof to Algorithm2.

Own scope conclusion: a product-covariance representation and its
independent-copy updates are plainly within this paper's scope. It supplies
neither a finite global iteration bound of the required accuracy dependence
nor the raw-factor preprocessing/second-moment arithmetic theorem. It is
prior art for the representation, not a substantiated subsuming complexity
bound. This distinction is stronger and more accurate than saying our
matrix class is outside published GEP models.

4. Kernel/dual PCA does not make product feature access free
----------------------------------------------------------
Lijun Zhang, Tianbao Yang, Jinfeng Yi, Rong Jin, Zhi-Hua Zhou, Stochastic
Optimization for Kernel PCA, AAAI2016:
https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/10242
https://cs.nju.edu.cn/_upload/tpl/01/0b/267/template267/zhouzh.files/publication/aaai16spca.pdf

Source facts: The method optimizes a strongly convex Frobenius fitting
objective with a nuclear-norm penalty and targets singular-value shrinkage
of K. It accepts low-rank unbiased estimates of K, for example sampled
kernel columns or random features. Its last-iterate convergence is
O(1/T); it is not the current inverse-square-root-accuracy spectral-tail
algorithm.

Own access analysis: the product can be viewed through the kernel
K_ij=a_i^T BB^T a_j, with a_i original A0 rows, or a small covariance
kernel after support whitening. Evaluating a generic such kernel column
requires true factor actions or a previously built Gram. A cheaper unbiased
kernel estimate still needs a variance/cost construction; kernel duality
does not supply it. Likewise a polynomial kernel over coefficient vectors
does not equal the noncommuting G Qc G product simply because both involve
polynomials. No kernel theorem inspected here removes those charges.

5. Sobczyk et al.: bounded explicit core use only
------------------------------------------------
Aleksandros Sobczyk, Marko Mladenovic, Mathieu Luisier, Invariant subspaces
and PCA in nearly matrix multiplication time, NeurIPS2024:
https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/21f6bdb10b7e019543daf35012e79210-Paper-Conference.pdf

Source facts: Theorem1.1 takes an explicit definite pencil and computes a
spectral projector with logarithms of inverse gap and mass conditioning.
Theorem4.1 explicitly starts given covariance H=X^T X. Theorem4.2 retains
q=Theta(log(n)/sqrt(epsilon_PCA)) block-Krylov iterations. AppendixB.1
imports the EIG routine for a bounded explicit matrix, with approximate
diagonalization in TMM(L) times polylogarithms of dimension/accuracy.

Own comparison conclusion: this is useful primary evidence for processing
an already constructed small core. It does not eliminate true Gram setup,
nor certify constant-cost implicit Gram entries. A gap-free additive Ritz
extraction would additionally need to state how its perturbation/accuracy
is chosen; exact invariant-subspace precision alone is gap-dependent.
The current growing-rank comparison already charges a conservative
O(L^4) max-pivot-Jacobi core cost below the leading input iterations, so
using a faster core solver cannot invalidate the claimed strict exponent
separation in that instance. No new solver claim is needed there.

6. Reasoned priority assessment
-------------------------------
Already present in the primary literature:
  * stochastic generalized Rayleigh objectives and covariance polynomials;
  * independent-copy unbiasing and signed sample Hessians;
  * the accelerated nonconvex-component CCA solve template, including its
    characteristic full-gradient-cost^(3/4) expression;
  * matrix-free metric simulation and approximate shifted inverses;
  * fast computation on a supplied explicit covariance/core.

The concrete extra obligation met by the current manuscript is different:
from arbitrary dense raw A0,B0, obtain product-support coordinates and an
O(mk)-cost sample action with bounded normalized expected square, using
~O(I+m^(omega+zeta)) setup and ~O(I) adaptive probability refreshes;
then retain gap-free spectral-tail accuracy through rank-r deflation and
include all explicit output work. The parameter controlling the solve is
theta relative to the product, not a factor condition or an actual gap.

The inspected named GEP papers allow the mathematical matrix/oracle class,
so describing the contribution as a new input model would be misleading.
Their stated concrete theorems do not discharge that raw-factor access
obligation. Once this obligation is proved, most of the new total upper
bound is indeed a corollary of old generic optimization tools. The current
manuscript's modest 'factor-access oracle and total-cost consequences'
description matches that evidence. Whether this application-specific
corollary, or an equivalent construction, appeared elsewhere remains a
priority question. The comparisons above support the stated improvement
over the checked routes; they do not establish global SOTA by exclusion.
