RAW FRACTIONAL-FUNCTION UPDATES AND THE REMAINING QUADRATIC BRIDGE
2026-11-15. Owner: /root/fresh_quadratic_bridge.

STATUS AND SCOPE
===============
The original product-LRA hard gate is still OPEN. In particular, nothing
here proves N^2 total time for N=m^(9/8), r=1, theta=1/m. The positive-part
of a nonzero threshold still contains an unknown Gram/inverse mass, and
the new square-root-update theorem cannot be substituted for that missing
operation without a further argument.

A useful broader conventional-arithmetic algorithm DOES follow. Low-rank
updates of both whole-line fractional Hermitian maps, and of rectangular
absolute/polar fractional maps, can be computed on their intrinsic Holder
scale in ~O(Nmr+m^3), without PSD promises, condition/gap parameters,
factor cancellation parameters or a supplied base matrix function. A
direct core formula avoids doubling perturbation rank. A bipartite parity
identity avoids another factor two when passing to rectangular dilations.

The generic O(log^2) rank/cost theorem below is fully explicit with the
ordinary dyadic trapezoid rule. The sharp leading-rank upgrade uses the
checked Gaussian localization lemma in sharp-fractional-holder.tex and
the constructive scalar realization being written by sqrt_update_sota in
sharp-rank-algorithm-search.txt. Those scalar details must receive their
own audit before promoting the sharp-rank IMPLEMENTATION claim.

No global novelty claim is made: general rational low-rank matrix-function
updates and rational Krylov methods are established work (BCKS2021).
The potential extra content is the complete raw-input, intrinsic-error,
condition-independent cost/rank theorem and its sharp leading constants.

1. GENERAL HERMITIAN INPUT AND ALGORITHM STATEMENT
================================================
Let X be real N-by-m, J an explicit real symmetric m-by-m matrix, and

  S=X J X^T.

Let the supplied real symmetric perturbation have a thin representation
E=U K U^T, with U N-by-r, K symmetric, 1<=r<=m<=N. Neither J nor K
needs to be invertible or an involution. Put T=S+E. Both endpoints may
be indefinite and singular. Fix 0<alpha<1, beta=1-alpha, and define

  f_e(x)=|x|^alpha,
  f_o(x)=sign(x)|x|^alpha,
  g_+(x)=(-i x)^alpha, g_-(x)=(i x)^alpha.

For f_e and f_o the algorithm returns a real signed factor
F=Z Sigma Z^T, Sigma diagonal with entries +1/-1, such that

  ||F-[f(T)-f(S)]|| <= epsilon ||E||^alpha

with probability >=1-delta. Its width is O_alpha(r log^2(2r/epsilon)),
and its conventional arithmetic is

  ~O_alpha(Nmr+m^3).

The logarithms suppressed are only dimensions, accuracy and confidence.
Output expansion is extra O(N^2 r log^2(2r/epsilon)). The complex branch
maps admit an analogous complex thin factor P Q^T of the same order of
width/cost. All matrices in this note are real except these temporary
complex shifted solves and complex-branch outputs.

For alpha=1/2, the computation uses arithmetic, comparisons and positive
scalar square roots, with fixed constants approximated by ordinary scalar
series. Dyadic alpha uses repeated square roots as well. Arbitrary fixed
alpha requires its input-dependent scalar-power evaluations to be added
to the model/charged explicitly; no hidden unit-cost power oracle is
asserted. There is no finite-word stability theorem here.

2. INTRINSIC CORE WITHOUT ARTIFICIAL RANK DOUBLING
================================================
Compute a thin rank-revealing QR U=Q R with Q^T Q=I_k, k<=r. Set

  C=R K R^T, E=Q C Q^T, a=||C||_F.

If a=0 return zero exactly. Otherwise

  ||E||=||C|| <=a<=sqrt(r)||E||.

This costs O(Nr^2+r^3), inside Nmr+m^3. C can be singular; none of the
following formulas uses C^-1 or its square root. In particular, replacing
C by a difference of shifted PSD cores is unnecessary and would obscure
the exact perturbation-rank count.

For any z off the real axis write

  R_z=(S-zI)^-1, R'_z=(T-zI)^-1,
  V_z=R_z Q, M_z=Q^T R_z Q,
  H_z=(I+M_z C)^-1, K_z=C H_z.

The determinant lemma proves H_z exists. The exact identities are

  H_z=I-Q^T R'_z Q C,
  K_z=C(I+M_z C)^-1=(I+C M_z)^-1 C,
  D_z:=R_z-R'_z=V_z K_z V_z^T.                 (1)

For real symmetric S,T the resolvents and K_z are complex symmetric;
the transposes in (1) are intentional, rather than adjoints. At z=it,
t>0, Hermitian resolvent bounds give

  ||H_z|| <=M(t):=1+a/t,
  ||K_z|| <=a M(t),
  ||D_z|| <=min(2/t,a/t^2).                    (2)

The H identity follows by multiplication or the usual Woodbury formula;
it remains valid for singular C. It is the key to eliminating dependence
on the supplied representation's conditioning or cancellation.

3. ACTUAL RAW RESOLVENTS, SYMMETRY AND CONJUGATION
================================================
Use one constant-distortion OSE to form G0=L^T L approximating G=X^T X:

  (1-eta0)G0 <=G<=(1+eta0)G0, eta0=1/4.

This is ~O(Nm+m^3) conventional arithmetic with logarithmic confidence
dependence (e.g. Nelson--Nguyen2013, Theorem9). L is full row rank on its
support, and no true Gram G is formed. Put K0=L J L^T. This is symmetric
even when J is singular.

For zeta=it let z=1/zeta, A_z=I-zJG, A0_z=I-zJG0, b=J X^T h. The
exact physical resolvent and the implemented iteration are

  (S-zeta I)^-1 h =-zeta^-1[h+zXw_*], A_z w_*=b,
  w_0=0,
  w_(ell+1)=w_ell+A0_z^-1(b-A_z w_ell).          (3)

Each true G action is X^T(XW) on the current thin RHS block. The coarse
inverse is explicitly

  A0_z^-1=I+z J L^T(I-zK0)^-1 L.

At a purely imaginary z, I-zK0 is invertible, so per-node small setup is
ordinary complex elimination, O(m^3), not an eigenvalue/inverse oracle.

Writing G-G0=L^T D L with ||D||<=eta0 for analysis, the coarse error
recurrence is

  L(w_(ell+1)-w_*)=zK0(I-zK0)^-1 D L(w_ell-w_*).

Since max_(lambda real)|z lambda/(1-z lambda)|<=1, and the physical
resolvent correction satisfies ||[(I-zS)^-1-I]h||<=||h||, the SAME fixed
iteration length ell yields an actual linear operator Rhat_zeta with

  ||Rhat_zeta-R_zeta|| <=C0 eta0^ell/t.          (4)

This is an operator-norm statement: the fixed iteration is linear and
the bound holds for every h. It therefore applies to the entire Q block,
not only separately to chosen columns. Set nu=C0 eta0^ell.

Symmetry matters in the next step. Put B0_z=A0_z^-1 J. Woodbury gives

  B0_z=J+z J L^T(I-zK0)^-1 L J,

which is complex symmetric. The finite inverse polynomial in (3) is

  sum_(j=0)^(ell-1) z^j B0_z[(G-G0)B0_z]^j.

Each alternating product begins and ends with B0_z and is symmetric
under transpose. Hence Rhat_zeta is exactly complex symmetric. Real
arithmetic and the same iteration also give

  Rhat_(-it)=conj(Rhat_(it)).                    (5)

No PSD or normality of Rhat is needed for the whole-line algorithm.

4. UNIFORM SMALL-CORE AND NODE ERRORS
===================================
Use Vhat=Rhat Q, Mhat=Q^T Rhat Q, Hhat=(I+Mhat C)^-1 and Khat=C Hhat.
If

  gamma(t):=nu (a/t)(1+a/t)<=1/2,               (6)

inverse perturbation and (2),(4) give

  ||Hhat||<=2M(t), ||Khat||<=2aM(t),
  ||Khat-K||<=2nu a^2 M(t)^2/t.

Khat is complex symmetric by the second identity in (1), so define the
actual symmetric approximate node Dhat=Vhat Khat Vhat^T. Its rank is
at most rank(C)<=r, even if the original U had redundant columns. Expanding
the two V factors and the core gives

  ||Dhat-D|| <=6nu a M(t)/t^2
                         +2nu a^2 M(t)^2/t^3.   (7)

Indeed ||V||<=1/t, ||Vhat||<=2/t, ||Vhat-V||<=nu/t; the V differences
contribute coefficients4+2, and the inverse-core difference coefficient2.
This works for singular C and uses no lower bound on any base/update
eigenvalue. Formula (7) is the shared core lemma also used for the
PSD-endpoint sharp-rank algorithm.

5. EXPLICIT WHOLE-LINE QUADRATURE
================================
Let phi=pi alpha/2, c_e=2sin(phi)/pi, c_o=2cos(phi)/pi, and use D_z
from (1). The exact differences are

  f_e(T)-f_e(S)=c_e int_0^infinity t^alpha Im D_(it) dt,
  f_o(T)-f_o(S)=-c_o int_0^infinity t^alpha Re D_(it) dt. (8)

These follow by subtracting the scalar resolvent representations and
functional calculus. For example R(it)-R(-it)=2it/(S^2+t^2), while
R(it)+R(-it)=2S/(S^2+t^2). At fixed alpha both integrals converge in norm
by (2). The complex branch is a single-pole linear combination, as in
factor-four-extensions.txt Section13.2.

Put t=a exp(y). In every fixed strip |Im y|<=d<pi/2 the logarithmic
integrands satisfy the UNIFORM operator envelope

  ||F(y)||<=C_(alpha,d) a^alpha
        min(exp(alpha Re y),exp(-beta Re y)).     (9)

This follows because the imaginary parts of poles +/-i a exp(y) have
magnitude >=a exp(Re y) cos(d), so both endpoint resolvents are bounded
by 1/[a exp(Re y)cos(d)]. The difference identity gives the other term.
No base spectral norm or condition parameter enters (9).

Choose rho=epsilon/[C_alpha(r+1)], a logarithmic trapezoid spacing
h=Theta_alpha(1/log(1/rho)), and dyadic positive nodes t_j=a2^(j/H),
where H is a power of two of order log(1/rho). Choose the least integer
tail cutoffs with s_min=t_min/a=Theta_alpha(rho^(1/alpha)) and
s_max=t_max/a=Theta_alpha(rho^(-1/beta)). Node count is
q=O_alpha(log^2(2r/epsilon)), quadrature error O_alpha(rho a^alpha).
At alpha=1/2 every node and t_j^(alpha+1) uses only scalar square roots
and products; h=log(2)/H and fixed coefficients can be approximated by
convergent scalar series to the requisite positive relative accuracy.

Weighting (7) by h*t_j^(alpha+1) and putting s_j=t_j/a bounds the total
node error by

  C_alpha nu a^alpha h sum_j
   [s_j^(alpha-1)(1+1/s_j)
                +s_j^(alpha-2)(1+1/s_j)^2]
  <=C'_alpha nu a^alpha s_min^(alpha-4).

Taking nu<=c_alpha rho^(4/alpha) makes this O_alpha(rho a^alpha) and
also implies (6) at every node. It takes only ell=O_alpha(log(1/rho))
raw iterations. Use exact integer halving budgets rather than silently
assuming input logarithms or arbitrary scalar powers for this choice.

Since a^alpha<=r^(alpha/2)||E||^alpha and rho r^(alpha/2)<=epsilon/C_alpha,
all quadrature/node/coefficient errors sum to epsilon||E||^alpha after
fixing constants. This proves the claimed intrinsic error theorem.

6. REAL FACTORS AND COMPLETE CONVENTIONAL COST
============================================
For one node write Vhat=P+iQ0 and Khat=A+iB, with real symmetric A,B.
Then

  Re Dhat=[P,Q0] [[A,-B],[-B,-A]] [P,Q0]^T,
  Im Dhat=[P,Q0] [[B,A],[A,-B]] [P,Q0]^T.         (10)

Each has real rank at most2r. Thin QR and symmetric congruence elimination
on the at-most2r core give a signed factor using only arithmetic,
comparisons and square roots. Zero pivots are handled by exact rank
tests or a basis change e_i ->e_i+e_j; no small eigendecomposition is
assumed. Concatenate these factors with the real quadrature weights.

Setup OSE/coarse Gram/factor/core costs ~Nm+m^3. Initial X^T Q and
per-node raw actions cost O(Nmr) each; each of ell=O_alpha(log(r/epsilon))
iterations also uses at most O(m^2r) small arithmetic. Each node has
O(m^3) coarse solve setup, O(Nr^2+r^3) factor extraction. Multiplying
by q polylogarithmic nodes gives ~O_alpha(Nmr+m^3). There is no hidden
full-width multiply X times an m-by-m transform. All node counts and
iterations are fixed in advance. On a failed sketch, a singular small
core is detected and returns zero, with the same dimensional runtime cap.

For every fixed real homogeneous scalar map a0*x_+^alpha+b0*(-x)_+^alpha,
combine the even/odd cores in (10) BEFORE extracting factors. They share
the same [P,Q0] feature block, so each combined real node still has rank
at most2r. This extends the same algorithm and sharp rank coefficient to
all nonzero real homogeneous maps in the 1:2:4 theorem.

7. RECTANGULAR ABSOLUTE AND POLAR-MAZUR UPDATES
=============================================
Let M be real N-by-m, N>=m, and let Delta=U V^T with U N-by-r,
V m-by-r, r<=m. Set M'=M+Delta. Its Hermitian dilation is

  D(M)=[[0,M],[M^T,0]]=Y Jswap Y^T,
  Y=diag(M,I_m), Jswap=[[0,I_m],[I_m,0]].

Ambient size is N+m, latent width2m, input work O(Nm+m^2). The update

  D(Delta)=diag(U,V) Jswap_r diag(U,V)^T

has rank <=2r and norm exactly ||Delta||. Apply Sections1--6 to the
dilated endpoints. Functional calculus gives

  f_e(D(M))=diag(|M^T|^alpha,|M|^alpha),
  f_o(D(M))=[[0,M|M|^(alpha-1)],
            [(M|M|^(alpha-1))^T,0]].

The zero singular values are assigned value zero. Block extraction
therefore returns low-rank approximations of the right/left absolute
power changes and the polar-Mazur change, each with error

  epsilon ||Delta||^alpha,
  conventional raw cost ~O_alpha(Nmr+m^3).

No gap or minimum singular value is needed; in particular these statements
remain valid at singular M or M'. Intrinsic normalization in Section2
removes arbitrary cancellation between U and V. This is a fractional
polar map with alpha>0; it does NOT establish a condition-free update of
the discontinuous alpha=0 polar factor.

For RIGHT absolute and polar-Mazur corrections, even forming every output
entry costs only O(m^2*r*q) or O(N*m*r*q), respectively. These are absorbed
by ~O_alpha(Nmr+m^3). Thus those two theorems include the full explicit
matrix output at the displayed total asymptotic cost. LEFT absolute power
has N-by-N output and separately costs O(N^2*r*q) to expand.

8. PARITY PRESERVES THE SHARP RECTANGULAR NODE RANK
=================================================
Generic dilation bookkeeping would give4r real node rank. That is
avoidable. Choose the OSE for Y blockwise, giving

  G0=diag(G0_M,I_m), G=diag(M^T M,I_m).

Let P=diag(I_N,-I_m) on physical space and Qsign=diag(I_m,-I_m) on
latent space. Then P Y=Y Qsign, Jswap anticommutes with Qsign, and G0
and G-G0 commute with Qsign. The fixed iteration in (3) consequently
satisfies

  P Rhat_(it) P=-Rhat_(-it)=-conj(Rhat_(it)).      (11)

One can check this directly from the symmetric finite inverse polynomial:
Qsign B0_z Qsign=-B0_(-z), so its ell-th polynomial transforms with one
overall minus sign when z is replaced by -z. The outer resolvent scaling
then gives (11). No true Gram is needed to enforce this algebra.

The exact update E_d=D(Delta) also anticommutes with P. The Woodbury
node can be written without an inverse Rhat as

  Dhat=Rhat E_d(I+Rhat E_d)^-1 Rhat.

Its parity is therefore P Dhat P=-conj(Dhat). This follows simply by
replacing the three occurrences of Rhat/E_d by their parity transforms;
the two factors inside I+Rhat E_d lose both signs. It does not assume
that Rhat itself is invertible. Hence Dhat's two diagonal blocks are
purely imaginary and its off-diagonal blocks are real.

But complex rank(Dhat)<=rank(E_d)<=2r. A purely imaginary diagonal
block, divided by i, has REAL rank equal to its complex rank; the same
is true of a real off-diagonal block. Thus each extracted node of the
absolute or polar-Mazur map has real rank <=2r, not4r. Compute a generic
realification first, then exact thin QR/core rank compression to realize
this rank. It costs O(Nr^2+r^3) and stays inside the theorem's budget.

9. SHARP LEADING RANK UPGRADE AND ITS IMPLEMENTATION OBLIGATION
============================================================
The checked Gaussian lemma in sharp-fractional-holder.tex replaces the
uniform trapezoid by q<=L^2/(4*pi*d*alpha*beta)+O(L^(7/4)) nodes in a
strip of half-width d, at error e^-L. For whole-line maps d<pi/2; for
positive-endpoint powers d<pi. All preceding raw/core perturbation
estimates still apply because the largest and smallest retained log
nodes are O_alpha(L), and nu can be set e^(-C_alpha L).

For fixed rank r and epsilon->0 the suggested leading widths are

  PSD fractional power:       [r/(4*pi^2*alpha*beta)+o(1)]log^2(1/epsilon),
  one complex branch:         [r/(2*pi^2*alpha*beta)+o(1)]log^2(1/epsilon),
  real whole-line map:        [r/(  pi^2*alpha*beta)+o(1)]log^2(1/epsilon),
  rectangular absolute/polar: [r/(  pi^2*alpha*beta)+o(1)]log^2(1/epsilon).

The rectangular last line uses (11) and rank2r per extracted node;
blindly applying a rank2r Hermitian theorem would incorrectly double it.
These coefficients match the checked worst-case lower bounds. At
alpha=1/2 they are respectively r/pi^2,2r/pi^2,4r/pi^2,4r/pi^2.

To obtain ONE uniform algorithm with the exact o(1) leading term, rather
than an arbitrarily-close fixed-strip constant, choose
d=d_boundary-L^(-1/4). The elementary sector bounds in (9) grow only
polynomially in L, so the Gaussian proof's slack O(L^(3/4)) absorbs them.
The scalar window/node construction must also be charged. The shared
implementation being developed by sqrt_update_sota uses positive clipped
polynomial-square integration for Gaussian window weights, and scaled
Taylor/exponentiation for exp(log-node), with poly(L) scalar arithmetic.
Its precise scalar proof is outside this file and needs independent audit.
Until then, the order-optimal uniform-grid algorithm in Sections1--8 is
the unqualified complete algorithm theorem; these sharp implementation
coefficients are a rigorously identified upgrade conditional on that
explicit scalar realization, not a free Gaussian/erf oracle.

10. AN ADDITIONAL PSD BASE GENERALITY
===================================
If S=X J X^T is itself promised PSD, the PSD root-update theorem need
not require an explicitly positive raw base factor. On the OSE event
write X=Z L, Z=X L^dagger, so (1-eta0)I<=Z^T Z<=(1+eta0)I.
K0=L J L^T is PSD by congruence (indeed this is true on every sketch
outcome, because row(L) is a subspace of row(X)). Compute a PSD
Cholesky K0=H H^T and use the IMPLICIT positive factor

  Y=X L^dagger H=Z H, YY^T=S.

Its coarse Gram proxy is H^T H. Y and Y^T actions on r columns require
only the corresponding X/X^T thin products and small transforms,
O(Nmr+m^2r). There is no full-width materialization of Y. Thus the
positive-base solver extends to promised signed raw PSD bases at the
same cost. This is standard factor/congruence algebra, not a claimed
new invention of positive feature factorizations.

11. WHAT STILL PREVENTS THE ORIGINAL NONZERO CUTOFF
================================================
For S=X J X^T the desired threshold map is (S-tI)_+ on the supported
space. Replacing it by |S-tI| requires sqrt((S-tI)^2), and

  (S-tI)^2=t^2 I+X(J G J-2tJ)X^T, G=X^T X.

The signed low-rank core JGJ-2tJ contains the unknown true Gram. Calling
the new function-update algorithm with that core would hide exactly
the forbidden Gram computation. A tI augmentation also has full ambient
rank rather than the desired thin raw access. In supported coordinates,
the threshold core instead contains inverse mass (Z^T Z)^-1; applying
it on thin vectors is cheap, but the partial-spectrum method requires
an explicit core, which is not supplied by that observation.

Likewise the poles of a sharp filter at a nonzero threshold approach
the real spectrum. The signed raw recurrence in (3) then contracts by
eta0*|z|/|Im z|, which can exceed one. The intrinsically scaled function
update theorem proves neither the required near-real inverse nor a
polynomial-mass separation filter. No original target or omega=2 claim
follows from this broader neighboring algorithm.
