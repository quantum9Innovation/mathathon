Sharp bounded-degree rank and the charged construction
====================================================

inputs; it does not replace their source audits or establish priority.

INPUTS

2. The sharp degree theorem under final-source audit in

      lim_{t->0} t W_{d,alpha}(t)=A_{d,alpha},
      A_{d,alpha}=alpha log[2d/(d+2)], d>=3.
   W ranges over finite connected simple unit-weight hosts, arbitrary
   existing-edge doubling, maximum combinatorial degree <=d.
   A bridge/bipartite subclass already gives the matching lower.

DEDUCTION
On the algorithm's simultaneous success event its output rank satisfies

       r <= N_C((1-rho)*epsilon)
         <= W_{d,alpha}((1-rho)*epsilon).

Let k be the least integer with 2^k>=4/epsilon, computed by repeated
doubling, and choose rho(epsilon)=1/k, for 0<epsilon<1/2. Thus no exact
logarithm oracle is needed. Then rho tends to zero, and the sharp uniform
degree theorem yields

       r <= [A_{d,alpha}+o_{d,alpha}(1)]/epsilon.

Its relative two-sided error remains at most epsilon. Expected work is
still nearly linear in n+m in the stated exact-real model: the only
additional accuracy dependence is polylogarithmic in 1/rho, hence here
polylogarithmic in log(1/epsilon). Confidence remains the same joint
success event for rank and error. Failed-output runtime guards and the
certified Las Vegas resistance preprocessing remain necessary.

Conversely, if any rank-r matrix F approximates the fractional update
with baseline relative operator error at most epsilon, its relative
matrix L^{-alpha/2} F L^{-alpha/2} has rank at most r. The elementary
best rank-r approximation theorem for the positive matrix C forces
       r >= N_C(epsilon).
Thus the sharp bridge/bipartite lower examples force worst-case rank
at least [A_{d,alpha}-o(1)]/epsilon for ANY construction attaining this
error, regardless of whether its returned factor is positive. The
algorithm consequently attains the sharp uniform leading rank constant.

LIMITS OF THIS COROLLARY
The o(1) is uniform over the graph class at fixed d and alpha, but no
effective rate in d,alpha,epsilon is supplied by the compactness proof.
This is not an implementation or a timing result. The algorithm permits
scalar positive powers in its general-alpha arithmetic model; alpha=1/2
uses arithmetic and square roots as detailed in its proof. Applying a
preconditioner inverse still separately charges the base inverse-power
action. The existential lower graphs may have enormous dimensions.
