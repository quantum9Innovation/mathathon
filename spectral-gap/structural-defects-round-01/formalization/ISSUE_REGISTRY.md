# Formal scope issues

## SF-1: finite inverse assumptions do not encode spectral-gap probability

Status: explicit unformalized bridge.

The host and motif inverses are actual objects, but their general invertibility assumptions remain hypotheses. The concrete K1,7 inverse is constructed explicitly. The random host gap and its implication for the shifted inverse are not formalized.

## SF-2: scalar-port algebra is narrower than the full graph theorem

Status: explicit unformalized bridge.

Inertia, polynomial locality, analytic derivatives, projector normalization, probability limits and fine resonance errors remain outside revision 1. The packet cannot be reported as a complete formal proof of the structural attachment theorem.

## SF-3: the next-scale arrow correction is outside revision 1

Status: finite identities encoded in revision 2, graph application remains open to review.

Revision 2 encodes the actual finite inverse quadratic and leading signed coupling. It preserves the first compiling revision. The asymptotic expansion, uniform remainder, graph complementary gap and probability convergence remain outside the formal packet.
