The note proves these statements directly, rather than assuming an
unproved type formula:

* Ordered stars are assigned to labeled vertices in
  product_i n_i!/product_s T_i(s)! ways. This already assigns root labels.
* Internal compatible-pairing counts are
  product_(x<y) m_ii(x,y)! times product_x(m_ii(x,x)-1)!!,
  divided by(n_i*a_i-1)!!. The pair count must be symmetric and its
  diagonal even. This retains all loops and edge multiplicities.
* Cross compatible-bijection counts are
  product_(x,y) m_ij(x,y)! /(n_i*d_ij)!, with reversed counts agreeing.
* Their product is exactly the contribution of that type to the
  expected number of all F_p^r intertwiners AY=YR.
* Uniform factorial estimates, including zero counts, give the exact
  exponent proposed by the root:
    Phi=sum_i n_i H(star_i)
       -sum_i(n_i*a_i/2)H(pair_ii)
       -sum_(i<j)(n_i*d_ij)H(pair_ij),
  with at most a fixed multiple of log(N+1) error per type.
* Symmetrization permutes slots only within a fixed neighbor-block type.
  It preserves the root law, each aggregated pair law, and the star
  constraint, while increasing star entropy. It need not produce a new
  integer empirical type; only the entropy comparison is used.
* For the symmetrized laws, deleting one internal neighbor is lossless,
  because its value is determined by the root and all retained labels.
  Weighted entropy cancellation then gives the exact identity
    Phi_sym=-sum_i n_i(a_i/2-1) I_ii
            -sum_(i<j) M_ij I_ij-sum_i n_i J_i <=0,
  where J_i is conditional total correlation of retained neighbors.
  The balance M_ij=n_i*d_ij=n_j*d_ji is precisely what cancels root
  entropy terms for unequal block sizes. Each internal degree>=2 is
  needed for the displayed nonpositivity argument.

Explicit polynomial exponent
-----------------------------
For actual class count k<=K, vector dimension r<=K, and actual total
degrees<=D, let
 q_*=p^K,
 S_*=K*p^(K*D),
 F_*=K+[K(K+3)/2]*p^(2K),
 B(K,D,p)=S_*+F_*.
The number of possible count collections is at most(N+1)^S_*.
The uniform factorial error is at most
F_*[log(DN+1)+1]. Consequently, for every admissible configuration size,
 E Z_R(A)<=(3D)^F_* (N+1)^B(K,D,p).
This holds for every R over the field, uniformly in its entries, and
does not require positive block proportions for the unconditioned bound.

For fixed connected degree frames, all factors are simple with a limiting
positive probability. The proof includes a direct small bad-pattern
factorial-moment/Bonferroni argument; its exponent is at most K^2*D^2.
Conditioning therefore costs at most2*exp(K^2*D^2) eventually. It yields
the intended product of uniform simple factors because every simple
factor has the same labeled-stub multiplicity. The finite family of
connected frames with k<=K,totaldegree<=D admits the same eventual
bound: balance gives min_i n_i>=N/[K*D^(K-1)]. Thus the exponent depends
only on K,D,p, and at p=2 only on K,D. Finite initial sizes can be
absorbed into a constant; no explicit convergence-rate claim is made.

Characteristic2 and parity
--------------------------
One-hot labels e_1,...,e_r are pairwise distinct in F_2^r. The omitted
neighbor has coefficient1, so neither its reconstruction nor the entropy
identity divides by2 or any degree. Internal diagonal stub counts must
still be even as integer counts; that constraint only reduces types.
The proof works whenever n_i*a_i is even, so odd n_i with even a_i are
allowed. Internal configuration loops use adjacency diagonal2, matching
their two incident stubs. This convention is essential before conditioning
on simplicity and harmless afterward.

Scope left to other audits
--------------------------
This result bounds the expected number of modular solutions and supports
polynomial enumeration with the explicit exponent. It does not by itself
prove zero-entropy real rigidity, small-support exclusion, compatibility
of nearby point maps, recovery with unknown frames, or historical novelty.
Those cannot be inferred merely from the moment bound.
