PRIMARY COMPARISON: SPARSE-MATRIX REPLICA KERNEL / EIGENSPACE COUNTS
2026-11-15
=================================================================

Target: the untruncated raw bound sup_N E #{Y:A_NY=YR}<infinity in
the fixed reversible frame configuration law under positive strict
symmetric omission weights. This is a priority comparison, not a
correctness certificate or historical-firstness certificate.

Primary source actually inspected:
Roberto C. Alamino and David Saad, Properties of sparse random matrices
over finite fields, Journal of Statistical Mechanics (2009), P04017.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/2009/04/P04017
Author-institution preprint, retained as root-sources/alamino-saad-2009.pdf:
https://publications.aston.ac.uk/id/eprint/7246/1/randmatrix.pdf

Read from the actual PDF extraction: Sections 3--5, including equations
(8)--(16), and Section 8's description of the conclusions. The model,
definitions and distinction below do not rely on a search snippet.
I did not audit the replica appendices as rigorous proofs.

The paper is genuinely close formal counting prior art, not merely a
spectral-density analogy. Equation (8) defines
  Omega_A(0)=sum_v 1{Av=0}=q^(nullity A).
Equation (11) writes its integer replica moment
  Z_n(0)=E_A Omega_A(0)^n
as a sum over matrix entries, row/column degree constraints and n legal
vectors. Equation (15) explicitly generalizes this to scalar targets:
  Omega_A(lambda)=sum_v 1{Av=lambda v}.
Thus neither scalar shifted finite-field solution counts nor replica
moments of them should be presented as a new counting object here.

But the quantity computed in the conclusions is different. Equation (9)
defines the entropy density
  s(0)=lim_(N->infinity) N^(-1) E_A log Omega_A(0),
and equation (10) uses the replica derivative at n=0 to compute it.
The eigenspace generalization likewise concerns averaged dimension
density. The saddle-point calculation does not provide a uniform bound
on E Omega_A(lambda)^r at each fixed positive integer r. A finite-size
raw O(1) bound is not implied by a zero entropy density or normalized
nullity tending to zero.

The model in Section 3 fixes row and column connectivities and weights
matrix entries independently before conditioning on those degrees. Its
displayed moment does not impose the target's reciprocal adjacency
symmetry, fixed old-type block degrees, pooled internal pairing factors,
or arbitrary matrix on-site target R. Section 7 illustrates graph
encodings but does not establish an ensemble transfer for the required
raw expectation. More importantly, replacing lambda by R in a formal
enumerator would still not supply the missing constant-order estimate.

This follows the same authors' earlier paper, Typical kernel size and
number of sparse random matrices over Galois fields: A statistical
physics approach, Physical Review E 77 (2008), 061123:
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.77.061123
The actual primary PDF was readable through the browser at
https://research.aston.ac.uk/files/215750/gfqsrm.pdf . Its equations
(11)--(14) likewise distinguish Omega, the raw replicated expression,
and the limiting averaged logarithmic density. Direct retention of
that PDF failed with HTTP403; the 2009 successor is retained locally.

Bounded conclusion: these sources already formulate scalar shifted
finite-field counts and raw replica enumerators in sparse prescribed-
degree ensembles. No directly subsuming all-phase untruncated raw
moment bound for the target symmetric fixed-frame model was found in
the inspected statements and computations. This is a scope comparison,
not a claim that every calculation or possible specialization of these
papers has been excluded.
