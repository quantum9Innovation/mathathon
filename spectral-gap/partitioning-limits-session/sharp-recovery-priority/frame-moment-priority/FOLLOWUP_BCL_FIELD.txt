  (A_G+I)v=1,
with the intended perfect-code indicator entries in {0,1}; it applies
echelon reduction and groups identical affine coordinate formulas. The
example formulas use three free coordinates and their complementary

this reduction, a CAS coefficient domain, or an attack implementation.

Immediately before the second attack, the report says IPCC7 CIPHERTEXT
polynomial coefficients are integers modulo 2^31. This does not specify
the coefficient domain for the public-graph row reduction. Moreover,
Z/(2^31) is not a field; ordinary field-style row reduction cannot simply
be inferred from this ciphertext parameter.

Mathematical interpretation, not source attribution:
the displayed graph equations are exact integer equations for indicators,
so one can use their rational affine relaxation or reduce them into a
specified finite field. Different characteristics can introduce different
extra kernel directions. Neither the reported seven-of-ten outcome nor
the cells of sizes 1,1,63,63,64,64 uniquely determines which domain was used.
Those observations are consistent with more than one implementation.

Accordingly describe the predecessor as echelon reduction and grouping of
affine coordinate forms. Do not call it a rational/real attack or an F_p
attack without another explicit primary source or implementation. The
direct algorithmic ancestry is unaffected; an exact comparison to the new
modular full-kernel enumeration must retain this unspecified-domain limit.
