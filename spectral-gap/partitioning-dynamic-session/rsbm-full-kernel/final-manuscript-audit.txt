1. PRIMARY PRECONDITIONER SCOPE: CORRECT
----------------------------------------
Chen et al. Theorems 4.2 and 4.5 supply exactly the two distinct
properties needed. The former handles cyclicity of the nonzero
primary part for arbitrary fields; the latter uses symmetry and
handles nilpotent zero blocks. Neither requires characteristic>n.
The manuscript correctly avoids the additional squarefree conclusion
of Theorem 4.3. This distinction is essential in fixed characteristic.
The union bound requires no independence between the properties.

2. ALL-INPUT LOWER RANK CERTIFICATE AND SOUND ACCEPTANCE: CORRECT
----------------------------------------------------------------
The shifted starting vector Tv lives in im T. A degree-k Cayley--
Hamilton relation on that invariant space gives a recurrence of
length at most k, including when the zero eigenvalue has nilpotent
blocks. Exact finite-prefix minimum length ell therefore cannot
exceed rank T. It is important that actual BM register length is
used, not a padded or guessed polynomial degree; the text requires it.

The independently verified kernel columns give the matching upper
rank bound only at acceptance. Consequently underestimated rank,
bad projection, bad g(T), and unlucky diagonals cannot yield false
acceptance. An underestimated ell instead asks for more than the
actual corank many independent kernel vectors, which is impossible.
This is genuinely all-input Las Vegas, not a corank-one acceptor or
a probabilistic rank verifier in disguise.

Rejection when g(0)=0 is safe. On a good trial it never happens.
In particular for full-rank input, a full-length correct recurrence
has nonzero constant coefficient, so the early rejection does not
remove the intended good full-rank acceptance.

3. GOOD-TRIAL PROBABILITY AND UNIFORM KERNEL SAMPLING: CORRECT
--------------------------------------------------------------
On the preconditioner good event, T=0 on its kernel and is invertible
cyclic on its image. The projected starting vector Tv is uniform on
the image; restricting a uniform u gives a uniform linear functional.
The two degree-k controllability/observability determinant bounds
therefore give the stated 2k/q failure probability, even with repeated
nonzero roots. The first 2n terms identify the scalar recurrence
exactly on that good event.

The identity g(T)=g(0)P onto the kernel along the image is then exact.
Thus the fresh probes produce independent uniform kernel vectors.
The expected span time <=r+2 is correct. The hyperplane union bound
for n+1 samples is correct and gives at most 1/[q(q-1)], uniformly
including r=n; for r=0 no draws are needed.

4. CONDITIONAL GOOD/BAD WORK AND RETRIES: CORRECT
-------------------------------------------------
Good-trial costs are bounded in expectation by the uncapped span
time; truncating at n+1 cannot increase that expectation. Each
polynomial action, residual test, and independence insertion is
charged. The n+1 cap gives a deterministic O(n^2(w+n)) bound on
EVERY bad trial. Multiplication by O(n^2/q) gives
O(n^4(w+n)/q), which is absorbed by n(w+n)(r+1) for the selected
q>=64(n+1)^6. No unproved expected stopping estimate on bad draws
is used.

The trial acceptance probability exceeds 1/2 uniformly over every
fixed input. The renewal identity remains valid when cost and
success in a trial are correlated, because a failed trial restarts
with independent fresh choices. It proves almost-sure termination
and the claimed expectation without a dense exact fallback.

Zero input has r=n, g=1 and ordinary random basis sampling; cubic
work is allowed by n^2(r+1). Full-rank input has r=0 and accepts
without kernel probes on a good trial. These boundary cases are
covered rather than excluded.

5. CANONICAL PIVOT/FROBENIUS DESCENT: CORRECT
--------------------------------------------
An accepted B is a full extension-field kernel basis of M, not just
of an operator with an unrelated kernel. D is invertible and all
retained vectors were checked against M. Selecting an invertible
r-row block and normalizing it to I_r makes the kernel basis unique
with those pivot values. Coefficientwise Frobenius preserves the
kernel, since M has base-field coefficients, and preserves I_r.
Uniqueness forces C^(p)=C. The fixed field in F_(p^s) is exactly F_p.
Checking zero positive-degree coefficients in the polynomial field
representation is therefore legitimate.

The cost O(nr^2+r^3) and memory O(nr) are properly charged. They fit
the main work bound because r<=n. The r=0 matrices and empty row set
have their usual empty-matrix conventions; r=n gives C=I_n.

6. CERTIFICATE SIZE AND IMPROVED VERIFIER BOUND: CORRECT
--------------------------------------------------------
The canonical identity pivot block makes a separate dense
independence elimination unnecessary. A verifier need only check
MC=0, C_J=I_r, and ell+r=n after recomputing the shifted BM length.
The kernel columns are then manifestly independent. The arithmetic
cost is O(n(w+n)+wr+r^2), at most O(n(w+n)) since r<=n.
Thus the manuscript's stronger verifier bound, independent of an
extra factor r, is valid.

The certificate encodings have O_p(nr+n log n) bits: C contributes
nr base elements, h contributes O(log n) base coefficients, D,u,v
contribute O(n log n) bits, and the pivot-row list contributes at
most n log n bits. Checking representation sizes and bounds on q
is explicitly included, so malicious giant encodings do not evade
the stated verification work model.

7. FIELD CONSTRUCTION AND SPACE: CORRECT
-----------------------------------------
For an irreducible degree-s polynomial, every gcd with X^(p^i)-X
for i<=floor(s/2) is one. Conversely every reducible degree-s
polynomial has an irreducible factor of degree at most floor(s/2),
including repeated-factor polynomials, and the gcd at that factor's
degree detects it. Thus the manuscript's elementary all-i criterion
is necessary and sufficient; it does not need a separate
X^(p^s)=X test. For s=1 the test set is empty and every monic
linear polynomial is indeed irreducible.

The irreducible density lower bound 1/(2s) is valid for fixed odd p,
so exact construction costs expected polylog(n) bits. Each field
operation and uniform field sample costs polylog(n) bits. The n+1
probe cap, at most r retained verified independent columns even
on bad trials, and ordinary linear-storage BM/Horner evaluation
give the claimed workspace. Retry counters have geometric tails
and finite uniformly bounded expected logarithmic storage. The
extension-construction counter also has finite expected size.

8. MOMENT AVERAGING AND ENUMERATION: CORRECT
--------------------------------------------
The per-input work bound is uniform and applies to every finite-
field matrix, so averaging it requires no rate for exceptional
real-corank events. The inequality r+1<=O_p(p^r) gives the kernel
expectation. Odometer/Gray updates avoid the unjustified extra r
factor in candidate enumeration. Odd characteristic distinguishes
integer signs, and exact integer residual and balance checks
remove all congruence artifacts. Every true integer sign kernel
vector is enumerated, so completeness is exact.

The expected output and workspace bounds follow from E p^r=O(1),
including storage of a complete list if desired. Conditioning a raw
configuration-model moment on simplicity of probability >=c>0
costs at most 1/c; the manuscript correctly warns that qualitative
contiguity would not supply that moment transfer.

9. SMALL CLARIFICATIONS RECOMMENDED; NOT CORE ERRORS
-----------------------------------------------------
(a) Near the integer listing application (lines 270--295 in the
    audited version), say that lambda is fixed/bounded by d, or
    explicitly precheck |lambda|<=d and charge reading its encoding.
    The stated O_d(n) integer-check cost is immediate in the RSBM
    application lambda=a-b, |lambda|<=a+b=d. A completely arbitrary
    known integer lambda with unbounded bit encoding would need
    its input-length charge. This does not affect the intended
    fixed-parameter RSBM corollary.

(b) In the hyperplane explanation (lines 171--173), replace
    'each missed with probability q^{-(n+1)}' by 'all probes lie
    in each fixed hyperplane with probability q^{-(n+1)}'. The
    formula is right; 'missed' could suggest the opposite event.

(c) An explicit recurrence convention would help an implementation:
    if BM outputs C(z)=1+c1 z+...+c_ell z^ell, the polynomial used
    in Horner actions is g(t)=t^ell+c1 t^(ell-1)+...+c_ell.
    The manuscript's phrase 'monic polynomial' is consistent with
    that convention, but stating it would prevent accidental
    reciprocal-polynomial evaluation.

10. INDEPENDENT SMALL-FIELD SANITY CHECK
-----------------------------------------
An ephemeral exact JavaScript experiment over F_101 tested 440
symmetric matrices of orders 1 through 8, covering every rank.
Matrices were formed by nonsingular congruence of a diagonal
rank-k matrix. It ran the shifted BM, polynomial actions, candidate
residual checks, and n+1 probe cap. Across 448 trials, eight trials
were rejected and all 440 matrices obtained correct complete kernel
certificates; there were zero false rank/kernel acceptances.
This checks conventions on small examples. The all-input correctness
and asymptotic expectation are established by the proofs above,
not inferred from the samples.
