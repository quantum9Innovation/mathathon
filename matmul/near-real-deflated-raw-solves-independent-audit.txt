1. SUPPLIED COARSE EVENT, EXACT SPLIT, AND NORMALIZATION
------------------------------------------------------

The theorem is conditional on S=ZKZ^T, T=Z^TZ=I+D with ||D||<=1/8,
and on a supplied exact orthogonal invariant split of the explicit K.
Removing zero eigenvalues of K by an orthonormal basis of its range
preserves S and compresses T to I+V^TDV, retaining the same bound.
This requires no true-Gram eigendecomposition. The support operations
are on the explicit small core and fit the imported arithmetic model.

P must include the entire coarse spectral interval [t/2,2t] for the
COMMON real center t of the q poles. Its exact split cost C_split is
charged separately. Exact invariant subspaces of a general core are
not supplied by ordinary field arithmetic; the source explicitly does
not substitute a free eigenvector or approximate diagonalization oracle.

The physical identity was reconstructed with its sign and scaling:

  w(S-wI)^-1=-I-Z(wK^-1-T)^-1 Z^T.

Thus the target is the normalized action w(S-wI)^-1h, not an
unnormalized resolvent action. All later error constants match this
convention. The metric factor T^(1/2) used in analysis is never computed.

3. SCHUR INVERSE AND THE FULL 5mu^2*delta PHYSICAL ERROR
------------------------------------------------------

same bound controls the exact Schur inverse.

For ||R_L-R||<=delta<=1/mu, the Schur perturbation is <=eta^2*delta.
Then g*eta^2*delta<=3/64, ensuring the computed core is invertible.
The inverse perturbation bound is <=2g^2*eta^2*delta. With
h0=1+eta*beta<=4/3, this gives

  ||u_hat_P-u_P|| <=(9/8)*mu^2*delta*||b||.

The Q-block error is bounded by
delta*(1+eta*g*h0)*||b||+(beta+delta)*eta*||u_hat_P-u_P||.

bounds is <=(201/64)*mu^2*delta*||b||<4*mu^2*delta*||b||.

5. FULL COST AND ORIGINAL EXPONENT GATE
--------------------------------------

Write r=q(k+1)<=m<=N. Elementary square blocking bounds each raw
Z^TZ action and dense coarse basis action by
O(Nm*r^(omega-2)) and O(m^2*r^(omega-2)), respectively. The latter
fits the former. Companion recurrences cost O(mr), also dominated.
The initial k band columns, q RHS actions and q physical lifts fit
this width bound; q+k<=q(k+1) and q<=r. Initial/final work is retained
even at epsilon=1 by the repaired logarithm.

Per pole, forming A R_L A^T costs O(mk^2), the small inverse costs
O(k^omega), and the remaining RHS/reconstruction products are smaller.
For k=0 no Schur product or inverse is needed. These observations
reproduce the complete displayed cost (10), plus the separately charged
split and shared setup. Reading RHS columns and writing outputs fit
the same raw term. No uncharged physical QR or dense Gram is assumed.

With the source's original feasible-exponent convention omega=9/4 and
N=m^(9/8), r<=~sqrt(m) gives Nm*r^(omega-2)<=~m^(9/4).
Also q*m*k^2<=m*r^2<=~m^2 and q*k^omega<=r^omega<=~m^(9/8).
Polynomial mu and epsilon^-1 add only logarithms. This arithmetic
instantiation does not establish a new feasible multiplication exponent,
nor does it provide C_split within budget.

The residual gate is substantive: k counts the full coarse band around

No unrestricted arbitrary-factor consequence is warranted.
