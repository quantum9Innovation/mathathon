3. Exact local mixed-resolvent identity
--------------------------------------
Write D=d-1 and let A be a signed d-regular adjacency matrix. Let
q_A(z)=E_eps det(zI-M_eps), M_eps=(A-D_eps A D_eps)/2, and
F_A(z)=E_eps adj(zI-M_eps)/q_A(z). Lin--Zhou prove that F_A is a
Cauchy transform of a positive matrix measure supported on the roots q_A.

The following universal TREE identity was derived independently by this worker
and signing_frontier. Put

 h(z)=1/(z-D h(z)/2),   h(z)~1/z,   t(z)=h(z)/2,
 g(z)=h(z)/(1-h(z)^2/2).

If P_l is the nonbacktracking polynomial for a d-regular tree,
P_0=1, P_1=x, P_2=x^2-d, P_l=x P_(l-1)-D P_(l-2) for l>=3,
then

 sum_(l>=0) P_l(x) t^l = (1-t^2)/(1-x t+D t^2).

Consequently the universal local series in a direction Au=theta u is

 f_theta(z)=g(z) sum_l P_l(theta)t(z)^l
 = 2t(1-t^2)/[(1-2t^2)(1-theta t+D t^2)].                 (A)

A direct tree proof: for a finite tree T, the averaged cut characteristic
polynomial equals det(zI-A_T/sqrt(2)), because every determinant permutation
is a matching, each matching edge is retained independently with probability
1/2, and signs square to one. The ij expected-adjugate numerator has its
unique simple path of length l forced present; its probability is 2^-l,
independent of the remaining matching edges because their vertices are
outside the path. Compared with the usual resolvent of A_T/sqrt(2), this
introduces one additional factor 2^(-l/2). In the infinite regular-tree
limit the usual resolvent diagonal is g and off-diagonal path factor is
h/sqrt(2). Thus the cut-averaged ratio has off-diagonal factor t^l.
Summing by distance gives (A).

For finite girth g, coefficients of z^(-s-1) with s<g have the same
local tree form: determinant cycle terms have degree at least g, while
in the quotient expansion disconnected matching components cancel.
The remaining connected diagrams of total degree s are trees. Equivalently,
expand the removed-path matching-polynomial ratio by its cavity recurrence;
a correction to tree recursion requires a cycle of degree at least g.
This last coefficient-localization statement is a combinatorial proof sketch,
not a fully typeset independent theorem in this note. The tree identity and
scalar obstruction below do not rely on promoting that sketch to a theorem.

Checks against published formulas: coefficients are
m0=1, m1=theta/2, m2=(theta^2+d)/4,
m3=[theta^3+(2d-1)theta]/8,
m4=[theta^4+(3d-2)theta^2+3d^2-d]/16,
exactly Lin--Zhou equations (3.11), (4.3), (5.2 short-cycle case).

4. A rigorous scalar moment barrier at 3/sqrt(2)
------------------------------------------------
For every integer D>=2 and |theta|<=C_D=(3/sqrt(2))*sqrt(D), formula (A)
is the Cauchy transform of a probability measure supported on
[-R,R], R=sqrt(2D). Hence positivity of ALL the local tree moments,
together with that support interval, cannot rule out theta=C_D.
This is an obstruction to a specified moment-information model; it is
NOT a construction of high-girth graphs attaining C_D and is NOT a
lower bound against arbitrary signing methods.

Proof. On the slit plane choose t~1/(2z), so z=1/(2t)+Dt and the
physical branch satisfies |t|<=1/sqrt(2D). The factor 1-2t^2 has no
zero on this branch for D>=2. Roots of 1-theta t+D t^2 are outside
the branch unless |theta|>C_D; at equality a single root reaches
an endpoint. Thus f has no poles outside [-R,R], has at most inverse
square-root endpoint singularities, and f(z)=1/z+O(1/z^2).

For x in (-R,R), write t=(x-i sqrt(2D-x^2))/(2D), v=conj(t),
so tv=1/(2D) and t+v=x/D. With
D0(t)=(1-2t^2)(1-theta t+D t^2), an exact subtraction gives

 [f(t)-f(v)]/(t-v)
 = {[(2D+1)(D+1)/2-theta*x]/D^2}/|D0(t)|^2.

The numerator is nonnegative, since |theta*x|<=3D and
 (2D+1)(D+1)/2 - 3D = (2D-1)(D-1)/2 >0.
As Im(t)<0, -Im f(x+i0)>=0. The density is explicitly

 sqrt(2D-x^2)*[(2D+1)(D+1)-2theta*x]
 ------------------------------------------------- .
 4*pi*D^3*|(1-2t^2)(1-theta*t+D*t^2)|^2

It is integrable, including at the endpoint where a root reaches it.
The elementary Cauchy contour formula then represents f as the Cauchy
transform of this nonnegative density; the coefficient 1/z makes total
mass one. This proves the barrier. In particular every finite Hankel
and support-localizing matrix made from these moments is PSD even at
theta=C_D. No choice of higher-degree polynomial test removes it.

The location of the barrier follows immediately from t(R)=1/sqrt(2D):
1-theta*t+D*t^2=0 iff theta=(3/sqrt(2))*sqrt(D).
The friend's limiting constant is therefore the natural ceiling of this
one-measure local-tree recovery scheme. A smaller O(1/g) term would still
be a refinement of that ceiling, rather than a route toward coefficient 2.

5. What would count as a new mechanism
--------------------------------------
A serious next signing target is a size-independent near-Ramanujan theorem
for high-girth graphs, coefficient 2+o_g(1), with a proof using information
not contained in the above single positive measure. Options include an
interlacing selection simultaneously controlling several nonbacktracking
matrix polynomials, or a constructive repair invariant retaining cycle
parity information. The local-tree measure alone cannot certify this.

The matrix-function project has a different candidate of potentially much
larger algorithmic scope: relative compression of fractional Laplacian
updates in terms of edge leverage (effective resistance), with deterministic
rank bounds independent of condition number. That direction uses the actual
near-linear graph solvers and raw thin-factor machinery instead of forcing
a permutation scalar estimate to solve an unrelated spectral task. A precise
candidate sharp relative-rank law is being developed by graph_breakthrough;
this worker will independently audit it next.
