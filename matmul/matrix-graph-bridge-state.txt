Investigated direct AMM routes
-----------------------------
1. The rank-one stationary part of products of regular graph adjacency
   matrices is already known explicitly. Exact multiplication of sparse
   fixed-degree factors plus expansion is itself O(N^2). Merely calling
   this a quadratic approximate product would not be an advance.

2. A candidate exact random-product residual constant was independently
   derived and then found in Alev--Rao, arXiv:2611.13693, November 10, 2026.
   permutation-product-priority-audit.txt records the full comparison.
   It must not be presented as new. The unequal-degree extension is a
   routine calculation with no novelty claim, not a research completion.

3. Balanced CountSketch is an actual unbiased identity sketch, unlike
   a centered permutation matrix, whose expectation is zero. Its Gram
   trace moments can be expanded into colored permutation cells, where
   SG-11/14/16 may help. However, a useful new width theorem still needs
   a collision-pattern enumeration and a sharp matrix moment transfer.
   Entrywise scalar moment upper bounds do not themselves prove a
   matrix OSE or remove the AMM accuracy cost. Existing OSNAP/sparse-JL
   and balanced-sampling prior art must be compared at the same guarantee.

4. Permutation fixed-point sampling n*diag(P) is unbiased for identity
   but selects only one column on average per permutation. The exact
   diagonal moments alone provide no better AMM cost. Uniform random
   permutations substituted for Rademacher preconditioning likewise
   give no established advantage over existing SRHT constructions.
