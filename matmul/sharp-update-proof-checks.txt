MAIN GUARANTEE
  Given a raw base X (N by m) and rank-r symmetric update E=UKU^T,
  1<=r<=m<=N, compute a low-rank fractional correction with operator error
  epsilon*||E||^alpha, probability >=1-delta, and total CONVENTIONAL
  arithmetic ~O_alpha(Nmr+m^3). Cancellation, singular endpoints, spectral
  gaps and base conditioning introduce no iteration parameter. The update
  core K and a signed raw base core J may be singular.

  PSD endpoints: B^alpha-A^alpha, A=XX^T and B=A+E>=0 promised.
  Whole-line signed base: f(XJX^T+E)-f(XJX^T) for even/odd/real homogeneous
  fractional maps, and complex analytic branches.
  Rectangular: right/left absolute-power and fractional polar corrections
  after M changes by UV^T, at error epsilon*||UV^T||^alpha.

  The width is O_alpha(r log^2(2r/epsilon)). At each FIXED r, as epsilon->0,
  its leading coefficient times log^2(1/epsilon) is
    PSD:                   r/(4*pi^2*alpha*(1-alpha)),
    one complex branch:    r/(2*pi^2*alpha*(1-alpha)),
    real full/rectangular: r/(  pi^2*alpha*(1-alpha)).
  These match the minimax lower bounds over unrestricted finite sizes.
  This is optimal LEADING OUTPUT RANK, not optimal runtime or exact
  per-input/fixed-epsilon best rank. At alpha=1/2 the coefficients are
  r/pi^2, 2r/pi^2, and 4r/pi^2.

  At alpha=1/2: exact field operations, comparisons, scalar square roots,
  and discrete randomness. Dyadic alpha also uses those primitives.
  Arbitrary non-dyadic alpha explicitly requires scalar power evaluations
  to be supplied/charged. No hidden eigenvalue, shifted-solve or root oracle.
  Rectangular right absolute and polar FULL output is within the same bound.
  Square/left absolute dense expansion adds O(N^2*width).
