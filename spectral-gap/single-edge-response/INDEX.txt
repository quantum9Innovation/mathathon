DISTINCTIONS TO PRESERVE
  * Spectral closure is approximation, not arbitrary exact graph attainment.
  * Fixed matrix dimension requires determinant equality. Growing-dimensional
    padded uniform graph closure permits a weak inequality; full-budget
    constructed sequences can have stronger l1 convergence.
  * The degree dichotomy doubles one existing unit edge. The host is
    unweighted; the edited edge has weight two afterward.
  * The computed factor has two-sided relative error. Its rank guarantee
    holds on the same success event as its error guarantee.
  * The algorithm is per-edit expected exact-real arithmetic, not finite-word
    stability or sublinear maintained dynamic update time. Baseline inverse
    fractional actions are separately charged for preconditioner application.
  * The centered-path coefficient 1/(2*pi^2) also equals the uniform
    finite-path/cycle worst-case leading coefficient. Leading constants
    are proved at every fixed degree d>=2. No effective higher-degree
    asymptotic rate is supplied.
  * Classical ingredients are credited. Historical novelty is not certified.
