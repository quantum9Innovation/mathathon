1. THE PRECISE THEOREM TO COMPARE
--------------------------------
The input is an N-by-m raw factor X and a supplied involution J, giving
S=X J X* without supplying its true dense Gram. At one supplied real
center t>0, q arbitrary supplied RHS/pole pairs are batched. The accuracy
is normalized forward action error epsilon||h_j|| for
w_j(S-w_j I)^-1h_j, including poles polynomially close to the real axis.
An intrinsic positive spectral count in [t/4,4t] bounds the band width.

The current construction explicitly pays for a constant coarse sketch,
a certified approximate coarse basis, true actions on the k band
columns, coefficient/point blocks of width q+kJ_s, and all Schur and
physical-output work. With J_s,L logarithmic in mu/epsilon, its cost is

  soft-O(I_X+m^omega)+O(I_X(q+kJ_s)^(omega-2)L)
    +O(mk^2 J_s+qk^2 J_s+mkq J_s+qk^omega).

This is an exact-field arithmetic theorem. It is not an online theorem
for sequential adaptive RHS, a numerical-stability theorem, or an LRA
output theorem. The q+kJ_s versus qk comparison improves the earlier
local per-pole coupling implementation; it is not itself a proved
comparison against every existing parametric solver.

2. PRIMARY PARAMETRIC AND RECYCLED-SOLVER COMPARATORS
----------------------------------------------------
A. Jarlebring--Correnty, Infinite GMRES for Parameterized Linear Systems,
SIMAX 43 (2022), primary complete preprint:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.04082
Read Theorem 2.1, Algorithms 1--2, Theorem 4.1 and Section 4. Taylor
companion linearization and a reusable parameter-to-solution function
are already explicit. The algorithms use A(0)^-1 actions, while the
convergence discussion relates the parameter and companion spectrum.
This directly precedes the general sharing mechanism. Its statements
do not by themselves supply the raw-factor access/preparation and
intrinsic-band forward-error theorem in Section 1; applying the method
here would require that additional access and preconditioning analysis.

B. Correnty--Jarlebring--Soodhalter, Preconditioned Infinite GMRES for
Parameterized Linear Systems, SISC (2023), primary complete article:
  https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/22M1502380
Read Algorithms/derivation in Sections 2--4 and Theorem 5.1. The latter
controls the inexact residual by an exact-GMRES polynomial minimum plus
an accuracy allowance, conditional on the inner-solve criterion. Thus
inexact preconditioned parameter reuse is established. A forward
normalized-resolvent guarantee, a uniform safe band and the price of
accessing an unknown dense raw Gram are additional obligations; the
residual theorem cannot be substituted for those automatically.

C. Gao--Jiang, Toeplitz Infinite GMRES for Parameterized Linear Systems,
arXiv:2610.01740v2, 26 October 2026:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.01740
Read Algorithm 3.1, Tables 1--2, Theorems 4.1/4.3 and Hypothesis 4.2.
Compact shared Taylor/Arnoldi structure is explicit. The basic
O(np^2+p^3) basis cost assumes linear-cost coefficient actions and
stored-factor triangular solves, and excludes setup; Algorithm 3.1
first factors A0. The rebased construction additionally uses fixed
separated coefficient structure and exact matrix-function actions.
These are relevant implementation comparators, not a matching charged
raw-factor setup theorem. Its residual lower bound is a restriction on
its normalized companion formulation, not a universal obstruction to
our safe-block/Schur approach.

D. Bruyninckx--Huybrechs--Meerbergen, Compact Rational Krylov for
Parametrized Systems with Application to BEM Frequency Sweeping,
arXiv:2607.07440v2, 6 August 2026:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07440
Read Theorems 3.1/3.2/3.7, Lemma 3.5, Sections 3.3--4 and Proposition
4.1. The paper supplies compact rational linearizations, explicit
parameter-dependent RHS augmentation and inexact-solve analysis.
Lemma 3.5 and the cost discussion still require original P(sigma)
solves during the iteration. Thus neither sharing nor handling an
affine RHS is new in isolation. The theorem is not directly a substitute
for constructing those solves from arbitrary dense raw factors within
the budget of Section 1.

E. Soodhalter--Szyld--Xue, Krylov Subspace Recycling for Sequences of
Shifted Linear Systems, primary complete preprint arXiv:1301.2650v3:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.2650
Read Theorems 3.2/5.1 and Algorithm 5.1. Recycled shifted solves are
classical. Theorem 3.2's incompatibility concerns simultaneous collinear
residuals over its fixed augmented GMRES space; it is not a general
impossibility for our coefficient blocks and arbitrary batched RHS.
Theorem 5.1 tracks a shift-dependent residual defect. The shared
correction space and residual tracking precede our work, while the
particular raw-access cost/forward-error guarantee remains to be checked
separately rather than read out of that theorem.

F. Autio--Hannukainen, Compound Krylov Subspace Methods for Parametric
Linear Systems, primary complete preprint arXiv:2109.12206v1:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.12206
Read Theorems 3.9/3.17/3.19 and the offline/online construction. The
paper builds parameter-independent compound spaces and controls
low-rank linearization errors for SPD affine families. Its displayed
solution bound contains the CG condition-number factor plus a cutoff
tolerance. It demonstrates established reduced-basis and approximate
series-space mechanisms. It does not directly cover the present
indefinite complex shifted pencil with its separately certified safe
compression and normalized raw-output guarantee.

These comparisons distinguish theorem scopes. They do not assert that
the old frameworks could never be adapted to obtain the same result.

3. CLASSICAL PIECES AND A SIMPLER INTERNAL BASELINE
--------------------------------------------------
Shah's 2024 Theorem 4.2 supplies approximate Hermitian diagonalization;
Neiger--Pernet--Villard's 2024 Corollary 5.2 supplies the Frobenius form
with transformation. Both are imported complete algorithmic results,
not new claims here. Schur elimination, analytic Taylor truncation,
Cauchy estimates, preconditioned Neumann iteration and shared polynomial
evaluation are also classical mechanisms. The bounded scalar transform
2x/(1+x^2) is not a claimed new scalar approximation family.

There is moreover a simple INTERNAL comparator showing that the near
soft-O rate need not depend on the sophisticated Frobenius-series
backend. Put H=B_QQ in the shared-series notation and let

  A=(H-I)^-1, T0=A H=I+A.

The certified safe spectrum gives ||A||<=2 and ||T0||<=2. Since
F(u)=A^-1+uH-D_QQ, fixed-preconditioner Richardson has multiplier

  A D_QQ-uT0,
  ||A D_QQ-uT0||<=2eta+2|u|.

This is <=3/4 on |u|<=1/4 and <=11/12 on |u|<=1/3. The same affine
coupling coefficients can therefore be computed from

  U_(ell+1)(u)=A Lambda(u)+A D_QQ U_ell(u)-uT0 U_ell(u)

with all operations truncated modulo u^J_s. Its actual coefficient and
point blocks have the same width q+kJ_s. Both fixed coarse products
batch across that width, as does the true D_QQ action. The source's
analytic truncation and Schur proof then apply with a larger fixed
iteration constant, replacing (3/8)^L by (11/12)^L in the coupling
bound and (1/4)^L by (3/4)^L in the pointwise bound. L is still
O(log(mu/epsilon)); shared postprocessing is unchanged.

Thus an elementary constant-coarse-preconditioner implementation gives
the same asymptotic near-pole budget after a split has been constructed.
This calculation was independently sanity-checked by bounded_algorithm.
It does not alter the already reviewed faster-contraction implementation;
far poles can retain the imported full-core shared resolver. It narrows
any novelty wording: no new general principle of analytic parameter
reuse or indispensable new Frobenius-series mechanism has been proved.

4. WHAT THE LIMITED SEARCH DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------------------------------
The inspected theorem statements do not directly combine all of:
an arbitrary raw XJX* input; no supplied true dense Gram; a constructed
coarse band basis with uniform weighted leakage; arbitrary batched RHS;
near-real normalized forward error; intrinsic band population; and the
fully charged rectangular-product cost of Section 1. The local proof
does establish that combined guarantee at its stated arithmetic scope.

The defensible description is therefore a proved specialized raw-access
construction built from established tools, with historical priority
unverified. It is premature to call it the first such solver, claim
general superiority to rational Krylov/model reduction, or identify the
q+kJ_s form alone as a new rate. A literature adaptation or an older
uninspected theorem could still match the precise guarantee.

5. FAIR CLASSICAL LRA COMPARATOR AT A TAIL-SCALE CENTER
-----------------------------------------------------
The primary source is Musco--Musco, Randomized Block Krylov Methods
for Stronger and Faster Approximate Singular Value Decomposition (2015):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.05477
Read Theorems 10/12/13, Algorithms 1--2 and Lemma 15. Theorem 13
allows p>=rank-target Gaussian starting columns; its iteration count
depends on the oversampled ratio sigma_k/sigma_(p+1), rather than the
adjacent singular-value gap. Theorem 12 controls per-vector Rayleigh
deficit. Lemma 15 transfers an additive squared-Frobenius excess to
the same additive squared-spectral excess. These are existing results.

The following is our comparison calculation for an arbitrary product
M=A_factor B_factor, with sigma_2(M)>0. Assume the supplied center is
at the LRA tail scale, for example

  sigma_2(M)/2<=t<=2sigma_2(M),
  #{i:t/4<=sigma_i(M)<=4t}<=kappa.                    (6)

At most one singular value exceeds 4t, since sigma_2<=2t. Therefore
with p=kappa+1,

  sigma_(p+1)(M)<t/4<=sigma_2(M)/2<=sigma_1(M)/2.     (7)

The oversampled ratio is a fixed constant or better. Hence the classical
block algorithm needs only O(log(N/theta)) iterations for rank-one
accuracy theta. Let Q be the orthonormal basis of its full collected
Krylov space, of width s=soft-O(kappa+1). We need not invoke a free
exact Ritz eigensystem: the published guarantee implies the EXISTENCE
within Q of a unit vector whose Rayleigh deficit is at most
(theta/4)sigma_2(M)^2. Thus for B=Q*M and C=B B*,

  lambda_max(C)>=sigma_1(M)^2-(theta/4)sigma_2(M)^2.  (8)

Both B and C are obtained using thin raw actions and a small Gram of
those explicit thin results; no full raw-factor Gram is formed.

To avoid an unpaid exact SVD in the final projected solve, run ordinary
rank-one simultaneous power iteration on B to accuracy theta/4. It
requires O(theta^-1 log(N/theta)) iterations by the classical gap-free
per-vector guarantee. Its iterates use the explicit s-by-s C; for rank
one its final reduced eigenproblem is scalar. Its unit output v obeys

  v*C v>=lambda_max(C)-(theta/4)sigma_2(B)^2,
  sigma_2(B)<=sigma_2(M).

Consequently z=Qv has global Rayleigh deficit at most
(theta/2)sigma_2(M)^2. This is the exact excess squared-Frobenius error
of z z*M over the optimal rank-one projection. The additive transfer
then gives spectral error <=sqrt(1+theta/2)sigma_2(M), hence no more
than (1+theta)sigma_2(M). No exact general eigenvector oracle is used.

Here is the complete arithmetic comparison at the original parameters
theta=1/m, N=m^(9/8), kappa<=soft-O(sqrt(m)):

  initial/block Krylov raw actions:
      soft-O(I_X (kappa+1)^(omega-2));
  ordinary thin QR and projected Gram:
      soft-O(N (kappa+1)^2);
  scalar-postprocessed power iteration on the projected core:
      soft-O(m (kappa+1)^2);
  final raw transpose action and explicit rank-one product:
      O(I_X+N^2).

At the feasible omega=9/4 convention, these are all bounded by
m^(9/4+o(1)). Confidence can be amplified with independent starting
blocks, concatenating their spaces; for the last scalar-output power
iteration select the largest exactly computed core Rayleigh value.
Only logarithmic factors are added. Gaussian sampling is charged under
the same discrete approximation convention as the present resolver.

This comparator assumes a tail-scale center as in (6). A resolver is
allowed to use any center, so it is not a theorem that LRA subsumes the
arbitrary-RHS resolvent output. The implication is narrower and decisive:
a small multiplicative band around sigma_2 already supplies a classical
fast LRA route. The present restricted resolver cannot, by itself, be
advertised as a new high-accuracy LRA regime or the original general
matrix-multiplication breakthrough.

6. AUDIT CONCLUSION
-------------------
The precise raw-access resolver is mathematically substantive at its
stated band-population promise, and its local qk implementation bottleneck
has been removed. Its building blocks and broad sharing principles have
clear prior literature. The limited primary search found no directly
matching complete theorem, but established neither priority nor a new
LRA frontier. Preserve these qualifications in titles, state summaries
and any eventual manuscript comparison.
