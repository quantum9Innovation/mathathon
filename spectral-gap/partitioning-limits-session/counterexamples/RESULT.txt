Theorem
-------
For n=4m, m>=2, use C4 square Pm on vertices (s,t,j), s,t=+-1.
Weights: s-flip edges a=4m/(4m+1); t-flip edges 1; path edges 2m^3.
Double ONE s-flip edge. Then:
* The host is simple, connected, planar, bipartite, maxdegree4, 2n-4 edges.
* The original simple Fiedler vector is x=s/sqrt(n); the updated simple
  Fiedler vector is exactly y=t/sqrt(n). Both are flat, orthogonal and have
  no zero coordinates. Fiedler projector distance equals1.
* Their sign cuts are the UNIQUE global minimum cardinality bisections,
  up to complements, and disagree on n/2 vertices even after best relabeling.
* Exact edge leverage tau=a r^T L^dagger r<1/m; therefore
  L <= L' <= (1+1/m)L. For every alpha in(0,1), the whole relative fractional
  correction C_alpha has norm <=(1+1/m)^alpha-1 and determinant budget
  tr log(I+C_alpha)=alpha log(1+tau)<alpha log(1+1/m).
Thus vanishing relative fractional response does not imply any nontrivial
gap-free stability of a selected spectral vector or selected optimal cut.

The proof is an exact product-spectrum and Schur-complement argument.
The fast path factor has gap Lambda>=8m. On the complement of constants and
y, L-2I has one negative direction x, value -a/(2m). The rank-one update
has x coefficient1/sqrt(m). Its positive block has resolvent scalar s0
with a*s0<=1/(2m)+2/(8m-2)<1. Its Schur complement becomes
  (a/m)[1/(1+a*s0)-1/2]>0.
This proves updated lambda2=2, simple. The Rayleigh inequality for balanced
sign vectors proves unique global optimality of the cuts, without a
separate combinatorial case analysis.

EXACT WORST-CASE LAW: for every epsilon>0, over even this sparse planar
weighted degree4 class with one edge doubling and relative order change
<=epsilon, the maximum normalized balanced cut distance is exactly1/2
and the maximum Fiedler projector distance exactly1. Both attained by
finite examples; choose m>=max(2,1/epsilon).

Scope necessity: with maxdegree d and all conductances in[c,C], doubling
an existing edge has tau>=2c/[(d+1)C]. Thus vanishing relative perturbation
necessarily escapes bounded degree or bounded conductance ratio. The
example's growing path weights are explicit and are not concealed.

Additional exact structural observation: along ONE edge-weight-increase
trajectory the entire optimal cardinality-bisection envelope is min(A+t,B),
where A is the best original cut separating that edge's endpoints and B
the best original cut not separating them. All optimizers come from those
two fixed families; there is at most one transition. Spectral embeddings
can have more complicated motion, but exact cut costs have only two slopes.

Four-vertex version: C4 with a=1-delta/4 and other axis1; increase one
a-edge by delta. Updated nontrivial x,z eigenvalues are
3+delta/2 +-sqrt(1+delta^2), so for0<delta<=1/4 Fiedler switches exactly
x to y, both flat and uniquely cut-optimal. Relative change tends0 with
delta while every weight remains in[15/16,5/4]. This permits tiny weight
changes but is not an edge DOUBLING example.
