Candidate extension of bounded intertwiner moments to ALL regular frames
=======================================================================
Root derivation November15. UNAUDITED proposal, not a final theorem.

Use the regular-no-internal frame model, common total degree d>=3,
connected reversible fixed Q and fixed positive size proportions. Factors
are independent configurations: internal pairings and cross bijections.
For fixed Fp vector alphabet V and fixed matrix R, conjectured bound
sup_N E #{Y:AY=YR}<infinity, with direct simplicity conditioning thereafter.
This would strengthen the current random-SIMPLE-BASE-LIFT bounded-moment
packet to general regular/biregular frame factors, including cubic RSBM.

The zero-phase and sparse-induction mechanisms appear unchanged. At zero
entropy, all slot pair MI and omission TC vanish; the finite-group lemma
forces uniform cosets of one common H on each even-walk component of the
old type support. Internal factors supply self-incidence; nonbipartite
support gives one H, bipartite support gives H_L,H_R. Each is invariant
under the on-site linear maps into the neighboring space. The compact
entropy complement is negative. Conditional forcing of E prescribed stub
pairs in pairings/bijections uses at most E switches, changes at most4E
adjacency rows, and controls the conditional bulk kernel by p^(4rE).
The old support count applies; configuration supports of size1 must now
be retained, but their upper bound O(N^(-(d-2)/2)) still tends to zero.
Induct on dimension (or sum of two dimensions for bipartite support).

The new central lemma can avoid a pooled Gaussian determinant calculation:

1. For EACH PHYSICAL SLOT retain its root-neighbor table M_i,s, rather
   than pooling slots of the same neighbor type. Let P sample uniform
   legal stars, Q sample root and all d neighbor labels independently.
   Their full-table means and covariance coincide, with positive covariance
   on the identical-common-root-row space. This is exactly the current
   central-lift-uniform.md covariance proof: d>=3 makes any two neighbor
   labels independent conditional on the root. Physical slots of repeated
   old types cause no change to this assertion.

2. Let W(M) be the exact pairing/bijection probability, which depends on
   POOLED tables and is zero unless their reversal/parity constraints hold.
   A configuration and root labeling still determine all ordered stars.
   Therefore E Z=q^(dn) E_P W and q^(dn) E_Q W=1 EXACTLY.
   Full-table legal lattice L has finite index relative to independent
   L0 (q^(kq) in the one-space version). Relative tilted local CLT bounds
   the logarithmic central window by this fixed index times Q's exact
   normalizer, hence a constant.

3. To control the surrounding fixed neighborhood, apply the tilted local
   CLT uniformly for ||M-E M||<=epsilon n. The P and Q rate functions have
   identical Taylor terms to second order. Thus, on the legal affine
   lattice, P_P(M=m)/P_Q(M=m) <= C exp(C||m-E M||^3/n^2).
   Different positive type proportions only alter fixed constants.

4. Under the NORMALIZED WEIGHTED Q law, first sample the configuration
   and then iid uniform root labels. Its full physical-slot pair tables
   have subgaussian deviations at scale sqrt(n): represent each internal
   pairing by a uniform stub permutation paired consecutively, each cross
   factor by a uniform permutation, and use bounded differences for these
   permutations and iid root labels. A transposition changes only O(1)
   table entries, and one root-label change affects O(d) stars. Means are
   the independent uniform tables plus O(1) from internal loops. Hence
   P_Q,weighted(||M-E M||>=t)<=C exp(-c t^2/n), uniformly
   sqrt(n)<=t<=epsilon n. Fixed number of coordinates.

5. For epsilon small the cubic ratio exponent is <=c/2 times squared
   normalized deviation. Integrating the preceding subgaussian tail shows
   weighted P mass outside sqrt(n)log n but inside epsilon n tends to zero.
   This supplies the central bound without computing a pooled precision
   matrix, and retains every polynomial/lattice prefactor through exact
   P/Q comparison. At infinity the compact entropy argument applies.

For the bipartite induction use two spaces u,v. Common total degree gives
equal TOTAL numbers of lift vertices on the two sides by reversibility,
even if individual old class sizes differ. The P and weighted-Q count
normalizers therefore still coincide. Full-table covariance and local
lattice index generalize exactly as in the existing two-space appendix.

Obligations before claiming this extension:
- Actual uniform tilted relative lattice estimate across fixed epsilon
  neighborhood, with positive-density support ensured on both affine lattices.
- Explicit bounded-difference proof and centering for the weighted Q law,
  including loops and permutations representing internal pairings.
- Exact conditional-law forcing coupling for internal prescribed pairings.
- Zero-phase classification with repeated neighbor types and parity graph.
- Full new source-bound independent audit and charged algorithm composition.

No claim is made here for nonregular row totals. K_(3,12) gives a separate
exponential information obstruction to such an extension in general.
