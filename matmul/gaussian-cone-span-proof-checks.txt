The operator S is a FIXED diagonal SPD matrix, independent of the
m-by-b standard Gaussian start block G. Its head is 1+4/m, its second
eigenvalue is one, and its remaining eigenvalues are the affine image
of a Chebyshev--Gauss grid in [1-c/4,1], for fixed 0<c<=1.
For b<=m^(1/8) and D=floor(sqrt(c/4)*sqrt(m)*log(m)/100), the probability
that ANY rank-one output C with range(C) contained in
span(G,SG,...,S^D G) satisfies ||S-C||<=1+1/m is at most
  C_c exp(-gamma_c m^(1/3)).
All polynomial coefficients and the output may be chosen after seeing
the whole Gaussian block. Nonsymmetric and complex outputs are covered.
This is a restriction on the entire span, not one fixed polynomial.

Normalized cone-resolvent actions on G can be approximated uniformly by
degree-O(log(m)) polynomials at any fixed inverse-polynomial accuracy.
Those responses all lie in the excluded span, including adaptive pole
choices and nonlinear coefficient fitting. Thus unrestricted absolute
operator accuracy alone cannot certify the proposed fixed-start robust
far-cone span bridge at the original theta=1/m target.

Queries on new arbitrary right-hand sides, composition on returned
vectors, exact additional data, a common exact backward-operator promise,
and information from raw factors are outside this oracle restriction.
No arithmetic lower bound or resolution of the general target is proved.

Prior work and research consequence
----------------------------------
Chebyshev Krylov lower bounds and inverse-square-root accuracy dependence
are established ideas. Bakshi--Narayanan (2023), Theorem 1.1, already
prove stronger general matrix-vector lower bounds for spectral rank-one
approximation; Remark 1.3 distinguishes query and arithmetic complexity:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03191
The independent centered matrix Bernstein bound is Mackey et al. (2014),
Corollary 5.2:
  https://lmackey.github.io/papers/matstein-aop14.pdf

The contribution is a concrete fixed-SPD, independent-Gaussian-block
witness with explicit simultaneous constants and its narrow robust
cone-oracle consequence. No historical-priority claim is certified.
The result rejects fresh independent starts as a sufficient justification
for that bridge. The next positive step needs genuinely additional
information or error structure, such as near-real shifts, a common
backward operator, or raw-factor access. The original arbitrary-factor
quadratic-time goal remains ACTIVE and open.
