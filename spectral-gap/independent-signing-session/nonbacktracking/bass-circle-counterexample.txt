An exact conditional Bass-circle obstruction at girth five
Date: 2026-11-15. Independent calculation; historical novelty not claimed.
Status: proved counterexample to a specified selection lemma, not a signing
lower bound against all signings and not a solution of the high-girth target.

1. The tempting selection statement and its failure

For a signed d-regular graph put q=d-1 and

  Q_sigma(u)=det((1+q*u^2)I-u*A_sigma).

This is the REDUCED Bass polynomial: the factors (1-u^2)^(m-n) in
det(I-u*B_sigma) are deliberately omitted. By eigenvalue factorization,

  Q_sigma(u)=product_lambda(1-lambda*u+q*u^2).

All its zeros have modulus q^(-1/2) if and only if every adjacency
eigenvalue belongs to [-2sqrt(q),2sqrt(q)]. The same statement holds
for a real-rooted averaged adjacency characteristic polynomial after the
substitution u^n*p(u^(-1)+q*u).

One might hope to adapt an MSS choice step as follows:

  If the conditional average Q has all zeros on the Ramanujan circle,
  at least one choice of the next edge sign has that property too.

This is false even for connected simple cubic graphs of girth five,
with only ONE edge left to sign. In fact the two ordinary adjacency
characteristic polynomials have a common interlacing, as MSS guarantees.
The obstruction is simultaneous selection of the two extreme roots.

2. Explicit graph and conditional distribution

Use vertex set {0,...,13} and the following 21 unordered edges:

  0-3, 0-6, 0-12,
  1-2, 1-4, 1-5,
  2-3, 2-7,
  3-11,
  4-6, 4-8,
  5-11, 5-13,
  6-9,
  7-9, 7-13,
  8-10, 8-12,
  9-10,
  10-11,
  12-13.

The graph is connected, simple and 3-regular. Its girth is five: one
five-cycle is 0-6-4-8-12-0. The exact breadth-first check in
verification.js verifies that no triangle or quadrilateral occurs.

Fix sigma(2-7)=-1; all other edge signs are +1 except e=4-6, which is
chosen uniformly from {+1,-1}. Call the two adjacency matrices A_+,A_-.
Their characteristic polynomials are exactly

  p_+(x)=(x^2-2)^2 [r(x)-s(x)],
  p_-(x)=(x^2-2)^2 [r(x)+s(x)],

  r(x)=x^10-17x^8+96x^6-204x^4+120x^2-4,
  s(x)=4x^5-16x^3-8x.

In particular p_-(x)=p_+(-x), and their average is

  p_0(x)=(x^2-2)^2 r(x).

The accompanying check obtains these identities by exact integer
matrix multiplication and Newton identities; it does not infer them
from numerically computed eigenvalues.

3. Exact certificate that the parent is good

Let f(y)=y^5-17y^4+96y^3-204y^2+120y-4, so r(x)=f(x^2).
At the six rational arguments

  y = 0, 1/16, 1, 4, 5, 8

the signs of f are respectively

  -, +, -, +, -, +.

The explicit values at integer arguments are -4,-8,28,-4,188;
at 1/16 the numerator after multiplication by 16^5 is 2858737>0.
The intermediate value theorem supplies five distinct roots, one in
each of these five intervals. Since f has degree five, these are all
its roots. Thus every root of p_0 belongs strictly to
(-2sqrt(2),2sqrt(2)). Consequently

  E[Q_sigma(u)] = u^14 p_0(u^(-1)+2u)

has all its zeros on |u|=1/sqrt(2).

4. Exact certificate that both children are bad

At R=2sqrt(2),

  p_+(R)=36[188-240sqrt(2)]<0,

whereas p_+(x)>0 for sufficiently large positive x. Hence A_+ has an
eigenvalue larger than R. By reflection, A_- has an eigenvalue smaller
than -R. Thus EACH child reduced Bass polynomial has zeros off the
Ramanujan circle. Numerically the corresponding outliers are
+2.8576218053... and -2.8576218053..., respectively, but these decimal
values play no role in the proof.

All convex combinations t*p_+ +(1-t)*p_-, 0<=t<=1, are real-rooted by
MSS Theorem 5.1, because this is a single independently biased edge
with every other sign fixed. Hence ordinary common interlacing,
real-rootedness of every convex combination, and circle support of
the parent are insufficient to choose a child with two-sided support.

This graph may admit other Ramanujan signings. The claim concerns only
this partial assignment and this last edge. It does not claim that the
underlying graph is a Bilu--Linial counterexample.

5. A separate exact limitation of direct singular-value control

Use directed edges as indices and define

  (B_sigma)_(u->v),(v->w)=sigma(vw) for w!=u,

and zero otherwise. Permute rows into groups of arcs arriving at v,
and columns into groups departing v. Independently permuting rows
and columns transforms B_sigma into a block diagonal matrix with
one block (J_d-I_d)D_v per vertex v, where D_v is a diagonal sign
matrix. Therefore its singular values are

  d-1 with multiplicity n, and 1 with multiplicity n(d-1).

In particular ||B_sigma||=d-1 for EVERY signing. The ordinary Hermitian
dilation [[0,B_sigma],[B_sigma^T,0]] has the same fixed norm. Applying
an interlacing or matrix-product tool solely to this one-step singular
spectrum cannot produce the desired spectral-radius scale sqrt(d-1).
Long powers, an adapted norm, or another invariant would be required.
This elementary identity is included as a mechanism audit, without a
priority claim.

6. Source comparisons and exact remaining gap

Primary sources inspected on 2026-11-15:

* Marcus--Spielman--Srivastava, Interlacing Families I, arXiv:1304.4132v2,
  https://arxiv.org/html/1304.4132v2 . Sections 3--5: expected signed
  adjacency characteristic polynomial equals the matching polynomial;
  independently biased signings give real-rooted expectations; common
  interlacing chooses one extreme root. Theorem 5.3 gives one-sided
  Ramanujan control, and bipartite symmetry supplies the other side.
  None of those statements supplies the false simultaneous choice above.

* Mohanty--O'Donnell--Paredes, Explicit near-Ramanujan graphs of every
  degree, https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/explicit-near-ramanujan.pdf ,
  Theorems 2.20--2.21, p.10: the usual Ihara--Bass identity also holds
  for edge signs. Their convention agrees with B_sigma above. Their
  method studies powers and traces rather than ||B_sigma|| itself.

* Xu, arXiv:2609.15591v4,
  https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15591v4 , Theorem 1.1: a connected simple
  cubic graph has no exact two-sided Ramanujan signing. That already
  forbids a universal exact selection theorem. The small certificate
  here exposes a conditional choice failure directly at girth five,
  without using Xu's much larger construction.

* Suvagiya, arXiv:2607.17343v2,
  https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17343v2 , Section 7: the expected squared-
  adjacency characteristic polynomial is not real-rooted even on C4.
  This is a different obstruction from the real-rooted ordinary pair
  and its circle-rooted conditional Bass average given above.

The explicit counterexample has girth exactly FIVE. No assertion is made
that the same conditional failure persists for arbitrarily large girth.
A selection theorem using an additional large-girth hypothesis, controlled
slack, or a globally chosen collection of future edge signs remains
unresolved by this calculation. In particular it neither proves nor
refutes existence of signings with coefficient 2+o_g(1). It decisively
rules out transporting the ordinary MSS choice lemma through the Bass
substitution with no additional hypothesis or invariant.
