1. All-parameter recovery, Sections 1--4
--------------------------------------

gives exactly the target independent uniform-simple laws. The ordinary
and bipartite criteria are credited to Janson and Blanchet--Stauffer.

For integer sign profiles with common flip fraction alpha, the auxiliary
law retains the conditional internal-neighbor law given the root, while
making cross slots independent of it. Its relative entropy is
J_++b I_cross <= -Phi. Conditional variance and Pinsker yield
  Phi <= -(2b^2/a^4) alpha^2(1-alpha)^2,

The binary affine system is necessary; integer opposite-neighbor checks
are essential. Candidate count cutoff n^27 has failure O_ab(N^-3).
Elimination plus Gray enumeration uses O_D(n^3+n2^r) elementary work,
O(n^2) storage, bounded O_D(n^28) work, and expected uncut O_ab(n^25).
