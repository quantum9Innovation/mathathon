The theorem that passes
----------------------
Fix K>=2,D>=3. A true fixed degree frame Q has2<=k<=K classes, internal
degrees a_i>=2, total degrees<=D, positive block sizes satisfying balance,
and connected off-diagonal support. All factors are independently sampled
uniform simple internal regular and active cross biregular graphs.
Along all admissible sizes, with probability tending to one, every
equitable partition into at most K nonempty classes is an equitable
coarsening of the planted partition. Given only the fixed bounds K,D,
the stated exact algorithm recovers the planted number of classes,
partition and degree frame with high probability. Its untruncated
expected bit work is polynomial and a polynomial cap gives a worst-case
polynomial algorithm with high-probability success (abstain at the cap).

Random independent cross factors, fixed finite K,D, internal degrees>=2,
and connected true cross-frame with k>=2 are retained throughout. No
claim is made for arbitrary fixed cross matrices in this theorem, growing
K, noisy edits, spectral simplicity, or a practical running time.

1. Exact count, weighted exponent and normalization
--------------------------------------------------
The exact factorial expression was derived independently and exhaustively
checked in the entropy-count module. It counts labeled root assignments
once, retains ordered stub slots, handles internal diagonal counts with
even parity, and uses reversed cross tables. Internal loops contribute2
to adjacency, matching their two stubs. Conditioning on simplicity gives
the intended simple model with only a fixed-factor expectation cost.

There are two conventions in the companion sources. Write Psi for the
UNNORMALIZED count exponent from the exact factorial formula:
 Psi=sum_i N_i H(star_i)-sum_i(N_i*a_i/2)H(pair_ii)
     -sum_(i<j)N_i*d_ij H(pair_ij).
The rigidity and synthesis use phi=Psi/n with alpha_i=N_i/n. Their
cancellation and gap formulas are for phi. Thus a normalized gap
phi<=-c*delta^2 becomes Psi<=-n*c*delta^2 in the count. The synthesis
uses precisely this factor n in its exponential macroscopic estimate.
There is no missing normalization or factor of2 at cross edges.

Symmetrizing slots within a neighbor type preserves all pooled pair
laws and the root laws, retains the local equation, and increases the
star entropy. Therefore any upper bound for the symmetrized exponent
also bounds the original empirical type. The symmetrized law need not
be an integer type. The averaged law is used analytically only.

2. Real one-hot entropy gap, including internal degree2
------------------------------------------------------
The real/integer star equation determines one omitted internal one-hot
label from the root and retained neighbors. Under slot symmetrization,
the cancellation is exactly
 L=-phi=sum_i alpha_i(a_i/2-1)I_ii
       +sum_(i<j)alpha_i*d_ij I_ij+sum_i alpha_i J_i.
All terms are nonnegative when a_i>=2. The auxiliary retained-star law
keeps root/internal conditionals, makes retained internal labels
conditionally independent, and replaces cross conditionals by the
neighboring root marginals. Direct relative-entropy expansion gives
 KL(P_i^-||Q_i)=J_i+sum_j d_ij I_ij.
Multiplication by alpha_i selects only cross edges incident to i from
the global single-edge sum, so alpha_i*KL<=L. This step does not count
an edge twice within the inequality.

The variance reconstruction in the source is exact. Let m_i(X) be the
internal conditional one-hot mean and v_i its averaged variance trace;
let V_j=1-||rho_j||_2^2. Under the actual retained law, the inferred
omitted W is the true omitted internal vector and E||W-m_i(X)||^2=v_i.
Under the auxiliary product law, the difference of expectations is
 (a_i-2)*v_i+sum_j d_ij*V_j
 +E||R_X-a_i*m_i(X)-sum_j d_ij*E e_(X_j)||^2.
No root/internal independence is assumed. This is why a_i=2 is valid.

Every occupied candidate row R_X is a nonnegative count vector of

All constants and inequality directions in this chain are correct.

Consequently, root laws of any zero-exponent type are point masses.

3. Macroscopic exclusion and finite candidate frames
----------------------------------------------------
For a candidate Y, delta*n equals its minimum number of changed rows
relative to any old-block point map JT, with T allowed to merge old
classes or omit labels. At distance at least eta*n from every point
map, the gap gives a type count of at most
 polynomial(n)*exp[-alpha_min*eta^2*n/(2D^4)].
The real one-hot alphabet has at most r labels. Deleting an internal
neighbor still determines it uniquely on legal integer support, so its
number of allowed stars is at most r^d_i. The exact factorial count and
uniform remainder apply to it unchanged. There are polynomially many
types even at zero counts. Hence first-moment/Markov exclusion is valid.

Every equitable partition of a maximum-degree-D simple graph has an
integer nonnegative quotient with row sums<=D. For r<=K, all its possible
quotients occur in the finite enumeration of at most
 sum_(r=1)^K(D+1)^(r^2) matrices. Taking this finite union keeps the
macroscopic probability vanishing. Candidate quotient connectedness is
NOT needed. No tiny nonempty candidate class is lost at this step.

4. Small-support estimate for arbitrary S

 e-s-w>=((d_min-2)/2)*s.

6. Unknown true frame, uniformity and exact algorithm
-----------------------------------------------------
The supplied K,D are fixed constants; Q,k and all sizes are unknown to
the algorithm. Connected true-frame balance implies
alpha_min>=1/[K*D^(K-1)] by following paths. There are finitely many
true degree frames under these bounds; a connected consistent frame
fixes its block proportions. Hence the simplicity constants, entropy
gap, support constants and sufficiently-large-size thresholds can all
be made uniform over the true-frame family. The algorithm never needs
to compute the proof's eta or discover Q beforehand.

For each candidate R, column vectorization gives
 vec(AY-YR)=(I_r tensor A-R^T tensor I_n)vec(Y).
This binary linear system has nr<=Kn variables and kernel size2^h_R.
The explicit modular moment bound is
 E_simple 2^h_R<=C(K,D)(n+1)^B,
 B=K*2^(KD)+K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K).
It is unconditional over the simple graph draw, including rare graphs
with large h_R. The vector-space alphabet F_2^r keeps one-hot labels
distinct; no scalar sign encoding or odd-prime hypothesis is used.

Gaussian elimination costs O_K(n^3) bit operations. Enumerating exactly
2^h_R vectors using any kernel basis costs at most O_(K,D)(n^2) per
vector, including integer verification. Matrix entries and verification
counters have bounded bit size because K,D are fixed. A basis plus one
current vector and current best partition uses polynomial space; the
full list is unnecessary. Summing over the fixed candidate family and
using the unconditional moment gives
 E[total work]<=C(K,D)*n^(B+3).
This argument does not discard expensive failure instances or condition
work on the rigidity event.

A deterministic n^(B+5) bit-operation cap therefore has probability
O_(K,D)(n^-2) of interrupting the complete enumeration by Markov. The
truncated version should return abstention if interrupted; on the
intersection of noninterruption and rigidity it is correct. This is a
worst-case polynomial algorithm with high-probability success, albeit
with an enormous exponent. It is an exact theoretical algorithm and
not an implementation, conditioning result, or timing measurement.

Every draw contains the planted candidate(J,Q) in the enumeration.
