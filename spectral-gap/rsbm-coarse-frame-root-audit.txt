2. EXACT VECTOR-ALPHABET INTERTWINER COUNT
----------------------------------------
Let n=sum_i N_i, a_i=d_ii>=2, d_i=sum_j d_ij<=D. In each class pair
its a_i N_i distinguished internal stubs uniformly; for each active
cross pair biject its two equal stub pools uniformly, independently.
A loop consumes two stubs and contributes two to adjacency. Thus AJ=JQ
holds exactly for the planted indicator matrix J, also before simplicity
conditioning.

For a candidate r<=K and arbitrary R over F_p, use the finite additive
alphabet A=F_p^r of size q=p^r. A row labeling Y satisfies AY=YR
precisely when the neighbor labels at a class-i root x sum to xR.
The d_i neighbor slots are ordered and their old neighbor-block types
are retained. Given x and all but ONE internal slot, that last vector
is uniquely determined. Hence the allowed star set has exactly q^d_i
elements. This does NOT require invertibility of R, a nonzero eigenvalue,
odd characteristic, or a nontrivial rank. R=0 and all rank-deficient
candidate matrices are covered without modification.

Let T_i(s) be counts of ordered stars and m_ij(x,y) the resulting
directed root/neighbor label counts. The internal m_ii are symmetric
with even diagonal; cross m_ij=m_ji^T. For a fixed star assignment,
the compatible internal pairings count

 product_(x<y) m_ii(x,y)! * product_x (m_ii(x,x)-1)!!,

and the compatible cross bijections count product_(x,y)m_ij(x,y)!.
Dividing by each factor's total pairing/bijection count and multiplying
by N_i!/product_s T_i(s)! gives EXACTLY equation (7) of the entropy
companion. Every configuration and Y determine one such assignment;
every compatible assignment satisfies its local linear equation.
This proves the exact identity for the whole modular solution count.

The multinomial for assigning ordered stars already chooses all root
labels on the labeled vertices. No omitted root-label factor remains.
Stub distinguishability, loops, parallel edges, unequal factor sizes,
and zero-degree cross factors are all handled by this formula; the
last are simply omitted. Its use of independent random cross factors
is essential and is present in the stated model.

3. PAIR MARGINALS AND WEIGHTED ENTROPY CANCELLATION
-------------------------------------------------
Normalize stars by N_i, internal tables by a_i N_i, and cross tables
by M_ij=N_i d_ij=N_j d_ji. Let mu_i be each root law, nu_ii its internal
pair law, and nu_ij its cross pair law. Internal table symmetry makes
both marginals of nu_ii equal to mu_i. Cross consistency and balance
make the marginals of nu_ij equal to mu_i,mu_j. This is where both the
cross reversal and unequal-size balance are required; they are used
correctly in the sources.

Average each star law over permutations WITHIN its slots of a given
old neighbor-block type. This preserves each pooled pair law, root law,
and linear support equation, and increases entropy. It need not produce
an integer empirical type: only a probability-law inequality is used.
No permutation of distinct old neighbor types is being assumed.

For the averaged law, delete one internal slot and denote the retained
conditional total correlation by J_i>=0. Expanding conditional entropy
gives

 H(star_i)=(2-d_i)H(mu_i)+(a_i-1)H(nu_ii)
                +sum_(j!=i)d_ij H(nu_ij)-J_i.

The logarithmic factorial main exponent is

 Phi_total=sum_i N_i H(star_i)
       -sum_i (N_i a_i/2)H(nu_ii)
       -sum_(i<j)M_ij H(nu_ij).

Substitute H(nu_ii)=2H(mu_i)-I_ii and
H(nu_ij)=H(mu_i)+H(mu_j)-I_ij. All root-entropy coefficients cancel:
the cross contribution is counted twice among stars and once among
edge factors, with the SAME M_ij in both directions. The exact result is

 Phi_total=-sum_i N_i(a_i/2-1)I_ii
           -sum_(i<j)M_ij I_ij-sum_i N_i J_i <=0.

Every coefficient is nonnegative when a_i>=2, so internal degree two
is covered without falsely asserting its internal mutual information
vanishes. Original unsymmetrized Phi is at most this averaged Phi.
The calculation is independent of R and permits mixed actual total
degrees d_i. It does not rely on a common regular degree for the graph.

4. UNIFORM FINITE-SIZE ERRORS, TYPE COUNT, AND SIMPLE CONDITIONING

The companion uses f(u)=u log u-u, f(0)=0, with
0<=log(u!)-f(u)<=log(u+1)+1. Its double-factorial identity gives
|log((u-1)!!)-f(u)/2|<=2[log(u+1)+1] for even u, including u=0.
Every table count is <=Dn. A root multinomial's upper error is only
one such term: nonnegative denominator errors can be dropped. Each

q(q-1)/2+2q+2 <=2q^2 (q>=2)
correctly accounts for its off-diagonal, diagonal, and denominator
errors. Thus the explicit F coefficient is valid uniformly, including

 S_*=K q_*^D,
 F_*=K+[K(K+3)/2]q_*^2,

bound the number of local types by (n+1)^S_* and each term by
exp[F_*(log(Dn+1)+1)]. Since e<3 and Dn+1<=D(n+1), this proves

 E_configuration |{Y:AY=YR}| <=(3D)^F_* (n+1)^B_*.

At p=2 this is exactly the synthesis's constants F,B. The degree-

Each simple internal graph has (a_i!)^N_i stub realizations; each simple

Connected true Q, balance, and bounded integer positive degrees compare

5. REAL ONE-HOT GAP, INCLUDING a_i=2 AND MIXED DEGREES
----------------------------------------------------
For candidate integer partitions use the abstract one-hot labels
e_1,...,e_r over the REALS, with the INTEGER identity

 sum_neighbor e_label = R_(root,*).

The exact count applies to these legal stars as well, with alphabet
size r and no modular collapse. Let alpha_i=N_i/n. Write Phi for
Phi_total/n and L=-Phi after slot symmetrization. The exact identity
from Section 3 becomes

 L=sum_i alpha_i(a_i/2-1)I_ii
     +sum_(i<j)alpha_i d_ij I_ij+sum_i alpha_i J_i.

For a fixed old class i, retain its root and all but one internal
neighbor. Let P_i^- be its actual retained-star law. Let Q_i preserve
the root law and each ORIGINAL conditional internal-neighbor law,
but make retained internal slots independent given the root and make
all cross slots independent with their neighboring root laws rho_j.
Expanding divergence gives exactly

 kappa_i=KL(P_i^-||Q_i)=J_i+sum_(j!=i)d_ij I_ij,
 alpha_i kappa_i<=L.

For the last inequality, incident cross edges occur ONCE in the
single edge sum for L. Only this fixed i is being compared; there is
no erroneous summation of both endpoint KL bounds against one copy
of that edge budget. Zero-probability roots cause no problem since
the auxiliary law has the same root law and is positive on every
positive actual retained-star atom.

Put m_i(X)=E[e_internal|X], v_i=E tr Var(e_internal|X), and
V_j=tr Var(e_(root_j))=1-||rho_j||_2^2. Infer the omitted vector

 W=R_(X,*)-sum_retained e_label,
 f=||W-m_i(X)||_2^2.

Actual slot exchangeability gives E_P f=v_i. Conditional independence
under Q_i gives EXACTLY

 E_Q f-E_P f=(a_i-2)v_i+sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j
       +E||R_(X,*)-a_i m_i(X)-sum_(j!=i)d_ij E e_(root_j)||^2
 >=sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j.

This retains the internal root dependence and so works at a_i=2.
For every occupied root label, legality forces its nonnegative row
R_(X,*) to have total mass d_i, the ACTUAL degree of class i. The
retained neighbor sum plus m_i(X) is also nonnegative of mass d_i.
Consequently 0<=f<=2d_i^2 under both laws. Mixed total degrees are
therefore harmless; an occupied candidate label cannot have a wrong
row total. Rows unused in old class i have zero probability and do
not affect this bound, even if they are unused altogether.

Pinsker and the bounded-range expectation inequality yield

 sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j <=2d_i^2 sqrt(kappa_i/2)
                   <=2d_i^2 sqrt(L/(2alpha_i)),
 L>=alpha_i[sum_(j!=i)d_ij V_j]^2/(2d_i^4).

Define delta=sum_j alpha_j(1-max_x rho_j(x)). This is exactly the
least fraction of rows changed in replacing the labeling by a planted

than r labels. Since V_j>=1-max rho_j, at least one j has V_j>=delta.
The true cross-frame is connected with k>=2, so it has a neighbor i
with d_ij>=1. Hence

 Phi<=-alpha_min delta^2/(2D^4).

All constants and directions check. The original unsymmetrized
exponent is no larger and has the same delta. This explicit deficit

If Phi=0, every old root law is a point. Matching consistency forces

6. ARBITRARY SMALL ROW SUPPORTS: CONSTANTS AND QUANTIFIERS
--------------------------------------------------------
The true connected cross-frame with k>=2 gives each old class at
least one cross neighbor. Together with a_i>=2 this implies minimum
TOTAL degree d_min>=3. Let rho=alpha_min and delta_0=d_min.

Call S bad if no outside vertex has exactly one incident edge into S.
Let s=|S|, w be its exterior neighbor count, a its internal edge count,
b its outgoing edge count, and e=a+b. Loops count once in a and twice
in degrees. For every bad set,

 delta_0 s<=2a+b<=Ds,  2w<=b,
 e-s-w>=((delta_0-2)/2)s.

The last quantity is integer on the left and is thus at least its
ceiling. This does not require equal support counts across planted
classes, eigenvector signs, or a scalar eigenvalue.

A witness chooses S,W, selected stubs at W, and the e prescribed pairs
covering all stubs of S. Each positive typed factor has at least rho n
stubs per pool. For s<=rho n/(4D), all needed internal or cross partial
pairing denominators are >=rho n/2, so any valid partial prescription
costs at most (2/(rho n))^e, independently across its factors. Wrong
types or incompatible prescriptions have probability zero and can be
overcounted for an upper bound.

The counts binom(n,s)binom(n,w), 2^(Dw) selected-stub choices, and
(2Ds)^e pairings give the companion's estimate. Its rearrangement is

 e_Euler^(s+w) 2^(Dw) (4D/rho)^e
        (s/n)^(e-s-w) (s/w)^w,

with (s/w)^w=1 at w=0. The inequality (s/w)^w<=exp(s/e_Euler),
w<=Ds/2,e<=Ds, and at most (Ds+1)^2 choices of e,w give exactly the
displayed conservative constant C in the support source and

 E Z_s<=C^s (s/n)^ceil[((delta_0-2)/2)s].

For s>=2, replacing the ceiling by its argument yields a geometric
ratio <=C exp(c) eta^c, c=(delta_0-2)/2. Choosing the stated eta
makes this <=1/2. The s=1 term also tends to zero in the configuration
model. In the simple model it is impossible at positive degree,
and summing s>=2 and dividing by simplicity probability gives
O(n^[-(d_min-2)]). All constants are uniform over compositions of S
and over the finite true-frame family.

This graph event implies SIMULTANEOUSLY: for every matrix R of every

would have, at an outside vertex with exactly one edge into S, a

 (QT)_i=(AJT)_v=(AY)_v=(YR)_v=(TR)_i.

8. UNKNOWN FRAME, RANK-ZERO CASES, AND POLYNOMIAL ENUMERATION
-----------------------------------------------------------
Enumerate all r=1,...,K and all nonnegative integer r-by-r matrices R
with row sums <=D. There are at most sum_r (D+1)^(r^2), a constant.
Any actual equitable partition with at most K classes has such a
neighbor-count matrix, including disconnected quotients and mixed
total degrees. Thus this enumeration covers every partition claimed
by the rigidity theorem. Empty candidate classes are rejected later,
not used as an unjustified counting assumption.

For each R reduce modulo two and solve L_R(Y)=AY-YR=0, on rn binary
variables. The Kronecker form I_r tensor A-R^T tensor I_n is correct
under column vectorization. Its solution count is exactly 2^h_R.
The entropy moment controls this full count, including its zero
solution, large exceptional kernels, zero R, and rank-deficient R.
R rank zero creates no exception to the count; integer one-hot
solutions with a zero occupied row of R are automatically impossible
because true total degrees are >=3.

All standard basis vectors e_1,...,e_r are distinct over F_2^r,
including when r=1. Every integer one-hot candidate therefore appears
in this binary kernel. Enumerate its entire kernel, then check one-hot
rows, nonempty classes, and the ORIGINAL integer equation AY=YR.
The integer verification is essential and is present. The planted
pair (J,Q) always appears at r=k; no class-size, eigenvalue, or frame
knowledge is supplied to the algorithm.

On the rigidity event every accepted Y equals JT. Nonempty candidate
classes force T onto, so r<=k. Since r=k is available, the largest
accepted r is k. An onto map between k old classes and k candidate
labels is a bijection, so every maximal accepted partition is exactly
the planted partition up to names. Its quotient is then uniquely
determined by exact neighbor counts. No comparison of numerical
spectral modes or presumed minimal modular nullity is used.

Computing each binary kernel costs O_K(n^3) bit operations. Enumerating
one vector, its checks, and basis updates fit the deliberately loose
O_K(n^2) work bound at fixed K,D. The unconditional simple-model
moment bounds the EXPECTED total enumeration work, including the
rare expensive graph draws. The claimed E work <=C(K,D)n^(B+3)
is therefore valid (and loose). It is not conditioned on correctness.

A hard cap n^(B+5), reporting failure if exceeded, has failure
probability <=C(K,D)n^-2 by Markov. It supplies a worst-case
polynomial-time truncated algorithm; intersecting with the rigidity
event gives high-probability recovery. A running candidate and a
kernel basis suffice for polynomial memory, so there is no need to
store the potentially huge full list. The theorem assumes K,D are
fixed bounds, not quantities allowed to grow with n.
