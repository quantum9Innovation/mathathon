VERDICT
-------
No inspected primary source states the candidate's exact small-s PSD
limit, exact full-line limit, exact ratio four, or the corresponding
uniform worst-case compression coefficient. This is a bounded negative
search result, not a certificate of priority.

There are substantial classical antecedents. In particular, the scalar
root-exponential exponent 2*pi*sqrt(alpha*(1-alpha)*n) is already explicit
in Pekarskii (1995), and positive rational/quadrature approximants with
that exponent occur in Faust--Fawzi (2023). Root-exponential approximation,
tapered clustering, weighted Cauchy/Hankel spectral laws, and rank-one
telescoping must not individually be presented as new discoveries.

The defensible potential contribution is the SHARP UNIVERSAL MATRIX
PERTURBATION theorem: exact limiting Schatten power constants, a matching
finite-dimensional rank-one lower mechanism, and the sharp distinction
between PSD and whole-line inputs. The compression statements give the
same phenomenon a useful minimax approximation interpretation. Their
novelty must be stated with the same precision as their quantifiers.

If correct and not already implicit in an uninspected source, this is a
credible, potentially substantial specialist research contribution. The
search does not justify calling it a seminal concept, a major general
breakthrough in operator Holder theory, or a solution of all optimal
Mazur-map constants. Exact constants in this singular regime are more
than a routine existence bound; the surrounding machinery and its
root-exponential geometry are well established.

1. THE CLAIM BEING COMPARED
--------------------------
Fix 0<alpha<1 and beta=1-alpha. For finite PSD A,B, write

  H^+_(s,alpha) = sup Tr|B^alpha-A^alpha|^s /
                       Tr|B-A|^(alpha*s),    0<s<=1.

The candidate is

  lim_(s down to 0) s^2 H^+_(s,alpha) = 1/(2*pi^2*alpha*beta).

For Hermitian inputs and either f(x)=|x|^alpha or
f(x)=sgn(x)|x|^alpha, the candidate coefficient is

  lim_(s down to 0) s^2 H^f_(s,alpha) = 2/(pi^2*alpha*beta).

The candidate gives that same latter coefficient for rectangular right
and left absolute powers and the homogeneous polar map X=U|X| ->
U|X|^alpha. The proposed normalized worst-case rank-count coefficients
at threshold exp(-L), L->infinity, are 1/(4*pi^2*alpha*beta) in the PSD
rank-one case and 1/(pi^2*alpha*beta) in the whole-line rank-one case;
fixed perturbation rank r multiplies the corresponding leading term.

These are supremal statements over arbitrary finite dimensions and
background spectra. They are not an eigenvalue asymptotic for every
fixed pair. For a fixed finite matrix the s->0 moment has a finite rank
limit, so its s^2 multiple tends to zero.

Normalization matters when comparing papers. If K_(p,alpha) denotes the
optimal constant in

  ||f(A)-f(B)||_(p/alpha) <= K_(p,alpha) ||A-B||_p^alpha,

then p=alpha*s and K_(alpha*s,alpha)^s=H^f_(s,alpha).
The factor four is a ratio of the leading POWER constants; it is not a
claim that the ordinary quasi-norm constants have ratio tending to four.
No exact formula for the best constant at each fixed s is claimed.

The independent PASS recorded elsewhere in this workspace is an internal
mathematical audit. It is not external peer review and does not establish
priority. This file audits literature rather than re-refereeing the proof.

3. CLOSEST CONSTANT-LEVEL PRIORITY HAZARDS

[Pek95] A. A. Pekarskii, Best uniform rational approximations of Markov
functions, Algebra i Analiz 7(2) (1995), 121--132; St. Petersburg Math. J.
7(2) (1996), 277--285.
https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/aa551
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=aa&option_lang=eng&paperid=551&what=fullt

Downloaded the original. Printed p.131, Corollary 3, (5.2), visually
checked, gives TWO-SIDED comparability

  R_n((1-x)^a(1+x)^(k-a),[-1,1])
       ~_comparability exp(-2*pi*sqrt(a*(k-a)*n/k)),

for integer k and noninteger a in (0,k). The lower bound is attributed
there to N. S. Vyacheslavov (1992), reference [13]; that original was not
retrieved here. Taking k=1 gives exactly the candidate PSD exponential
coefficient. This is scalar minimax rational approximation, not a stated
universal Schatten perturbation theorem. A naive Mobius change of
variables leaves unbounded (A+I) factors, so the uniform matrix transfer
does not follow merely from scalar uniform error. This is nevertheless
a mandatory comparison, including its matching scalar lower bound.

7. WHAT COULD STILL DEFEAT A PRIORITY CLAIM
-----------------------------------------
The most serious residual risk is an equivalence that is not expressed
in the literature as a Holder-constant theorem. A classical weighted
Hankel or rational approximation result might already give the sharp
uniform matrix correction estimate after a standard but unadvertised
transfer. The scalar coefficient match in Pekarskii is evidence to
investigate that risk, not evidence that the candidate is automatically
new or automatically old.

Three points should be explicit in any expert priority comparison:

(i) Uniformity over unbounded background spectra after normalizing only
the perturbation. Scalar fixed-interval rational error is insufficient
without an argument controlling the induced matrix correction error.

(ii) Realization of a matching extremal counting/moment law by a
normalized rank-one update, and finite-dimensional transfer. A generic
weighted Cauchy operator with known spectral asymptotics need not be a
valid perturbation correction with the required normalization.

(iii) The exact whole-line coefficient and factor four. A classical
self-adjoint dilation proves relationships between maps, but by itself
does not give the candidate's sharp comparison with PSD inputs.

The rectangular extensions are valuable for scope, but some are direct
corollaries of dilation and adjoint symmetry once the Hermitian result
is known. Fixed rank r linear scaling is also natural once the rank-one
sharp theorem is established. The strongest contribution should not be
diluted by presenting each such corollary as an independent breakthrough.

Suggested conservative novelty language:

  We determine the leading small-exponent asymptotics of the optimal
  dimension-free Schatten power constants for fractional matrix maps,
  together with the associated sharp worst-case compression laws.
  The underlying root-exponential approximation geometry has classical
  antecedents in BKS, weighted Hankel theory, and scalar rational
  approximation. We have not found these exact universal perturbation
  constants in the sources inspected.
