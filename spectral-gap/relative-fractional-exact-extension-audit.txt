1. EXACT NECESSITY
------------------
Fix 0<alpha<1, A SPD, u nonzero. Let theta_1<...<theta_m be precisely
the eigenvalues of A with nonzero u spectral projections. The cyclic
subspace has dimension m; on its complement A and B=A+uu^T agree, so
all relative correction eigenvalues there are zero. In the active
eigenbasis one may choose every u_i positive by changing basis signs.

The relative correction has entries

 C_ij=d_i d_j integral_0^infinity
              w(t)/[(theta_i+t)(theta_j+t)] dt,
 d_i=u_i theta_i^(-alpha/2)>0,
 w(t)=(sin(pi alpha)/pi)t^alpha /
                       (1+sum_k u_k^2/(theta_k+t))>0.

For every pair of ordered row/column index subsets I,J of cardinality k,
continuous Cauchy--Binet gives the minor as the ordered integral of the
product of two Cauchy determinants times prod_l w(t_l), with the
positive row and column diagonal factors. Both Cauchy determinants
are positive on 0<t_1<...<t_k. Thus every minor is strictly positive.
The improper-integral justification is valid: first truncate to a compact
t interval, apply finite-measure Andreief, then take increasing domains.
The positive integrand integral increases, while the matrix entries and
therefore their determinant converge. Finiteness follows from the
convergent entries, not from an unproved multiple-integral estimate.

The active matrix is SPD as the Gram matrix of linearly independent
rational functions 1/(theta_i+t). Each compound matrix has strictly
positive entries, and Perron--Frobenius makes its top eigenvalue simple.
Since compound eigenvalues are products of positive eigenvalues of C,
lambda_k=lambda_(k+1) would repeat the top eigenvalue of compound k.
Hence every positive correction eigenvalue is simple. The only repeated
relative eigenvalue permitted is q=1 from the inactive complement.

2. EXACT SUFFICIENCY
-------------------
For a target q_1>...>q_m>1 with product( q_i )=(1+tau)^alpha, let
x_i*=log(q_i)/alpha and S=log(1+tau). Choose a small closed Euclidean
ball K about x* inside the affine hyperplane sum x_i=S and inside the
strict chamber x_1>...>x_m>0. This is possible because the target is
strict and m finite. For m=1 the affine space is a point; use the scalar
identity directly.

The source's separated-scale construction uses
a_i=delta^(m-i), z_i=(exp(x_i)-1)exp(sum_(j>i)x_j).
All z_i and their relevant denominators vary in compact sets on K.
The diagonal dominated-convergence argument is uniform: for fixed s>0
the denominators converge uniformly, and the common integrable majorant
is a fixed multiple of s^alpha/(1+s)^2. Applying dominated convergence
to the pointwise supremum error is legitimate. The off-diagonal estimate
has bounded prefactors sqrt(z_i z_j) and tends to zero uniformly too.

Thus the ordered logarithmic spectral map F_delta converges uniformly
to the identity on K. It is continuous, and the exact determinant identity
keeps its image in sum x_i=S. The map

  T_delta(x)=x*+x-F_delta(x)

maps K into itself once sup_K ||F_delta(x)-x|| is smaller than the
ball radius. Brouwer provides a fixed point, proving exact realization
for a finite delta>0. Padding A by an arbitrary positive inactive block,
and u by zero coordinates, supplies trailing q=1 entries exactly.
This proves the stated iff, not just closure. No quantitative bound on
the required condition number or inversion algorithm is obtained.

3. GENERAL REVERSE-SCHUR LIMIT
-----------------------------
For fixed SPD S and D_delta=diag(delta^(n-i)), let
B_delta=D_delta^(1/2) S D_delta^(1/2). The claimed limit

 D_delta^(-alpha/2) B_delta^alpha D_delta^(-alpha/2)
     ->diag(p_i^alpha),

where p_i are reverse Schur pivots of S, is correct.

Diagonal: after t=a_i s, the integrand is
s^(alpha-1)[1-s(S+s a_i D_delta^-1)^-1_ii]. The earlier-coordinate
diagonal shifts diverge, the later-coordinate shifts vanish, and the
ith shift is s. Schur elimination leaves the trailing matrix plus
s e_1e_1^T. Its first inverse diagonal is 1/(p_i+s). For S<=M I,
B_delta<=M D_delta; monotonicity of X(X+tI)^-1 gives the integrable
majorant s^(alpha-1) M/(M+s). Hence the normalized diagonal tends to
p_i^alpha.

Off diagonal: W=diag sqrt(a_i/(a_i+t)), E=S-I, H=I+W E W.
H=I-W^2+W S W >= min(1,lambda_min S) I. The second resolvent identity

 H^-1-I=-W[E-E W H^-1 W E]W

is correct, and the middle bracket has a uniform norm bound. The
resulting entry bound is a fixed constant times

 (a_i a_j)^((1-alpha)/2) (a_j^alpha-a_i^alpha)/(a_j-a_i),

which tends to zero for i<j, precisely for 0<alpha<1. On compact SPD
families all constants, principal inverses and diagonal dominators are
uniform, proving compact-family uniformity.

4. GENERAL FIXED-RELATIVE-SPECTRUM CLOSURE
----------------------------------------
The proposed extension with fixed eigenvalue list r of A^-1/2 B A^-1/2
is logically sound provided its classical inputs are stated correctly:
ALT gives log(q) majorized by alpha log(r); determinant gives equality
of total logarithms. Conversely the multiplicative triangular/Horn
theorem gives an SPD S with eigenvalues r and prescribed reverse Schur
pivots p whenever log(p) is majorized by log(r). Apply the audited
graded limit with p_i=q_i^(1/alpha). Real SPD realization requires the
real positive-diagonal triangular form of that classical theorem; a
complex-only theorem should not silently be substituted. A constructive
real Givens proof or a directly checked primary reference resolves this.

This general statement is a natural synthesis of classical ALT,
multiplicative triangular diagonals, and graded asymptotics. Its novelty
must be assessed separately; the rank-one total-positivity exclusion and
the unit-mass path lower theorem are more specific conclusions.

5. GRAPH SCOPE
--------------
The existing path lower construction has growing dimension. Its fast
internal modes yield relative eigenvalues converging to zero, usually
strictly positive at finite internal conductance. Therefore a target
whose positive entries consume all alpha log(1+tau) budget cannot be
realized EXACTLY together with additional positive eigenvalues. Matrix
exact attainability plus approximate graph transfer proves the graph
closure and exact threshold suprema already stated; no exact fixed-
dimension ordinary-path spectrum classification follows automatically.
