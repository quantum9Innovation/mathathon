1. Imported finite-field mixing statement: primary-source check
-------------------------------------------------------------
Read Mészáros 1806.03736v4, model definition and Theorem 1.6 with its
preceding definitions (PDF pp.1,3-4):
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.03736v4

The model really is a sum of d1 independent uniform perfect matchings on
even N. The theorem supplies the UNSCALED sum, over all x in F_p^N, of
maximum atom errors. It is stronger than a typical-vector statement.
For the additive group F_p with p odd, a nonconstant coordinate vector
has full minimal affine coset, and the symmetric tensor restriction is
vacuous because the symmetrized generator 1 tensor 1+1 tensor 1 generates
the whole tensor group. Constants have their deterministic target d1*a*1.
Theorem 1.6 imposes no p-not-dividing-d1 restriction. In particular the
paper's use of a prime dividing d1 is legitimate provided p does not
divide d2. The source's notation d*MinC is Minkowski sum of the coset,
not scalar dilation that would collapse F_p when p divides d.

2. Affine bound and two-block replacement: reconstructed
-------------------------------------------------------
The nonconstant reference constraint for q_x(delta*x+b) is exactly
  d2*s(x)=s(b),
so it has at most p^(N-1) eligible vectors of mass p^(-N+1).
The constant-vector contribution is at most one, because its condition
is b=d2*a*1 and p does not divide d2. This proves the uniform bound two
directly, including when p divides N or d1.

Independence of C,D gives the displayed product kernel first moment.
After replacing the C factor, summing the D factor over y at each fixed
x invokes the affine bound with b=-B^T*x. Error <=(2+e_N)e_N.
Replacing the D factor then costs <=2e_N, because at each fixed y the
remaining reference sum over x is <=2. No exponential amplification of
the mixing error is hidden. Total 4e_N+e_N^2 is correct and uniform in B.

3. Adversarial cross matrices and reference moment: reconstructed
----------------------------------------------------------------
For two nonconstant vectors, the two reference constraints coincide and
say s(x)+s(y)=0, using BOTH row and column sums d2 and p not dividing d2.
The total contribution is bounded by p. Constants contribute exactly p.
If exactly one vector is constant, a linear system with coefficient B
or B^T gives the bound p^(2-rank_p B) in that orientation.

The greedy disjoint-row-support argument gives
  rank_p B >= N/[d2(d2-1)+1]
over every field, with no spectral or connectivity hypothesis on B.
Thus disconnected unions of complete bipartite pieces, repeated row
patterns, and rank-deficient cross matrices do not break the proof:
their rank is still at least linear in N for fixed d2. A permutation
matrix at d2=1 has full rank. Allowing growing d2 would lose the claimed
uniform asymptotic; the source explicitly fixes both degrees.

If real nullity is >=2, all integer minors of order 2N-1 vanish and
mod-p nullity is >=2. The Markov bound 2/p is valid. Letting fixed odd
primes not dividing fixed d2 tend to infinity after taking limsup_N is
legitimate. It does not use an N-dependent prime or produce a rate.

4. Contiguity and uniformity over B: primary-source and direct check
------------------------------------------------------------------
Nguyen--Wood 1806.10068v2, proof of Theorem 1.6, Section4, PDF p.8,
explicitly gives the required matching-union to loopless-configuration
contiguity and subsequent simplicity conditioning:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.10068v2
Its chain names Janson Theorem11 and MRRW Theorem3. The primary MRRW
publisher abstract also states the all-fixed-d>=3 contiguity result:
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/(SICI)1098-2418(199705)10:3%3C305::AID-RSA1%3E3.0.CO;2-%23

The model chain is not an equality assertion about a matching union and
a uniform simple regular graph. The distinction is handled correctly.

Direct product argument: write Q contiguous with respect to P. For every
epsilon>0 there are delta>0 and n0 such that P_n(E)<=delta implies
Q_n(E)<=epsilon for all n>=n0; otherwise a violating subsequence yields
an event sequence contradicting contiguity. If (P_n x P_n)(E_n)=a_n->0,
the set of first coordinates whose P_n-section probability exceeds
sqrt(a_n) has P_n probability <=sqrt(a_n), hence Q_n probability o(1).
On its complement all Q_n-section probabilities are o(1), by the uniform
small-set formulation. Therefore (Q_n x Q_n)(E_n)->0.

This applies to any fixed deterministic sequence B_N. Conditioning each
loopless configuration factor on simplicity costs a bounded factor for
fixed d1 and yields independent uniform simple factors. If the supremum
over prescribed B did not tend to zero, selecting a violating B_N would
contradict this event-sequence result. Thus the supremum conclusion is
not an illicit attempt to obtain quantitative uniform rates from
qualitative contiguity. Independent random B follows immediately by
averaging the already uniform conclusion.

5. Exact bit complexity and recovery certificate
------------------------------------------------
Let n=2N, D=d1+d2 and H=sqrt(D+delta^2). The integer matrix M=A-delta*I
has every row Euclidean norm at most H. Every k-minor is bounded by H^k,
so numerator/denominator minors have O(N) bits for fixed degrees.
Fraction-free elimination with pivoting and exact integer arithmetic
therefore has polynomial bit complexity, including rank-deficient cases.
An exact rational kernel basis has coordinate ratios of such minors.

On the event of dimension one, the nonzero kernel is precisely Q*sigma,
so its first coordinate is nonzero and normalizing to first coordinate
one returns exactly +/-1 entries. No floating-point eigenvalue isolation
or inverse eigengap precision is needed. A deterministic certificate can
check x in {+/-1}^n and Ax=delta*x. Since A is D-regular and delta!=D,
the equation also gives 1^T x=0; each vertex's same/cross degrees are
(D+delta)/2=d1 and (D-delta)/2=d2. The stated algorithm is given delta;
the source does not silently assume an unknown parameter can be guessed
without additional argument.

6. Parity and excluded parameters
--------------------------------
Matching inputs need N even. For odd d1 this is forced by handshake.
For even d1>=4, odd N is genuinely outside this proof. d1<3 with positive
d1>d2 has only (2,1), also outside the mixing input. d2>0 is essential:
at d2=0 the two internal constant vectors force multiplicity at least two.
The sets of simple graphs are nonempty for all sufficiently large even N
at the fixed degrees, so finite inadmissible sizes do not affect the limit.

7. Strong attempted counterexample and exact scope boundary
----------------------------------------------------------
For (d1,d2)=(3,1), seeing both internal graphs first and pairing clean
cycles with the opposite boundary produces a second (3,1) partition.
The accompanying adaptive-clean-cycle.md and adaptive_witness.py prove
and exactly check this, including a 12-vertex K3 Cartesian C4 example.
An adaptive rule can even be symmetrized so C,D,B have the same pairwise
laws as the independent model while planted-eigenvalue simplicity fails.
This does NOT contradict the positive theorem: B there is prescribed or
independent of the JOINT pair (C,D). Uniformity over prescribed B cannot
be moved inside probability to permit choosing B after seeing C,D.

No source amendment is required for correctness of the present theorem,
which explicitly uses prescribed B and independent sampling. The joint
independence assumption should be retained in every external summary.
