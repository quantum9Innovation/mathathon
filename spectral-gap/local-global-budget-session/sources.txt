Primary-source receipts and scope, November 15, 2026
==================================================

1. Charles Bordenave, A new proof of Friedman's second eigenvalue
   theorem and its extension to random lifts, Ann. Sci. ENS 53 (2020).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04482
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1502.04482
   Inspected actual Theorem 1, PDF page 2 (printed p.2), lines45--54 in
   the browser's extracted text. For fixed d>=3 and all sufficiently
   large admissible n, simple d-regular graphs exist with nontrivial
   adjacency eigenvalues bounded in absolute value by
   2 sqrt(d-1)+eta. Only d=3 and fixed eta<(3-2sqrt2) are used.
   This yields the required cubic gap and the two-sided bound for
   connected bipartite double covers. No expander local-tree or port
   delocalization assertion is assumed. Multiport contact dilution is
   proved directly using the spectral gap.

2. Folkmar Bornemann, On the numerical evaluation of Fredholm
   determinants, Math. Comp. 79 (2010), 871--915.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2543
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/0804.2543
   Inspected Section3 (PDF pp.9--11) determinant definitions, ordinary
   trace-class determinant product and finite-rank approximation, and
   Section4 equation(4.1) (PDF p.12) trace-norm continuity estimate.
   These are recalled as standard machinery, not claimed as novel.
   The fractional relative identity at gap zero is derived in full in
   the session from resolvents, bounded regularization and continuity.

3. Russell Lyons and Yuval Peres, Probability on Trees and Networks,
   author-hosted book/edition page:
   https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/prbtree/
   Nash-Williams cutset inequality/criterion, Section2.5 (2.13)--(2.14),
   inspected in the primary book's indexed source excerpt. Whole PDFs
   exceeded the browser size limit. The manuscript includes an explicit
   radial capacity trial as an independent direct proof of the exact
   half-tree resistance bound used, and specifies both free and wired
   exhaustion conventions, so no unchecked book theorem is essential.

4. Florian Doerfler and Francesco Bullo, Kron reduction of graphs with
   applications to electrical networks, IEEE Trans. Circuits Syst.I 60
   (2013),150--163.
   https://motion.me.ucsb.edu/pdf/2011d-db.pdf
   Inspected author-hosted actual PDF, introduction pp.1--2, showing
   electrically equivalent terminal Schur-complement reduction. The
   session proves the one-vertex nonnegative conductance elimination
   formula explicitly. It does not cite this source as proving the new
   free/wired escape-conductance bound.
