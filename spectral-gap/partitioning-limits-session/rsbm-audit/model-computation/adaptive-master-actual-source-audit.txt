1. Primary clean-cycle input checked directly
--------------------------------------------
Read the definitions, Theorem2 and Equation(2.9) in McKay--Wormald--Wysocka,
Short cycles in random regular graphs:
  https://users.monash.edu/~nwormald/papers/girth.pdf
The source explicitly uses uniform labelled simple regular graphs.
Theorem2 permits a growing forbidden-length set within 3,...,g provided
(d-1)^(2g-1)=o(N). With d=3, g=ell=floor(log log N), and the singleton
length set {ell}, its exponent is -2^ell/(2ell)+o(1), tending to -infinity.
Equation(2.9) gives exactly the o(1) overlap event used in the source.

For all sufficiently large N, ell>=4. A chord forms a different cycle
using a shorter arc, of length <=floor(ell/2)+1<=ell. A repeated outside
neighbor forms one of length <=floor(ell/2)+2<=ell. Both share a cycle
edge with the original ell-cycle. Therefore excluding the cited overlap
event gives the clean cycle condition. There is no unproved passage from
a local tree heuristic to the asserted growing-length probability.
Finitely many small N can use the fallback matching and do not affect
the asymptotic statement.

2. Deterministic construction reconstructed on all vertex classes
----------------------------------------------------------------
The clean condition makes X,U disjoint sets of equal size, with every U
vertex incident to exactly one X vertex; similarly Y,W. The three proposed
bijections are disjoint, exhaust both blocks, and form a simple perfect
matching. For zeta=(1_X,-1_Y), A*zeta equals +2 on X, -2 on Y, and zero
on U,W and both remainders. Thus it is an exact eigenvector at eigenvalue2.
It is linearly independent of sigma because it is nonzero and vanishes
outside X union Y. Sigma'=sigma-2zeta is a sign vector, balances because
the flipped sets have equal size, and has eigenvalue2. Four-regularity
then gives same/cross degrees3 and1 at every vertex. Since 0<ell<N,
it is neither sigma nor -sigma.

3. High probability and computation
-----------------------------------
The two internal graphs have the required clean cycles simultaneously
with probability1-o(1), by the single-factor result and a union bound.
Canonical choices give a deterministic rule. Every simple ell-cycle
appears among non-backtracking walks of length ell. There are at most
3N*2^(ell-1) such walks; checking simplicity, closure and the boundary
uses O(ell) work with ordinary marking arrays. The total claimed
O(N*ell*2^ell) bound is valid and polynomial; here
2^ell <= (log N)^(log 2). The extra eigenvector has exact support2ell.

4. Marginal uniformity and pairwise independence: independently checked
----------------------------------------------------------------------
For independent uniform internal graphs C,D and independent uniform
permutations pi,rho, the transformed pair C',D' is independent of pi,rho
by separate relabelling invariance. Thus m'=F(C',D') is independent of
the permutations. Conditional on m', the matching rho^-1*m'*pi is
uniform over all N! bijections.

For independence from C separately, condition on C,pi,D'. The matching
m' is fixed and rho remains independent uniform: the pair (D',rho) is
independent and the original C,pi are independent of it. The returned
matching is then uniform. Hence its conditional law given C is uniform.
Conditioning on D,rho,C' proves the symmetric assertion. C,D retain
their initial independence. This proves all three pairwise laws agree
EXACTLY with those in the independent RSBM, not merely asymptotically.

Relabelling by pi,rho sends the original-label graph with returned m to
the primed-label graph with m'. The partition and eigenvalue event are
isomorphism-invariant, so the failure probability1-o(1) is preserved.
Pairwise independence does not imply independence of B from the joint
pair (C,D); the source maintains this distinction correctly.

5. Explicit exact witness and scope
----------------------------------
The separate adaptive_witness.py uses only exact integer/Fraction
arithmetic. It checks the 12-vertex instance C=D=triangular prism,
with cross edges X-W and U-Y, in every vertex's two partitions. The graph
is K3 Cartesian C4, and its characteristic polynomial is independently
verified through exact traces/Newton identities. Its planted eigenvalue2
has rational nullity exactly2 and its two sign vectors are orthogonal.
This finite witness illustrates the deterministic construction; the
master theorem deliberately claims only 2ell=o(N) changed assignments
for the random asymptotic example.

The final source explicitly disclaims an automatic statistical recovery
lower bound for an estimator knowing the adaptive generative law. This
is correct: two admissible partitions or a multiple planted eigenvalue
alone do not supply such a distributional impossibility theorem.
