Channel moment theorem
----------------------
1. The hypotheses distinguish present factors from active factors.
   Symmetric 0 <= q_ij < 1/2 and physical row budget one imply at least
   three active physical stubs at every vertex. Slot multiplicity, not
   the number of neighboring types, is the relevant quantity. The
   theorem permits disconnected active support and dimension-zero
   spaces. Active channels are isomorphisms; all ambient channels are
   injective. No inverse relation between opposite channels is assumed.

2. The ordered-star count assigns roots exactly once. The cross and
   internal factorial weights are the correct labeled-stub matching
   probabilities, including even internal diagonal counts and loops.
   Repeated-target symmetrization preserves legality and pooled pairs.
   The entropy identity cancels root entropies using reversibility and
   the physical omission budgets. Every present pair mutual information
   has a strictly positive coefficient, including q=0 factors. Only
   active omissions force conditional retained-product identities.

3. The entropy-zero classification uses precisely those identities.
   Conditioning outside three active slots preserves the three pair
   product laws needed by the reflection lemma. The lemma yields
   uniform cosets of subspaces over F_p, including p=2. Every root type
   is seen through an active opposite channel, so every root marginal
   has this form. Active images equal K_i; inactive images are contained
   in K_i by the retained independent-sum support argument. Varying the
   root yields the on-site and offset inclusions. Conversely, any active
   omission parametrizes the complete uniform legal affine support.
   This proves both sufficiency and uniqueness of each finite phase.

4. Restriction to (H_i,K_i) preserves the entire induction class.
   A proper phase decreases the sum of root dimensions; a full-root
   phase forces full outputs. The dimension-zero base case is valid.
   The forced-zero example correctly requires heterogeneous subspaces.
   Inactive quotient maps are expressly allowed to lose injectivity.

5. The full-uniform central argument keeps physical slot tables with
   their possibly different widths. Its reference normalization is
   exact: active isomorphisms and weighted reversibility give
       product_i |U_i|^(n_i) = product_i |V_i|^(n_i),
   hence K_N E_Q W = 1. The weighted Q law really is a configuration
   followed by independent uniform roots. This avoids an unjustified
   exponential prefactor in the heterogeneous case.

6. Under the legal uniform star law, the root and any two neighbor
   labels are independent uniform: an unused active slot absorbs the
   equation. Thus the legal and unrestricted table means/covariances
   agree, and the covariance is positive on the stated deviation space.
   The character calculation covers inactive, non-surjective channels
   as well as active ones. Active within-row transfers make the row
   congruence map surjective. Consequently the maximal legal lattice
   has exactly the asserted index product_i |U_i|^(|V_i|), with no
   unaccounted periods or admissible-size residues.

7. The tilted finite-support Fourier argument gives a relative local
   limit estimate on the maximal affine lattice. Matching quadratic
   rate functions yields the cubic likelihood-ratio bound; it does not
   claim support saturation at arbitrary small sizes. Permutation
   exposure and root-label exposure give the required subgaussian tail
   under the exact weighted reference law. The internal self-partner
   bias is only O(1). The cubic ratio is integrated after shrinking a
   fixed neighborhood, so no polynomial prefactor spoils the bound.

8. The proper-phase certificate handles the noninjective inactive
   quotient step correctly. Select all active edges incident to the
   nonzero quotient support S. At an outside zero vertex having exactly
   one active support incidence, that contribution is nonzero because
   the active quotient channel is an isomorphism. Its equation needs
   another nonzero contribution from S, necessarily inactive. Selecting
   one such cancellation edge gives support degree at least three and
   selected outside degree at least two. Therefore
       E - (|S| + t) >= |S|/2.
   This does not assume every inactive quotient map is injective, nor
   that every inactive support stub is saturated. Loops, parallel edges,
   and singleton supports remain in the count.

9. Certificate forcing is exactly conditional uniform sampling. Cross
   image swaps have constant preimage count m; internal forcing has
   constant count m-1, including the already-paired case. Earlier
   prescriptions persist. At most four physical equation rows change
   per switch, giving the claimed rank comparison and conditional
   expectation bound without an independence assumption. Enumeration
   includes the subset choice of support stubs, including inactive
   ones. All prescribed-edge probabilities use still-large pools.
   Dropping selection tests and unselected-edge conditions overcounts.
   The resulting B^s(s/n)^(s/2) bound sums from s=1 to O(n^-1/2).

10. The central neighborhood, finitely many proper-phase root-coset
    neighborhoods, and the compact strictly negative-entropy remainder
    close the induction. Direct simplicity conditioning uses a positive
    limiting probability and exact uniform-simple projection; it does
    not infer an unbounded moment from qualitative contiguity. Direct
    sums prove every fixed power, and the raw intertwiner specialization
    uses the same field and arbitrary fixed target R.

Recovery composition
--------------------
11. The one-hot entropy gap is valid with zero weights. The weighted
    divergence A_q is bounded by 2L/c. The integer one-hot equation
    gives the displayed actual/reference second-moment difference and
    the range needed for Pinsker. Averaging with q itself, followed by
    weighted Cauchy--Schwarz and reversibility, yields exactly
       Phi <= -[9c^3/(4D^4)] delta^2.
    There is no division by an omission weight and no required positive
    mass for every candidate label. Boundary laws are included.

12. The ordinary AV=VR support certificate appropriately uses all
    support edges. This is separate from the heterogeneous quotient
    certificate. Its excess, witness enumeration, simple-model exclusion
    of singleton bad supports, and small-support union bound check out.
    Connected balance supplies a fixed lower bound on old class sizes.
    A nearby point map has a clean vertex in every old type, giving
    QT=TR over the integers; sparse exclusion then gives Y=JT. Nonempty
    candidate classes make T onto. Completing the entire finite search
    before maximizing class count recovers the planted finest partition.

13. The required moment is p^(h_R), for every candidate R, under the
    true qualifying frame. The channel theorem supplies precisely this
    raw moment for the same fixed odd prime as the kernel solver. A
    candidate need not itself admit an omission certificate.

14. Column vectorization uses I_r tensor A - R^T tensor I_n. The symmetric
    block operator has rank twice that of this operator and kernel
    ker(K_R^T) direct-sum ker(K_R). Projection of a complete doubled
    basis spans the right kernel. Independent-column selection and
    normalization cost only the charged polynomial factor. Enumeration
    is over the right kernel alone, so no second exponential moment is
    silently required. The retained shifted-recurrence certificate and
    independent right-kernel columns give matching deterministic rank
    bounds, including the zero- and full-rank cases.

15. Odometer updates charge O_p(n p^h) for vector generation; one-hot,
    nonemptiness, and integer equation checks are charged per vector.
    The per-input Las Vegas cost is
       soft-O(n^2(h+1) + n p^h),
    including retries, projection, descent and certificate verification.
    Averaging this valid all-input bound uses the raw moment, without
    conditioning on structural success. Expected workspace and streamed
    output costs are consistent. At fixed K,D the qualifying connected
    integer frames and candidate targets form finite families, so the
    stated uniform constants and thresholds follow.
