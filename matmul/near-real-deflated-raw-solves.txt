NEAR-REAL RAW SOLVES THROUGH A SMALL COARSE SPECTRAL BAND
=======================================================

Working deduction, 2026-11-15 UTC. This is a conditional positive
construction, not an arbitrary-factor LRA theorem or a novelty claim
for Schur complements / deflation. It gives a precise escape from the
fixed-start far-cone span obstruction. The price is explicit: a supplied
coarse invariant split and q(k+1) batched columns for q poles, where k is
the coarse spectral-band dimension. Neither is silently free.

1. INPUT AND THE CLAIM THAT IS ACTUALLY PROVED
=============================================
Use the supported coarse coordinates from raw-factor-fresh-search.txt:

  S=Z K Z^T,       T=Z^T Z=I+D,       ||D||<=eta<=1/8.       (1)

K is an explicit real symmetric small core. Z and Z^T are available only
through thin raw-factor products; the true dense T is not supplied.
For example Z=X L^dagger and K=L J L^T on the common support, after
an OSE of X. All statements below are conditional on that coarse event.

Zero eigenvalues of K may first be removed: choose an orthonormal basis
V of range(K), replace Z by ZV and K by V^T K V. This uses only the
explicit small core, preserves (1), and leaves S unchanged. Thus assume
K is invertible. Orthogonal support reduction is ordinary rank-revealing
linear algebra; it does not require an eigendecomposition of the true T.

Fix t>0. In addition, SUPPLY an orthogonal invariant decomposition

  K=diag(K_P,K_Q),       dim(P)=k,
  spec(K_Q) subset (-infinity,t/2) union (2t,infinity).    (2)

Here the first interval includes all negative eigenvalues. Equivalently
P contains every coarse eigenvector with eigenvalue in [t/2,2t]; it may
contain more. The cost C_split of producing a certified split is charged
separately. In particular (2) is not a free exact-eigenvector oracle for
a general explicit core. Coordinate changes in (2) are folded into Z.

For q supplied poles w_j=t+i y_j, y_j!=0, and supplied right-hand sides
h_j, put mu=max_j |w_j|/|y_j|>=1. All q normalized actions

  w_j (S-w_j I)^-1 h_j

can be approximated to error epsilon||h_j||, 0<epsilon<=1, using
O(log(2mu/epsilon)) batched raw iterations of width r=q(k+1), plus the
small-core algebra in Section 5. Contraction is independent of y_j.

The scalar pole may approach the real axis polynomially fast. This
construction uses raw queries on the data-dependent entire subspace P
and its couplings; its responses need not remain in any short Krylov
span of the original Gaussian start block.

2. THE SAFE BLOCK HAS A UNIFORM CONTRACTION
==========================================
Write w=t+i y and introduce the dimensionless supported pencil

  M(w)=w K^-1-T.

Woodbury gives the exact physical identity

  w(S-wI)^-1 h=-h-Z M(w)^-1 Z^T h.                      (3)

In the split (2), let A=T_PQ=T_QP^T. Then ||A||<=eta and

  M(w)=[ w K_P^-1-T_PP,       -A       ]
       [       -A^T,          F(w)     ],
  F(w)=w K_Q^-1-T_QQ.

The explicit coarse safe inverse is

  A_0(w)=(w K_Q^-1-I)^-1=K_Q(wI-K_Q)^-1.

For every real lambda outside [t/2,2t],

  |lambda/(w-lambda)|<=2.

For lambda<0 this ratio is at most one; for 0<lambda<t/2 it is
at most one; and for lambda>2t it is at most two. Consequently

  ||A_0(w)||<=2,
  ||A_0(w) D_QQ||<=rho:=2eta<=1/4,
  ||F(w)^-1||<=beta:=2/(1-2eta)<=8/3.                  (4)

These bounds hold uniformly as |y| tends to zero. For a prescribed
right-hand-side block b, the finite Neumann/Richardson operator

  R_L(w)=sum_(ell=0)^(L-1) [A_0(w)D_QQ]^ell A_0(w)

therefore obeys the OPERATOR estimate

  ||R_L(w)-F(w)^-1||<=beta rho^L=:delta.                (5)

Applying D_QQ requires one thin action of T, followed by projections
and subtraction of the identity. It does not form T_QQ. If eta=0,
the inverse is simply A_0 and no refinement is needed.

3. ALL REMAINING ILL CONDITIONING IS IN A k-BY-k SCHUR CORE
=========================================================
One true T-action on the k coordinate vectors of P reveals T_PP and
T_QP. These are the k supplied coarse-band columns. If P spans the whole
supported core, they are the whole supported Gram, and that work is
charged. Let b=Z^T h and
R=F(w)^-1. Exact block elimination gives

  C(w)=wK_P^-1-T_PP-A R A^T,
  u_P=C(w)^-1 [b_P+A R b_Q],
  u_Q=R b_Q+R A^T u_P,                                 (6)

where u=M(w)^-1 b. Thus each pole needs the safe solve on b_Q and on
the k columns of A^T. This is the source of width q(k+1).

The small inverse can be large, but its size has a clean bound. Put
H=T^(1/2) K T^(1/2), a Hermitian matrix used only in the proof. Then

  M(w)^-1=T^(-1/2) H(wI-H)^-1 T^(-1/2),
  ||M(w)^-1||<=2|w|/((1-eta)|y|)=:g<=3mu.              (7)

Here |lambda/(w-lambda)|<=1+|w|/|y|<=2|w|/|y|.
The P,P block of M(w)^-1 is C(w)^-1, so ||C(w)^-1||<=g.

Replace each occurrence of R in (6) by R_L, including in the Schur
core, and invert that computed k-by-k core exactly. Call the result
u_hat. If delta<=1/mu, the computed Schur core is invertible and

  ||u_hat-u||<=4mu^2 delta ||b||.                       (8)

For completeness, with beta<=8/3, g<=3mu and eta<=1/8, put
h_0=1+eta beta<=4/3. The Schur perturbation is at most eta^2 delta,
and g eta^2 delta<=3/64. The inverse perturbation bound is
2g^2 eta^2 delta. Hence

  ||u_hat_P-u_P||
    <=delta[2g^2 eta^2 h_0+2g eta]||b||
    <=(9/8)mu^2 delta ||b||.

The Q-block error is bounded by

  delta[1+eta g h_0]||b||
    +(beta+delta)eta ||u_hat_P-u_P||.

Since delta<=1, the sum of both block norms is less than
4mu^2 delta||b||, proving (8). This bound uses an ordinary triangle
inequality, so also bounds their Euclidean combined norm.

Finally ||Z||^2<=1+eta. Equations (3),(8) imply normalized physical
error at most 5mu^2 delta||h||. It suffices to choose

  beta rho^L<=epsilon/(10mu^2).                         (9)

Thus L=O(log(2mu/epsilon)). Dependence on the distance to the real axis
appears in a precision logarithm, rather than in a 1/|y| contraction
factor. No finite-word stability guarantee for the exact small inversions
is asserted here.

4. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SAFE COARSE PRECONDITIONERS
===================================================
The split need not diagonalize K_Q. Its explicit Frobenius normal form
with transformation can be prepared once in ~O(m^omega) field operations,
as in raw-factor-fresh-search.txt Section 5. The transformed companion
recurrences apply K_Q(wI-K_Q)^-1 to differently shifted columns in
one batch, without q dense inversions. Nonreal w avoids every real
eigenvalue of K_Q. K_P^-1 is a supplied-core inverse of size k.

The primary preprocessing ingredient is Neiger--Pernet--Villard,
Computing Krylov iterates in the time of matrix multiplication,
ISSAC 2024, Corollary 5.2:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2402.07345v2#S5.SS1

All q pole/right-hand-side pairs must be available together when each
raw block action is performed. A later adaptive query is not retroactively
included in this batch. The spectral split P itself may depend on the
coarse sketch; it does not depend on an original Gaussian RHS seed.

5. COST, SCOPE, AND THE ORIGINAL HARD GATE
========================================
Let the physical raw factor have size N-by-m, I_X=Nm, N>=m, and suppose
1<=r=q(k+1)<=m. Under the same elementary blocking convention as the raw
resolver, the construction costs

  C_split + ~O(m^omega)
    + O(I_X r^(omega-2) log(2mu/epsilon))
    + O(q m k^2 + q k^omega).                           (10)

The first true T-action on P, initial Z^T h_j actions and final physical
lifts fit the displayed raw width term. The q Schur products can use
ordinary O(mk^2) multiplication; each small inverse uses O(k^omega).
Only the k coarse-band columns of T are queried explicitly; if that
band fills the supported core, they reveal its whole Gram. The thin
buffers have width r, plus the explicit coarse/split data and q small
Schur cores. All such queries and storage are charged in (10).

At the original exponents N=m^(9/8), omega=9/4, the non-setup terms in
(10) are within ~O(m^(9/4)) whenever r<=~sqrt(m), mu and epsilon^-1 are
polynomial in m. This is CONDITIONAL on C_split fitting that budget.
Producing exact invariant subspaces of a general core by field arithmetic
is not supplied by the statement; an approximate certified split requires
its own error analysis. Fast approximate Hermitian diagonalization is
available in the literature, but substituting it into (2) is not done
silently here. A relevant primary reference is Rikhav Shah, Fast Hermitian
Diagonalization with Nearly Optimal Precision, 2024:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09880

The central remaining dimension gate is equally explicit. There is no
universal bound k<=~sqrt(m)/q. Even the well-conditioned diagonal matrices
in gaussian-cone-span-obstruction.txt have essentially their entire
positive spectrum in [t/2,2t] at t=1, so their natural k is m. Their
coarse metric can happen to be exact, which makes solves easy for a
different reason; the band-count condition itself still supplies no
worst-case compression. One cannot replace k by the number of true
eigenvalues above t: a large cluster immediately below t also enters P.

This gives a precise useful alternative to fixed far-cone filtering:
near-real inversion can be reduced to a raw-access Schur core supported
on a coarse spectral band. To solve the arbitrary-factor original gate,
one must additionally avoid the qk width when that band is large, exploit
special metric structure in it, or prove another sufficient condition.
Threshold selection, a sharp final filter, and rank-one extraction are
not claimed by this resolver alone.

6. RETAINED FINITE DIAGNOSTIC RECORD
===================================
A NumPy 2.5.3 diagnostic was run with seed 2026111509, dimensions
m=8,16,32, four independent trials per dimension, and y=1,.1,.01,.001.
Each trial used a random SPD metric with ||T-I||=1/8 and coarse positive
and negative eigenvalues over six orders of magnitude. Forty-eight cases
compared the Neumann inverse, Schur formula and normalized physical action
with direct dense inverses. Recorded maxima were:

  safe coarse multiplier norm:                  1.5560290591483574
  actual safe contraction norm:                 0.11439429755395875
  Schur inverse norm / bound in (7):             0.4477965674225128
  normalized action error / requested 1e-3:      2.8544839100101536e-6

These are finite algebra diagnostics, not proof of the asymptotic runtime,
an exact split algorithm, or a finite-precision theorem. The proof of
(4)--(10), including their scope restrictions, is the substantive result.
