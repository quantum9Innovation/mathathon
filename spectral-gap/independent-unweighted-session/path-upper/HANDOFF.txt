PROVED: UNIFORM UNIT-WEIGHT PATH RELATIVE COMPRESSION UPPER

For fixed 0<alpha<1, any n-vertex unit-weight host path, and any one
edge doubled, the strict count of relative fractional correction
eigenvalues above epsilon is O_alpha((1+log(1/epsilon))^2), uniformly
in n and the edge location. No weighted or asymptotic graph model is
used in this upper bound.

Detailed proof and compiled three-page PDF:
  uniform-path-upper.tex
  uniform-path-upper.pdf

Proof core:
  lambda_k=4sin^2(k pi/(2n));
  z_k=|<b,phi_k>|^2/lambda_k=(2/n)sin^2(k pi p/n).
  Therefore nu([0,delta])=sum_{lambda_k<=delta}z_k<=sqrt(delta).

For any SPD rank-one update, relative C is PSD and is Loewner-bounded
by the relative Frechet derivative D. In the A eigenbasis,
  D_kk=alpha z_k.
Hence, for Q the low spectral projection,
  tr(QCQ)<=alpha sqrt(delta).
The PSD pinching inequality C<=2(PCP+QCQ) shows that delta=(eps/(2alpha))^2
gives N_C(eps)<=N_PCP(eps/2). Both endpoint spectra are in [0,6].
Approximate x^alpha by a type-(q,q) rational function, use rank<=q of
its rank-one update, and conjugate/compress on P. Error multiplies by
at most delta^(-alpha). Stahl's classical root-exponential bound gives
  limsup_(L->infinity) sup_(n,p) N_C(exp(-L))/L^2
      <=(1+2alpha)^2/(4pi^2 alpha).
This constant is explicitly NONSHARP.

General sufficient condition: uniformly bounded endpoint spectra and
nu([0,delta])<=K delta^beta for fixed beta>0 give the same logarithmic-
squared upper; this proof gives coefficient
  (1+alpha/beta)^2/(4pi^2 alpha).

Primary comparison: the only external theorem used is Stahl (1993),
Best uniform rational approximation of x^alpha on [0,1], Theorem 1,
equation (2.1), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9301217 . Primary abstract
checked in this session. The rational rank proof is included and agrees
with matmul/bounded-spectrum-fractional-counts.txt. The n-uniform
leverage estimate is elementary and fully derived here.

Suggested sharp-order lower route (NOT PROVED IN THIS FILE):
For n even and p=n/2, z_k=2/n on odd k and zero on even k. After
n->infinity this gives a continuous log-energy measure with density
proportional exp(x/2) as x->-infinity. The relative derivative kernel is
  D_ij=sqrt(z_i z_j) h_alpha(log(lambda_i/lambda_j)),
  h_alpha(t)=sinh(alpha t/2)/sinh(t/2), h_alpha(0)=alpha.
For bridge doubling the resolvent denominator is between 1 and 2, so
  D/2<=C<=D.
Thus a weighted-convolution lower can establish sharp order for C.
The elementary proof above does not establish a lower bound or an
optimal leading coefficient.
