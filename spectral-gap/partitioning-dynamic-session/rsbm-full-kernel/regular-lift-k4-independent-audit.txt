COLUMN VECTORIZATION AND INTEGER CONSTRAINTS: CORRECT
------------------------------------------------------
Let X be an n-by-t candidate frame, n=12 and t=4. The flattening index
is a*n+i for X[i,a], i.e. column stacking. At row index a*n+i the
operator contains

  sum_j A[i,j] X[j,a] - sum_b R[b,a] X[i,b].

Thus quotient_rows is exactly

  calK = I_t tensor A - R^T tensor I_n,
  calK vec(X) = vec(A X-X R).

This orientation is correct. Here A and R are symmetric, so calK is
symmetric and may be passed directly to the frozen symmetric solver;
no nonsymmetric dilation is being omitted. The independently rebuilt
48-by-48 operator agreed with this formula and was symmetric. It has
288 nonzero entries.

The candidate filter checks more than a modular row sum. It requires
all entries to be actual residues 0 or 1, exactly one 1 in each vertex
row using an INTEGER sum, and exactly three vertices of each label.
It then checks INTEGER neighbor counts against R[label_i,:]. Thus
accepted X is a genuine one-hot equal-fiber map satisfying A X=X R
over the integers. Field congruences alone are not substituted for
one-hot or balance conditions.

For R=A_K4 these integer local conditions say: zero neighbors inside
a cell and exactly one neighbor in each of the other three cells.
They are exactly the regular-lift covering-map conditions in this
fixture. Every genuinely valid such frame reduces into the certified
finite-field kernel, so exhaustive enumeration of that kernel cannot
miss a valid integer frame.

WHY THE TEN-DIMENSIONAL KERNEL AND 24 MAPS ARE CONSISTENT
---------------------------------------------------------
The script's additional validation lifts the K4 commutant through the
hidden planted indicator J, after recovery. This is a sound independent
explanation of the observed complete kernel.

Over F3, write R=J_4-I_4, where J_4 is the all-one 4-by-4 matrix.
Since 4=1 in F3, J_4^2=J_4. Its image has dimension one and its kernel
has dimension three. R acts on those two summands by distinct scalars
0 and -1. Its commutant therefore has dimension 1^2+3^2=10. Lifting
Z to J Z is injective because every planted fiber is nonempty.
The independently checked full intertwiner kernel also has dimension
ten, so these lifted old intertwiners comprise the entire kernel.

Every candidate frame is consequently constant on each old fiber.
One-hot rows force each old fiber to choose a new color; equal size
three forces different old fibers to choose different colors. The
corresponding 4-by-4 matrix is a permutation matrix. Every permutation
commutes with R=A_K4. This accounts for exactly 24 maps and exactly
one unlabeled partition, consistently with both exhaustive runs.
