UNIFORM SADDLE FOR THE (2,1) FINITE-FIELD TYPE SUM
2026-11-15. Independent calculation; no historical novelty claim.

RESULT AND SCOPE

For every fixed odd prime p, the Gaussian coefficient of the completely
uniform profile in finite-field-21-entropy.txt is exactly p. Its dependence
on p is therefore explicit and linear, not merely O_p(1).

The calculation below supplies covariance, positive restricted Hessian,
the lattice indices, and all Gaussian/Stirling constants. A fixed-p
Laplace argument can then turn it into a uniform-neighborhood asymptotic
p+o_N(1). This does not control the p constant phases. It therefore does
not by itself establish the complete first-moment bound or simplicity.

1. RANDOM PROFILE REPRESENTATION

Let A,D,B,E be independent uniform elements of F_p, and put

  C=A+D-B,       F=A+D-E.

For one coordinate define two p by p integer arrays

  X+_{uv}=1{A=u}(1{B=v}+1{C=v}),
  X-_{uv}=1{D=u}(1{E=v}+1{F=v}).

The table pair (m+,m-) is the sum of N iid copies of (X+,X-).
Let P denote this profile law. The exact type sum can equivalently be
written as

  p^{4N} E_P[ W(m+) W(m-) 1{pairable} ] / ((2N-1)!!)^2.       (U1)

For comparison let Q consist of two independent iid uniform ordered
stars (A,B,C) and (D,E,F), without the two local equations. The mean
and covariance of the table pair are identical under P and Q.

2. COVARIANCE

For either table the mean is 2/p^2 in each entry. On the Euclidean
space V={z in R^{p by p}: sum z=0}, write P_row for orthogonal
projection onto matrices constant along each row. Then the covariance
of X+ (and of X-) is

  Sigma=(2/p^2)(I+P_row).                                  (U2)

In coordinates the covariance is

  Cov(X_{uv},X_{st})
     =(2/p^2)1{u=s,v=t}+(2/p^3)1{u=s}-4/p^4.

The row-constant, total-zero subspace has dimension p-1 and variance
4/p^2; its orthogonal complement in V has dimension p^2-p and
variance 2/p^2. Consequently, with n=p^2-1,

  det Sigma=(2/p^2)^{p^2-p}(4/p^2)^{p-1}.                  (U3)

Every plus-table indicator is independent of every minus-table
indicator. For example, (A,B,D,E) are independent uniform, as are
the four linear forms obtained by replacing B by C and/or E by F.
Thus cross covariance is zero. The joint Gaussian covariance is
diag(Sigma,Sigma), although the joint integer lattice is not a product.

3. WEIGHTED HESSIAN AND STRICT COERCIVITY

Put q=p(p-1)/2. The symmetric, total-zero table subspace S has
dimension h=p(p+1)/2-1=q+p-1. At m0=2N/p^2, writing
Z=(m-m0 11^T)/sqrt(N), Stirling's expansion gives

  log W(m)=constant+(p^2/8)||Z||_F^2+o(1).                 (U4)

The effective precision on S is therefore the restriction, as a
quadratic form, of

  H=Sigma^{-1}-(p^2/4)I=(p^2/4)(I-P_row).                 (U5)

There is an orthogonal splitting of S into

  S_add={z_uv=a_u+a_v: sum a=0}, dimension p-1;
  S_0={z symmetric: every row sum is zero}, dimension q.

The compression of P_row to S_add is (1/2)I and to S_0 is zero.
Thus the eigenvalues of H on S are exactly

  p^2/8, multiplicity p-1;
  p^2/4, multiplicity q.                                  (U6)

In particular the saddle is nondegenerate after imposing symmetry.
There is no hidden degree-two flat direction. The full profile Hessian
is also positive definite on the constrained profile tangent space:
integrating the profile Gaussian along the feature-map fibres gives
the feature covariance (U2), and its Schur complement is precisely
the positive table form above. This proves the fixed-p quadratic
coercivity needed to discard intermediate deviations inside a small
uniform neighborhood.

4. THE JOINT PROFILE LATTICE: THREE p-CONGRUENCES

For a table m define the following quantities in F_p:

  r(m)=sum_{u,v} u m_uv,      c(m)=sum_{u,v} v m_uv,
  s2(m)=sum_{u,v} u^2 m_uv,  t(m)=sum_{u,v} uv m_uv.

Each table has total sum 2N and even row sums. Beyond these ordinary
one-star lattice restrictions, the P-law table pair obeys

  2c(m+) - r(m+) - r(m-) = 0,
  2c(m-) - r(m+) - r(m-) = 0,
  2t(m+) - 2t(m-) - s2(m+) + s2(m-) = 0.                 (U7)

The third, quadratic conservation law is easy to overlook. It follows
from A(B+C)-D(E+F)=A^2-D^2 at every coordinate.

These are all extra lattice restrictions. Here is an annihilator
classification proving that assertion, rather than only exhibiting
necessary congruences. Suppose circle-valued arrays f(u,v),g(u,v)
have

  f(A,B)+f(A,C)+g(D,E)+g(D,F)=constant                    (U8)

for every A,D,B,E, with C=A+D-B and F=A+D-E. Vary B and E
separately. Since A+D ranges over all F_p for fixed A, respectively
fixed D, the reflection identity implies

  f(a,b)=u_a+(t_a b)/p,    g(d,e)=v_d+(w_d e)/p mod 1,

with t_a,w_d in F_p. Indeed the row function after subtracting its
value at zero is a homomorphism from F_p to R/Z. Substituting in
(U8), then setting a=0 and d=0, shows

  (t_a-t_0)d+(w_d-w_0)a=0 in F_p.

Hence for some alpha,beta,gamma in F_p,

  t_a=alpha a+beta,       w_d=-alpha d+gamma,
  2u_a=constant-[alpha a^2+(beta+gamma)a]/p,
  2v_d=constant+[alpha d^2-(beta+gamma)d]/p mod 1.          (U9)

The arbitrary binary choices in u_a,v_d encode the even row sums;
the two continuous constants encode the two fixed total sums. The
three parameters alpha,beta,gamma encode exactly (U7). Since p is
odd, these torsion factors are coprime to the parity factors. This
classifies the entire lattice annihilator. Therefore the full joint
P-lattice has index p^3 in the product of the ordinary one-star
lattices. A fixed-p lattice local CLT consequently has central
P/Q mass ratio p^3 on the P-lattice and zero elsewhere.

5. AFTER PAIRABILITY: ONLY TWO INDEPENDENT p-CONGRUENCES

Pairability requires both tables to be symmetric with even diagonals;
the even row sums remain part of the profile lattice. Symmetry gives
c(m)=r(m). The first two congruences of (U7) then reduce to the single
condition r(m+)=r(m-). The third remains independent. Thus the index
of the P-lattice inside the ordinary product pairable lattice is p^2.

To verify independence, it is enough to use diagonal perturbations:
on a diagonal table the two remaining forms are proportional to
the first and second moments of the diagonal entries. The functions
1,u,u^2 on F_p are linearly independent for every odd p, including
p=3. Parity constraints do not change this mod-p rank, because
2 is invertible modulo p. The affine right-hand sides do not affect
the covolume or Gaussian integral.

The joint period correction is consequently p^3/p^2=p.               (U10)

6. ALL ONE-TABLE GAUSSIAN CONSTANTS CANCEL TO ONE

This explicit calculation independently checks the normalization
shortcut described below. Equip table spaces with Frobenius norm.
The one-star lattice L0 in V is the integer total-zero lattice with
even row sums. Its covolume is

  vol(L0)=p 2^{p-1}.                                      (U11)

The pairable lattice Ls additionally requires symmetry and even
diagonal. Its covolume in S is

  vol(Ls)=p 2^{2p+q/2-2}.                                 (U12)

For (U12), use coordinates k_u=m_uu/2 and k_uv=m_uv for u<v.
They satisfy sum k=0. Their Gram matrix is diagonal with entries
4 on p diagonal coordinates and 2 on q edge coordinates. The
hyperplane lattice image therefore has covolume

  sqrt(det G) sqrt(1^T G^{-1}1)
     =2^{p+q/2} sqrt(p/4+q/2)
     =p 2^{p+q/2-1}.

The even row sums impose that the off-diagonal complete-graph edge
vector has even degrees. Its binary incidence map has rank p-1,
giving the extra factor 2^{p-1} in (U12).

Uniform Stirling expansion of the pairing ratio supplies

  W(m)/(2N-1)!!
    =p^{-2N} N^{q/2} K_p exp((p^2/8)||Z||^2)(1+o(1)),
  K_p=(4pi/p^2)^{q/2} 2^{(p-1)/2}.                       (U13)

The local-CLT density contributes

  vol(L0) N^{-n/2}(2pi)^{-n/2}(det Sigma)^{-1/2}.

The symmetric-lattice sum contributes N^{h/2}/vol(Ls), and
the Gaussian integral is (2pi)^{h/2}(det H)^{-1/2}.
Since n=h+q, the powers of N cancel. Moreover,

  (det Sigma)^{-1/2}(det H)^{-1/2}
     =p^q 2^{(p-1)/2}.

The complete one-table coefficient is exactly

  K_p [vol(L0)/vol(Ls)] (2pi)^{-q/2}
      (det Sigma)^{-1/2}(det H)^{-1/2}
    = [2^{q/2+p-1}] [2^{-p-q/2+1}]
    =1.                                                   (U14)

There are two tables, so their product contributes one. Multiplying
by the joint lattice correction (U10) yields exactly p.

7. EXACT NORMALIZATION CHECK AND REMAINING ANALYTIC WORK

Under one independent iid uniform star Q, the identity

  p^{2N} E_Q[ W(m) 1{pairable} ]/(2N-1)!! = 1             (U15)

holds for every N. To see this, sum over pairings and iid uniform
vertex labels; the endpoint-label profiles are then determined.
This reweighted law is the usual configuration model with iid
vertex labels. Its tables are sqrt(N)-tight (the degree is two).
Thus the saddle coefficient one in (U14) agrees with an exact
finite-N normalization, and provides a second way to avoid carrying
the determinants.

For a rigorous fixed-p uniform-neighborhood asymptotic, one may use
either the full profile multinomial Laplace sum or the table local
CLT, with its maximal lattice established by (U8)-(U9). In a
sqrt(N)log N central window, standard fixed-dimensional lattice CLT
and Stirling errors are uniform. The coercivity in (U6), equivalently
the full constrained profile Hessian, makes the remainder inside a
sufficiently small fixed uniform neighborhood negligible. Constants
and the allowed neighborhood may depend on p, since N tends to
infinity before p. The resulting leading coefficient is still the
explicit p in (U10)-(U14).

This calculation does not justify discarding neighborhoods of the
constant profiles. Those phases are a separate remaining issue.
