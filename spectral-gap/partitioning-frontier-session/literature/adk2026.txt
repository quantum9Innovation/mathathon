                                                     A (ϕ, ϵ)-expander-decomposition of a graph G (with n vertices and m edges) is a partition
                                                 of V into clusters V1 , . . . , Vk with conductance Φ(G[Vi ]) ≥ ϕ, such that there are O(ϵm) inter-

    Cut-matching: The cut-matching game, played by a cut player and a matching player to
construct an expander, is a well known technique to reduce the approximation task for sparsest
cut to a polylogarithmic number of maximum flow problems [KRV09]. Khandekar et al. [KRV09]
devised a strategy for the cut player that translates to an O(log2 n) approximation algorithm for
the sparsest cut problem. This cut player of [KRV09] is robust, in the sense that it is simple and
can be flexibly adapted for other applications [RST14, SW19]. A more sophisticated spectral cut
player was devised by Orecchia et al. [OSVV08] which yields O(log n) approximation. This cut
player is less flexible and harder to modify.
    By modifying the cut-matching game and the cut player of [KRV09], [SW19] achieved a (ϕ, ϕ log3 n)-
expander decomposition in Õ( m  ϕ ) time. In a recent result [ADK23] were able by a careful spectral
analysis, to combine the techniques of [SW19] and the spectral cut player of [OSVV08] to get a
(ϕ, ϕ log2 n)-expander decomposition in Õ( m  ϕ ) time. Using the recent fair-cuts result [LNPS22],
both expander decomposition algorithms [SW19, ADK23] can be implemented in Õ(m) time.

A.2    Fair Cuts
In this section, we prove Lemma 3.6 and Theorem 5.1, concerning the flow routines in the cut-
matching step and the trimming step. The proofs are based on fair cuts, a stronger notion of
approximate min-cut, introduced by [LNPS23].
Definition A.1 (Fair Cut, [LNPS23, LL25]). Let G = (V, E) be an undirected graph with edge
capacities c ∈ RE >0 . Let s, t be two vertices in V . For any parameter α ≥ 1, we say that a cut (S, T )
is a α-fair (s, t)-cut if there exists a feasible (s, t)-flow f such that f (u, v) ≥ α1 · c(u, v) for every
(u, v) ∈ E(S, T ), where u ∈ S and v ∈ T .
Theorem A.2 (Fair Cut, [LNPS23, Theorem 1.1]). Given a graph G = (V, E) whose positive
edge capacities have ratio poly(n), two vertices s, t ∈ V and ϵ ∈ (0, 1], we can compute with high
probability a (1 + ϵ)-fair (s, t)-cut, together with a corresponding feasible flow f , in Õ( ϵm3 ) time.
