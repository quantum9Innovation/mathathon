1. EXACT GRAPH CLASS AND EFFECTIVE RESISTANCE

Each cell K_(m_i,m_i) is finite, simple, unweighted and bipartite. A cell
is attached to the external network at ONE port. This detail is essential
and is present explicitly in the source.

For a nondistinguished rational conductance p/q, the gadget has 2p
internally disjoint paths of length 2q. Its conductance is 2p/(2q)=p/q.
Its terminals have the same color. Distinct internal vertices make the
network simple even for q=1; no parallel terminal edge is introduced.

The macro graph is a path. Orient cell bipartitions so every port on
one side of the distinguished edge has one color and every port on the
other side has the opposite color. All even gadgets then have consistent
coloring. Each distinguished length-three path has opposite-color ends.
The three length-three paths use distinct new internal vertices. Before
updating, no direct terminal edge exists. Inserting that direct edge
therefore preserves simplicity, unit edge weights, connectivity and
bipartiteness in BOTH graphs.

The three length-three paths have total effective conductance 3/3=1.
Removing that connector separates the remainder into networks attached
at only one of its two terminals. Cells and side components therefore
carry no terminal-to-terminal current. Consequently the effective
resistance is EXACTLY one, independent of all cell sizes. There is no
asymptotic or rounded resistance constraint here.

2. FIXED-DIMENSIONAL DATA BEFORE THE CELL LIMIT

The matrix witness, finite ground mass in the mass-path transfer,
macro conductances and masses, and rational resistor gadgets are all
chosen and fixed before s tends to infinity. Thus d and the gadget
dimension |Z| are fixed during the cell limit. Rational denominators
and gadget sizes may be large, but do not grow with s in that limit.

Common scaling of K and K' leaves the relative fractional spectrum
unchanged, so setting the distinguished conductance/increment to one
is valid. Rational approximation of the other positive conductances
preserves connectivity and the common fixed kernel sqrt(M); finite-rank
functional calculus is continuous on the positive support. The edited
edge remains exactly one-to-two after this approximation.

3. DIMENSION-UNIFORM RESOLVENT ELIMINATION

Set A_s=s L_s, and use the P,Z,F decomposition in the actual source.
There is no illicit assumption that F has fixed dimension.

With 2m_i(s)/s -> M_i>0, the cell gap on F is at least c s^2 after
time scaling. The external Laplacian is PSD, so it cannot lower that
principal-block bound. Each port has O(s^-1/2) amplitude in a normalized
cell indicator, and the fixed external network has only finitely many
edges. Hence all constants below are independent of |F|:
  PF=O(s^1/2), ZF=O(s), FF^(-1)=O(s^-2).
Eliminating F gives corrections
  PP: O(s^-1), PZ: O(s^-1/2), ZZ: O(1).

The fixed Dirichlet matrix K_ZZ is positive definite because each gadget
component has terminal boundary. The corrected ZZ block is
  s K_ZZ + O(1),
whose inverse is s^-1 K_ZZ^(-1)+O(s^-2). The PZ blocks have leading order
sqrt(s) R_s K_PZ. Their Schur product therefore tends to
  R_s K_PZ K_ZZ^(-1) K_ZP R_s.
The final Schur complement is H+tI+o(1), invertible for every fixed t>0.
This proves the compressed resolvent convergence. If no gadgets are
present, omission of Z leaves the same conclusion directly.

The PP block of A_s is R_s K_PP R_s, uniformly bounded. External
conductances in the update are fixed, so the same statements hold for
both endpoints and for the insertion gadget.

4. FRACTIONAL COMPRESSION: CORRECT DOMINATION

For an isometry J and A>=0, the Schur-complement inverse inequality is
  J^T(A+tI)^(-1)J >= (J^T A J+tI)^(-1).
Subtracting t times this inequality from I has the correct direction:
  0 <= J^T A(A+tI)^(-1)J
     <= (J^T A J)(J^T A J+tI)^(-1)
     <= min(1,M/t) I.
Multiplication by t^(alpha-1) gives an integrable scalar majorant at
both endpoints precisely for 0<alpha<1. The compressed resolvent limit
therefore passes through the fractional integral, even though the full
matrices have growing dimensions and unbounded high eigenvalues.

No full positive-power convergence is assumed. No inverse-power limit
is asserted. This is the appropriate argument for the relative metric.

5. RITZ SUBSPACE AND THE COMMON KERNEL

The physical sum of J_s x equals g_s^T x, where
  g_s=(sqrt(2m_i(s))).
All gadget coordinates of J_s x are zero. Consequently J_s V_s maps
into the physical constant-orthogonal space EXACTLY when V_s maps into
g_s-perp. The source does precisely this. No limiting kernel alignment
is substituted for exact orthogonality.

The normalized g_s converge to normalized sqrt(M), so converging
orthonormal complements V_s exist. Compressed fractional powers give
a convergent SPD generalized pair on this d-1 dimensional subspace.
Its generalized Ritz eigenvalues mu_s,j converge to the macro q_j.
Loewner--Heinz gives mu_s,j>=1. Generalized min--max gives
  lambda_s,j >= mu_s,j, j<=d-1,
for the top full relative eigenvalues on the physical positive support.

6. DETERMINANT BUDGET EXHAUSTION: EXPLICIT ERROR CERTIFICATES

Write k=d-1 and T=alpha log2 for insertion (or alpha log(1+tau) for
the bridge version). Every full relative eigenvalue lambda_s,j is at
least one and their logarithms sum EXACTLY to T. The macro logarithms
also sum to T. Define
  Delta_s=T-sum_(j<=k) log mu_s,j >=0.
The source's nonnegative discrepancy identity is correct:
  sum_(j<=k)(log lambda_s,j-log mu_s,j)
    +sum_(j>k)log lambda_s,j = Delta_s ->0.

It implies the useful dimension-independent bound
  max_(j>k)(lambda_s,j-1) <= exp(Delta_s)-1 ->0.
Thus there is no assumption that the number of extra modes is bounded.

In fact the same proof gives a stronger metric than the source claims.
Pad the macro q_j by ones to the full positive-support dimension.
Since all log eigenvalues lie in [0,T], exp is e^T-Lipschitz there:
  sum_j |lambda_s,j-q_j(padded)|
    <= sum_(j<=k)|mu_s,j-q_j| + e^T Delta_s ->0.
The relative correction eigenvalue lists therefore converge in l^1,
not just uniformly coordinatewise. The physical constant adds one
exact zero correction eigenvalue on both sides and changes nothing.
This is a derived audit certificate, not needed to repair the source.

7. FINAL SEQUENTIAL LIMITS AND STRICT COUNTS

First choose a finite matrix/mass-path approximation; then choose finite
rational conductances; then choose a sufficiently large finite cell
parameter. Allocate the tolerance among these steps. All graph outputs
are finite and retain the exact resistance identity at every cell stage.
No simultaneous uniformity across changing macro paths is needed.

For m eigenvalues above epsilon, the exact determinant and positivity
give (1+epsilon)^m<2^alpha. The ceiling-minus-one formula correctly
handles integral thresholds. Equal positive targets are used only with
a strict margin and approximation; no false finite multiplicity
attainment is required.

8. NONERROR SCOPE CAVEAT: GROWING-DIMENSION CLOSURE

The theorem says every finite target with product(q)=2^alpha is
realizable as a limit. It does NOT claim that this determinant equality
is necessary for every possible padded-sup-norm limit over growing
dimensions. Such a converse would be false: spread T equally over
m->infinity directions, so all corrections tend uniformly to zero
while the finite-stage product stays 2^alpha. The all-ones list is
therefore also a growing-dimensional uniform limit.

The actual source avoids that false converse. Its chosen sequence
exhausts the Ritz budget and even has l^1 convergence, as shown above.
The distinction should be preserved in summaries and priority claims.
