Checks reconstructed independently:

1. The integral follows directly from the scalar positive-power formula.
   F_t is bounded by D and L/t, giving convergence for 0<alpha<1.

2. The general Schur-complement-update lemma follows by completing the
   base block quadratic form and minimizing the remaining positive update.

3. The three-column update is correctly scaled. In the two-layer network
   C=K_t/t and the internal-edge column is sqrt(delta/t)b. Thus its
   corrected terminal column is sqrt(delta t)D K_t^{-1}b. A mass-edge
   column corrects to sqrt(delta)(I-tD K_t^{-1})e
   =sqrt(delta)L K_t^{-1}e. Denominator rescaling gives exactly the
   displayed V_t. These expressions are generally nonsymmetric, but their
   product factor is correctly symmetric. All columns sum to zero.

4. The normalized adjacency eigenvalues are exactly {+-1,+-rho}; the
   tridiagonal determinant verifies rho^2=w/((w+h)(1+h)). Its expansion,
   the component-normalized limiting slow vector, and the projection
   squared 8w/(w+1) give the asserted fractional energy asymptotic.
   Although the two high eigenvalues coalesce at h=0, their JOINT
   spectral projector is analytic because that pair is separated from
   the two small eigenvalues. Therefore the stated O(h^2) high energy
   bound is valid.

5. The cutoff bound uses z=Lx=L'x=(0,2h,-2h,0), x^TLx=x^TL'x,
   K'_t>=K_t>=tI. Its integral is exactly bounded by
   8 c_alpha h^2 a^(alpha-2)/(2-alpha). This is a failure of this low
   truncation method, not an algorithmic lower bound. The source states
   that limitation and distinguishes weighted from unweighted hosts.

6. The generalized spectral claim is immediate by the D^(1/2) change
   of variables: Hx=lambda^alpha D x iff Lx=lambda D x. It correctly
   prevents interpreting powering alone as a new rounding theorem.
