Baseline checks that prevent false advances
------------------------------------------
L^alpha has the same eigenvectors and ordering as L for alpha>0.
Applying a fractional power alone cannot repair Fiedler localization.
A small relative energy change does not by itself imply stable labels.
Hard eigenvector cutoffs are discontinuous at nearly coincident eigenvalues.
One must distinguish changing an embedding from changing cut quality,
global versus cluster eigengaps, normalized versus unnormalized Laplacians,
edge versus vertex separators, and labels up to a global permutation.

Concrete big-if-true target currently prioritized for additional work
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Zhu--Zhu, Non-backtracking eigenvalues and eigenvectors of random regular
graphs and hypergraphs, arXiv2312.03300v3, September 25, 2026. Root inspected
the primary abstract and HTML Introduction1.3, especially lines69--71.
It explicitly says the full Barucca efficient exact-recovery conjecture
for the regular stochastic block model remains open: d1>d2>0.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03300
https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03300v3

Two equal blocks each have internal degree d1; cross degree is d2.
The planted label vector sigma satisfies A sigma=(d1-d2)sigma exactly.
That eigenvalue can be buried in the bulk, so an outlier-gap method can
miss it. A sufficient stronger target is to prove, with high probability,
  ker[A-(d1-d2)I]=span(sigma).
If true, exact rational/modular kernel recovery gives a polynomial-time
algorithm without requiring an outlier gap. This is a TARGET, not a proved
theorem. Check the exact sampling model, parity, low-degree case(2,1),
identifiability, alternate equitable bisections, and current nonsingularity
theorems for random regular block graphs/lifts. A general recovery theorem
does not require this sufficient simplicity condition, so a counterexample
to simplicity would not alone refute Barucca's conjecture.

Other ongoing proposals, not accepted results
--------------------------------------------
The dynamic and limits teams are constructing weighted bounded-degree
examples where one unit edge doubling has vanishing leverage but changes
both unique Fiedler signs and unique exact optimal bisections by a linear
number of vertices. Actual full proofs and independent audits are pending.
These would prune a false general stability target; their historical
novelty is a separate issue.

The frontier team is studying the normalized fractional energy
H_alpha=D^(1/2)(D^(-1/2)LD^(-1/2))^alpha D^(1/2)=D#_alpha L.
Its common-kernel monotonicity and determinant identity follow classical
operator means and matrix-tree formulas. With D as generalized mass its
eigenvectors still correspond to those of the normalized Laplacian.
An actual partition-quality or new computation theorem is needed before
this operator observation can count as a substantive advance.

choose a focused positive theorem against the latest primary literature,
