1. THE ONE-COLOR SECOND MOMENT IS ENOUGH FOR THE STATED QUADRATIC CASE
==================================================================
Normalize ||A||=||B||=1 and put A_0=A Pi, B_0=Pi B. Orthogonal
projection cannot increase either operator norm or Frobenius norm.
Thus ||A_0||,||B_0||<=1 and ||A_0||_F^2,||B_0||_F^2<=r for the
supplied upper bound r>=max(1,sr(A),sr(B)).

The exact covariance map in the source gives

  E[XX^T]=a(A_0B_0)(A_0B_0)^T
        +b||B_0||_F^2 A_0A_0^T
        -kappa A_0 diag(B_0B_0^T) A_0^T,
  X=A(W-I)B.

The final term is negative semidefinite because kappa>=0. Taking traces
and discarding it yields

  E||X||_F^2
    <=a||A_0B_0||_F^2+b||A_0||_F^2||B_0||_F^2
    <=a r+b r^2.

The source coefficient algebra is correct:

  b=(m-s)(m-1)/[(s-1)m(m-3)]<=3/(s-1),
  a=b[1+2/((m-1)(m-2))]<=(4/3)b<=4/(s-1),

for all m>=4 and 2<=s<=m. Therefore

  E||X||_F^2 <=(4r+3r^2)/(s-1)
             <=3(r^2+2r)/(s-1).

Taking s=ceil(1+3(r^2+2r)/(eps^2 delta)) makes the final expression
at most eps^2 delta. Markov's inequality for ||X||_F^2 and
||X||<=||X||_F give exactly the advertised failure bound delta at
operator error eps||A||||B|| after restoring the original scales.
This proof requires only one random partition and no logarithmic
dimension or confidence parameter.

The actual arithmetic conclusion is also correct. For fixed r,eps,delta,
the chosen s is a fixed constant as N grows. If s<m, pad to
M=s ceil(m/s), so m<=M<m+s<2m and norms/stable ranks are unchanged.
Bucket aggregation, centering, and all factor entries cost
O(M(n+p)+s(n+p)). The explicit product costs O(nps). With n=p=N and
m<=N, these costs are O(N^2), including every output entry.

If s>=m, the exact-identity fallback multiplies the supplied factors
directly. This costs O(nmp)<=O(N^2 s)=O(N^2) in the same fixed-parameter
regime. It is always accurate and its factor width m is at most s.
Zero factors are returned exactly. The m>=4 concentration setup causes
no missing asymptotic case: for m<4, direct multiplication already has
O(N^2) cost and can be used as an additional finite-size fallback.

The promise is bounded stable rank, not bounded algebraic rank. Full-rank
factors with decaying singular values may satisfy it. No error relative
to ||AB|| under cancellation or relative low-rank residual is implied.

2. THE LOG-CUBED CONCENTRATION PROOF WAS ALSO CHECKED IN FULL
===========================================================
The reviewer independently checked the complete main proof rather than
assuming the one-color corollary justified it:

* The exact overlap covariance values v,u,w lead to the stated map
  E[TGT]=aG+b tr(G)Pi-kappa Pi diag(G)Pi. The map's negative diagonal
  correction is only discarded when G>=0. The resulting operator
  variance bound V=3(r+2)/(s-1) follows for both rectangular sides.

* For the combinatorial dilation array a_j u_i^T, every entry has norm
  <=1, the total array sum is zero, and the second-moment proxy is
  diag((||U||_F^2/s)Pi_s,(1-1/s)U^TU). It is <=I when s>=r.
  The uniform permutation creates the actual balanced bucket sum.
  No independent permutation-entry assumption enters this step.

* Mackey et al. Corollary 10.3 gives the quoted denominator
  12 sigma^2+4sqrt(2)Rt. At t=16z and z>=1, that denominator is
  <=128z, yielding exp(-2z). Rectangular dilation has largest
  eigenvalue equal to the rectangular norm, so no missing lower-tail
  union factor occurs. Two endpoint bucket bounds give
  Pr(||X||>513z^2)<=(2s+n+p)exp(-2z).

* The tail integral, retained tail atom, and h^2 exp(-h)<=1 give the
  stated trimming bias. Trimming is purely an analysis device. For
  dilations, E(Y-EY)^2=EY^2-(EY)^2<=EY^2<=EX^2, so centering/trimming
  does not inflate the variance proxy. Matrix Bernstein with norm bound
  2R gives precisely denominator 12V+8R eps after rescaling by d.

* The sufficient degree inequality (17), the union probabilities, and
  explicit C_0=2^20 choice are conservative and valid. The construction
  counts d bucket passes, centering, shared constant channel, every
  factor entry, and explicit output expansion. The complete Gram
  factor is sparse plus rank one, not literally column-sparse.
