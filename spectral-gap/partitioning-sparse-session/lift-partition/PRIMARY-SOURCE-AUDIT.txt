Research note: flat-outlier-obstruction.tex
This is an exact elementary obstruction, not a historically certified novel
result and not a solution of the requested scale of research breakthrough.
Its flat-band mechanism has strong direct primary antecedents.

1. Tatsuro Kawamoto, Localized eigenvectors of the non-backtracking matrix.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07543v3
   Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/1505.07543v3
   v3 February 8, 2016; J. Stat. Mech. 023404 (2016).
   Inspected Sections 4--6, especially equations (5)--(14), in HTML and
   downloaded PDF/extracted text (retained under sources/). These explicitly
   construct opposite-sign eigenvectors on doubled motifs and discuss
   nonbacktracking partitioning failures, including regular motifs.
   Therefore our motif cancellation is not a new discovery. Our first
   connector-gadget idea was already directly covered by this mechanism.

2. Mostafa Sabri and Pierre Youssef, Flat bands of periodic graphs.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06465v2
   Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2304.06465v2
   v2 April 27, 2023.
   Inspected main-results introduction, Theorem 3.1 and its full proof,
   Proposition 3.3 and Remark 3.4. The Cartesian-product generator in
   Theorem 3.1 is the direct algebraic antecedent of the tensor construction
   in our note. Replacing the infinite chain-type host by an expander and
   applying Hadamard mixing does not by itself establish significant novelty.
   The exact full-eigenspace incoherence and Bayes ambiguity statements are
   proved in our note, but their historical firstness was not established.

3. Jiaoyang Huang and Ruizhe Xu, Local Law and Eigenvector Delocalization
   for Random Lifts.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37463v1
   https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37463v1
   Submitted October 27, 2026, 70 pages. Verified directly at arxiv.org,
   not just at a mirror or search snippet.
   Inspected abstract, main-result introduction, Definition 1.1,
   Theorems 1.2, 1.3 and 4.2, and adjacent scope explanations.
   Exact scope: fixed finite connected simple base, min degree at least3;
   main stated high-polynomial-probability laws sample from geometric class
   Omega-bar in Definition 1.1 (all logarithmic neighborhoods have at most
   one cycle and few vertices have a cyclic neighborhood). Energies exclude
   singular universal-cover energies. This is a near-microscopic local law
   and complete delocalization result for that regular regime. The abstract
   states the corresponding random-lift consequence. No full proof audit
   of this 70-page paper was performed here, and no growing-base-uniform
   constants may be inferred from its fixed-base statement.
   This very recent work must be included in a current priority sweep.

4. Ludovic Stephan and Yizhe Zhu, Community detection with the Bethe-Hessian.

The growing-base random-lift theorem's rational-trace proof already implies
an expected outlier-count bound, not just a norm-event probability. Treat
counting as a direct consequence, not as a new mechanism. The localized
diamond event does not automatically prove a full critical outlier process
or localization of every outlier. Such a theorem would be substantial but
is not established here.

Sharp limitation of the current obstruction
-------------------------------------------
For the fixed-motif deterministic family the outlier rank is proportional
to graph size. The independent-lift corollary allows the rank to grow
arbitrarily slowly while its fraction tends to zero. It rules out certificates
based only on above-cover eigenvalues, displayed-vector delocalization,
rank-aware incoherence, fixed maximum degree, and global expansion. It does
not refute fixed-rank low-rank recovery, independent-edge or uniform regular
SBM results, independent random-lift local laws, or certificates which also
inspect outlier rank or local blocks in the full spectral projector.
The lifted counterexample has an exactly ambiguous growing old eigenspace;
the lift theorem controls the new spectrum and correctly leaves that old
space untouched. There is no contradiction with the supplied lift result.

Remaining substantive research target
--------------------------------------
A basis-invariant informative/defect projector decomposition for independently
lifted or semirandom sparse block models, robust to mixed near-degenerate
defect eigenspaces. Exact model, edit budget, rank assumptions, and notion of
label recovery are indispensable. No theorem of this strength is claimed
here. Parent session is separately examining local-kernel certificates.
