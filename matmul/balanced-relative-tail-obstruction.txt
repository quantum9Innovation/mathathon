AN EXPLICIT RELATIVE-TAIL OBSTRUCTION FOR DIRECT BALANCED AMM
===========================================================

This note concerns exactly the estimator C=A Wbar B in
balanced-permutation-amm.txt and balanced-graph-multiplication.tex.
It identifies an algebraic obstruction that stronger scalar permutation
moments cannot remove. It is not a lower bound for all AMM algorithms,
adaptive sketches, preconditioned methods, or low-rank approximation.
The zero-tail obstruction for generic oblivious sketches is classical
in spirit; historical priority for the explicit construction is not
asserted.

1. FIXED SKETCH PARAMETERS AND THE CLAIM
======================================
Fix integers m=sq, s>=2, q>=2, d>=1, and assume

  ell:=1+d(s-1)<m.                                      (1)

Let H_1,...,H_d be arbitrary balanced partition incidence matrices,
each having s buckets of size q. The partitions may be drawn randomly,
independently or dependently, or selected by any distribution that
does not change the estimator below. Define

  p=(q-1)/(m-1),
  W_c=(H_c^T H_c-p11^T)/(1-p),
  Wbar=d^-1 sum_c W_c, Tbar=Wbar-I.

There is ONE explicitly constructed deterministic unit vector x with
x^T1=0, depending only on m,s,q,d, such that for EVERY possible d-tuple
of these partitions,

  |x^T Tbar x|>=mu>0.                                  (2)

An explicit positive mu appears in (9) below. In particular x is
chosen before the partitions are drawn and does not depend on them.

For every N>=m, theta>0, and desired stable-rank bound R>1, there are
fixed full-column-rank A in R^(N by m) and full-row-rank B in
R^(m by N) such that

  ||A||=||B||=1, sr(A),sr(B)<=R,
  sigma_1(AB)=1, sigma_2(AB)>0,

but the direct balanced estimator ALWAYS violates

  ||A Wbar B-AB|| <=(1+theta)sigma_2(AB).                (3)

Thus the probability of success in (3) is ZERO under every distribution
on the stated sketches. The product nevertheless has an explicit
rank-one approximation of error exactly sigma_2(AB). The obstruction
therefore concerns the direct estimator rather than the attainable
rank of an answer.

2. WHY EVERY COMPRESSED REALIZATION IS A NONZERO QUADRATIC FORM
=============================================================
The exact identities in balanced-permutation-amm.txt give

  W_c=J+lambda P_c,
  J=11^T/m, lambda=(m-1)/(s-1),

where P_c is an orthogonal rank-(s-1) projection supported on 1-perp.
Consequently

  rank Wbar<=1+d(s-1)=ell<m.

Therefore Tbar!=0 for EVERY realization. Moreover

  Tbar=Tbar^T, Tbar_ii=0, Tbar1=0.                       (4)

For i!=j let K_ij in {0,...,d} count colors in which i,j collide.
The exact entry formula is

  Tbar_ij=[(m-1)K_ij-d(q-1)]/[d(m-q)].                  (5)

Write n_ij=(m-1)K_ij-d(q-1). These are integers, not all zero, and

  |n_ij|<=d(m-1)=:C_0.                                 (6)

The entry identity follows by averaging H_c^T H_c and substituting
1-p=(m-q)/(m-1). The bound (6) is deliberately loose; it is sufficient
for a uniform construction independent of the partitions.

3. ONE EXPLICIT VECTOR SEPARATES ALL REALIZATIONS
================================================
Let X=C_0+2 be an integer, and number coordinates i=0,...,m-1. Define

  z_i=X^(2^i), z_0bar=m^-1 sum_i z_i,
  zc=z-z_0bar*1, x=zc/||zc||.                            (7)

The entries of z are distinct, so zc!=0 and x is a well-defined
zero-sum unit vector. Since Tbar1=0,

  x^T Tbar x = z^T Tbar z/||zc||^2
   =2/[d(m-q)||zc||^2] sum_(i<j) n_ij X^(2^i+2^j).     (8)

All integers 2^i+2^j for i<j are different: their binary expansions
have exactly the two indicated nonzero positions. Thus the polynomial
R(t)=sum_(i<j)n_ij t^(2^i+2^j) is not identically zero when Tbar!=0.
Every coefficient has absolute value at most C_0. Let D be its largest
nonzero exponent. Its leading coefficient has absolute value at least
one, while

  sum_(j=0)^(D-1) C_0 X^j
    =C_0(X^D-1)/(X-1)<X^D,

because X-1=C_0+1. Its leading term therefore dominates ALL lower
terms, including any missing exponents. Hence R(X)!=0. Since R(X)
is an integer, |R(X)|>=1. Equation (8) proves the uniform bound

  |x^T Tbar x|>=mu,
  mu:=2/[d(m-q)||zc||^2]>0.                             (9)

The scale mu can be extremely small, and no practical separation or
bit-complexity claim is made. Its positivity is sufficient for the
relative-tail obstruction. Both x and mu are fully specified before
any partition choice. The large integer powers only establish the
existence of a fixed adversarial input; they are not a proposed AMM
algorithm or charged preprocessing step of the balanced method.

A nonconstructive version is shorter: each Tbar is a nonzero symmetric
quadratic form on 1-perp; finitely many nonzero polynomial zero sets
cannot cover that vector space. The integer construction above also
supplies one common nonzero lower bound for all of them.

4. RANK-ONE AND FULL-RANK FIXED INPUTS
====================================
The immediate zero-tail example is A=u x^T, B=x v^T for any fixed unit
u,v in R^N. Then AB=u v^T has sigma_2=0, whereas

  A Wbar B-AB=(x^T Tbar x)u v^T

has norm at least mu. Thus every sketch fails (3) already on these
rank-one factors. The following construction removes both rank
deficiency and the zero-tail endpoint from the example.

Let R_N be the N-by-m coordinate inclusion [I_m;0]. For a scalar
0<tau<1 define

  D_tau=tau I+(1-tau)xx^T,
  A=R_N D_tau, B=D_tau R_N^T.

Both factors have full latent rank m. Their operator norms are one,
and their stable ranks are

  sr(A)=sr(B)=1+(m-1)tau^2.                              (10)

Since D_tau x=x and D_tau acts by tau on x-perp, the product is

  AB=R_N[xx^T+tau^2(I-xx^T)]R_N^T.

It has sigma_1=1 and sigma_2=tau^2, with the latter repeated m-1
times. The rank-one approximation R_N xx^T R_N^T has error tau^2.
For the unit vector u=R_N x, the direct sketch error satisfies

  |u^T(A Wbar B-AB)u|
   =|x^T D_tau Tbar D_tau x|
   =|x^T Tbar x|>=mu.                                  (11)

Therefore its operator norm is at least mu for every realization.
For example, choose tau by

  tau^2=min{1/4, mu/[2(1+theta)], (R-1)/[2(m-1)]}.      (12)

Then (10) is at most R, while

  (1+theta)sigma_2(AB)=(1+theta)tau^2<=mu/2<mu.

Equations (11),(12) prove the full-rank statement (3) with failure
probability one. The input is the same for all sketches with the fixed
parameters. The stable ranks can be made arbitrarily close to one
without changing the conclusion, by choosing R arbitrarily close to
one or decreasing tau further.

5. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ORIGINAL MATRIX-PRODUCT TARGET
====================================================
The relative target in the matrix-product research asks for error
(1+theta)sigma_(r+1)(AB) for every fixed factor pair, including thin
full-rank inputs and arbitrary cancellation/spectral tails. Its rank-one
case r=1 already includes the fixed inputs in Section 4. Padding by
R_N allows any dimensions N>=m, including the original thin-factor
regime N=m^(9/8+o(1)). The direct balanced estimator with ell<m cannot
satisfy that target uniformly, regardless of how sharp its moment or
concentration analysis becomes.

In particular SG-11, SG-14 and SG-16 in spectral-gap/CLAIM_LEDGER.md
bound the EXACT mixed permutation monomials in its trace expansion.
Those valid estimates can support absolute/stable-rank error theorems
or restricted input regimes. They cannot establish the false uniform
relative-tail guarantee (3) for this unmodified estimator. The gap
between epsilon||A||||B|| and (1+theta)sigma_2(AB) is a real algorithmic
issue rather than an uncompleted manipulation of the same moments.

The scope is precise. The proof does NOT prohibit adaptive subspace
recovery, sketch-based preconditioners, residual correction, exact
fallbacks, or algorithms returning an object other than A Wbar B.
It does not prohibit processing the rank-one structure in the input
to return its exact product. It also does not prove that every width
ell<m sketch misses every input. The bad input was constructed for
the fixed finite balanced-sketch family, independently of its draw.

If the method takes the exact identity fallback whenever its parameter
choice would give ell>=m, (1) no longer applies in that branch. The
theorem specifically explains why the compressed direct branch alone
cannot close the original relative-spectral target.

6. STATUS AND CHECKABLE INGREDIENTS
==================================
This is a general exact proof, rather than an inference from numerical
enumeration. Its dependencies are the already checked rank/row-sum
identities of the balanced sketch, rational entry formula (5), uniqueness
of binary-sum exponents, leading-term dominance, and the explicit
eigenvalues of tau I+(1-tau)xx^T. The lower separation mu in (9) is
conservative and is used only to choose a strictly positive tail.

The basic zero-tail limitation is standard for direct oblivious
compression. The added value here is an explicit single vector,
uniform across ALL balanced partition draws at the chosen parameters,
with a finite common separation and full-rank bounded-stable-rank
inputs having strictly positive residual tail. No unrestricted sketch
lower bound or first-discovery claim follows.
