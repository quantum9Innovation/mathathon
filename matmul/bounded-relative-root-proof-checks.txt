Mathematical content and qualifications retained
------------------------------------------------
1. Fixed R>1 endpoint-to-update promise, fixed alpha in(0,1), fixed r:
   constructive width [r/(4*pi^2*alpha)+o(1)]log^2(1/eps), uniformly
   over finite dimensions, conventional (Nmr+m^3) times logarithms.
   High shifts t>=2Ra share W=[Q0,(A/T)Q0,...] of degree O(L).
   Low prefix density gives coefficient r/(4*pi^2*alpha). All large
   shifts have a fully charged polynomial implementation; no free
   best rational pole oracle is invoked. Sign preservation is proved.
   R=1 has a separate rank-saturation exception and is not claimed sharp.

2. Relative principal-root correction, singular PSD endpoints and
   arbitrary symmetric low-rank update:
   rho=4r*Lambda/a, d0=a/(2sqrt(r*Lambda))<=||Delta||.
   Explicit dyadic ADI yields kr two-factor width, k=O(L[L+logrho]).
   Low-low estimate ||L0*Delta*L1||<=2lambda avoids falsely separating
   spectra meeting at zero. Root actions are not supplied: positive
   Stieltjes quadrature and actual common A/B shifted inverses execute
   them. The inexact adaptive-product estimate is included in full.
   Symmetric output uses at most2kr signed columns; PSD output and
   optimal joint relative-eps/rho rank are NOT claimed.

3. Literal reviewed details added in the typeset proof:
   k_high>=C[L+log(L+2)+1]=O(L); ordinary comparisons suffice.
   nu_A<=nu_B/[100(1+a/t)^4] gives common B inverse precision.
   (1+eta)(1+2eta)^k<2 bounds computed factors by2/tau, supporting36.
   eta=eps^2/[10^6*rho*(k+1)^2] supplies the explicit error margin.
   Work overbound Nmr*H^5+m^3*H^4+H^5, H=O(L+logrho+logr+1),
   plus only dimension/confidence logs in the OSE. Loop caps apply
   on bad events; failed small-core operations return zero.

4. The deterministic no-m^3 backend assumes3/4D<=X^TX<=5/4D,
   D the actual diagonal column Gram, computed in O(Nm). Its work is
   O(Nmr*H^5) for the relative algorithm. The promise allows arbitrary
   column magnitudes but is not produced for arbitrary raw X. Dense
   expansion is separately charged throughout. No general N^2 result.

5. Theorem1.1 uses the existing sharp bounded-spectrum LOWER theorem;
   this manuscript supplies its actual sharp-width algorithmic upper.
   Classical ADI/displacement, Stieltjes, Krylov and preconditioner
   antecedents are explicitly credited. No certified novelty, global
   state-of-the-art, external/formal review, finite-word stability or
   general polynomial-root/exponential oracle is asserted.
