1. Model and genie
------------------
The source fixes b>r>=3, a connected simple base K_{r,b}, and every
fiber size N. Revealing the r left fibers can only help recovery. Right
vertices still have distinct labels and their ordered b-way balanced
partition is sampled uniformly. Conditional on that partition, all rb
cross matchings are independent uniform perfect matchings, exactly as
in the standard random-lift model. The graph is automatically simple.
Observable degrees are b on the left and r on the right; thus the
obstruction persists with the two degree classes and substantially
more information revealed.

The inference assertion is stated for the random hidden-partition prior,
or a relabelling-invariant interpretation. This avoids the invalid claim
that an arbitrary estimator cannot memorize one publicly fixed partition.
The structural multiplicity claim is separate and does hold for every
fixed planted partition by invariance.

2. Exact marked sample-space count
---------------------------------
There are (bN)!/(N!)^b ordered right partitions. Each has (N!)^(rb)
matching tuples. The tuple is uniquely determined by graph plus that
partition: restrict the observed graph to each L_i,R_j. Hence no hidden
factor for multiple factorizations is missing, and the uniform marked
sample-space size is exactly
  T_N=(bN)!(N!)^((r−1)b).

For each known left fiber, a possible graph is exactly a balanced map
from the bN right vertices onto its N left vertices, all preimages of
size b. This has (bN)!/(b!)^N possibilities. Choosing the r maps gives
distinct simple graphs because their images lie in disjoint left fibers.
Thus
  U_N=[(bN)!/(b!)^N]^r
is exact for the larger observation class, and an upper bound for the
actual support. It need not be the actual support; the proof does not
assume every such observation has a compatible fiber partition.

3. Posterior, label quotient and many partitions
-----------------------------------------------
Every compatible ordered partition gives exactly one uniform marked
pair. Consequently Z(G) counts equally likely posterior possibilities
and P(G)=Z(G)/T_N. The permutation action on b nonempty right classes
is free, so W(G)=Z(G)/b! is an integer and its unordered posterior is
uniform. Optimal conditional success is 1/W; randomization cannot help.
The exact average identity
  E[1/W]=b!*|support(G)|/T_N
and the support upper bound give the source's finite s_N without an
extra or missing b! factor.

Applying Markov to 1/W, not to W, gives P(W<=t)<=t*s_N. This proves
exponentially many partitions with high probability directly; no
first-moment-to-existence fallacy, second moment, or concentration of W
is needed. Relabelling the right vertex set transports every fixed
planted-partition law to every other while leaving W invariant. Thus
the exact W distribution, and its high-probability multiplicity bound,
hold separately under each fixed planted partition as stated.

Each compatible unordered right partition adjoined to the revealed left
fibers is a complete equitable partition with the SAME quotient K_{r,b},
the SAME r+b classes, and the SAME size N per class. At equal nonempty
class count, coarsening forces identity. Therefore this is actual fine
fiber nonrigidity and failure of a unique finest partition among <=r+b
classes, not only alternate coarse quotients or a failure of an entropy
certificate. It also shows Bayesian blind nonrecoverability, even with
the base and left fibers known.

4. Constants and thresholds
---------------------------
Stirling gives exactly
  s_N=b!*b^((r−1)/2)*(2*pi*N)^(-theta)*exp(-gamma*N)
                                      *(1+O_{r,b}(1/N)),
  theta=(r−1)(b−1)/2,
  gamma=r*log(b!)−(r−1)*b*log b.
Checked both the b factor and the full 2*pi*N prefactor. There is no
unaccounted power of N.

For r=3,b=12,
  exp(gamma)=7133328125/5159780352>1,
  gamma=0.3238838920736...,
  theta=11.
The positivity is an integer comparison, independent of rounding.
The stated finite checks for 3<=b<=11 hold. The claim is explicitly
limited to the first positive example in this counting family, not a
sharp recovery threshold or smallest possible counterexample. The
large-b expansion at fixed r,
  gamma=b*log b−r*b+(r/2)*log(2*pi*b)+O_r(1/b),
is correct and yields some positive examples for every fixed r.

5. Connectedness
----------------
A spanning-tree gauge change makes tree permutations identity. Given
the original tree-edge permutations, the beta=|E|−|V|+1=(r−1)(b−1)
transformed non-tree permutations are independent uniform permutations;
their conditional law is independent of the tree, so this is true
unconditionally as well. Components are precisely the orbits of the
generated sheet action.

A disconnected action has an invariant nonempty set of size <=N/2.
For a fixed s-set, one uniform permutation preserves it with probability
1/binom(N,s), so beta permutations do so with probability binom(N,s)^(-beta).
Union over all s-sets gives the displayed sum. The s=1 term is N^(1−beta).
For s>=2 there are at most N/2 terms bounded by binom(N,2)^(1−beta),
whose total is O(N^(3−2beta)), dominated by O(N^(1−beta)) since beta>=2
(indeed beta>=6 in the stated b>r>=3 scope). Thus connectedness has
probability 1−o(1). Conditioning either small-W or estimator success
on it costs only 1/P(connected)=1+o(1), retaining the exponential bounds.
