# Terminology for the proposed lattice extreme law

November 15, 2026, America/Los_Angeles.

This is a bounded terminology addendum to [PRIORITY-v1.md](PRIORITY-v1.md). It is not acceptance of a limiting-law proof.

The established term max-semistable describes distributions satisfying an extreme-value scaling identity at a fixed multiplicative scale. Dias and Temido give the definition and the family exp[-x^(-alpha) v(log x)] with positive bounded periodic v, explicitly including discontinuous distribution functions. Inspection depth: the publisher's PDF, introduction and page 898, definition and displayed families. [Random fields and random sampling, 2019](https://www.kybernetika.cz/content/2019/6/897/paper.pdf). The authors attribute the general class to earlier work by Pancheva and Grinevich, whose original papers were not inspected in this bounded check.

For the proposed pure pair maximum, the candidate identity G(qx)=G(x)^(1/q) on x>0 is consistent with this classical class, with exponent one and a periodic factor. Calling that a wholly new extreme-value distribution family would overstate the result.

The actual proposed top variable also has the deterministic collective floor kappa. Its distribution is zero below kappa and agrees with the pair-maximum distribution above it. The simple scaling identity therefore holds only above the floor. A precise descriptive name is a lattice extreme-value law with a collective floor. The full variable should not be called max-semistable solely from an identity on that restricted interval.

A spectral point process containing each pair's positive and negative amplitudes has correlated mirror points. Adding the deterministic collective pair adds further non-Poisson structure. The independent Poisson description belongs to the geometric shell counts or the positive pair process before that deterministic addition, not to the full signed spectrum.
