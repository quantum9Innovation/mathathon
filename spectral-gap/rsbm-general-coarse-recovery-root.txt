Root's coarse entropy route to full fixed-degree recovery
=======================================================
2026-11-15. This records a derived proof strategy and explicit calculations.
It is NOT yet a substitute for the exact type formula / full source audit.
The operator and kernel sessions are independently formalizing these steps.

Model: two size-N blocks, independent internal uniform simple a-regular
graphs, independent uniform simple b-biregular cross graph. Fix integers
a>b>0, lambda=a-b. All admissible N, not just even N. Put d=a+b.
The graph always satisfies A sigma=lambda sigma for planted signs sigma.

1. Polynomial modular moment requires only nonpositive entropy
--------------------------------------------------------------
In the configuration model, a local ordered star on the plus side has
root X, a internal-neighbor labels A_1,...,A_a, b cross-neighbor labels
B_1,...,B_b; the minus star has root Y, neighbors C_1,...,C_a and
D_1,...,D_b. Values lie in any fixed finite prime field. The constraints
are sum A_i+sum B_j=lambda X, and sum C_i+sum D_j=lambda Y.
Stub exchangeability makes internal pair laws symmetric and all slots
of each type identical; the cross pair laws agree after reversal.

The exact factorial type count should have entropy exponent
 Phi=H(star_plus)+H(star_minus)
       -(a/2)[H(X,A_1)+H(Y,C_1)]-b H(X,B_1).

Since the linear constraint determines one internal neighbor from the
root and the others, conditional subadditivity gives
 H(star_plus)<=H(X)+(a-1)H(A_1|X)+b H(B_1|X),
and the corresponding minus inequality. Substitution gives exactly
 Phi<=-(a/2-1)[I(X;A_1)+I(Y;C_1)]-b I(X;B_1)<=0,
for a>=2. There are only polynomially many fixed-field, fixed-degree
types, and uniform factorial estimates lose only O(log N) in each term.
Consequently E|ker_Fp(A-lambda I)|<=N^C(a,b,p) should hold for every
admissible N, with no local CLT or precise saddle constant needed.
Conditioning on simplicity costs a constant at fixed degrees.

Enumerating the fixed-field kernel and checking which vectors encode
balanced sign assignments then costs expected polynomial time. An
explicit polynomial cutoff gives a worst-case polynomial algorithm with
small abstention probability. For p=3, encode signs by 1,-1, enumerate
all field kernel vectors, and verify the candidate over the integers.
For p=2, use the affine parity system L z=b*1 instead and verify cuts;
all sign labels collapse in the homogeneous binary eigenvector equation.

2. Boolean rigidity: macroscopic alternatives
---------------------------------------------
For an actual equitable sign vector, the same star labels lie in {1,-1}
and the star constraints are INTEGER identities. A stronger conditional
variance argument treats ALL a>=2, including the cubic case uniformly.

After symmetrization let J_plus be the conditional total correlation of
the retained a-1 internal and b cross neighbors given X, and similarly
J_minus. Put L=-Phi and c=(a-2)/2. The entropy cancellation is exact:
 L=c[I_internal_plus+I_internal_minus]+b I_cross+J_plus+J_minus.

Define an auxiliary distribution R_plus with the SAME root law and
the SAME conditional internal-neighbor law given that root. Under it,
the retained internal neighbors are conditionally independent, and the
b cross neighbors are independent with the root-independent minus law.
Then, for P_plus the actual truncated star distribution,
 KL(P_plus||R_plus)=J_plus+b I_cross<=L.

Let m(X)=E[A_1|X], v(X)=Var(A_1|X), and
 W=lambda X-sum_(i<a) A_i-sum_j B_j.
Under P_plus this equals the omitted internal sign A_a, so exchangeable
stub slots give E_P(W-m(X))^2=E v(X). Under R_plus its conditional
variance is (a-1)v(X)+b Var(Y), hence
 E_R(W-m(X))^2-E_P(W-m(X))^2
      >=(a-2)E v(X)+b Var(Y)>=b Var(Y).
This DOES NOT require internal root-neighbor independence, and therefore
works also when a=2. The integer square f=(W-m(X))^2 lies in [0,4a^2],
since lambda=a-b>0. Pinsker's inequality now yields
 b Var(Y)<=4a^2 sqrt(L/2),
 L>=b^2 Var(Y)^2/(8a^4).

For an alternative balanced partition flipping a fraction r in each
block, Var(Y)=4r(1-r). Therefore the explicit macroscopic bound is
 Phi<=-(2b^2/a^4) r^2(1-r)^2, ALL a>=2,b>0,a>b.

In particular equality forces both root laws constant; balance makes
them opposite, the planted unordered partition. This closes the cubic
Boolean macroscopic step too, without a separate field-phase analysis.
The exact type enumeration and this quantitative Pinsker argument still
need their final actual-source audit before a consolidated theorem claim.

3. Boolean rigidity: small-support witness count
------------------------------------------------
An alternative balanced partition differs from sigma on k vertices of
each block; choose complement so k<=N/2. Let S be these 2k vertices.
For every vertex outside S, exact preservation of opposite degree b
implies equal numbers of internal and cross neighbors in S. Thus every
outside neighbor of S has at least TWO incident edges from S.

Let W be the outside neighbor set. The subgraph of all edges incident
to S has every S vertex of degree d and every W vertex of degree>=2.
If it has e edges and v=2k+|W| vertices, then
                    e-v >=(d-2)k.
Also |W|<=dk. Count S in at most (eN/k)^(2k) ways, W in at most
(2eN/|W|)^|W| ways, selected stub matchings in at most C_d^k(C_d k)^e
ways, and each prescribed partial configuration in at most (C_d/N)^e
probability for k<=eta_d N. Using (k/w)^w<=exp(O_d(k)) gives
 E[number of such witnesses of size k]
       <=[C_d (k/N)^(d-2)]^k,
after summing the O_d(k^2) choices of e,w. For sufficiently small fixed
eta_d the sum over k=1,...,eta_d N is O_d(N^(-(d-2))). Typed stub
constraints only reduce this upper count. This is the concrete estimate
to verify independently; a similar small-support eigenvector lemma is
already recorded in the kernel session's deterministic-reductions.md.

Combined with the strict macroscopic entropy deficit, this proves
Boolean uniqueness with high probability for all fixed a>b, a>=3.
The already checked cubic case supplies a=2,b=1. Combining uniqueness
with the polynomial modular moment would establish efficient exact
recovery in the FULL constant-degree assortative RSBM without spectral
separation. This is the precise candidate to audit, not a headline claim
before the exact counts, symmetry averaging, and uniform remainders pass.
