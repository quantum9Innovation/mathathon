EXACT OPERATOR VARIANCE AND A SHARP LOW-DEGREE COLLISION BARRIER
==============================================================

This note develops the particular balanced-permutation sketch in
balanced-permutation-amm.txt. It proves a full covariance map, not just
its Frobenius trace, and an exact success probability on a coordinate
contrast subspace. Neither statement is a general sketch lower bound
or a historical-priority claim. In particular they do not close the
higher-moment matrix-pattern problem in that companion note.

1. SETUP
========
Let m=sq, 2<=s<=m, and let a uniformly random balanced partition have
s buckets of q coordinates each. Its incidence matrix is H. Write

  p=(q-1)/(m-1), J=11^T/m, Pi=I-J,
  W=(H^T H-p11^T)/(1-p), T=W-I,
  c=(m-s)/(s-1).

The companion proves W>=0, W1=1, E W=I, T_ii=0, T1=0,
and E T^2=c Pi. For d independent partitions put Tbar=sum_a T_a/d.
Throughout this note an epsilon embedding means squared norms lie
between (1-epsilon)||x||^2 and (1+epsilon)||x||^2.

2. FULL VARIANCE MAP
===================
Assume m>=4 and define

  v=c/m,
  u=-v/(m-2),
  w=2v/[(m-2)(m-3)],
  a=v-2u+2w,
  b=v-2u+w,
  kappa=2(v-4u+3w).

Equivalently,

  a=v(m^2-3m+4)/[(m-2)(m-3)],
  b=v(m-1)/(m-3),
  kappa=2vm(m-1)/[(m-2)(m-3)].

For every real symmetric G satisfying G1=0, the exact identity is

  E[T G T]=aG+b tr(G)Pi-kappa Pi diag(G)Pi.             (V1)

Here diag(G) is the diagonal matrix of G's diagonal entries. The
formula is linear in G, so positivity is not an assumption for (V1).
If G>=0, the subtracted term is PSD. Dropping that term gives the
useful Loewner upper bound

  E[T G T] <= aG+b tr(G)Pi.                            (V2)

Proof of the entry covariances. Because the distribution is invariant
under coordinate permutations, covariances between off-diagonal entries
depend on whether the two unordered pairs agree, overlap at one vertex,
or are disjoint. The identical-pair value is v because E T^2=cPi and
all m(m-1) off-diagonal squares have the same mean. From
0=E[T_ij sum_k T_ik] the overlapping-pair value is -v/(m-2)=u.
From 0=E[T_ij sum_(k,l)T_kl] the disjoint-pair value is
-2[v+2(m-2)u]/[(m-2)(m-3)]=w. Both row sums vanish exactly.

Put g=tr G and ell_i=G_ii. Splitting the entries of TGT by those
three classes gives

  (E TGT)_ii=(v-u)g+(-v+2u)ell_i,

  (E TGT)_ij=(v-2u+2w)G_ij+(u-w)g
               +(-2u+2w)(ell_i+ell_j), i!=j.           (V3)

For example, in an off-diagonal entry the coefficient sum for the
overlapping pairs is g-2ell_i-2ell_j-2G_ij, while that for disjoint
pairs is 2ell_i+2ell_j+2G_ij-g. These follow from G1=0.
Expanding Pi diag(G)Pi entrywise and using the displayed constants
recovers (V3), proving (V1).

3. PROJECTED SUBSPACE AND MATRIX-PRODUCT CONSEQUENCES
===================================================
Take U^T U=I_r, U^T1=0, let P=UU^T, ell_i=P_ii, and set

  tau=2c(m-1)/[m(m-3)].

Then X=U^T T U has exact OPERATOR variance

  E X^2 = (tau/2)[r+1+2/((m-1)(m-2))] I_r
               -(tau m/(m-2)) U^T diag(ell) U.        (V4)

Its trace is exactly the companion's projected-Gram formula (12a).
In particular

  ||E X^2|| <= (tau/2)[r+1+2/((m-1)(m-2))],

with leading size (r+1)/(s-1) when m is large relative to s.
Independent averaging divides (V1) and (V4) by d.

For centered factors A1=0 and 1^T B=0, let C=AB and Z=ATB.
Applying (V1) to BB^T and A^T A gives

  E ZZ^T = a CC^T+b||B||_F^2 AA^T
                  -kappa A diag(BB^T) A^T,

  E Z^T Z = a C^T C+b||A||_F^2 B^T B
                  -kappa B^T diag(A^T A) B.           (V5)

Both negative terms are PSD. Hence the rectangular variance parameter
is bounded by

  V <= a||AB||^2
         +b max{||B||_F^2||A||^2,||A||_F^2||B||^2}.  (V6)

General factors are reduced to this case by replacing A with A Pi and
B with Pi B, since T=Pi T Pi. The stationary contribution A J B is
represented exactly by the sketch and has no error.

This is a spectral/stable-rank variance bound, in place of the earlier
full-vector covariance loss proportional to m/s. However a direct
bounded-summand matrix Bernstein argument still has the deterministic
range ||Z||<=max(1,c)||A||||B||. Its linear-tail term therefore retains
m/s. A better one-partition spectral norm or a higher-moment bound is
needed to turn (V6) into the sought improved compressed AMM theorem.
The covariance map itself does not prove that missing theorem.

4. EXACT SUCCESS LAW ON A CONTRAST SUBSPACE
==========================================
Fix a deterministic set I of t=r+1 coordinates, with 2<=t<=m, and let

  V_I={x: support(x) is contained in I, sum_i x_i=0}.

This is an r-dimensional subspace. Let 0<epsilon<1 and suppose

  p/(1-p) <= epsilon,
  1/d > p+epsilon(1-p).                                (B1)

Then the averaged sketch is an epsilon embedding of V_I if and only
if, in EVERY one of the d partitions, the t coordinates occupy
different buckets. Its exact probability of success is therefore

  Pr(success on V_I)=pi_t^d,
  pi_t=(s)_t q^t/(m)_t,                               (B2)

where pi_t=0 when t>s and (z)_t denotes a falling factorial.

Proof. If no selected coordinates collide in a color, H^T H restricted
to those coordinates is I. For x in V_I the mean-subtraction term
vanishes, so x^T W x=||x||^2/(1-p). This holds for every color and
every x in V_I. The first inequality in (B1) makes this acceptable.

Conversely, suppose i,j in I collide in K>=1 of the d partitions.
The contrast x=e_i-e_j belongs to V_I. Its squared-norm distortion
under the averaged sketch is exactly

  x^T Tbar x/||x||^2 = (p-K/d)/(1-p)
                    <=(p-1/d)/(1-p)<-epsilon.

The second condition in (B1) therefore forces failure. Finally there
are (m)_t equiprobable assignments of distinct permutation slots to
the t fixed coordinates. Choosing t distinct labeled buckets gives
(s)_t choices and choosing one slot from each gives q^t choices.
This proves (B2). The partitions are independent across colors.

The strict inequality in (B1) is intentional: at equality a contrast
can attain the allowed endpoint and is not a failure witness.

5. SHARP BIRTHDAY SCALE AND ROW LOWER BOUND FOR THIS SCHEME
========================================================
For t<=s, the no-collision probability can also be written

  pi_t=product_(j=0)^(t-1)[1-j(q-1)/(m-j)].           (B3)

Using 1-x<=exp(-x) gives

  pi_t^d <= exp[-d(q-1)t(t-1)/(2m)].                 (B4)

Consequently, under (B1), a success requirement at least 1-delta
necessarily implies

  s >= d(1-1/q)t(t-1)/[-2 log(1-delta)].             (B5)

The total shared-constant factor width is 1+d(s-1), so its necessary
lower bound is

  1-d + d^2(1-1/q)t(t-1)/[-2 log(1-delta)].          (B6)

For q>=2, fixed failure probability, and degrees d satisfying (B1),
this is a quadratic-in-r birthday obstruction. Thus the sharp scalar
permutation moment bounds cannot justify a near-linear-in-r row count
for this scheme in its low-degree regime. Raising d to order at least
1/epsilon is necessary before that particular obstruction disappears
when p is small. This is not a lower bound for general embeddings.

There is also an exact asymptotic transition. Along any parameter
sequence with t/s tending to zero and

  lambda_m=d(1-1/q)t(t-1)/(2s) tending to lambda<infinity,

expanding (B3) yields

  d log pi_t=-lambda_m+O(d t^3/s^2),
  pi_t^d tends to exp(-lambda).                       (B7)

The error is o(1), since it is O(lambda_m t/s) whenever q>=2.
At q=1 there are no collisions and pi_t=1 identically. If (B1) also
holds along the sequence, (B7) is the exact limiting embedding-success
law, not merely a second-moment prediction.

6. VERIFICATION AND LIMITS
==========================
scripts/check_balanced_variance_birthday.py checks the full map (V1)
on a spanning family of symmetric zero-row-sum matrices using exact
rational arithmetic and exhaustive small balanced partitions. It also
checks (V4), (V5), and the success law (B2) against exact PSD conditions
on rational contrast bases for small one- and two-color cases.
Retained output is matmul/balanced-variance-birthday-checks.txt.
Finite enumeration corroborates the algebra, not a new concentration
theorem or historical-priority claim.

Permutation entry covariance, birthday obstructions, matrix variance
methods, and sparse-embedding lower bounds all have substantial prior
literature. The contribution here is an explicit exact analysis of the
specific centered unsigned balanced sketch already proposed in this
worktree. It supplies useful variance data and rejects an overly
optimistic low-degree width claim. The weighted higher-moment pattern
bound remains to be proved before claiming the requested AMM advance.
