1. A. A. Laptev, Spectral asymptotic behavior of a class of integral
   operators, Mathematical Notes 16 (1974), 1038-1043.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01149794
   Primary author-hosted PDF:
   https://www.ma.ic.ac.uk/~alaptev/Papers/MatZam74.pdf

   Inspected visually: rendered pp. 1038 and 1039 (PDF pages 1 and 2).
   Kernel: x^beta*y^gamma/(x+y)^a on L^2(0,1).
   Hypotheses: a>0, beta,gamma>-1/2, rho=beta+gamma-a+1>0.
   Theorem (3): lim N(epsilon;T)/log^2(1/epsilon)=1/(2*pi^2*rho).
   The page states positivity when beta=gamma. The strict >epsilon
   convention appears on p. 1038.

   Application for the centered-path lower bound:
   a=1, beta=gamma=1/2, rho=1. Factor 2 in the kernel does not alter
   the leading logarithmic-square coefficient.

   Application for sharpness of the general exponential-tail theorem:
   a=1, numerator exponents beta=gamma=tail-exponent>0, rho=2*beta.

2. NIST DLMF Table 1.14.1, Fourier transforms:
   https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.14.T1
   Fourier convention: (2*pi)^(-1/2) integral f(t) exp(i*x*t) dt.
   Table entry: sinh(a*t)/sinh(pi*t) transforms to
   (2*pi)^(-1/2)*sin(a)/(cosh(x)+cos(a)), for -pi<a<pi.

   Set a=pi*alpha and t=s/(2*pi); switch to the unnormalized transform.
   The kernel is even, so the Fourier sign convention has no effect.
   Result:
     Khat_alpha(xi)=2*pi*sin(pi*alpha)/
                       [cosh(2*pi*xi)+cos(pi*alpha)].
   Hence Khat is positive and <= C_alpha*exp(-2*pi*abs(xi)).
