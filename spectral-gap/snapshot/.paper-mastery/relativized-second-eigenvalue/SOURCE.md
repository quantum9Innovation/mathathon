## Source cautions

This is a research manuscript with visible notation slips. Most importantly, Section 2.7 has dropped trace operators and inconsistent powers, Section 3.3 uses the full Hashimoto radius where the new radius is required, and Theorem 3.3.4 conflicts with the paper's own bouquet calculation. These are documented separately in `09-assumptions-and-weak-points.md`, with direct counterexamples where available. The dossier does not silently present editorial repairs as literal paper statements.
