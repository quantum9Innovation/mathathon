Current positive result
-----------------------
unequal-block-integer-mode.tex / unequal-block-integer-mode.pdf (6pages)
  Two unequal EVEN blocks, fixed internal degrees a1,a2>=3, fixed cross
  degrees b1,b2>=1 with n1*b1=n2*b2. Each internal graph is independent
  uniform simple regular; the simple biregular cross graph may be ANY
  graph fixed before sampling them. Every INTEGER quotient eigenvalue
  is simple with high probability, without an outlier-gap condition.
  The smaller quotient eigenvector recovers the classes exactly by signs,
  with polynomial bit complexity via exact algebraic elimination.
  Imported machinery: Mészáros1806.03736v4 Theorem1.6 and classical
  contiguity. This is an unequal-block extension of the primary session's
  balanced finite-field argument, not a claim to reprove that machinery.

  Equal-total-degree example: Q=[[5,2],[4,3]], sizes2:1, degree7,
  planted lambda1 with eigenvector values1,-2, far inside the tree bulk.
  Every scalar BH gives that direction positive energy for every realr,
  yet exact kernel recovery succeeds. No independent-edge SBM or KS
  threshold claim is made.

  Independent full-proof PASS:
    literature-robust/UNEQUAL_BLOCK_PROOF_AUDIT.txt
  Frozen source SHA256:
    6beafed0a61406f947c94bdcec2342921a8cc0930a1692e55c5c673be0b9dbc2
  Finite diagnostics:
    check_unequal_blocks.py, unequal-block-checks.json (48/48 certified)
    unequal-block-example.json (standalone exact finite-field certificate)
    literature-robust/UNEQUAL_MOMENT_CHECKS.json (exhaustive matching model)
  Diagnostics corroborate formulas; they do not prove the probability limit.

irrational-quotient-simplicity.tex / .pdf (3pages)
  Complementary deterministic fact: on ANY connected two-type equitable
  graph, an irrational quotient pair has equal Galois multiplicities.
  Its larger member is Perron and simple, hence the smaller is simple.
  Thus the random unequal-block result covers all quotient modes on even
  blocks, and the irrational case also covers odd admissible blocks.
  Vertex degrees already identify two classes when their total degrees
  differ, so this elementary observation is not a new hard-case algorithm.
  Independent PASS: literature-robust/SUPPLEMENT_PROOF_AUDIT.txt

algebraic-modular-reduction.txt
  Correct monic-minor reduction at algebraic lambda and elementary
  Schur infinitude of primes admitting a root. Useful for a separate

  The same supplement audit checks this reduction and the48-case code.

Literature status and remaining scope
------------------------------------
PRIORITY_REVIEW.txt gives the positive-result primary comparison.
literature-robust/LITERATURE_REPORT.txt covers current robust/BH literature,
including October2026 low-degree BH recovery and known robust KS results.
detection-versus-recovery.txt explicitly credits already-known efficient
noiseless equitable-model detection; no new detection claim is made.

The inspected primary results do not directly state the exact no-outlier
recovery theorem above. This is NOT a historical-firstness certificate.
The difficult published mixing theorem receives full credit. The exact
theorem, its external dependencies, and novelty are separate obligations.

The positive proof does not cover internal degree2, odd blocks for integer
quotient modes, adaptive cross-graph choices after seeing the internals,
nonzero noisy-edit budgets, or a nearly-linear/finite-precision solver.
The exact kernel equation can be destroyed by one local degree-preserving
switch, so a robust inference theorem requires additional work.

Reproducibility
---------------
Use Python3 with numpy and scipy for the two main diagnostic scripts.
Their fixed seeds are recorded in source/JSON. The diagnostic's graph
samplers use configuration rejection, conditional on simplicity, giving
the stated uniform simple internal/cross laws; the structured unequal
cross controls are deliberately fixed disjoint complete bipartite pieces.
The independent matching-moment checks enumerate finite sample spaces
exactly and keep their rational results, not floating-point ranks.

Each TeX file builds with pdflatex (two passes for references); final logs
were checked for unresolved references and overfull boxes. No implementation
of Mészáros's mixing theorem, sparse runtime benchmark, or asymptotic
simulation result is represented by these checks.
