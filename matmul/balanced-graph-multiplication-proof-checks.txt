The result
----------
For every fixed real A(n by m), B(m by p), with supplied upper bound r
on both stable ranks, random balanced partitions and a deterministic
mean correction produce an unbiased low-rank estimator with
  Pr(||C-AB||>epsilon||A||||B||)<=delta.
The high-confidence construction has bucket count O(r/epsilon),
O(epsilon^-1 log^3((r+n+p)/(delta*epsilon))) independent bucket passes,
and width O(r epsilon^-2 log^3((r+n+p)/(delta*epsilon))).
The note supplies an explicit sufficient parameter inequality and a
conservative closed-form choice. Integer padding removes divisibility.

The one-partition corollary uses
  s=ceil(1+3(r^2+2r)/(epsilon^2*delta)).
Its construction is input-linear and expansion costs O(np*s).
For n=p=N, m<=N and fixed r,epsilon,delta, total ordinary arithmetic is
O(N^2), including every output entry. Full algebraic rank is allowed.
The error scale is the PRODUCT OF FACTOR NORMS. The result does not
provide relative error against ||AB|| or sigma_(k+1)(AB) under arbitrary
cancellation. The stable-rank bound is supplied, not assumed from a
wishful sketch width. The full Gram factor contains deterministic dense
centering; only its incidence part is unsigned sparse. Application is
fast through sparse-plus-rank-one structure.

Critical proof checks
---------------------
  * Exact full covariance map, with its negative semidefinite diagonal
    correction, rather than an unjustified inference from a trace bound.
  * Left and right rectangular variances, scaled by stable ranks.
  * Deterministic combinatorial-sum array, correct dilation variance
    proxy and full permutation law in Mackey et al. Corollary10.3.
  * One-color norm tail independent of the ambient m/s range bound.
  * Integrated truncation bias, with no hidden ambient-dimension loss.
  * Centered truncated covariance contracted in Loewner order.
  * Independent matrix Bernstein constants and explicit degree choice.
  * Shared constant channel, implicit Householder costs, all factor
    entries, naive dense expansion, and O(N^2) fallback arithmetic.

Primary source statements independently checked in the full PDF:
  Mackey--Jordan--Chen--Farrell--Tropp (2014), Corollaries5.2 and10.3.
  https://lmackey.github.io/papers/matstein-aop14.pdf
The cited results are classical inputs. This is internal mathematical
review, not external peer review or an end-to-end formal proof.

Comparison and remaining goal
------------------------------
Cohen--Nelson--Woodruff2016 already give optimal stable-rank row dependence
O(r/epsilon^2). SparseStack2026 reports optimal OSE row count and signed
per-column sparsity. Their primary statements were checked and are
credited in the paper and independent audit. Our result closes the
concentration question for this PARTICULAR regular bucket construction.
It is not a new asymptotic stable-rank AMM bound or historical-priority
certificate. The higher SG-11/14/16 moment constants are not needed for
this proof. Their potential to improve logarithms or dependence remains
open, as do the original arbitrary-factor high-accuracy and general
quadratic multiplication objectives.
