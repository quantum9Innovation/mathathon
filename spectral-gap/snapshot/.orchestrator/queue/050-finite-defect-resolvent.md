## Goal

Make one bounded mathematical advance on the one-defect secular equation. Do not run
another broad survey. Determine whether fixed finite vertex sets have a deterministic
Kesten-McKay resolvent limit away from the regular-tree bulk, and use that result to
resolve or delimit the proposed limiting eigenvalues of a random regular graph with one
added degree-defect vertex.

## Six member roles

1. **Local-cycle concentration:** test self-averaging rigorously, distinguish fixed from
   growing defect sets, and prove finite local-pattern contributions or a counterclaim.
2. **Source applicability:** audit theorem hypotheses and attempt an exact literature
   reduction, including graph ensembles, degree regimes and complex spectral domains.
3. **Nonbacktracking exterior expansion:** derive a geometrically convergent local
   resolvent expansion and determine whether it yields the required probability limit.
4. **Local-law boundary extension:** isolate what the existing Green-function proof needs
   to pass from the bulk domain to real energies beyond the tree edge.
5. **Eigenvalue reduction:** starting from the exact secular identity, derive the root
   limits under an explicit minimal resolvent hypothesis and identify every host-pole
   and uniformity issue.
6. **Adversarial verifier:** seek counterexamples, circular uses of delocalization and
   local convergence, incorrect uniform limits and claims of certified uniform sampling.
