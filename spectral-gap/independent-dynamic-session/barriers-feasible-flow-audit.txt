Audit of the feasible-flow repair proposed by the main agent
Independent dynamic session, 2026-11-15

The following repairs the generic energy-solve issue in Section 3 of
barriers-positive-shift-oracle.txt. It uses more structure than the
restricted oracle lower-bound model and therefore does not conflict
with that theorem.

All statements concern exact-real arithmetic. Let L=B^T W B be a
connected weighted graph Laplacian, d>0, u=sqrt(d)(e_p-e_q),
tau=u^T L^dagger u, and alpha in (0,1). Work on 1-perp.

1. Bounded approximate unshifted voltage
--------------------------------------
Let v=L^dagger u and let q satisfy ||q-v||_L<=eta_0 sqrt(tau), where
eta_0<1. Then

  |u^T(q-v)| <= sqrt(tau)||q-v||_L <= eta_0 tau,

so q_p>q_q and their difference is at most
(1+eta_0)tau/sqrt(d). Clip every coordinate of q to [q_q,q_p], and
subtract its mean to obtain vhat. Clipping fixes both terminal values,
so preserves u^Tq. It is 1-Lipschitz, so decreases q^T L q. The
identity

  ||q-v||_L^2=q^T L q-2u^T q+tau

shows that clipping and centering cannot increase the L-energy error.
Furthermore

  ||vhat||^2 <= n(1+eta_0)^2 tau^2/d.

Thus a standard graph solve plus O(n) clipping/centering yields both
energy accuracy and a gap-independent Euclidean bound.

2. Filtered demand and feasible flows
------------------------------------
For t>0, let y=(L+tI)^{-1}vhat and x=L y=P_t vhat, where
P_t=L(L+tI)^{-1}. Add a ground vertex joined to each original vertex
by conductance t. Solve the electrical-flow problem with demand vhat
on the original vertices and demand 0 at ground (vhat has mean zero).
Write a feasible flow as (f,g), with original-edge flows f and flows
g from original vertices to ground. Feasibility is exactly

  B^T f+g=vhat.

The electrical optimum is (f_*,g_*)=(W B y,t y). Define

  xhat=B^T f=vhat-g,
  E=||f-f_*||_{W^{-1}}^2+||g-g_*||^2/t.

The two expressions for xhat give

  e=xhat-x=B^T(f-f_*)=-(g-g_*).

Thomson's principle on the ORIGINAL graph and the ground contribution
therefore imply, simultaneously,

  ||e||_{L^dagger}^2 <= E,       ||e||^2 <= t E.

Since e has mean zero, spectral Holder interpolation yields

  ||L^{-alpha/2}e||^2
      <= (||e||_{L^dagger}^2)^alpha (||e||^2)^{1-alpha}
      <= t^{1-alpha} E.

This is exactly the error structure absent from a generic energy-accurate
potential solver. It uses exact feasibility; a solver returning only
approximate demands would need an additional repair proof.

3. Relative flow-energy accuracy and initial voltage error
---------------------------------------------------------
Every feasible error flow is a circulation on the augmented graph,
and is energy-orthogonal to the optimum. Thus excess primal energy is
exactly E. If the feasible solver returns energy at most (1+xi)OPT,

  E <= xi OPT <= xi ||vhat||^2/t,

where the last inequality is (L+tI)^{-1}<=I/t. Consequently

  ||L^{-alpha/2}(xhat-P_t vhat)||^2
       <= xi t^{-alpha} ||vhat||^2.

For the initial error z=vhat-v,

  ||L^{-alpha/2}P_t z||^2
     = sum_i lambda_i^{2-alpha}/(lambda_i+t)^2 z_i^2
     <= K_alpha t^{-1-alpha} ||z||_L^2,

  K_alpha=max_{r>0} r^{1-alpha}/(1+r)^2
    =(1-alpha)^{1-alpha}(1+alpha)^{1+alpha}/4 <= 1.

Because P_t v=(L+tI)^{-1}u, triangle inequality gives the usable
raw-factor error bound

  ||L^{-alpha/2}[xhat-(L+tI)^{-1}u]||
   <= sqrt(K_alpha tau) eta_0 t^{-(1+alpha)/2}
        +sqrt(xi) ||vhat|| t^{-alpha/2}.

Choosing tolerances polynomial in the gap-free node scales from
barriers-voltage-tail-observation.txt gives only logarithms in n,tau,
1/eps in the electrical-solver cost, with no graph spectral ratio.
The main construction still has to allocate weighted factor errors and
scalar denominator errors and charge any final rank compression.

4. Precisely verified primary-source solver contract
----------------------------------------------------
Kelner, Orecchia, Sidford, Allen Zhu, A Simple, Combinatorial Algorithm
for Solving SDD Systems in Nearly-Linear Time, STOC 2013:

  https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6628
  https://math.mit.edu/~kelner/publications/ASimpleCombinatorialAlgorithmforSolvingSDDSystemsinNearly-LinearTime.pdf

Definition 2.4 requires exact demand feasibility and relative primal
energy. Theorem 3.2 proves the needed excess-energy bound in expectation.
Theorem 4.1 preserves feasibility at each iteration and geometrically
decreases expected excess. Theorem 2.11 supplies a weighted low-stretch
tree with total stretch O(m log n loglog n), without a conductance-ratio
parameter.

To amplify success, run independent copies with expected excess at
most xi OPT/4. Markov gives at least 3/4 probability of excess at most
xi OPT. Choose the copy of lowest computed primal energy; all copies
have exactly the same demand, so lowest primal energy also has lowest
unknown excess. O(log(1/delta)) copies give failure at most delta while
preserving feasibility. No oracle for OPT is needed for this selection.

For the augmented graph there are n+1 vertices and m+n edges. Extraction
of the final xhat through vhat-g costs O(n) once the ground flows are
materialized, or near-linear time including data-structure extraction.
All solver/logarithmic claims here use the exact-real weighted-graph
model; no finite-word condition-independent theorem is asserted.

5. Final Gram compression can also avoid fractional-whitened solves
------------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose the raw construction has produced Z in R^{n x q}, with each
column z_j represented as B^T phi_j by a known original-edge flow.
Retain the computable scalar bounds

  T_0=||Z||_F^2,
  T_1=sum_j phi_j^T W^{-1} phi_j
       >= tr(Z^T L^dagger Z).

Only these bounds need be retained, not the full flow representation.
The desired factor Gram is S=Z^T L^{-alpha} Z. Use

  S=c_alpha integral_0^infinity s^{-alpha} Z^T(L+sI)^{-1}Z ds.

The low and high Gram tails have norm bounded respectively by

  c_alpha T_1 s_min^{1-alpha}/(1-alpha),
  c_alpha T_0 s_max^{-alpha}/alpha.

Thus neither endpoint uses a graph spectral parameter. At logarithmic
scale s=(T_0/T_1)exp(y), the integrand is bounded by a constant times

  K min(exp((1-alpha)y),exp(-alpha y)),
  K=T_0^{1-alpha}T_1^alpha.

This also controls a fixed complex strip. An absolute gamma-accurate
Gram rule therefore uses O_alpha(log^2(2+K/gamma)) nodes. If T_0=0,
Z=0 and no compression is necessary.

For each node, solve (L+sI)y_j=z_j with ordinary relative energy
tolerance eta, obtaining yhat_j. Unlike construction of raw factors,
here only scalar bilinear forms are used. Their errors obey

  |z_i^T(yhat_j-y_j)|
     <= eta sqrt(z_i^T(L+sI)^{-1}z_i)
                    sqrt(z_j^T(L+sI)^{-1}z_j).

Hence the Frobenius (and therefore operator) error of the node Gram
is at most eta tr(Z^T(L+sI)^{-1}Z)
<=eta min(T_1,T_0/s). Symmetrization cannot increase its norm error.
Taking eta of order gamma/K, with an appropriate fixed constant, makes
the accumulated quadrature solve error O(gamma). There is no need to
output a vector accurate after L^{-alpha/2} in this Gram computation.

If the raw relative correction X X^T, X=L^{-alpha/2}Z, has error e_0
to the true C, and the resulting symmetric Gram approximation Shat has
||Shat-X^T X||<=gamma, let P be its spectral projector for eigenvalues
strictly above theta. The explicit output is F=Z P Z^T, formed from
an orthonormal basis of that projector. Then

  ||L^{-alpha/2}(ZZ^T-F)L^{-alpha/2}||
       =||(I-P)S(I-P)|| <= theta+gamma,

and therefore final error to C is at most e_0+theta+gamma.
Weyl's inequality and equality of the nonzero spectra of X^T X and
XX^T also give

  rank(P) <= #{eig(C)>theta-gamma-e_0}.

For fixed delta in (0,1), choose e_0=gamma=delta eps/4 and
theta=(1-delta/2)eps. The final two-sided relative norm error is eps
and the determinant count gives

  rank(P) <= max(0,ceil[b/log(1+(1-delta)eps)]-1),
  b=alpha log(1+tau).

This has an arbitrarily near-sharp fixed leading rank factor as
delta tends to zero. It proves a PSD factor F and a two-sided relative
error, not a Loewner lower approximation F<=B^alpha-A^alpha.

Arithmetic for a q-column rule includes nq^2 Gram bilinear work,
q^3 small symmetric eigendecomposition, and nq rank(P) final factor
formation. If q is polylogarithmic in n,tau,1/eps, these are the allowed
near-linear polylogarithmic factors, with no uncharged dense whitening.
The main theorem still has to bound K by its previously charged
flow-energy and node-scale estimates and allocate all confidence/error
budgets. The identities and compression argument here have been audited;
they should not be replaced by an uncharged oracle for L^{-alpha}.
