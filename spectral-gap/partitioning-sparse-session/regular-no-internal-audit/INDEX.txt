1. Positive theorem: common total degree at least three
-----------------------------------------------------
Read THEOREM_AND_COMPOSITION.txt for the full statement and derivation.
Fix supplied constants K>=1,D>=3. A connected fixed integer degree frame
Q has k<=K nonempty classes and EVERY row sum equal to the SAME d,
3<=d<=D. Its internal degrees may be ZERO or ONE. Sizes obey edge
balance and necessary parity. Sample all nonzero internal regular and
cross biregular factors independently and uniformly among simple graphs.

With high probability along all admissible sizes, EVERY equitable
partition into at most K nonempty classes is a compatible coarsening of
the planted partition. Thus the planted partition is the unique finest
within that bounded class family. Unknown k,Q and the planted partition
are recovered by the stated exact algorithm using only the fixed bounds.

In particular, this recovers the hidden fibers of a random N-lift of
EVERY fixed connected SIMPLE d-regular base, d>=3, whose order is at most
K. Each base edge supplies an independent uniform matching. Simplicity
is automatic, all N are admissible, and no old/new spectral gap is used.
The one-class case also covers ordinary uniform simple d-regular graphs.

The proof averages deletion over ALL physical neighbor slots. After
within-type slot symmetrization the exact normalized entropy identity is
  -Phi=(d-2)/d *sum_(undirected types) w_ij I_ij
                          +(1/d)sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it.
Its real one-hot form gives the explicit gap
  Phi<=-alpha_min (d-2)^3 delta^2/[4d^6(d-1)]
      <=-alpha_min delta^2/(8D^6).
Here delta is the minimum fraction of row changes to an old-block point
map. The exact count exponent is n*Phi. A complete arbitrary-support
count and exact near-point QT=TR argument close all small defects.

The same count bounds the UNCONDITIONAL expected number of binary
intertwiners. With
  F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K),   B=K*2^(KD)+F,
the full algorithm has expected bit work O_(K,D)(n^(B+3)). Its deterministic
n^(B+5) cap interrupts with probability O_(K,D)(n^-2); interruption MUST
return FAILURE/ABSTENTION. Space is O_K(n^2) bits. The exponent is enormous
and this is not a practical implementation or runtime claim.

2. Degree-two cycle boundary: exact optimal success
-------------------------------------------------
Read cycle-boundary/cycle-cover-posterior.tex (clean five-page PDF).
Fix a SUPPLIED known simple cycle C_k,k>=3. Sample its UNCONDITIONED
random N-lift and independently shuffle all kN vertex names uniformly.
Observe only the labeled graph and recover the UNORDERED hidden fibers.
If R is its number of components, the number of compatible unordered
partitions is exactly(2k)^(R-1). The posterior is uniform on them.

Consequently the exact Bayes-optimal success is
  P_(k,N)=2k Gamma(N+1/(2k))/[Gamma(1/(2k)) Gamma(N+1)]
         ~[2k/Gamma(1/(2k))] N^(-1+1/(2k)) ->0.
This is also the randomized minimax success over unknown balanced named
projections. It is an information limitation with no computation bound.

Each component has2k covering maps; quotient by ONE GLOBAL Aut(C_k),
not by independent component automorphisms. A compatible named map and
observed graph have joint probability1/(kN)!, proving uniform posterior.
Repeated component lengths do not introduce an extra factorial quotient
because observed vertex names are retained.

The lift is connected with probability1/N. Conditioning on that event
makes the unordered partition unique FOR THE SUPPLIED known k; it is a
different distribution. Unknown base size is a separate question. An
estimator hardcoded to one fixed parameter is not covered by a pointwise
Bayesian upper bound; the shuffle prior or minimax/invariant formulation
is essential and explicit in the source.

Together these results give a sharp degree-two boundary within the stated
family of blind, unconditioned lifts of fixed connected SIMPLE REGULAR
bases. They do not assert a universal boundary for arbitrary decorated
unicyclic bases, connected conditioning, or different observation models.
