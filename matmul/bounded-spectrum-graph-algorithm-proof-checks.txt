Deliverable and exact model
--------------------------

The inputs are two graph conductance Laplacians with a common positive
diagonal vertex-mass matrix and k positive edge changes, with promise
max(||A||,||B||)<=R||B-A|| for fixed R>1. The theorem returns factors
for the correction B^alpha-A^alpha, at error epsilon||B-A||^alpha with
probability at least 1-delta. Thin construction has expected work
~O_(alpha,R)((M+N)k), and dense expansion is separately charged.

At fixed k, the leading output width is
  k/[4*pi^2*alpha] times log^2(1/epsilon),
with explicit remainder O_(alpha,R)(k L^(15/8)) at
L=log(1/epsilon)+O_(alpha,R)(log(2k)).
The common-mass connected-path lower in the separately audited
bounded-spectrum-fractional-counts.txt matches this coefficient.
The theorem therefore realizes the optimal leading width rather than
only proving existence of best rational approximants.

Exact arithmetic and scalar square roots suffice at alpha=1/2.
At general fixed alpha, scalar powers are supplied/charged explicitly.
The algorithm uses no eigennorm, best-rational-pole, Gaussian integral,
exponential, or error-function oracle. The endpoint bound is supplied
as a promise, and its usable scale T=R||B-A||_F is computed sparsely.
The expected-work guarantee invokes the existing shifted-SDD solver.
No deterministic runtime cap or finite-word stability is asserted.

Proof coverage
--------------
The key global collection identity leaves q ORIGINAL rank-k resolvent
nodes and ONE polynomial difference of degree p-1. Its total width is
k(q+p-1), rather than the incorrect product kpq. The polynomial part
has explicit forward-power and backward-Horner factors and all its
scalar coefficients use binomial recurrence and the actual node values.

The moving-strip calculation is uniform for p equal to the least power
of two with p^8>=L. Its growing-slope envelope has logarithmic amplitude
O(p log(L+1)+p^2)=o(L^(3/4)). The Gaussian localization error budget
absorbs this growth. The explicit variable-slope Riemann error is
O(L*ell+p*ell^2), ell=L^(3/4), so no fixed-p hidden constant is used.
This gives q<=L^2/(4*pi^2*alpha)+O(L^(15/8)).

The constructive scalar window/node procedure remains polynomial in L.
The approximate ORIGINAL node errors are accumulated after global
polynomial compensation, not bounded as independent p-rank remainder
nodes. The required solve precision has logarithm O(L+log(2k)).
Positive updates give chi=tr(E)/||E||_F<=sqrt(k) even with arbitrary
mass ratios. Expected solver work and confidence amplification are
charged, including all 2kq column solves and all factor entries.

Priority and scope
------------------

pole-plus-polynomial approximation, Gaussian localization, and SDD solvers
have established prior literature. The particular theorem here accounts
for their graph implementation, uniform growing subtraction degree and
matching restricted matrix/graph lower bound. Historical priority is not
