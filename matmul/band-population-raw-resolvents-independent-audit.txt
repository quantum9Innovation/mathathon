1. RAW COARSE COORDINATES AND RANK DEFICIENCY
--------------------------------------------

On the stated embedding event, the two PSD Gram inequalities imply
that X and L0 have the same right nullspace and hence the same row
space. Thus X=X L0^dagger L0 and

  X J X^T=(X L0^dagger)(L0 J L0^T)(X L0^dagger)^T.

Since L0 has full row rank, congruence by L0^dagger gives
(1-eta)I<=Z^TZ<=1+eta. These identities hold without full column
rank of X or nonsingularity of the initial K. Only the sketched Gram
is explicitly formed, with the charged ~O(I_X+m^omega) preparation.

For Hermitian K, an orthonormal basis V of range(K) gives
K=V(V^*KV)V^*. Its compression V^*KV is invertible, and replacing
Z by ZV preserves S and the metric inequalities. An ordinary exact
rank-revealing small-core factorization supplies this basis; no true
Gram or generic exact spectral basis is obtained for free.

If the coarse K is zero, S is zero on the embedding event, so returning
-h_j is exact there. Off the event it is a permitted arbitrary answer.
All support operations still have finite dimension-bounded costs.

The later complex unitary coarse coordinate changes preserve S with
adjoints and preserve T=I+D by unitary compression/conjugation. The
shared-series component explicitly allows those coordinates. Their
physical actions use the original raw factor and small coefficient maps.

2. THE INDEFINITE POSITIVE-BAND COMPARISON
----------------------------------------

Let H=T^(1/2)KT^(1/2). The nonzero spectrum of S equals that of H:
Z=U T^(1/2) with U^*U=I, so S=U H U^*, plus zero eigenvalues on
the orthogonal complement. This is used only for analysis.

For every s>0 the metric inequalities give

 K-s/(1-eta) I <= K-sT^-1 <= K-s/(1+eta) I.

H-sI is congruent to K-sT^-1. Monotonicity of positive inertia under
these Loewner comparisons proves that the corresponding ordered
POSITIVE eigenvalues satisfy

 (1-eta)lambda_j^+(K)<=lambda_j^+(H)
                           <=(1+eta)lambda_j^+(K).

Congruence guarantees the same number of positive eigenvalues, so
every positive index is legitimate. K need not be PSD, and neither K
nor T is assumed to commute with the other.

With eta=1/8, each coarse eigenvalue in [t/3,3t] maps by its index to
a distinct physical eigenvalue in [7t/24,27t/8], strictly inside
[t/4,4t]. Thus the coarse buffered-band count is <=the supplied
intrinsic cap kappa. Indexing handles multiplicities, and the strict
outer enlargement handles all closed-interval endpoint cases.
