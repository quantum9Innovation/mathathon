SPECTRAL PARTITIONING UNDER EDGE EDITS: PROVED OBSTRUCTIONS
Research session, 15 November 2026

Outcome
-------
Two precise gap-free stability targets were falsified by complete graph
constructions and proofs. No new positive partitioning algorithm or
approximation theorem is claimed. The fractional relative response
budget does not transfer to hard spectral projectors or unique exact
balanced-cut labels. Its soft-filter counterpart is classical.

All session artifacts are in this directory. Frozen papers were read,
not edited. No persistent goal was created.

1. Unique balanced cuts can flip half the labels under vanishing leverage
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Big-if target: a modulus omega(tau)->0 bounds the fraction of changed
labels of the unique optimal bisection, or the operator distance of the
unique Fiedler projector, under one positive edge edit of leverage tau.

FALSIFIED in theorem-agent.tex / theorem-agent.pdf, Theorem 1.

For n=4r, r>=2, use the weighted Cartesian graph C4 square P_r, with
ordinary unit vertex masses, cardinality balance |S|=n/2, underlying
simple bipartite graph, and maximum combinatorial degree at most four.
The square rails have weights 1 and 1+1/n; internal path edges have
weight 2nr^2. Double one existing unit edge.

Before and after the edit, the Fiedler eigenvalues are simple and their
unit eigenvectors are EXACT balanced sign vectors, respectively
  x = (1,1,-1,-1) on the four equal rails, divided by sqrt(n),
  y = (1,-1,1,-1) on the four equal rails, divided by sqrt(n).
The Fiedler cuts are also the unique exact minimum bisections, up to
complementation, by equality in the Rayleigh variational principle.
Their distance, even after complement alignment, is n/2 vertices.

The edit leverage is tau<13/(4n), while
  ||C_alpha|| <= (1+tau)^alpha-1 <= alpha*tau -> 0
for every fixed 0<alpha<1. The Fiedler projector distance is nevertheless
one in operator norm. Initial optimum weight: n/2. Updated optimum:
n/2+1/2. The old cut after the edit has weight n/2+1.

Important scope: host edges are WEIGHTED. Large internal conductances
are essential to this particular construction. No unweighted-host or
normalized-cut assertion follows. In a fully unweighted graph, one
unit-edge insertion/doubling cannot switch a unique exact bisection to
a different unique exact bisection, because all old cut costs are
integers; a tie can appear. Approximate optimal cuts can remain stable
in this example: the old cut is still 1+O(1/n) optimal.

2. Arbitrarily many hard-projector directions move at EACH fixed leverage
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Big-if target: for every tolerance eta>0, the number of singular values
of a sharp projector difference above eta has a finite bound F(tau,eta)
independent of graph size, perhaps from the fractional logdet budget.

FALSIFIED in projector-no-go.tex / projector-no-go.pdf, Theorem 2.

For EVERY prescribed tau_*>0, however small, and every h, there is a
finite connected positively weighted COMPLETE graph with unit masses,
one positive edge increment of leverage EXACTLY tau_*, and a cutoff in
a COMMON spectral gap of the old/new Laplacians, with equal projector
ranks, having at least h singular values of the projector difference
above 1/600. In the quantitative family this count is Omega(log n).
The reference positive spectrum is contained in [1,1+1/(2n)], so its
algebraic connectivity is bounded away from zero and its condition
number tends to one. The missing separation is at the selected cutoff.

Construction: r=n-1, delta=1/(2n), Pi=I-11^T/n. Choose an isometry U
onto 1-perp satisfying U^T b=sqrt(2/r)1. Set
  H=U diag(j/r) U^T, L=Pi+delta H.
Every off-diagonal entry is <=-1/(2n), proving an actual graph
realization, rather than merely an SPD example. The edited spectrum
reduces exactly to diag(1,...,r)+gamma*11^T. Secular-root estimates and
geometrically separated Cauchy compressions prove the singular count.

Important scope: these hosts have growing degree, and the positive edit
is not required to double the edge. This is a projector theorem, not a
label-recourse lower bound. The Anderson orthogonality mechanism,
rank-one secular equations, and Cauchy overlaps are established prior
art; novelty of this finite graph formulation has not been certified.

3. Even all-scale averaging does not rescue the hard-projector budget
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Big-if target:
  integral_0^infinity ||1_(0,t)(L)-1_(0,t)(L')||_F^2 dt/t
       <= K log(1+tau),
with universal K independent of graph size and spectral gaps.

FALSIFIED in projector-no-go.tex / projector-no-go.pdf, Theorem 1.

Use the same graph realization, but now fix 0<gamma<=1/32 and let
d=gamma*delta/2. Then tau is between gamma*delta/(1+delta) and
gamma*delta, and the ratio of the displayed integral to log(1+tau) is
at least gamma*log(1+floor(r/16))/128, tending to infinity.

This small-gamma family is a separate quantified regime from the
fixed-tau count theorem. The latter alone would not establish a
diverging integrated ratio, since its cutoff windows shrink with n.

4. What the existing results actually certify
--------------------------------------------
The exact fractional logdet budget controls the relative fractional
energy correction and the number of its large eigenvalues. The local/
global determinant split limits that correction in fixed-degree
unweighted hosts. Neither assertion controls eigenvector orientation
across a dense spectral band. L^alpha has exactly the eigenvectors and
ordering of L, so taking a positive fractional power alone cannot
repair spectral partition quality or hard-projector instability.

The existing nearly linear factor algorithm reconstructs the relative
fractional response per edit. It does not maintain a sublinear dynamic
partition data structure, certify a rounding margin, or automatically
return a balanced cut. No such additional result is claimed here.

Classical positive baseline (theorem-agent.tex, Proposition 3): for
F_L(s)=s(L+sI)^-1 and a positive single-edge edit,
  D_s=F_L(s)-F_L'(s) >= 0, rank(D_s)=1,
  integral tr(D_s) ds/s = integral ||D_s|| ds/s = log(1+tau),
  integral ||D_s||_F^2 ds/s <= tau/(1+tau)*log(1+tau).
This is Sherman-Morrison plus determinant differentiation, not a new
matrix inequality or a label-stability theorem.

Another elementary baseline: because L<=L'<=(1+tau)L, a previous
kappa-approximate cardinality-balanced cut is still
kappa*(1+tau)-approximate after the positive edit. Objective retention
is compatible with exact optimizer discontinuity. A rebuild rule based
only on this comparison is a classical lazy-rebuilding corollary.

5. Primary SOTA audit and novelty boundaries
-------------------------------------------
See literature-audit.md for exact assumptions and primary sources.

* Jourdan-Macgregor-Schwartzman, ICML 2026, Fully Dynamic Coreset
  Spectral Clustering:
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/jourdan26a.html
  Published weighted fully dynamic normalized-cut objective guarantee;
  O(log n) edge updates with material query/coreset/optimization costs.
  It does not claim small partition-label recourse or unit-cardinality
  balance. A near-linear response rebuild alone does not beat it.
* Peng-Yoshida, KDD 2020, Average Sensitivity of Spectral Clustering:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04094
  Random edge removal sensitivity with spectral cluster assumptions;
  not arbitrary adversarial edge stability without separation.
* Li, 1998, Relative Perturbation Theory II:
  https://doi.org/10.1137/S0895479896298506
  Relative sin-theta estimates are classical and use relative gaps.
* Bhaskara et al., NeurIPS 2024:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14315
  Semirandom internal-edge modifications; normalization materially
  affects robustness. A normalized-Laplacian update changes masses.
* Kuettler-Otte-Spitzer, 2014:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.4923
  Logarithmic Anderson integral is classical continuum prior art.
* Gebert, 2018:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02796
  Exact rank-one projector determinant has common-gap and spectral-
  shift assumptions; it is not a gap-free Hilbert-Schmidt bound.

No first-ever gap-free clustering, dynamic-clustering breakthrough,
new Davis-Kahan theorem, or new soft-resolvent identity is claimed.

6. Validation and next viable target
-----------------------------------
check_partitioning.py and partitioning-checks.json verify:
  * actual graph signs, row sums, masses and unit-edge normalization;
  * simple Fiedler switch for n=8,12,16,32,64;
  * unique exact bisections by full enumeration for n=8,12,16;
  * fractional PSD response, relative norm and determinant identities;
  * rank-one secular spectra, bulk shifts, Cauchy overlaps and hard
    integrated identities for r=64,128,256,512;
  * exactly fixed tau=0.001 in an independent parameter regime.
Both proof PDFs compile cleanly. The projector proof received a
separate full mathematical audit (projector-independent-audit.md),
including every constant and the prescribed-leverage extension.

A meaningful next positive target must specify an ambiguity allowance
or a certifiable partition/rounding margin, and prove actual cardinality
balance, approximation, query/update cost, or label recourse beyond
existing dynamic results. A generic stability assumption saying that
all near-optimal cuts are close merely restates the desired conclusion.
The unconditional hard-projector/unique-label targets above should be
retired, rather than rescued by relabeling a classical soft bound.
