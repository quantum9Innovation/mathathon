2. The theorem that passes

For integer b >= 1 and eta = 2^(-b), the proof constructs positive rational
poles and residues defining an entropy lower rule and an upper rule equal
to the lower rule plus eta*f_2, valid for every x > 0. The pole count is
m <= log(1/eta)^2/(2*pi^2) + O(log(1/eta)), with natural logarithms.
All coefficient preparation, transcendental approximation, rational
linear algebra and polynomial root isolation have polynomial bit cost in
b. Output rational encoding lengths are polynomial in b. This is a bound
in the requested accuracy bits b, not in the binary encoding length of b.

3. Node selection, acceptance and termination

The two rational-enclosure ceiling candidates cover the exact Ganelius
