2. PRECISE ENLARGED BIPARTITE CLASS
----------------------------------
Let the fixed connected simple d-regular base have sides L,R, each
with m base vertices; equality of side sizes follows from regularity.
Let e=dm be its number of edges. Give left variables V_L=F_p^r_L,
right variables V_R=F_p^r_R, and linear maps T_i from each vertex's
side space into the opposite side's space. The maps MAY vary with
the base vertex; the companions explicitly prove this larger class.

At a lifted vertex of old type i, the sum of its d opposite-side
neighbor labels must equal T_i applied to its own label. This is a
well-defined linear system even when the side dimensions differ.
The original matrix equation AY=YR is the special case r_L=r_R=r
with the same endomorphism R used at every old vertex.

The source's simpler two-map formulation is already closed under
common-subspace restriction, and its proved vertex-dependent version
is also closed. No switch to an unproved class is needed when phase
representatives introduce vertex-dependent affine constants: those
affine fibers are bounded by the HOMOGENEOUS restricted-system moment.

If both dimensions are zero there is exactly one assignment. If only
one dimension is zero the same proof applies with that alphabet's
size one. The finite-space formulas below explicitly include it.

3. EXACT COUNT AND ALL-SLOT ENTROPY FOR UNEQUAL ALPHABETS
-------------------------------------------------------
At a root with alphabet size q_i and opposite alphabet size q'_i,
its label and any d-1 neighbor labels determine the omitted neighbor
with coefficient one. Thus the number of legal stars is exactly
q_i(q'_i)^(d-1), independently of injectivity, surjectivity, or Jordan
structure of T_i, and independently of p dividing d.

For empirical ordered-star counts, the exact matching count is the
product of vertex multinomials N!/product T_i! and edge factors
product m_ij(a,b)!/N!, with m_ji=m_ij^T. The root labels are already
chosen by the multinomials. A configuration here is an actual random
lift with independent edge matchings; there is no simplicity
conditioning or different regular-graph law.

The entropy exponent per fiber size N is

 Phi=sum_i H(P_i)-sum_edges H(nu_ij).

For every omission t, the retained tuple plus root determines the
whole star. Average the corresponding entropy equalities over all
d slots. Regularity and edge reversal cancel all root-entropy terms,
giving exactly

 Phi=-(d-2)/d sum_edges I_ij
       -(1/d)sum_i sum_t J_it <=0.

Different side alphabets do not change H(nu_ij)=H(mu_i)+H(mu_j)-I_ij.
The coefficients are REAL entropy coefficients; no division by d
in the finite field is being used. All zero entries are handled by
zero-safe factorial bounds. Fixed alphabets and degree give only
polynomially many empirical types, with uniform polynomial factorial
errors. Nonpositivity alone would NOT prove bounded expectation;
the remaining steps are essential and are supplied in these sources.

4. ALL ZERO PHASES, INCLUDING PROPER MIXED PHASES
------------------------------------------------
At Phi=0 all edge mutual informations and all J_it vanish. Given a
root, every retained d-1 neighbor tuple is a product of its marginals,
independent of that root.

The finite-group lemma is correct. Fix two neighbor slots and condition
the other d-2 slots to any support values. Independence of each retained
tuple ensures these conditioning events are positive and each of the
two remaining conditional marginals is its original marginal. Their
sum is fixed, giving reflected marginals and equal support-difference
spaces. Varying one of the conditioned variables (available because
d>=3) gives invariance under the generators of this common subspace.
Each marginal is therefore uniform on a full coset of it. Translation
invariance here generates the F_p-linear subspace, since the additive
group has exponent p.

Applying this at every old vertex makes neighbor subspaces identical
along every two-step walk. A connected bipartite base has exactly two
even-walk classes. Thus its complete phases are

 H_L<=V_L, H_R<=V_R,
 mu_i=Uniform(a_i+H_side(i)),
 T_i(H_side(i)) subset H_opposite(i),
 T_i a_i-sum_(j~i)a_j in H_opposite(i).

Every displayed condition is necessary. Conversely it permits a
uniform root and any d-1 independent uniform opposite-side coset
labels, with the last label determined in its required coset. This
constructs the UNIQUE uniform legal-star law on that affine support
and gives zero entropy. Representative changes are valid because of
the subspace inclusions. No other zero phases remain.

Zero-dimensional sides are included. Every root law is still observed
as a neighbor law at an adjacent old vertex. Mixed H_L,H_R phases are
real, notably for zero targets, and are not incorrectly collapsed into
one common subspace. The number of phases is finite for fixed data.
The full-support phase is exactly (H_L,H_R)=(V_L,V_R).

The optional fixed-support Hessian calculation was also reconstructed:

9. QUOTIENT AFFINE FIBERS AND CONDITIONAL FORCING
------------------------------------------------
At a proper phase, quotient each side by H_side and subtract its

Let C prescribe E edge-matching pairs in a valid sparse-support

swapping images of u and the present preimage w of v. Earlier pairs
are preserved because both new domain and target were unused.
For EACH output satisfying the new condition, there are exactly N-t
preimages before the forcing step, one per unused possible w.
Therefore the output is EXACTLY uniform conditional on prescribed
pairs. Separate base-edge maps retain their required independent

Each image swap changes at most two left and two right vertex

 E[Z_H(G)|C]<=p^[4(r_L+r_R)E] E Z_H(G).
