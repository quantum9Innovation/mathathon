BOOLEAN MACROSCOPIC VARIANCE GAP: INDEPENDENT ALGEBRA AUDIT
2026-11-15. This audits the root's proposed simplification for every
fixed a=d1>b=d2>0. No historical novelty claim is made.

VERDICT
The entropy/KL/variance argument is correct for ALL a>=2, including
a=2,b=1. The internal variance cancellation at a=2 is harmless.

SETUP
Set delta=a-b. A symmetric first-block star has Boolean root X,
internal neighbor slots A_1,...,A_a, and cross neighbor slots
B_1,...,B_b. Its support obeys

  sum_i A_i + sum_j B_j = delta X.

All variables take values in {-1,+1}. Slots of each kind are
exchangeable, as obtained by entropy-increasing symmetrization of
distinguished stubs. Let mu+ be the root law in the first block,
mu- the root law in the second block. Internal edge laws are
symmetric, with both marginals mu+ or mu-; the cross edge law
has marginals mu+,mu-.

Delete A_a, which is determined by the remaining variables:

  W=delta X-sum_(i=1,...,a-1) A_i-sum_(j=1,...,b) B_j.

Let P+ be the law of this truncated star and let

  J+=TC(A_1,...,A_(a-1),B_1,...,B_b | X),

the conditional total correlation relative to the product of its
ACTUAL conditional one-slot laws. Define J- analogously in the
second block. Write I_int+,I_int- for the two internal edge mutual
informations, and I_cross for the cross edge mutual information.

ENTROPY IDENTITY
Let H_star+ mean H(X,full neighborhood), which equals H(P+) because
the deleted slot is determined. Direct conditional entropy expansion
gives

 H_star+ = (a-1) H_int+ + b H_cross
           -(a+b-2) H(mu+) - J+.

The analogous expression holds in the second block. Therefore the
configuration entropy exponent

 Phi=H_star++H_star--(a/2)(H_int++H_int-)-b H_cross

obeys the exact identity

 L:=-Phi = ((a-2)/2)(I_int++I_int-)
            + b I_cross + J+ + J- >=0.                 (1)

Here use H_int+=2H(mu+)-I_int+, the corresponding minus identity,
and H_cross=H(mu+)+H(mu-)-I_cross. The root-entropy terms cancel
because (a-2)+b-(a+b-2)=0. All entropies use natural logarithms.

KL COMPARISON
Let R+ retain the actual root law mu+. Conditional on X, give its
a-1 internal slots independent copies of the actual law A|X, and
give its b cross slots independent copies of the UNCONDITIONAL
law mu-, independent of X and all internal slots. Then

 D(P+ || R+) = J+ + b I_cross <= L.                    (2)

The identity follows by inserting the product of the actual
conditional marginals: replacing each cross conditional marginal by
mu- adds one cross mutual information. This does not replace the
actual internal conditional law by an unconditional law.

VARIANCE COMPARISON
Put m(X)=E[A_a|X] and V=E Var(A_a|X). Exchangeability gives the
same conditional moments for every internal slot. For

  f=(W-m(X))^2,

the actual law P+ has W=A_a, hence

  E_P+ f = V.                                          (3)

Under R+, the retained variables are independent conditional on X,
so

 Var_R+(W-m(X)|X)
       =(a-1) Var(A_a|X)+b Var(mu-).

The conditional squared mean is nonnegative. Consequently

  E_R+ f-E_P+ f >= (a-2)V+b Var(mu-)
                    >= b Var(mu-)                     (4)

for every a>=2. At a=2 the first term is exactly zero; no strict
internal-degree inequality is needed.

RANGE AND PINSKER CONSTANTS
Because delta=a-b>0 and every sampled value and m(X) lie in [-1,1],

  |W-m(X)| <= delta+(a-1)+b+1 = 2a.

Thus 0<=f<=4a^2 under BOTH P+ and R+. Using total variation with
the convention TV=(1/2)||P-R||_1,

 |E_R+f-E_P+f| <= 4a^2 TV(P+,R+)
                <= 4a^2 sqrt(D(P+||R+)/2)
                <= 4a^2 sqrt(L/2).

Together with (4),

  L >= b^2 Var(mu-)^2/(8a^4).                          (5)

The symmetric second-block comparison gives the same bound with
mu+ in place of mu-. If r is the mass at +1 in either root law,
Var(mu)=4r(1-r), so

  L >= 2b^2 r^2(1-r)^2/a^4.                            (6)

This is an explicit strict macroscopic entropy gap whenever either
block's Boolean root law stays away from a point mass. In particular,
L=0 forces both block root laws to be point masses. Internal and
cross edge marginal consistency then makes every neighbor constant;
the local equation a x+b y=(a-b)x forces y=-x. Thus the only Boolean
zero phases are the two planted orientations.

LIMIT OF THIS AUDIT
Equations (1)--(6) establish the macroscopic Boolean entropy step.
They do not by themselves enumerate near-planted flip sets or prove
a finite-field moment estimate. Exact type prefactors and a separate
small-overlap count are still needed when using this gap in a full
identifiability theorem. In particular this is not a standalone
new proof of real planted-eigenvalue simplicity.
