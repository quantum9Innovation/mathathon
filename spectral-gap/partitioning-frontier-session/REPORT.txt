PARTITIONING FRONTIER SESSION -- 2026-11-15
=========================================

Outcome
-------
Two field-scale algorithm targets survive the inspected CURRENT primary
literature. The stronger recommended target is nearly-linear-time
flow-expander decomposition with the almost-optimal overhead already
achieved in polynomial time by Bansal--Jambulapati--Saranurak (2026).
The second is nearly-linear-time O(sqrt(log n)) multiplicative balanced
edge cutting, without an eigengap promise.

No such algorithm is proved here. We did prove an exact normalization-aware
one-edge response theorem and several explicit barriers to using the
repository's fractional response machinery for partitioning. These are
useful research results, with classical ingredients and unverified
historical novelty; they are not a field-scale breakthrough claim.

All new writes are in spectral-gap/partitioning-frontier-session/.
The existing RESEARCH_SESSION_2026-11-15.txt was read in full. No frozen
paper, root coordination file, README, or persistent goal was modified.

What the repository actually supplies
-------------------------------------
The relevant input is the gap-free, expected-nearly-linear exact-real
construction of a PSD thin factor approximating

    (L + delta bb^T)^alpha - L^alpha

relative to L^alpha, after one edge increase, with rank controlled by
alpha log(1+delta R_uv). That theorem may rebuild graph-solver data and
scan the graph on EACH edit. It is not a polylogarithmic dynamic-update
theorem. Its latest degree theorem is a sharp relative-mode count, not
a cut-rounding theorem. The matrix work supplies rational/resolvent and
cluster-sensitive numerical machinery, not a separation oracle or an
unrestricted quadratic-time matrix-product algorithm.

For alpha>0, L and L^alpha have exactly the same eigenvectors and spectral
order. Fractional powering alone cannot repair Fiedler localization or
improve a sweep's quality. A PSD correction also need not be a positive
graph-edge correction. Both distinctions matter below.

The two BIG IF TRUE problems
---------------------------
1. STRONGEST FIELD TARGET: near-linear almost-optimal FLOW decomposition.

   Input: an n-vertex m-edge undirected unit-capacity graph and
   n^(-C) <= phi <= 1, for any fixed C. Output an edge set Q such that
   every component of G-Q is a phi-FLOW-expander and

       |Q| <= C0 phi m log n exp(C1 sqrt(log log n)).

   Desired total work: expected O(m polylog n), with no 1/phi factor
   hidden outside logarithms. A flow-expander means every multicommodity
   demand respecting the component's degrees is routable with congestion
   at most 1/phi. It is stronger than a cut-expansion certificate.

   Bansal--Jambulapati--Saranurak already prove this quality in POLYNOMIAL
   time, Theorem 1.1 (v2, 28 Apr 2026):
     https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15015v2
   Their concurrent-flow LP / dual-metric clustering is the actual
   benchmark. The problem here is runtime with that quality preserved,
   not inventing log^(1+o(1)) overhead. General overhead o(log n) is
   impossible; the hypercube already forces the logarithm.

   Possible role for our tools: recycling a small response certificate
   through recursive reweighting and restriction might reduce repeated
   global work. Required missing theorem: such a certificate must support
   the ACTUAL concurrent-flow dual and total recursive cut charge.
   An electrical energy bound does not alone certify multicommodity
   congestion. Induced restriction is not a PSD single-edge increase;
   Schur complements do not commute with fractional powers. No reduction
   overcoming these issues is supplied here.

2. SECOND FIELD TARGET: near-linear optimal-quality balanced EDGE cut.

   Fix b in (0,1/2). OPT_b is the minimum conductance over cuts having
   at least b times the total degree volume on each side. On every
   connected undirected graph with polynomially bounded integer
   capacities, return an Omega(b)-balanced cut S with

       Phi(S) <= C_b sqrt(log n) OPT_b

   in expected O(m polylog n) work, without an eigengap promise.

   This is a MULTIPLICATIVE guarantee, not O(sqrt(OPT_b)). The latter
   spectral guarantee, with gap-free tilde O(m) work, is already given
   by Orecchia--Sachdeva--Vishnoi, STOC 2012:
     https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.1491
   The inspected current chaining tradeoff has O(sqrt(log n/epsilon))
   quality and n^epsilon polylog(n) maxflow calls; its parallel span does
   not imply nearly-linear sequential work:
     https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00115v5

   Possible role: compress matrix-function updates in an SDP/chaining
   implementation. Missing obligations are metric triangle inequalities,
   violating-path discovery, adaptive accumulated error, and the total
   work for multi-edge weight changes. A one-edge factor routine does
   not settle any of these by itself.

Neither target is contradicted by the inspected conditional hardness.
RST's SSE-hardness distinguishes a small-conductance bisection from
graphs whose moderately balanced cuts have conductance Omega(sqrt(eps)).
It is not an unconditional NP-hardness result, and it does not forbid a
sqrt(log n) multiplicative factor growing with n:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.2586

Concrete missing algorithmic bridge
----------------------------------
This is a precise enabling problem, not a third claimed field-scale
advance. For fixed alpha in (0,1), define

    H = D^(1/2) (D^(-1/2) L D^(-1/2))^alpha D^(1/2).

For an existing weighted edge doubled, with L',D',H' updated, construct
X with columns orthogonal to constants such that, on one success event,

    -eps H <= H' - H - XX^T <= eps H,
    rank X < B_alpha / log(1+(1-rho)eps)       if rank X > 0,

in expected (m+n) polylog(n,1/eps,1/rho,1/failure_probability) exact-real
work. Require all factor entries, preprocessing, solve tolerances and
small-core work charged, with no hidden spectral gap or conductance-ratio
dependence. Arithmetic and positive square roots suffice as the natural
model at alpha=1/2. The exact budget B_alpha is proved below. The same
quantifiers for arbitrary positive insertions can allow logarithmic
dependence on 1+tau. Finite-word stability is an additional question.

This would extend the repository's fixed-mass algorithm to the canonical
degree-normalized energy. It still needs a separate partitioning/rounding
theorem. The four-vertex obstruction below identifies why merely copying
the existing quadrature proof does not establish it.

Proved in this session
----------------------
A. Exact normalized fractional response budget.

   H is the classical geometric mean D #_alpha L, has common kernel
   span{1}, and increases in PSD order after a positive edge edit even
   though the ordinary normalized Laplacian has a moving kernel.

   If the edit adds weight delta on uv, tau=delta R_uv, and v=Tr D,

     B_alpha = log[pdet(H')/pdet(H)]
       = alpha log(1+tau)
         +(1-alpha) log[(1+delta/d_u)(1+delta/d_v)/(1+2delta/v)].

   Hence relative eigenvalues above eps number strictly less than
   B_alpha/log(1+eps), when positive. Also
   B_alpha <= (2-alpha)log(1+tau). This is a rank UPPER BOUND; no sharp
   relative-mode count or attainability classification is asserted.

   The exact congruence identity is

     pdet H = (pdet L)^alpha (n det D / Tr D)^(1-alpha).

   Proof: singular-safe parallel-sum monotonicity, pseudodeterminant
   congruence, matrix-tree theorem, and determinant lemma. These are
   classical ingredients. Full source and PDF:
     higher-cheeger/markov-form-budget.tex / .pdf  (3 pages).

   A separate exact extremum: on connected simple unweighted graphs
   with m>=2, an existing unit-edge doubling has

     B_alpha <= alpha log2 +(1-alpha)log[3m/(m+1)],

   with equality precisely for a bridge of endpoint degrees {1,2}.
   For m=1 the budget is log2. This is sharp TOTAL BUDGET only, not
   a sharp count: see dynamic-target/independent-normalized-audit.md.

B. Positive rank-three shifted response.

   For K_t=L+tD and F_t=D-tD K_t^(-1)D,

     H = (sin(pi alpha)/pi) integral_0^infty t^(alpha-1) F_t dt.

   A physical edit adds three edges to a two-layer electrical network
   whose terminal Schur complement is F_t. The resulting exact response
   has a PSD three-column Woodbury factor at each t. This does NOT mean
   the integrated response has rank at most three. Its factor columns
   all sum to zero. The elementary Schur-update proof and exact matrices
   are in normalized-resolvent-barrier.tex / .pdf (4 pages).

C. Four weighted vertices defeat the inherited low-shift cutoff.

   On the weighted path with edge weights (1,h,1), double the first edge
   and take x=(1,1,-1,-1). Here n=4 and tau=1, independently of h. Yet

     lim_(h->0) x^T(H'-H)x / x^THx = (4/3)^(1-alpha)-1 > 0.

   For every fixed cutoff a>0, retaining ONLY shifts t>=a in the exact
   positive response integral gives x-energy at most

     [8 sin(pi alpha)/(pi(2-alpha))] h^2 a^(alpha-2),

   whereas x^THx ~ 4 h^alpha. The retained relative response tends to
   zero. Thus an n,tau,accuracy-only positive low cutoff cannot simply
   be inherited from the old fixed-mass theorem. This is a proof about
   that truncation method, not a computational impossibility theorem.
   The explicit host is weighted; no unweighted analogue is inferred.

   Source: normalized-resolvent-barrier.tex. Entire actual source passed
   an independent audit recorded in dynamic-target/ACTUAL_SOURCE_AUDIT.txt.

D. Even an unweighted host has a response that is not a graph Laplacian.

   On unweighted P4, double the first edge. For EVERY alpha in (0,1),

     Delta H is PSD, Delta H 1=0, but (Delta H)_34 > 0.

   An explicit strictly positive scalar integral certifies the entry.
   Every nonnegative graph-edge insertion has nonpositive offdiagonals,
   so their sums cannot realize or arbitrarily closely approximate this
   response. Each full H remains a graph Laplacian; its UPDATE need not
   be one. General PSD factors are not ruled out.
   Source: dynamic-target/positive-offdiagonal-obstruction.tex / .pdf
   (2 pages). The scalar check and dense powers agree below 1e-14 for
   alpha=.25,.5,.8 on the stated examples.

E. Relative positive-kernel cost is not relative normalized-cut cost.

   For a k-partition Pi, the standard positive graph-kernel construction
   K_sigma=D^(-1) A D^(-1)+sigma D^(-1) has weighted kernel-means cost

     J_sigma(Pi)=k NC(Pi)+Tr(D^(-1)A)-k+sigma(n-k),

   where NC is AVERAGE normalized cut. Exact optimizers agree, but the
   large additive constant invalidates a generic multiplicative transfer.

   On even cycles C_n, k=2, sigma=2, a balanced contiguous partition has
   NC=2/n and a parity partition has NC=1. Their positive-kernel costs
   differ by only the factor n/(n-1). Thus arbitrarily accurate relative
   kernel optimization alone can be Theta(n)-wrong for true NC, even on
   connected, regular, exactly balanced, degree-two hosts.

   This independently derived obstruction applies to the conversion,
   not to every execution or formulation of a published data structure.
   We have NOT proved an execution of the ICML2026 algorithm outputs
   the bad partition. Read literature/NORMALIZED_CUT_SHIFT_AUDIT.md
   before using its quoted approximation ratio as a black box.

F. Boundary and locality barriers for multiway repair.

   An explicit eight-vertex path with an inserted unit edge has an old
   good three-partition and a new good three-partition, but retaining the
   remote old cluster and changing only the two endpoint clusters forces
   conductance >=1/3 while lambda_3 tends to zero. Its insertion leverage
   is 4+3/eta, so this is NOT a bounded-leverage instability theorem.
   In fact bounded leverage already preserves any fixed-family old
   conductance approximation within (1+tau)^2 by elementary comparisons.
   Source: higher-cheeger/local-repair-obstruction.tex / .pdf (2 pages).

   A rank-one residual on cycles also shows eps H-relative error does
   not uniformly imply any fixed multiple of L-relative error as the
   normalized gap tends to zero. This concerns the allowed residual
   guarantee alone, not every structured rounding algorithm.

Current-literature exclusions and scope corrections
-------------------------------------------------
* Log^(1+o(1)) flow-decomposition overhead in polynomial time is already
  BJS2026. Near-linear cut decomposition with log^2 overhead and arbitrary
  vertex measures is Agassy--Dorfman--Kaplan v3 (29 Oct 2026).
* Warm-starting weak expander decomposition is already explicit in
  Fleischmann--George Z. Li--Jason Li, ICALP2026. A qualitative warm-start
  proposal is not a new result.
* Li's 2609.35369v2 (5 Oct 2026) freshly claims sharp same-index EXACT-k
  higher-order Cheeger. Its complete proof was not independently audited
  here; nevertheless proposing precisely that inequality as unexplored
  would be inappropriate.
* Reweighted spectral separators for minor-free/genus/geometric classes,
  deterministic linear-time minor-free separators with poly(h) constants,
  and fresh O(h sqrt n) minor-free size claims are all in current primary
  literature. Vertex separators are not balanced edge cuts.
* Jourdan--Macgregor--Schwartzman ICML2026 already has O(log n) worst-case
  dynamic edge updates, with material degree-dependent/query/static-solver
  terms. It does not supply small recourse or a fixed global labeling over
  queries. Its positive-kernel-to-NC multiplicative conversion needs the
  shift check in item E.
* Peng--Yoshida's average-sensitivity theorem concerns random deletions
  under explicit assumptions; it is not adversarial one-edge recourse.
* A tempting additional target -- k disjoint low-conductance sets after
  merging two old clusters, with O(h+sqrt(lambda_k)) quality -- is already
  supplied by a specialization of the classical core machinery in Lau's
  current Advanced Spectral Graph Theory notes, Section 7.7. It should
  not be advertised as a fresh Cheeger frontier. The coverage/algorithmic
  distinction is documented in the follow-up source notes.

Validation and practical next step
----------------------------------
The root diagnostics independently compare the direct fractional form,
the determinant formula, and the explicit rank-three response:
27 graph/exponent budget cases, 45 shifted factor cases, and 12 four-path
limits pass. Other agents retain independent checks and exact rational
cycle calculations. These are corroboration, not proofs or near-linear
timing measurements. The TeX arguments, actual-source audits and scope
qualifications carry the claims.

The useful immediate project is the missing normalized compressor,
explicitly handling arbitrarily low-scale component-mass changes and
signed effective-edge changes. In parallel, a serious attempt at the
strongest field target must work inside BJS's actual product-demand LP
and recursive charge, not substitute a generic spectral certificate.
No proved bridge from the current low-rank response to that LP is known
from this session. That missing bridge is the central research risk.
