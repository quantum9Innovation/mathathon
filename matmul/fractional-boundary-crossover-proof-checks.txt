Fix 0<alpha<1, tau>=0, epsilon=exp(-L), h=exp(-tau L). Among all finite
PSD pairs A and B=A+ww^T<=(1+h)I with ||w||=1, let N_tau(L) be the
supremal number of eigenvalues of B^alpha-A^alpha STRICTLY exceeding
epsilon. Dimension may depend on L. The theorem proves

  N_tau(L)/L^2 -> C_alpha(tau),

  C_alpha(tau) = (1/alpha-tau^2)/(4*pi^2),          0<=tau<=1;
              = (1-alpha*tau)^2/
                (4*pi^2*alpha*(1-alpha)),        1<=tau<=1/alpha;
              = 0,                              tau>=1/alpha.

Moreover N_tau(L)=1 for EVERY L>0 in the final region, including equality
tau=1/alpha. A positive rank-one correction F satisfies
  D-F>=0, ||D-F||_1<=h^alpha, D=B^alpha-A^alpha.
This finite tail estimate proves the exact cutoff separately from the
asymptotic argument. The limit already holds within single-edge increases
on connected weighted paths with arbitrary common positive vertex masses.
Dimension and masses may depend on L, and no mass-ratio bound is imposed.
