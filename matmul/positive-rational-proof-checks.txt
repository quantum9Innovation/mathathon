RESULT
  For 1<=a<b<=2, positive m-pole approximations to the power divergence
    f_a(x)=[x^a-a(x-1)-1]/[a(a-1)]
  (with its a=1 limit) have relative error against f_b bounded by
    C_(a,b) exp[-2*pi*sqrt(a*(b-a)*m/b)].
  The constant is independent of m. The negative-parameter range follows
  by f_a(x)=x f_(1-a)(1/x). These are the exact upper bounds asked for
  in Faust--Fawzi2023 Conjecture 1, including a=1,b=2.

  For 0<c<d<=1, positive Stieltjes sums R_m of type [m-1/m] satisfy
    0<R_m(x)<=x^(-c) for all x>0,
    sup_(0<x<=1) x^d [x^(-c)-R_m(x)]
        <= C_(c,d) exp[-2*pi*sqrt((d-c)*m)].
  This proves the stated bound of their Conjecture 2, with a stronger
  rational-degree restriction and positive residues. No matching lower
  bound or exact asymptotic multiplicative constant is claimed here.
