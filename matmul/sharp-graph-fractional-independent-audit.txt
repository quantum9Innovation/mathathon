Proof and complete-cost checks
-------------------------------
4. For positive changes and arbitrary fixed positive masses, E=UU^T
   is PSD. The formulas for a^2=||E||_F^2 and b=||U||_F^2=tr(E) are
   exact sparse formulas, including normalized diagonal and off-diagonal
   weights. Positive eigenvalues give
     ||E||<=a<=sqrt(k)||E||, 1<=chi=b/a<=sqrt(k).
   Unlike general signed changes with arbitrary masses, this entails
   no hidden mass-ratio or representation-cancellation parameter.

5. R-S=R E S=(RU)(SU)^T is the exact rank-k resolvent product. Both
   normalized endpoint operators are PSD, so ||R-S||<=1/t and the
   update identity gives ||R-S||<=a/t^2. The logarithmic fractional
   integrand has exactly the PSD strip envelope required by the actual
   constructive lemma, with strip half-width pi and no spectral scale.
   Its real-line derivative bound also transfers from the resolvent
   identity. Disconnected and singular endpoints cause no obstruction.

6. A column energy error eta implies Euclidean error <=eta||u||/t.
   Summing gives block error <=eta sqrt(b)/t. Expanding the cross
   product gives (2eta+eta^2)b/t^2, hence <=3eta b/t^2 for eta<=1.
   No common linear or symmetric approximate inverse is assumed.

7. The congruence identity
     (A+tI)^-1=H^1/2(K+tH)^-1H^1/2
   and its energy-error transport are exact. K+tH is SPD SDD. Each
   application and quadratic-distance check requires sparse edge work
   plus diagonal scalings, with no polynomial or logarithmic mass ratio
   in the exact-arithmetic transfer. The standard SDD theorem's expected
   work qualification is correctly retained.

8. The constructive quadrature supplies ACTUAL positive nodes s_j and
   nonnegative coefficients a_j with
     q<=L^2/[4pi^2 alpha(1-alpha)]+O_alpha(L^(7/4)),
     |log s_j|<=B_0=O_alpha(L+1), sum_j a_j=O_alpha(L+1).
   The source specifies a computable B_0 from pre-rounded logarithmic
   locations; it does not require a logarithm of a spectral scale or
   a node as a free primitive.

9. The decisive weighted solve-error calculation is correct:
     sum_j omega_j error_j
       <=C_alpha eta chi a^alpha (L+1) exp((1-alpha)B_0).
   With eta<=c_alpha exp(-L-(1-alpha)B_0)/[chi(L+1)], this is at most
   O_alpha(a^alpha exp(-L)). Its precision is O_alpha(L+log(2k)).
   The proposed dyadic implementation is sufficient because
   2 log(2)>1 and 1-alpha<1. Repeated halving and comparisons cost
   O_alpha(L+1), not an uncharged exponential oracle.

10. L=log(1/epsilon)+O_alpha(log(2k)) with exp(-L) small enough absorbs
    a^alpha/||E||^alpha<=k^(alpha/2), together with all fixed quadrature,
    coefficient and solve budgets. For fixed k the O(L^(7/4)) term is
    o(L^2), so neither the scalar realization nor the solve precision
    loses the claimed leading width coefficient.

11. Each actual node uses the general real factors
      P_j=sqrt(omega_j)Vhat_j, Q_j=sqrt(omega_j)What_j.
    They need no n-by-k by k-by-k multiplication. The 2kq solves,
    O(Nkq) physical factor work and O_alpha(L^3) scalar construction
    all fit the displayed soft-O expected bound. Sorting/merging edits
    has only dimension-logarithmic overhead. Dense output is separately
    charged. General fixed alpha retains the source's explicit extra
    scalar-power qualification; alpha=1/2 uses ordinary arithmetic,
    comparisons and scalar square roots.

12. Arbitrary confidence follows from the referenced robust solver
    amplification. Use odd s and include zero self-distances in each
    median, as explicitly specified in the underlying graph audit.
    Constant-success solves at eta/3, majority selection by sparse
    inner-system energy distances, and a union bound give simultaneous
    accuracy for all 2kq calls. The added s^2 distance work is only
    logarithmic overhead. This does not upgrade expected work to a cap.

13. Exact symmetrization has width at most 2kq and the same work order.
    The source correctly claims the SHARP coefficient only for general
    PQ^T output. Approximate symmetry of low-rank factors is not enough
    to claim a sharp-width PSD or exactly symmetric factor at the same
    variable-k construction cost.

Lower bound and scope checks
-----------------------------
14. The path realization supplies positive rank-one graph updates with
    exact unit norm. k disjoint witnesses have norm-one rank-k positive
    update. Adding arbitrarily weak unchanged bridge conductances makes
    one connected path and preserves every strict count for sufficiently
    small bridges. These examples are in the algorithm's MASS-WEIGHTED
    POSITIVE-update class, and chi<=sqrt(k) holds on them.

15. The companion PSD count theorem therefore gives matching leading
    width k/[4pi^2 alpha(1-alpha)] already for those graph examples.
    Any rank-l approximant within spectral error epsilon must have
    l>=N_epsilon(target), irrespective of symmetry or randomness. A
    randomized output with a smaller guaranteed width cannot succeed
    on such an input even with positive probability. The lower and upper
    classes therefore match for the leading worst-case width constant.

16. This is a minimax statement over all finite dimensions and all
    allowed positive masses; the graph and masses may depend on epsilon.
    It is not an asymptotic for one fixed graph, a lower bound on runtime,
    a bounded-mass theorem, or a sharpness theorem on unit-mass paths.
    Signed unit-mass changes retain the sharp upper coefficient, but no
    matching graph-restricted lower has been established there. Signed
    arbitrary-mass changes correctly retain the extra log(chi) cost.
