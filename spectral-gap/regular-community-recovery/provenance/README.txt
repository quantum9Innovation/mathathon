Proof-input and review provenance
================================

SOURCE-HASHES.txt records workspace-relative versions of selected
proof-development sources and review records. proof-inputs/ mirrors those
paths so that mathematical dependencies travel with the packet. These
are provenance records, not extra unstated hypotheses or substitutes for
the proofs in the active manuscript.

Dependency map:
  Active sections 02--03: strict-q-recovery positive theorem AND separate
    zero-omission addendum; zero-omission-channel-session central,
    boundary, zero-phase and assembled channel sources.
  Sections 04--06: positive-omissions scalar source; rsbm-full-kernel
    certified primitive and nonsymmetric extension; channel composition.
  Sections 07/07b: regular-lift-moments, cyclic-old-new, and bipartite
    phase/lattice/transfer sources, with actual-source gates.
  Section 08: sharp complete-base counting and cycle-cover posterior.
  Sections 09--10/13: all-parameter exact ambiguity source, bounded
    moments, and certified listing sources.
  Section 12 and appendix: prescribed-cross kernel sources and odd-rsbm
    all-target configuration, atom/tail and parity arguments.
  Section 16 source: nonregular-fiber-counting-obstruction and root audit.
  Introduction: primary-source priority comparisons, including direct
    cover-recovery and classical counting/finite-field predecessors.

The final-integrated-*-audit records describe full integrated-source
reviews. The two plain-language reviews also read the final nonregular
obstruction addition. Earlier audits bind their explicitly named source
versions, not later changed text.

Active packet files are listed in SHA256SUMS.txt; proof-input snapshots
are bound through SOURCE-HASHES.txt. Historical drafts and build
intermediates are not active manuscript sources. The preserved 22-page
audit is explicitly historical and superseded.

frozen-packet/ holds exact copies of the three earlier independent
single-edge-response files named in FROZEN-PACKET-HASHES.txt. This permits
their integrity check without requiring the original workspace. They are
historical provenance, not an additional result imported into this paper.
