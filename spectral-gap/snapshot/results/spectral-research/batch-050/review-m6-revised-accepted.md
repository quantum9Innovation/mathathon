# Revised m6 submission: accepted with scope

## Outcome

The revised independent exterior-resolvent proof and bounded-pattern correction are
accepted. This corroborates the accepted moment proof rather than replacing it.

The stochastic-Montel repair is now powered by a real spectral bound. Subtracting the
Perron projector leaves a holomorphic function on the complex slit plane outside the
non-Perron spectral interval, bounded by a continuous $n$-independent function of $z$.
Replacing the function by its limit on the exceptional event gives an honest normal
family, and the large-energy convergence set identifies the limit. This addresses the
main objection to the first submission.

The simplicity probability is now supported by Angel, van der Hofstad and Holmgren,
Corollary 1.2, equations 1.15 and 1.16, rather than a citation recalled from memory.
The argument supplies $P(\text{simple})=\exp(-(d^2-1)/4+O(1/n))$ for fixed degree and
$dn$ even.

## What is now available

Two independent routes establish the fixed-label exterior resolvent limit. The disclosed
bounded-diameter examples derive correct off-diagonal values on the infinite regular
tree. In particular the limiting clustered form involves
$G_d(\lambda)\sum_{s,t}r(\lambda)^{\operatorname{dist}(s,t)}$, not simply
$\Delta G_d(\lambda)$. The accepted examples are adjacent attachment pairs and all
neighbours of one centre. Arbitrarily graph-dependent random attachment sets were not
certified.

## Remaining limits

This submission alone did not establish the complete eigenvalue ordering, a rate, the
bulk edge, growing defect sets or a novelty claim. The correct clustered correction also
means that simple degree-only thresholds are limited to separated attachment points.
Those are mathematical distinctions, not caveats added after the fact.
