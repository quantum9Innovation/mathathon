Independent adversarial rigidity audit: PASS
15 November 2026
==========================================

The real one-hot rigidity claim in regular-frame-no-internal-root.txt
passes. The self-contained proof is all-slot-onehot-rigidity.tex, with
a clean six-page PDF. This is a local entropy/rigidity audit, not an
audit of the full configuration count, support estimate, algorithm,
historical priority, or their composition.

The precise local theorem
------------------------
Let every row of the old integer degree frame Q sum to the same d>=3.
Let positive old proportions alpha obey alpha_i*dij=alpha_j*dji.
For legal finite-alphabet real one-hot star laws satisfying the exact
INTEGER equation sum(neighbor unit vectors)=R_(root,*), use pooled
pair laws with the exact internal symmetry and cross reversal constraints.

Then, writing delta=sum_i alpha_i(1-max rho_i),
  Phi <= -alpha_min*(d-2)^3*delta^2/[4*d^6*(d-1)]
      <= -alpha_min*delta^2/(8*D^6), 3<=d<=D.
Phi is NORMALIZED by total graph size. The unnormalized configuration
count exponent is n*Phi. Delta is exactly the minimum normalized row
disagreement with an old-block point map. Unused candidate labels and
non-onto point maps are allowed.

Equality Phi=0 holds precisely for deterministic old-block maps T with
QT=TR. This covers internal degree zero or one, including all-cross
matching lifts, unequal positive proportions, and k=1 uniform regular
graphs with d>=3. The LOCAL statement does not require connectedness:
every positive-degree old type has an incoming neighbor by reversibility,
possibly itself. The parent's connected-frame scope is included.

Critical reconstruction
-----------------------
Within-type slot symmetrization preserves root laws, pooled edge laws,
and the legal equation while increasing star entropy. No symmetrization
across distinct old neighbor types is used. Every physical slot can be
omitted without losing entropy because its unit vector is determined
by the retained data. Averaging all d omissions yields
  L=-Phi=(d-2)/d *E+J/d,
  E=sum_(i<=j)w_ij I_ij,
  J=sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it.
Here w_ii=alpha_i*dii/2 and w_ij=alpha_i*dij for i<j. The root-entropy
coefficients cancel exactly by regularity and reversibility, including
the factor two at internal edges.

For omission t, the auxiliary law keeps the root marginal but makes all
retained neighbor labels independent from their root-free marginals.
Its divergence is kappa_it=J_it+sum_(s!=t)I_is. For a fixed old type i,
  alpha_i sum_t kappa_it <=2*d*(d-1)/(d-2)*L.
This uses alpha_i sum_s I_is<=2E and compares the E/J coefficients
separately; it does not incorrectly sum incident edges globally.

Use mu_t=E e_(Y_t), V_t=1-||mu_t||^2, and
  f_t=||R_X-sum_(s!=t)e_(Y_s)-mu_t||^2.
Under the actual retained law E f_t=V_t. Under the product law,
  E f_t=sum_(s!=t)V_s+B_i,
  B_i=E||R_X-sum_s mu_s||^2>=0,
with the SAME B_i for every omission. Thus the summed differences are
  (d-2)sum_s V_s+d*B_i.
Every occupied R row has nonnegative entries and mass d by the integer
equation, so 0<=f_t<=2d^2 under both laws, including illegal auxiliary
tuples. Pinsker and Cauchy--Schwarz yield the displayed explicit gap.
No root-image injectivity, distinct quotient rows, or internal slot is
assumed. Individual expectation differences may be negative; only their
sum is asserted nonnegative. At zero entropy the root proposal's simpler
conditional-variance identity V_t=sum_(s!=t)V_s is valid for every t.

Finite corroboration (not asymptotic proof premises)
--------------------------------------------------
check-all-slot-rigidity.py uses only the Python standard library and
checks all set partitions of five explicit graphs:
  * K4 two-fold lift, no internal edges:4140 partitions,36 equitable;
  * cubic frame with internal matchings:4140,36 equitable;
  * unequal2+6 cubic frame with one zero internal degree:4140,36;
  * one-class K4, internal degree3:15,all15 equitable;
  * degree4 K4,4, all cross:4140,356 equitable.
It also checks all75 base/component product partitions in a trivial
three-fold K4 cover with odd fiber size3. These are selected partitions,
not all set partitions of its12 vertices. Six additional checks pad the
candidate quotient with an unused label. Repeated quotient rows occur
in354 checked degree4 partitions, directly testing noninjectivity.

All554 equitable laws plus the6 unused-label variants pass cancellation,
direct KL-chain equality, product-law expectation, actual expectation,
summed variance, Pinsker, local and global gap, and equality tests.
Maximum observed numerical error is2.31e-14. The excluded d=2 even-cycle
alternating labeling is also checked: its positive defect has Phi=0.
