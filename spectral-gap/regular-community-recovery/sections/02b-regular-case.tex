\subsection{Regular frames: average over all omitted slots}\label{sec:all-slot}
Assume now $d_i=d\ge3$ for every old class. No internal slot is
required. For physical neighbor slots $Z_{i1},\ldots,Z_{id}$, let
$J_{it}$ be conditional total correlation of all but slot $t$,
given the root. Lossless deletion and averaging over all $d$ slots give
\[
 H(P_i)=H(\mu_i)+\frac{d-1}{d}\sum_sH(Z_{is}\mid X_i)
                       -\frac1d\sum_tJ_{it}.
\]
Only slots within one old neighbor type are symmetrized. Put
$w_{ii}=\alpha_i a_i/2$ and $w_{ij}=\alpha_id_{ij}$ for $i<j$.
Balance and row regularity give the exact cancellation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:all-slot-entropy}
 L=-\Phi=\frac{d-2}{d}\sum_{i\le j}w_{ij}I_{ij}
                 +\frac1d\sum_i\alpha_i\sum_tJ_{it}\ge0.
\end{equation}
Internal terms have the factor one-half; absent factors contribute
nothing. Averaging by $d$ is an operation on real entropies and
requires no division in the finite field.

For real one-hot labels, fix $i,t$ and compare its actual retained
law to the same root law times independent root-independent neighbor
marginals. Its divergence is
$\kappa_{it}=J_{it}+\sum_{s\ne t}I(X_i;Z_{is})$.
If $E=\sum_{i\le j}w_{ij}I_{ij}$ and
$J_* =\sum_i\alpha_i\sum_tJ_{it}$, then for this fixed old class
\begin{equation}\label{eq:all-slot-KL}
 \alpha_i\sum_t\kappa_{it}
 \le J_*+2(d-1)E
 \le\frac{2d(d-1)}{d-2}L.
\end{equation}
The internal incidence is counted twice in $2E$, while each cross
incidence for this class is counted once; the inequality is an upper
bound and does not assume local edge budgets are disjoint.

Let $\mu_s=\E e_{Z_{is}}$ and $V_s=1-\|\mu_s\|_2^2$.
For omission $t$ put
$U_t=R_{X_i,*}-\sum_{s\ne t}e_{Z_{is}}$ and
$f_t=\|U_t-\mu_t\|_2^2$.
Its actual expectation is $V_t$ and its product-law expectation is
$\sum_{s\ne t}V_s+B_i$, where
$B_i=\E\|R_{X_i,*}-\sum_s\mu_s\|_2^2\ge0$ is independent of $t$.
The sum of expectation differences is therefore
$(d-2)\sum_sV_s+dB_i$. Individual differences can be negative;
only their sum is used. Since $0\le f_t\le2d^2$ on both laws,
Pinsker, Cauchy--Schwarz, and \eqref{eq:all-slot-KL} give
\[
 (d-2)\sum_sV_s
 \le2d^2\sqrt{\frac d2\sum_t\kappa_{it}}
 \le2d^2\sqrt{\frac{d^2(d-1)}{\alpha_i(d-2)}L}.
\]
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:all-slot-local-gap}
 L\ge\frac{\alpha_i(d-2)^3}{4d^6(d-1)}
                      \left(\sum_sV_s\right)^2.
\end{equation}
Some old root law has variance trace at least $\epsilon$ of
\eqref{eq:unknown-distance}. Because it has positive degree and
the pair laws reverse, it occurs in a slot at some old type $i$,
possibly the same type. Thus $\sum_sV_s\ge\epsilon$ there and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:all-slot-gap}
 \Phi\le-\frac{\rho(d-2)^3}{4d^6(d-1)}\epsilon^2
       \le-\frac{\rho}{8D^6}\epsilon^2.
\end{equation}
This includes $k=1$. At zero entropy every root law is a point,
and the local constraints are exactly $QT=TR$, as before.

Both model conditions therefore have the common strict bound
$\Phi\le-\rho\epsilon^2/(8D^6)$ and the modular nonpositivity
needed for \eqref{eq:unknown-moment}.
