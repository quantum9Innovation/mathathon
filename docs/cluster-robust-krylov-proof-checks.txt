RESULT AND LIMITS
  Theorem1: actual fundamental matrix-polynomial growth is bounded by
    [1000 d^8 b^5 beta^-4 L/Delta]^(d-1), simultaneous all roots/z,
    for fixed admissible equal-size disjoint target blocks.
  Relative-tail theorem: for positive target nodes, the uniform lower-tail
    bound is [20000 d^12 b^5/(beta^4 g_rel)]^(d-1), no condition number.
  Packing and analysis-only padding give D<=2ceil(k/b)-1 for positive top-k
    spectra with a positive sliding relative b-th gap. Standard good-start
    LRA gives depth O([D log(Db/(beta gamma_b))+log(n/(beta epsilon))]
    /sqrt(epsilon)), plus the analogous external-gap bound.
  This addresses the convergence question up to extra logarithms, not the
    literal raw-monomial conditioning conjecture. Generic accuracy exponent
    remains 1/2. Neither a new MM exponent nor unrestricted N^2 is proved.
