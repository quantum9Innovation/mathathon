RAW NEAR-REAL RESOLVENTS CONTROLLED BY AN INTRINSIC BAND POPULATION
================================================================

Coordinator composition, 2026-11-15 UTC. Independent review pending.
This combines the independently reviewed certified coarse split and
shared coupling-series solver. It removes a supplied eigenbasis and
replaces the coarse-dependent dimension condition by a sufficient
condition on the actual physical spectrum. It is a resolver theorem,
not the original rank-one LRA or full matrix-product theorem.

1. RAW INPUT, INTRINSIC PROMISE, AND GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------
Let X be an arbitrary real N-by-m factor, N>=m, and let J be the
supplied symmetric involution in the existing raw-factor model. Its
actions are charged as there. Define S=X J X^T without forming S or
the true Gram X^T X, and write I_X=Nm. Fix a positive real center t,
q>=1 supplied poles w_j=t+i*y_j with y_j!=0, and q supplied physical
RHS columns h_j. They are all available together for each block action.

Let 0<epsilon<=1, 0<delta<1, and mu=max_j |w_j|/|y_j|. Supply an
integer cap 0<=kappa<=m with the INTRINSIC spectral promise

  #{lambda in spec(S): t/4<=lambda<=4t}<=kappa.       (1)

Eigenvalues are counted with multiplicity, and the endpoints are closed.
No positive/negative gap or separation of neighboring eigenvalues is
assumed. Let J_s,L>=1 be the explicit integer parameters of the
shared-series proof at delta_s=epsilon/(100mu^2), so

  (3/5)^L<=delta_s/100, (3/4)^J_s<=delta_s/100,
  J_s,L=O(log(2mu/epsilon)).                         (2)

Suppose R_cap=q+kappa*J_s<=m. There is an algorithm returning columns
a_j with probability at least 1-delta such that simultaneously

  ||a_j-w_j(S-w_j I)^-1 h_j||<=epsilon||h_j||.       (3)

Including preprocessing, every input action and every output column,
its arithmetic work is bounded on all outcomes, with the usual fixed
matrix-multiplication exponent slack, by

  ~O(I_X+m^omega)
   +O(I_X R_cap^(omega-2)*L)
   +O(m kappa^2 J_s+q kappa^2 J_s
                       +m kappa q J_s+q kappa^omega). (4)

Tildes include dimension and confidence logarithms and the polylogarithmic
scalar/Frobenius preparation overheads. They hide no ||S||/t, factor
condition number, or input-magnitude logarithm. The arithmetic model is
exact real/complex field operations, comparisons, positive square roots
and paid discrete randomness as in the component proofs. No stable
finite-word or bit-complexity theorem is asserted.

For a spectral action guarantee on an orthonormal q-column input basis,
use epsilon/sqrt(q) in the construction. This adds logarithms; per-column
accuracy is not silently treated as a spectral-block bound.

2. THE COARSE REPRESENTATION IS CONSTRUCTED FROM THE RAW FACTOR
-------------------------------------------------------------
Use the checked constant-distortion sparse embedding from
raw-factor-fresh-search.txt, Sections 1--3, with eta=1/8 and failure
at most delta/4. Its formation and coarse factorization cost
~O(I_X+m^omega). On the embedding event a full-row-rank coarse factor
L_0 satisfies

  (1-eta)L_0^T L_0 <= X^T X <= (1+eta)L_0^T L_0.

Set Z=X L_0^dagger and K=L_0 J L_0^T. These are represented by thin
raw products and explicit coarse matrices; they satisfy EXACTLY

  S=ZKZ^T, T=Z^T Z, ||T-I||<=eta.                   (5)

The equality follows from X=X L_0^dagger L_0 on the common row support.
The mass inequalities give the bound on T by congruence. Remove zero
coarse directions using exact orthogonal support reduction on K,
preserving (5) and its Gram bound. No true Gram is formed by this step.
The remaining supported dimension is at most m.

On failed sketch outcomes the algorithm still follows the finite
operation caps and the guards below; (5) is needed for correctness,
not assumed to hold on those outcomes. The zero raw factor returns
the exact actions -h_j. If a zero coarse core is produced, the same
answer is valid on the embedding event, and is an allowed arbitrary
answer off that event.

3. WHY THE ACTUAL SPECTRAL PROMISE BOUNDS THE COARSE BAND
--------------------------------------------------------
On (5), let H=T^(1/2) K T^(1/2), used only for analysis. Its nonzero
spectrum equals that of physical S, and congruence preserves the positive
inertia of K. For s>0,

 K-[s/(1-eta)]I <= K-s T^-1 <= K-[s/(1+eta)]I.       (6)

Moreover H-sI is congruent to K-s T^-1. Comparing positive inertias
and then ordered positive eigenvalues therefore gives, for every
positive-eigenvalue index j,

 (1-eta)lambda_j^+(K)<=lambda_j^+(H)
                         <=(1+eta)lambda_j^+(K).    (7)

This argument is valid when K is INDEFINITE; no PSD commutation
assumption is used. Every coarse positive eigenvalue in [t/3,3t]
maps, by its ordered index, to a distinct physical positive eigenvalue
in [7t/24,27t/8], contained strictly in [t/4,4t]. Hence

 #{lambda(K):t/3<=lambda<=3t}<=kappa.               (8)

Endpoint multiplicities cause no difficulty because all intervals
in (7),(8) are closed and the final enlargement has strict room.

4. ACTUAL PREPARATION, POLE SHARING, AND ALL-OUTCOMES GUARDS
-----------------------------------------------------------
Apply certified-buffered-coarse-split.txt to the explicit supported
K and center t. Use at most ceil(log_2(4/delta)) independent certifying
attempts, found by doubling and comparisons. Every accepted output meets
its exact residual-based theorem;
the probability that no attempt is accepted is at most delta/4. Its
bounded per-attempt operation guards give the first term of (4).

For B=tK^-1, the computed P,Q split has

  spec(B_QQ) outside [1/2,2], ||B_PQ||<=1e-5<1/16,
  k=dim(P)<=#{lambda(K):t/3<=lambda<=3t}.             (9)

If k>kappa, abort BEFORE querying the k true-band columns. By (8),
this abort cannot occur on the embedding event. Otherwise apply the
shared coupling-series algorithm. It needs raw width q+kJ_s<=R_cap;
all its affine couplings, actual true-band queries, formal-series
actions, Schur products and output reconstruction are charged in (4).

Every small inversion tests for singularity and aborts on an undefined
operation. On the embedding event and an accepted split, the proved
error/perturbation bounds ensure that these aborts do not occur. Work
uses the displayed fixed L,J_s even off that event; there is no
unbounded inaccurate iteration or uncharged recovery branch.

An abort returns arbitrary zero columns (or an equivalent failure flag).
The bad embedding and exhausted split attempts have total probability
at most delta/2, leaving slack in the stated 1-delta guarantee. All
other steps are deterministic exact arithmetic on those successful
events. The constants and proof in near-real-shared-coupling-series.txt
then give (3). Formula (4) follows from its complete cost and k<=kappa.

5. ORIGINAL EXPONENT BUDGET AND ITS LIMITS
-----------------------------------------
For N=m^(9/8), the research direction's feasible omega=9/4 convention,
q,kappa<=~sqrt(m), polynomial mu and epsilon^-1, and constant/inverse-
polynomial confidence, (4) is m^(9/4+o(1))=N^(2+o(1)). The postprocessing
terms are at most ~m^2 except q*kappa^omega, which is ~m^(13/8).
The raw term is Nm*(sqrt(m))^(1/4)=m^(9/4) up to logarithms. Every
normalized resolver-output entry is included. This is not an assertion
that an N-by-N matrix product has been formed at that cost.

For arbitrary product factors A,B use the fixed swap dilation
S=[[0,AB],[(AB)^T,0]] and X=diag(A,B^T), as in the imported raw model.
Its positive eigenvalues are the singular values of AB. Thus (1) has
an intrinsic product interpretation: at most kappa singular values
lie in [t/4,4t]. Constants in the doubled dimensions do not change the
exponents. The conclusion remains about supplied resolvent actions.

The intrinsic band may have size Theta(m). No unrestricted sublinear
bound is proved or assumed away. A sharp final filter, threshold
selection and rank-one extraction are also separate obligations.
Classical block Krylov may already exploit a small multiplicative band
for LRA, so this resolver theorem alone is not a new LRA SOTA claim.
The concrete improvement is a fully charged raw-access near-real solver
with an actual certified split and q+kJ_s shared work. Classical EVD,
Frobenius, Cauchy, series and Schur mechanisms are credited; historical
priority remains unverified. The full arbitrary-factor goal stays ACTIVE.
