THEOREM. Let C,D be independent uniform simple cubic graphs on even N.
There is a polynomial-time deterministic rule choosing a simple cross
perfect matching B AFTER observing C,D such that, with probability1-o(1),
  dim ker_R(A_B-2I)>=2,
and the graph has a second balanced(3,1)-equitable partition. An extra
eigenvector can have support2 floor(log logN).

Construction: let ell=floor(log logN). Choose a clean ell-cycle X in C,
with ell distinct outside third-neighbors U, and Y in D with boundary W.
Match X to W, U to Y, and all remaining vertices across arbitrarily.
Then zeta=(1_X,-1_Y) satisfies A_B zeta=2zeta: cycle vertices contribute
two, and each boundary vertex's internal and matching contributions cancel.
Thus sigma'=sigma-2zeta is another balanced sign vector of eigenvalue2.
Total graph degree4 turns this identity into same/cross degrees(3,1).
The new partition differs on2ell labels, so no macroscopic whp jump
claim is made.

STRONGER COROLLARY: ALL THREE FACTORS CAN BE PAIRWISE INDEPENDENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
Start with C,D independent uniform and independent uniform label
permutations pi,rho. Put C'=pi C pi^-1, D'=rho D rho^-1, choose the
deterministic adaptive matching m'=F(C',D'), and return
  m=rho^-1 m' pi.
Relabelling invariance makes(C',D') independent of(pi,rho), so m is
a uniform perfect matching marginally. More strongly, conditional on
(C,pi,D'), m' is fixed while rho remains uniform, so m is uniform
conditional on C. Symmetrically it is uniform conditional on D.
Hence C,D,B have exactly the ordinary RSBM one-factor and PAIRWISE
laws, while their joint dependence still forces multiplicity>=2 whp.

This is the sharp logical distinction needed in the positive statement:
  sup_FIXED B P_(C,D)(failure for B)=o(1)
is compatible with
  P_(C,D)(exists B causing failure)=1-o(1).
Theorem's B must be independent of the JOINT internal pair(C,D).
Pairwise independence or a uniform B marginal is not enough.
