1. SPECIALIZATION TO THE TWO-CLASS MODEL

Take the old degree frame Q and candidate quotient R both equal to

  [[a,b],[b,a]],       a>=0, b>=1, D=a+b>=3.

There are two old classes of size N, so alpha_+=alpha_-=1/2 and the
total vertex count in the source is n=2N. The positive cross degree
makes the old frame connected; the local entropy inequality does not
even require this connectedness. Internal zero and one slots are
explicitly allowed by both source statements.

Encode a candidate sign by two distinct real one-hot vectors, not
modular scalar signs. At every vertex, the exact integer equation is
sum_neighbor e_label = R_root,*; it determines any omitted physical
neighbor label from the root and the D-1 retained labels. This works
for every physical slot, including cross slots and when a=0.

If the balanced candidate differs from the plant in k vertices on
each old side, orient it so r=k/N<=1/2. Its two old-block root laws
are (1-r,r) and (r,1-r). The source's weighted point-map defect is
therefore exactly

  Delta(P)=sum_i alpha_i(1-max rho_i)=r.                 (A1)

This remains true at a=b, where the quotient has equal rows; the
source never assumes distinct candidate quotient rows or invertibility.

2. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE ALL-SLOT IDENTITY AND GAP

After symmetrizing only within each old-neighbor type, omission of
each physical slot t gives a conditional total correlation J_it.
Write E for the edge-weighted sum of pair mutual informations, with
each internal weight alpha_i a/2 and cross weight alpha_+ b=b/2.
The exact normalized entropy cancellation is

  L=-Phi=(D-2)E/D + (sum_i alpha_i sum_t J_it)/D.        (A2)

The factor one-half for internal pairing categories is correct.
No same-type slot is assumed to exist. The cancellation uses reverse
pair marginals and equal total degree, not a lower bound on a.

For a fixed old type i and omission t, compare its retained-star law
P_i^{-t} with the law Q_it retaining the actual root marginal but
making all retained neighbor labels independent with the corresponding
neighboring root marginals. Their divergence is

  kappa_it = J_it + sum_{s!=t} I(X_i;Y_is).

Consequently

  alpha_i sum_t kappa_it <= 2D(D-1)L/(D-2).              (A3)

Let mu_s be the one-hot mean of slot s and V_s=1-||mu_s||^2. On
retained data define W_t=R_root,*-sum_{s!=t} e_Ys and
f_t=||W_t-mu_t||^2. Under P, W_t is the omitted actual one-hot label,
so E_P f_t=V_t. Under Q,

  E_Q f_t=sum_{s!=t}V_s+B_i,
  B_i=E||R_root,*-sum_s mu_s||^2>=0.

Individual differences can be negative, but their sum is

  sum_t(E_Q f_t-E_P f_t)
     =(D-2)sum_s V_s + D B_i >= (D-2)sum_s V_s.         (A4)

This summed identity avoids the invalid requirement that each single
omission variance increase separately. On BOTH laws, even illegal
auxiliary tuples, the two vectors being compared are nonnegative
vectors of mass D. Hence 0<=f_t<=2D^2. Range-bounded total variation,
Pinsker, Cauchy--Schwarz, and (A3) give

  L >= alpha_i(D-2)^3 [sum_s V_s]^2/[4D^6(D-1)].        (A5)

Some old neighbor type has variance at least Delta(P); a positive
slot of some old type reaches it. Since alpha_i>=1/2, (A1)--(A5)
yield the source's valid specialization

  Phi <= -(D-2)^3 r^2/[8D^6(D-1)],
  Psi=2N Phi <= -N(D-2)^3 r^2/[4D^6(D-1)].              (A6)

Thus the exact candidate entropy per BLOCK size N has the second
coefficient in (A6), not the first. This normalization distinction
does not affect the claimed exponentially small probability.

3. FROM THE ENTROPY GAP TO THE NEEDED PROBABILITY ORDER

The second source supplies an exact labeled-stub type count: root
and ordered-star multinomials, one factorial/double-factorial ratio
for each active internal pool, and one factorial ratio for the cross
pool. Root labels are already included in the star multinomial;
there is no extra root-label factor.

For the two one-hot labels, a legal ordered star has at most 2^D
possibilities: root and D-1 retained labels determine the omitted
one uniquely. Thus the number of empirical types is polynomial in N,
with an exponent depending only on D. Uniform zero-safe factorial
bounds also have a polynomial prefactor; they apply to boundary
types and absent/internal-one factors. The source's final paragraph
explicitly permits the exact integer one-hot equation independently
of its finite-field moment application.

For r in [epsilon/2,1/2], (A6) therefore bounds the expected number
of candidates by poly_D(N) exp(-c_{D,epsilon}N). Markov bounds the
probability of ANY such candidate by the same expression. This is
o(N^{-M}) for every fixed M, hence in particular
o(N^{-nu-1}) in each proposed parameter regime.

All three factor simplicity probabilities have a positive limit:
internal a=0 is absent; a=1 is a perfect matching and automatically
simple; active higher internal degrees use the usual loop/double-edge
limit. The cross factor is b-biregular, with double-edge mean
(b-1)^2/2. The exact limit is

  s_infty = exp(-I_a -(b-1)^2/2),
  I_a=0 for a=0 or 1;
  I_a=(a^2-1)/2 for a>=2.                              (A7)

Conditioning costs only a fixed positive reciprocal and preserves the
exponential macro bound. In particular one must NOT substitute a=0
into -(a^2-1)/2; that would give an incorrect factor exp(1/2).

For small supports, the same no-unique-outside-neighbor exposure
argument remains valid: absent pools are skipped, and every active
typed pool has Theta(N) stubs when h<=epsilon N. With m the least
balanced flip count (t or s), the next possible support size is 2m+2.
The resulting exponent is

  -(D-2)(m+1) = -nu-(D-2) <= -nu-1.                   (A8)

The D=3,s=1 case has nu=1, but (A8) still gives the required N^-2
remainder. The macro and small-support bounds therefore have exactly
the precision needed for the proposed leading ambiguity asymptotic.

4. NEGATIVE-DELTA LEADING PATTERN AND RESIDUAL SIMPLICITY

Let s=b-a>=1. A size-s support on each side must have no within-side
support edge and all s^2 cross support edges, by equality in
-s=internal_support-cross_support>=-s. The support graph is K_(s,s).
Every center then uses a internal and a cross edges to the outside.
For a leading pattern, W_+,W_- each have size as and each outside
vertex has one internal and one cross support neighbor. Thus all four
boundary maps have a preimages per center, exactly as in the candidate.

Each internal factor prescribes as edges. The cross factor prescribes
s^2+2as=sD edges. After prescribing physical stubs, the residual totals
are

  L_int=aN-2as,
  L_cross=bN-sD.                                        (A9)

For a>=1, internal residual degrees are 0 on s centers, a-1 on as
outside vertices, and a elsewhere. Cross residual degrees are 0 on
s centers, b-1 on as outside vertices, and b elsewhere. These lists
are identical up to labels for all boundary functions and physical
stub assignments. Every prescribed edge touches a saturated center,
so no prescribed edge has two residual-positive endpoints. Full
simplicity is exactly residual-factor simplicity, with no forbidden
additional parallel-edge correction.

For a>=1 and a growing residual internal pool,

  sum r(r-1)/L_int = (a-1)(1-as/L_int) -> a-1.

For the cross residual pool, on each side,

  sum r(r-1)/L_cross = (b-1)-as(b-1)/L_cross -> b-1.

Bounded-degree loop/double-edge factorial moments therefore give the
same limit (A7) for original and residual factors. Their ratio tends
to 1. Uniformity over patterns is immediate because the residual
degree lists, and hence their residual simplicity probabilities,
are exactly the same finite-N quantities up to relabeling.

At a=0 the internal factors and outside sets are absent. Here s=b;
the prescribed motif is an isolated K_(b,b), leaving a uniform
b-regular bipartite configuration on N-b vertices per side. Its
simplicity probability has the same positive limit as the original.
At a=1, all internal prescribed edges remove 2s matched vertices per
side and leave a perfect matching, automatically simple. Admissibility
has N even in this case, and N-2s remains even. No additional parity
restriction appears in (A9).

5. ZERO DELTA AND CHECK OF THE CONSTANTS

If a=b, D>=3 forces a=b>=2. Then t=1, and the positive-delta proof's
two cliques are singletons. The leading underlying motif is K_(2,2b),
with two center vertices of degree D and 2b outside vertices of degree
two. It is connected with minimum degree two. Its excess is D-2,
and the proposed constant reduces to (a!)^2. No zero internal pool
or zero-to-zero exponent needs interpretation in this case.

For s=b-a>0, the denominator leading powers are a^{2as} from the two
internal factors and b^{s^2+2as} from the cross factor. With physical
stub numerator (a!b!)^{2s}(ab)^{2as}, their product leaves
(a!b!)^{2s} b^{-s^2}. Multiplying the boundary-data count gives

  C=((as)!)^2 (b!)^{2s}/[(s!)^2(a!)^{2s}b^{s^2}].       (A10)

For a=0 use (A10) directly: it gives
(b!)^{2b-2}/b^{b^2}, without introducing 0^0. The checks
(a,b)=(1,2): C=2,nu=1; (1,3): C=16,nu=4; and
(2,2): C=4,nu=2 all agree.

All these leading motifs have center degree D>=3, other vertices
degree two, are connected, and have positive excess nu>=1. The
proper-intersection factorial second-moment argument still loses
at least one power of N; disjoint pairs have order N^{-2nu}, which
is O(N^{-nu-1}) even at nu=1. Boundary collisions are empty at a=0
or a=1, because outside balance cannot have multiplicity exceeding
the internal degree. At other parameters they lose at least one
outside vertex exactly as in the earlier argument.

No correction to the proposed constants or exponents was found in
this audit. The all-slot source above is the actual replacement for
the old deleted-internal-slot proof, whose assumption a>=2 should
not be reused for a=0 or 1.
