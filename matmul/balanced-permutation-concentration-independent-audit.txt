1. STATEMENT, MODEL, AND SCOPE
=============================
The source constructs the actual averaged balanced-partition estimator
C=A Wbar B. It proves absolute spectral error epsilon||A||||B|| with
failure at most delta for supplied r>=max(1,sr(A),sr(B)). It does not
claim relative low-rank product error (1+theta)sigma_(k+1)(AB).

The row count is O(r epsilon^-2 log^3((r+n+p)/(delta epsilon))) and the
number of partition colors is O(epsilon^-1 log^3). Each incidence H_c
has one unsigned bucket assignment per input coordinate. However the
actual Gram factor has a deterministic dense centering correction.
The source correctly calls this sparse plus rank one, and does not
assert literal d-sparsity or a purely nonnegative embedding matrix.

All factors are fixed before the randomness. The supplied stable-rank
bound is a real hypothesis, not a free input norm oracle or an optimistic
choice that could be substituted without verification. Zero factors
return zero. Exact arithmetic, comparisons, square-root scaling and
uniform random permutations suffice for the described implementation.

Corollary 10.3 on printed page 933 (PDF page 28) has exactly the

  diag((||U||_F^2/s)(I_s-J_s),(1-1/s)U^T U).

This follows from sum_j a_j a_j^T=q(I_s-J_s) and
sum_j||a_j||^2=m(1-1/s). Thus it is bounded by identity when s>=r.
The permuted sum is the dilation of H U because sum_i u_i=0.

second tail or an extra factor two is not needed.

4. TRUNCATION BIAS, VARIANCE, AND THE FINAL TAIL
==============================================
With h=log(8192Dd/(delta epsilon)) and R=513h^2, the exact tail integral
plus R Pr(||X||>R) gives

  E[||X||1_(||X||>R)]
   <=513D exp(-2h)(h^2+h+1/2)
   <=1283D h^2 exp(-2h)
   <=delta epsilon/(4d).

The source's use of h^2 exp(-h)<=1 is valid for h>=1. This is a norm
bias bound independent of the ambient deterministic sketch range.
The probability that any of the d samples is trimmed is at most
dD exp(-2h)<=delta/4.

After self-adjoint dilation, Y=X1_good satisfies
E[(Y-EY)^2]=EY^2-(EY)^2<=EY^2<=EX^2. This is a valid Loewner identity
for Hermitian dilations, including nonsymmetric rectangular X before
dilation. Thus trimming and recentering preserve variance at most V
and give summand norm at most 2R.

The same primary PDF's Corollary 5.2 on printed page 922 (PDF page 17)
gives independent mean-zero matrix Bernstein with denominator
3 sigma^2+2Rt. Substitution of sigma^2<=dV, R_summand=2R, and
t=d epsilon/2 gives precisely

  (n+p) exp[-d epsilon^2/(12V+8R epsilon)].

The sufficient degree inequality (17) therefore makes this at most
delta/4. Adding the trimming event and the bias bound proves the
claimed original UNTRIMMED estimator guarantee. The algorithm never
needs to compute realized norms, trim samples, or estimate the bias.

5. EXPLICIT CONSTANTS, PADDING, AND COMPLETE COST
===============================================
The C_0=2^20 proof is conservative but correct. For
L=log(C_0D/(delta epsilon)), one has L>13.8,
d<=2C_0 epsilon^-1 L^3, log d<3L, h<4L, and
log(4(n+p)/delta)<=L. Therefore the sufficient RHS (17) is at most

  epsilon^-1(108+65664L^2)L < C_0 epsilon^-1 L^3.

The ceiling in d only increases the available degree. The hypotheses
0<epsilon,delta<1 keep every logarithm positive. Integer parameter
selection can be done by comparisons and conservative scalar series
without a unit-cost logarithm or floor oracle.

For s=ceil(1+r/epsilon), s-1>=r/epsilon as required. If s<m but m is
not divisible by s, zero padding to M=s ceil(m/s)<m+s<2m leaves the
factor norms and stable ranks unchanged. The proof then uses M, and
the physical output dimensions remain n,p. If s>=m, exact factors A,B
are an admissible fallback with width m<=s=O(r/epsilon), within the
displayed row bound.

Each color is applied by bucket sums plus mean correction. A fixed
Householder reflection on s bucket coordinates removes the redundant
centered row in O(s) work per data column. Sharing the constant channel
therefore gives width 1+d(s-1), without a dense s-by-s transform or
hidden orthogonalization. The stated factor cost O(dm(n+p)+ds(n+p))
and explicit expansion O(np[1+d(s-1)]) count all factor entries and
output arithmetic. The theorem is not a fixed-rank N^2 theorem with
logarithms omitted.

6. PRIMARY PRIOR-ART COMPARISON AND REMAINING OBJECTIVE
====================================================
Cohen--Nelson--Woodruff, Optimal approximate matrix product in terms of
stable rank, ICALP 2016, arXiv:1507.02268v3, already establishes spectral
AMM at epsilon||A||||B|| with optimal stable-rank row scaling, including
O(r/epsilon^2) rows for suitable oblivious maps and black-box transfer
from OSE moment guarantees. The primary abstract, theorem/application
discussion and definitions were inspected:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.02268
  https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2016.11
Consequently this note's stable-rank dependence or absolute AMM error
guarantee cannot itself be claimed new or asymptotically superior.

Diar Heidary, SparseStack Is an Optimal Oblivious Subspace Embedding,
arXiv:2609.02978v2, September 2026, states in Theorem 1.1 an OSE with
optimal rows O((r+log(1/delta))/epsilon^2) and
O(log(r/delta)/epsilon) literal nonzeros per column. The full primary
PDF's construction, statement, assumptions and scope were inspected:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02978
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02978
Its matrix stacks fully independent signed CountSketch blocks, with
independent bucket choices and Rademacher signs. This is a different
distribution from uniform balanced unsigned partitions with a common
centering channel. Its stated row and sparsity bounds are stronger than
the present log-cubed bounds. This audit does not independently verify
that paper's entire proof or its reported Lean artifact. Its explicit
primary theorem nevertheless rules out presenting the present parameter
tradeoff as an unqualified new state-of-the-art bound.

The current work proves a useful construction-specific concentration
theorem and removes a real gap from the prior balanced-sketch candidate.
Its exact covariance and lack of independent random signs distinguish
its distribution. No historical firstness of this precise distribution
or proof is certified. Further novelty needs an actual guarantee or
cost improvement beyond matched existing results. In particular the
SG-11/14/16 sharp high-order weighted pattern sum remains unproved here.
