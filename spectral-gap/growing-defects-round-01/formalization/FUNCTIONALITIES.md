# Formalization capabilities

- Checks an explicit real matrix of arbitrary finite size with one central coordinate and equally coupled defect coordinates.
- Proves its two bright eigenvectors, excludes every other nonzero eigenvalue, and characterizes the dark kernel by a zero central coordinate and zero defect sum.
- Specializes the coupling to obtain the exact effective eigenvalues plus and minus 3/sqrt(8).
- Proves the projected-resolvent polynomial identity and its marked Schur compression while retaining the stationary correction.
- Encodes actual coordinate-selection matrices for distinct marked labels and verifies their isometry and stationary compression.
- Constructs the centered inverse from the invertible matrix dI-A+P, proves its two inverse equations and centering, and applies the marked identity at z=d using constant row and column sums.
- Recompiles source-only proof files using a pinned existing dependency cache, with bounded resources, source hashes and an explicit axiom audit.

The random-graph estimates, shrinking-window approximation, inertia argument and growing-dimensional root ordering are not formalized here.
