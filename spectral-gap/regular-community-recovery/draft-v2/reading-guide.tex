{\LARGE Reading guide: unknown-frame recovery}\\[3pt]
{\large Sparse regular graphs, certified algorithms, and sharp ambiguity}\\
November 15, 2026. Companion to \texttt{regular-community-recovery.pdf}.

\textbf{The leading result.}
Given only a graph $A$ and fixed bounds $K,D$, recover the hidden classes,
their sizes, and their degree frame $Q$. The true frame is connected and
reversible, with at most $K$ types and maximum degree $D$. Internal regular
and cross biregular factors are independent and uniform simple. Sizes
may be unequal, total degrees may vary, and internal factors may vanish.
The hypothesis is the existence of symmetric weights on present factors:
\[
 q_{ij}=q_{ji},\qquad 0\le q_{ij}<\tfrac12,\qquad
 \sum_jd_{ij}q_{ij}=1.
\]
Weights belong to physical slots, including internal slots. They are
not supplied to the algorithm, and zero weights are allowed. Every
admissible size with existing simple factors is covered.

With failure $O_{K,D}(n^{-(d_{\min}-2)})$, every equitable partition into at
most $K$ classes coarsens the planted partition. Thus the plant is the
unique finest one. The algorithm searches all bounded integer quotients,
checks $AX=XR$ exactly, and keeps a partition with the largest class count.
It receives neither $Q$ nor $k$, and imposes no candidate-size promise.

\begin{tabular}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.31\textwidth}
                >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.59\textwidth}}
\textbf{Algorithmic guarantee} & \textbf{Meaning}\\ \hline
Expected $\widetilde O(n^2)$ bit work
& Always-correct Las Vegas complete listing on every input; expectation
includes rare graphs with large kernels.\\[3pt]
Expected $\widetilde O(n)$ working bits; expected $O(n)$ output bits
& Stream partitions and reuse workspace across the fixed quotient family.\\[3pt]
Deterministic $\widetilde O(n^3+n^{d_{\min}})$ work
& Binary elimination plus a size cap; its extra failure
$O(n^{-(d_{\min}-1)})$ preserves the structural rate.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here $d_{\min}$ is the observed minimum degree, necessarily at least three.
The bounds are for fixed $K,D$; $\widetilde O$ suppresses logarithmic
bit factors. Expected nearly quadratic work is not a worst-case
quadratic claim.

\textbf{Why the fast bound is justified.}
For every fixed prime, vector dimension, and matrix target $R$,
the actual configuration and simple laws satisfy
$\sup_n\E|\{Y:AY=YR\}|<\infty$. The proof covers every affine-coset
entropy phase and controls sparse defects together with their bulk
kernel. A certified sparse algorithm computes the complete right
kernel of $I\otimes A-R^T\otimes I$, using symmetric doubling.
It enumerates only the right kernel in incremental order, so the
single bounded moment suffices. Integer checks reject modular artifacts.
A deterministic certificate verifies kernel completeness.

\textbf{Covered models and the boundary.}
Every common degree $d\ge3$ has certificate $q_{ij}=1/d$.
All connected frames with internal degrees at least two are also covered,
including varying total degrees. Further examples have no internal edges
and mixed degrees, or have factors on which every certificate is zero.

A fixed connected simple base satisfying the certificate has its hidden
fibers and base recovered from independent uniform random lifts at every
positive lift size. For all simple \emph{regular} bases of degree at least
three, including bipartite ones, old eigenvalues also retain their exact
multiplicities with high probability, including irrational modes.

Degree two differs statistically. For an unconditioned lift of known
$C_k$, $k\ge3$, with uniformly hidden fibers, optimal success is
\[
 \frac{2k\,\Gamma(N+1/(2k))}
 {\Gamma(1/(2k))\Gamma(N+1)}
 \asymp N^{-1+1/(2k)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Conditioning the lift connected changes that model and gives unique
supplied-base fibers.

{\Large Two-class constants, spectral scope, and reading routes}

\textbf{Every fixed two-class parameter pair.}
There are two classes of size $N$, independent uniform simple
$a$-regular internal graphs and a $b$-regular cross factor, with
$a\ge0$, $b\ge1$, $a+b\ge3$, and $n=2N$. Every admissible $N$ is covered.
All valid $(a,b)$ partitions can be listed in expected
$\widetilde O_{a,b}(n^2)$ bit work. The pair $a,b$ can be inferred from $A$ with high probability.
For $a\ge3,b\ge1$ and for $(2,1)$, the runtime also holds over any prescribed
simple cross graph independent of the internals. No real-corank-one or numerical eigengap promise is required.

For the following sharp statistical constants, the cross graph is
\emph{independent uniform simple}, and the balanced plant is hidden
uniformly. Put
\[
 \delta=a-b,\quad
 m=\begin{cases}\delta+1,&\delta\ge0,\\-\delta,&\delta<0,\end{cases}
 \qquad\nu=(a+b-2)m.
\]
The leading constant is
\[
 C_{a,b}=\begin{cases}
 \dfrac{((bt)!)^2(a!)^{2t}}{(t!)^2(b!)^{2t}a^{t\delta}},
       &\delta\ge0,\ t=\delta+1,\\[4pt]
 \dfrac{((as)!)^2(b!)^{2s}}{(s!)^2(a!)^{2s}b^{s^2}},
       &\delta<0,\ s=-\delta.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then
\[
 \Prob(\text{at least two valid partitions})\sim C_{a,b}N^{-\nu},
 \qquad R_N^{\rm Bayes}\sim\tfrac12C_{a,b}N^{-\nu}.
\]
At least three possibilities have probability $O(N^{-\nu-1})$.
The posterior on valid unordered partitions is exactly uniform, so
returning any member of the complete list attains the exact Bayes
risk. A deterministic binary cap at $n^{\nu+1}$ also attains its
leading constant, with bounded $O(n^3+n^{\nu+2})$ elementary work.
For $(2,1)$ ambiguity and Bayes error are $4/N^2$ and $2/N^2$;
for $(1,2)$ they are $2/N$ and $1/N$.
They use \emph{block} size $N$, not total size $n$.

For base $K_{d+1}$, noncoarsening ambiguity is
$c_dN^{-(d-2)}(1+o(1))$, with $c_d=\binom{d+1}{2}$.
With that base known and fibers
uniformly hidden, optimal Bayes error has half this constant.
These asymptotics survive connected conditioning.

\textbf{The separate prescribed-cross spectral theorem.}
A supplied connected symmetric frame with equal class sizes and
internal degrees at least three has exactly its old quotient
eigenspaces with high probability, uniformly over prescribed simple
cross factors independent of the internals. Every admissible size is
allowed. Exact rational computation of $\ker m_Q(A)$, followed by
grouping equal rows, recovers the classes, including repeated and
irrational quotient modes. The cubic two-class pair is also covered.
This theorem gives qualitative real-collision probability, not the
sharp Boolean ambiguity rate above.

Unknown-frame recovery does not require this spectral conclusion.
The paper gives a reversible cubic frame with sizes $(N,3N,2N)$
that forces a new zero eigenvalue on every realization and still
falls under the recovery theorem.

\textbf{Suggested reading routes.}

\begin{itemize}
\item For the main result, read the overview, unknown-frame theorem,
bounded channel moment, and certified vector algorithm.
\item For limits of identifiability, read the complete-base/cycle
boundary and exact two-class ambiguity sections.
\item For spectral statements, read both lift sections and
the frame theorem for prescribed cross graphs. The appendix on
configuration mixing supplies its uniform cross-factor input.
\item For implementation guarantees, read the full-kernel certificate,
right-kernel projection, and binary truncation proofs.
\end{itemize}

constraints; no growing-parameter or numerical-stability conclusion
