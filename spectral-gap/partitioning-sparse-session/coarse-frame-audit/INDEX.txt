Precise mathematical conclusion
------------------------------
Fix supplied constants K>=2 and D>=3. The true frame Q has 2<=k<=K
nonempty planted classes, connected off-diagonal support, internal degrees
d_ii>=2, and total degrees at most D. Class sizes obey edge balance and
all necessary parity conditions. Sample all internal regular and active
cross biregular factors independently and uniformly among simple graphs.

Along every admissible size sequence, with probability tending to one,
EVERY equitable partition into at most K nonempty classes is a compatible
coarsening of the planted partition. The planted partition is therefore
the unique finest partition within that bounded class family. The number
k, the partition, and Q are recoverable without knowing them beforehand.

This is not a claim that the planted partition is globally finest among
all equitable partitions: the partition into n singletons always exists.

Proof and algorithm
-------------------
1. The exact ordered-star factorial count, uniform factorial remainder,
   within-type slot symmetrization, and weighted entropy cancellation
   prove a polynomial expectation for the number of binary intertwiners
   AY=YR, for every fixed candidate R of order at most K.
2. For integer one-hot candidates the real variance argument strengthens
   this to the explicit normalized entropy gap
      Phi <= -alpha_min*delta^2/(2D^4),
   where delta is the fraction of rows changed to the nearest old-block
   point map. This covers d_ii=2 without assuming root/internal-neighbor
   independence. It excludes all macroscopic alternative partitions.
3. The arbitrary-support witness count excludes every small nonzero row
   support of AV=VR simultaneously for all R and all vector dimensions.
   An unchanged closed neighborhood in every old class gives QT=TR
   exactly before that support argument is applied. This closes small
   defects, including candidates with tiny or unused labels.
4. Enumerate all bounded integer candidate frames R and their complete
   binary kernels; retain integer-verified nonempty one-hot solutions
   with the largest number of classes. On the common rigidity event,
   every solution is Y=JT, so the largest class count identifies Q and k.

An explicit conservative exponent is
   F=K+[K(K+3)/2]*2^(2K),
   B=K*2^(KD)+F.
The unconditional expected bit work is O_(K,D)(n^(B+3)). A deterministic
n^(B+5) bit-operation cap is interrupted with probability O_(K,D)(n^-2).
The capped algorithm MUST return abstention if interrupted. It has
worst-case polynomial work and high-probability correct recovery.
This is a mathematical algorithm specification, not a benchmarked or
practical implementation; its displayed exponent is enormous.

Finite corroboration
--------------------

check the factorial count in seven cases. They include vector F_2^2
labels with a Jordan target, odd 3+3 sizes, and unequal 2+4 sizes.
rigidity/check-rigidity.js and rigidity-checks.json check every partition
of two explicit graphs (203 and 21147 set partitions), including unequal
proportions and internal degree two. These finite checks corroborate the
written identities; they are not premises of the asymptotic proof.

Scope and limitations
---------------------
The theorem uses RANDOM INDEPENDENT cross factors. It does not assert
the same conclusion for arbitrary chosen cross matrices. K and D are
fixed bounds. There is no growing-K, noisy-edit, spectral simplicity,
numerical-stability, or practical-runtime claim. The connected k>=2
assumption ensures total minimum degree at least three; k=1 with internal
degree two has genuine cycle obstructions.

Historical priority is not certified. The closest inspected primary
comparisons are recorded in ../PRIORITY_REVIEW.txt and Section 6 of the
synthesis. In particular, efficient noiseless detection is already known;
the mathematical claim audited here is exact bounded-frame recovery.
