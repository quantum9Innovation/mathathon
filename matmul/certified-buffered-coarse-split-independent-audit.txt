1. EXACT COARSE TRANSFORM AND CERTIFICATES
=========================================
The algebra R=2tK(K^2+t^2I)^-1=2H(I+H^2)^-1, H=tK^-1,
is valid for every invertible Hermitian K. Both matrices commute and
are Hermitian. The scalar maxima give ||R||<=1 and ||HR||<=2.
Forming them uses explicit coarse products/inverses only, with no T
query. Ill conditioning of those exact core inversions is explicitly
outside the finite-word claim, rather than hidden in a precision bound.

The two tests use exact squared Frobenius sums of computable coarse
matrix residuals. They imply operator residuals <=xi and invertibility
of U. Writing its unitary polar factor W for analysis only gives

  ||U-W||<=xi,
  ||Lambda||<2,
  ||R-W Lambda W*||<=6xi.

For the second inequality, ||Lambda|| is bounded by
(1-xi)^-1(||R||+xi). For the third, expand the two occurrences of U
and use ||U||<=sqrt(1+xi). Thus all constants used subsequently follow
from the exact certificates; no correct random trajectory is needed
after acceptance. The algorithm never computes W.

I checked the scalar-model paragraph rather than treating Shah's

The Gaussian primitive in Shah's Definition 1.4 has a probability-one

Taylor approximation to the Gaussian CDF on this range can have
absolute error exp(-C'b), with C' as large a fixed constant as needed.
The density there is bounded below by exp(-O(b)), so quantile inversion
and dyadic sampling supply relative error smaller than Shah's rounding

Independently repeated attempts therefore have constant expected count.

3. GREY-BAND GAP, EXACT QR AND OPERATOR SUBSPACE CONTROL
=====================================================
The interval [7/10,3/4] has length 1/20. At most m diagonal values
split it into at most m+1 gaps. A largest-gap midpoint is at distance
at least g=1/[40(m+1)] from EVERY diagonal value, including those
outside the interval. Thus appending endpoints and sorting supplies
the claimed computable separation even when the interval contains
no diagonal entries.

The unitary surrogate differs from R by e<=6xi<g/4. Weyl's inequality
therefore preserves the selected rank k above c. If W_P contains the
selected columns of the analysis-only W, the cross projection
X=(I-P_*)W_P satisfies a Sylvester equation. Its ordered spectra
are separated by at least 2g-e: selected Lambda>=c+g and the R
complement has spectrum <=c-g+e. The exponential integral formula
therefore gives the OPERATOR norm bound ||X||<=e/(2g-e), without a
dimension or Frobenius loss. The ordering of the two spectral sets
is essential and is present here.

For the actual columns U_P, their smallest singular value is at least
sqrt(1-xi); their difference from W_P is <=xi. Exact thin QR preserves
their span. Combining these statements bounds its cross projection
by theta=16xi/g, with ample slack. Equal-rank orthogonal projections
then differ in norm by this maximum sine angle. The source correctly
uses exact QR rather than assuming that its analysis polar factor
is available algorithmically.

The selected spectrum is positive and satisfies
2(lambda/t)/(1+(lambda/t)^2)>c>=7/10. The scalar transform equals
3/5 at lambda/t=1/3 and 3; its selected interval is strictly inside
[1/3,3]. The rank bound in the statement is consequently valid. This
does not assume a spectral gap in K; the gap was chosen in the
candidate diagonal values by an explicit grey-band procedure.

4. FILTERED WEIGHTED LEAKAGE AND SAFE COMPRESSION
===============================================
Using A0 alone would not bound K^-1 leakage. The additional Y=R A0
step is necessary. Its projection onto P_* has smallest singular
value >=(7/10)sqrt(1-theta^2)>.69, so Y is full column rank.
An exact QR factor V_P=Y C^-1 has ||C^-1||<=2. Commutation of H,R
with P_*, together with ||R||<=1 and ||HR||<=2, gives

  ||(I-P_*)V_P||<=2theta,

On P_* the Hermitian H is positive with norm <3. Consequently the

  ||QHV_P||<=6theta+4theta=10theta,
  ||V_P*HV_P||<=3+4theta<4

follow even when ||H|| is arbitrarily large. With the given xi,g,
theta<=3.2e-10 for m>=1, so 10theta<1e-5 by a wide margin.
Completing the exact orthonormal basis and forming H-blocks uses
only explicit coarse algebra. No true Gram action is hidden there.

I independently checked the safe-complement residual contradiction.
For c<=3/4, the complement's H spectrum lies outside

  [(4-sqrt(7))/3,(4+sqrt(7))/3].

Its distance from [1/2,2] exceeds 1/25. An eigenvector v of QHQ

5. COMPLEX AFFINE PENCIL AND VERTICAL-POLE ERROR CONSTANTS
========================================================
The Woodbury identity for M(u)=(1+u)H-T is correct in the supported
coordinates. On |u|<=1/3 and h outside [1/2,2],

  |(1+u)h-1|>=1/3.

This follows separately for h<=0, 0<h<1/2 and h>2 by the reverse
triangle inequality. Thus the safe base inverse has norm <=3;
perturbation by ||D||<=1/8 gives contraction <=3/8 and inverse
norm <=3/(1-3/8)=24/5. Offblocks obey
eta+(4/3)*1e-5<1/4. These are valid on the FULL complex disk,
including its real points. In a complex basis B(u)=A(conj(u))*;
the source explicitly keeps the two affine coefficient families
separate instead of applying a false same-u adjoint identity.

For vertical u=iy/t WITHIN the disk, the scalar safe inverse improves
to <=2 and contraction to <=1/4. Its inverse norm is <=8/3. The
full M inverse is bounded by 3mu, as in the predecessor raw note.
I repeated its Schur perturbation with offblock bound a=1/4,
beta=8/3, g<=3mu and h0=1+a beta=5/3. If delta<=1/mu, then
g a^2 delta<=3/16<1/2 and the approximate Schur inverse exists.
The P-error is at most

  delta[2g^2 a^2 h0+2ga]||b||
     <=(27/8)mu^2 delta||b||.

The Q-error is at most

  delta[1+a g h0]||b||+(beta+delta)a*(P-error)
    <=(171/32)mu^2 delta||b||.

Their sum is <=(279/32)mu^2 delta||b||<9mu^2 delta||b||.
Multiplying by ||Z||^2<=9/8 gives normalized physical error less
than 12mu^2 delta||h||. Thus delta<=epsilon/(32mu^2) is sufficient
in the inherited regime 0<epsilon<=1; it also implies delta<=1/mu.
The source's O(log(mu/epsilon)) refinement count follows. No
offblock tolerance shrinking as y approaches zero is needed.

Outside |y|<t/4 the original fixed-cone raw solver applies, because
|Im w|/|w|>=1/sqrt(17)>eta. The clarified disk restriction prevents
