1. PRECISE MODEL AND QUANTIFIERS

Fix a connected reversible integer frame D=(d_ij), fixed positive integer
multipliers a_i, and n_i=a_i N, with a_i d_ij=a_j d_ji. Independent internal
stub pairings and cross-stub bijections give the typed configuration law.
An internal loop contributes two adjacency incidences. Admissibility
requires internal pool parity. Absent factors impose no artificial
parity condition. All local degrees and the number of types are fixed.

On every active factor, including internal factors, assume symmetric
0<q_ij<1/2 and sum_j d_ij q_ij=1. These are physical-slot weights: no
extra factor one half enters q_ii. Necessarily every local d_i>=3.
A rational certificate exists whenever a real strict one does: a rational
affine solution space has dense rational points, and the inequalities
are relatively open. Neither graph sampling nor the algorithm needs
the certificate as input.

The claimed bounded moment fixes p,r before N and allows every matrix
target R over Fp. Uniformity in R is harmless because that family is
finite. The enlarged induction class allows arbitrary type-dependent
linear maps, one common vector alphabet for nonbipartite support, and
two possibly different side alphabets for bipartite support. The latter
extension is essential; a common-alphabet-only induction would not close.

2. THE WEIGHTED ENTROPY AND ITS STRICT EQUALITY CASE

The exact type count has one star-profile multinomial per vertex type,
cross pooled factors prod m_ij!/(n_i d_ij)!, and internal symmetric-table
double-factorial factors. Root labels are counted once. Symmetric internal
tables retain even diagonal. Uniform Stirling bounds include zero cells.
The actual exponent per N is

 Phi=sum_i a_i H(P_i)
     -sum_(i<j) a_i d_ij H(nu_ij)
     -sum_i (a_i d_ii/2)H(nu_ii).

Symmetrize only slots aimed at the same old type. This preserves legal
sums and pooled tables. Entropy can only increase; strict concavity
implies that zero symmetrization gain means the original star law was
already invariant under those permutations.

Omitting a physical slot loses no information once the root and retained
neighbors are given, because that neighbor is determined with coefficient
one. Averaging its chain rule with q_it gives

 H(P_i)=(2-d_i)H(mu_i)
       +sum_j d_ij(1-q_ij)H(nu_ij)-sum_t q_it J_it.

On substitution, reversibility and q_ij=q_ji produce the coefficient
1-2q_ij on a cross pooled pair entropy and the same coefficient with
the internal half-edge weight on an internal entropy. Replacing pair
entropy by its two marginal entropies minus mutual information leaves
the coefficient a_i[2-2sum_j d_ij q_ij]=0 at each root. Thus

 Phi=-sum_(i<=j) w_ij(1-2q_ij)I_ij
     -sum_i a_i sum_(physical t) q_it J_it,

with w_ij=a_i d_ij cross and w_ii=a_i d_ii/2 internal. There is no
missing factor two or assumption of common total degree.

Strict positivity of EVERY active q and EVERY 1-2q forces every pair
mutual information and every omission total correlation to vanish at
zero entropy. This includes repeated slots and internal slots. The
same conclusion holds before symmetrization. The argument uses real
entropy coefficients and divides no field element by a degree or q.

3. COMPLETE ZERO PHASES AND THE CLOSED INDUCTION CLASS

At a zero, every neighbor law is independent of the root, and conditional
on each root every retained d_i-1 tuple has its product law. For d_i>=3,
the finite-group reflection argument makes all neighbor laws uniform on
cosets of one common additive subgroup. Here these subgroups are exactly
Fp-linear subspaces. One can condition all but two coordinates: the
remaining marginals stay unchanged and are reflected translates; varying
a third coordinate gives translation invariance by every support
difference. This proves uniformity on the full coset, including the
zero subspace case.

Matching feasibility identifies neighbor laws with root laws at the
corresponding old types. Hence subspaces agree along old even walks.
Connected nonbipartite support gives one H; bipartite support gives H_L
and H_R. A positive internal factor is an odd self-incidence, regardless
of its multiplicity. Repeated slots do not create extra parity components.

The remaining conditions are precisely T_i(H_i) subset H_neighbor and
T_i(c_i)-sum_j d_ij c_j in H_neighbor. Addition of copies of a subspace
has that same subspace as its range even when a degree is zero modulo p.
The resulting legal-star law is uniquely uniform on its affine legal
support. Thus there are finitely many phases and every proper phase
lowers the common dimension, or the sum of the two side dimensions.
Restrictions retain the same frame, certificate, and induction class.

This phase argument applies also in characteristic two. Odd characteristic
is needed later for the exact scalar congruence calculation, not for the
bounded vector-moment theorem.

4. EXACT CENTRAL NORMALIZER: PHYSICAL BIPARTITE BALANCE

At type i let V_i be the root space and U_i its common neighbor space.
Legal stars P_i have |V_i||U_i|^(d_i-1) equiprobable choices. Independent
stars Q_i choose all coordinates independently. With the exact pairing
weight W and K_N=prod_i[|V_i||U_i|^(d_i-1)]^n_i, direct counting gives

 E Z=K_N E_P W,
 K_N E_Q W=prod_i(|V_i|/|U_i|)^n_i.

The last factor is one for a common alphabet. For two side alphabets of
sizes u,v it is (u/v)^(n_L-n_R), which cannot be discarded without proof.
The omission certificate gives EXACTLY

 sum_(i in L)n_i
 =sum_(i in L,j in R)n_i d_ij q_ij
 =sum_(j in R,i in L)n_j d_ji q_ji
 =sum_(j in R)n_j.

This is physical vertex balance, not equality of the number of old types
or equality of their local degrees. It survives every side-alphabet
restriction, including a singleton alphabet. Thus K_N E_Q W=1 in both
induction classes. The normalized weighted Q law is exactly independent
configuration factors followed by iid uniform root labels.

5. CENTRAL COVARIANCE, MAXIMAL LATTICE, AND WEIGHTED TAILS

Retain separate tables for EVERY physical slot, pooling only in W.
At type i their real common-row direction space has dimension
(|V_i|-1)+d_i|V_i|(|U_i|-1). Under P_i and Q_i the covariance is

 Sigma_i=(|V_i||U_i|)^(-1)[Id+(d_i-1)Pi_row,i].

Given the root, any two legal neighbors are independent uniform because
d_i>=3 leaves another slot to absorb the equation. The eigenvalues are
d_i/(|V_i||U_i|) on common-row modes and 1/(|V_i||U_i|) on the remaining
modes, positive on the actual span. Singleton spaces remove modes;
they do not introduce a singular covariance on a retained direction.

The legal support-difference lattice is the kernel of one vector
neighbor-sum congruence at each root row. A character constant on legal
stars, tested by three-slot transfers, has affine slot functions with
one common additive character at that root. Varying the root supplies
exactly the stated root correction. Independent within-row transfers
show that all |V_i| congruences are independent. Hence the exact local
index is |U_i|^|V_i|, independent of d_i. All periods have been included.

The finite-support exponential-tilt lemma is adequate for the relative
estimate: positive covariance gives a smooth local mean-to-tilt map;
compactness gives common positive tilted probabilities and covariance
bounds; maximal-lattice Fourier inversion has a uniform strict modulus
gap off the origin. Its Gaussian integral gives a relative local estimate
uniform at affine lattice targets. Matching means and covariances make
the rate-function difference cubic. With n_i=a_i N and fixed positive a_i:

 P_N(M)/Q_N(M)<=C I exp(C||Delta||^3/N^2),
 I=prod_i |U_i|^|V_i|,

on a fixed small neighborhood, and the ratio is I(1+o(1)) on the legal
lattice in the sqrt(N)log N window. Affine containment holds at every
admissible size; infeasible compatibility only removes terms. A zero
local real dimension contributes probability one. There is no unnoticed
extra residue condition or division by type size.

Under the EXACT normalized weighted Q law, stub-permutation transpositions
change at most four physical incidences. A root-label change affects at
most 2Dmax incidences. Exposing O(N) permutation entries and iid labels
therefore gives a bounded-difference subgaussian tail. Internal loops
produce only O(1) mean bias per table, since their same-owner pairing
probability is (d_ii-1)/(n_i d_ii-1). Cross factors have no such bias.

For T=||Delta||<=epsilon N, the cubic likelihood factor is bounded by
exp(C epsilon T^2/N). Choose epsilon so this exponent is smaller than
half the subgaussian tail exponent. Tail integration from sqrt(N)log N
is then exp(-c log^2 N), with NO polynomial loss. The inner window costs
at most I+o(1), using the exact reference normalizer. This proves the
required finite central limsup without an uncomputed pooled Gaussian
precision or a common-degree assumption.

6. PROPER PHASES, AFFINE FIBERS, AND THE RANK CHARGE

Quotient by a proper phase and subtract its type-constant quotient point
map. That quotient point map is an exact solution in every configuration,
because its incidence counts are fixed d_ij; no exact lift of its chosen
representatives to an ambient old solution is assumed. A fixed quotient
assignment has either no ambient lifts or an affine fiber of cardinality
exactly the restricted homogeneous kernel count Z_H(G).

For support S, size s, an exterior zero vertex cannot receive exactly
one support incidence. With i edges internal to S, j boundary edges,
and t reached exterior vertices:

 2i+j=sum_(v in S)d_v>=dmin s,
 t<=j/2,
 E=i+j<=Dmax s,
 E-(s+t)>=(dmin-2)s/2=beta s, beta>0.

These are incidence counts. A loop contributes twice to support degree,
once to E; configuration support size one is retained.

The forcing couplings are exact. For a cross pair, swapping an image
with the current preimage has a constant number of preimages among the
free stubs. For an internal pair on m free stubs, each output has one
no-switch preimage and two inverse orientations for each of its other
(m-2)/2 pairs, hence m-1 preimages. Earlier prescribed pairs are preserved.
After E switches the graph has precisely its original law conditional
on the prescribed certificate.

A switch changes at most four physical rows. The restricted operator
rank changes by at most 4r_* per switch, also for rectangular two-space
operators. Consequently

 E[Z_H|certificate]<=p^(4r_* E) E Z_H.

This explicitly controls dependence between the rare certificate and
the bulk kernel; there is no independence premise.

7. THE MIXED-DEGREE SUPPORT SUM AND DIMENSION CLOSURE

Every active stub pool has size at least lambda n for some fixed lambda>0.
For s<=epsilon n with epsilon sufficiently small relative to lambda/Dmax,
prescribing E pairs has probability at most (C/n)^E by the exact falling
factorial denominators. Support/exterior placements cost n^(s+t)/(s!t!),
values cost at most p^(r_*s), and selecting/pairing their stubs costs at
most a fixed exponential factor times s^E. Include the conditional
kernel charge above. The remaining powers obey

 s^E n^(s+t-E)/(s!t!)
 <=(s/n)^(beta s)(s^s/s!)(s^t/t!).

Using s^s/s!<=e^s and summing s^t/t!<=e^s gives

 E[number of solutions with s defects]
 <=M_H(n) Dmax s B^s(s/n)^(beta s).

For sufficiently small fixed epsilon the consecutive-term ratio is at
most one half. Summation starts at s=1 and is O(n^-beta)M_H(n).
Thus a proper-phase root-coset neighborhood costs at most
[1+O(n^-beta)]M_H(n). The min/max degree uses are distinct and correct:
dmin controls beta, while Dmax controls forcing and fixed constants.

Induct on the ambient dimension or the sum of side dimensions. The
zero-dimensional moment is one; every proper restriction has smaller
dimension and the same certificate and physical side balance. The central
bound controls the full phase. Finitely many phase neighborhoods cover
all entropy zeros, and the remaining compact feasible region has a
strictly negative entropy maximum. Its exact factorial count, with only
a fixed polynomial type/Stirling loss, is exponentially small. This
closes the full raw-moment bound, not just its exponential rate.

Finite limsup implies a supremum by absorbing finitely many smaller
admissible scales. Fixed field and dimensions give finitely many maps,
so a uniform maximum over R is justified.

8. SIMPLICITY, FIXED PROPORTIONS, AND EXISTENCE

At fixed frame, reversibility fixes the positive proportions; connected
integer frames have rational ratios and hence an integer-multiplier
scaling. Internal parity is the only configuration obstruction. At
sufficiently large admissible scales, each fixed-degree simple internal
or biregular factor exists. The conditional claim is restricted to scales
where all simple factors exist, covering smaller-scale exceptions.

Each present internal factor has positive limiting simplicity probability
exp(-(d_ii^2-1)/4) for d_ii>=1; each cross factor has positive limiting
probability exp(-(d_ij-1)(d_ji-1)/2). Matching factors cause no loss.
The standard disjoint bad-pattern count with vanishing overlapping
contributions justifies these limits for fixed degrees. Their finite
independent product is bounded below. Therefore

 E[Z_R|all simple]<=E Z_R/P(all simple)=O(1).

This is direct conditioning of an unbounded statistic, not contiguity.
Every simple factor has the same number of labeled-stub realizations,
so the resulting law is exactly the intended independent uniform simple
factor law. No conditioning argument asserts preservation of an exact
fixed-prime moment constant.

9. SCALAR REFINEMENT: THE ACTUAL INTEGER CONGRUENCE IMAGE

For nonbipartite support, scalar lambda and odd p, only point phases and
the full scalar phase remain. Point maps number p^t, where
t=dim_Fp ker(D-lambda I). Each has exact zero-defect contribution one
and extra contribution O(N^-beta) by the support sum.

For the uniform phase, retain the actual integer compatible subgroup K0:
physical-slot common rows, pooled cross transposes, and internal symmetry
with even diagonal. At every admissible scale all-zero stars are legal
and compatible. Bounded compatible translations make Q local masses
asymptotically equal in the central window. Exact factorial ratios also
tend uniformly to one: cross leading powers cancel by zero pool total;
internal double-factorial powers plus off-diagonal powers cancel by
one half the symmetric pool total. The reference tail removes the
complement. Hence each coset of K0/(K0 intersect L) has mass 1/J+o(1),
and the uniform coefficient is I/J.

The annihilator computation uses genuine integer directions, so it is
valid even when a multiplicity or a size multiplier vanishes modulo p.
A pooled cross 2-by-2 interaction can be put in one slot and its reverse
in one opposite slot. This forces affine functions h_i(x)=s_i x+c_i,
with s_j=-s_i. A doubled symmetric internal interaction has even diagonal
and forces h_i constant in odd characteristic. If there is no internal
factor, an odd cross cycle kills every alternating slope. Connectivity
therefore leaves only constants c_i.

Their functional on a compatible direction has coefficient
[(D-lambda I)c]_i on the root moment at i; it is D, not D^T. Cross
transpose compatibility turns the incoming pooled column moment into
d_ij times the corresponding root moment. To vary those root moments
freely modulo p, the source realizes twice arbitrary integer zero-total
root margins: cross anchor tables with margins 2d_ij r_i, internal
diagonal tables 2d_ii r_i, then physical-slot decomposition with common
row 2r_i. Pooled internal tables stay symmetric with even diagonal.
Two is invertible, and no degree is divided out.

Thus the annihilator dimension is t, J=p^(kp-t), I=p^(kp), and the
uniform coefficient is p^t. Adding p^t point contributions gives the
configuration-law exact moment 2p^t+o(1), through all admissible scales.

For a fixed integer ell choose arbitrarily large odd primes avoiding
one nonzero maximal minor of D-ell I. Its old modular nullity equals
the rational nullity t_R. Any extra real nullity forces extra modular
nullity by reduction of integer minors. Subtract the deterministic old
p^t_R baseline and use the scalar moment to get limsup failure at most
1/(p-1). Take N first, then these fixed primes. Direct conditioning of
the resulting vanishing real failure event proves the simple-law real
corollary; it does not transfer the exact fixed-prime expectation.

11. COVERED EXAMPLES AND THE ESSENTIAL EXCLUSIONS

Common degree d>=3 has q=1/d on every active factor. If every internal
degree h_i>=2 and there are at least two connected types, each cross
total b_i is positive. Choose epsilon=1/(4D), put all active cross weights
epsilon and q_ii=(1-epsilon b_i)/h_i. These weights are positive; at
h_i=2 strict inequality q_ii<1/2 follows from b_i>0, and at h_i>2 it is
immediate. Every weighted row is one.

For K5 minus an edge, its two degree-three vertices have three edges of
weight 1/3. Each degree-four vertex has two such edges and two edges of
weight 1/6; its row is also one. All active weights satisfy the strict
condition. Thus this genuinely mixed-total-degree no-internal example
is covered.

Minimum degree three alone is not sufficient: K_(3,12) lifts have physical
side populations 3N and 12N, hence nullity at least 9N over every field.
Their zero moment is exponential. The symmetric unit-row balance identity
shows that such a frame cannot satisfy this certificate.

Active q=0 removes control of some omission total correlations; q=1/2
removes a pair mutual-information coefficient. The packet correctly
excludes both. This audit asserts no bounded-moment theorem for those
broader cases and no all-minimum-degree-three result.
