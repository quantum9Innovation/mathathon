FRACTIONAL GRAPH UPDATE VERIFICATION RECORD
==========================================

Scope and outcome
-----------------
graph-fractional-updates.tex/pdf contains two proved deductions:

1. For k distinct signed conductance changes, nonnegative endpoint graphs,
   the correction L'^alpha-L^alpha has a width O_alpha(k log^2(2k/eps))
   approximation with intrinsic operator error eps*||L'-L||^alpha.
   Expected construction cost is ~O_alpha((m+n)k), including all factors.
   A symmetric signed output has at most twice the width. Connectedness
   is unnecessary, so edge deletions may change the kernel.

2. Every PSD positive rank-one fractional correction is a norm limit of
   one-edge updates on connected paths with arbitrary positive masses.
   Supremal strict singular counts are therefore equal at every tolerance.
   The companion PSD theorem then gives exact leading count coefficient
   1/[4*pi^2*alpha*(1-alpha)], or r times it for r edge increases.
   This sharp lower statement is not proved for unit-mass paths.

The algorithm does not claim the sharp leading width coefficient.
The universal count theorem is an existence/optimal-rank result.
The output is a correction, not a recomputation of the base function.
General alpha charges scalar power evaluations. At alpha=1/2, arithmetic,
comparisons, scalar square roots, and fixed-constant series suffice.
No finite-word stability or deterministic runtime bound is asserted.

Priority and goal limits
------------------------
Shifted SDD solvers are due to Koutis--Miller--Peng and predecessors.
Rational low-rank matrix-function updates and graph applications are
established work. The Jacobi-to-weighted-path mechanism is classical.
The new deductions in this worktree are the precise sparse intrinsic-error
bound and exact transport of fractional compression extrema to paths.
Bounded primary-source comparison does not certify their historical novelty.

results. Its mixed-permutation extrema are not premises of this manuscript.
No arbitrary-factor matrix-product improvement or general N^2 result is
proved. The broader user goal remains active.

Additional theorem using the imported graph gap
-----------------------------------------------
expander-polynomial-updates.txt uses the specific Friedman--Bordenave
gap input already carried in spectral-gap/. On a common positive
spectral interval on 1-perp, the correction has width kq and full
factor construction O((m+n)kq), q=O(log(2/eps)) for fixed interval ratio.
The proof uses binomial-tail difference bounds to retain intrinsic
error eps||E||^alpha, and a forward-power/backward-Horner factor identity.
It needs no shifted solver, QR, small eigendecomposition, or update-scale
lower bound. The mechanism is classical polynomial analyticity.

For a fixed-degree uniform simple random regular host, bounded-total-
weight additions retain the common interval on the host good event.
The guarantee holds simultaneously for every such update, including
choices after observing the host. The primary source and model are
checked in expander-polynomial-independent-audit.txt. The exact mixed
permutation extrema are still not used to prove a new AMM guarantee.
