1. MINIMUM SUPPORT AND THE COMPLETE MINIMAL CLASSIFICATION
---------------------------------------------------------
If A tau=(a-b)tau and tau is a sign vector, then tau is balanced:
1^T A tau=D 1^T tau=(a-b)1^T tau, and 2b>0. Thus relative to the plant
there are equally many flips k in each old class. Replacing tau by -tau
changes k to N-k, so an alternative unordered partition has an orientation
with k<=N/2. For N>2t a minimal k=t orientation is unique.

Put w=(sigma-tau)/2. Its supported values are +1 in the old plus class
and -1 in the old minus class. At a supported plus vertex,

 a-b = neighbors in S_+ - neighbors in S_- <= k-1.

Simplicity is used here: distinct internal support neighbors number at
most k-1. Hence k>=t=a-b+1. This assertion is correctly used only after
conditioning simple, not for a configuration graph with loops.

At equality k=t, both inequalities must be tight. Every support vertex
is adjacent internally to ALL t-1 other vertices of its own support set
and to NO vertex of the opposite support set. Thus the support is exactly
two K_t cliques with no cross edge between them. Each support vertex
has exactly b internal and b cross boundary edges, and all its physical
stubs are consumed by this description.

At an exterior plus vertex, Aw=0 requires the number m_v of its internal
neighbors in S_+ to equal its number of cross neighbors in S_-. The
minus statement is identical with the roles reversed. On each side the
sum of these positive multiplicities is bt. Therefore each W side has
at most bt vertices. Equality on both sides means EVERY m_v=1 and is
exactly the leading case. No additional leading topology is omitted.

Its vertex and edge counts are

 v_0=2t+2bt,
 e_0=t(t-1)+4bt=t(a-b)+4bt,
 e_0-v_0=t(a+b-2)=nu.

Each W joins the two support cliques by a two-edge path. Both cliques
are connected since t>=2, so every allowed boundary pattern is connected.
Support vertices have motif degree D>=3 and W vertices motif degree two.

2. NONMINIMAL SUPPORTS AND COLLISION PATTERNS ARE LOWER ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------
The support-exposure estimate in Section 2 is valid in the colored
configuration model. For a fixed h-vertex set S, expose all incident
edges, skipping stubs paired earlier. A collision hits S or an already
touched exterior vertex. While h<=epsilon N, every relevant typed pool
still has Theta_(a,b)(N) unmatched stubs, while collision vertices contain
only O_(a,b)(h) possible target stubs. The conditional collision chance
is therefore at most C h/N at every genuine step.

If e edges lie in S and B leave it, there are e+B actual exposures.
The necessary exterior zero-equation excludes any exterior vertex hit
exactly once, so |T|<=B/2. There are therefore at least

 e+B-|T| >= e+B/2 = Dh/2

collisions. Counting an internal S edge as one exposure while it consumes
two S stubs is essential; the displayed equality handles it correctly.
Padding by dummy noncollisions to Dh steps preserves the conditional
bound. Summing index sets of collision steps gives the stated
2^(Dh)(C h/N)^(Dh/2), with harmless rounding if Dh is odd.

After the support union bound this is [A(h/N)^((D-2)/2)]^h.
Its consecutive ratio has the bound in the source. Consequently the
sum starting at h=2t+2 is

 O(N^(-(D-2)(t+1)))=O(N^(-nu-(D-2))).

The macroscopic Boolean entropy input is also correct. Deleting an
internal neighbor from a slot-symmetric star gives the exact loss

 L=((a-2)/2)(I_++I_-)+b I_cross+J_++J_-.

The comparison law retains the ACTUAL internal conditional one-slot law
and makes opposite-block cross labels independent with their actual
unconditional root marginal. Its KL divergence is J_++bI_cross<=L.
If W is the inferred deleted internal sign and m(X) its conditional mean,
the comparison increases E(W-m(X))^2 by at least

 (a-2) E Var(internal|X)+b Var(opposite_root)
 >= b Var(opposite_root).

The squared variable is in [0,4a^2] under both laws. Pinsker therefore
gives exactly L>=2b^2 r^2(1-r)^2/a^4. This works at a=2,b=1 because
the first variance term may vanish; its strict positivity is not needed.
The root-label multinomials are part of the exact star count. With fixed
degrees, the number of types and factorial errors are polynomial in N,
so r bounded away from 0 and 1 gives an exponentially small probability.

Combining both ranges proves Pr(E_large)=O(N^(-nu-1)), since D>=3.

For a nonleading minimal flip, some exterior multiplicity is at least
two, so the total exterior vertex count is at most 2bt-1. The support
still uses EXACTLY e_0 edges: boundary collisions identify vertices,
not any of its distinct prescribed support-edge incidences. There are
only finitely many possible simple typed patterns. Each has at most
2t+2bt-1 vertices and e_0 edges, hence expected occurrence O(N^(-nu-1))
by its prescribed-stub upper bound. Impossible incidence patterns are
discarded, not interpreted as parallel graph edges. Positive simple
conditioning suffices for this upper bound. When b=1, m_v<=b forbids
all collisions, as the proof states.

3. ALL LEADING BOUNDARY MAPS, WITH NO AUTOMORPHISM FACTOR
-------------------------------------------------------
For fixed labeled support sets S_+,S_- of size t and exterior sets
W_+,W_- of size bt, the four endpoint-type pairs are distinct:

 W_+ -> S_+ internal,  W_+ -> S_- cross,
 W_- -> S_- internal,  W_- -> S_+ cross.

Each function has exactly b preimages at each specified support vertex
and hence (bt)!/(b!)^t choices. The support vertices are a labeled target
SET; they are not abstract indistinguishable vertices. No t! division
belongs in this function count. Each W has only one edge of each listed
kind, so these functions cannot introduce a parallel prescribed edge.

A realized leading flip uniquely determines its W sets and all four
functions. Conversely these prescribed edges saturate every support
vertex, and at every W the +1 and -1 support contributions cancel.
Extra edges among W or elsewhere do not affect the flip. Thus no induced
subgraph condition or additional completion constraint is required.

It follows EXACTLY that the number of leading-data choices is

 A_N=binom(N,t)^2 binom(N-t,bt)^2 [(bt)!/(b!)^t]^4.

Its leading coefficient is

 A_N ~ N^(v_0) ((bt)!)^2/[(t!)^2(b!)^(4t)].

The two W-binomial denominators cancel two, not all four, of the (bt)!
numerators from the functions. Counting flip SETS and using N>2t removes
the otherwise possible drawing-automorphism and global-orientation
ambiguities. There is no missing factor two.

4. EXACT STUB PROBABILITY AND THE SIMPLICITY RATIO
-------------------------------------------------
Each support vertex prescribes all a internal and all b cross neighbors,
contributing a! b! physical stub assignments. Each W prescribes one stub
from each distinct pool, contributing ab, not D(D-1). Thus

 c_H=(a!b!)^(2t)(ab)^(2bt).

Different assignments are disjoint. A configuration containing the motif
has all support stubs matched to its required distinct neighbors. A
second copy of any required support-edge would force the loss of a
different required neighbor. Therefore its actual matching determines
the physical stub assignment uniquely, even before global simplicity.

The internal prescribed edge count is e_int=t(a-b)/2+bt on EACH side,
and the cross count is 2bt. One physical assignment has probability

 P_N^*=prod_(j=0,...,e_int-1)(aN-2j-1)^(-2)
           *[(bN)_(2bt)]^(-1).

There is no undirected-edge orientation factor. The unconditioned event
probability is exactly c_H P_N^*.

After that assignment, residual pools are uniform and independent.
S vertices have residual degrees zero in both pools; W vertices have
residual internal degree a-1 and cross degree b-1. Every prescribed edge
has an S endpoint with residual degree ZERO. Hence a residual edge
cannot duplicate any prescribed edge. Full simplicity is exactly the
intersection of the three residual-factor simplicity events. This is
stronger than the assertion that conditioning costs a positive factor.

The residual degree lists are IDENTICAL up to labels for every leading
boundary topology and every physical stub assignment. In particular
s_N^* is the same finite-N probability for all those choices.

An explicit check of the limiting simplicity parameters is useful.
The internal residual stub total and its normalized second factorial
moment are

 L_a=aN-tD,
 gamma_a^*=sum_v r_v(r_v-1)/L_a
           =(a-1)(1-bt/L_a) -> a-1.

On either cross side they are

 L_b=bN-2bt,
 gamma_b^*=(b-1)(1-bt/L_b) -> b-1.

The bounded-degree pairing defect calculation therefore gives loop and
double-edge means gamma_a^*/2 and (gamma_a^*)^2/4 in each internal
factor, and cross double-edge mean (gamma_b^*)^2/2. These are the same
limits as before prescription. Thus BOTH s_N and s_N^* tend to

 s_infty=exp[-(a^2-1)/2-(b-1)^2/2]>0,

and s_N^*/s_N=1+o(1), uniformly over all data. This supplies precisely
the RATIO required for the leading coefficient. At b=1 the residual
cross degrees are at most one, so that factor is automatically simple.

The residual internal totals are even because
tD=(a-b)(a-b+1)+2b(a-b+1) is even; cross totals agree on both sides.
There is no new parity or residue-class restriction.

Primary reference checked for the bounded-degree simplicity method:
  Janson, arXiv:1307.6344v1, Theorem 1.2 and Section 5 / Theorem 5.3,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.6344 .

5. RECONSTRUCTION OF THE EXACT COEFFICIENT
------------------------------------------
The internal matching denominators contribute
a^(-t(a-b)-2bt)N^(-2e_int), and the cross denominator contributes
b^(-2bt)N^(-2bt). The W factor (ab)^(2bt) cancels both boundary-degree
powers. Therefore one leading pattern has simple-model probability

 (1+o(1))(a!b!)^(2t)a^(-t(a-b)) N^(-e_0).

Multiplying by the exact data count yields

 EX ~ N^(-nu)
      ((bt)!)^2(a!b!)^(2t)
        /[(t!)^2(b!)^(4t)a^(t(a-b))]
    = C N^(-nu).

Independent small-degree algebra checks give

These are BLOCK-size-N constants. Rewriting in n=2N multiplies them
by 2^nu. The proof claims a first-order asymptotic, not an unproved
O(N^(-nu-1)) remainder for EX: the 1+o(1) simplicity ratio is sufficient.

6. PROPER INTERSECTIONS AND THE FACTORIAL SECOND MOMENT
-------------------------------------------------------
The graph lemma used in Section 8 is correct for every connected H of
minimum degree at least two. If F spans H but is proper, at least one
edge is missing. Otherwise delete r>0 vertices. With E_R removed-set
internal edges and B>=1 edges to the nonempty retained set,

 2E_R+B>=2r,  E_R+B>=r+B/2>r.

Thus at least r+1 edges are removed. Further deletions among retained
vertices only reduce excess, proving
 e(F)-v(F)<=e(H)-v(H)-1.
This includes retained isolated vertices; dropping common isolated
vertices from v(F) would invalidate the application, but the source
explicitly includes them.

All leading motifs have support vertices exactly identifiable by motif
degree D>2. Thus identical vertex-and-edge images imply the same support
sets. A realized support set determines exactly one leading boundary
data choice, as checked above. Different data with the same support
cannot co-occur. Therefore the diagonal excess-nu image case is truly
absent from X(X-1), not merely renamed.

For any proper nonempty intersection F, including all shared vertices
and edges, the typed union J has

 e(J)-v(J)=2nu-[e(F)-v(F)] >= nu+1.

For fixed a,b there are finitely many pattern and overlap identification
types. A compatible union has O(N^(v(J))) placements and prescribed-stub
probability O(N^(-e(J))) in the actual simple model; incompatible degree
or color identifications have probability zero. Disjoint motifs have
excess 2nu, which is at least nu+1 since nu>=2. No independence of
disjoint occurrences is assumed or needed.

Consequently E[X(X-1)]=O(N^(-nu-1)), and the two elementary integer
bounds in Section 8 give

 Pr(X>=1)=EX+O(N^(-nu-1)),
 Pr(X>=2)=O(N^(-nu-1)).

Outside E_large union E_collision, every alternative partition is one
unique leading minimum flip, so K=1+X. This proves the stated ambiguity
asymptotic and Pr(K>=3)=O(N^(-nu-1)). In particular the conditional
probability of exactly two states on ambiguity is 1-O(1/N).

7. POSTERIOR NORMALIZATION AND THE UNIQUE FACTOR ONE-HALF
---------------------------------------------------------
For a fixed balanced planted assignment, every compatible labeled graph
has exactly the likelihood 1/[G_a(N)^2 B_b(N)]. Its internal and cross
decomposition is uniquely determined by the assignment and G. These
component counts depend only on the class sizes and degrees. Thus a
uniform hidden balanced prior gives an exactly uniform posterior on
valid ordered signs, and exactly two such signs per unordered partition.
The posterior on K(G) unordered valid partitions is uniform.

The Bayes conditional success is exactly 1/K(G), attained by ANY verified
valid partition, including a deterministic selection depending only on
G. Hence R_N=E[1-1/K(G)]. The hidden-model ambiguity probability equals
the fixed-plant probability because the event is invariant under all
vertex relabelings. Its existing size bias through K in the marginal
graph law does not authorize another factor-two correction.

On K=2 the error is one-half; on K>=3 it lies between two-thirds and one.
Therefore

 0<=R_N-(1/2)Pr(K>=2)<=(1/2)Pr(K>=3)
                         =O(N^(-nu-1)).

This proves exactly C/2 as the leading Bayes coefficient. With a known
deterministic plant there would be a trivial zero-error constant output,
so the explicitly stated hidden-prior qualification is essential.

8. OPTIONAL POLYNOMIAL ESTIMATOR: ACTUAL PREMISE ALSO CHECKED
-------------------------------------------------------------
The aggregate-fiber source was read, not merely assumed from an older
summary. Its exact fiber count includes all ordered vertex and slot
arrays. Fiber permutation invariance gives their exact marginal edge
tables; entropy subadditivity and the a>=2 star inequality cancel the
exponential pairing weights. There are only polynomially many aggregate
table triples. Its explicit N exponent is 5p^2+p+2, equal to 50 at p=3.
Conditioning the three fixed-degree factors simple preserves this bound
on every admissible size sequence. Thus the optional premise

 E_simple |ker_F3(A-(a-b)I)|=O_(a,b)(N^50)

is supported by its actual source.

For algorithmic clarity, enumerate the field kernel, reject vectors with
zero coordinates, lift residues 1 to +1 and 2 to -1, and verify the
ORIGINAL INTEGER equation (or exactly b opposite neighbors per vertex).
Modulo-three satisfaction alone is insufficient, but the source explicitly
requires integer verification. Every true sign solution appears in this
list because the two signs are distinct modulo three. Passing vectors
are automatically balanced, as in Section 1.

Cap the kernel size at N^(50+nu+2). Markov bounds the cap event by
O(N^(-nu-2))=o(N^(-nu)). The cap decision and every subsequent operation
depend only on the observed graph and known parameters. A base-three
odometer changes O(1) digits amortized, so adding the corresponding
kernel basis columns costs O(N) amortized per field vector. Fixed-degree
integer verification is O(N) per vector. Gaussian elimination and all
index/counter bit lengths are polynomial. The running time is therefore
a deterministic fixed polynomial, albeit with a large theoretical
degree-dependent exponent.

On a completed run at least one verified partition exists and selecting
any of them is conditionally Bayes-optimal, including when K>=2. On a
cap event the additional error is at most one. The estimator's Bayes
risk is consequently between R_N and R_N+O(N^(-nu-2)), and has the same
leading constant C/2. Reporting ambiguity itself as an automatic error
would instead lose that coefficient; the audited source does not do so.

FINAL SCOPE
-----------
This PASS concerns fixed a>b>0, the stated independent uniform-simple
component distributions, all sufficiently large admissible N, and the
uniform hidden prior for the statistical assertion. Randomness of the
cross factor is used in both typed counts and leading motif probability;
the exact coefficient is not asserted for an adversarial fixed cross
matrix. The result concerns Boolean partition ambiguity. It does not
provide a matching probability asymptotic for arbitrary extra real
eigenvectors, an analytic eigengap, noisy observations, or growing degrees.
