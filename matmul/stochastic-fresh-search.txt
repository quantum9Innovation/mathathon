========================================

Nothing in this note completes that objective. Only this note is agent-owned.

Starting interfaces
-------------------
M=AB, I=m(n+p). The audited coarse support gives M=Z L B with
G=Z^T Z approximately I and Q=LBB^T L^T. Near the top squared norm c,
D_s=sG-GQG/c is positive definite at a safe shift, with minimum eigenvalue
mu. A symmetric unbiased degree-three raw-row batch estimator has constant
second moment and O(m^2) action cost (batch m). Generic signed SVRG+APPA
therefore costs ~I+I^(3/4)m^(1/2)/sqrt(mu). This is ALREADY AUDITED, not new.

The practical literature note's linear mass surrogate and balanced-stack
lemma do not change that exponent. The raw_partial_Krylov and negative-inertia
directions have other agents; this search initially isolates variance
cancellation, volume sampling, inverse-Wishart and conditional regression.

1. Convex component caution and batch tradeoff
----------------------------------------------
If Q=sum u_i u_i^T<=I and Y=u_i u_i^T/p_i is an unbiased raw covariance
sample, I-Y need not be positive semidefinite. A block of b samples only
reduces typical norm deviations by sqrt(m/b); making every shifted block
convex at a shift gap mu costs b of order m/mu^2 (and logarithms), unless
there is an additional construction. A coarse mean proxy does not provide
a PSD raw-feature representation for its residual.

More generally, if hypothetical components H_i have spectrum in [-h,C]
and mean D>=mu I, the scalar inequality on [-h,C] gives
 E H_i^2 <= (C-h)D+Ch I.
Thus the relevant SVRG condition contains C/mu+Ch/mu^2. Ordinary row
blocks with h~sqrt(m/b) and cost d=b*m do not improve the product d*h:
for b>=m, d*h=b^(1/2)m^(3/2)>=m^2. The existing constant-size batch scale
already minimizes this elementary tradeoff. This is a scoped calculation,
not an algorithmic lower bound.

2. Exact zero-variance regression phenomenon, and its missing bridge
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Volume-rescaled least squares is promising because any sampled full-rank
regression recovers x EXACTLY when labels satisfy y=A x. This is much
stronger than mean control: regression error is tied to residual energy,
and can have 1/epsilon rather than 1/epsilon^2 sample dependence.

For K=t P^{-1}-Q, P=A^TA, one tempting construction is to regress a
Gaussian label vector y with covariance t I_n-MM^T. Then the covariance
of the exact regression solution is
 P^{-1} A^T(t I_n-MM^T)A P^{-1}=tP^{-1}-Q=K.
This identity is correct, and would turn K into positive regression
features. But generating y already requires a factor of the unknown PSD
complement tI-MM^T. At Q=tP^{-1}, generating the coupled cancellation
requires an isometry between the two factor ranges. Neither follows from
coarse whitening or raw independent sampling. The identity by itself is
circular and is not an algorithm.

Independent Gaussian sketches produce Wishart(P) and Wishart(Q), and a
degrees-of-freedom correction makes the inverse of the first unbiased for
P^{-1}. That does not make their difference low variance when Q=P^{-1}.
An exact coupling through a common latent standard Gaussian needs the
true covariance roots or equivalent regression/isometry operations; a
full-width such transform costs I*m with ordinary arithmetic.

An orthogonal projection of raw A rows gives a genuine PSD lower Gram
A^T Pi A, but an O(m)-row random projection captures only O(m/n) of P
when n>>m. Rescaling it to an OSE loses the raw PSD complement. Thus
conditioning a Gaussian raw sketch on its row span does not by itself
produce a cheap small-residual Gaussian covariance.

3. Determinant tilts and the negative-ridge obstacle
---------------------------------------------------
For rank-one q_i, det(lambda I-sum_i z_i q_i) is multiaffine in z_i.
When lambda I-Q>0, all unscaled subset downdates lambda I-Q_S are SPD.
Bernoulli subset sums of determinants and adjugates give the determinant
and inverse of lambda I-sum_i p_i q_i. This is an exact algebraic identity,
but p_i<1 moves away from Q. Rescaling q_i/p_i to preserve Q can make
subset downdates indefinite. Thus this simple negative-volume analogue
does not supply a positive inexpensive unbiased inverse sampler at the
near-critical shift.

Likewise an L-ensemble DPP samples PSD ridge geometry Q(Q+lambda I)^-1.
The desired complement / negative-ridge geometry has L=Q(I-Q)^-1,
whose construction requires the very shifted inverse being sought. Fast
positive-volume samplers cannot be invoked as a free negative-ridge oracle.

4. Recent sample reuse: query counts are not automatically arithmetic
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Jin--Karmarkar--Sidford--Wang, Reusing Samples in Variance Reduction,
ALT 2026, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v313/jin26a.html, gives a broad
pseudo-independence framework. The primary source explicitly distinguishes
batch/sample QUERY counts from runtime, and says its general FSM result
does not automatically yield a worst-case runtime improvement.

Its covariance tradeoff sqrt(alpha/gap) full batch queries and
~sr(A)/alpha^2 distinct reused row queries is real. Reusing a row still
requires new dot products at adaptively changing vectors in arithmetic.
Materializing a fixed quadratic subsolver can avoid repeated dots, but
constructing its full m-by-m action from k rank-one updates costs k*m^2
classically (or must supply a better product algorithm). No new factor
runtime is presently inferred just by charging each distinct row once.

Primary volume sources checked for their exact scope:
 Derezinski--Warmuth, Reverse Iterative Volume Sampling for Linear
 Regression, JMLR 2018, https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/v19/17-781.html.
 Derezinski--Warmuth--Hsu, Correcting the bias in least squares regression
 with volume-rescaled sampling, JMLR 2022,
 https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume23/19-571/19-571.pdf.
 Derezinski--Warmuth, Leveraged volume sampling for linear regression,
 NeurIPS 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.06749.

5. A precise antithetic Gaussian test (new check, negative result)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Even in dimension one, sharing Gaussian randomness is the WRONG sign for
cancelling an inverse-Wishart estimate against a Wishart covariance.
Let (x_l,y_l), l=1,...,s, be independent joint centered Gaussian pairs,
Var(x)=p, Var(y)=q, correlation rho, s>4. Put
 U=sum x_l^2/p, V=sum y_l^2/q,
 Hhat=(s-2)/(p U), Qhat=q V/s.
Then E Hhat=1/p, E Qhat=q and
 Cov(Hhat,Qhat)=-2 q rho^2/(p s).
Indeed E[V|U]=rho^2 U+s(1-rho^2), and
Cov(1/U,U)=1-s/(s-2)=-2/(s-2).
At the exact cancellation threshold t=pq,
 Var(t Hhat-Qhat)
   =q^2[2/(s-4)+2/s+4 rho^2/s].
The minimum over Gaussian correlations is attained at rho=0. A common
Gaussian seed increases this variance rather than suppressing it. This
does NOT exclude non-Gaussian quantile/volume couplings or matrix-aware
transport; it prevents invoking antithetic Gaussian coupling without a
different mechanism.

An exact Gaussian sketch with s=Theta(m) independent rows costs I*s by
ordinary multiplication. Fast OSE application does not retain the exact
Wishart distribution. One cannot simultaneously cite fast OSE cost and
exact Wishart inverse identities without an extra distributional proof.

6. Positive Poisson-volume inverse, and why the negative version changes
------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is a clean exact algebraic positive-ridge identity. Let
 P=sum_i u_i u_i^T, choose probabilities p_i>0, let N~Poisson(gamma),
and draw i_1,...,i_N independently. Set
 S=lambda I+sum_{l=1}^N u_{i_l}u_{i_l}^T/(gamma p_{i_l}), lambda>0.
Poisson factorial moments and rank-one multiaffinity give
 E det S=det(lambda I+P), E adj S=adj(lambda I+P).
Under determinant tilting by det S / det(lambda I+P),
 E_tilt S^{-1}=(lambda I+P)^{-1}.
All sample matrices are SPD, so the tilt is nonnegative and
0<S^{-1}<=lambda^{-1}I. In particular its centered second moment obeys
 E_tilt[(S^{-1}-H)^2] <=lambda^{-1}H-H^2,
where H=(lambda I+P)^{-1}. This is a useful operator bound, not just an
entrywise inverse-mean formula. A complete algorithm must still implement
the determinant tilt with the appropriate known volume/DPP sampler.

Replacing + by - breaks the positivity that makes this a probability law:
the Poisson number of repeated downdates can exceed the safe total
covariance even if lambda I-P>0. Restricting to unscaled subsets keeps
each sample safe, but averages to lambda I-sum p_i u_i u_i^T, rather than
lambda I-P. Rescaling the selected terms restores the desired mean but
again can make individual downdates indefinite. This isolates the exact
probabilistic obligation for a negative-volume proposal.

For fixed-design unregularized volume subsets, the commonly quoted exact
inverse expectation needs a general-position/support qualification: zero
determinant, rank-(m-1) subsets can have nonzero adjugates. Regression
solution unbiasedness is more robust because the adjugate annihilates
the rank-deficient normal-equation RHS. Do not transfer a regression
unbiasedness statement to a naked inverse without checking this issue.
For example A has rows (1,0),(0,1),(1,0), and size-2 volume sampling
chooses either of the two full-rank pairs, each with Gram I. Thus
E[(A_S^T A_S)^{-1}]=I, whereas the general-position formula would be
2(A^TA)^{-1}=diag(1,2). The rank-one pair has zero determinant but a
nonzero adjugate, exactly the missing term.

7. Positive-ridge Cayley reformulation of the threshold
------------------------------------------------------
For SPD P,Q, a,b>0 and t=ab,
 t P^{-1}-Q>=0 iff a(P+aI)^{-1}+b(Q+bI)^{-1}>=I.
This follows by adding bI to Q<=ab P^{-1}, inverting, and using
 b[ab P^{-1}+bI]^{-1}=P(P+aI)^{-1}=I-a(P+aI)^{-1}.
The corresponding inertia equality follows from inverse-difference
congruence (or Schur complements), not just a commuting scalar argument.

At a=b=sqrt(t), this is the sum of two Cayley transforms. Each positive
ridge inverse has bounded positive volume estimators / projector-valued
representations. However their sum-minus-I is still signed per sample.
A coupling making every paired sum positive would be a genuine new
convexification. Its existence follows abstractly when the mean is PSD,
but sampling it appears to require the unknown complement geometry.
Taking a tiny and b=t/a scales both signal and optimal batch variance by
the same factor; it does not by itself improve the signed-SVRG condition.

8. Lorentz-cone barrier bootstrap check
---------------------------------------
For index-1 K, -log(-x^T K x) on one negative-cone component has barrier
parameter 2. This does not imply a cheap raw-factor algorithm. One first
needs an interior negative witness; for K=diag(-eps,1,...,1), a random
unit vector enters the negative cone only with probability exponentially
small in dimension when eps is small. The barrier Hessian is a fixed
multiple of K plus rank 2, so Newton inverses hide K^{-1}. More broadly,
even an ellipsoidal barrier of constant parameter can encode an arbitrary
SPD linear solve. A bootstrap or a new implicit Hessian solver is needed.

The CARE agent has a promising PSD residual certificate for the positive
part, but no cheap solve yet. Low-rank inverse-free polynomial CARE/sign
iterations remain block Krylov algorithms, so mere low-rank truncation
cannot remove the known worst-case accuracy-dependent action count.

9. What the new positive-volume identity does and does not buy
-------------------------------------------------------------
The Poisson choice in Section 6 is convenient, not essential. If the
independent scalar reweightings c_i are nonnegative and E c_i=1, then
rank-one multiaffinity directly gives
 E det(lambda I+sum c_i u_i u_i^T)=det(lambda I+P)
and the same equality for the adjugate. Positive lambda makes every
sample invertible, avoiding the fixed-design general-position problem.

A negative-volume convexification would need a cheaply generated
unbiased sampled covariance Qhat satisfying Qhat<=lambda I on every
outcome. At the boundary Q=lambda I, PSD and unbiasedness force
Qhat=Q almost surely, since E tr(lambda I-Qhat)=0. This is a useful
stress test for claims based on raw subset sampling: at a generic tight
frame the capped estimator cannot retain ordinary sampling noise.
This observation is not a general complexity lower bound. For example
exact isotropy can itself be tested with one true randomized probe, so
an adaptive algorithm could have special equality branches.

The creative open direction is a positive hyperbolic/volume measure on
LOW-INDEX WITNESSES or CARE residual factors, rather than a positive
measure on full shifted covariances. It would have to avoid both the
negative-ridge cap above and the already-known Krylov query barrier by
using the raw separate factors in a genuine new way. No sampler or
residual-reducing iteration with the required arithmetic cost is known
from this investigation.
