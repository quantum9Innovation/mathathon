ADVERSARIAL RSBM AUDIT: RESULTS AND EXACT SCOPE
2026-11-15. No historical novelty claim is made.

1. THE LOW-DEGREE CASE IS POSITIVE, NOT A COUNTEREXAMPLE

For two independent uniform simple 2-regular blocks of size N and an
independent uniform perfect matching across them,

  P[ker_R(A-I)=span(sigma)] -> 1

along ALL N tending to infinity. There is no even-N restriction.

The complete assembled argument is in the cooperating moment branch:
  ../rsbm-cubic-moment-session/full-proof.md
with entropy-types.md, central-types.md, and near-constant.md there.
I read all four files and independently audited their assembly. The
counting, entropy identity, equality classification, Gaussian constants,
integer lattice, sparse-support count, conditioning, and sequential-prime
argument all pass. One definition was corrected during audit: the
symmetric lattice K must additionally require even diagonal entries;
the numerical covolume calculation had already included this factor.

Our independent derivations, kept in this directory, are:
  finite-field-21-entropy.txt    exact type count, entropy, equality cases
  uniform-saddle-21.txt         covariance, Gaussian and lattice constants
  near-constant-21.txt          uniform sparse-support enumeration

For each fixed odd prime p, in the two degree-two configuration blocks,

  E |ker_Fp(A-I)| = 2p+o_p(1).

The uniform phase contributes p, and the p planted constant phases
contribute p+o(1). All other types have a strictly negative entropy gap.
The uniform coefficient keeps the crucial lattice correction: the joint
profile lattice has index p^3 beyond independent-star lattices, while
pairable symmetric tables leave two independent congruences, costing
p^2. Thus the net coefficient is p, rather than an uncontrolled C_p.

Both configuration blocks are simple with probability s_N->exp(-3/2).
Subtracting the p deterministic planted vectors before conditioning gives

  limsup_N E_simple |ker_Fp(A-I)| <= (1+exp(3/2)) p.

Real nullity >=2 forces nullity >=2 over EVERY F_p (all integer minors
of order 2N-1 vanish). Therefore

  limsup_N P[real nullity>=2] <= exp(3/2)/(p-1).

Let odd primes tend to infinity only AFTER the N limit superior. This
proves the claim; no uniformity for growing p was used.

2. LARGER INTERNAL DEGREE: AN INDEPENDENT SOURCE-BASED PROOF

source-audit.md section 8 derives simplicity for d1>=3 and EVEN block N
directly from Meszaros, arXiv:1806.03736v4, Theorem 1.6. Two independent
internal sums of perfect matchings give E|ker_Fp|<=4p+o(1) for every fixed
odd p not dividing d2, uniformly over any independent cross matrix with
row and column sums d2. GJKW Corollary 1.1 transfers this to independent
uniform simple d1-regular internal blocks, including d1=3. There is no
need to replace the cross graph by a sum of matchings.

This thread does NOT supply an odd-block-N extension for even d1>=4;
other root branches are handling that case. For odd d1 admissibility
already forces N even. Do not silently infer the remaining parity case
from the perfect-matching mixing theorem.

3. IDENTIFIABILITY IS DISTINCT FROM REAL SIMPLICITY

combinatorics-21.txt independently proves that the expected number of
alternative equitable (2,1) bisections is O(N^-2). In particular the
planted bisection is unique up to exchange with probability 1-O(N^-2).
This uses an exact flip-overlap first moment and analytic entropy
negativity, with a uniform small-overlap bound and direct simple-model
conditioning. It does not itself prove real simplicity.

For example, exact Bareiss certificates in certified-small-collisions.json
give N=30 instances with dim ker(A-I)=2 but only the planted sign pair.
For a two-dimensional kernel, every nonconstant gauge eigenvector has
the same number of distinct coordinate values under affine change;
more than two values rules out a second sign vector. Two recorded
examples have four and nine such values, respectively. These finite
instances are not asymptotic counterexamples.

The cooperating operator branch also gives an independent fallback:
  ../rsbm-cubic-operator-session/POLYNOMIAL-RECOVERY.md
The nonpositive entropy exponent already implies a polynomial bound on
E 2^dim ker_F2(A-I). Enumerating the binary affine solutions of
(A+I)z=1 with a polynomial cutoff and exact checks, together with Boolean
rigidity, yields polynomial recovery with probability 1-O(N^-2).
This fallback proof was independently read and passes without the sharp
uniform-saddle calculation.

4. WHY A SPECTRAL-RADIUS PROOF WOULD HAVE FAILED

The Petersen graph has a (2,1) partition: its outer and inner 5-cycles,
joined by the spoke matching. Its spectrum is 3, 1 with multiplicity 5,
and -2 with multiplicity 4. In the matching relabeling A=[[C,I],[I,D]],
where C is the step-one 5-cycle and D the step-two 5-cycle. One checks
C+D=DC=J-I, so the reduced matrix C+D-DC is identically zero.

Lifts retain all these old eigenvectors. Bordenave's random-lift theorem
bounds the NEW spectrum by 2sqrt(2)+o(1), while the old eigenvalue 1 still
has multiplicity at least five. This gives admissible near-Ramanujan
(2,1) support graphs with repeated planted eigenvalue. Their lift law is
a different, rare subensemble of the desired model. It proves that a
new-spectrum radius bound alone cannot establish in-bulk simplicity;
it does not contradict the positive asymptotic theorem above.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04482 .

Fixed compact-support cycle/twin motifs also cannot give a constant-
probability obstruction for the requested degrees. If a nonzero
eigenvector has support k in a d-regular graph, its full incident motif
has every outside neighbor touching the support at least twice, hence
edge-minus-vertex excess >=(d/2-1)k>0. Every fixed colored motif has
expected O(N^(vertices-edges)) embeddings in this planted model.
This excludes fixed-size motifs, not growing supports, by itself.

5. SOURCE WARNINGS THAT REMAIN IMPORTANT

source-audit.md records exact primary sources and scopes:

  Recovery and Rigidity: https://arxiv.org/html/1507.00930v4
  Barucca: https://arxiv.org/html/1610.02668v5
  Meszaros: https://arxiv.org/html/1806.03736v4
  GJKW: https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~csg/papers/gjkw-revised.pdf

Zhu--Zhu Definition 3.9 is the requested uniform-component model. Their
Theorem 3.10(3) and Corollary 3.11 require even d1 and the separated
inequality (d1-d2)^2>4(d1+d2-1). Lemma 9.1 has an overbroad statement:
its cited random-lift proof only excludes collisions in that separated
regime. The introduction explicitly calls full efficient recovery open.
Our positive proofs are deductions/arguments here, not an application
of that blanket lemma, and historical priority has not been established.

Uniform simple degree-two blocks and permutation loop lifts are NOT
contiguous. A uniform permutation gives a simple 2-factor weight
proportional to 2^(number of cycles), whereas the requested law is
uniform. GJKW explicitly excludes total degree two. All cubic arguments
above use the direct configuration pairing count and condition simple.

6. COMPUTATIONAL MODEL AND VERIFICATION

Given fixed parameters, lambda=d1-d2 is a known integer. If the kernel
is the planted line, exact rational Gaussian elimination recovers sigma
up to scale and sign in polynomial bit complexity. For fixed degrees,
Hadamard bounds every minor of A-lambda I by
(d1+d2+lambda^2)^(n/2), so its bit length is O(n). No macroscopic
eigenvalue gap, numerical rounding assumption, or real-RAM oracle is
needed. Unknown parameter identification is a separate specification;
the statements here fix d1,d2 as in the question.

kernel-experiment.js and kernel-experiment-results.json record seeded
exploratory modular-rank samples. A full-rank cofactor modulo one prime
certifies real simplicity; larger modular corank is only a candidate
until independently certified. exact-kernel.js and the collision JSON
give exact integer witnesses for three small examples.

check-small-types.js and small-exact-moments.json exhaustively compare
the exact type formula with all distinguished-stub pairings for N=1,2,3
over p=2,3. Every numerator agrees: for p=2, 2,36,744; for p=3,
3,33,1323; denominators are respectively 1,9,225. These checks validate
the counting convention but do not replace the asymptotic proof.
