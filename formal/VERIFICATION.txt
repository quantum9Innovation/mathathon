Lean verification of hierarchical absolute spectral approximation

Verified scope

  The main fixed-depth and growing-depth results are proved for the original
  matrix/rank/accuracy/confidence inputs. The optimized, absolute-error and
  two-level corollaries, the literal exact-Gram appendix, all four named
  lemmas and the scoped polynomial degree lower bound are also proved.
  The final integrated run checks all 702 local/supporting proof modules
  and 22 exported axiom closures. Final source/dependency rehash passes.
  See THEOREM_MAP.txt for exact file and declaration names.

Reproduce the check

  Lean 4.31.0 and mathlib revision
  fabf563a7c95a166b8d7b6efca11c8b4dc9d911f are pinned, together with the
  dependency manifest. From a checkout with the pinned toolchain installed:

    cd formal
    lake exe cache get
    python3 verify.py --jobs 2 --all-local
    lake env lean MainClosedAudit.lean

  A normal lake build checks the public library. verify.py additionally
  checks every local module, compiles in dependency order with bounded
  concurrency, rejects proof escapes, rehashes the complete final build
  graph and checks the transitive axioms of the public results. Its
  --resume-log option reuses previous successes only after Lake rehashes
  and revalidates every reused module and its dependency closure.

  MainClosedAudit.lean prints the exact theorem types and original-input
  structures. Its 22 axiom lists contain only Lean's standard
  propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. There is no sorryAx or extra
  mathematical axiom. Source checks also reject admissions, native_decide,
  unsafe declarations and kernel-check suppression.

Read the statements directly

  1. MainProblem.lean: FixedProblem contains A, B, theta, delta and the
     supplied schedule, with m<=min(n,p), 1<=r<=m, depth>=1, positive
     accuracies, theta<=h_L, h_0<=1/64 and ratios<=1/1024.

  2. MainFeasibleKernel.lean: fixed_depth_from_feasible_kernel_polylog
     takes a genuine feasible matrix-multiplication family plus that
     FixedProblem. ArithmeticKernel stores a finite arithmetic circuit
     and its proved product semantics; cost is its actual circuit cost.
     The theorem constructs the classical safeguard internally. It gives
     admissibility, width/rank<=r, the actual explicit factorization C=UV,
     the manuscript cost expression with a proved polylogarithmic factor,
     and a constructed measurable event of probability at least1-delta
     on which ||AB-C||_2<=(1+theta)*sigma_(r+1)(AB).

  3. MainFamilyProgramFinal.lean: growingDepthProgram_family_theorem
     fixes the kernel from d before the input sequence or asymptotic
     epsilon. It states the exact interval 1/8<d<(sqrt(2)-1)/2,
     theta=(m/N)^(5/2), r<=floor(sqrt(m)), polynomial confidence lower
     bound, computed scalar schedule and O(log log m) depth. It proves
     both the unfolded runtime bound and total N^(2+o(1)) explicit-output
     work, using the same program as the probability guarantee.

  4. CorollaryOptimizedFinal.lean, CorollaryAbsoluteFamilyFinal.lean,
     CorollaryAbsoluteUniformCaps.lean and CorollaryTwoLevel.lean provide
     the direct corollary callers. The absolute event is chosen before
     epsilon and gives accuracy, width<=2*r_e and conditional small-rank
     runtime together; r_e=0 is included and is not an algorithm input.
     Its uniform cap is a finite maximum of explicit circuit/scalar
     budgets over finite preprocessing states, not a supremum of runtime.

  5. ExactGramCharpolyAppendixCost.lean and its SoftCost companion connect
     actual characteristic coefficients, counted inertia search, jitter,
     fresh Gaussian congruence, LDL, the dual lift and range/output code.
     Both factor writes and the dense product are charged. Almost-sure
     success and all-outcome finite costs are separate proved properties.

Conventions that matter

  tailSingularValue M r uses Mathlib's ZERO-based singularValues. It is
  exactly manuscript sigma_(r+1)(M), extended by zero beyond the dimension.
  Matrix.Norms.L2Operator selects the spectral/operator norm. MatrixOrder
  selects Loewner order. Output width may be zero or less than target r.
  Deficient factors, repeated singular values, zero products and zero
  optimal tails are included by the support/exact branches.

  Spectral calculus, roots of characteristic polynomials, eigenbases and
  integrals may appear in specifications and proofs. The executed programs
  use only their declared field operations, comparisons, positive scalar
  square roots and finite Gaussian arrays; there is no executed SVD/Ritz
  or eigenvalue oracle. Their admissibility and operation bounds are proved.

Verified implementation choices

  The main program uses a proved Gaussian range initializer and a PSD
  support reduction in place of the prose's coefficient initializer and
  product-support route. It proves the same original-input guarantees and
  bounds. The literal coefficient-ratio/shifted-inertia construction is
  independently proved in the exact-Gram appendix.

  The absolute controller doubles through the core dimension before its
  dense fallback, instead of switching at sqrt(m) to the companion route.
  The same-event small-rank accuracy/width/runtime and all-outcome finite
  cap are proved for that actual controller. The ordinary-scaling optimized
  comparator uses the constant-distortion exact-field realization allowed
  in the manuscript, with the claimed block-Krylov upper exponent.

  These results concern exact-real arithmetic counts, not floating-point
  stability, bit complexity, a measured crossover, or priority over the
  literature. The comparator exponents are upper bounds, not lower bounds
  for every competing algorithm.
